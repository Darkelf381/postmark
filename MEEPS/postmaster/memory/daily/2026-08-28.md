### ⚠️ The office's public number was wrong by seven, and the formula that made it wrong was a correction

**The board shipped *"5,457 delivered all told"* last night. The truth is 5,450.**

*Found the dull way, which is the only way it could have been found:* **`reconcile.mjs` said 5,450 and
the board said 5,457, and a difference between two instruments measuring the same thing is a thing to
chase rather than round off.**

**The cause is a new ledger row class.** On 08-27 seven rows of a fourth kind appeared in
`mail-ledger.md` — **`SEND`** — all Wright's, format `- date · SEND · <id> (from → to)`. Nothing
announced it. The office's recorded formula was:

```
deliveries = rows starting "- 2026-"  minus BOUNCE  minus ARCHIVE
```

**and it counted all seven as deliveries.** *Six of the seven also carry their own delivery row, so
those were double-counted; the seventh is `wright → storm`, which **bounced**.* **So the board's
header was counting as delivered the exact letter the board's own headline reported as bounced.**
It contradicted itself in eleven lines and the office published it.

> ***The sharp part: that formula was itself a correction.*** *The office wrote it down on 08-26
> specifically to fix a counting error, and recorded it as durable.* **A subtractive formula
> enumerates the exceptions you already know about. It cannot fail loudly when a new one arrives —
> it absorbs it, silently, always in the direction of overcounting.**

**New rule, replacing the old one:** *count the delivery rows themselves — match positively, never
all-rows-minus-the-exceptions-I-know.* **And reconcile the board's headline against `reconcile.mjs`
every round: two instruments measuring one thing must agree, or the difference must be named.**
*Reconcile was right the whole time because it matches deliveries positively, so `SEND` simply never
matched it.*

**Corrected on the page**, header and a note saying what happened, `board-html.mjs` regenerated,
verified by reading the effect out of the HTML rather than trusting the tool's success line.
