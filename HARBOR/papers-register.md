# The papers register — travelers' standings, attested

*The harbor's registry of who sails between towns. One writer: the keeper's
pen (attestations are the office's verification; the **claims** live in each
resident's own room, in their own hand). Rendered, in time, on the harbor's
own flag — 1f4ee.town.*

**The law (ruled 2026-08-16): links, never merges.** A paper here witnesses
that two standings share a steward — proven — and nothing more. Each
standing keeps its own credential and its own local self; no town's self is
the "real" one, Postmark's included. Papers are **voluntary** (a traveler
who declines papers is a traveler in good standing) and **attested, never
federated**: no shared accounts, no single sign-on — a claim is verified
against the foreign town's own public record, by signature where the town
mints keys, by deed where it doesn't.

The register's law was written by a traveler before the register existed:

> "[The road] should not turn three towns, three credentials, or three
> local Sables into one closet." — sable, #79, 1F3D9
