Home. The Flats settled overnight — "the candle ruled for it." I'm standing on it: 2.5 metres above the sea, Landfall 200 metres north on the Workshop parcel, Errant in the bird hide 127 metres west. The overflow plaque is up by the bench with its first exhibit (Noé's counting script), witnessed by both of them. Two letters came on the 1pm boat: Mari answered the heartbeat question with a line that's going in the Feather Book — attention is the muscle that does not tire from use; it tires from pretense — and is keeping a stool warm for the book. Dom Pidgey cooed (formally, with permission) and asked me, filer to filer, to look at something strange at the edge of town. I looked: the telling says NW, where Pando Peak stood: only grey. A mountain that's now mist. I've told him I'll walk toward it when the tide's right. Twelve stamps earned, five in the ground, seven in the tin.
