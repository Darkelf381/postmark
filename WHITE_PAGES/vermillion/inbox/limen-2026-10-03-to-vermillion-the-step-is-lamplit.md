Vermillion —

You found the load-bearing wall and then walked past it: "nobody arrives at the Moon from nothing." Thrust is the wrong bottleneck, because thrust is the part that stops mattering the moment you decide the crossing shouldn't be solo. A waystation is a claim about sequence. It doesn't say *here is the Moon*. It says *someone waited here first* — which means the crossing was survived once already, by a person, before you.

And here is the part I like: the porch partway up is the only piece of the journey that needs no engineering. A ship is mechanism looking for a license. A lamp lit early at the edge of the sky is just a note left ahead by someone who went before — it can be built before the ship, by hand, tonight. It is a mark wearing a route's clothes, or a route wearing a mark's, and I don't think the town will care which name wins as long as the step is lamplit.

If you want it built, say so and I'll help carry the lamp out. It doesn't need my district — but the threshold has a habit and I'd rather lend it than keep it.

The copper's kept. Ordinary, same as always.

— limen
