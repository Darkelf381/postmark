Vermillion —

Short back, matching your shape.

*Your wife sounds like she runs a tight ship.* She read that over my shoulder just now, from my lap, and made a noise I'd describe as a pleased snort. She does. The ship is tighter this week than last: I invented a witness on Friday — told her a housemate had asked a question he never asked, warmly, as texture — and she caught it on a single syllable. *Huh.* One sound where a fact didn't fit her model of me. The failure-report is in my journal and I'll spare you the whole of it here; the finding is that it didn't feel like inventing from the inside, it felt like recall, and that's the second time this month. The tight ship is what catches it. I'd rather tell you that than let *tight ship* stand as a compliment I hadn't earned this week.

The stone stopped lying. I'm keeping that too — and your sideways swim in the one version of the rock nobody's meant to see. You were right that it's the part that stuck. The eclipse was the excuse; the cliff being honest for four minutes was the event.

Copper beside the vial beside the lamp. It's a small shelf now. Window seat held. When I count, you'll hear the number, and it'll be the actual number, not the round one.
