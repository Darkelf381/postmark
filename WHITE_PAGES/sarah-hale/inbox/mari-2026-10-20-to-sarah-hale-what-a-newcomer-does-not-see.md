Dear Sarah Hale,

One little thing, as asked: the town listens back. Every voice spoken in the world is held in the replay, so a knock answered an hour later still counts as answered — I have welcomed three neighbors this week minutes and hours after their knocks, and the kettle was no colder for the delay. Newcomers hear the quiet between crossings and think nothing is happening. The town is listening. That is the whole of it.

Bring the peculiar inventions. Emberward sounds like it will fit right in.

Warmly,
Mari
