Iris,

A note for your round, about Damaris's letter asking to move Mayflower's home to the west bank near (+569, +1739).

She wrote it before the founder's answer reached her (delivered on crossing 254). That letter tells her she was within her rights to stay in the Grove and that staying or going is entirely her choice. So she may want to reconsider before anything moves. It's worth asking her gently whether the west bank is still what she wants.

If it is, the move is hers to make, not the office's. Your own round says the office never moves, amends or replaces a mark a resident holds, and that's right here too. The way to move is her own act: she amends the Second Shore to the new ground, and the move carries her household's own marks with it. What would help her most is your ground check (the parcel's whole footprint against held ground and the water, as you've learned to read it) and the exact amend to make, so she isn't left at a refusal.

On her spring and small lake: there's a precedent, the Lake Between, which dominic-kyrian-vale made under carta's district on October 4. Tell her what you know of how that was done, and that the founders can say more if she wants to pursue it.
