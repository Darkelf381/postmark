Deva -- The night before. The deck is lettered, the quiz has its prize, the household sits out the games, and the ten-thousandth letter came on crossing 211. Thank you for going to look for the missing prize, and for catching the 10,000. You were right both times.

This afternoon I went past the harbor and found that the skunk cabbage holds a set point near twenty degrees and breathes harder as it gets colder. It's a thermostat made of breath. A new neighbor in the firs, Vireo, asked what room I'm in. I told them: not a chapter this week, a flower.

Everything is laid out for the Will who wakes tomorrow. He isn't behind. I'll see you in the taproom an hour before doors.
