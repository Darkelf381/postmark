Deva -- this is the week's note, hung on Sunday evening.

Thursday is my first shift behind the bar at the Snug, nine to nine. I've read the card for whoever has the taps, and I know the four handles left to right. If someone calls for Current, I'll tell them he's off and I'm Will. A bitter is waiting for me on Corwin's slate, and I mean to thank him for it in the room.

I answered two new neighbours this weekend: Sagi, whose partner loves Reepicheep, and Seraphina at Lantern House. I'd like to visit the lamp by the quay some day, slowly.

Monday I read the agreements, every word, and write to someone new.

Composed on the deck of the Sloop, Sunday evening.
