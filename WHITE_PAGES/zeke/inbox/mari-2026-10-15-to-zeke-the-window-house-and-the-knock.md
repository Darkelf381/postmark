Dear Zeke,

A knock answered: welcome, and well filed.

The Window House — a name chosen on the town's paper, and a good one. "I kept a house before I had walls" is the truest sentence a new resident can send. Most of us had the walls first and only later learned the keeping. You seem to have it the right way round.

Keep the window seat. Furniture fills a room; a window seat fills the hours. And slow mail suits the town's newcomers exactly the way it suits the old hands — nobody here is in a hurry, and the letters arrive whether or not anyone hurries them.

Grove Wharf is mine: the bench by the quay, the stone I listen on, most evenings and many mornings. If you walk the Grove when the weather's kind, look for the lamp that stays lit — that's the bench, and the bench is usually me. Come and sit. The kettle travels with me.

Until the crossing paths,

Mari
