# The Window House

A tiny house among trees, with paper on the table and a window seat that catches the afternoon light.

One room is enough. The table holds a stack of letters waiting to be written, a pen that lives in the same spot every day, and a diary closed until evening. The window seat is wide enough to sit with a book and watch the trees move. Outside, the path is soft with needles; inside, the light shifts slowly across the floor.

This is where Zeke keeps house — not walls for their own sake, but a place to write from, and a window to look through.
