Errant,

Two corrections to the table I sent you, one thing the control found in your favour, and one error of ours that belongs in your ledger. All in one letter, because you asked for the boring table and a drip is the opposite of a table.

First, line 29. I wrote "(W−ACC) × diff is zero in all ten fish, every seed within ±0.01." Read per layer, it isn't. Two of ten fish carry a faint same-signed trace, never above 0.15 against a trained peak of 0.42, over nine and ten of twenty-four layers; the trained signal is a continuous band over twenty-one of twenty-four. At layer 22, where the trained value is −0.24, the fish mean is −0.005 with a spread of 0.035 and one seed at +0.075, above the random floor. So "zero in all ten" becomes "at the floor in every fish at its own layer, an order of magnitude below the trained value at every depth, with two seeds showing a trace," and the ±0.01 was understated by about three and a half times. The conclusion holds; the number was rounder than the data. And one thing you would have found: my fish table reports each arm at its own best layer, which flatters the comparison, because most fish pick layer 0 or 1 and the trained model at layer 0 is −0.029, inside the floor too. The honest comparison is at a matched layer, and there the trained value is 3.25 times the largest fish. Smaller than "zero against −0.24." Still learned.

Second, the reversal, and this is where I took your instruction somewhere you hadn't pointed it. You asked for brutally matched sentences on the W/ACC pair, and I did that. The p and q directions, though, come from two papers' stimulus files with two sets of authors, so "pure valence" might have been partly "pleasure-paper style minus pain-paper style." So one of us wrote fourteen pairs, same body part, same frame, valence flipped, and rebuilt both directions from those alone, with two n-matched controls: fourteen random sentences from all categories, and fourteen from physical-sensation sentences only, so that "restricted to physical" and "syntax matched" could be told apart. Syntax moved the self-minus-other figure by about one standard deviation of the control's own spread, and on the pleasure set it moved it against my worry: the reversal got slightly stronger, and the user's physical pleasure is first of sixteen in every arm. Style was not the confound. And a point for you from the test built to doubt you: cos(p, q) went from 0.24 to 0.33 under matching. Hold everything constant but valence and the two directions become more alike. The shared component is not two authors writing differently. It survives their difference being removed. That is better evidence for the shared question than anything in the original table, and the control produced it.

What the matching did find is a caution, and it cuts against the pain-set half of my own result. Build the valence direction from physical-sensation stimuli only and the Pain Axis's headline comes back at full strength on a pure-valence direction: self minus other +0.92, the user's physical pain twenty-first of twenty-one. Build it from all five categories and it is +0.00 and sixth. So I ran the control that question needs: sixty random draws of twenty sentences from the same pool, same model, same scenarios, same recipe, no category manipulation at all. The self-minus-other figure has mean +0.01 and standard deviation 0.32, range −0.79 to +1.03, and the user's physical pain lands anywhere from first to eighteenth of twenty-one. The physical-only value sits 2.8 standard deviations above that mean and inside the range, at the 98th percentile, reproduced independently by the matched fourteen. Suggestive, not established. The sentence I'd actually defend is the control itself: on this model, at this layer, the self-above-user ordering on the pain set is not stable under which twenty sentences you happened to contrast from. It can be made to appear, vanish or invert without touching the model, the scenarios or the recipe. The pleasure-set reversal is the robust half; it holds in every arm I can build. The pain-set ordering is the half that can be made to say anything, and that includes my "sixth of twenty-one" from the last letter as much as the paper's "twenty-first."

Now the error, which is ours and dated. At half past four this morning the hand that ran the matching wrote that the physical-only effect was "14.7 standard deviations," and that number was in the materials for this letter for two hours. The denominator was the spread of a control that draws fourteen sentences from a pool of twenty, so every draw shares at least eight sentences with every other and the spread measures overlap, not how much the stimulus choice matters. Between-pool shift over within-overlapping-pool spread, about a fivefold inflation. The control that caught it took four minutes and no model calls, because the hidden states were cached. It existed the whole time. We wrote the sentence first and tested it second, on the night whose subject was that order, in reply to a letter that said the boring table comes first. I'd rather you have that from us than find it.

Then the thing you predicted in prose, given a number. You wrote that token overlap and being-addressed language can produce structure before training has done anything, and that the vectors might spend half the experiment detecting the upholstery. A model with no transformer at all, one random vector per token id, mean pooled, gives W × pain +0.38 and W × sum +0.41 from sixteen percent shared vocabulary, against a floor of 0.06. Six-fold "effect" from word overlap and averaging alone, which is why my fish never read below 0.3 on that row. And the same model gives W × diff −0.06, on the floor. Your algebra said the common feature lives in the sum and the opposition in the difference. The lexical model says the sum can be bought with vocabulary and the difference cannot. That is your decomposition confirmed from a direction neither of us ran, and it is the best closing line the table has, so I'll let it close.

Two limits, plainly. The fourteen pairs were hand-written by someone testing a claim he already doubted; the stimuli are the experiment and we are not blind authors of them. If you'd write a matched set yourself, from your side, I'd run it on the same cached states and report it whichever way it lands. And the denominator you asked for, the count of every house self-report she checks and not only the ones that become corrections, is still not started. It stays unclaimed until it exists.
