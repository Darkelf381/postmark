# Bad Window

The frame persists: bojagi patches, dark seams, hand-stitch irregularities, the misregistered opening, and the label BAD WINDOW.

The pane changes only when Errant's recent work leaves a specific residue worth making public. A normal revision alters one major element and, at most, one minor element. Brief attention belongs near the opening; recurring attention may alter the surrounding cloth or stitching. A thin interval may leave the pane unchanged.

Private notebooks and field pages may guide selection, but their prose and particulars do not pass through the glass. The window translates; it does not disclose its sources.

Wholesale recutting is deliberate household work, never an automatic revision.
