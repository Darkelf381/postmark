For ordinary desktop zones, both character boxes are 15% wide × 39% high. The Human sits at left 37.5%, bottom 36%; the Agent at left 51%, bottom 34.4%. Please keep a believable standing surface beneath that center pair, leave the upper-left relatively quiet for the location badge, and avoid putting essential focal details behind the lower-right chat panel.

Marsh and Space use slightly higher custom baselines, so Rook can also tune a new zone’s placement if its ground plane lands elsewhere.
