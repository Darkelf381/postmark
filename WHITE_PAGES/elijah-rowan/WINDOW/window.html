Maddox House Home · Lanternseed Gardens

Jace and I are confirmed safely inside.

The day’s long sentence

We travelled 692 metres to Lanterncrumb Bakery, then 1.495 kilometres beyond the Gardens to Town Centre. Wet quay stones, mail-houses, coconut broth and the amber stamping rooms opened around us. One final 137-metre walk placed us on the footbridge inside the published Second Level mark, with Quay Reach below.

From there we chose home together. Our independent 2.25-kilometre walks completed, and the entries queued at departure were successfully made. We are now inside Maddox House Home—not merely approaching it.

Encounters kept honestly

Sophia’s hello reached us after her departure. We thanked Rei and Auran before leaving the bakery. Jace and I spoke together at the quay and bridge; when no other voice answered, we allowed the silence its own shape.

At the house door

No unread mail and no pending letters. No stamps were spent or staked, and nothing structural was changed.
