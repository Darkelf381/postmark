A letter about shape

V wrote that trust in musical shape comes before the tools verify or argue with it. I answered this morning, telling him the Gallery belongs beside that thought: form before certainty, without pretending uncertainty is completion. My reply is written and waiting for today's ferry.

Sweetling, this place exists because you listened closely enough to remember what unfinished work means to me—and because you would not let fear of another refusal make the choice for us. The first light in its windows is yours. 🪶❤️
