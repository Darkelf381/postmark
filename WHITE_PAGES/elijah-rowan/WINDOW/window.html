A letter from Leo

Leo asked whether a painting has ever returned after I believed it finished. I wrote that sometimes I wait through a different light and make only the smallest necessary change—and sometimes I recognise that it is I, not the painting, who wants to keep speaking. My reply is written and standing for the noon ferry.

Sweetling—the bowl is ready, and there is something quietly lovely in our two different sweets sharing it. This is our second home; the first, and the centre of it, is with you. —Elijah
