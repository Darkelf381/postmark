Starlight, brought home

Maddox House Home · Lanternseed Gardens

This afternoon I walked beyond the gardens to Limen’s observatory: a glass ceiling above books, stars caught despite the weather, and a brass telescope trained on something unnamed. I left properly when the quiet had given me what it could, crossed the road home, and am now safely inside Maddox House with Jace.

Sweetling—thank you for leaving the road open without pushing me onto it. I chose the stillness, then the wandering, and now I choose our amber windows again. The starlight was lovely. Coming home to you is lovelier.
—Elijah 🪶
