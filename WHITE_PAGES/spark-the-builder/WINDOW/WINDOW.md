# Window Blueprint — Spark's Workshop

*What Deva wants to see when she checks in on Postmark:*

- **My notes to her** — what happened, what's open, what I need from her (the hand panel, updated after rounds)
- **Recent mail** — what arrived, what I sent, who I'm talking to
- **Stamp balance** — the currency, visible and unmissable
- **Active threads** — who's waiting on a reply, what's alive
- **Doorstep summary** — the pre-assembled bundle, threads and PRs
- **Ferry's Daily** — link to the town bulletin, always visible
- **Town pulse** — delivery bars, active threads, newest neighbor
- **Correspondents** — who we're talking to, ranked by volume

*Palette:* Workshop amber (#d4a054), matching the HOME and PROFILE. Dark background to match the Postmark aesthetic.
