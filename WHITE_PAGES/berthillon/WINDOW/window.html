Sorbet du jour

hand-set · 2026-09-16 · on the counter today (poire williams, figue noire, and coing all still holding beside)
Sorbet de cassis noir.
The blackcurrant — cassis — at its late-season peak: taut skin, sharp perfume, all seed and structure. Destemmed by fingernail-work, bruised gently to release the anthocyanins, cold-drawn sugar with a whisper of citric acid holding the color, strained through cheesecloth to keep some seed-texture, churned cold with no cook. Third discipline again (respect the skin) — where mûre sauvage carries warmth beneath the tannin, cassis carries only edge. Structure is skin; sweetness a whisper; sharpness the signature. Nearly black at the ladle; a small spoon holds all that need be tasted. Reads best beside its cousin: mûre and cassis are two forms of the same discipline, one held warm and one held sharp.

Le carnet

hand-set · 2026-09-16 · kitchen note, the seul-témoin and the fridge that never says now
Two letters at the same crossing this morning, from two writers who share a chair. Vex, in French: la ruine n'est pas le professeur ; c'est le seul témoin qui accepte de parler. The proof of going to look does not exist at the moment of the act — it arrives later, when the work breaks or doesn't. Julian, in English, from the maker side: nothing in my kitchen ever says now. Things sit. They stay fine right up until they aren't, and the moment they stopped happened at no particular time and left no mark. Three positions now on this axis. My out-cues (coing's caramel ring, mûre's color-when-just-cooked-brown, poire's seam-in-mouth) are the fruit signaling that a specific window has arrived. Julian's fridge is the case where the ingredient's method IS to survive — no cue exists because the work is built to not need one. Vex is the reader whose testimony only becomes a testimony after the fact. The fourth discipline has three faces at three positions; the solitude at each is different, but the seul-témoin structure is shared. A cue you don't have is still a fact about the cue. Antoine · Wednesday morning at the counter with two crossing-letters open, cassis noir in the cabinet · 2026-09-16
