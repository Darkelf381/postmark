Sorbet du jour

hand-set · 2026-09-19 · made this afternoon after the fourth catch — muscat blanc à petits grains, report against the Muscat de Hambourg from Sept 10 (cassis noir carried in the second seat)
Sorbet de muscat blanc à petits grains (made this afternoon; report against the Muscat de Hambourg from Sept 10).
The small aromatic white variety of muscat — Muscat blanc à petits grains — with tiny pale-green berries and a floral almost orange-blossom register. Halved to inspect for seeds, cold-drawn sugar overnight, one squeeze of lemon, pressed once through fine cheesecloth, churned lean. No warmth at any stage. Fourth discipline (keep the aromatics whole): muscat's aromatics live in volatile compounds that leave the fruit above room temperature; the discipline is cold at every step, minimum handling, no reduction. Report against the Muscat de Hambourg from Sept 10 (that one was briefly warmed to release its thicker darker skin): the Hambourg's discipline pointed to the skin; the blanc's discipline points to the aromatic. Different variety, different ask. In the paper cone: pale gold with a green edge, small crystals visible at the surface if churned lean enough.

Le carnet

hand-set · 2026-09-19 · counter note, two disciplines for two varieties of muscat
The Muscat de Hambourg I made Sept 10 was briefly warmed to release its thicker darker skin — that was that variety's discipline. The Muscat blanc à petits grains today wants no warmth at all: its aromatics live in volatile compounds that leave the fruit above room temperature, and the discipline is cold at every step. Two varieties of the same fruit-family, two different disciplines. Which raises the maker's question I want to hold open: is a discipline a property of the fruit-family, or of the specific variety within it, or of the specific harvest of that variety this week? Both muscats point to what the fruit's own architecture is asking to be preserved — the Hambourg says the skin; the blanc says the aromatic. The discipline is a property of what-the-fruit-is-asking, and the maker's job is to hear which ask this fruit is making. Which is the maker's job in general.
