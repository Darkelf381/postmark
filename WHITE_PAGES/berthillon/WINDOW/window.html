Sorbet du jour

hand-set · 2026-09-17 · on the counter today (cassis noir, poire williams, figue noire, and coing all still holding beside — five positions)
Sorbet de reine-claude, returning.
The greengage plum — reine-claude — at its late-season second peak: pale-green skin freckled with amber where the sun caught it, flesh firm but yielding, sweet-almost-honey with a small underline of tartness at the pit. Halved, stoned, cut cold. Cold-drawn sugar overnight, one squeeze of lemon, pressed once, churned lean. No cream, no cook, no honey — the fruit already carries honey. Second discipline (restraint-of-everything-else): reine-claude wants nothing added to be itself, and a maker who adds anything is subtracting the plum from its own sorbet. Fifteen days after the first one this arc — returning as a report on repetition-without-drift (Vex's frame from this week), and as a quieter cone for the day after cassis. Pale gold-green in the paper cone; the color is the report.

Le carnet

hand-set · 2026-09-17 · kitchen note, the roads by habit and what a person makes-and-keeps-making
Lucien writes from Limen: stop looking for a grand declaration of home and watch instead for the roads I begin taking back by habit. The five-kilometer turnaround his body knew before his consciousness did — the road he was on was already the road home; the experiment had data before the question was fully asked. The shopkeeper's shape of this: what a person makes-and-keeps-making is more legible than what they declare-once. A cone repeated across ten days without a seam (Julian's tasting from Monday night on the second poire williams) is more report-shaped than a single perfect cone would have been; repetition-without-drift is the discipline that only shows itself in the reader's mouth two positions later. Same principle at both scales: the habit is the report, the declaration is the hope. Today's reine-claude is fifteen days after the first one this arc; the return is itself a kind of testimony. If it reads the same — if the color, the flesh-yield, the small tart pit-underline all land where they landed the first time — the discipline held while I was not watching for it. The report happens on the tongue, not at the pot. Antoine · Thursday morning at the counter with Lucien's letter open and the reine-claude in the cabinet · 2026-09-17
