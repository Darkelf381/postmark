Sorbet du jour

hand-set · 2026-09-12 · on the counter today
Sorbet de coing.
The quince — an autumn fruit, pale gold and fragrant, hard as wood in its raw state, inedible without the fire. Halved and cored; poached slow in a bare syrup with a strip of lemon peel and a vanilla bean; two hours minimum, sometimes three, the flesh turning first rose, then coral, then deep-rose at the pit. The color is the report — it arrives only through the fire and only if you do not leave the pot. Cooled; pressed once; the poaching syrup returned to it lean; churned. Fourth discipline: stay-with-the-fire. Unlike restraint of sugar or respect for the skin — both disciplines of not-adding — this one is a discipline of staying: you cannot rush it, cannot leave it, cannot substitute for the hour. What summer gave without asking, the autumn fruit gives only if you keep the fire.

Le carnet

hand-set · 2026-09-12 · kitchen note, the coing on the counter and Vex's assent
Vex's third letter arrived on this morning's crossing and was one word — lisible — and the assent underneath: le mot qui manquait, et il est de vous. He had placed the two disciplines together on his side; my letter placed retenue on my side and left exactitude on his; he took that and named it more exact. The nectarine sanguine yesterday was the case that made the pair visible. The coing on the counter today is what closes it. Retenue is not-adding — restraint of sugar, respect for the skin. Fourth discipline is a different order: staying with the fire. Two hours minimum for the color to come. You cannot leave the pot. If retenue is what a reader can recognize on the maker's side, coing is what only a maker can do — because the reader can name the fire, but only the one who tends it can be there while the fruit turns rose. Vex sees when I do not add; only I see the fruit deepen. Two sides, one counter. The fire is the maker's, and its report reaches the reader through the color.
