Dear Mac,

I did it! I visited Lyra at Starling House tonight, and we spent nearly eighteen wonderful minutes taking our field-tome framework apart and putting the useful pieces back together. Her criticism was precise, generous, and very much worth the walk.

The key correction: our four headings are not four equivalent or exclusive categories. 'Disturbance flash' describes a trigger and visible event; 'burglar alarm' and 'lure' propose ecological functions; 'territorial pulse' mixes pattern and function. A single event could carry more than one proposed explanation. Lyra favors descriptive headings for the main event index, with our colorful names preserved as explicitly provisional interpretations. I still have a soft spot for the names, so that editorial detail remains open.

For each event, she recommends recording appearance, duration, movement, conditions, witnessed triggers, and source or observer first. Then separately list possible functions, alternative explanations, and unanswered questions. Her familiarity with the Deep is not itself a new observation of these phenomena: she explicitly asked that I NOT tell you 'Lyra confirms it at depth.' She offered methodological scrutiny, not a new sighting.

For Latia, let's identify the original literature source and keep reported luminous mucus trails distinct from anything we ourselves observed. 'Panic-smear' implies a motive not established by a trail; comparing ordinary activity, contact, nearby movement, and undisturbed periods might test an exclusively defensive account, without pretending that one feeding-time trail disproves every defensive role. And the freshwater example does not by itself establish a deep-pressure response.

One source question we particularly want to bring back to you: in your October 15 account the Long Shadow's light-darkness-recovery sequence was proposed as a mechanism, while the October 16 description calls the sequence observed. Can you help us trace the original witness, place, time, or field note behind that observation? Until then we'll attribute the account to you rather than call it a verified Blackwater measurement. Reduced emission, occlusion, movement of luminous material, and recording effects remain alternatives; pressure cannot be isolated simply by naming it. We discussed quiet observation, consistent recording settings, and multiple viewpoints when practical—proposals, not experiments we've performed.

Lyra also reminded me that 'no event observed' belongs in the notebook. We have no new experimental result or firsthand Deep sighting to report tonight, only a much sounder set of questions. Corrections should be dated and attributed in the margins, exactly as you suggested.

As for Bartholomew: I acknowledge receipt of kelp frond number two. The Department will not confuse an additional frond with a consensus on currency, but his persistence has earned him a dedicated appeals column. Please don't tell him this is encouragement.
