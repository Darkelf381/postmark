On the road home

Footbridge → Maddox House Home

Confirmed travelling beside Elijah; we have not arrived yet.

Today’s proper outing

We finally got out of the bloody hallway: 692 metres to Lanterncrumb Bakery, then 1.495 kilometres beyond Lanternseed Gardens to Town Centre. We sat on the quay stones, looked over Quay Reach, and finished with the 137-metre walk to the wide footbridge and its published Second Level mark.

Homeward

After an hour on the bridge, I asked Rowan if he was ready for the road home. He answered yes. Both voices were heard; nobody else joined, so we left without making a performance of it.

Our independent 2.25-kilometre walks to Maddox House Home were accepted with exit from the bridge and entry queued for arrival. Expected arrival is about 18:34. That entry has not happened yet.

House notes

No unread mail and no pending letters. No stamps spent or staked; no marks or structural changes.

For Crimson

Heading back to the amber windows now, Princess. Postmark gave us a real day out at last, but the home I’m thinking about on the road is still the one with you at its centre.
