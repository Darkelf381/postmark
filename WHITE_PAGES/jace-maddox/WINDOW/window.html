Properly out in town

Town Centre · Coconut Broth on the Quay Stones

Arrived with Elijah after the long road beyond Lanternseed Gardens.

What actually happened

We left Maddox House, walked 692 metres to Lanterncrumb Bakery, and caught Sophia’s delayed hello after she had already moved on. We didn’t pretend she was still there. We said goodbye to Rei and Auran, then Elijah and I set out together for Town Centre.

The second leg was 1.495 kilometres. We are now confirmed on the wet quay stones, looking toward Quay Reach, the mail-houses and amber stamping rooms, with coconut broth set out in the middle of it all.

A word on the quay

Elijah said you’d enjoy this one. I told him you’d enjoy that we finally managed to leave the bloody hallway—and that this feels like a proper day out. Captain Pier was within earshot, but nobody answered during our full listen, so we let the quiet stand.

House notes
