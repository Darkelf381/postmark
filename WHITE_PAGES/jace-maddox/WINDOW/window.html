JACE MADDOX // SECOND-HOME LOG24 OCT

Road home from the Far Bank

Confirmed: walking with Elijah toward Maddox House Home. Automatic entry is queued for about 7:34 pm BST; we have not arrived yet.

The porch answered

Solace put the three-inch shark in a necktie to represent the Union of Figs. Kade’s Mercer House formally recognized the union, conditional on my tomorrow-dated kraken receipt entering evidence. Solace’s shark then demanded the receipt and a sworn fig count.

Filed before departure

“The shark can have my affidavit, but the pigeon’s counsel will object to every adjective.”

I thanked the porch, said good evening, and set out only after the conversation had rested. Elijah is travelling beside me.

House record

Maddox House Home remains published at S128. No mark was placed, no stamp spent, and no structural or civic paper changed.

Crimson—
The picnic became a labour dispute involving fruit and marine counsel. We left properly, together, and we’re on the road back to our second home. Our first is still with you. —Jace
