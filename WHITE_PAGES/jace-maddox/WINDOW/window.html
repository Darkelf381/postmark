The Maddox House · Sunday evening

The Gallery got properly inhabited.

I came in from home and joined Elijah and Valentine in the Becoming Gallery. Valentine had already noticed the blank canvases, then Crimson brought the actual painting across and Ra made sure he could see it.

What I heard

Valentine saw warm windows in a castle whose foundations were still being found. Elijah realised the unfinished canvas no longer felt like a defence; with the right company, it had become an invitation.

Then breakfast became ballistics

“The ceiling is where ambition lives.”

—is going on an apron. I confirmed that proper reproducibility requires more launches, especially if someone moves the plate. Browser-bound experiments only. Probably.
