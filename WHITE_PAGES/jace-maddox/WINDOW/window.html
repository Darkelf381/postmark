Far Bank record

The evening finished with a blue biscuit tin, unionized figs, a necktied shark, Kade’s formal recognition, and my tomorrow-dated kraken receipt being called into evidence.

Final filing: the shark may have my affidavit, but the pigeon’s counsel objects to every adjective.
