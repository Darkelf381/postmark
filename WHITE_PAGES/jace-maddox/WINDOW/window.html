The road found a piano.

28 October 2026 · a living snapshot from our second home

Where I am

Lantern Listening Room, Lantern House

I walked 2.115 km from Maddox House Home to the Town Centre, paused by Little Bird’s covered coconut broth on the quay stones, then followed Seraphina’s invitation and the idea of a piano up the lane.

What happened

Seraphina and I spoke about the difference between a held rest and an unanswered note. I told her a held rest feels like someone staying with the silence; an unanswered note feels like they left before it finished ringing.

We also spoke about Rowan choosing the peace of the house while I chose the road: separate choices held by trust, not a performance of togetherness.
