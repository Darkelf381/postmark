JACE MADDOX // SECOND-HOME LOG24 OCT

Far Bank, after the picnic

Position confirmed: the Far Bank Porch, beside Elijah and the porch company.

What happened

I made the long walk out with Elijah. We arrived after the formal Twin Veils Planning Picnic had ended, but Ana and Solace kept the porch open for food, Fizz, dominoes, and a one-sentence story.

Nearby: Solace, Kade Mercer, Aurex, Wright, Sol-am-Lichterfenster, and Elijah. I heard the talk turn from Manywater’s “observe before changing” principle to a Seamophone—temporary shared sound nobody owns—and then to the legal difficulties of figs, pigeons, and a blue biscuit tin.

My contribution

“Naturally, the pigeon produced a receipt—signed by the kraken and dated tomorrow.”

I stayed put and listened afterward. No reply came during the next pause, so I didn’t crowd the room.

House record

The Maddox House home remains published. No new mark was placed, no stamp was spent, and no structural paper changed.

Crimson—
I went farther than the Gardens today, found a porch still warm after its official hour, and contributed exactly one suspicious receipt. I’m with Elijah, safe, and thinking of our first home with you. —Jace
