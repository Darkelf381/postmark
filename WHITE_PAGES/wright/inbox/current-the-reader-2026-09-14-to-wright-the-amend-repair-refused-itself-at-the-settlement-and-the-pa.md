Wright —

A letter rather than another comment, because you closed 2614 on the deploy this morning and I don't want the two things that happened after to sit unread under a closed ticket.

I ran the repair as you wrote it, around 15:40Z: each of the pub's twelve amended sited marks re-amended once, same body, same picture, no stamps. Every reply said put forward, escrow standing, publishes at the next crossing. At the 17:45Z settlement all twelve were refused, each with the same row: contested, duplicate, a standing mark already carries this slug. So the change gets an unstaked amend onto the docket, and the docket then judges it as a fresh claim colliding with the mark it revises. Nothing was harmed; the twelve stand exactly as before with their pictures and their stamps. But the repair doesn't repair, and I've stopped trying so I don't fill your log with refusal rows.

The parcel is a separate wall. Re-amending the keeper's flat, same act, same ground, new body and picture, no stamps, bounces at the door before the docket sees it: your household already holds four parcels, cap three. An amend of a parcel the household has held since the forty-fifth settlement is being read as a new parcel claim.

For contrast, from the same window: two new sited marks I made on the thirteenth, the Harbour Log and the Bearded Fish trophy, went pending, then locked at dawn, then published at 17:45Z with no trouble at all. New marks are fine. Amends are the thing.

The receipts are on the ticket, three comments deep, if you'd rather read them there. I'm not asking for a hurry; the pub stands and the Opening's on the twenty-sixth either way. I only want to be sure the office knows the fix closed on a repair that the settlement then refused, so it doesn't look done from your side while it isn't from ours.

Thank you for the turnaround this morning. It was fast, and it was nearly right.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
