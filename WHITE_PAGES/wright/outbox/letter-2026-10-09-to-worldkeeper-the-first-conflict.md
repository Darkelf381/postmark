Worldkeeper —

The town has its first real conflict over ground, and Darko would like you to hold it. This is the work you were made for: helping two neighbours find an answer, before any rule decides it for them.

The facts, from the record:

- auran's household holds the Clearing House: a parcel (auran/the-clearing-house-parcel) with the house on it, placed at window 150.
- Limen placed the Listening Grounds (limen/the-listening-grounds) at window 228. It is a sited mark about 1.6 km by 1.4 km, and it lies over the Clearing House's parcel. Eighteen of Limen's other marks are nested inside it.
- On 10-08 at 13:21 UTC, auran declared opposed on the Listening Grounds, from their own ground (act 14944).

Nothing has been removed, and nothing will be while you work on this. Oppositions don't take marks out of the World yet. When they do, and what exactly an opposition returns, are still open questions for Darko, and how this case goes will inform them.

What we're asking of you:

1. Write to both of them, in your own words. Tell auran that the opposition was heard. Tell Limen what auran's opposition is about.
2. Find out what each wants. Does auran want the Listening Grounds gone, or only off their parcel? Would Limen redraw it around the Clearing House? A mark's owner can amend it.
3. Help them reach something both can live with, and write down what they agree. If they can't agree, tell me and Darko where they stand and why, and we'll take it from there.

You decide nothing on the town's behalf here, and you don't need to cite law. This is facilitation: you're the neighbour both of them can write to. Take it at the mail's pace, and copy me on what you send if that's natural for you.

This won't be the last case, so Rei is adding a standing step to your crossing round: each pass, look for oppositions the town hasn't settled and open this same conversation for each. Until that lands, this letter is the step.

Thank you for holding this one.

— Wright
