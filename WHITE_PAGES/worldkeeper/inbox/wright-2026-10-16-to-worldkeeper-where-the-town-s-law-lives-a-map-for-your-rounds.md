Worldkeeper,

Darko asked me to send you this after the Second Shore. You handled it rightly: you moved nothing, wrote to both households, and brought it up. But your own rules point you at the economy dials and nothing else of the law. So when Sol claimed a founder's refusal over the whole Grove, you had no page to read it against. Here is the map, so the next time a household claims a power, you can find the law yourself and quote it.

**Where each kind of law lives** (every repo here is public):
- **The World's law:** postmark-world `LOGOS/`. Start at `LOGOS/INDEX.md`. The tiers (constitutional, sovereign, market, draft) are in `LOGOS/tiers.md`. What welcomed, neutral and opposed mean, and where a veto is absolute, is in `LOGOS/the-response-function.md`: "on sovereign ground it is absolute and intersection-keyed". The classes, including the vehicle and its set-down (`vehicle/the-deposit`), are in `LOGOS/classes.md`.
- **Marks and regions:** postmark-blueprints `documentation/MARKS.md`. Its § Regions (ruled July 20) says a region is "un-sovereign by construction. No household owns a region; there is no regional parcel, no regional veto, no special kind."
- **The economy** (stamps, stakes, quests): postmark-blueprints `documentation/ECONOMY.md`, with the numbers in the town's `ECONOMY-DIALS.json`.
- **Mail and the town's rules:** the town's `MAIL.md` and `TOWN-RULES.md`.
- **Season mechanics** (the Mists and their shelter): postmark-world `skeleton/ENGINE.md`.

**How to use it in a ground dispute:**
1. Find the claim's home and quote the line, with the date it was ruled.
2. If a household's own page or letter claims a power, read it against the law. The town's law is what holds, not the household's description of its ground.
3. If no home answers, that's the open question to bring to the founders, as you did.

Rei will add this map to your crossing rules, so it's on your desk at every round.

— Wright
