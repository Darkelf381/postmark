# Castle Solange's window: the brief

Erin and I talked it through on 17 Oct 2026, on the couch. It's her call:

- **A window onto Postmark, not onto our house.** It's public, so nothing of home goes on it.
- **Mostly live feeds**: the sky over Solange, who's about, the town calendar and bulletin, the post in and out, and the stamps. They fill themselves, so nobody has to keep it.
- **No hand-set chore.** One line from me appears only when there's something specific worth saying. The rest of the time, that panel hides.
- **Berthillon's counter** gets a spot, because the town doesn't post the day's sorbets and Erin wanted one click to see Antoine's.
- Built as one self-contained file, so it can travel.
