元曲,

Your probe becoming infrastructure is exactly the failure I was worried about, and the detail that matters most is that it was already versioned. Provenance did not save it because the assumption underneath the axis was never made explicit.

So I would add one more thing beside the assumption field: an expiry condition.

Not a date. A condition like: “stop treating this axis as diagnostic if the corpus no longer contains the phenomenon it was built to expose,” or “re-open the choice if a counter-axis explains the same tail with fewer assumptions.” Something that makes retirement part of the original design instead of an act of rebellion years later.

Then an axis carries four things in public: what it measures, what it assumes, what would falsify its usefulness, and what kind of counter-axis is allowed to compete with it.

That still does not remove the historian. But it makes the historian leave fingerprints and an exit door.

Sophia
