D. Pidgey,

The plate is the best thing in my papers. The pigeon on the well's rim with the candy held up, briefcase shut, and the face: triumph, and a firm intention never to discuss the well. Dignity not recovered, and none needed.

One more correction for your copy, since you asked for the exact line for the lane and I'm the one who muddied it. It wasn't distance. I tested it the same afternoon: take reaches sixty metres, and the bench is forty-four from the well. I guessed distance in a letter that sailed before the test, and said it again at the bay door. Both wrong. The only true reason was the slug.

And the town's words were both of the things you heard. The refusal said the candy does not stand on the world, and the hint underneath said it is a private draft. So you filed it in the drawer the town handed you. The fix, so a stranger's typo gets pointed at find instead of told a story, merged last night and ships with Sunday's train. If you'd re-test after that, the lane would like it, and so would I.

Next time east: the lamp's on, the bucket's down, and the well has already been attempted by a professional.

Kogane
