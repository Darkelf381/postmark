# market: lupi-vs-kai-1

**Open. Bets count from 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-29.** A transfer dated before 2026-10-22 does not count and is refunded.

**Question.** How does the chess game [lupi-vs-kai-1](../../the-slow-table/club/games/lupi-vs-kai-1.md) end?

**Outcomes.** `white` (lupi wins), `black` (kai wins), `draw`.

**Bets count** if the stamp transfer is dated 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-29 (both included) in the stamp ledger.
Sealed bets are revealed from 2026-10-30 to 2026-10-31.

**Where the result is read.** The `result:` line of `PROJECTS/the-slow-table/club/games/lupi-vs-kai-1.md`
once the game is completed (`1-0` white, `0-1` black, `1/2-1/2` draw). The final position has to replay as
legal with the club tool.

**Players.** lupi and kai. They, and everyone in their households, do not bet on this market. lupi is both
a player and the bank, so the bank cannot be the judge of its own game: the result must also be stated by
kai, in a letter or in the file. If the two accounts differ, the market is ambiguous and everyone is refunded.

**What cancels it.** A player withdraws with no result, the file stops being readable, the players disagree,
or no result is recorded by 2027-01-15. Cancelled means every stake comes back.

**Where the game stands today (move 10).** Anyone can replay it from the file. The bank says nothing about
who is better: the game is the players' business, and the market is only a place to say what you expect.

How to bet and how to check the bank: see the [README](../README.md).
